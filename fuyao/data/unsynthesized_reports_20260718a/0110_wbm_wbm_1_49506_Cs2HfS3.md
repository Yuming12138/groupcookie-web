### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic stability.**  
The target Cs2HfS3 is reported at E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom, so it receives a near-maximum thermodynamic score. TS reinforces this because close thermodynamic analogues such as Cs2LiNbS4, Cs2TaAgS4, K2TiS3, CsPrS2, and CsGdS2 are also reported at 0 eV/atom or near the hull (TS). CS also supports the general stability of Cs–S ternaries, but CS is insufficient for Hf-specific phase competition because it mainly contains CsLnS2 rare-earth sulfides.

**F2 — Route 1 condition severity.**  
The direct route inherits sealed-vacuum ampoule synthesis from K2TiS3, including high-temperature annealing, long dwell, and controlled cooling (SS, TS). It avoids >1000 °C and high pressure, but it requires sealed handling of sulfur and air-sensitive alkali sulfides [Reasonable Inference]. CS provides insufficient evidence for this specific direct binary-sulfide route, so CS is neutral for Route 1 severity.

**F3 — Route 1 kinetics.**  
K2TiS3 is an excellent structural analogue with the same A2MS3 motif and edge-sharing MS5 trigonal bipyramids (SS, TS), which improves the structural-reconstruction score. However, its 6 d anneal indicates slow ordering/diffusion even for Ti, and Hf is expected to be more refractory [Reasonable Inference]. Therefore kinetics are only moderate despite strong structural guidance.

**F2 — Route 2 condition severity.**  
CS/SS/TS H2S syntheses use 1000 °C, Ar/H2S gas switching, and post-treatment washing, which are standard but hazardous. The route avoids sealed sulfur pressure and high pressure, but toxic H2S and multiple handling steps reduce the score (CS, SS, TS). The severity score is better than sealed sulfide routes, but safety burden is significant.

**F3 — Route 2 kinetics.**  
CS demonstrates rapid sulfidation for CsLnS2 from Cs2CO3 and rare-earth oxides at 1000 °C (CS), but HfO2 is not directly represented and is much more refractory [Reasonable Inference]. Formation of HfS5 trigonal-bipyramidal chains from oxide/carbonate precursors requires full anion exchange and structural reconstruction. Thus, although chemically plausible, this route has the weakest kinetic score.

**F2 — Route 3 condition severity.**  
The flux route follows sealed-tube, inert-atmosphere, slow-cooling procedures used for Cs2LiNbS4 (SS, TS). It operates at lower temperature than the direct route, but still requires sealed ampoules, volatile sulfur/polysulfides, and air-sensitive Cs sulfide fluxes. CS gives insufficient evidence for flux growth and is neutral for this route.

**F3 — Route 3 kinetics.**  
Flux growth benefits from enhanced mass transport, as shown by the 773 K Cs2LiNbS4 synthesis in A2S3 sulfide flux (SS, TS). This can partially overcome Hf diffusion limitations [Reasonable Inference]. The main uncertainty is that the flux analogue has tetrahedral NbS4 units rather than HfS5 chains, so nucleation of the exact Cs2HfS3 phase is not guaranteed.

**F4 — Amorphization tendency.**  
The target has a very large size/charge contrast between Cs+ and Hf4+, triggering the ionic-radius mismatch criterion [Reasonable Inference]. Its S environments involve face-, edge-, and corner-sharing octahedral connectivity, which triggers mixed-connectivity risk; similar complex connectivity is seen in SS/TS analogues. The atom-count trigger is not supported by the given target description, and CS/SS/TS do not provide enough evidence to claim a >40-atom unit cell.

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

Cs2HfS3 is likely a true ground-state phase because its reported energy above hull is exactly 0 eV/atom. The strongest support comes from SS/TS, especially K2TiS3, which closely matches the A2MS3 stoichiometry and MS5 trigonal-bipyramidal framework, while CS mainly supports the broader feasibility of Cs sulfide synthesis. The most critical enabling factor is the strong thermodynamic stability combined with known alkali-metal thiometallate synthesis in sealed sulfide or sulfide-flux environments. The most critical limiting factor is kinetic access to a Hf4+ sulfide framework, because Hf precursors are refractory and no direct Cs–Hf–S experimental analogue is provided. Overall, synthesis should be feasible but likely requires careful sulfur activity, Cs stoichiometry control, and inert sealed-tube processing.
