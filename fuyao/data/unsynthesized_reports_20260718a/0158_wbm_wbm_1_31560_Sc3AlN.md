### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

#### F1 — Thermodynamic stability

The target is reported at E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom, which places it in the highest thermodynamic stability bin (target). Thermodynamic analogues Sm3AlN, Lu3InN, Tb3InN, Gd3InN, and Tm3InN are also reported with E_hull = 0.0 eV/atom (TS1–TS5), supporting that this antiperovskite topology can be a ground-state motif. Chemically similar Ti3AlN is slightly above hull at 0.0297 eV/atom but still in the ≤0.05 eV/atom high-stability range (CS1). Remaining uncertainty is that computed hull stability does not guarantee an accessible kinetic pathway [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2/F3 — Route 1

Route 1 is supported by direct synthesis of Sm3AlN from rare-earth metal plus AlN in evacuated quartz tubes at 600–800 °C for up to 800 h (SS1, TS1). For Sc3AlN, a higher temperature is proposed because Sc is smaller and Sc/AlN reaction may be less facile than rare-earth metal/AlN systems [Reasonable Inference]. The severity score is reduced mainly by vacuum/sealed processing, long multi-step treatment, and air-sensitive Sc handling [Reasonable Inference; SS1/TS1 for sealed processing]. Kinetics are only moderate because AlN is refractory and the analogue required long annealing (SS1, TS1).

#### F2/F3 — Route 2

Route 2 is supported by structurally and thermodynamically similar R3InN antiperovskites made from RN and R2In binary precursors by arc-fusion and sealed-ampoule annealing at 1220 K for at least one week (SS2–SS5, TS2–TS5). The ScN + Sc2Al adaptation is a stoichiometric extrapolation from RN + R2In chemistry [Reasonable Inference]. Severity remains moderate because it requires alloy pre-synthesis, inert handling, sealed ampoules, and long annealing (SS2–SS5, TS2–TS5). Kinetics score higher than Route 1 because binary precursor matching should reduce long-range cation redistribution [Reasonable Inference], although ScN remains refractory.

#### F2/F3 — Route 3

Route 3 is supported by Ti3AlN, which has the same M3AlN stoichiometry and cubic Pm-3m structure as Sc3AlN (CS1; target). Ti3AlN synthesis used arc-melted Ti–Al master alloys, Ti/AlN/TiN powders, cold pressing, and sintering under vacuum/Ar at 1273–1673 K for 30–800 h (CS1). The route is penalized for high-temperature solid-state processing, multi-step handling, atmosphere control, and likely Al volatility/loss [Reasonable Inference; CS1 for method complexity]. Kinetics are supported by the exact structure match but limited by the very long equilibration times reported for the Ti analogue (CS1).

#### F4 — Amorphization tendency

The target has a simple cubic Pm-3m cell and a small formula cell, so the unit-cell-size amorphization risk is low (target). The target and analogues show regular corner-sharing octahedra plus face-sharing cuboctahedra, without reported edge-sharing or tilt complexity (target; CS1; SS1–SS5; TS1–TS5). A single amorphization trigger is assigned for substantial Sc/Al cation size and bonding-environment mismatch [Reasonable Inference]. Remaining uncertainty is that bulk solid-state routes usually crystallize readily, while any rapid-quench or thin-film route could increase amorphous fraction [Reasonable Inference].

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

Sc3AlN is likely a thermodynamic ground-state or near-ground-state phase because its reported energy above hull is 0.0000 eV/atom (target). The strongest support comes from same-topology nitride antiperovskites: Sm3AlN is directly Al/N matched (SS1, TS1), R3InN phases demonstrate binary nitride + intermetallic precursor routes (SS2–SS5, TS2–TS5), and Ti3AlN gives a chemically close M3AlN precedent (CS1). The most critical enabling factor is the robust cubic Pm-3m antiperovskite framework, which appears across multiple chemically related systems (CS1; SS1–SS5; TS1–TS5). The most critical limiting factor is kinetic: refractory ScN/AlN chemistry and slow ordering may require long sealed-ampoule anneals and careful regrinding [Reasonable Inference; long anneals in CS1, SS1, TS1, SS2–SS5, TS2–TS5]. The recommended route is ScN + Sc2Al because it best balances stoichiometric control, structural precedent, and precursor reactivity [Reasonable Inference from SS2–SS5/TS2–TS5].
