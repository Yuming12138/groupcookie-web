### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

#### F1 — Thermodynamic stability

The target E_hull is 0.0500 eV/atom, which receives a high score under the provided rubric because it lies at the ≤0.05 eV/atom boundary. TS data show several experimentally synthesized materials with similar hull energies, including RbMnTe₂ at 0.057 eV/atom (TS2), K₂CoS₂ at 0.0506 eV/atom (TS3), Rb₂CoS₂ at 0.0476 eV/atom (TS4), and CsMnAs at 0.0556 eV/atom (TS5). The uncertainty is that Rb₆Te₆S may be metastable relative to binary rubidium tellurides/sulfides, and the provided data do not list its competing decomposition phases.

#### F2/F3 — Route 1

Route 1 is moderately severe because related alkali tellurides require evacuated or inert sealed ampoules at high temperature, as seen for K₃SbTe₃, K₃AlTe₃, and Rb₂ZrTe₃ (CS1/CS3/CS4). The kinetic score is favorable because Rb–Te coordination environments and bond lengths are similar in Rb₂ZrTe₃ and Rb₂Te₃ (CS3/SS1), suggesting compatible local bonding. The main uncertainty is structural reconstruction: no exact cubic Fm-3m Rb₆Te₆S analogue is provided, so formation may compete with Rb₂Te₃, Rb₂S, or other polychalcogenides [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2/F3 — Route 2

Route 2 has similar severity to Route 1 because H₂ charged with chalcogen is a controlled and reactive atmosphere, as used in CS5, SS2, TS3, and TS4. Kinetically, carbonate/chalcogen gas routes can be fast, with 9–12 h syntheses reported for Rb₃SbSe₃-type and Rb₂CoS₂-type compounds (SS2/TS4), so precursor activity is good. The uncertainty is chemical transferability: the cited examples contain transition metals or pnictogens, whereas Rb₆Te₆S is a Rb–Te–S polychalcogenide; this limitation is a [Reasonable Inference] and motivates the expert penalty.

#### F2/F3 — Route 3

Route 3 has low thermal severity but high equipment severity because Rb₂Te₃/Cs₂Te₃ and Cs₂Se require 1000–2000 bar NH₃ autoclaves (SS1/SS4/TS1). The kinetic score is strong because ammonothermal transport can crystallize polytellurides at only ~500 K (SS1/SS4), avoiding long solid-state diffusion. The uncertainty is S incorporation: the provided ammonothermal structural analogues are Rb/Cs tellurides or selenides, not mixed Te/S compounds, so this is partly extrapolated [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2/F3 — Route 4

Route 4 is feasible only as an exploratory route because related chalcogenide crystals can be grown under chalcogen-loaded vapor conditions (CS5/SS3/TS4). Kinetics may benefit from vapor transport, but composition control for volatile Rb, S, and Te is likely difficult [Reasonable Inference]. Insufficient direct evidence from CS/SS/TS exists for CVT growth of Rb₆Te₆S specifically; therefore this route is scored below Routes 1–3.

#### F4 — Amorphization tendency

The target has only one cation type, so the cation-radius mismatch criterion is not triggered [Reasonable Inference]. The target structure description reports corner and edge sharing but not simultaneous face sharing, so the mixed-connectivity amorphization criterion is not fully triggered by the provided target description. The likely conventional Fm-3m cell may exceed 40 atoms [Reasonable Inference], giving one trigger and a low–medium amorphization risk.

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

Rb₆Te₆S is likely near the boundary between a ground-state and a shallow metastable phase because its E_hull is exactly 0.0500 eV/atom. The CS and SS analogues strongly support the existence of Rb/K alkali telluride and polytelluride frameworks synthesized by sealed ampoule, gas-flow, and ammonothermal routes (CS1/CS3/CS4/SS1/SS4). The most critical enabling factor is the chemical compatibility of Rb–Te coordination distances: the target Rb–Te distance of 3.65 Å matches the Rb–Te range in Rb₂ZrTe₃ and Rb₂Te₃ (CS3/SS1). The most critical limiting factor is stoichiometry/phase control in a volatile Rb–Te–S system, because related Rb tellurides show impurity and phase-control issues (CS3), and thermodynamic analogues can require narrow conditions or sensitive handling (TS1/TS2). Overall, synthesis appears plausible but will likely require sealed, inert, chalcogen-activity-controlled conditions.
