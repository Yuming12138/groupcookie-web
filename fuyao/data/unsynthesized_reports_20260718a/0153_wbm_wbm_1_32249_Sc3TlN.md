### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

#### F1 — Thermodynamic stability

The target has E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom, which places it at the top of the scoring rubric for thermodynamic stability (target). Several structurally and thermodynamically similar anti-perovskite nitrides, including Lu₃InN, Tm₃InN, Tb₃InN, and Gd₃InN, are also listed at E_hull = 0.0 eV/atom (SS-1 to SS-5; TS-1 to TS-5). CS evidence is partly contradictory because some chemically similar reported materials have high listed E_hull values, such as Ca₃PbN and Nd₃AlN, so chemical-similarity thermodynamics alone is not fully reliable (CS-1; CS-4). The remaining uncertainty is whether DFT ground-state stability translates to a wide experimental stability window under N₂/Tl vapor conditions [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2 — Route 1 condition severity

Route 1 is moderately severe because chemically similar nitrides were synthesized near 950 °C to 1070 K under N₂, which is accessible for solid-state synthesis (CS-2; CS-5). The main deductions arise from possible need for >1000 °C adaptation for Sc kinetics, Tl volatility, and toxic/air-sensitive handling [Reasonable Inference]. There is no evidence for high pressure or exotic reactive gases from the closest nitride analogues, which supports feasibility at ambient pressure (CS-2; CS-5). The principal uncertainty is whether ambient N₂ provides sufficient nitrogen activity for Sc₃TlN rather than forming ScN or Sc–Tl binaries [Reasonable Inference].

#### F3 — Route 1 kinetics

The direct nitridation route benefits from analogues where melt beads or elemental mixtures convert to anti-perovskite nitrides under N₂ (CS-2; CS-5). However, dense Sc-containing intermetallics and Sc’s strong nitride formation tendency may slow uniform nitrogen insertion [Reasonable Inference]. Structural reconstruction is moderate rather than severe because the final Pm-3m framework is directly represented in Ca₃AuN/Sr₃BiN analogues and in the target structure (target; CS-2; CS-5). Remaining uncertainty is whether N diffusion into a Sc–Tl bead is fast enough at 900–1050 °C without overforming binary ScN [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2 — Route 2 condition severity

Route 2 uses the well-established RN + R₂In strategy for R₃InN analogues, which requires sealed ampoules, Ar handling, and week-long anneals at 1220 K (SS-1; TS-1). It avoids sustained temperatures above 1000 °C but adds multi-step complexity through Sc₂Tl alloy preparation and sealed ampoule processing [Reasonable Inference]. The sealed environment is justified by the analogue procedures and by Tl containment concerns (SS-1; TS-1; [Reasonable Inference]). The main uncertainty is whether a stable Sc₂Tl precursor can be made cleanly and reacts with ScN at a practical rate [Reasonable Inference].

#### F3 — Route 2 kinetics

The RN + R₂In route has strong precedent among R₃InN analogues, many of which achieved single-phase products after prolonged annealing (SS-1; TS-1). ScN is likely refractory and less reactive than rare-earth nitrides, lowering the precursor-activity score [Reasonable Inference]. Regrinding and repeated annealing may be required because related R₃InN systems sometimes needed multiple anneals and showed nitrogen-content variation (SS-2; SS-3; TS-2; TS-3). The uncertainty is the mismatch between Sc/Tl chemistry and rare-earth/In chemistry, despite the shared structure [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2 — Route 3 condition severity

Route 3 is supported by the exact cation analogue Sc₃TlC, prepared by arc-melting and long annealing around 900 °C in Ar/sealed tubes (SS-4; TS-4). It is not high-pressure and need not exceed 1000 °C during annealing, but it requires specialized arc-melting/sealed-tube processing (SS-4; TS-4). Tl volatility/toxicity and multi-step handling impose significant practical penalties [Reasonable Inference]. The remaining uncertainty is whether nitride formation can proceed under the same annealing conditions used for carbide analogues [Reasonable Inference].

#### F3 — Route 3 kinetics

The strongest kinetic advantage of Route 3 is that Sc₃TlC has almost the same Sc–Tl framework and similar bond distances to the target, suggesting minimal cation rearrangement (target; SS-4; TS-4). The drawback is that anion chemistry changes from C to N, and ScN/Sc₃N₂ may not dissolve or redistribute rapidly in the metallic matrix [Reasonable Inference]. The analogue literature explicitly notes difficulty preparing single-phase samples and incomplete equilibrium in related carbides, lowering the kinetic confidence (SS-4; TS-4). Thus Route 3 is viable but likely slower and less reproducible than direct nitridation [Reasonable Inference].

#### F4 — Amorphization tendency

The target has a simple cubic Pm-3m 5-atom cell and an ordered anti-perovskite network, which strongly lowers amorphization risk (target). The structure shows corner-sharing NSc₆ octahedra and face-sharing with TlSc₁₂ cuboctahedra, but the required vertex + edge + face coexistence is not present by the provided description (target). A size mismatch between Sc and Tl is significant and triggers one amorphization-risk criterion [Reasonable Inference]. The uncertainty is mostly practical: rapid quenching from melts may trap disorder, while slow annealing should promote crystallinity [Reasonable Inference].

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

Sc₃TlN is likely a thermodynamic ground-state or near-ground-state phase because its calculated E_hull is exactly 0.0000 eV/atom (target), and multiple structurally/thermodynamically similar R₃InN and Sc₃TlC analogues are also on the hull (SS-1 to SS-5; TS-1 to TS-5). The analogue support is strong structurally, especially from Sc₃TlC, which has the same Sc–Tl framework and very similar bond lengths, but nitride-specific support comes mainly from Ca/Sr/rare-earth analogues rather than the exact Sc–Tl–N composition (CS-2; CS-5; SS-4; TS-4). The most important enabling factor is the robust cubic Pm-3m anti-perovskite topology observed across chemically and structurally related nitrides/carbides (CS-2; CS-5; SS-1; SS-4; TS-1; TS-4). The most important limiting factor is controlling Tl volatility/toxicity and preventing competing ScN or Sc–Tl binary phases during nitrogen incorporation [Reasonable Inference]. Overall, the material is plausibly synthesizable, but it should be treated as a controlled-atmosphere, high-purity solid-state target rather than a routine ceramic synthesis [Reasonable Inference].
