### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

#### F1 — Thermodynamic stability

The target BeIr is reported at E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom, placing it in the highest thermodynamic-stability scoring range. The closest analogue IrAl is also Pm-3m Tetraauricupride and E_hull = 0.0 across CS/SS/TS, and other Ir intermetallic analogues in the reference set are also hull-stable (CS/SS/TS). This supports BeIr as a likely ground-state phase, though calculated hull stability does not resolve Be volatility, toxicity, or kinetic ordering barriers [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2 — Route 1 severity

Arc melting is a high-temperature method; HfCo was made by arc melting and Ir intermetallics were prepared by arc melting in Ar (CS/SS/TS). The route avoids high pressure and avoids reactive H₂/vacuum atmospheres, but it uses toxic Be and likely requires temperatures above 1400 °C locally in the arc [Reasonable Inference]. CS/SS/TS provide useful support for arc melting of Ir compounds, but insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS exists for Be loss behavior during arc melting, so that part is handled by expert inference.

#### F3 — Route 1 kinetics

The kinetics benefit from melting or near-melting homogenization, as arc-melted Ir analogues are reported in CS/SS/TS. However, elemental Ir is refractory and dense, so precursor activity is low [Reasonable Inference]. Structural ordering is favorable because the target is the same B2/Tetraauricupride structure as IrAl and HfCo analogues (CS/SS/TS).

#### F2 — Route 2 severity

The sealed Ta/Nb route is supported by MgIr₂ in sealed Ta tubes and Mg₂Pt in sealed Nb ampoules (CS), plus sealed Ta/quartz ampoule syntheses of Ir compounds (SS/TS). It operates at high but not necessarily >1400 °C temperatures, reducing thermal severity relative to arc melting [Reasonable Inference]. The penalties arise from Be toxicity, sealed-system handling, and multi-step ampoule preparation [Reasonable Inference].

#### F3 — Route 2 kinetics

Solid-state diffusion between Be and Ir is expected to be slower than arc melting because Ir remains refractory and Be/Ir interdiffusion must proceed through solid or partially molten contact regions [Reasonable Inference]. MgIr₂ required multiple heating/cooling steps and initially contained impurities, indicating phase-purity challenges for related Ir intermetallics (CS). The B2 target structure is simple and supported by IrAl/HfCo analogues, which helps the reconstruction score (CS/SS/TS).

#### F2 — Route 3 severity

The H₂ reduction route is directly supported by the IrAl Pm-3m analogue, which required 1200–1750 °C, purified H₂, long reaction times, and specialized furnace/crucible setups (CS/SS/TS). This is a severe condition set because it uses reactive H₂ and very high temperature. The BeO/Be toxicity and likely need for rigorous gas purification add additional practical severity [Reasonable Inference].

#### F3 — Route 3 kinetics

The same B2 structure was produced for IrAl by the oxide-reduction route (CS/SS/TS), so there is structural precedent. However, BeO is expected to be harder to reduce than many base-metal oxides and Ir is refractory, making precursor activity and diffusion poor [Reasonable Inference]. CS/SS/TS also report long high-temperature holds and phase-control difficulties in oxide-reduction intermetallic synthesis, lowering confidence.

#### F4 — Amorphization tendency

The target has a simple Pm-3m two-atom B2 cell and a single Be–Ir body-centered-cubic coordination environment, which reduces topological frustration [Reasonable Inference from target structure]. CS/SS/TS do not provide direct amorphization evidence for BeIr; insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS, so each source is neutral for this specific factor. One risk trigger is assigned for Be/Ir size mismatch [Reasonable Inference], giving a low–medium amorphization risk.

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

BeIr is likely a ground-state or near-ground-state intermetallic because its reported E_hull is 0.0000 eV/atom and several close Ir-containing analogues are also hull-stable (CS/SS/TS). The strongest support comes from IrAl, which has the same cubic Pm-3m Tetraauricupride/B2 structure and E_hull = 0.0 (CS/SS/TS), and from HfCo, another B2 intermetallic made by arc melting (CS). The most critical enabling factor is the simple B2 structure, which should be accessible by direct alloying and annealing [Reasonable Inference]. The most critical limiting factor is practical handling of Be: toxicity, possible loss during high-temperature processing, and stoichiometry control are not directly resolved by the analogues [Reasonable Inference]. Overall, synthesis appears feasible but should be attempted only in facilities qualified for Be metallurgy.
