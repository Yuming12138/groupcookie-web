### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

#### F1 — Thermodynamic stability

The target has E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom, which directly supports a high thermodynamic-stability score (target data). The TS list contains several stable Ce/Th transition-metal nitrides, including Ce₂CrN₃, Ce₂MnN₃, Th₂MnN₃, Th₂CrN₃, and La₃Ta₂N₆ with E_hull = 0.0 eV/atom, which supports the broader stability of related nitride chemistries (TS). CS also contains chemically similar nitrides with E_hull = 0.0 eV/atom, but CS provides insufficient direct evidence for Ce–Re–N specifically, so the main uncertainty is whether DFT stability survives finite-temperature and competing binary Re-nitride effects (CS, [Reasonable Inference]).

#### F2 — Route 1, direct solid-state nitridation

Route 1 uses conditions analogous to Ce₂MnN₃ at 900 °C under N₂ and actinide X₂YN₃ phases at 1200–1600 °C under 1 atm N₂ (SS, TS, CS). It is penalized for T > 1000 °C in the recommended Re-containing adaptation, multi-step grinding/pressing, and air-sensitive CeN handling (SS, TS, [Reasonable Inference]). CS/SS/TS give insufficient evidence for direct CeReN₂ formation, so the severity score remains moderate rather than easy.

#### F3 — Route 1, direct solid-state nitridation

The kinetics are poor because Re metal is refractory and will likely have slow nitridation/diffusion compared with Mn or Cr [Reasonable Inference]. Ce₂MnN₃ required multiple grinding/pressing cycles for full conversion, indicating that even more reactive Mn analogues can be kinetically demanding (SS, TS). CS/SS/TS provide insufficient direct Re kinetic evidence, so the route is scored conservatively.

#### F2 — Route 2, Li₃N flux

Route 2 benefits from lower temperatures than high-temperature sintering, because Ce₂CrN₃ crystallized at 900 °C in Li₃N flux and La₃Ta₂N₆ was made at 1030 °C by Li₃N flux (SS, TS, CS). It is penalized for sealed-tube handling, multi-step flux processing, and air-sensitive CeN/Li₃N precursors (SS, TS, CS). The atmosphere is controlled Ar rather than H₂/NH₃/vacuum, so the specified reactive-atmosphere deduction is not applied (SS, TS).

#### F3 — Route 2, Li₃N flux

Li₃N flux improves precursor activity and nitrogen chemical potential, as shown by Ce₂CrN₃ and La₃Ta₂N₆ flux syntheses (SS, TS, CS). Re remains a kinetic risk because no Re-containing flux analogue is supplied, and Re may dissolve/nitride slowly [Reasonable Inference]. Structural reconstruction is more favorable than dry solid-state synthesis because crystallization from flux can form complex nitride frameworks, but impurity contamination in related flux syntheses lowers the score (SS, TS).

#### F2 — Route 3, high-temperature Ga or Li₃N flux

Route 3 is harsher because the Ga-flux La₃Ta₂N₆ precedent reaches 1600 °C and requires RF furnace-style high-temperature flux processing (CS, TS). It is also penalized for sealed ampoules, multi-step handling, and air-sensitive rare-earth/Li₃N components (CS, TS). The route is plausible but operationally more demanding than Route 2.

#### F3 — Route 3, high-temperature Ga or Li₃N flux

The La₃Ta₂N₆ analogue has a tetragonal rare-earth/refractory-metal nitride framework with NLa₄Ta₂ octahedra, giving relatively favorable structural-transfer evidence for CeReN₂ (CS, TS, target data). Flux transport partially mitigates Re diffusion limitations, but Re remains more refractory and chemically distinct from Ta [Reasonable Inference]. CS/TS also report nonstoichiometry and phase-yield issues for La₃Ta₂N₆-type flux syntheses, so kinetics and phase control are not scored as easy (CS, TS).

#### F4 — Amorphization tendency

The target has mixed face-, edge-, and corner-sharing NCe₄Re₂ octahedra, a connectivity pattern explicitly present in the target structure and also common in Ce₂CrN₃/Ce₂MnN₃ analogues (target data, SS, TS). The large Ce/Re size and bonding mismatch likely increases local strain and disorder tendency [Reasonable Inference], while the small P4/mmm formula unit argues against amorphization from unit-cell complexity [Reasonable Inference]. CS/SS/TS provide insufficient direct amorphization evidence, so F4 is based primarily on structural heuristics.

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

CeReN₂ is predicted to be a ground-state phase because its reported energy above hull is 0.0000 eV/atom (target data). The strongest synthesis support comes from Ce₂CrN₃ and Ce₂MnN₃, which show that Ce–transition-metal nitrides with square-planar transition-metal–N coordination and mixed NCe₄M₂ octahedral networks can be synthesized (SS, TS). The most critical enabling factor is the availability of nitride flux chemistry, especially Li₃N, which provides high nitrogen activity and enhanced transport at 900–1030 °C in close analogues (SS, TS, CS). The most critical limiting factor is Re incorporation, because no CS/SS/TS analogue contains Re and Re metal is expected to be refractory and slow to nitride [Reasonable Inference]. Overall, the target is moderately synthesizable, with the best chance coming from Li₃N flux screening rather than direct dry sintering.
