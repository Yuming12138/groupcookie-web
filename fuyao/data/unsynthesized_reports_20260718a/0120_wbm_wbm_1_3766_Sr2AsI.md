### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

#### F1 — Thermodynamic stability

The target is reported with E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom, which places it in the highest thermodynamic stability bin. The closest analogue Ca₂AsI, structurally similar Sr₂NI, Eu₂PI, and Ba₃GeI₂/Ba₃SiI₂ also have E_hull = 0.0 in the CS/SS/TS sets, reinforcing that this family contains ground-state phases (CS1, SS1, SS5, TS1, TS5, TS2, TS3, TS4). Remaining uncertainty is computational: E_hull = 0 does not guarantee kinetic accessibility or absence of finite-temperature competing phases [Reasonable Inference]. Insufficient contradictory thermodynamic evidence from CS/SS/TS; neutral source score would be 50 where no additional signal is present, but the dominant TS evidence is strongly positive.

#### F2 — Route 1

Route 1 avoids >1000 °C temperatures because the Ca₂AsI analogue is reported around 720 °C and Sr₂NI around 1023–1123 K, so the thermal severity is moderate (CS1, SS1, SS5, TS1, TS5). Deductions come from the need for sealed ampoules, multi-step grinding/sealing/annealing, and toxic/air-sensitive Sr arsenide/Sr iodide handling [Reasonable Inference supported by hydrolysis sensitivity and glovebox/ampoule use in CS1, SS5, TS5]. Insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS for high-pressure requirements; this is scored neutral/no penalty.

#### F3 — Route 1

Kinetically, Route 1 is favored by direct structural analogy: Ca₂AsI has the same Caswellsilverite R-3m motif, and Sr₂NI confirms Sr₂XI iodide formation in this topology (CS1, SS1, SS5, TS1, TS5). Binary arsenide/iodide precursors are moderately active, not refractory oxides, but long holds of 2–5 days in analogues indicate that diffusion/order development still require time (CS1, SS5, TS5). The main uncertainty is whether Sr₃As₂ + SrI₂ cleanly selects Sr₂AsI rather than SrI₂/Sr₃As₂/Sr-rich impurity phases [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2 — Route 2

The flux route is more severe operationally because it likely requires sealed vacuum or rigorously sealed metal ampoules, slow cooling, and post-growth flux separation [Reasonable Inference]. Ba₃GeI₂/Ba₃SiI₂ analogues use sealed Nb ampoules at 1170 K with controlled cooling, and telluride analogues use sealed flux conditions, supporting the need for specialized handling (CS3, CS5, SS2, SS3, TS2, TS3). Insufficient direct CS/SS/TS evidence for SrI₂ flux growth of Sr₂AsI specifically, so transferability is partly extrapolated and scored with caution.

#### F3 — Route 2

Flux/liquid transport should improve reaction kinetics and crystal growth relative to dry solid-state diffusion [Reasonable Inference], and this is consistent with flux-grown chemically similar materials and Ba iodide analogues (CS2, CS3, CS5, SS2, SS3, TS2, TS3). The structural reconstruction score is high because the target framework is close to known Caswellsilverite/Caswellsilverite-like iodides (CS1, SS1, SS2, SS3, TS1, TS2, TS3). The limiting uncertainty is phase selectivity, since some flux syntheses produce by-products or impurity phases rather than the intended phase (CS2, CS3).

#### F2 — Route 3

High-temperature post-annealing is more severe than Route 1 because crystal-growth analogues may require temperatures near or above 1000 °C and long durations, as seen for Eu₂PI-related single-crystal annealing and Ca₂AsI microcrystalline annealing (CS1, SS1, SS4, TS1, TS4). The method still avoids high pressure and exotic apparatus, but sealed ampoules, air-sensitive materials, and toxic pnictide/iodide handling remain significant [Reasonable Inference supported by CS1, SS4, TS4]. Insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS for needing >1400 °C for Sr₂AsI; no additional high-temperature penalty is applied.

#### F3 — Route 3

Starting from pre-reacted Sr₂AsI minimizes compositional reconstruction, which is favorable [Reasonable Inference]. However, the analogous reports emphasize long anneals and specialized ampoules for single crystals, indicating slow ordering/crystal-growth kinetics (CS1, SS1, SS4, TS4). This route is therefore useful for crystal quality but less efficient for initial phase discovery than Route 1.

#### F4 — Amorphization tendency

The target has a simple single-cation A₂XI composition and the closest analogues are ordered Caswellsilverite phases, so no strong amorphization signal appears (CS1, SS1, SS5, TS1, TS5). The connectivity includes corner- and edge-sharing octahedra but not the full vertex/edge/face coexistence criterion that would trigger high amorphization risk (CS1, SS1). Sr₂AsI is likely a ground-state or near-ground-state phase because its reported E_hull is 0.0000 eV/atom and several close analogues in CS/SS/TS are also on the hull (CS1, SS1, SS5, TS1, TS5).
