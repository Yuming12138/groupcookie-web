### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

#### F1 — Thermodynamic stability

The target has E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom, giving a high thermodynamic score by the requested rubric. CS analogues RbHoSe₂, RbTbSe₂, RbErSe₂, RbLuSe₂, and RbGdSe₂ are also reported with E_hull = 0.0, showing stable Caswellsilverite chalcogenide analogues (CS). SS/TS analogues Ba₃GeI₂, Ba₃I₂Si, Ca₂AsI, Ba₂PCl, and Eu₂PI also have E_hull = 0.0, providing stronger structural/thermodynamic support for stable pnictide/iodide or pnictide/halide frameworks (SS; TS). Remaining uncertainty is that calculated hull stability does not guarantee kinetic accessibility or phase purity.

#### F2 — Route 1 condition severity

Route 1 uses moderate-to-high temperatures but avoids >1000 °C if run near 850–950 °C, consistent with Eu₂PI at 900 °C and Ba₂PCl at 500–1000 °C (SS/TS: Eu₂PI; SS/TS: Ba₂PCl). The main deductions arise from sealed handling of volatile P/iodide components, multi-step grinding/annealing, and air/moisture-sensitive precursors, all reflected in the Ba₂PCl and Eu₂PI reports (SS/TS). CS provides insufficient evidence for this exact elemental Sr–P–I solid-state route and is treated as neutral 50 for this route-specific condition assessment. Uncertainty is ampoule compatibility with molten/volatile iodide species.

#### F3 — Route 1 kinetics

Direct element/halide reaction is chemically plausible but slower than binary-precursor or flux routes because Sr metal, red P, and SrI₂ must interdiffuse and reconstruct into the ordered A₂PX phase [Reasonable Inference]. The score is supported by the fact that Eu₂PI and Ba₂PCl can be made by solid-state routes, but Ba₂PCl requires repeated grinding/tempering and Eu₂PI uses sealed ampoule heating (SS/TS: Eu₂PI; SS/TS: Ba₂PCl). CS provides insufficient evidence for Sr–P–I solid-state kinetics and is neutral 50 for this route. The main uncertainty is competing formation of Sr₃P₂, SrI₂-rich phases, or off-stoichiometric phosphide iodides.

#### F2 — Route 2 condition severity

Route 2 inherits moderate temperatures from Eu₂PI binary Eu₃P₂ + EuX₂ synthesis at 900 °C and Ca₂AsI/Ca₂PI-type ampoule annealing near 720 °C and above (SS/TS: Eu₂PI; SS/TS: Ca₂AsI). The route still requires sealed dry Ar handling because Sr₃P₂ and SrI₂ are moisture sensitive and iodide/phosphide phases are hydrolysis sensitive by analogy to Ba₂PCl/Eu₂PI (SS/TS: Ba₂PCl; SS/TS: Eu₂PI). CS provides insufficient evidence for this binary phosphide/iodide precursor strategy and is scored neutral 50 for Route 2 condition severity. The condition burden is moderate rather than severe because no high pressure, no H₂/NH₃ reactive gas, and no >1000 °C powder synthesis is required.

#### F3 — Route 2 kinetics

Sr₃P₂ + SrI₂ is kinetically favorable because it uses preformed Sr–P and Sr–I binaries and exactly matches the target stoichiometry, directly mirroring Eu₃P₂ + EuX₂ → Eu₂PX chemistry (SS/TS: Eu₂PI) [Reasonable Inference]. The one-cation Sr framework reduces cation-ordering complexity, and A₂PX analogues exist in the same or very similar Caswellsilverite structure types (SS/TS: Ca₂AsI; SS/TS: Eu₂PI; SS/TS: Ba₂PCl). CS again provides insufficient direct kinetic signal for Sr₂PI and is neutral 50 for this route. The remaining uncertainty is whether Sr₂PI has a narrow stability field requiring iodine chemical-potential control.

#### F2 — Route 3 condition severity

Flux growth has favorable temperatures near 900 °C, consistent with RbLnSe₂ flux synthesis at 1173 K and Ba₃I₂Si/Ba₃GeI₂ at 1170 K (CS; SS/TS: Ba₃I₂Si/Ba₃GeI₂). However, it receives stronger severity deductions because it commonly requires evacuated or sealed ampoules, slow cooling, flux separation, and handling of air-sensitive/volatile halide-rich mixtures (CS; SS/TS). SS/TS supports halide-mediated Caswellsilverite-like synthesis, but not direct Sr₂PI flux growth, so transferability is plausible rather than proven. The key uncertainty is whether a flux can be chosen that does not dissolve, decompose, or contaminate Sr₂PI.

#### F3 — Route 3 kinetics

Flux growth is kinetically attractive because molten iodide flux should enhance mass transport and crystallization, as seen for RbLnSe₂ reactive flux and Ba iodide Caswellsilverite-like analogues (CS; SS/TS: Ba₃I₂Si/Ba₃GeI₂). Structural reconstruction is likely easier in flux than in dry powders because the target network can nucleate from mobile Sr–P–I species [Reasonable Inference]. SS/TS Eu₂PI/Ba₂PCl provide phase-family support but not flux-specific Sr₂PI evidence, so uncertainty remains. The kinetic score is high, but the route is not selected as best because condition severity and phase-control risks are higher.

#### F4 — Amorphization tendency

The target has only one cation species, Sr, so cation-size disorder is not expected to drive amorphization [Reasonable Inference; SS/TS: single-cation Ca₂AsI and Eu₂PI analogues]. Caswellsilverite A₂PX analogues are simple ordered structures rather than large-cell complex phases, reducing amorphization risk (SS/TS: Ca₂AsI; SS/TS: Eu₂PI; SS/TS: Ba₂PCl). The mixed corner/edge/face-sharing connectivity in the target raises the risk to low–medium, because face-sharing polyhedra can impose local strain [Reasonable Inference; target structure; SS/TS analogues show edge/corner-sharing networks]. Remaining uncertainty is whether the predicted R3m distortion with two inequivalent Sr sites is kinetically accessible without forming a simpler R-3m polymorph.

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

Sr₂PI is likely a thermodynamic ground-state phase rather than merely metastable because its reported energy above hull is 0.0000 eV/atom, and many CS/SS/TS analogues are also on the hull (CS; SS; TS). The strongest support comes from SS/TS analogues, especially Eu₂PI, Ba₂PCl, and Ca₂AsI/Ca₂PI-type chemistry, which demonstrate that A₂PX Caswellsilverite pnictide-halide frameworks can be synthesized by sealed-ampoule methods (SS/TS). CS supports general Caswellsilverite flux crystallization but is less chemically direct because RbLnSe₂ is a chalcogenide rather than a Sr phosphide iodide, so CS is mainly secondary support (CS). The most critical enabling factor is the exact binary-precursor stoichiometry Sr₃P₂ + SrI₂ → 2 Sr₂PI, inferred from the demonstrated Eu₃P₂ + EuX₂ route (SS/TS: Eu₂PI) [Reasonable Inference]. The most critical limiting factor is moisture/oxygen sensitivity and volatile iodide/phosphorus control in sealed ampoules, as hydrolysis sensitivity and sealed inert processing recur across the SS/TS analogues (SS/TS: Ba₂PCl; SS/TS: Eu₂PI; SS/TS: Ca₂AsI).
