The uncertainty is that no CS/SS/TS example contains fluorine, so oxyfluoride decomposition into Re oxides plus Re fluorides is not directly assessed; insufficient direct F evidence from CS/SS/TS, neutral = 50 for that sub-signal.

**F2/F3 — Route 1: sealed-tube solid-state oxyfluorination.**  
The severity score is reduced because evacuated sealed tubes are needed, and likely volatile/toxic fluoride precursors must be used [Reasonable Inference]; similar sealed-tube Re oxide syntheses use evacuated silica/quartz ampoules at 500–800 °C (CS1; CS2; CS5). Kinetically, this route benefits from ReO₃-derived chemistry and moderate temperatures shown in PbRe₂O₆ and SbRe₂O₆ analogues (CS1; CS5), but Re cation diffusion and F stoichiometry are likely limiting [Reasonable Inference]. The largest uncertainty is whether direct solid-state mixing can incorporate F uniformly without forming volatile Re fluorides; CS/SS/TS provide insufficient direct evidence for that, neutral = 50.

**F2/F3 — Route 2: chemical vapor transport.**  
The severity score is moderate-low because CVT requires evacuated sealed tubes, halogen/fluorine chemistry, gradients, and often multi-day runs, as seen for RePO₅ and BiRe₂O₆ (SS1; CS4; TS1; TS3). The uncertainty is high F incorporation: iodine/NH₄Br transport is demonstrated for oxides (SS1; CS4), but fluorinated transport chemistry is [Reasonable Inference] and not directly supported by CS/SS/TS.
