### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

#### F1 — Thermodynamic stability

The target has E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom, which falls in the highest stability bin. Similar Pd–B and A–Pd–B analogues are also computed on hull: Li₂Pd₃B is E_hull = 0.0 (CS-1; SS-2; TS-2), SrPd₄B is E_hull = 0.0 (SS-1; TS-1), and Pd₃B/Pd₂B are E_hull = 0.0 (SS-3, SS-5; TS-3, TS-4). The remaining uncertainty is whether the P6/mmm NaBPd₃ polymorph is kinetically reachable rather than another Na–Pd–B/Pd–B phase being favored experimentally [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2 — Route 1 condition severity

Route 1 is moderately demanding because the closest analogue Li₂Pd₃B was synthesized at 900 °C for 48 h in sealed Ta tubes under Ar, which is standard but requires inert/sealed handling (CS-1; SS-2; TS-2). The main deductions come from Na volatility and air sensitivity, which require sealed metal containment and glovebox operations [Reasonable Inference]. There is insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS that pressures above ambient or temperatures above 1000 °C are required, so those factors are not penalized.

#### F3 — Route 1 reaction kinetics

The precursor activity is limited by elemental Pd and B, which are not as reactive as salt/solution precursors [Reasonable Inference], but Li₂Pd₃B forms directly from elements at 900 °C (CS-1; SS-2; TS-2). Pd–B bond lengths in Li₂Pd₃B, ~2.14 Å, match the target Pd–B distance of 2.15 Å, supporting structural transferability (CS-1; SS-2; TS-2). The uncertainty is whether Na diffusion and ordering into two inequivalent Na sites in P6/mmm will occur cleanly before competing Pd–B binaries crystallize [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2 — Route 2 condition severity

Route 2 inherits the proven SrPd₄B protocol of arc melting under purified Ar, sealing in Ta/quartz, annealing at 950 °C for 5 days, and quenching (SS-1; TS-1). It is more severe than Route 1 because it involves multiple operations: arc melting, repeated remelting, sealed annealing, and quenching (SS-1; TS-1). Direct use of Na during arc melting is not supported by CS/SS/TS and is risky because Na is volatile [Reasonable Inference], so Na should be added only during sealed anneal.

#### F3 — Route 2 reaction kinetics

Arc melting improves Pd–B homogenization and is experimentally successful for Pd₃B, Pd₂B, and SrPd₄B (SS-1, SS-3, SS-5; TS-1, TS-3, TS-4). However, the target requires Na incorporation into a ternary ordered phase after forming or partially forming Pd–B networks, which may require significant reconstruction [Reasonable Inference]. The long 5-day anneal in SrPd₄B indicates ordering and phase equilibration are not instantaneous (SS-1; TS-1).

#### F2 — Route 3 condition severity

The iodine-assisted route has lower temperature, supported by BePd₃ synthesis at 500–550 °C for 48 h in evacuated quartz ampoules with I₂ (TS-5). However, it incurs penalties for vacuum/sealed ampoule operation, I₂ toxicity/corrosivity, Na air sensitivity, and likely Na volatility [Reasonable Inference]. There is insufficient CS and SS evidence for iodine-assisted Na–Pd–B synthesis, so those sources are neutral rather than supportive.

#### F3 — Route 3 reaction kinetics

TS-5 supports vapor/transport-assisted formation of a Pd-rich intermetallic, but it does not demonstrate B incorporation or alkali-metal incorporation. I₂ could enhance transport but could also trap Na as NaI, narrowing composition control [Reasonable Inference]. Therefore this route is kinetically uncertain and is best considered exploratory.

#### F4 — Amorphization tendency

The target has strong size/coordination contrast between large Na sites and the Pd–B framework, triggering one amorphization risk factor [Reasonable Inference]. The target structure description reports edge-sharing BPd₆ units but does not report mixed vertex/edge/face sharing, so the mixed-connectivity trigger is not applied. The small formula and high symmetry suggest the cell is unlikely to exceed 40 atoms [Reasonable Inference], but the exact CIF atom count is not provided in CS/SS/TS, so this remains a mild uncertainty.

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

NaBPd₃ is likely a thermodynamic ground-state or near-ground-state phase because the supplied target E_hull is 0.0000 eV/atom and many close Pd–B/A–Pd–B analogues are also on hull (CS-1; SS-1–3; TS-1–4). The strongest synthesis support comes from Li₂Pd₃B, which is chemically and structurally close and was made directly from elements in sealed Ta tubes at 900 °C (CS-1; SS-2; TS-2). The most critical enabling factor is the robust Pd–B framework chemistry, with multiple stable Pd–B and alkaline/alkali-earth Pd borides synthesized under Ar/sealed high-temperature conditions (SS-1, SS-3, SS-5; TS-1, TS-3, TS-4). The main limiting factor is Na retention and stoichiometry control, because Na is volatile and air-sensitive at the required temperatures [Reasonable Inference]. Overall, synthesis appears feasible, with the best first attempt being a sealed Ta-tube elemental reaction near 900 °C.
