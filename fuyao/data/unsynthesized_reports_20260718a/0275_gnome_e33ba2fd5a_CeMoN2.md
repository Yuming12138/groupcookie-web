### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

#### F1 — Thermodynamic stability

CeMoN₂ has E_hull = 0.0 eV/atom, so it is predicted as a ground-state phase by the supplied target thermodynamic data. The closest thermodynamic analogues Ce₂CrN₃ and Ce₂MnN₃ also have E_hull = 0.0 eV/atom, strengthening confidence that Ce–transition-metal nitrides can be stable compounds (TS1, TS2). The remaining uncertainty is that hull stability does not guarantee accessible kinetics or absence of competing binary nitrides during synthesis [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2/F3 — Route 1: Li₃N flux

The condition severity is moderate because the best analogue Ce₂CrN₃ forms at 900 °C in Li₃N flux under Ar in a sealed silica tube, without high pressure or >1000 °C operation (CS1/SS1/TS1). The kinetic score is moderate because Li₃N flux improves mass transport and crystallization, but Mo is refractory and not directly tested in the supplied Ce-flux analogues [Reasonable Inference]. Phase-purity uncertainty remains because Ce₂CrN₃ analogues mention contamination/removal issues and flux growth can stabilize competing nitrides (CS1/SS1/TS1).

#### F2/F3 — Route 2: Direct solid-state nitridation

The solid-state route is supported by Ce₂MnN₃ synthesis from CeN + Mn + N₂ at 900 °C and by actinide X₂YN₃ syntheses from mononitrides plus transition metals under N₂ (CS3/SS2/TS2, SS3/SS4/TS3/TS4). Its condition score is lowered by repeated grinding/pressing and possible need for >1000 °C to react Mo [Reasonable Inference]. Its kinetic score is the lowest because Mo metal is refractory and bulk cation diffusion is likely slow, while even Mn analogues required multiple cycles and showed condition sensitivity (CS3/SS2/TS2).

#### F2/F3 — Route 3: Ammonolysis precursor route

The ammonolysis route has lower temperature severity because CrWN₂ and MnWN₂ were obtained at 750–800 °C under NH₃ (CS2, CS4). Its severity is penalized by NH₃, multi-step precursor chemistry, and air/moisture-sensitive or corrosive reagents (CS2, CS4). Kinetically it benefits from molecular-scale cation mixing, but structural transferability is uncertain because no Ce–Mo ammonolysis analogue appears in CS/SS/TS and MnWN₂ had persistent impurity phases (CS4).

#### F2/F3 — Route 4: High-temperature reactive flux

The high-temperature flux route is supported by La₃Nb₂N₆ synthesis using Ga flux at 1600 °C or Li₃N flux at 1030 °C in refractory ampoules (CS5). Its condition score is low because it uses >1000 °C, sometimes >1400 °C, sealed ampoules, and air-sensitive flux/metal handling (CS5). Its kinetic score is moderate because flux helps transport and the tetragonal rare-earth/early-transition-metal nitride topology is relevant, but high temperature and possible nonstoichiometry create phase-control risk (CS5).

#### F4 — Amorphization/ordering tendency

The target has mixed face-, edge-, and corner-sharing NCe₄Mo₂ octahedra, which triggers the mixed-connectivity criterion and matches complex connectivity observed in close analogues (target structure; CS1/SS1/TS1). The Ce–N and Mo–N bond lengths differ strongly, implying a large cation size/coordination mismatch and triggering the radius-difference criterion [Reasonable Inference from target bond lengths]. The unit cell is not large by the supplied P4/mmm description, so the atom-count trigger is not applied; the uncertainty is that ordering defects or Ce/Mo site disorder could still occur [Reasonable Inference].

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

CeMoN₂ is likely a thermodynamic ground-state candidate because its supplied E_hull is 0.0 eV/atom, and several close Ce/actinide transition-metal nitride analogues are also on the hull (TS1–TS4). The synthesis support is strongest from Ce₂CrN₃ and Ce₂MnN₃, which share Ce–N/transition-metal–N nitride frameworks and were experimentally synthesized by flux or solid-state nitridation routes (CS1, CS3; SS1, SS2). The most critical enabling factor is the availability of Li₃N flux chemistry, which both supplies nitrogen activity and improves transport/crystallization at ~900 °C (CS1/SS1/TS1). The most critical limiting factor is Mo incorporation, because Mo is more refractory than Cr/Mn and no direct Ce–Mo nitride analogue is provided in CS/SS/TS [Reasonable Inference]. Overall, the target is reasonably synthesizable, with best prospects via Li₃N flux and careful control of oxygen exclusion, cation stoichiometry, and competing nitride phases.
