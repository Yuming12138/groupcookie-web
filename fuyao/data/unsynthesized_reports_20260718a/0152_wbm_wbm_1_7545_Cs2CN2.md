### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

#### F1 — Thermodynamic stability

The target has **E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom**, which directly places it in the highest thermodynamic stability bin. The closest structural and thermodynamic analogue **K₂CN₂** is also hull-stable and isostructural in C2/m (SS1, TS1), while **Li₂CN₂** is also hull-stable, supporting stability of alkali cyanamide/carbodiimide phases (SS4, TS2). CS gives weaker and mixed evidence because chemically similar Cs acetylides include metastable hull energies around 0.30 eV/atom, although Cs₃BS₃ is stable; therefore CS is not decisive for F1 and is treated as partially neutral (CS1–CS5). Remaining uncertainty is mainly whether the computed Cs₂CN₂ ground state is experimentally accessible before competing Cs–C–N decomposition or hydration occurs [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2 — Route 1 condition severity

Route 1 inherits demanding but chemically validated conditions from K₂CN₂: **900 °C**, **100 h**, **7 MPa N₂**, sodium flux, autoclave, tungsten crucible, and glovebox handling (SS1, TS1). Under the required scoring table, 7 MPa is not penalized as >5 GPa, but practically it is still a specialized high-pressure gas synthesis [Reasonable Inference from SS1/TS1]. The main deductions are sealed/autoclave alkali handling, multistep operation, and air-sensitive alkali reagents. CS offers insufficient direct evidence for high-pressure Cs₂CN₂ synthesis and is neutral for Route 1 severity except for general Cs/alkali air sensitivity (CS1, CS3).

#### F3 — Route 1 kinetics

The sodium-flux/N₂ route should enhance mass transport and nitrogen incorporation, and it already produced the exact K₂CN₂ structure type (SS1, TS1). Long synthesis time and controlled cooling imply that nucleation/growth and ordering are not instantaneous, so kinetics are good but not trivial (SS1, TS1). The K→Cs substitution should expand the lattice and may lower cation diffusion barriers [Reasonable Inference], but Cs volatility and flux compatibility remain uncertain. CS provides insufficient direct kinetic signal for the high-pressure N₂ route and is neutral except for showing reactive Cs acetylide chemistry at low temperature (CS1, CS3).

#### F2 — Route 2 condition severity

Route 2 avoids high pressure but uses very air-sensitive alkali nitride and acetylide precursors, as indicated by Li₂CN₂ synthesis under Ar and Cs acetylide syntheses under vacuum/glovebox handling (SS4, TS2, CS1, CS3). The expected 400–650 °C temperature range is moderate compared with high-pressure ceramics, but Cs volatility and sealed-container compatibility are important [Reasonable Inference]. The multistep nature arises from precursor preparation, grinding/pelletizing, sealed heating, and inert workup (SS4, TS2, CS1, CS3). TS and SS strongly support the nitride/acetylide route for Li₂CN₂ but do not directly verify Cs₂CN₂, so the severity score remains conservative.

#### F3 — Route 2 kinetics

Li₃N + Li₂C₂ forms Li₂CN₂ at 600 °C in 10 h, showing that alkali nitride/acetylide precursors are kinetically competent for cyanamide formation (SS4, TS2). Cs₂C₂-containing acetylide mixtures react at only 200 °C under vacuum, suggesting high mobility/reactivity in Cs acetylide chemistry (CS1, CS3). Structural reconstruction is moderate because the target C2/m phase matches K₂CN₂, not Li₂CN₂, so the reaction must both form CN₂ units and adopt the larger-alkali structure (SS1, SS4, TS1, TS2). Remaining uncertainty is the possible formation of Cs metal-rich byproducts or alternative Cs–C/N phases [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2 — Route 3 condition severity

Route 3 is mild in temperature and pressure, because Na(HNCN)-derived material is obtained by vacuum treatment and dicyanamide salts can be formed in water at room temperature to 80 °C (SS2, SS3, SS5). However, vacuum, drying, gas evolution, and atmosphere-sensitive molecular salts still trigger deductions (SS2; [Reasonable Inference]). The route is multistep because it requires precursor preparation, drying, decomposition, and likely annealing for crystallization (SS2, SS3, SS5). CS provides insufficient direct evidence for molecular precursor routes to Cs₂CN₂ and is neutral for this route.

#### F3 — Route 3 kinetics

Molecular cyanamide/dicyanamide precursors already contain C–N bonds, so the kinetic barrier to forming CN₂ units should be lower than starting from graphite and nitride [Reasonable Inference from SS2, SS3, SS5]. SS2 reports single-phase material after vacuum treatment of Na(HNCN), supporting favorable low-temperature decomposition kinetics. The main kinetic uncertainty is crystallization into the specific C2/m Cs₂CN₂ structure rather than forming amorphous or nonstoichiometric cesium cyanamide/hydrogen cyanamide phases [Reasonable Inference]. Because no direct Cs₂CN₂ molecular precursor synthesis is provided, this route scores high for chemical reactivity but lower for structural certainty.

#### F4 — Amorphization tendency

The target has only one cation, Cs, so cation-radius disorder is absent [Reasonable Inference from target formula]. The target structure is simple, containing linear CN₂ units and one Cs site, with no reported mixed vertex/edge/face-sharing polyhedral network; the K₂CN₂ analogue is similarly simple (SS1, TS1). There is insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS that the target has a >40 atom unit cell, so that check is not triggered. Remaining amorphization risk is mainly from low-temperature molecular precursor routes, where incomplete crystallization could occur [Reasonable Inference].

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

Cs₂CN₂ is likely a **ground-state phase**, because its computed energy above hull is exactly **0.0000 eV/atom** and the closest analogue K₂CN₂ is also hull-stable and experimentally synthesized in the same C2/m structure type (SS1, TS1). The strongest support comes from SS/TS rather than CS: K₂CN₂ is essentially a direct structural template, while Li₂CN₂ confirms alkali cyanamide chemistry by a nitride/acetylide route (SS1, SS4, TS1, TS2). The most critical enabling factor is the presence of preformed or readily formed linear CN₂ units, which appear robust across Li, Na, and K alkali cyanamide/dicyanamide systems (SS1–SS5, TS1–TS2). The most critical limiting factor is practical handling of Cs-rich, moisture-sensitive, and potentially volatile precursors/products, especially under sealed or high-temperature conditions (CS1, CS3; [Reasonable Inference]). Overall, synthesis is plausible to likely, with the high-pressure N₂ flux route offering the highest structural confidence and the molecular precursor route offering the mildest but less certain pathway.
