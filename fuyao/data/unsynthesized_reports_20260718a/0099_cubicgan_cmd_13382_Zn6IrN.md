### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

#### F1 — Thermodynamic stability

The target E_hull is exactly 0.0500 eV/atom, placing it at the favorable boundary of the prompt’s 90–100 range. Thermodynamic analogues with similar metastability are present: Nd₂Zn₁₅Ge₂ has E_hull = 0.0413 eV/atom (TS-1), Li₂HgGe has 0.0522 eV/atom (TS-4), and EuAg₄In₈ has 0.0526 eV/atom (TS-5). The remaining uncertainty is that these TS analogues are mostly intermetallic or Zintl-like compounds rather than Zn–Ir nitrides, so chemical transferability is incomplete.

#### F2/F3 — Route 1

Sealed-tube solid-state synthesis is well supported for Zn-rich analogues at 400–600 °C or 600 °C in vacuum/Ar (SS-2, TS-1), and longer sintering at 700–900 °C is also reported (SS-3). Severity is penalized because the proposed route needs controlled atmosphere, sealed handling for volatile Zn, multiple grind/anneal steps, and air-sensitive nitride precursor handling [Reasonable Inference]. Kinetics are limited by refractory Ir and structural reconstruction, while Zn-rich sealed environments may improve transport as suggested by Zn-rich analogues forming under moderate temperatures (SS-2, TS-1).

#### F2/F3 — Route 2

Arc melting and post-annealing are strongly supported for chemically similar intermetallics (CS-1, CS-4, CS-5). The route is penalized because arc melting introduces high transient temperatures, Zn volatility, and subsequent nitridation complexity [Reasonable Inference]. Kinetically, alloying may improve Ir–Zn mixing, but nitrogen insertion into a preformed Ir–Zn alloy is not demonstrated by CS, SS, or TS, so the route remains uncertain.

#### F2/F3 — Route 3

Reactive sputtering has no direct CS/SS/TS support, so its favorable F2 score reflects extrapolated low-temperature processing rather than precedent [Reasonable Inference; insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS]. It is attractive because atomic-scale co-deposition can bypass slow bulk Ir diffusion [Reasonable Inference]. The F3 score remains only moderate because Zn volatility, N incorporation, and narrow Zn:Ir:N composition control are expected to be challenging, and narrow phase windows are reported in multiple analogues (SS-1, SS-2, TS-2, TS-3).

#### F2/F3 — Route 4

High-pressure Zn-rich synthesis is explicitly supported by SrZn₁₁ at 7 GPa and 1273 K (SS-5) and by high-pressure CaZn₂/SrZn₂-type materials at 7–10 GPa and 400–1000 °C (TS-3). The F2 score is low because >5 GPa pressure, encapsulation, multi-step processing, and air-sensitive nitride use are severe. Kinetically, pressure may help form dense phases, but HP analogues also show decomposition, unidentified by-products, and narrow stability windows (SS-5, TS-3), so phase selectivity is uncertain.

#### F4 — Amorphization tendency

The target structure is simple in composition and local connectivity: IrZn₁₂ cuboctahedra share corners and faces with NZn₆ octahedra according to the target description. The strict mixed-connectivity trigger requiring vertex + edge + face coexistence is not met because edge-sharing is not reported for the target. The remaining uncertainty is that no CS/SS/TS source directly measures amorphization tendency for Zn₆IrN, so the low amorphization risk is a structural inference [Reasonable Inference].

Zn₆IrN is likely a low-energy metastable or borderline ground-state-accessible phase because its E_hull is 0.0500 eV/atom, which is at the favorable stability threshold in the prompt and close to synthesizable TS analogues (TS-1, TS-4, TS-5). The CS and SS analogues strongly support Zn-rich intermetallic synthesis by arc melting, sealed-tube annealing, and sintering (CS-1, CS-4, CS-5, SS-1, SS-2, SS-3), but they do not directly support Ir–Zn–N nitride formation. The most critical enabling factor is the relatively low thermodynamic penalty plus the availability of Zn-rich sealed or low-temperature synthesis environments (TS-1, SS-2). The most critical limiting factor is controlling Zn and N chemical potentials simultaneously while overcoming slow Ir diffusion [Reasonable Inference]. Thin-film reactive sputtering is therefore the best exploratory route, while sealed-tube solid-state nitridation is the best bulk backup route [Reasonable Inference].
