### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

#### F1 — Thermodynamic stability

The target’s E_hull is 0.0500 eV/atom, which falls exactly at the high-synthesizability threshold specified by the prompt. TS provides strong support that Co-rich compounds with similar metastability, such as Sm₂Co₁₅Ga₂ at 0.053 eV/atom, Nd₂Co₁₅Ga₂ at 0.062 eV/atom, and Ce₂Co₁₅Ga₂ at 0.073 eV/atom, are experimentally accessible by metallurgical routes (TS: Sm₂Co₁₅Ga₂; TS: Nd₂Co₁₅Ga₂; TS: Ce₂Co₁₅Ga₂). CS also shows known nitride/intermetallic analogues with E_hull values comparable to or higher than the target, including Ce₂Fe₁₇N₃ and Sm₂Co₁₇N₃ (CS: Ce₂Fe₁₇N₃; CS: Sm₂Co₁₇N₃). Remaining uncertainty is that TS analogues are mostly Ga/Sn intermetallics rather than Sb nitrides, so TS is strong for metastability tolerance but weaker for nitrogen incorporation.

#### F2/F3 — Route 1

Route 1 benefits from mild temperatures and ambient pressure because InCo₃N and CdCo₃N were made at 600–650 °C and 743 K under NH₃, respectively (SS: InCo₃N; SS: CdCo₃N). Its F2 is reduced by NH₃ handling, Sb volatility/toxicity, and likely repeated grinding/gas-refresh steps [Reasonable Inference]. Kinetically, the structural match is strong because the SS antiperovskites contain ACo₁₂ cuboctahedra and NCo₆ octahedra like the target (SS: InCo₃N; SS: CdCo₃N). The main uncertainty is that CS provides insufficient direct evidence for Sb-containing cobalt nitride ammonolysis, so CS is neutral, score 50, for this specific route.

#### F2/F3 — Route 2

Route 2 is well supported for parent intermetallic preparation because many Co-rich materials are made by arc/induction melting and annealing (CS: Ce₂Co₁₅Ga₂; TS: UCo₄Sn). It is also supported for post-synthetic nitrogen insertion because Sm₂Co₁₇N₃ is prepared by gas-phase nitrogenation at 450–550 °C (CS: Sm₂Co₁₇N₃; SS: Sm₂Co₁₇N₃). The F2 score is low because arc melting, controlled atmospheres, sealed/vacuum annealing, volatile Sb, and multiple steps all apply [Reasonable Inference]. The F3 score is limited because CS/SS explicitly note slow nitrogen diffusion, incomplete nitrogenation, and α-Co precipitation risks in cobalt nitride analogues (CS: Sm₂Co₁₇N₃; SS: Sm₂Co₁₇N₃).

#### F2/F3 — Route 3

High-pressure N₂ nitridation is the most forceful route and is supported by Co₂N synthesis at about 10 GPa and 1800 K in supercritical nitrogen (SS: Co₂N). It receives a poor F2 score because >5 GPa pressure, high temperature, laser/multi-anvil handling, and Sb volatility/toxicity are severe [Reasonable Inference; SS: Co₂N]. Kinetics are not ideal because direct assembly from elements requires complete reconstruction into the F-43m ternary structure [Reasonable Inference]. CS high-pressure Ce₂Fe₁₇N₃ at 15 MPa gives some evidence that high N chemical potential helps nitride formation, but it is not directly analogous in structure or chemistry, so CS support is only moderate (CS: Ce₂Fe₁₇N₃).

#### F2/F3 — Route 4

Route 4 is favored because it combines a local Co–N source with sealed-tube retention of volatile components [Reasonable Inference]. Co₂N has Co–N distances of 1.86–1.88 Å, essentially matching the target Co–N distance of 1.88 Å, supporting local bonding compatibility (SS: Co₂N). Sb-containing Co-rich intermetallics can be made by powder reaction in quartz tubes, and Co–Sn F-43m intermetallics can be prepared by induction melting plus sealed annealing (CS: Co₂₁B₆Sb₂; TS: UCo₄Sn). Remaining uncertainty is that no CS/SS/TS reference directly demonstrates Co₂N + Sb conversion to Co₆SbN, so the route is partly extrapolated [Reasonable Inference].

#### F4 — Amorphization tendency

The target has only one significant amorphization warning: the large size/chemical mismatch between Co and Sb [Reasonable Inference]. Structurally similar antiperovskite-like Co nitrides tolerate large A-site cations in ACo₁₂ cuboctahedra, which lowers concern about amorphization (SS: InCo₃N; SS: CdCo₃N). The formula is small and the described connectivity does not include simultaneous vertex-, edge-, and face-sharing polyhedral networks, so only one trigger is counted [Reasonable Inference]. Remaining uncertainty is that F-43m cation ordering may broaden peaks or create antisite disorder rather than a fully amorphous product [Reasonable Inference].

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

Co₆SbN is best viewed as a borderline thermodynamic phase rather than a deep ground-state compound because its E_hull is 0.0500 eV/atom, exactly at the high-synthesizability cutoff supplied in the prompt. The strongest positive evidence comes from SS: InCo₃N and SS: CdCo₃N, which show that Co–N antiperovskite frameworks with NCo₆ octahedra and ACo₁₂ cuboctahedra can be synthesized at moderate temperature under NH₃. CS and TS support the broader feasibility of Co-rich intermetallic and nitride synthesis, especially through arc/induction melting, sealed annealing, and nitrogenation (CS: Sm₂Co₁₇N₃; TS: UCo₄Sn). The most critical enabling factor is the excellent Co–N bond-length match between the target and known Co nitrides/antiperovskites (SS: Co₂N; SS: InCo₃N; SS: Sm₂Co₁₇N₃). The most critical limiting factor is the lack of direct Co–Sb–N synthesis precedent, combined with Sb volatility/toxicity and possible competition from Co metal, Co nitrides, and Co–Sb intermetallics [Reasonable Inference].
