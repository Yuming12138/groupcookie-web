### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

#### F1 — Thermodynamic stability

The target has **E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom**, so it is predicted to be a thermodynamic ground-state phase by the supplied hull data. The closest analogue **Zr3AlN** is also on the hull with E_hull = 0.0 and is experimentally synthesized (CS; SS; TS). Related Zr/Hf nitride and Zr-rich intermetallic analogues also show E_hull = 0.0 in the supplied data, supporting stability of this chemistry (CS; SS; TS). The main uncertainty is that calculated 0 K hull stability does not guarantee kinetic accessibility or resistance to Ga/N loss at synthesis temperature [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2 — Route 1 condition severity

Route 1 is supported by the exact-structure Zr3AlN analogue, which was made by melting under pure Ar and slow cooling (CS; SS; TS). The severity score is reduced because melting Zr-rich compositions likely requires >1400 °C and controlled Ar atmosphere [Reasonable Inference based on the melt-growth route in CS/SS/TS]. CS/SS/TS do not provide an exact temperature for the Zr3AlN melt, so the high-temperature deduction is partly inferred rather than directly measured. Ga volatility adds an extra uncertainty not present for AlN-to-Zr3AlN synthesis [Reasonable Inference].

#### F3 — Route 1 kinetics

The melt route has favorable kinetics because liquid-phase processing reduces diffusion barriers, and the closest analogue Zr3AlN formed as a phase-pure product by melting/slow cooling (CS; SS; TS). Structural reconstruction is very favorable because the target and Zr3AlN share the same Cmcm structure and nearly identical Zr–N/Zr–metalloid coordination (CS; SS; TS). The precursor activity before melting is only moderate because Zr and GaN are refractory/dense solids [Reasonable Inference]. The uncertainty is whether GaN survives long enough during melting to deliver N without Ga loss [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2 — Route 2 condition severity

Route 2 uses lower temperatures than melt growth, based on sealed-tube analogues at 500–900 °C for Zr2InN and 750 °C for Ti3InN (CS; SS; TS). The score is penalized for sealed controlled atmosphere, volatile Ga containment, and multi-step processing, all of which are supported by sealed ampoule/tube analogue procedures (CS; SS; TS; [Reasonable Inference]). CS/SS/TS do not directly report Zr3GaN synthesis in sealed ampoules, so the route is extrapolated from related Zr/In and Ti/In nitrides. The lower temperature helps preserve Ga/N stoichiometry but worsens diffusion [Reasonable Inference].

#### F3 — Route 2 kinetics

Kinetics are the limiting factor for Route 2 because ZrN and Zr are refractory and solid-state diffusion is slow [Reasonable Inference]. The related Zr2InN synthesis required 750–850 h, and the Ti3InN analogue required 600 h, demonstrating long reaction times for these nitride/metallide systems (CS; SS; TS). Zr2InN also showed mononitride impurity, indicating incomplete conversion or competing phase persistence (CS; SS; TS). Ga liquid formation may assist transport, but this is an extrapolation rather than explicitly shown in CS/SS/TS [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2 — Route 3 condition severity

Route 3 is more severe because Zr5Sn3N-type reactive powder sintering used 1000–1350 °C, vacuum or evacuated tubes, glovebox handling, and refractory-metal containment (CS). These conditions directly trigger high-temperature, controlled-atmosphere, multi-step, and handling penalties (CS). SS and TS provide insufficient direct evidence for this specific reactive powder sintering route beyond overlapping analogues, so they are neutral for detailed Route 3 condition scoring where not overlapping with CS. The main advantage is better equilibration than low-temperature ampoule synthesis [Reasonable Inference].

The precursor activity remains modest because Zr/ZrN powders are refractory [Reasonable Inference]. ### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

Zr3GaN is likely a **ground-state phase** because its supplied energy above hull is 0.0000 eV/atom, and the closest analogue Zr3AlN is also a synthesized hull-stable Cmcm phase (CS; SS; TS). The analogue support is strong structurally and thermodynamically because Zr3AlN has the same space group, same NZr6 octahedral motif, and very similar bond lengths (CS; SS; TS). The most important enabling factor is the existence of a directly transferable **Zr3AlN Cmcm melt-grown analogue** (CS; SS; TS). The most important limiting factor is maintaining **Ga and N stoichiometry** during high-temperature processing, because Ga volatility and nitride decomposition are plausible risks [Reasonable Inference]. A sealed-ampoule route is therefore recommended as the best balance between stoichiometry control and feasible reaction conditions, despite slower kinetics (CS; SS; TS; [Reasonable Inference]).
