### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic stability.**  
The target has E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom, so it is predicted to be a ground-state phase by the provided thermodynamic data. Chemically similar and thermodynamically similar references also include multiple stable Eu/rare-earth nitrides and oxides, such as BaCeN₂, Yb₂O₂S, Eu₂VO₄, and Eu₃NbO₆ (CS-1, CS-3; SS-2, SS-3; TS-1, TS-2). The main uncertainty is that calculated hull stability does not guarantee kinetic accessibility or correct N/O ordering [Reasonable Inference].

**F2 — Route 1.**  
Route 1 uses moderate temperatures comparable to BaCeN₂ at 850 °C and SrHfN₂ at 1000 °C (CS-1, CS-4), so no >1000 °C penalty was applied. It still requires controlled Ar/N₂ atmosphere, glovebox handling, and air-sensitive nitride/oxide precursors, as seen in Ba₂NF and SrHfN₂ syntheses (CS-2, CS-4). The uncertainty is whether EuO + EuN reacts cleanly before EuO oxidizes or EuN decomposes [Reasonable Inference].

**F3 — Route 1.**  
The kinetics are favorable because the target contains only one cation, Eu, and the EuN/EuO route minimizes cation interdiffusion complexity [Reasonable Inference]. The route is structurally plausible because Caswellsilverite-like Ba₂NF and related SrHfN₂ networks are made by solid-state nitride chemistry (CS-2, CS-4). The penalty comes from long annealing times reported in analogous nitrides, e.g., 70 h to 5 days (CS-1, CS-2, CS-4).

**F2 — Route 2.**  
Route 2 is harsher because reduced Eu oxide analogues typically require high temperatures and evacuated or sealed conditions, e.g., Eu₂VO₄ at 1473 K and Eu₃NbO₆ at 1523 K (SS-2, SS-3; TS-1, TS-2). It also uses air-sensitive EuN and Eu metal [Reasonable Inference], and phase-purity workups may require regrinding/refiring as in Eu₂VO₄ (SS-2; TS-1). CS gives insufficient direct evidence for this exact reductive Eu oxynitride route, so CS is neutral 50 for this route-specific severity assessment.

**F3 — Route 2.**  
The Eu₂O₃ + EuN + Eu route has a clear stoichiometric logic, 3EuN + Eu₂O₃ + Eu → 3Eu₂NO [Reasonable Inference], but Eu₂O₃ is refractory and requires reduction/reconstruction. Eu₂VO₄ and Eu₃NbO₆ syntheses show that reduced Eu oxide phases can be formed, but impurities and repeated firings are common (SS-2, SS-3; TS-1, TS-2). The uncertainty is whether nitrogen incorporation and oxygen-potential control can be achieved simultaneously without forming EuO/Eu₂O₃/EuN mixtures [Reasonable Inference].

**F2 — Route 3.**  
The flux route can operate at ~900 °C, as Eu₂SiN₃ was synthesized in Li flux at 900 °C (TS-4), avoiding the >1000 °C penalty. However, sealed Ta/Nb crucibles, Ar glovebox handling, reactive Li/Na flux, and possible volatile/reactive components make the severity high (TS-4, TS-5). CS and SS provide insufficient direct evidence for alkali-flux Eu₂NO synthesis, so CS and SS are neutral 50 for this route-specific condition factor.

**F3 — Route 3.**  
Flux should improve mass transport and crystallization relative to dry solid-state synthesis [Reasonable Inference], supported by Eu₂SiN₃ growth using Li flux and slow cooling (TS-4). The risk is that Li may enter the structure, because a thermodynamically similar Eu nitride, LiEu₄(BN₂)₃, is itself Li-containing (TS-5). No provided source directly demonstrates flux growth of Eu oxynitride Caswellsilverite, so structural selectivity remains uncertain.

**F4 — Amorphization tendency.**  
The target has one cation species, Eu, so the cation-radius mismatch check is not triggered [Reasonable Inference from target formula]. Its structure is a simple corner/edge-sharing octahedral network with no face-sharing reported, and the formula is small, favoring crystallization over amorphization (target structure). SS/TS give insufficient direct amorphization evidence for Eu₂NO, but crystalline Eu oxide/nitride analogues are well represented (SS-1 to SS-5; TS-1 to TS-5).

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

Eu₂NO is likely a predicted ground-state phase because its E_hull is 0.0000 eV/atom in the target data. The synthesis is reasonably supported by chemically similar nitride and Caswellsilverite-like materials, especially Ba₂NF and SrHfN₂-type solid-state syntheses under Ar/N₂ (CS-2, CS-4), and by Eu reduced oxide chemistry under sealed/vacuum conditions (SS-2, SS-3, SS-5; TS-1, TS-2). The most important enabling factor is that Eu₂NO contains only one cation, which reduces cation-ordering complexity and makes a direct EuN/EuO route plausible [Reasonable Inference]. The most important limiting factor is simultaneous control of nitrogen activity, oxygen activity, and Eu mixed valence, because Eu analogues frequently show impurities, by-products, or phase-purity challenges (SS-1, SS-2, SS-3; TS-3). Overall, synthesis appears feasible but will likely require strict glovebox handling and careful sealed or controlled-atmosphere firing.
