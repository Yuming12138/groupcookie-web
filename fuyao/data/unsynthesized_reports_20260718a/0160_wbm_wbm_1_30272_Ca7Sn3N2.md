### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

#### F1 — Thermodynamic stability

TS gives multiple nearby stable analogues with E_hull = 0.0 eV/atom, including Ca₅Sn₃H, Ca₃GeN, SrCaSn, CaMgSn, and Ca₄In₂N (TS-1–TS-5). The main uncertainty is that CS-1 lists Ca₃SnN at high E_hull despite its reported synthesis, so the Ca–Sn–N energy landscape may contain database/ordering subtleties or competing phases (CS-1).

#### F2/F3 — Route 1

Route 1 has moderate condition severity because Ca₄In₂N/Sr₄In₂N analogues were made at 1420 K under N₂ with simple crucible/furnace conditions (CS-2, CS-4, SS-5, TS-5). Kinetically, elemental Ca/Sn are not highly activated precursors, but low-melting Sn/Ca-rich phases should aid diffusion [Reasonable Inference], and the analogue’s Ca–N/Ca–metal polyhedra closely match the target (CS-2, SS-5, TS-5). The uncertainty is phase control: Ca₄In₂N/Sr₄In₂N reports explicitly state phase-pure product was difficult despite broad condition variation (CS-2, CS-4, SS-5, TS-5).

#### F2/F3 — Route 2

Route 2 is more severe because Ca₃SnN/Ca₃GeN-type syntheses require 1300 °C and Nb sealing for Sn/Ge/Pb systems (CS-1, SS-2, TS-2). Ca₃N₂ improves nitrogen delivery relative to N₂ gas, but the target structure is not the same as cubic perovskite Ca₃SnN/Ca₃GeN, so the structural-reconstruction score is only moderate (CS-1, SS-2, TS-2). Remaining uncertainty is whether the route will lock into Ca₃SnN-like perovskite or other Ca–Sn binaries rather than Ca₇Sn₃N₂ [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2/F3 — Route 3

Route 3 is condition-severe because welded Ta/Nb containers, vacuum/sealed environments, glovebox handling, pre-alloying, and long anneals are all implicated by Ca₅Sn₃H and SrCaSn analogue syntheses (CS-3, SS-1, TS-1; SS-3, TS-3). Its kinetic advantage is a preorganized Ca–Sn framework, but the nitrogen insertion step is not directly demonstrated in the provided Ca–Sn intermetallic analogues, making the reconstruction risk significant [Reasonable Inference]. The strongest support is structural/thermodynamic similarity to Ca₅Sn₃H, the top SS/TS analogue (SS-1, TS-1).

#### F2/F3 — Route 4

Route 4 has relatively mild temperature severity because Ca₂InN flux growth occurs at 800 °C, but it requires sealed Nb tubes, Na metal, NaN₃, slow cooling, and pressure management (CS-5). Flux transport gives the best kinetic score because molten Na and NaN₃ can enhance mass transport and nitrogen availability (CS-5). The uncertainty is high phase selectivity: Ca₂InN itself was difficult to obtain phase-pure, and transferring In-nitride flux chemistry to Ca–Sn–N is [Reasonable Inference] rather than direct evidence (CS-5).

#### F4 — Amorphization tendency

The target contains mixed corner/edge/face-sharing polyhedra, and closely related Ca₄In₂N also contains cuboctahedral and NCa₆ units with mixed sharing modes (CS-2, SS-5, TS-5). The Ca/Sn size and bonding-role contrast is large enough to trigger the cation-size criterion by [Reasonable Inference] from the different Ca/Sn coordination environments reported in Ca–Sn analogues (CS-3, SS-1, TS-1). The full unit-cell atom count is not directly established by CS/SS/TS, so that check is treated as insufficient evidence and not triggered.

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

Ca₇Sn₃N₂ is likely a thermodynamic ground-state or near-ground-state phase because the target E_hull is 0.0000 eV/atom, and TS contains several nearby E_hull = 0.0 Ca–Sn/Ca–nitride/Ca–intermetallic analogues (TS-1–TS-5). The synthesis evidence is reasonably strong because close Ca–In–N analogues share NCa₆ octahedra and Ca-rich metal cuboctahedral motifs (CS-2, SS-5, TS-5), while Ca₃SnN provides direct Ca–Sn–N chemical precedent (CS-1). The most critical enabling factor is the existence of high-temperature N₂/Ca₃N₂ routes that form Ca-rich nitrides with similar Ca–N bond lengths and polyhedral motifs (CS-1, CS-2, SS-2, SS-5). The most critical limiting factor is phase competition/phase purity, since Ca₄In₂N/Sr₄In₂N and Ca₂InN analogues explicitly report difficulty obtaining phase-pure products (CS-2, CS-4, CS-5, SS-5, TS-5). Overall, the target is plausible but will likely require careful stoichiometry, moisture exclusion, and iterative annealing.
