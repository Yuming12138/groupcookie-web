### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

#### F1 — Thermodynamic stability

Ca₂TlN has E_hull = 0.0 eV/atom in the supplied target data, so it is predicted to be on the convex hull and receives 100/100. This is reinforced by Ca₂AuN and Ca₂InN analogues that also have E_hull = 0.0 eV/atom in the reference data (CS1; SS1/TS1). The remaining uncertainty is computational: a hull value does not guarantee a large kinetic formation window or absence of experimentally favored polymorphs [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2 — Route 1 severity

The Ca₂AuN analogue requires 820 °C, dry high-pressure N₂, glovebox handling, and a pressure vessel, making the route demanding but not ultrahigh-pressure by the >5 GPa rubric (CS1). Ca₃N₂ is air/moisture sensitive according to the precursor reasoning in CS1, and Tl adds toxicity/possible volatility concerns [Reasonable Inference]. Insufficient evidence from SS/TS for this exact Ca₃N₂ + Tl high-pressure N₂ route, so SS/TS are neutral for the Route 1 condition-severity score.

#### F3 — Route 1 kinetics

Ca₃N₂ is a chemically active nitride precursor and gave Ca₂AuN under high-pressure N₂ (CS1). The target framework is closely related to Ca₂AuN in Cmcm Ca₂MN coordination, which supports structural transferability (CS1; target data). Kinetic uncertainty remains because replacing Au with Tl may alter metal–metal bonding and competing phase stability [Reasonable Inference], and SS/TS provide no direct high-pressure Tl-route evidence.

#### F2 — Route 2 severity

The Ca₂InN analogue was synthesized at 800 °C in sealed Nb tubes using Na flux and NaN₃, with very slow cooling and specialized arc-welded containment (SS1/TS1). The temperature is moderate, but the sealed azide/Na system, air-sensitive metals, and flux removal make the route operationally challenging (SS1/TS1). Insufficient evidence from CS for Na-flux synthesis of Ca₂TlN specifically, so CS is neutral for Route 2 severity.

#### F3 — Route 2 kinetics

This route receives the best kinetic score because Ca₂InN is the closest structural and thermodynamic analogue to Ca₂TlN: both are Ca₂MN Cmcm phases with the same NCa₆ octahedral framework and 18° tilt (SS1/TS1; target data). Molten Na flux and NaN₃ should enhance transport and nitrogen chemical activity compared with dry metal powders (SS1/TS1). The score is reduced because the Ca₂InN literature notes that attempts to synthesize phase-pure samples mostly failed, indicating a narrow phase field (SS1/TS1).

#### F2 — Route 3 severity

Ambient-pressure nitridation is experimentally simpler than sealed flux or pressure-vessel synthesis, as Ca₃AuN used an alumina crucible/quartz tube under Ar then flowing N₂ at 950 °C (CS4). However, related Ca₂N and Ca–In nitride syntheses can require >1000 °C and long or controlled treatments (SS3/TS3; SS5/TS5), so the route is still moderately severe. Tl toxicity and probable need for sealed containment lower the score [Reasonable Inference].

#### F3 — Route 3 kinetics

Direct elemental nitridation can work for chemically related nitrides such as Ca₃AuN and Sr/Ba pnictide nitrides (CS4, CS5). However, those analogues are not the same Ca₂MN Cmcm structure, and the Ca₄In₂N analogue had explicit phase-purity problems despite varied conditions (SS5/TS5). Therefore, reaction kinetics may be acceptable, but structural selectivity toward Ca₂TlN is uncertain [Reasonable Inference].

#### F4 — Amorphization tendency

The target is a small, ordered crystalline nitride framework rather than a large complex cell; the closest Ca₂InN analogue is listed as Ca₄In₂N₂, i.e., an 8-atom formula representation, supporting low unit-cell complexity (SS1/TS1). The target has mixed corner- and edge-sharing NCa₆ octahedra, but not the full corner/edge/face-sharing coexistence trigger (target data). The main amorphization/structural-disorder concern is Ca–Tl size/electronic mismatch [Reasonable Inference], giving one trigger and a low–medium risk score.

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

Ca₂TlN is likely a predicted ground-state phase because its target E_hull is 0.0 eV/atom, and the closest Ca₂AuN/Ca₂InN analogues are also hull-stable (CS1; SS1/TS1). The synthesis evidence is strong structurally because Ca₂InN is nearly a direct Cmcm template for Ca₂TlN, while Ca₂AuN provides a chemically similar Ca₂MN nitride synthesized from Ca₃N₂ under high-pressure N₂ (SS1/TS1; CS1). The most critical enabling factor is use of a high-activity nitrogen/transport medium, especially Na flux + NaN₃ or high-pressure dry N₂, to form the Ca₂MN framework before competing Ca-rich phases dominate (SS1/TS1; CS1). The most critical limiting factor is phase selectivity: even Ca₂InN reportedly had poor phase-pure reproducibility, and ambient Ca–metal nitridation often gives other stoichiometries such as Ca₃MN-type phases (SS1/TS1; CS4). Tl toxicity and potential volatilization add a serious practical constraint that is not directly resolved by the analogues [Reasonable Inference].
