### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

#### F1 — Thermodynamic Stability

The target is reported to lie exactly on the convex hull, E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom, so it receives the maximum thermodynamic score. Chemically similar CsCoO₂ and CsCuO₂ are also reported at E_hull = 0.0 eV/atom, reinforcing that alkali transition-metal oxides of this type can be thermodynamic ground states (CS-1, CS-2). Structurally and thermodynamically similar K/Li/Ir, K/Pt, Cs/Ir, Rb/Ru, and Cs/Ru oxides also include 0 eV/atom phases (SS-1–SS-5, TS-1–TS-5). Remaining uncertainty is kinetic rather than thermodynamic because CS/SS/TS do not provide direct experimental CsRhO₂ data.

#### F2/F3 — Route 1

Route 1 is supported by the direct CsCoO₂ analogue, which was synthesized by CsN₃/CsNO₃/Co₃O₄ metathesis at 833 K for 60 h under Ar (CS-1). The condition severity is moderate because no high pressure or extreme temperature is needed, but CsN₃ hazards, multiple handling steps, and a closed Ag-lined vessel reduce F2 (CS-1). Kinetics are favorable relative to conventional refractory oxide synthesis because the azide/nitrate reaction is chemically active, but Rh oxide diffusion and Rh incorporation are extrapolated uncertainties [Reasonable Inference]. Insufficient direct evidence from SS/TS for azide/nitrate CsRhO₂ synthesis, so SS/TS are neutral for this specific route.

#### F2/F3 — Route 2

Route 2 has the strongest structural basis because CsCuO₂ is Cmcm and has the same Cs 6-coordinate environment and four-coordinate transition-metal oxide motif as the target (CS-2). The low reported CsCuO₂ synthesis temperature of 380 °C under oxygen makes F2 high, with the main penalty being air-sensitive Cs₂O handling (CS-2). F3 is moderate because the structure match is excellent, but Rh₂O₃/RhO₂ may react more slowly than CuO and may require higher temperature or repeated grinding [Reasonable Inference]. Insufficient direct Rh-specific kinetic evidence from CS/SS/TS, so the Rh mobility estimate remains uncertain.

#### F2/F3 — Route 3

Route 3 is supported by Cs₂RuO₄ and Cs₄IrO₄ syntheses that use Cs₂O₂/CsOₓ plus RuO₂/IrO₂ in sealed Pd/Ag containers under dry Ar for long times (SS-3, SS-5; TS-3, TS-5). F2 is lower than Route 2 because sealed noble-metal tubes and air-sensitive peroxide/superoxide precursors are needed, although temperatures remain below 1000 °C (SS-3, SS-5). F3 is limited by the long anneals of 31–86 d and by the fact that the closest sealed-tube analogues are not the same CsRhO₂ stoichiometry or Cmcm structure (SS-3, SS-5). This route is best viewed as a fallback when oxygen-sintering fails to crystallize the target [Reasonable Inference].

#### F4 — Amorphization Tendency

The target has a large alkali/transition-metal size mismatch, supported by long Cs–O distances versus short M–O distances in CsCuO₂ and CsCoO₂ analogues (CS-1, CS-2). The target also explicitly contains mixed face-, corner-, and edge-sharing connectivity, which increases structural frustration/amorphization risk; similar mixed connectivities appear in CsCoO₂ and K₃(PtO₂)₂ analogues (CS-1, SS-2/TS-2). The unit-cell atom-count trigger is not clearly met because the formula is simple and the close CsCuO₂ analogue has a small reported cell formula (CS-2). Overall amorphization risk is medium, not prohibitive.

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

CsRhO₂ is likely a ground-state phase because its reported E_hull is 0.0000 eV/atom, and many chemically/structurally related alkali transition-metal oxides are also on the hull (CS-1, CS-2, SS-1–SS-5, TS-1–TS-5). The strongest synthesis support comes from CsCuO₂, which is the same Cmcm structure type and was prepared by low-temperature oxygen solid-state sintering from Cs₂O and CuO (CS-2). The key enabling factor is the existence of highly transferable CsMO₂ alkali oxometallate chemistry with stable Cs–O coordination frameworks (CS-1, CS-2). The main limiting factor is Rh-specific redox and kinetic uncertainty, because no direct CsRhO₂ experimental synthesis is included and Rh oxides may require more stringent oxygen-potential or sealed-tube conditions [Reasonable Inference]. Overall, the target appears moderately to highly synthesizable, with Route 2 as the preferred first attempt and sealed peroxide chemistry as a contingency.
