### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic stability.**  
The target E_hull is 0.050 eV/atom, which falls at the upper edge of the 90–100 scoring bin specified in the prompt. Thermodynamic analogues at comparable hull energies, including Hf₃Ir₅B₂ at 0.0538 eV/atom and TiCrIr₂B₂ at 0.0677 eV/atom, are reported as synthesized Ir-rich borides, supporting accessibility of slightly metastable Ir-rich phases (TS1, TS4). The uncertainty is that these analogues are borides, not nitrides, so nitrogen chemical potential and anion retention are not directly proven by TS; insufficient nitride-specific evidence from TS, neutral 50.

**F2 — Route 1 condition severity.**  
Arc melting is strongly supported by the closest structural/chemical analogue Zr₂Ir₆B, which was synthesized under Ar using a water-cooled copper crucible and tungsten electrode with repeated remelting (CS2, SS4). Similar Ir-rich intermetallics and borides also required arc-melting and high-temperature homogenization, including ScIr₃ and Sc₂VIr₅B₂ (SS3, TS3). The severity penalty is high because arc melting implies very high local temperatures and a multi-step remelt/anneal workflow [Reasonable Inference].

**F3 — Route 1 kinetics.**  
The similar Zr₂Ir₆B framework supports the structural endpoint because it contains early-metal Ir₁₂ cuboctahedra and light-anion Ir₆ octahedra like the target (CS2, SS4). Kinetics are limited by refractory Nb/Ir diffusion and possible nitrogen loss, neither of which is solved directly by the B-based analogue [Reasonable Inference]. The melt step improves mixing, as repeated remelting yielded homogeneous Zr₂Ir₆B and ScIr₃ samples (CS2, SS3).

**F2 — Route 2 condition severity.**  
Sealed ampoule routes are supported by Ce₂Pt₈P and Pt₅InAs syntheses that used vacuum-sealed ampoules, long anneals, and regrinding (CS3, CS4). The route avoids arc-melt extremes but still requires controlled vacuum/sealed conditions and long high-temperature annealing, giving a lower F2 than thin film. Nitrogen retention makes the sealed system important, but this is an extrapolation because CS3/CS4 involve P/As rather than N [Reasonable Inference].

**F3 — Route 2 kinetics.**  
CS3 and CS4 show that noble-metal-rich solid-state reactions can be very slow, requiring 7 d per anneal for Ce₂Pt₈P and up to 90–120 d for Pt-containing arsenides (CS3, CS4). Since NbN and Ir are refractory powders, the solid-state route likely has poor precursor activity and slow diffusion [Reasonable Inference]. Structural reconstruction from NbN + Ir to ordered cubic NbIr₆N is substantial, and no CS/SS/TS source directly demonstrates this nitride conversion; insufficient direct evidence from SS/TS, neutral 50.

**F2 — Route 3 condition severity.**  
Thin-film reactive sputtering avoids the >1000 °C and arc-melt penalties, which improves F2 [Reasonable Inference]. However, it requires reactive N₂ plasma/vacuum deposition and composition optimization, so controlled-atmosphere and multi-step deductions apply [Reasonable Inference]. CS/SS/TS provide no direct evidence for sputtering; insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS, neutral 50.

**F3 — Route 3 kinetics.**  
Thin-film deposition can bypass bulk diffusion limitations by atom-by-atom mixing and can kinetically trap modestly metastable nitrides [Reasonable Inference]. The major uncertainty is crystallizing the ordered F-43m structure without forming NbN or segregated Ir, because no CS/SS/TS analogue demonstrates this pathway; insufficient thin-film evidence from CS/SS/TS, neutral 50. Composition control is expected to be demanding because the target is very Ir-rich, Nb:Ir = 1:6 [Reasonable Inference from target formula].

**F4 — Amorphization tendency.**  
The target structure is compact and ordered, with NbIr₁₂ cuboctahedra and NIr₆ octahedra sharing corners/faces, and it does not show the full vertex + edge + face mixed connectivity criterion (target description). The formula is small, NbIr₆N, and there is no evidence for a >40-atom primitive cell [Reasonable Inference]. CS/SS/TS do not provide direct amorphization data, so the low-risk score is based mainly on the target structural description plus analogy to crystalline Ir-rich cuboctahedral phases (CS2, SS3, SS4).

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

NbIr₆N is best considered a borderline ground-state/low-metastability phase because its E_hull is exactly 0.050 eV/atom, at the favorable threshold but not clearly 0 eV/atom. The synthesis evidence is strongest for Ir-rich cuboctahedral frameworks and light-anion Ir₆ octahedra, especially the Zr₂Ir₆B analogue synthesized as single-phase material by arc-melting (CS2, SS4). The most critical enabling factor is the close structural match to Zr₂Ir₆B, including comparable Ir–N/Ir–B distances and analogous octahedral light-anion coordination (target description; CS2, SS4). The most critical limiting factor is nitrogen incorporation/retention in a highly Ir-rich refractory matrix, which is not directly demonstrated by any CS/SS/TS source. Therefore, bulk synthesis is plausible but challenging, while thin-film reactive nitridation may offer the best kinetic route despite weaker direct analogue support [Reasonable Inference].
