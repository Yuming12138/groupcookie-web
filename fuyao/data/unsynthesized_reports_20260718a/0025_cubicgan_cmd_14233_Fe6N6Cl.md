### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic stability.**

Thermodynamic analogues include CrN at E_hull ≈ 0.048 eV/atom and successful nitride synthesis under high nitrogen pressure (TS2), while several TS references are reported as synthesizable nitrides. Remaining uncertainty is that Fe6N6Cl may be metastable with respect to Fe nitrides plus Fe chlorides despite its low hull energy [Reasonable Inference].

**F2/F3 — Route 1.**  
CS5 strongly supports Fe–chloride/ammonium chloride sealed-ampoule chemistry at moderate temperature, so severity is moderate rather than extreme. Kinetics are aided by volatile/ionic chloride transport, but the structural mismatch between CS5 FeCl6 chain/ribbon chemistry and the target Fe–N–Cl cubic network makes reconstruction difficult (CS5; Target). The main uncertainty is whether NH4Cl chemistry can yield a Cl-poor Fe6N6Cl phase rather than chloride-rich ammonium iron chlorides [Reasonable Inference].

**F2/F3 — Route 2.**  
SS1/TS1 support high-pressure NH3/NaNH2 routes for N-rich transition-metal nitrides at 400–600 °C, but the long durations and 6 kbar NH3 autoclave conditions make the route challenging. Kinetics are favorable for nitride formation because NaNH2/NH3 are reactive nitrogen media (SS1/TS1). The uncertainty is Cl retention, since SS1/TS1 do not demonstrate chlorine incorporation into the nitride product.

**F2/F3 — Route 3.**  
SS3 and TS3 support ambient-pressure NH3 ammonolysis of transition-metal nitride precursors at 625–750 °C, giving moderate severity. The metal-organic/chloride precursor route in TS3 improves cation-level mixing and should improve kinetics [Reasonable Inference from TS3], but SS3 warns that phase-pure nitrides may require narrow temperature windows. The largest uncertainty is maintaining one Cl per Fe6N6Cl formula during NH3 ammonolysis because chloride may volatilize as HCl/NH4Cl [Reasonable Inference].

**F2/F3 — Route 4.**  
SS2/TS4 and SS4/TS2 show that high-pressure nitrogen can form dense 3d-metal nitrides, including Fm-3m-type nitride analogues in the CrN case. Kinetics may be acceptable because milling increases defect density and mixing [Reasonable Inference], but long-range cubic ordering and Cl incorporation are not demonstrated by SS5/TS5. This route has low operational burden but high phase-selectivity uncertainty.
