### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

#### F1 — Thermodynamic Stability

The target E_hull is 0.0500 eV/atom, which falls at the prompt’s most favorable threshold, so a high F1 score is justified. Chemically similar TlAlCl4, TlGaCl4, Tl2TeCl6, and HgGaCl4 are on the hull, showing that related Tl/chloride molecular salts can be stable compounds (CS1, CS2, CS3, CS5). Structurally similar zero-dimensional chlorides such as SnCl5PCl4, GeCl4, and Tl2SnCl6 are also on the hull, reinforcing the stability of molecular chloride cluster phases (SS1, SS2, SS3). Thermodynamic analogues are near-hull rather than universally stable, so TS provides supportive but not decisive evidence; e.g., FeCl3 and SiCl2 have E_hull around 0.06–0.07 eV/atom (TS1, TS2).

#### F2 — Route 1

The severity penalties come from vacuum sealing, volatile chloride handling, and toxic/air-sensitive precursors; glovebox and sealed ampoule methods are explicit in CS1/CS2, and drybox handling is explicit for hydrolyzable chloride systems in SS1. Insufficient evidence from TS for this exact Tl–Si sealed-ampoule chemistry; TS is treated as neutral for the precise condition severity but supports general chloride volatility/transport behavior through TS1.

#### F3 — Route 1

Reaction kinetics should be favorable relative to refractory solid-state synthesis because CS1/CS2 show low-temperature reaction of TlCl with Al/Ga trichlorides. ### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

TlSiCl6 is likely near-metastable rather than a robust ground-state phase because its E_hull is exactly 0.050 eV/atom and because the closest experimentally supported Tl hexachlorometallates are Tl2MCl6 rather than TlMCl6 (CS3, SS3). The CS and SS analogues strongly support the broader chemistry of zero-dimensional thallium chlorometallates and isolated MCl6 octahedral clusters, especially Tl2TeCl6 and Tl2SnCl6 (CS3, SS3). The most critical enabling factor is the availability of mild chloride-rich solution or sealed-ampoule routes that can form molecular chloride cluster salts without high-temperature diffusion barriers (CS1, CS2, CS3, SS3). The most critical limiting factor is stoichiometry/charge-balance control for the unusual Tl:Si:Cl = 1:1:6 composition, which may favor competing Tl2SiCl6 or TlCl/SiCl4-derived phases [Reasonable Inference]. Overall, synthesis is plausible as a chloride-rich molecular salt, but confirming the exact target composition and structure would require careful phase analysis.
