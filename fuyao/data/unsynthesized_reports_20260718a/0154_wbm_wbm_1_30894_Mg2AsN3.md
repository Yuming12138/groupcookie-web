### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

#### F1 — Thermodynamic stability

The target has E_hull = 0.0 eV/atom, so it is predicted as thermodynamically stable by the supplied target data. Closely related Mg2PN3, Zn2PN3, and Ca2PN3 analogues also have E_hull = 0.0 eV/atom, showing that A2PnN3-type nitride frameworks can be true ground-state phases (CS-1, SS-1, SS-2, SS-5, TS-1, TS-2, TS-4). Remaining uncertainty is chemical: CS/SS/TS contain no direct Mg–As–N experimental analogue, so arsenic nitride stability is inferred rather than proven.

#### F2 — Route 1 condition severity

Route 1 receives a moderate severity score because the closest Mg2PN3 analogue is synthesized at only 800 °C and ambient pressure, avoiding GPa conditions (CS-1, SS-1, TS-1). However, the synthesis requires inert handling, sealed ampoules, long heat treatment, and moisture-sensitive nitrides (CS-1, SS-1, TS-1). The arsenic component adds toxicity and likely volatility beyond the phosphorus analogue [Reasonable Inference], while CS/SS/TS provide insufficient direct arsenic-specific evidence.

#### F3 — Route 1 kinetics

Kinetics are favorable compared with refractory oxide solid-state reactions because Mg2PN3 forms from binary nitrides at 800 °C, indicating adequate precursor reactivity and manageable diffusion (CS-1, SS-1, TS-1). The target framework is very close to Mg2PN3 in symmetry and tetrahedral connectivity, lowering structural reconstruction penalty (CS-1, SS-1, TS-1). The long 7–14 d times in Mg/Ca nitridophosphates indicate slow ordering and justify not scoring kinetics higher (CS-1, SS-5, TS-1, TS-4).

#### F2 — Route 2 condition severity

Route 2 is severe because Zn2PN3 requires 8 GPa and 1200 °C in a multianvil assembly (SS-2, TS-2). That route also requires glovebox loading, BN crucibles, high-pressure equipment, and careful quenching/decompression (SS-2, TS-2). It is retained because pressure may suppress arsenic/nitrogen loss [Reasonable Inference], but the equipment burden is much larger than Route 1.

#### F3 — Route 2 kinetics

HPHT kinetics are strong because Zn2PN3 forms phase-pure in only 10 min at 1200 °C and 8 GPa (SS-2, TS-2). The target has the same enargite-like tetrahedral framework motif, so structural reconstruction under HPHT is plausible (SS-2, TS-2). The main uncertainty is whether Mg2AsN3 needs pressure at all, since Mg2PN3 forms at ambient pressure (CS-1, SS-1, TS-1), and whether As nitride precursors survive HPHT without side reactions [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2 — Route 3 condition severity

Route 3 avoids >1000 °C and GPa pressure because molten-salt nitrides can form at 760–950 °C (TS-5, SS-3, SS-4, TS-3). It is still operationally severe because it uses reactive alkali metals/azides, welded Nb/Ta ampoules, vacuum/inert sealing, slow cooling, and flux removal (TS-5, SS-3, SS-4, TS-3). CS/SS/TS do not directly demonstrate Mg–As–N flux synthesis, so this is a more speculative backup route.

#### F3 — Route 3 kinetics

Flux/azide chemistry can improve mass transport and nitrogen activity, as shown by NaN3/Na/Li nitride synthesis at 760 °C and LiN3/Li metathesis nitride syntheses at 950 °C (TS-5, SS-3, SS-4, TS-3). However, TS-5 reports product mixtures, and SS-3/SS-4 report heterogeneous products/side phases in complex nitrides, indicating phase selection can be difficult. Thus kinetics may be good, but selective crystallization of Mg2AsN3 is uncertain.

#### F4 — Amorphization tendency

The target consists of ordered MgN4 and AsN4 tetrahedra with corner-sharing connectivity, which generally lowers amorphization risk relative to mixed edge/face-sharing networks [Reasonable Inference from target description]. The Mg/As cation size and charge contrast triggers one amorphization/ordering concern [Reasonable Inference]. CS/SS/TS analogues such as Mg2PN3 and Zn2PN3 crystallize as ordered Cmc2_1 frameworks, supporting crystallizability rather than glass formation (CS-1, SS-1, SS-2, TS-1, TS-2).

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

Mg2AsN3 is likely a ground-state phase because its reported E_hull is 0.0 eV/atom, and several close nitridophosphate analogues are also on the hull (CS-1, SS-1, SS-2, TS-1, TS-2). The strongest support comes from Mg2PN3, which has the same Mg2PnN3 stoichiometry family, Cmc2_1 symmetry, and similar nitride framework, and is experimentally accessible at 800 °C in sealed ampoules (CS-1, SS-1, TS-1). The key enabling factor is the close structural and chemical match to Mg2PN3, making a direct P→As substitution route plausible [Reasonable Inference]. The key limiting factor is the absence of direct As-containing CS/SS/TS precedents and the likely toxicity/volatility/instability of arsenic nitride precursors [Reasonable Inference]. Overall, synthesis appears feasible but will require stringent sealed-ampoule stoichiometry control.
