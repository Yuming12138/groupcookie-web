### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

#### F1 — Thermodynamic stability

The target E_hull is exactly 0.0500 eV/atom, which lies at the favorable threshold for potentially synthesizable metastable compounds. TS analogues with similar hull values, such as CsMnAs at 0.0556 eV/atom (TS-3), Cs₂Se at 0.0751 eV/atom (TS-1), and Rb₂S at 0.0876 eV/atom (TS-4), indicate that alkali-rich phases modestly above the hull can be made. CS analogues include higher-hull alkali intermetallic/hydride-related compositions near 0.19–0.24 eV/atom (CS-1, CS-3, CS-5), but these are chemically less directly relevant to nitrides. Remaining uncertainty is that E_hull alone does not capture kinetic decomposition of K₆BaN into K₃N, Ba nitrides, metallic K, or electrides [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2/F3 — Route 1

Route 1 receives a moderate-low F2 score because it requires sealed reactive alkali handling, air-sensitive K₃N/K/Ba, and likely multiple operations under Ar, consistent with sensitivity issues in K₃N (SS-1) and alkali chalcogenides (TS-4). The temperature severity is not high because relevant alkali phases were made near 320–350 °C (CS-1, CS-5, TS-4). Kinetically, the route is favored because K₃N already contains NK₆ octahedra closely matching the target NK₆ geometry (SS-1). The largest uncertainty is whether Ba can insert/order without decomposing K₃N or forming Ba nitrides [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2/F3 — Route 2

Route 2 has a somewhat better F2 score than Route 1 under the provided deduction scheme because it avoids high temperature and bulk sealed pressure, but it requires UHV and activated nitrogen, as shown for K₃N deposition (SS-1). Its F3 score is lower because composition control for K:Ba:N = 6:1:1 is expected to be narrow, especially with volatile K [Reasonable Inference]. SS-1 supports the feasibility of non-equilibrium K–N deposition but also reports metastability, poor crystallinity, and decomposition sensitivity. CS and TS provide insufficient evidence for Ba-containing thin-film nitride growth, so that part remains speculative.

#### F2/F3 — Route 3

Route 3 is penalized in F2 for NH₃ pressure, sealed autoclave operation, and air-sensitive alkali handling, directly analogous to the high-pressure NH₃ synthesis conditions in TS-1. Its F3 is intermediate-high because ammonothermal transport can improve mass transport at moderate temperature, as TS-1 demonstrates for a new alkali-rich Cs₂Se modification. However, the structural reconstruction score is low because no CS/SS/TS source demonstrates K–Ba–N nitride formation in ammonothermal media. The key uncertainty is whether NH₃/amide chemistry yields the desired nitride rather than amides, hydrides, or binary nitrides [Reasonable Inference].

#### F4 — Amorphization tendency

The target has only one clear amorphization trigger: significant K/Ba cation size mismatch, inferred from standard ionic-radius trends [Reasonable Inference]. The target framework is relatively simple in the description, with NK₆ octahedra and BaK₁₂ cuboctahedra, and the reported sharing is corner/face rather than full vertex+edge+face coexistence. SS-1 warns that K₃N-type K–N products can have poor crystallinity, so amorphous/poorly crystalline products remain a practical risk even if the formal F4 score is not severe. CS/TS do not provide direct amorphization data for K–Ba–N; insufficient evidence from CS and TS for this specific factor.

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

K₆BaN is best regarded as a near-hull metastable or borderline-stable phase because its E_hull is 0.0500 eV/atom, exactly at the favorable cutoff. The structural analogue support is strongest from K₃N, which has very similar NK₆ octahedra and K–N distances (SS-1), while chemical support from CS is mostly indirect alkali-rich synthesis under controlled atmospheres (CS-1, CS-2, CS-5). Thermodynamic analogues show that alkali-rich compounds around 0.05–0.09 eV/atom can be synthesized using sealed or high-pressure methods (TS-1, TS-3, TS-4). The most critical enabling factor is the availability of a K₃N-like NK₆ structural motif that can plausibly template the nitride sublattice (SS-1). The most critical limiting factor is chemical/stoichiometric control of extremely reducing, air-sensitive, K-rich material with possible K volatility and competing binary nitride/metal phases [Reasonable Inference].
