# SECTION 1 — Possible Synthesis Routes

# SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

## F1 — Thermodynamic Stability

The target has a calculated physical \(E_\text{hull}\) of 0.0000 eV/atom, placing it in the highest stability category under the supplied rubric. **[Target record]** The CS set contains BaCrF₆ and RbCrF₆ with \(E_\text{hull}=0\), and the TS set contains CsRuF₆ and RbRuF₆ with \(E_\text{hull}=0\), supporting a stable alkali-transition-metal hexafluoride family. **[CS; TS]** **Insufficient evidence from SS** for a target-specific thermodynamic score; SS is therefore neutral for this factor. The remaining uncertainty is that the supplied target is theory-only and has no reported experimental synthesis. **[Target record]**

## F2 — Route 1

The photochemical route requires anhydrous HF, F₂ at approximately 3 bar, UV irradiation, and 10–20 days of reaction time. **[CS]** These conditions are mild in temperature but severe in chemical handling and process control, resulting in a moderate-low F2 score. The route benefits from direct ACrF₆ precedent, but V-specific kinetics and oxidation-state behavior are not experimentally established. **[CS] [Reasonable Inference]**

## F2 — Route 2

The direct-fluorination route is based on CsRuF₆ and RbRuF₆ preparations at 350 °C for approximately 2 h under elemental fluorine. **[CS; SS; TS]** It avoids the prolonged UV/HF operation of Route 1, but elemental F₂ and possible volatile vanadium fluorides remain major hazards. The score is therefore higher than Route 1 but still substantially below an ordinary inert-atmosphere solid-state synthesis. **[CS; SS; TS]**

## F2 — Route 3

Hydrothermal V oxyfluoride syntheses use HF-containing media at 100–160 °C for about 24 h in sealed Teflon-lined or polymeric vessels. **[SS; TS]** The low temperature is favorable, but HF, sealed operation, and multiple solution-processing steps impose significant practical penalties. The largest concern is that the reported chemistry produces oxyfluorides rather than pure CsVF₆. **[SS; TS]**

## F3 — Route 1

The ACrF₆ photochemical precedent forms the desired structural family at ambient temperature, implying that fluoride transport and framework assembly can occur without high-temperature bulk diffusion. **[CS]** The use of pre-fluorinated precursors improves precursor activity, while the highly fluorinating medium should facilitate formation of a high-valent MF₆ unit. **[CS] [Reasonable Inference]** However, the 10–20 day reaction time and possible V oxidation-state or solubility differences introduce kinetic uncertainty. **[CS] [Reasonable Inference]**

## F3 — Route 2

CsRuF₆ and RbRuF₆ show the same type of 12-coordinate alkali-metal/MF₆ framework, similar tilt angles, and comparable M–F distances to the target. **[CS; SS; TS]** This gives Route 2 a strong structural-reconstruction score. The use of CsF and VF₃ also reduces the required anion rearrangement relative to an oxide-based synthesis, although V(III) oxidation to the target V(V) state must still occur. **[Target record; CS] [Reasonable Inference]**

## F3 — Route 3

Hydrothermal synthesis provides excellent ion transport and can produce crystalline V–F-containing materials at 100–160 °C. **[SS; TS]** Nevertheless, the supplied vanadium precedents are VOF₃/VOF₅-type oxyfluorides, and the presence of V₂O₅, water, or oxygenated solvents makes formation of pure CsVF₆ less kinetically and chemically direct. **[SS; TS]** Consequently, Route 3 receives the lowest F3 score despite favorable transport kinetics.

## F4 — Amorphization Tendency

The target contains a very large Cs⁺ site and a much smaller octahedral V site, triggering the cation-radius mismatch criterion. **[Target record] [Reasonable Inference]** Its CsF₁₂ and VF₆ units also exhibit corner, edge, and face-sharing connectivity, triggering the mixed-connectivity criterion. **[Target record]** The unit-cell atom count is not supplied, so there is **insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS** to trigger C2.
