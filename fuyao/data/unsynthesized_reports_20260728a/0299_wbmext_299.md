(Target; CS1–3, CS5, SS1–5, TS1–4) The TS set is especially supportive because the top thermodynamic matches include Sm, Nd, Pr, and La iridium germanides with E_hull = 0.0 eV/atom. (TS1–4) Remaining uncertainty is experimental rather than thermodynamic, because the target is described as a theory-only WBM candidate without identified experimental synthesis. (Target)

**F2 — Route 1**  
Route 1 has moderate severity because it avoids high pressure and stays near 1000 °C, but it requires evacuated ampoules, purified Ar handling, and multiple processing steps. (CS1–3, SS1–5, TS1–5) The air-sensitive Y penalty is an extrapolation from rare-earth glovebox handling and general Y oxidation behavior. (CS1–3, SS1–5, TS1–5; [Reasonable Inference])

**F2 — Route 2**  
Route 2 has milder temperature severity because CS4 reports 700 °C synthesis, but it still requires Ar protection and regrind/temper processing. (CS4) SS and TS provide insufficient evidence for this low-temperature ceramic route, so those source families are neutral at 50 for route-specific condition support. (insufficient evidence from SS/TS)

**F2 — Route 3**  
Route 3 is condition-severe because CS5 uses UHV evacuation, Ar pressurization, induction heating, quenching, and annealing. (CS5) The >1400 °C deduction is an adaptation for Y/Ir complete-fusion processing rather than a direct CS5 value, because Ce melts much more readily than Y. (CS5; [Reasonable Inference])

**F3 — Route 1**  
Route 1 is kinetically moderate: solid elemental precursors and refractory Ir slow diffusion, but the same P4/nmm framework is already demonstrated across many RE(IrGe)₂ analogues. (CS1–3, SS1–5, TS1–4) The likely presence of molten Ge near 1000 °C may help reaction transport, but that is an inferred kinetic benefit rather than directly reported. (CS1–3; [Reasonable Inference])

**F3 — Route 2**  
Route 2 has weaker kinetics because 700 °C ceramic processing is supported only for a Pt analogue, while direct Ir analogues use the higher 1273 K route. (CS4, CS1–3, SS1–5, TS1–4) The CS4 need for regrinding and several-day tempering indicates incomplete initial reaction, which lowers the kinetics score. (CS4)

**F3 — Route 3**  
Route 3 has better homogenization kinetics because CS5 directly reports melt uptake of Ir and Ge into the rare-earth melt. (CS5) Its remaining kinetic uncertainty is transferability to Y, since the Ce route benefits from easy Ce melting while Y likely requires more aggressive melting conditions. (CS5; [Reasonable Inference])

**F4 — Amorphization Tendency**  
The small RE₂Ge₄Ir₄ analogue cell and repeated crystallization of the same P4/nmm family indicate low amorphization risk. (CS1–3, SS1–5, TS1–4) One radius-mismatch trigger is retained as a conservative [Reasonable Inference], while mixed polyhedral connectivity is not counted because CS/SS/TS do not explicitly establish target-like vertex/edge/face coexistence.
