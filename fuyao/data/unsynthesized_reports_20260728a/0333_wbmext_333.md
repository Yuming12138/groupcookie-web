### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability.** The target has supplied E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom, and the nearest TS analogues LuAgSn, YAgSn, TmAgSn, and GdAgSi are all reported at E_hull = 0.0, so the stability score is high [TS1, TS2, TS3, TS4]. CS and SS analogues also include multiple E_hull = 0 phases and only slightly metastable alternatives near 0.018–0.020 eV/atom, supporting a robust near-hull R–Ag–Sn chemistry [CS2, CS4, CS5, SS1, SS2]. The remaining uncertainty is that CS/SS/TS do not provide direct experimental synthesis evidence for exact GdAgSn, so exact phase stability is inferred from analogues [CS, SS, TS; Reasonable Inference].

**F2/F3 — Route 1.** Route 1 has moderate condition severity because it uses ambient-pressure arc melting under Ar and quenching, which is standard for LuAgSn/YAgSn analogues [CS5, SS1, SS3, TS1, TS2]. Kinetics are favorable because melting bypasses much of the slow diffusion problem, and the P6₃mc structure is already demonstrated in LuAgSn/YAgSn/TmAgSn analogues [CS5, SS1, SS3, TS1, TS2]. The main uncertainty is Sn loss and whether Gd stabilizes the same quenched P6₃mc polymorph as Lu/Y/Tm [CS5, SS3, TS2; Reasonable Inference].

**F2/F3 — Route 2.** Route 2 is penalized by vacuum ampoule annealing, multiple operations, and long thermal treatments, but it is experimentally well supported for RAgSn analogues [CS3, SS3, SS5, TS2, TS5]. Kinetics are slower than direct quench because ordering depends on solid-state annealing over 10–30 days, although the analogues show this can produce phase-pure materials [SS3, TS2]. The uncertainty is whether annealing drives target P6₃mc GdAgSn or instead converts it to a competing RAgSn polymorph [CS1, CS3, CS4; Reasonable Inference].

**F2/F3 — Route 3.** Route 3 has moderate severity because CS4 uses sealed Ta tubes and high-frequency heating without >5 GPa pressure, but SS and TS provide insufficient direct high-frequency evidence and are neutral = 50 for that method class [CS4]. Kinetics should be reasonable because high-frequency melting gives rapid reaction contact, but structural transfer is only moderate because the CS4 YbAgSn analogue is P-6m2 rather than P6₃mc [CS4]. The uncertainty is polymorph selectivity and tube/charge compatibility for GdAgSn [CS4; Reasonable Inference].

**F2/F3 — Route 4.** Route 4 is kinetically credible because high-pressure P6₃mc TmAgSn is very close to the target and uses high T plus annealing, but its condition severity is low-scoring due to 11.5 GPa and multistep multianvil processing [CS2, SS4, TS3]. This route is best viewed as a backup if ambient-pressure arc/quench does not produce the target polymorph [CS2, SS4, TS3; Reasonable Inference]. The uncertainty is whether GdAgSn needs high pressure at all, since ambient P6₃mc LuAgSn/YAgSn analogues exist [CS5, SS1, SS3, TS1, TS2].

**F4 — Amorphization Tendency.** The target-like P6₃mc RAgSn analogues are small, ordered intermetallic structures rather than large complex cells, so the atom-count risk is low [CS5, SS1, SS3, TS1, TS2]. Mixed polyhedral connectivity is prominent in P-62m alternatives but not in the P6₃mc target-like analogues, so only the size-mismatch check is conservatively triggered [CS1, CS3, SS2, TS4; Reasonable Inference]. The remaining uncertainty is that rapid quenching could trap disorder or secondary phases if Gd favors a competing polymorph [CS1, CS3, CS4; Reasonable Inference].
