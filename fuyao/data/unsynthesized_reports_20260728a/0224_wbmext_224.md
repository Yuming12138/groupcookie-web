### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability**  
The target’s supplied E_hull is 0.0000 eV/atom, and the CS/SS/TS analogue sets contain many zero-hull fluoride phases, including CuSnF6, ZnPdF6, Na2GeF6, BaGeF6, and LiInF4 (CS1; CS2; SS1; SS3; SS4; SS5; TS1–TS5). This supports a high F1 score because the target is not thermodynamically isolated relative to stable fluoride chemistries (CS1; CS2; TS1–TS5). Remaining uncertainty is experimental, not DFT-based: CS/SS/TS do not prove ZnGeF6 has already been synthesized, so the score is 95 rather than 100.

**F2 — Route 1**  
Route 1 is penalized for dry inert/sealed operation, volatile GeF4 use, multi-step sealed-tube processing, and hazardous fluorides (CS1; CS4; SS3; TS2; SS5; TS5). It is not penalized for very high temperature or high pressure because the analogues operate below ~1000 °C and do not use >5 GPa equipment (CS1; SS3; TS2; SS5; TS5). Pressure-specific high-pressure evidence is insufficient from CS/SS/TS and is treated as neutral 50.

**F3 — Route 1**  
Kinetics are moderate-to-good because fluoride precursors and volatile GeF4 should be more reactive than dense refractory oxides [Reasonable Inference], and ABF6 corner-sharing analogues are close structural templates (CS1; CS2; SS1; TS1). The score is reduced because long anneals, slow cooling, and impurity risks are explicitly present in sealed fluoride analogues (CS1; SS3; TS2).

**F2 — Route 2**  
Route 2 is less thermally severe but chemically severe because it requires flowing F2 and multiple fluorination/tempering steps (CS2). Exact CS2 reaction temperature is not supplied, so that source is neutral 50 for the precise temperature sub-factor; the proposed 350–550 °C window is inferred from the 100 °C prefluorination plus 530–620 °C fluoride analogues [Reasonable Inference] (CS2; CS1; SS3; TS2).

**F3 — Route 2**  
Direct F2 fluorination gives the best kinetics because CS2 demonstrates formation of the same Zn-containing R-3 ABF6 motif from intimate oxide/salt mixtures (CS2). ### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

ZnGeF6 is best interpreted as a likely **DFT ground-state candidate** rather than a clearly metastable phase because its supplied E_hull is 0 and many CS/SS/TS fluoride analogues are also zero-hull or near-zero-hull phases (CS1; CS2; SS1; SS3; SS4; TS1–TS5). The analogue support is strong structurally and chemically for ABF6 corner-sharing fluoride networks, especially ZnPdF6 and CuSnF6, but no CS/SS/TS entry directly reports ZnGeF6 itself (CS1; CS2; SS1; TS1). The most important enabling factor is the demonstrated accessibility of Zn-containing or GeF6-containing fluoride frameworks under direct fluorination or sealed fluoride conditions (CS2; SS3; TS2; SS4; TS3). The most important limiting factor is fluorine chemistry control: F2/HF/GeF4 hazards, volatile Ge loss, and competing phase formation are recurring risks in the closest synthetic analogues (CS2; CS3; CS4; SS3; TS2). Overall, the target is moderately high-probability for a specialized fluorination laboratory, with Route 2 as the most practical first attempt (CS2).
