### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability:**  
The target has E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom, which places it in the highest thermodynamic-stability bin. (Target) Thermodynamic analogues are mostly on-hull, including TS1–TS5 and several SS analogues, which supports the idea that related alkali-rich oxides can be stable crystalline phases. (SS2–SS5, TS1–TS5) The main conflict is CS1: a chemically identical Na₂O₂ analogue is Pbcm and 0.279 eV/atom above hull, so the uncertainty is polymorph selection rather than Na₂O₂ composition itself. (CS1, Target)

**F2/F3 — Route 1:**  
The wet route has mild thermal and pressure requirements because the Li₂O₂ analogue is made by reflux/boiling precipitation for 30 min. (CS2, SS1) Its condition score is reduced because Na peroxide handling likely requires stricter dryness than the Li case, and the analogue route has multiple operations. (CS2, SS1, SS2, TS2; [Reasonable Inference]) Kinetics are favorable for nucleation because solution precipitation avoids bulk diffusion, but structural transfer is uncertain because target Na₂O₂ has NaO₆ coordination while Li₂O₂ has LiO₄ coordination; **insufficient evidence from TS**, neutral 50 for this wet-route factor. (Target, CS2, SS1)

**F2/F3 — Route 2:**  
The dry sealed route is moderate in severity because Na-rich analogues are synthesized at 400–640 °C without high pressure, but dry Ar/vacuum/O₂ control and air-sensitive Na–O precursors reduce the F2 score. (CS4, SS2, SS4, SS5, TS2, TS3) Kinetics are favorable because Na₂O₂/Na₂O are reactive Na–O precursors and no refractory cation diffusion is present in the binary target. (CS4, SS2, SS5, TS2, TS3; [Reasonable Inference]) The limiting kinetic issue is selecting the target P6₃/mmc polymorph rather than a competing Na₂O₂ polymorph, as CS1 demonstrates. (CS1, Target)

**F2/F3 — Route 3:**  
The high-pressure route is condition-severe because it requires DAC or multi-anvil-style operation, inert loading, pressure near or above 5 GPa, and quench/decompression handling. (CS1, CS3) Kinetics are acceptable for a simple Na–O system under pressure, but structural reconstruction is uncertain because the direct Na₂O₂ high-pressure analogue is not the target structure. (CS1, Target; [Reasonable Inference]) **Insufficient evidence from SS and TS** for high-pressure synthesis, so those source families are neutral 50 for Route 3 transferability. (SS1–SS5, TS1–TS5)

**F4 — Amorphization Tendency:**  
The target has only one cation species, a simple Na₂O₂ composition, and no reported coexistence of vertex/edge/face-sharing networks in the target description, so none of the three amorphization checks trigger. (Target) Crystalline alkali oxide analogues in CS/SS/TS support that related compositions can crystallize, but they do not directly measure target amorphization. (CS1–CS5, SS1–SS5, TS1–TS5) The remaining uncertainty is experimental phase selection for a theory-only prototype. (Target, CS1)
