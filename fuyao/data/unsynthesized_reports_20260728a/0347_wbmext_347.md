### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability:**  
The target is reported at E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom, and CS/SS/TS analogues are overwhelmingly on-hull or near-hull, including SmAgPb, NdAgPb, YAgPb, TmAgPb, and GdCuPb at 0.0 eV/atom. [Target data/CS/SS/TS] This supports a high F1 score of 96, but the score is capped below 100 because the target is still theory-only in the supplied validation. [Target data]

**F2 — Route 1:**  
Arc melting plus 870 K annealing is experimentally standard for the RAgPb analogues, but the route still requires controlled Ar/vacuum handling, sealed annealing, multiple processing steps, and Pb-containing charges. [CS/SS/TS] High pressure is not required, which prevents the condition score from falling further. [CS/SS/TS]

**F3 — Route 1:**  
The main kinetic advantage is that arc melting produces a liquid alloy and the exact P6₃mc analogue structures already exist for SmAgPb and NdAgPb. [CS/SS/TS] The main kinetic penalty is the reported 720 h anneal and repeated remelting, which imply slow ordering or phase-homogenization requirements. [CS/SS/TS]

**F2 — Route 2:**  
The induction/Ta-vessel route is supported most directly by TS GdCuPb, but CS/SS give insufficient direct induction-melt evidence and are neutral 50 for that route-specific condition basis. [TS] Its severity is similar to Route 1 because it still uses high-temperature metallic melting, inert containment, Pb-bearing charge, and post-melt annealing. [TS; Reasonable Inference]

**F3 — Route 2:**  
Induction melting should improve mixing, and TS shows a Gd–Pb intermetallic can be made by this style of processing. [TS] The lower F3 relative to Route 1 comes from structural mismatch: the TS analogue is GdCuPb in P6₃/mmc, while the target is GdAgPb in P6₃mc. [TS/CS/SS]

**F2 — Route 3:**  
The flux route avoids a deliberate >1000 °C hold by [Reasonable Inference], but it still requires sealed/inert handling, excess Pb, decanting, and toxicity controls. CS/SS/TS provide insufficient direct flux evidence, so their direct route signal is neutral 50 and the route remains lower-confidence. [CS/SS/TS]

**F3 — Route 3:**  
A Pb-rich liquid flux could improve transport and crystal growth by [Reasonable Inference], but no CS/SS/TS source demonstrates that GdAgPb or an RAgPb analogue crystallizes from Pb flux. The score is therefore near-neutral despite the attractive diffusion pathway. [Reasonable Inference; insufficient direct evidence from CS/SS/TS]

**F4 — Amorphization Tendency:**  
The structure is a small-cell 3D intermetallic rather than a large complex framework, and close P6₃mc analogues are crystalline products after conventional annealing. [CS/SS/TS] One risk trigger is assigned for likely Gd/Ag/Pb size mismatch by [Reasonable Inference], while mixed vertex+edge+face polyhedral sharing is not established for the target from CS/SS/TS. [TS/CS/SS]

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

GdAgPb is likely a DFT ground-state or very near-ground-state phase because the target E_hull is 0.0000 eV/atom and the closest CS/SS/TS analogues are mostly on-hull. [Target data/CS/SS/TS] The analogue support is strong because RAgPb compounds across several rare-earth elements are synthesized from pure metals, and SmAgPb/NdAgPb match the target P6₃mc structure closely. [CS/SS/TS] The most important enabling factor is the existence of a directly transferable arc-melt + long-anneal protocol for R–Ag–Pb phases. [CS/SS/TS] The main limiting factor is not thermodynamics but phase/stoichiometry control, especially Pb volatility/toxicity and competition between P6₃mc and P-62m RAgPb variants. [CS/SS/TS; Target data; Reasonable Inference] Overall, the target is a credible synthesis candidate, with Route 1 recommended as the highest-confidence route. [CS/SS/TS]
