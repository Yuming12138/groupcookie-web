### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

InWO4 is likely a **DFT ground-state candidate** because its supplied E_hull is 0.0000 eV/atom, but it remains experimentally unvalidated. (Target) The strongest support is the high-pressure AlWO4 analogue, which shares the Cmmm A3+W5O4 octahedral framework. (SS2, TS1, Target) Chemical analogues show that many tungstates are synthetically accessible, but most CS examples are divalent-cation W(VI) tungstates and therefore overstate the ease of making In(III)–W(V) InWO4. (CS1, CS3, CS4, CS5; Target) The key enabling factor is **octahedral-framework transferability from AlWO4**. (SS2, TS1) The key limiting factor is **W(V) redox control**, especially avoiding more oxidized In–W–O competing phases. [Reasonable Inference from SS2/TS1 and CS W(VI) analogues]

### SECTION 7 — Synthesis Challenges

| # | Route | Challenge | Severity | Mitigation Strategy |
|:--:|:--:|:--|:--:|:--|
| 1 | All | Maintaining In(III)–W(V) charge balance rather than forming W(VI) tungstates. | High | Use WO3/WO2 redox-buffered stoichiometry as in AlWO4-type chemistry. (SS2, TS1; [Reasonable Inference]) |
| 2 | All | Competing phase formation from easier W(VI) MWO4 chemistry. | High | Prefer low-pO2 sealed or high-pressure conditions instead of open-air firing. (CS3, CS4, CS5, SS2/TS1) |
| 3 | Route 1 | Slow diffusion through refractory oxide pellets. | Medium | Use fine powders, pellet pressing, long holds, and regrinding as in sealed solid-state analogues. (SS1, SS4) |
| 4 | Route 2 | Residual carbon or over-reduction during gel-to-oxide conversion. | Medium | Use staged drying/calcination and controlled Ar/H2 anneal. (TS5; [Reasonable Inference]) |
| 5 | Route 3 | Need for specialized high-pressure synthesis equipment. | High | Start from the AlWO4 condition: ~40 kbar, 850°C, short dwell. (SS2, TS1) |
| 6 | Route 4 | Flux contamination and fluoride corrosion/toxicity. | Medium | Treat flux growth as a secondary crystal-growth route after powder synthesis; use slow cooling as in CdWO4. (CS1; [Reasonable Inference]) |
| 7 | Route 5 | In/W transport mismatch and halide-agent sensitivity. | High | Use pre-synthesized feed, purified transport agent, dry sealed ampoule, and conservative temperature gradient. (TS3; [Reasonable Inference]) |
| 8 | All | Ordering/amorphization risk from In/W size mismatch. | Medium | Favor high-pressure crystallization or extended annealing to improve octahedral ordering. (Target, SS2/TS1; [Reasonable Inference]) |
