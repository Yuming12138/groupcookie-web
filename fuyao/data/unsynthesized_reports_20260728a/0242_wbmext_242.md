### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability:** MgPd5Se is predicted at E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom from the target data, which places it in the 90–100 bracket. CS, SS, and TS all contain on-hull or near-hull Pd-rich analogues, including Pd5InSe, Pd5HgSe, Pd5As, Pd4Se, and Pd17In4Se4. (CS1–CS5; SS1–SS5; TS1–TS5) Remaining uncertainty is experimental rather than DFT stability, because the target is theory-only in the supplied target data.

**F2 — Route 1:** CVT avoids >1000 °C and high pressure, but it requires vacuum sealing, iodine, volatile Se/Mg control, and multiple thermal steps. (CS1/SS4; TS5; [Reasonable Inference]) The severity score is therefore moderate-low at 55 despite strong structural transferability. (CS1/SS4)

**F3 — Route 1:** Iodine-enhanced transport improves precursor activity and reduces diffusion limitations, as explicitly reported for Pd5InSe. (CS1/SS4) Structural reconstruction is favorable because Pd5InSe and Pd5HgSe share the Pd5XSe P4/mmm motif, but phase control is still sensitive. (CS1/SS4; CS3/SS1/TS1)

**F2 — Route 2:** Direct solid-state synthesis has simpler operations than CVT, but still needs sealed vacuum/inert handling and volatile/toxic element control. (CS5/SS3; CS4; [Reasonable Inference]) If repeated grinding becomes necessary, this route would lose another 10 severity points, consistent with the long multi-step annealing reported for Pd5InAs. (CS4)

**F3 — Route 2:** Bulk diffusion is the main kinetic limitation because related direct solid-state routes use long anneals of 7–10 d to 30–40 d. (CS5/SS3; CS4) The route benefits from related Pd-rich structural chemistry, but CS5 reports low ternary yield/competing binary phases, which lowers the kinetic score. (CS5/SS3)

**F2 — Route 3:** High-frequency melting is fast and avoids high pressure, but target adaptation likely requires controlled Ar/vacuum containment, a melt step, and a post-anneal due to Mg/Se volatility. (CS2; [Reasonable Inference]) SS and TS provide insufficient evidence for this exact high-frequency Mg–Pd–Se route, so those source signals are neutral = 50.

**F3 — Route 3:** The kinetic advantage is rapid alloying, supported by homogeneous MgRuSn4 formation in 15 min under high-frequency melting. (CS2) The limitation is structural specificity, because CS2 is Mg–Ru–Sn rather than Pd–Mg–Se and does not confirm the Pd5XSe P4/mmm topology. (CS2; CS1/SS4)

**F2 — Route 4:** Halide/iodine flux operates at lower temperatures, but uses sealed halogen chemistry and adds flux separation or byproduct-control steps. (TS4; TS5; CS1/SS4) The route therefore has similar condition severity to CVT. (CS1/SS4; TS4)

**F3 — Route 4:** Mineralizers improve precursor activity and mass transport, supported by iodine-assisted Pd5InSe and Pd-rich Be–Pd/Pd–Al phases. (CS1/SS4; TS4; TS5) The main uncertainty is that chloride/halide flux for MgPd5Se is not directly shown in CS/SS/TS, so the route remains partially extrapolated. ([Reasonable Inference])

**F4 — Amorphization:** The target likely has a compact Pd-rich unit cell, which lowers amorphization risk, but mixed polyhedral sharing and Mg–Pd size mismatch increase disorder risk. (CS1/SS4; CS5/SS3; [Reasonable Inference]) CS/SS/TS do not directly provide ionic radii or exact target unit-cell atom count, so those sub-check source signals are neutral where not structurally inferable.

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

MgPd5Se is most likely a DFT ground-state or near-ground-state phase because the target E_hull is 0.0000 eV/atom and the closest CS/SS/TS analogues are also on or near the hull. (Target data; CS1–CS5; SS1–SS5; TS1–TS5) The analogue support is strong structurally because Pd5InSe and Pd5HgSe share the same Pd-rich P4/mmm Pd5XSe family. (CS1/SS4) The most important limiting factor is Mg/Se stoichiometry control under sealed high-temperature conditions, which is [Reasonable Inference] based on Mg-containing intermetallic handling and Se-bearing ampoule routes. (CS2; CS5/SS3; [Reasonable Inference]) Overall, MgPd5Se looks **moderately synthesizeable but experimentally delicate**, with CVT or iodine-assisted sealed ampoule synthesis the best first attempt. (CS1/SS4; TS5)
