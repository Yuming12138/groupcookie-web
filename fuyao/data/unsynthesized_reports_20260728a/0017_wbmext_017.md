### SECTION 7 — Synthesis Challenges

| # | Route | Challenge | Severity | Mitigation Strategy |
|:--:|:--:|---|:--:|---|
| 1 | All | Chloride volatility and Cl stoichiometry loss | High | Use sealed ampoules, slight FeCl₂ excess, and slow cooling; volatility concern is supported by FeBr₂ sublimation and CVT analogues. [TS3, SS3] |
| 2 | All | Fe²⁺ oxidation or hydrolysis | High | Load in glovebox, use evacuated/Ar conditions, and avoid water unless Route 3 is intentionally used; inert/vacuum handling is supported by Fe oxychalcogenide and VOX₂ analogues. [SS2, SS5, CS4, TS2] |
| 3 | Route 1 / Route 2 | Slow solid-state ordering and incomplete reaction | Medium | Use fine grinding, pelletization, flux assistance, and repeated anneals; repeated cycles are reported for related Fe oxychalcogenides. [SS2, SS5] |
| 4 | Route 2 | Flux composition and crystal-growth window | Medium | Use FeCl₂-rich flux, controlled cooling, and post-growth flux separation; chloride flux growth and controlled cooling are supported by TS4. [TS4] |
| 5 | Route 3 | Hydroxide/hydrate byproduct formation | High | Use chloride-rich, deoxygenated, mildly reducing media and consider post-annealing under dry Ar/vacuum; hydrothermal transfer is based on CuOCl but Fe²⁺ solution chemistry is riskier. [CS3, CS4, TS2] |
| 6 | Route 4 | Vapor-transport composition drift | High | Use pre-reacted source powder, low transport-agent loading, and a conservative temperature gradient; SS3 shows oxychloride CVT requires careful gradients and long times. [SS3] |
| 7 | Route 4 | Corrosive transport-agent/ampoule compatibility | Medium | Use dry reagents, small HCl/Cl₂/FeCl₃ loading, and validated sealed-ampoule protocols; SS3 used sealed silica ampoules with HCl/HBr transport agents. [SS3] |
| 8 | All | Competing Fe chloride / iron oxide / oxychloride phases | Medium–High | Use redox-buffered precursor ratios, phase checks after each heat treatment, and regrinding/annealing if impurities remain; related Fe oxychalcogenides required repeated cycles and sometimes showed impurity formation. [SS2, SS4, SS5] |
