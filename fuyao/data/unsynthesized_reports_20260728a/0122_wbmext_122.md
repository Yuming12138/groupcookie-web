### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability**  
SmWN₃ is predicted at E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom, which places it in the highest F1 bracket. (Target) The strongest CS/TS analogues La₃Ta₂N₆ and La₃Nb₂N₆ are also on-hull, supporting thermodynamic plausibility for rare-earth / high-valent transition-metal nitrides. (CS1, CS2, TS1, TS2) The main caution is that structurally similar W₂N₃ is far above hull and requires high pressure, showing that W–N chemistry can be metastability-prone depending on composition. (SS4)

**F2 — Route 1**  
The Li₃N route has moderate-to-high severity because it uses 1030 °C, sealed Ta/Nb ampoules, inert handling, and moisture-sensitive Li₃N. (CS1, CS2) It avoids >1400 °C and high pressure, so it is less severe than Ga flux or multi-anvil routes. (CS1, CS2, SS4)

**F3 — Route 1**  
Li₃N improves precursor activity because it acts as both flux and nitrogen source in close analogues. (CS1, CS2) Kinetics remain limited by W incorporation and structural mismatch between La₃T₂N₆ analogues and monoclinic SmWN₃. (SS2, SS3; [Reasonable Inference])

**F2 — Route 2**  
Ga flux is more severe because the analogue route reaches 1600 °C and requires an Ar-to-N₂ atmosphere sequence. (CS1, CS2) It avoids high pressure but still requires specialized RF/high-temperature equipment. (CS1, CS2)

**F3 — Route 2**  
Ga flux can aid crystal growth, but N₂ is a less reactive nitrogen source than Li₃N for forming a W-rich nitride. (CS1, CS2; [Reasonable Inference]) W dissolution and nitridation are expected to be slower than Nb/Ta analogue formation. (CS1, CS2; [Reasonable Inference])

**F2 — Route 3**  
Direct sintering inherits the 1200–1600 °C and 1 atm N₂ severity of the X₂YN₃ analogue route. (CS4, CS5) It is operationally simpler than flux growth but has high thermal severity and air-sensitive nitride precursor handling. (CS4, CS5; [Reasonable Inference])

Solid-state kinetics are the weakest because dense SmN/W/WNₓ powders must reconstruct into a ternary nitride framework. **F2 — Route 4**

(SS1, TS3, TS4, TS5) Severity remains moderate because sealed/vacuum conditions, precursor preparation, and washing steps are required. (SS1, TS3, TS4, TS5)

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

SmWN₃ is most likely a predicted ground-state or near-ground-state phase because the target E_hull is 0.0000 eV/atom. (Target) The strongest support comes from La₃Ta₂N₆ and La₃Nb₂N₆, which are chemically, structurally, and thermodynamically close flux-grown rare-earth transition-metal nitrides. (CS1, CS2, SS2, SS3, TS1, TS2) The critical enabling factor is a reactive nitride flux such as Li₃N, because it supplies nitrogen activity and liquid-mediated transport at ~1030 °C. (CS1, CS2) The critical limiting factor is W incorporation into the correct SmWN₃ framework, because W-containing structural analogue W₂N₃ requires high-pressure synthesis and is thermodynamically metastable in SS. (SS4; [Reasonable Inference]) Overall, synthesis is plausible but experimentally challenging, with the best first attempt being Li₃N flux rather than direct sintering or high-pressure screening. (CS1, CS2, SS4; [Reasonable Inference])
