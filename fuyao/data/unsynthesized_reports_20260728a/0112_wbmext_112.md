The route requires approximately 1030 °C, sealed ampoules, multiple processing stages, and moisture-sensitive Li₃N, producing deductions for temperature, multistep handling, and air-sensitive precursors. **[CS; SS; TS]** Its severity is lower than a 1600 °C Ga-flux route because the preferred Li₃N analogue operates near 1030 °C. **[CS; SS; TS]** No evidence supports a high-pressure requirement or an additional deduction for precursor volatility. **Insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS; neutral.**

Li₃N provides a chemically active nitrogen source and a flux-assisted transport mechanism, improving precursor activity and diffusion relative to direct solid-state mixing. **[CS; SS; TS]** However, Mo is refractory and the target’s monoclinic MoN₅/PrN₆ framework is not identical to the structures of the analogues, limiting the structural-reconstruction score. **[CS; SS; TS; Reasonable Inference]** The resulting intermediate F3 score is therefore moderate rather than high.
