### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability**  
The target is assigned a very high F1 because its supplied E_hull is 0.0000 eV/atom, and close CS analogues Sm3AlN, Tm3InN, Lu3InN, and Pr3AlN are also reported at E_hull = 0.0. [CS1–CS4; target data] TS analogues are also on-hull, which supports the general synthesizability of nitride phases in this thermodynamic neighborhood. [TS1–TS5] SS gives structural examples but no direct target E_hull, so SS is useful only indirectly and is neutral for exact target thermodynamics where needed. [insufficient exact target thermodynamic evidence from SS = neutral 50]

**F2 — Route 1**  
Route 1 is penalized for sealed/vacuum handling, multi-step pellet/anneal/quench processing, and air-sensitive rare-earth/nitride precursors. [CS1, CS4, CS5] The temperature is not severely penalized because CS routes operate mainly in the 600–900 °C range. [CS1, CS4, CS5] The main uncertainty is elemental P volatility, which is not directly addressed by CS/SS/TS and is therefore an extrapolated risk. [Reasonable Inference; insufficient direct P volatility evidence from CS/SS/TS = neutral 50]

**F3 — Route 1**  
Kinetics are moderate because rare-earth metal/nitride solid-state routes can work but require long anneals up to 800 h in CS examples. [CS1, CS4] Structural reconstruction is favorable because the target topology matches CS Pm-3m anti-perovskites, but P substitution is not directly demonstrated. [CS1–CS4; Reasonable Inference] SS/TS do not provide a direct rare-earth Pm-3m P-site route, so their route-specific kinetic signal is neutral. [insufficient evidence from SS/TS for Route 1 kinetics = neutral 50]

**F2 — Route 2**  
Route 2 has the best severity score because it avoids elemental P during the final anneal if a Yb-rich phosphide precursor is preformed, while using CS-supported Ar/vacuum ampoule handling. [CS2, CS3; Reasonable Inference] It is still penalized for multi-step precursor preparation and air-sensitive RN/binary precursor handling. [CS2, CS3] SS/TS do not materially improve this rare-earth binary-precursor route assessment and are neutral for Route 2 severity. [insufficient evidence from SS/TS for Route 2 severity = neutral 50]

**F3 — Route 2**  
Route 2 has moderate kinetics because RN + R2In analogues require arc fusion plus at least one week at 1220 K. [CS2, CS3] The structural pathway is the strongest among routes because RN + R2M → R3MN maps directly onto YbN + Yb-rich phosphide → Yb3PN. [CS2, CS3; Reasonable Inference] The uncertainty is whether a clean Yb2P-like precursor or equivalent Yb/YbP mixture gives the needed local P activity. [Reasonable Inference]

**F2 — Route 3**  
Route 3 is penalized for N₂ pressure containment, Na flux handling, multi-step slow-cooling growth, and air-sensitive metals. [SS4, SS5, TS4, TS5] It is not penalized for >1000 °C or >5 GPa because SS/TS flux routes use sub-1000 °C temperatures and MPa-scale pressures. [SS4, TS4] CS provides insufficient direct evidence for Na-flux synthesis of rare-earth Pm-3m Yb3PN and is neutral for this route. [insufficient evidence from CS for Route 3 = neutral 50]

**F3 — Route 3**  
Flux growth scores well for ion diffusion because Na flux/melt media and slow cooling support crystal formation in SS/TS nitride analogues. [SS4, SS5, TS4, TS5] Structural reconstruction scores lower because the flux analogues are not cubic rare-earth R3PN anti-perovskites. [SS4, SS5, TS4, TS5] The route is plausible but more exploratory than the CS-derived solid-state routes. [Reasonable Inference]

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

Yb3PN is likely a computational ground-state candidate because the supplied E_hull is 0.0000 eV/atom, and multiple chemically similar rare-earth anti-perovskite nitrides are also reported on-hull. [CS1–CS4; target data] The strongest synthesis support comes from CS, especially R3InN made from RN + R2In and R3AlN made from rare-earth metal + AlN, both in cubic Pm-3m structures. [CS1–CS4] SS/TS support the broader feasibility of nitride synthesis under controlled solid-state or flux conditions, but they are less chemically specific to Yb3PN. [SS1–SS5, TS1–TS5] The key enabling factor is the close structural match to known rare-earth anti-perovskite nitrides. [CS1–CS4] The key limiting factor is the lack of direct P-site analogue evidence and the need to control P activity/volatility during synthesis. [Reasonable Inference; insufficient direct P analogue evidence from CS/SS/TS = neutral 50]
