**Route 1 F2/F3 — Direct Elemental Solid-State**  
Route 1 receives a moderate F2 penalty because dry oxygen-free Ar and air-sensitive/toxic precursor handling are required, but the temperature is below 1000 °C and no pressure is required. (CS-1; SS-1; TS-1) Kinetics are favorable because the closest Na–Al–Sb analogue forms directly from the elements after only 2–3 h at 1100 K. (SS-2; SS-3; SS-4; SS-5; TS-2; TS-3; TS-4; TS-5) Kinetics are still reasonable because analogues form at 627–900 °C, but 20–24 h holds imply slower ordering than Route 1. (SS-2; SS-4; SS-5; TS-2; TS-4; TS-5) This route is attractive if Route 1 loses Na or gives multiphase products. (SS-3; SS-4; TS-3; TS-4)

(CS-1; SS-1; TS-1) Unit-cell atom count and target-specific mixed-sharing topology are not directly established by CS/SS/TS, so those checks are treated as neutral/no confirmed trigger.
