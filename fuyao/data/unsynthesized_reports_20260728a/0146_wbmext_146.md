### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability**  
The target E_hull is reported as 0.0000 eV/atom, so it receives a high thermodynamic score in the ≤0.05 eV/atom bin. (Target data) Multiple closest chemical analogues and all listed structural/thermodynamic analogues are also reported at E_hull = 0.0, except Yb₃SnO at 0.189 eV/atom, which still sits in a moderately accessible range. (CS1–CS5; SS1–SS5; TS1–TS5) The main uncertainty is that the target is theory-only with no experimental synthesis identified. (Target data)

**F2 — Route 1**  
Route 1 is penalized for controlled Ar/vacuum ampoule processing, multi-step binary precursor preparation, and air-sensitive nitride/arsenide handling. (CS1–CS4) It is not penalized for >1000 °C hold temperature because the cited anneal is 1220 K, about 947 °C. (CS1–CS4) The remaining uncertainty is whether Yb₂As behaves as cleanly as R₂In alloys during fusion and annealing. [Reasonable Inference from CS1–CS4]

**F3 — Route 1**  
The same Pm-3m R₃EN framework in CS analogues strongly supports structural reconstruction. (CS1–CS4) Kinetics are reduced by the use of preformed binary solids and one-week anneals, and some analogues require multiple annealing cycles for phase purity. (CS1–CS4) The route is feasible but not kinetically fast. (CS1–CS4)

**F2 — Route 2**  
Route 2 benefits from ambient-pressure N₂ nitridation and sub-900 °C analogue temperatures. (SS2, SS5, TS2, TS5) It is penalized for controlled N₂ atmosphere, Yb/As air sensitivity, and As volatility/toxicity. (SS2, SS5, TS2, TS5; target safety note; [Reasonable Inference]) This route is less severe than Route 1 because the analogues use only melt-bead formation plus nitridation rather than week-long binary precursor annealing. (SS2, SS5, TS2, TS5)

**F3 — Route 2**  
Sr₃SbN and Sr₃BiN are the strongest kinetic analogues because they form single-phase cubic Pm-3m A₃EN powders directly from A₃E melt beads under N₂. (SS2, SS5, TS2, TS5) The melt-bead pathway should reduce diffusion limitations relative to dry solid-solid reaction. (SS2, SS5, TS2, TS5; [Reasonable Inference]) The main uncertainty is whether Yb₃AsN has the same nitridation window as Sr/Ba Sb/Bi nitrides. [Reasonable Inference from SS2, SS5, TS2, TS5]

**F2 — Route 3**  
Route 3 avoids >1000 °C temperatures but requires N₂ pressure, Na flux, glovebox loading, slow cooling, and flux removal. (SS4, TS4) It is not a >5 GPa route because the cited pressure is 7 MPa. (SS4, TS4) Severity remains challenging because Na and As introduce air-sensitivity and safety constraints. (SS4, TS4; target safety note; [Reasonable Inference])

**F3 — Route 3**  
Na flux and pressurized N₂ improve precursor activity and ion transport. (SS4, TS4) The structural-transfer score is lower because the flux analogue is orthorhombic and quaternary, not cubic ternary Yb₃AsN. (SS4, TS4) This route is attractive for crystal growth but less certain for phase-selective bulk synthesis. (SS4, TS4; [Reasonable Inference])

**F4 — Amorphization Tendency**  
The target-like Pm-3m analogues are simple 3D frameworks with corner/face sharing and no edge-sharing coexistence, so the structural amorphization checks are not triggered. (CS1–CS4; SS2, SS5; TS2, TS5) The one-cation Yb framework avoids mixed-cation radius-disorder risk. [Reasonable Inference from target structure; CS1–CS5] Direct amorphization data are absent, so experimental amorphization evidence from CS/SS/TS is insufficient and treated neutral where direct evidence would be needed.

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

Yb₃AsN is likely a ground-state or near-ground-state phase because the target E_hull is reported as 0.0000 eV/atom and several chemically/structurally related nitrides are also hull-stable. (Target data; CS1–CS4; SS1–SS5; TS1–TS5) The synthesis case is strongly supported by two analogue families: rare-earth R₃InN phases made by RN + R₂In annealing, and alkaline-earth A₃Sb/BiN phases made by A₃E melt-bead nitridation. (CS1–CS4; SS2, SS5; TS2, TS5) The most critical enabling factor is the robust cubic Pm-3m A₃EN/R₃EN framework across multiple cations and p-block elements. (CS1–CS4; SS2, SS5; TS2, TS5) The most critical limiting factor is maintaining arsenic stoichiometry and safety during high-temperature nitridation. (target safety note; [Reasonable Inference]) The evidence conflict is mild: CS analogues support rare-earth chemistry but use In, while SS/TS analogues support pnictide nitride chemistry but use Sr/Ba; Route 2 is preferred because it most directly matches the A₃pnictide nitride framework. (CS1–CS4; SS2, SS5; TS2, TS5)
