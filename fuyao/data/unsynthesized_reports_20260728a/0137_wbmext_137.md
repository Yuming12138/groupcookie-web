### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability**  
The target’s supplied hull value is 0.0000 eV/atom, and all closest CS/SS/TS entries also report E_hull = 0.0, so the thermodynamic score is near the top of the rubric [Target record; CS1–CS5; SS1–SS5; TS1–TS5]. The remaining uncertainty is experimental, not thermodynamic: CS/SS/TS provide insufficient direct CeNF synthesis proof, so direct experimental precedent is neutral 50.

**F2 — Route 1**  
Route 1 avoids >1000°C and high pressure, which keeps severity moderate [SS3, SS4, TS3, TS4]. The main deductions are NH₃ as a reactive/toxic atmosphere and moisture-sensitive fluoride handling [SS3, SS4, TS3, TS4; Reasonable Inference].

**F3 — Route 1**  
Gas-solid ammonolysis should provide higher nitrogen activity than dense solid-state N diffusion, and ThF₄ → ThNF is a direct synthetic precedent [SS3, SS4, TS3, TS4]. Kinetic uncertainty comes from transferring Th⁴⁺ nitride-fluoride chemistry to Ce and from the target’s fluorite-derived R-3m structure rather than the Matlockite structures listed for ThNCl/ThNBr [Target record; SS3, SS4, TS3, TS4].

**F2 — Route 2**  
Route 2 uses accessible 750–950°C temperatures consistent with CS solid-state fluoride analogues, but it requires sealed tubes, glove-box handling, and multi-step grinding/annealing [CS1–CS5]. It is penalized for air-sensitive nitride/fluoride precursors and sealed containment needed to preserve F/N stoichiometry [CS2–CS5; Reasonable Inference].

**F3 — Route 2**  
Solid-state Ce₃N₄/CeF₄ diffusion is slower than ammonolysis or flux growth, and CS analogues show repeated grinding/heating or long anneals can be needed [CS1–CS5]. The balanced reaction Ce₃N₄ + CeF₄ → 4 CeNF is chemically attractive, but no CS/SS/TS source demonstrates this exact Ce–N–F ordering, so direct evidence is neutral 50.

**F2 — Route 3**  
Route 3 stays near 900°C but is penalized for sealed Nb/Ta ampoules, azide hazards, controlled atmosphere, and multi-step slow cooling [SS2, TS2]. CS provides insufficient flux/azide signal and is neutral 50 for this route.

**F3 — Route 3**  
Flux growth scores well kinetically because SS2/TS2 demonstrates Ce–N crystallization from RbN₃/Ce/CeCl₃ at 900°C with slow cooling [SS2, TS2]. The penalty is structural/compositional transfer: the analogue contains Se and Cl, while CeNF requires F as the second anion [SS2, TS2; Reasonable Inference].

**F2 — Route 4**  
Route 4 is the harshest because SS5/TS5 uses 1500–1700°C under N₂, triggering both high-temperature penalties [SS5, TS5]. Adapting this to a fluoride requires additional sealed containment or F-buffering, which increases severity [CS1–CS5; Reasonable Inference].

**F3 — Route 4**  
High temperature helps diffusion, but the route relies on refractory solid-state chemistry and lacks direct nitride-fluoride precedent [SS5, TS5]. Fluorine volatility and competing CeN/CeF₃ formation are expected limiting factors [Reasonable Inference; CS1–CS5].

**F4 — Amorphization Tendency**  
The target has only one cation, so cation-radius mismatch does not trigger C1 [Target record; CS1–CS5]. The target description shows corner- and edge-sharing tetrahedra but no face-sharing, so C3 does not trigger; CS/SS/TS do not give exact target unit-cell atom count, so that source signal is neutral 50 for C2 [Target record; insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS].
