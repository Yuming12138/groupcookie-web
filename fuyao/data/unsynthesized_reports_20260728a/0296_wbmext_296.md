(CS-1, CS-3, CS-5; SS-1, SS-2, SS-3; TS-1, TS-2, TS-3) The 60/100 score reflects moderate severity rather than exotic synthesis because standard furnaces, corundum/alumina crucibles, and silica/quartz ampoules are sufficient. (CS-3, CS-5; SS-1, SS-2, SS-3; TS-1, TS-2, TS-3)

YIrP is likely a thermodynamic ground-state or near-ground-state phase because the supplied target E_hull is 0.0000 eV/atom and closely related CS/SS/TS analogues are also at or near the hull. (CS-1–CS-5; SS-1–SS-5; TS-1–TS-5) The strongest synthesis support comes from same-prototype LaIrP, PrIrP, and NdIrP made by solid-state sintering, plus chemically close YPtP made by direct elemental reaction. (CS-1, CS-3, CS-5; SS-1, SS-2, SS-3; TS-1, TS-2, TS-3) The key enabling factor is structural and thermodynamic continuity across REIrP/REPtP omega-derived phases.
