### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability**  
KYSe₂ is scored near the top of the ≤0.05 eV/atom bin because the target input reports E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom. The CS/SS/TS analogues also repeatedly report E_hull = 0.0 eV/atom for Caswellsilverite or closely related K/Rb rare-earth selenides, which reinforces the ground-state likelihood. Remaining uncertainty is that the target metadata says KYSe₂ is theory-only and not experimentally validated. (CS; SS; TS; target metadata)

**F2 — Route 1 Flux Growth**  
The flux route avoids >1000 °C operation because RbLnSe₂ analogues use 1173 K ≈ 900 °C, but it requires Ar/vacuum sealed-tube handling, a volatile selenium-containing flux, and multiple preparation/workup steps. Those conditions justify a moderate severity score of 55 rather than a high score. The main uncertainty is whether K₂Seₓ/KI behaves as cleanly as Rb₂Se₃/RbI for the smaller K analogue. (CS; SS; TS; [Reasonable Inference])

**F3 — Route 1 Flux Growth**  
Flux growth receives the best kinetics score because RbLnSe₂ analogues form the same Caswellsilverite topology across many rare-earth cations, and KSmSe₂ is nearly structurally identical to the target. The liquid/reactive flux should improve mass transport despite refractory Y. The uncertainty is the unreported exact KYSe₂ flux window. (CS; SS; TS; [Reasonable Inference])

**F2 — Route 2 Direct Solid-State**  
The direct sealed-ampoule route has similar safety severity to Route 1 because it still requires inert handling, sealed containment, and toxic/volatile selenium chemistry. It avoids the flux workup but likely requires binary precursor preparation and possibly regrinding, so it remains moderate–challenging. SS/TS support closed-system alkali rare-earth chalcogenide synthesis but do not provide a complete optimized KYSe₂ protocol. (SS; TS; [Reasonable Inference])

**F3 — Route 2 Direct Solid-State**  
Kinetics are weaker than flux because binary selenides must interdiffuse and order without a strong liquid transport medium. KSmSe₂ strongly supports the target structure, but SS/TS note phase-control challenges and difficult pure phases for related solid-state products. This lowers the structural-reconstruction and diffusion components relative to Route 1. (SS; TS)

**F2 — Route 3 Gas-Flow Chalcogenization**  
CS supports gas-flow chalcogenization through KYS₂ in H₂S and K/Rb manganese selenides in H₂/chalcogen streams. The route is penalized for >1000 °C KYS₂ conditions, reactive gas atmosphere, multiple gas-handling operations, and toxic chalcogen species. SS/TS provide insufficient direct gas-flow selenization evidence for KYSe₂, so their route-condition signal is neutral at 50. (CS; SS; TS)

**F3 — Route 3 Gas-Flow Chalcogenization**  
Kinetics are least favorable because K₂CO₃/Y₂O₃ must undergo full oxide/carbonate-to-selenide reconstruction. KYS₂ supports the K–Y chalcogenide structure, but S→Se chemistry and Y₂O₃ conversion are extrapolations. The main uncertainty is whether the desired Caswellsilverite phase forms before competing Y–Se, K–Se, or oxide/oxyselenide phases. (CS; [Reasonable Inference])

**F4 — Amorphization Tendency**  
Only the cation-radius mismatch check is triggered, because K⁺ and Y³⁺ differ substantially in size. The small R-3m Caswellsilverite analogue family and the absence of face-sharing connectivity reduce amorphization concern. Explicit unit-cell atom counts are insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS, so that sub-signal is treated neutrally, but the repeated ABX₂ prototype descriptions support low structural complexity. (CS; SS; TS; [Reasonable Inference])
