## F2: Route 1

Route 1 receives a moderate-to-low condition-severity score because the temperature is modest and the reported pressure is far below the rubric’s \(>5\) GPa threshold. **[CS: mp-559161; SS: mp-557519]** The major deductions arise from elemental fluorine, sealed high-pressure equipment, and toxic cadmium-containing precursors. **[CS: mp-559161; SS: mp-557519; Target safety note]** The route is operationally difficult despite being thermodynamically favorable. **[CS: mp-559161; Target validation data]**

## F3: Route 1

The oxide/carbonate route is assigned the highest kinetic score because oxide/carbonate fluorination is directly demonstrated for chemically related \(M\)CrF\(_6\) compounds. **[CS: mp-559161; SS: mp-557519; TS: mp-554955]** High fluorine chemical potential and pressure can improve fluorination and transport at moderate temperature, although that enhancement is an inference rather than direct evidence for VCdF\(_6\). **[Reasonable Inference]** The remaining concern is the substantial structural and chemical reconstruction required to convert oxide/carbonate precursors into the ordered corner-sharing fluoride framework. **[Target structure; Reasonable Inference]**

## F2: Route 2

Route 2 avoids extreme pressure and uses temperatures comparable to successful NbFeF\(_6\) and CuMoF\(_6\) sealed-tube reactions. **[CS: mp-541138; CS: mp-19859]** It is penalized for requiring volatile fluoride containment, specialized VF\(_4\), and likely multistep precursor preparation. **[SS/TS: mp-554799; CS: mp-19859]** Ar or N\(_2\) is less chemically aggressive than F\(_2\), but the route remains sensitive to moisture, oxygen, and fluoride loss. **[CS: mp-541138; CS: mp-19859; SS/TS: mp-554799]**
