### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability**  
The target E_hull is 0.0000 eV/atom, which maps to the 90–100 band, and TS1–TS5 all report E_hull = 0.0 for thermodynamically similar oxides. SS analogues are also near the hull or on the hull, including SS1–SS5 with E_hull values near 0.0, supporting strong thermodynamic plausibility. **F2/F3 — Route 1**  
Solid-state firing is experimentally supported by SS2, SS5, and TS2, but those analogues require high temperatures, repeated grinding, and long dwell times. The main kinetic penalty is HfO2’s refractory nature and the absence of CS/SS/TS evidence that simple annealing selects the beta-VN-like HfO2 structure. CS gives insufficient direct ambient solid-state evidence and is neutral = 50.

**F2/F3 — Route 2**  
Flux synthesis is supported by SS3 and TS1/TS4/TS5, where NaCl flux at 1000 °C for 13 days yields phase-pure oxide products. Kinetics improve relative to dry solid-state synthesis because flux-assisted transport can reduce diffusion limitations [Reasonable Inference], but CS/SS/TS do not prove that flux stabilizes this HfO2 polymorph. CS gives insufficient flux-specific evidence and is neutral = 50.

**F2/F3 — Route 3**  
High-pressure synthesis is strongly supported by CS1, CS2, and SS4 for dense oxide polymorphs requiring 6–17 GPa and rapid quench. This route has good structural transferability because CS2 contains MO6 octahedra and SS1 contains HfO6 octahedra with Hf–O distances close to the target. The route loses severity score because GPa pressure, quenching, and specialized equipment are explicitly required in CS1/CS2/SS4; TS gives insufficient high-pressure evidence and is neutral = 50.

**F2/F3 — Route 4**  
Thin-film sputtering has the best combined severity/kinetics score because CS3 demonstrates crystalline oxide film growth at 600 °C using an oxide target and O2/Ar, avoiding bulk diffusion. The key extrapolation is that HfO2 could be deposited from an HfO2 target and polymorph-selected by substrate/oxygen pressure [Reasonable Inference]. SS and TS give insufficient thin-film evidence and are neutral = 50, which is the main uncertainty.

**F2/F3 — Route 5**  
Hydrothermal/co-precipitation has favorable precursor activity because CS5 uses nitrate/PEG/NaOH hydrothermal chemistry and SS1 uses hydroxide co-precipitation followed by heat treatment. The route is mild and accessible, but the structural reconstruction score is limited because CS/SS/TS do not show the beta-VN-like HfO2 phase forming from a hydrated precursor. TS3 supports co-precipitated precursor heat treatment but not the exact target composition.

**F4 — Amorphization Tendency**

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

The target is most likely a DFT ground-state or near-ground-state phase because its reported E_hull is 0.0000 eV/atom and the closest thermodynamic analogues TS1–TS5 are also on the hull. The CS/SS/TS evidence supports oxide synthesis broadly, with especially relevant Hf–O structural evidence from SS1 and dense octahedral oxide evidence from CS2. If thin films are acceptable, sputtering/PLD is the best first route based on CS3; if bulk powder is required, flux-assisted growth or high-pressure quench should be prioritized as follow-up routes based on TS1/TS4/TS5 and CS1/CS2/SS4.
