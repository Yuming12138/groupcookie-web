The key enabling factor is the very close structural match to K2TeO3, including trigonal non-coplanar p-block oxide coordination and face-sharing alkali–oxygen polyhedra (**CS4 / SS2 / TS2**, **Target**). The key limiting factor is selenium chemistry: SeO2 / selenite species are toxic, potentially volatile, and sensitive to redox or hydration conditions (**SS1 / TS1**, **CS3**, **Target**). Mild aqueous routes are attractive for formula-level synthesis, but they are less reliable for accessing the specific anhydrous Krennerite-derived polymorph (**CS1 / CS2**, **Target**).
