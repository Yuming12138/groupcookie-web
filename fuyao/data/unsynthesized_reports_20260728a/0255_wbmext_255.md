### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability.**  
The target is reported at E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom, which places it in the highest thermodynamic-stability bin; closely related CS and TS entries are also mostly 0.0 eV/atom or near-zero, including ZrPdAs, ZrPdSb, ZrPdGe, ZrRhP, HfRuAs, and ZrRuP. **(CS1, CS2, CS3, SS1, TS1, TS2, TS4, TS5)** The main uncertainty is that the target is theory-only in the supplied data, so the score is 96 rather than 100. **(CS, SS, TS)**

**F2 — Route 1.**  
Solid-state synthesis uses standard sealed ampoule and furnace conditions, matching ZrPdAs/ZrPdSb procedures, so there is no high-pressure or exotic-equipment penalty. **(CS1, CS2)** Deductions come from >1000 °C annealing, multi-step grinding/annealing, and toxic/volatile As requiring sealed containment. **(CS1, CS2, SS4; [Reasonable Inference])**

**F3 — Route 1.**  
The structural-reconstruction burden is favorable because Pnma ZrPdSb and ZrRhP share very similar coordination environments with the target. **(CS2, SS1, TS1)** Kinetics are only moderate because elemental Zr/Rh are refractory and the analogue syntheses require long annealing and grinding, indicating slow diffusion. **(CS1, CS2; [Reasonable Inference])**

**F2 — Route 2.**  
Arc/induction melting improves mixing but carries severe-condition penalties because refractory-metal melting requires very high local temperatures and controlled Ar/vacuum conditions. **(SS1, CS3)** Additional deductions arise from the need to add volatile/toxic As in a sealed anneal after master-alloying. **(CS1, SS4; [Reasonable Inference])**

**F3 — Route 2.**  
Kinetics are better than Route 1 because master-alloying reduces the Zr/Rh diffusion mismatch before arsenidation. **(SS1, CS3; [Reasonable Inference])** The structural target remains plausible because ZrRhP is a direct Pnma Zr–Rh–pnictide analogue, but post-annealing is still required to order the ternary phase. **(SS1, TS1)**

**F2 — Route 3.**  
Flux growth avoids high-pressure synthesis and can operate near the same high-temperature window as related pnictide flux methods, but it requires sealed capsules, flux handling, and post-growth acid dissolution. **(CS4, SS2, SS3, TS5)** Exact ZrRhAs flux time/temperature are not supplied, so those settings are inferred and carry uncertainty. **Insufficient exact-condition evidence from CS/SS/TS; [Reasonable Inference]**

**F3 — Route 3.**  
Flux should improve mass transport and crystal growth relative to powder sintering, consistent with reported Cu-flux pnictide syntheses. **(CS4, SS2, SS3, TS5)** The limitation is indirect transferability: most flux analogues involve Ru/P or Hf/Ru/As rather than exact Zr/Rh/As, and related systems show phase-competition risk. **(SS2, SS3, TS5)**

**F4 — Amorphization Tendency.**  
The target is a relatively small Pnma intermetallic/pnictide cell by analogy to Zr4P4Rh4 and Zr4Sb4Pd4, so the >40-atom-cell amorphization trigger is not met. **(SS1, TS1, CS2)** One size-mismatch trigger is assigned from Zr/Rh metallic-size contrast, but CS/SS/TS do not provide ionic radii directly, so this is an explicitly labeled inference; mixed polyhedral sharing is not directly established for the target Pnma structure. **Insufficient direct C1/C3 evidence from CS/SS/TS; [Reasonable Inference]**

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

ZrRhAs is likely a thermodynamic ground-state or very near-ground-state phase because its reported E_hull is 0.0000 eV/atom and close thermodynamic analogues are also on or near the hull. **(TS1, TS2, TS4, TS5)** The analogue support is strong for bulk synthesis: chemically similar ZrPdAs/ZrPdSb are synthesized by sealed-tube solid-state methods, and structurally similar ZrRhP adopts the same Pnma motif. **(CS1, CS2, SS1, TS1)** The key enabling factor is the existence of multiple experimentally realized Zr/Hf–late-transition-metal–pnictide frameworks with similar coordination and low hull energies. **(CS1, CS2, SS1, TS1, TS2)** The key limiting factor is practical rather than thermodynamic: arsenic volatility/toxicity plus slow refractory-metal diffusion require sealed ampoules, careful stoichiometry, and long annealing. **(CS1, CS2, SS4; [Reasonable Inference])** Overall, ZrRhAs should be experimentally accessible, with Route 1 being the most defensible first attempt. **(CS1, CS2, SS1, TS1)**
