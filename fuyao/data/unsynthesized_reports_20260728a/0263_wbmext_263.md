### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

MgPt is likely a ground-state or near-ground-state phase because the target E_hull is 0.0000 eV/atom, and the CS/SS/TS nearest analogues are also reported at E_hull = 0.0 eV/atom (Target data; CS; SS; TS). The strongest synthesis support comes from sealed-ampoule Mg–Pt analogues such as Mg₂Pt and Mg₈Pt₄B, which use Mg chips, Pt powder, Ar atmosphere, Nb ampoules, and 650–1000°C annealing (CS-2; SS-3/4; TS-3/4). The most important enabling factor is the simple B2/Tetraauricupride-like Pm-3m connectivity with short-range Mg–Pt bonding similar to existing Mg–Pt compounds (Target data; CS-2; SS-3/4; TS-3/4). The main limiting factor is stoichiometric control of volatile Mg against competing Mg–Pt phases, especially because the provided dataset does not identify an experimental MgPt synthesis (Target data; CS-2; SS-3/4; TS-3/4). Overall, MgPt is a credible bulk synthesis target, with Route 1 being the most defensible first attempt (CS-2; SS-3/4; TS-3/4).
