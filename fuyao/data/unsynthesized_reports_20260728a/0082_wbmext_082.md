Sr₃BN₃ is most likely a ground-state or very near-ground-state phase because the target E_hull is 0.0000 eV/atom and the WBM hull-improvement signal is favorable. [Target] The synthesis case is strong because SS/TS analogues directly support Sr–B–N frameworks with linear B–N units and high thermodynamic stability. [SS1, SS2, TS1, TS2] CS analogues further show that Sr nitrides can be made by both flux and direct high-temperature nitride routes. [CS1, CS2, CS4, CS5] The key enabling factor is the simple stoichiometric precursor mapping Sr₃N₂ + BN → Sr₃BN₃, which preserves Sr–N and B–N chemistry. [Target, SS1, TS1; Reasonable Inference] The main limiting factor is kinetic/phase control: BN is refractory, Sr nitrides are air-sensitive, and alkali flux routes may preferentially form Na/Li-containing competing boron nitrides. [SS1, SS2, SS3, TS1, TS2, TS3; Reasonable Inference]

### SECTION 7 — Synthesis Challenges

| # | Route | Challenge | Severity | Mitigation Strategy (from CS/SS/TS) |
|:--:|:--:|:--|:--:|:--|
| 1 | All | Air/moisture sensitivity of Sr nitride precursors | High | Handle and grind in Ar glovebox; use sealed Nb/Ta ampoules or controlled N₂/Ar systems. [SS1, TS1, CS4, CS5] |
| 2 | Route 1 / 3 / 4 | Slow reaction of refractory BN | Medium | Use fine h-BN powder, intimate grinding/pelletizing, multi-day anneals, and optional regrind/reanneal. [SS1, TS1; Reasonable Inference] |
| 3 | Route 2 | Na volatility and NaN₃ hazard | High | Use welded Ta/Nb/steel ampoules, secondary evacuated tubes, conservative fill volumes, and controlled vacuum Na removal. [CS1, CS2, SS3, TS3] |
| 4 | Route 2 | Competing Na/Li incorporation into stable alkali Sr–B–N phases | High | Avoid Li, minimize alkali activity where possible, and prioritize alkali-free Route 1 for phase-pure bulk synthesis. [SS1, SS2, TS1, TS2; Reasonable Inference] |
| 5 | All | Competing phase formation from Sr nitride, BN, or alkaline-earth boron nitride side phases | Medium | Maintain exact stoichiometry, use long anneals, and check products after staged heating before increasing temperature. [SS1, SS5, TS1, TS5; Reasonable Inference] |
| 6 | Route 3 | Insufficient B nitridation or incomplete formation of linear B–N units | Medium–High | Prefer preformed BN over elemental B for first trials, or switch to Route 1 if N₂-flow reactions leave B-rich residues. [SS1, TS1, CS4; Reasonable Inference] |
| 7 | Route 4 | Narrow phase window and impurity risk under pressure | High | Use HP only as fallback; start from the ambient sealed-ampoule route, then vary pressure/temperature in small increments. [SS4, TS4; Reasonable Inference] |
| 8 | Route 2 | Crystal intergrowth or unidentified byproducts during flux growth | Medium | Use slow cooling profiles and post-growth flux removal protocols similar to Sr nitride flux examples. [CS1, CS2] |
