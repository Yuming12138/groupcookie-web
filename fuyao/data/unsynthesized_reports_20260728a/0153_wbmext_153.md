### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability.** The target is reported at E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom, which places it in the highest thermodynamic-stability bracket (Target). CS and TS analogues are overwhelmingly at E_hull = 0, while SS analogues are either at the hull or close to it, supporting that similar complex fluorides are thermodynamically accessible (CS, SS, TS). The remaining uncertainty is that the target is a theory-only WBM candidate rather than an experimentally validated phase (Target).

**F2 — Route 1.** Route 1 has mild temperature, ambient pressure, and simple evaporation equipment, directly supported by CsReF6 and Cs/Pa fluoride solution crystallizations (CS2, CS4, TS5). The main severity penalty is concentrated HF toxicity, while SS gives insufficient evidence for an aqueous ABF6 route and is treated as neutral 50 for this route-specific signal.

**F2 — Route 2.** Route 2 is penalized for inert/sealed handling, volatile fluoride containment, multi-step ampoule work, and hazardous fluoride precursors [Reasonable Inference]. These penalties are supported by sealed Pt/Ni tube fluoride analogues, long anneals, Ar/vacuum conditions, and phase-control issues (CS1, CS5, SS3, SS5, TS4).

**F2 — Route 3.** Route 3 shares the sealed/volatile/hazardous fluoride penalties of Route 2 because flux growth still requires sealed fluoride containment [Reasonable Inference]. Slow-cooling crystal-growth analogues support the method, but exact CsF-rich flux evidence for CsTaF6 is insufficient from CS/SS/TS and is neutral 50 for flux-composition specificity (CS5, TS4).

**F3 — Route 1.** Route 1 scores highest kinetically because dissolved fluoride complexes avoid bulk solid diffusion [Reasonable Inference], and CsReF6 shows the exact R-3 topology can crystallize from solution (CS2). The main uncertainty is Ta(V) speciation and avoidance of hydrolysis/oxyfluoride impurities during evaporation [Reasonable Inference].

**F3 — Route 2.** Route 2 has moderate precursor activity because binary fluorides work in several analogues, but long annealing and sealed-tube handling indicate slower ordering kinetics (CS1, SS3, SS5, TS4). High-melting Ta creates a diffusion concern by rubric logic [Reasonable Inference], partly offset by fluoride analogues forming well below Zr/Hf cation melting temperatures (CS1, SS3, SS5, TS4).

**F3 — Route 3.** Route 3 improves kinetics by using flux/liquid transport and slow cooling [Reasonable Inference], consistent with fluoride crystal-growth analogues (CS5, TS4). The uncertainty is composition control because no CS/SS/TS source directly reports a CsF-rich CsTaF6 flux window, so that sub-signal is neutral 50.

**F4 — Amorphization Tendency.** The target triggers mixed-connectivity risk because corner-, edge-, and face-sharing polyhedra coexist, and this is mirrored by the exact CsReF6 analogue (Target, CS2). The large Cs/Ta cation-size contrast triggers the ionic-radius check [Reasonable Inference], while the >40-atom cell check is not indicated by the simple ABF6 R-3 description (Target). The main uncertainty is that CS/SS/TS do not provide the exact target unit-cell atom count, so that source-specific signal is neutral 50.

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

CsTaF6 is likely a ground-state or near-ground-state phase because the target E_hull is 0.0000 eV/atom and many similar fluorides in CS/SS/TS are also at or near the hull (Target, CS, SS, TS). The strongest synthesis support is the exact CsReF6 analogue, which shares the R-3 CsF12/MF6 framework and was obtained by slow evaporation from acid solution (CS2). The most critical enabling factor is stabilization of isolated TaF6-based octahedral chemistry in a Cs-containing fluoride lattice [Reasonable Inference] supported by the CsReF6 structural analogue (CS2). The most critical limiting factor is fluoride chemistry control: concentrated HF, hydrolysis avoidance, volatile TaF5 risk, and competing fluoride/oxyfluoride phases remain credible concerns [Reasonable Inference] (CS1, CS4, TS5). Overall, the material appears synthetically plausible, with solution crystallization clearly preferred over dry solid-state synthesis (CS2, CS4, TS5).
