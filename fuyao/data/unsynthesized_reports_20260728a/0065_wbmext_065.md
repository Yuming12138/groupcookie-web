### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability**  
The target’s reported hull distance is **0.0000 eV/atom**, so it receives a near-maximum thermodynamic score. (Target data) The strongest analogue support comes from KNO₂ and CsNO₂, which are chemically, structurally, and thermodynamically close and have E_hull values at or near zero. (CS1/SS1/TS1; CS2/SS2/TS2) Remaining uncertainty is experimental rather than thermodynamic because the WBM record is theory-only and does not identify a synthesis. (Target data)

**F2/F3 — Route 1**  
Route 1 has essentially no condition-severity penalties because the KNO₂ analogue was grown by room-temperature aqueous evaporation in a desiccator without high pressure, high temperature, or special atmosphere. (CS1/SS1/TS1) Kinetics are strong because solution ions bypass solid-state diffusion and KNO₂ already has the same **Cm** structural motif as the target. (CS1/SS1/TS1) The main uncertainty is slow crystal growth and moisture sensitivity, since KNO₂ required many months and careful handling. (CS1/SS1/TS1)

**F2/F3 — Route 2**  
Route 2 is slightly more severe because the CsNO₂-like metathesis route includes multiple operations such as double decomposition, filtration/decanting, evaporation, recrystallization, and desiccator drying. (CS2/SS2/TS2) Kinetics remain favorable because the key chemistry occurs in aqueous solution and produces an alkali nitrite directly. (CS2/SS2/TS2) The main uncertainty is impurity control from Ba²⁺/SO₄²⁻ or barium nitrite impurities, which the Cs/Tl nitrite evidence indicates can matter. (CS2/SS2/TS2)

**F2/F3 — Route 3**  
Route 3 remains mild because low-temperature or room-temperature slow cooling/evaporation is repeatedly successful for alkali nitrite/nitrate/sulfate crystals. (CS2/SS2/TS2; TS5; CS5) Kinetics are favorable because crystallization occurs from solution, but phase and crystal-size control may require careful supersaturation management. (CS2/SS2/TS2; TS5) This route is best viewed as an optimization variant of Route 1 rather than a fundamentally different chemistry. [Reasonable Inference] (CS1/SS1/TS1; CS2/SS2/TS2)

Route 4 receives lower scores because its support is indirect: RbAsO₂/KAsO₂ show alkali oxosalts can be made at 500 °C under Ar, and K₃NO₃ shows dry nitrate chemistry can require strict moisture/CO₂ exclusion. (CS3; CS4; TS3) There is insufficient evidence from SS for direct solid-state RbNO₂ synthesis, so the SS signal is neutral, 50, for this route. (insufficient evidence from SS) The main uncertainty is whether nitrate/nitrite redox or dry metathesis can produce phase-pure RbNO₂ without decomposition or competing oxides/nitrates. [Reasonable Inference] (TS3)

**F4 — Amorphization Tendency**  
The target is a simple single-alkali nitrite and the closest analogues KNO₂/CsNO₂ are crystalline salts, so amorphization risk is low.
