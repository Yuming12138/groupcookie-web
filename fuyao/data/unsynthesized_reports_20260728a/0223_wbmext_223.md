### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability**  
The target E_hull is 0.0000 eV/atom, which places it in the highest stability bin by the required rubric (Target data). Multiple close analogues are also hull-stable, including LiYSe₂, LiScS₂, LiHoSe₂, LiTbSe₂, LiGdSe₂, and NaErSe₂, supporting that this chemistry/prototype family contains experimentally accessible low-energy phases (CS1–CS3; SS1–SS5; TS1–TS5). Remaining uncertainty is that the target is identified as a theory-only WBM candidate, so thermodynamic stability does not by itself prove experimental synthesis (Target data).

**F2 — Route 1**  
Route 1 avoids >1000 °C and high pressure, but it requires Ar/vacuum sealed-tube handling, volatile/toxic Se control, air-sensitive Li handling, and multiple long annealing/grinding steps (CS1; SS1–SS4; TS1–TS4; [Reasonable Inference]). These penalties give F2 = 55 despite the route being standard solid-state chemistry rather than exotic synthesis (CS1; SS2–SS4; TS2–TS4).

**F3 — Route 1**  
The same Caswellsilverite LiMSe₂ structure strongly supports structural reconstruction, but elemental Sc diffusion is expected to be slower than alkali/Se transport **[Reasonable Inference]** (CS1; SS1–SS4; TS1–TS4). Long anneals and regrinding in analogues justify a moderate kinetics score rather than a high one (CS1; SS2–SS4; TS2–TS4).

**F2 — Route 2**  
Route 2 keeps the recommended reaction at 900–1000 °C by combining LiScS₂ cation-transfer evidence with lower-temperature selenide ampoule analogues **[Reasonable Inference]** (CS1–CS3). It still receives deductions for sealed/vacuum conditions, volatile Se/selenide management, multi-step precursor and annealing work, and air-sensitive/toxic selenide precursors (CS2; CS3; [Reasonable Inference]).

**F3 — Route 2**  
Binary selenides should be more chemically activated toward LiScSe₂ than elemental Sc/Se mixtures **[Reasonable Inference]**, and exact LiScS₂ synthesis from Sc₂S₃/Li₂S is the strongest transferable evidence (CS2). The target and analogues share the Caswellsilverite octahedral framework, so reconstruction is favorable, while Sc transport and ordering still require controlled high-temperature treatment (CS1; CS2).

**F2 — Route 3**  
Flux growth benefits from a lower 850–900 °C temperature window, but it still requires sealed Nb/Ta ampoules, dry halide/selenide handling, volatile Se containment, and multi-step growth/flux-removal operations (SS5; TS5; [Reasonable Inference]). CS provides insufficient route-specific flux evidence and is neutral 50 for this factor.

**F3 — Route 3**  
A halide flux can improve transport and crystallization kinetics **[Reasonable Inference]**, and NaErSe₂ confirms that Caswellsilverite selenides can form under sealed flux conditions (SS5; TS5). The route is less direct than Route 2 because Na/Er/NaCl chemistry must be extrapolated to Li/Sc/Li-halide chemistry, and NaErSe₂ formation as an unintended product signals competing-phase risk (SS5; TS5).

**F4 — Amorphization Tendency**  
The Caswellsilverite framework is simple, ordered, and octahedral, with analogues showing corner/edge-sharing but not face-sharing polyhedral coexistence (CS1; CS2; SS1–SS5; TS1–TS5). The Li–Sc cation size mismatch is not expected to exceed the >15% trigger **[Reasonable Inference]**, but exact ionic radii are not supplied by CS/SS/TS, so that sub-evidence is neutral 50. Overall amorphization risk is low, giving F4 = 95.

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

LiScSe₂ is most likely a ground-state or near-ground-state phase because the target E_hull is 0.0000 eV/atom and close Caswellsilverite analogues are also hull-stable (Target data; CS1–CS3; SS1–SS5; TS1–TS5). The analogue support is strong structurally because LiYSe₂/LiLnSe₂ and LiScS₂ share the same R-3m Caswellsilverite octahedral framework with the target (CS1; CS2; SS1–SS4; TS1–TS4). The most important enabling factor is the existence of both a same-anion LiMSe₂ family and an exact-cation LiScS₂ analogue, which together make LiScSe₂ a chemically coherent extrapolation (CS1; CS2). The most important limiting factor is high-temperature Li/Se chemical-potential control under sealed, dry, oxygen-free conditions, because analogues require sealed tubes, controlled atmospheres, excess alkali chalcogenide, long anneals, and washing (CS1–CS3; SS2–SS4; TS2–TS4). The final assessment is “likely synthesizable but experimentally nontrivial,” with Route 2 preferred for balancing chemical activity and structural transfer (CS1–CS3).
