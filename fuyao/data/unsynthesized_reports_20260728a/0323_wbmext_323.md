### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamics.** WB₂ is scored high because the target E_hull is 0.0000 eV/atom, and the closest SS/TS analogues are also hull-stable. **[Target data, SS1–SS5/TS1–TS5]** The score is not 100 because the target is explicitly theory-only with no experimental synthesis identified. **[Target data]** CS evidence is supportive because TaB₂, NpB₂, Os₂B₃, and WSi₂ are reported at E_hull = 0.0, while Re₄Si₇ at 0.0907 eV/atom is still only moderately metastable. **[CS1–CS5]**

**F2 — Conditions.** Route 1 is moderate because it needs 1500 °C and multiple steps but only ambient-pressure Ar processing. **[SS1/TS1]** Route 2 is penalized for vacuum and very long sintering, while Route 3 is penalized for extreme temperature and controlled atmosphere/anneal handling. **[SS2/TS2, SS3/TS3, SS4/TS4]** Routes 4–5 avoid high pressure but require molten salts or combustion cleanup, and SS/TS give insufficient route-specific evidence for these methods, so those source families are neutral 50 for those route-condition signals. **[CS1, CS5]**

**F3 — Kinetics.** Flux growth is best kinetically because liquid Al transport reduces the penalty from refractory W, although W diffusion/dissolution remains slow relative to Mo. **[SS1/TS1] [Reasonable Inference]** Solid-state sintering is weakest kinetically because exact TcB₂ analogue requires 120–200 h and multiple boride phases are reported. **[SS2/TS2]** Arc/induction melting improves mixing but risks B loss and W-rich inhomogeneity, while molten-salt and combustion routes improve precursor reactivity but have weaker structural selectivity for P6₃/mmc WB₂. **[SS4/TS4, CS1, CS5] [Reasonable Inference]**
