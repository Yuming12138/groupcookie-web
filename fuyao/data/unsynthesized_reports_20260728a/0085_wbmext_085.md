### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability.** The target is given as E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom, which maps to the 90–100 range and supports a high score [Target]. The closest analogue Zr3AlN is also E_hull = 0.0 across CS/SS/TS, and the top SS/TS analogues are all E_hull = 0.0 [CS, SS, TS]. The main uncertainty is that one CS oxide analogue is reported at E_hull = 0.545 eV/atom, so chemical similarity alone is not uniformly favorable [CS].

**F2 — Route 1.** Route 1 avoids >1000 °C operation by using the SS/TS sealed-ampoule precedent at 500–900 °C [SS, TS]. The score is reduced for sealed/vacuum operation, multi-step annealing, volatile Cd containment, and Cd toxicity [SS, TS, CS, Target, Reasonable Inference]. CS/SS/TS provide insufficient direct Cd toxicity and Cd vapor-pressure evidence, so those source-specific signals are neutral 50 and handled by extrapolation [CS, SS, TS, Reasonable Inference].

**F3 — Route 1.** Kinetics are moderate because Zr/ZrN are refractory, and SS/TS analogues require very long anneals [SS, TS]. The exact Cmcm Zr3AlN analogue improves structural confidence, but solid-state diffusion and Cd ordering remain slow [CS, SS, TS, Reasonable Inference].

**F2 — Route 2.** Route 2 keeps temperature below 1000 °C and avoids direct arc exposure of Cd, which reduces thermal severity relative to Route 3 [SS, TS, Reasonable Inference]. It still requires a sealed controlled atmosphere, volatile Cd handling, multiple processing steps, and toxic Cd precautions [Target, Reasonable Inference]. Direct Cd self-flux evidence is insufficient from CS/SS/TS and scored neutral 50 for that route-specific precedent [CS, SS, TS].

**F3 — Route 2.** The liquid Cd environment should improve transport and crystallization relative to a dry powder route, but this is extrapolated rather than directly reported [Reasonable Inference; insufficient direct evidence from CS/SS/TS]. The same Cmcm Zr3AlN framework and slow-cooling precedent support crystallization of the target topology [CS, SS, TS]. The main uncertainty is whether the Zr–Cd–N phase has a sufficiently wide stoichiometric window under Cd-rich conditions [Reasonable Inference].

**F2 — Route 3.** Route 3 is penalized because arc/prealloy methods and high-temperature anneals in the analogues reach >1000 °C and sometimes >1400 °C [CS, SS, TS]. It also requires controlled atmosphere, multiple steps, and sealed Cd annealing because Cd cannot be treated like the less volatile analogue metals under open arc conditions [CS, SS, TS, Reasonable Inference].

**F3 — Route 3.** Arc prealloying improves Zr homogeneity but produces dense material that may slow later Cd diffusion [CS, SS, TS, Reasonable Inference]. The route benefits from melt-derived success of Zr3AlN but is limited by the need to incorporate Cd after high-temperature processing [CS, SS, TS, Reasonable Inference].

**F4 — Amorphization Tendency.** The target has a simple Zr3CdN formula and close Cmcm analogue Zr3AlN, so unit-cell complexity and structural disorder risk are not high [Target, CS, SS, TS]. The only cautious trigger is the likely Zr/Cd ionic-size mismatch, but this is an extrapolation because the CS/SS/TS data do not provide Cd ionic-radius evidence and are neutral 50 for that check [Reasonable Inference; CS, SS, TS]. Edge/corner NZr6 connectivity is present, but face-sharing coexistence is not indicated for the target prototype [Target, CS, SS, TS].

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

Zr3CdN is likely a thermodynamic ground-state or near-ground-state phase because the supplied target hull energy is 0.0000 eV/atom and the top thermodynamic analogues are also hull-stable [Target, TS]. The strongest synthesis support is the exact Cmcm Zr3AlN analogue, which shares the Zr3MN/NZr6 framework and has an experimental melt-growth route [CS, SS, TS]. The most critical enabling factor is the strong structural and thermodynamic match to Zr3AlN and related Zr–metametal nitrides [CS, SS, TS]. The most critical limiting factor is Cd handling, because Cd is toxic and volatile under the temperatures needed for Zr/ZrN diffusion [Target, Reasonable Inference]. Overall, the phase looks plausible but experimentally nontrivial, with sealed Cd-rich growth the best first attempt [SS, TS, CS, Reasonable Inference].
