### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability**  
The target is predicted at E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom, which supports a likely ground-state or near-ground-state thermodynamic driving force [Target]. CS, SS, and TS reinforce this because the closest La–Cr/Nb/Ta and Ce–Mn nitride analogues include multiple E_hull = 0 experimentally synthesized phases [CS, SS, TS]. The uncertainty is that the target is theory-only and the WBM dataset does not identify an experimental synthesis [Target].

**F2/F3 — Route 1: Li₃N Reactive Flux**  
The Li₃N route is strongly supported by La₃Nb₂N₆/La₃Ta₂N₆ flux analogues, but it requires sealed reactive flux and >1000 °C processing [CS, SS, TS]. Kinetically, Li₃N improves nitrogen activity and transport, but Mo substitution introduces refractory-cation diffusion and possible solubility limits [CS, SS, TS; Reasonable Inference]. The remaining uncertainty is whether Mo stabilizes the same nitride network under Li-rich conditions rather than binary Mo nitrides [Reasonable Inference].

**F2/F3 — Route 2: Na/Ga Reactive Flux**  
Route 2 has the best analogue match because La₃Cr₂N₆ is simultaneously chemically, structurally, and thermodynamically close and was made by Na/Ga/NaN₃ flux at 900 °C [CS, SS, TS]. Its condition severity is reduced relative to 1030–1600 °C flux routes, but Na, NaN₃, sealed Nb tubes, and slow cooling keep it challenging [CS, SS, TS]. The main uncertainty is phase selectivity, since La₃Cr₂N₆ was obtained in small amounts with competing phases [CS, SS, TS].

**F2/F3 — Route 3: High-Temperature Ga Flux**  
Route 3 is supported by rare-earth Nb/Ta nitride flux syntheses, but the 1600 °C temperature imposes high condition severity [CS, SS, TS]. Kinetically, Ga flux helps cation transport, but N₂ activation and Mo nitridation are less favorable than reactive azide or Li₃N nitrogen sources [CS, SS, TS; Reasonable Inference]. The remaining uncertainty is whether GdMoN₃ remains stable at such high temperature or decomposes into GdN/Mo nitride competitors [Reasonable Inference].

**F2/F3 — Route 4: Solid-State Nitridation**  
Route 4 is supported by SS/TS through Ce₂MnN₃ solid-state synthesis from CeN and Mn under purified N₂ at 900 °C [SS, TS]. Its condition severity is moderate, but reaction kinetics are weak because repeated grinding/pressing was required even for the Mn analogue, and Mo is expected to diffuse more slowly than Mn [SS, TS; Reasonable Inference]. Insufficient evidence from CS for this route is treated as neutral 50 [CS].

**F2/F3 — Route 5: Molecular Precursor Ammonolysis**  
Route 5 has lower temperature severity because CrWN₂ was formed by precursor ammonolysis at 750 °C in NH₃ [CS]. The route may improve Gd/Mo mixing, but SS and TS provide insufficient evidence that this chemistry crystallizes the target P2₁/m GdMoN₃ framework, so SS/TS signals are neutral 50 [SS, TS]. The largest uncertainty is whether ammonolysis yields crystalline GdMoN₃ or amorphous/binary nitride products [CS; Reasonable Inference].

**F4 — Amorphization Tendency**  
Cation-size mismatch is triggered because GdN₆ and MoN₅ coordination implies substantially different cation sizes and bonding preferences [Target; Reasonable Inference]. The target description shows corner and edge sharing but not face sharing, so the strict mixed vertex+edge+face criterion is not triggered [Target]. CS/SS/TS provide insufficient direct evidence for the target unit-cell atom count and ionic-radius check, so unavailable source signals are neutral 50 for those subchecks [insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS].

GdMoN₃ is likely a ground-state or very near-ground-state candidate because its reported E_hull is 0.0000 eV/atom [Target]. The analogue support is good but not perfect: rare-earth transition-metal nitrides are repeatedly synthesized by flux and solid-state methods, yet no source reports GdMoN₃ itself [CS, SS, TS; Target]. The most critical enabling factor is use of a reactive flux or azide/Li₃N nitrogen source to overcome Mo nitridation and cation-transport limits [CS, SS, TS; Reasonable Inference]. The most critical limiting factor is phase selectivity, because close analogues show small yields, nonstoichiometry, repeated-cycle requirements, or competing phases [CS, SS, TS]. Overall, the target is synthesizable but likely requires exploratory flux screening rather than a single-shot ceramic reaction [CS, SS, TS; Reasonable Inference].
