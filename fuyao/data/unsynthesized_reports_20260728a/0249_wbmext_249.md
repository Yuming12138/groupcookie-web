### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability.** The target’s supplied E_hull is 0.0000 eV/atom, placing it in the highest stability bin [Target]. TS evidence is strongly supportive because the nearest thermodynamic analogues include multiple zero/near-zero-hull fluorides, including ZrCdF7Tl and NaZr2ZnF11 [TS1, TS2, TS3]. CS evidence is mixed because CoZrF6 is stable/low-hull while CuZrF6 is high-hull and structurally different, so the conflicting Cu case is weighted less heavily [CS1, CS4, CS5].

**F2 — Route 1.** The direct sealed-tube route avoids >1000 °C and avoids GPa pressure, which keeps severity moderate [CS4, CS5, SS2]. Deductions come from sealed/anhydrous fluoride handling, volatile fluoride containment, and Cd toxicity [CS4, SS2, Target, Reasonable Inference]. Remaining uncertainty is whether CdF2/ZrF4 stoichiometry alone stabilizes cubic ZrCdF6 rather than Cd-rich or Zr-rich fluorides [SS2, TS2].

**F3 — Route 1.** Binary fluorides are sufficiently reactive because multiple Zr fluoride analogues are synthesized from dried fluoride mixtures over several days [CS1, CS4, SS1, SS2]. The same ABF6 corner-sharing topology in FeZrF6/CoZrF6 lowers structural reconstruction risk [CS2, CS4, CS5]. Kinetic uncertainty remains due to Cd/Zr size mismatch and the need for long-range B-site ordering [SS2, Reasonable Inference].

**F2/F3 — Route 2.** Flux/Bridgman growth improves kinetics and crystallinity because FeZrF6 and CoZrF6 single crystals were obtained by flux or Bridgman methods [CS2, CS4]. The condition severity is high because Bridgman growth may exceed 1000 °C, requires controlled atmosphere, and uses multi-step crystal-growth handling [CS2, CS4, SS1]. CS2 lacks detailed flux temperature/time, so that sub-factor is treated as insufficient evidence from CS2 and neutral=50 for detailed thermal programming [CS2].

**F2/F3 — Route 3.** Solvothermal conditions are mild in temperature and use reactive soluble precursors, as shown for ScF3 [SS5, TS3]. The weakness is that no CS/SS/TS source demonstrates mixed Cd–Zr ABF6 solvothermal ordering, so CS is insufficient and neutral=50 for this route’s transferability [SS5, TS3]. The route is plausible but not preferred because Zr hydrolysis and competing ammonium/cadmium fluorides are likely risks [SS5, Reasonable Inference].

**F2/F3 — Route 4.** HF/F2 fluorination is chemically powerful and directly supported in related fluoride synthesis contexts [CS4, CS5, SS3]. Its severity is penalized for reactive gas handling, multi-step processing, sealed/corrosive containment, and Cd toxicity [CS4, SS3, Target]. TS provides insufficient route-specific evidence for HF/F2 fluorination of ZrCdF6 and is neutral=50 for that route-specific factor [TS1–TS5].

**F4 — Amorphization Tendency.** Only one amorphization check is triggered: the Cd2+/Zr4+ octahedral radius mismatch is >15% by standard ionic-radius reasoning [Reasonable Inference]. The target and closest CS analogues are simple corner-sharing octahedral frameworks rather than mixed edge/face-sharing networks, which lowers amorphization risk [Target, CS2, CS4, CS5]. Remaining uncertainty is whether larger Cd2+ prefers higher coordination, as seen in ZrCdF7Tl where Cd is seven-coordinate rather than octahedral [SS2, TS2].

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

ZrCdF6 is likely a **DFT ground-state candidate** because the supplied E_hull is 0.0000 eV/atom, but it remains theory-only with no reported experimental synthesis in the supplied target validation [Target]. The synthesis case is well supported by CS analogues because FeZrF6 and CoZrF6 share the ReO3-derived corner-sharing ABF6 framework, and by SS/TS evidence because ZrCdF7Tl demonstrates stable Cd–Zr–F framework chemistry [CS2, CS4, CS5, SS2, TS2]. The most critical enabling factor is the availability of simple binary fluoride precursors and sealed-tube fluoride chemistry around 550–800 °C [CS4, CS5, SS2, TS2]. The most critical limiting factor is Cd/Zr size and coordination mismatch, since Cd is larger and appears seven-coordinate in the closest Cd–Zr–F analogue [SS2, TS2, Reasonable Inference]. Overall, the material is **moderately high feasibility**, best attempted by dry sealed-tube solid-state synthesis before more complex flux growth [CS4, CS5, SS2].
