### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability**  
Ba₂FeH₆ has target-supplied E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom, and close analogues in CS/SS/TS also sit on or essentially on the hull [CS1, CS4, CS5, SS1, TS1]. The thermodynamic score is high but not 100 because the target is explicitly theory-only in the supplied validation note and no experimental synthesis is identified there [Target data; CS/SS/TS analogues only support plausibility].

**F2 — Route 1**  
Route 1 avoids >1000 °C and >5 GPa conditions, but it requires high-pressure H₂ and inert handling of hydrides [CS1, SS1, TS1]. The source evidence is consistent across CS/SS/TS, so no source is neutral for this route [CS1, SS1, TS1, CS5].

**F3 — Route 1**  
Kinetics are favorable relative to other routes because Sr₂FeH₆ and Eu₂FeH₆ already demonstrate FeH₆ formation in the same Fm-3m family [SS1, TS1, CS5]. The remaining kinetic uncertainty is Fe metal conversion and multi-day ordering, which analogues address through long H₂ treatments [SS1, TS1].

**F2 — Route 2**  
Flux synthesis has moderate temperature but more operational complexity because LiH/LiD flux, high-pressure H₂/D₂, and inert handling are all required [SS2, TS2, SS3]. **Insufficient evidence from CS** for the Mg-free flux route, so CS is neutral for this route-specific factor (50).

**F3 — Route 2**  
LiH/LiD flux should improve hydride mobility, but the known flux analogues are BaMg₂FeH₈/BaMg₂RuH₈ rather than Ba₂FeH₆ [SS2, TS2, SS3]. The main uncertainty is whether flux stabilizes the desired Ba₂FeH₆ phase or diverts material into Li/Mg-containing competing phases [SS2, TS2, SS3, Reasonable Inference].

**F2 — Route 3**  
Ultra-high H₂ pressure is experimentally demanding, but the rubric only deducts pressure above 5 GPa, so the main formal deductions are H₂ atmosphere, sealed H₂ reactant, and air-sensitive hydrides [CS2, CS3]. **Insufficient evidence from SS and TS** for 1500–1800 bar H₂ synthesis, so SS and TS are neutral for the route-specific pressure evidence (50).

**F3 — Route 3**  
The Fm-3m A₂MH₆ topology is well supported by K₂PtH₆/Rb₂PtH₆, but Pt/alkali chemistry is less transferable to Ba–Fe than the Sr/Eu Fe analogues [CS2, CS3, SS1, TS1]. The route may help hydrogen uptake, but it does not directly solve Fe diffusion or prove Ba₂FeH₆ phase selectivity [CS2, CS3, Reasonable Inference].

**F2 — Route 4**  
The low-pressure route avoids high-pressure equipment but uses 800 °C, vacuum handling, oxygen-free H₂, and air-sensitive hydrides [SS4, TS4]. **Insufficient evidence from CS** for the low-pressure/vacuum route, so CS is neutral for this route-specific factor (50).

**F3 — Route 4**  
A₂MH₆ formation at low pressure is supported for Sr₂RuH₆, but Fe-containing analogues in SS/TS use higher H₂ pressures [SS1, TS1, SS4, TS4]. This makes Route 4 a lower-confidence screening route with higher risk of incomplete hydrogenation or decomposition [SS4, TS4, Reasonable Inference].

**F4 — Amorphization Tendency**  
The structure is a simple cubic A₂MH₆-type hydride, which lowers amorphization risk relative to large, low-symmetry frameworks [CS1, SS1, TS1]. One risk trigger is retained for the large Ba/Fe size and coordination contrast, while the atom-count and fully mixed connectivity triggers are not clearly met from the Fe analogue descriptions [CS1, SS1, TS1, Reasonable Inference].

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

Ba₂FeH₆ is likely a thermodynamic ground-state or near-ground-state phase under the computed hydride chemical space because the supplied target E_hull is 0 and multiple close analogues are also zero-hull [CS1, CS4, SS1, TS1]. The strongest synthesis support comes from the convergence of Ba₂RuH₆ chemistry and Sr₂FeH₆/Eu₂FeH₆ structural chemistry [CS1, SS1, TS1, CS5]. The critical enabling factor is maintaining sufficient H₂ chemical potential for the FeH₆ octahedral hydride framework during long solid-state ordering [SS1, TS1]. The critical limiting factor is practical phase control in air-sensitive, high-pressure hydride chemistry, especially avoiding residual BaH₂/Fe or competing hydride phases [SS1, TS1, SS5]. Overall, Ba₂FeH₆ is a credible but nontrivial target, best approached first through direct high-pressure H₂ hydrogenation [CS1, SS1, TS1].
