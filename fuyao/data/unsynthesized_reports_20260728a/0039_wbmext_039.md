### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability**  
The target’s E_hull is 0.0000 eV/atom, which strongly supports thermodynamic feasibility in the supplied computed hull. [Target data] The analogue set reinforces this because Cd(AsO3)2, LiAsO3, AsPO5, CsAs3O8, and BiAsO4 thermodynamic-neighbor entries are all listed at E_hull = 0.0 eV/atom, while Pd/Cu arsenates in CS/SS are also near-hull. [CS1–CS5, SS1–SS5, TS1–TS5] The remaining uncertainty is experimental: the target is explicitly theory-only in the supplied target data. [Target data]

**F2/F3 — Route 1 Hydrothermal**  
Condition severity is low because the strongest analogues crystallize under moderate hydrothermal temperatures of ~220–300 °C without vacuum, H2/NH3, or multi-GPa pressure. [CS1, SS1, TS1, SS2, TS2, TS4] Kinetics are favorable because solution-mediated transport avoids the slow dry-oxide diffusion seen in solid-state routes, and the same Hydrophilite-derived Cd analogue provides strong structural transfer. [CS1, SS1, TS1, SS2, TS2] The main uncertainty is precursor choice because CS1/SS1/TS1 have internally inconsistent precursor metadata, so that specific precursor signal is neutral 50. [CS1, SS1, TS1]

**F2/F3 — Route 2 Solid-State**  
The route is more severe because PdAs2O6 requires vacuum sealing, drying, pelletizing, and 100 h at 973 K, while Sr arsenate requires 700 °C for 36 h. [CS3, SS5, SS3] Kinetics are acceptable but not excellent because same/similar octahedral arsenate frameworks exist, yet dry oxide reaction requires long holds and arsenic oxide containment. [CS3, SS5, SS3] TS gives stability context but insufficient low-pressure solid-state route evidence, so TS route-specific F2/F3 signal is neutral 50. [TS1–TS5]

**F2/F3 — Route 3 CVT**  
The route is conditionally plausible because Cu arsenate single crystals are grown by chlorine-assisted CVT, but it is not directly demonstrated for Zn(AsO3)2. [CS4, CS5] Kinetics and composition control are penalized because chlorine/oxygen arsenate chemistry can be poorly reproducible and can produce secondary phases, as reported for GaAsO4 gas–solid synthesis. [CS2] SS and TS provide insufficient CVT-specific evidence for this route, so SS and TS route-specific signals are neutral 50. [SS1–SS5, TS1–TS5]

**F2/F3 — Route 4 High-Pressure Oxidizing Hydrothermal**  
The route is a credible fallback because high-pressure O2 hydrothermal conditions stabilize AsO6-containing AsPO5 at 650 °C and 1.2 kbar.
