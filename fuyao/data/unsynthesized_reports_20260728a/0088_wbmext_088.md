**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability**  
The target E_hull is 0.0000 eV/atom in the supplied target data, and every listed top CS/SS/TS analogue also has E_hull = 0.0, so the thermodynamic score is near the top of the ≤0.05 eV/atom band (CS#1–5; SS#1–5; TS#1–5). **F2 — Route 1**  
Route 1 is penalized for controlled atmosphere, sealed volatile Na/NaN3 chemistry, multi-step processing, and air-sensitive/toxic reagents, all of which are explicitly present in the flux analogues (CS#1–3; SS#3–5; TS#3–5). It avoids extreme temperature and high pressure because direct Sr–Ge–N flux chemistry occurs at 760 °C and AE–Si–N flux analogues occur at 900–1100 °C, not >1400 °C or >5 GPa (CS#1–3; SS#3–5; TS#3–5).

**F3 — Route 1**  
Na flux improves kinetics by enabling liquid-mediated transport and crystallization, which is supported by single-crystal AESiN2 growth and coarsely crystalline Sr5(GeN3)2 formation (CS#1–3; SS#3–5; TS#3–5). The main kinetic uncertainty is structural selection because the direct Sr–Ge–N product in the evidence is Sr5Ge2N6 rather than SrGeN2, so the score is reduced for partial framework transferability [Reasonable Inference] (CS#3; SS#4; TS#4).

**F2 — Route 2**  
Route 2 has milder temperature severity because SS/TS nitride analogues form at 800 °C, but it still requires controlled N2/Ar atmosphere, sealed ampoules, ball milling, and air-sensitive nitride precursors (SS#1–2; TS#1–2). CS gives only indirect support for this route and no exact Sr–Ge binary-nitride sintering precedent, so CS is treated as neutral 50 for route-specific evidence (CS#4).

**F3 — Route 2**  
Binary nitrides should be more chemically active than elemental powders, and the exact stoichiometric reaction Sr3N2 + Ge3N4 → 3 SrGeN2 is attractive [Reasonable Inference] (SS#1–2; TS#1–2). The kinetic penalty is substantial because structurally similar solid-state nitrides required 7–14 days, implying slow diffusion and framework reconstruction (SS#1–2; TS#1–2).

**F2 — Route 3**  
Route 3 is severely penalized because the closest high-temperature ceramic precedent uses 1550–1650 °C, specialized furnace/crucible equipment, and controlled N2 atmosphere (CS#4). SS/TS do not specifically validate direct elemental Sr–Ge nitridation, so SS and TS are neutral 50 for that route-specific evidence (SS#1–2; TS#1–2).

**F3 — Route 3**  
High temperature helps diffusion, but elemental or weakly nitrided Ge chemistry is likely less reactive than Na flux or binary nitride reagents [Reasonable Inference] (CS#4; SS#1–2; TS#1–2). The absence of an exact SrGeN2 ceramic precedent and likely complete structural reconstruction reduce the kinetic score (CS#4).
