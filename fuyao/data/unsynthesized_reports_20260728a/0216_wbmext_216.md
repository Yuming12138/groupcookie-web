### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

HgGeF₆ is most likely a predicted ground-state phase because its calculated E_hull is 0.0000 eV/atom, but it remains theory-only with no reported experimental synthesis in the provided target record. (Target) The synthesis case is strongly supported by CS1 because the closest analogue has the same R-3 corner-sharing ABF₆ octahedral framework and is made by direct F₂ fluorination. (CS1) SS and TS evidence broadly support that dense fluoride frameworks and near-hull fluorides can be synthesized, but many analogues require sealed tubes, long anneals, or harsh fluorine chemistry. (SS1, SS3, TS1, TS2, TS5) The critical enabling factor is the close structural/chemical match to known ABF₆ fluorides under F₂ conditions. (CS1, CS5) The critical limiting factor is practical handling: Hg toxicity, F₂ corrosiveness, possible Hg/Ge fluoride volatility, and phase-purity control. (Target, CS1, CS5, [Reasonable Inference])
