### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability**  
CaTiN₂ is computed exactly on the hull with E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom, placing it in the 90–100 score bin. 【Target data】 The closest SrTiN₂ analogue is also on-hull and appears as the top structural and thermodynamic match, which strengthens the stability argument. 【CS-4 / SS-1 / TS-1】 The main uncertainty is experimental, because the target is a theory-only WBM candidate with no reported synthesis in the supplied data. 【Target data】

**F2 — Condition Severity**  
Route 1 scores 55 because it requires >1000 °C, inert/controlled handling, multiple processing steps, and air-sensitive alkaline-earth nitride chemistry. 【CS-4 / SS-1 / TS-1】 Route 2 scores 45 because Li₃N introduces volatility/sublimation risk and the Ca₂VN₃ analogue uses long staged heating under N₂. 【CS-2 / SS-2 / TS-2】 Route 3 scores 55 because it avoids >1000 °C but requires sealed Nb tubes, alkali flux, azide/nitride handling, and post-growth flux removal; insufficient evidence from SS and TS keeps the non-CS source signal neutral at 50. 【CS-3】 Route 4 scores 45 because direct elemental nitridation needs 1000–1200 °C, Ar/N₂ switching, reactive Ca metal, and likely sealed/excess-Ca control. 【CS-1; Reasonable Inference】

**F3 — Reaction Kinetics**  
Route 1 has the best kinetics score because the SrTiN₂ analogue has the same P4/nmm topology, although the 5-day firing indicates slow diffusion through refractory TiN. 【CS-4 / SS-1 / TS-1】 Route 2 benefits from Li₃N reactivity, but Ca₂VN₃ is structurally different and requires long 100 h treatment, so reconstruction and time penalties remain. 【CS-2 / SS-2 / TS-2】 Route 3 benefits from liquid flux transport, but no SS/TS source demonstrates Ti-containing P4/nmm flux growth; insufficient evidence from SS and TS is scored neutral 50 for transferability. 【CS-3】 Route 4 is kinetically weakest because element-to-ternary reconstruction is broad and Ti can be trapped as TiN before CaTiN₂ ordering. [Reasonable Inference from CS-1 and TiN-based exact analogue in CS-4 / SS-1 / TS-1]

**F4 — Amorphization Tendency**  
Only the cation-size mismatch check is triggered; CS/SS/TS do not provide explicit ionic radii, so that sub-check uses [Reasonable Inference] and neutral 50 source scores for ionic-radius evidence. The unit cell is likely small because the closest P4/nmm analogue is Sr₂Ti₂N₄, well below 40 atoms. 【CS-4 / SS-1 / TS-1】 The target and SrTiN₂ descriptions show corner/edge sharing but not face sharing, so the mixed vertex+edge+face-sharing check is not triggered. 【Target data; CS-4 / SS-1 / TS-1】
