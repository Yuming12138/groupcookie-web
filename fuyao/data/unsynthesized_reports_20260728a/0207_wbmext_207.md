### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability**  
The target is listed at E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom, so it falls in the highest thermodynamic-stability bin. Evidence: Target record. CS and TS reinforce this because multiple close T–Pn–Ch analogues are at or near the hull, including PdSbSe, PdAsSe, NiAsSe, CoSbS, and CoAsS. Evidence: CS1–CS5, TS1, TS2, TS4. Remaining uncertainty is that the target is theory-only and not experimentally validated in the source dataset. Evidence: Target record.

**F2 — Route 1**  
Route 1 avoids high pressure and >1000 °C temperatures, which is favorable for condition severity. Evidence: CS1, CS2, TS1, TS2. The main deductions are sealed vacuum/Ar handling, volatile Se/Sb control, multi-step binary/ternary processing, and selenium toxicity. Evidence: CS1, TS1, Target safety note. Overall severity is moderate rather than easy because the same sources require controlled ramps, sealed tubes, quenching, and sometimes long annealing. Evidence: CS1, CS2, TS1, TS2.

**F3 — Route 1**  
Kinetics are acceptable because close T–Pn–Ch analogues form by solid-state sintering at ~800 °C, but the route still relies on bulk diffusion and ordering. Evidence: CS1, CS2, TS1, TS2. CoSe + Sb is expected to be more reactive than fully elemental Co/Sb/Se, but that precursor choice is a [Reasonable Inference] from the binary chalcogenide route. Structural reconstruction is relatively favorable because PdSbSe/NiAsSe are spinel-like analogues, but the target’s orthorhombic distortion adds uncertainty. Evidence: Target record, CS1, TS1, TS2, SS4.

**F2 — Route 2**  
Route 2 remains below 1000 °C and does not require high pressure, but it uses reactive halogen transport agents. Evidence: CS5, TS3. The major severity penalties are Cl₂/I₂ handling, evacuated sealed-tube work, volatile Se containment, repeated processing, and toxicity/corrosion. Evidence: CS5, TS3, Target safety note. Insufficient evidence from SS for halogen-assisted CoSbSe synthesis, so SS is neutral = 50 for this route.

**F3 — Route 2**  
Halogen transport should improve kinetics by mobilizing components, and this is supported indirectly by CoAsS direct reaction with Cl₂ and CoSb₃ CVT/recrystallization. Evidence: CS5, TS3. The weakness is structural transferability: CoAsS is spinel-like but has As/S rather than Sb/Se, while CoSb₃ has Sb/Co chemistry but a skutterudite structure. Evidence: CS5, TS3. Composition fractionation during transport remains a [Reasonable Inference] risk because Co, Sb, and Se may transport at different rates.

**F2 — Route 3**  
Route 3 is much harsher because HP CoSbS analogues use 60 kbar and 1000–1700 °C. Evidence: CS3, CS4, SS1, SS2. The proposed adaptation avoids >1400 °C to protect Se/Sb stoichiometry, but pressure, capsule handling, multistep processing, and toxicity remain severe penalties. Evidence: CS3, CS4, SS1, SS2, Target safety note. Insufficient evidence from TS for high-pressure CoSbSe synthesis, so TS is neutral = 50 for this route.

**F3 — Route 3**  
High temperature and pressure can accelerate reconstruction, but short dwell times and dense prereacted pellets limit equilibration. Evidence: CS3, CS4, SS1, SS2. HP analogues explicitly indicate metastability, partial transformations, and phase-control difficulty, which lowers the structural-reconstruction score. Evidence: CS3, CS4, SS1, SS2. This route is best treated as a rescue route if ambient synthesis fails. Evidence: CS3, CS4, SS1, SS2; rescue-route framing is [Reasonable Inference].

**F4 — Amorphization Tendency**  
The target has 3D periodic connectivity and no face-sharing criterion reported, which supports a low amorphization tendency. Evidence: Target record. Close analogues are ordered crystalline T–Pn–Ch frameworks rather than glass-forming networks, and the atom-count evidence from analogues is below the >40-atom trigger. Evidence: CS1, CS3, CS4, SS1, SS4. The remaining uncertainty is that formal ionic radii and exact target cell atom count are not supplied by CS/SS/TS, so that part is treated as neutral = 50 where source evidence is absent.

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

CoSbSe is most likely a predicted ground-state or near-ground-state phase because the target record gives E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom, although it remains theory-only with no identified experimental synthesis. Evidence: Target record. The strongest synthesis support comes from ambient-pressure sealed-tube analogues PdSbSe, PdAsSe, and NiAsSe, which share the T:Pn:Ch = 1:1:1 chemistry and spinel-like frameworks. Evidence: CS1, CS2, TS1, TS2, SS4. The most important enabling factor is the combination of on-hull thermodynamics and very close structural/chemical analogues. Evidence: Target record, CS1, TS1, SS4. The main limiting factor is controlling Se/Sb volatility and phase purity during long sealed-tube anneals. Evidence: CS1, CS2, TS1, TS2, Target safety note. High-pressure evidence is useful as a backup but not preferred because CoSbS analogues require specialized equipment and show phase-control difficulty. Evidence: CS3, CS4, SS1, SS2.
