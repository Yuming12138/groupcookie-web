### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability**  
The target E_hull is reported as 0.000 eV/atom, placing it in the highest thermodynamic score band. [Target] The CS/SS/TS analogues all include multiple E_hull = 0.0 fluoride phases, so thermodynamic similarity is strongly favorable rather than contradictory. [CS1–CS5, SS1–SS5, TS1–TS5] The uncertainty is that the target is a WBM theory-only candidate with no identified experimental synthesis. [Target]

**F2/F3 — Route 1**  
Direct flowing-F₂ fluorination is supported by CdPdF₆, ZnPdF₆, and MgPdF₆, which use pre-fluorination, heating, tempering, and cooling in F₂. [CS1, CS2, CS4] The F2 penalty is moderate-high because F₂ and Cd are hazardous, but the route avoids autoclave pressure. [CS2, Target] Kinetics are penalized because the CS route proves Cd–Pd fluoride formation but not Ni(IV) formation at ambient pressure; SS/TS are insufficient for that specific ambient-pressure Ni(IV) claim and are neutral 50. [CS2; insufficient evidence from SS/TS]

**F2/F3 — Route 2**  
High-pressure F₂ is the best-supported route for Ni(IV) fluoride formation because SrNiF₆ and BaNiF₆ are made under pressure fluorination, while NiPtF₆ supports related R-3 fluoride synthesis under compressed F₂. [SS1, TS1, SS3, TS3, CS5] F2 is lower because the route requires sealed high-pressure F₂ hardware, multistep fluorination, and toxic/reactive materials. [SS1, TS1, CS5, Target] F3 is high because high F₂ chemical potential lowers the oxidation and diffusion barrier relative to conventional refractory solid-state synthesis. [SS1, TS1, CS5; Reasonable Inference]

**F2/F3 — Route 3**  
The aHF metathesis route has severe handling requirements but favorable low-temperature kinetics because K₂NiF₆ already contains the desired [NiF₆]²⁻ unit. [SS2, TS2] The route avoids bulk Ni oxidation and long-range solid diffusion, which supports a high precursor-activity and diffusion score. [SS2, TS2; Reasonable Inference] The main kinetic uncertainty is whether CdNiF₆ precipitates as the ordered R-3 crystalline phase rather than a metastable or poorly crystalline fluoride. [SS2, TS2; Reasonable Inference]

**F2/F3 — Route 4**  
The photochemical route is supported only indirectly by ACrF₆ synthesis in F₂/aHF under UV irradiation, so its transferability to CdNiF₆ is weaker than Routes 1–3. [SS5, TS5] Its condition severity is lowered by ambient temperature but remains hazardous due to F₂/aHF and specialized FEP/PTFE equipment. [SS5, TS5] Its kinetics are uncertain because CS provides insufficient photochemical Cd/Ni evidence and is neutral 50 for this route. [insufficient evidence from CS; SS5, TS5]

**F4 — Amorphization Tendency**  
The target has one clear amorphization trigger: a large Cd/Ni size mismatch inferred from Cd–F = 2.26 Å versus Ni–F = 1.80 Å. [Target; Reasonable Inference] The framework itself is favorable because the target uses only corner-sharing octahedra, matching CdPdF₆ rather than the mixed corner/edge/face sharing seen in Sr/Ba Ni fluorides. [Target, CS2, SS1, SS3] Exact unit-cell atom count is not supplied by CS/SS/TS, so that source-level signal is neutral 50; the simple described R-3 formula does not suggest a >40-atom cell trigger. [Target; insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS]
