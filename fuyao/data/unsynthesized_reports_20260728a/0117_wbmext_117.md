Condition severity is moderate-to-high because the route requires sealed ampoules, dry Ar handling, Li₃N flux, and up to 1030 °C. [CS][SS][TS] Kinetics are comparatively favorable because Li₃N flux can dissolve/react precursors and the exact La₃Nb₂N₆ framework is already known. [CS][SS][TS][Reasonable Inference] This route is credible but less preferred than Li₃N flux for Eu-containing products. [CS][SS][TS][Reasonable Inference]

**F2/F3 — Route 3: Na/Ga/Azide Flux**
