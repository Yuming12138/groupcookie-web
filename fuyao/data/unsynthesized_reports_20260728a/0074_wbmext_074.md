### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

GaAsO4-wbmext_074 is likely a DFT ground-state candidate because its reported E_hull is 0.0000 eV/atom, but it remains experimentally unvalidated in the supplied dataset (Target record). The strongest synthesis support is the exact-composition GaAsO4 gas–solid reference with the same GaO4/AsO4 corner-sharing motif, although that analogue is orthorhombic rather than tetragonal (CS1, SS1). Flux evidence is also meaningful because GaAsO4 appears in Ga–As–O flux chemistry, but that same evidence shows competition with KGaAs2O7 (SS3, TS2). The key enabling factor is the chemically natural Ga3+/As5+ tetrahedral oxide framework, already observed in GaAsO4 analogues (CS1, SS1). The key limiting factor is polymorph and phase selectivity, amplified by arsenic volatility/toxicity and Cl2 handling requirements (CS1, SS1, CS4).
