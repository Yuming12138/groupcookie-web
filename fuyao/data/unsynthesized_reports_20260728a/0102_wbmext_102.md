### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability.** EuReN₃ is assigned 98/100 because the target E_hull is 0.000 eV/atom in the provided target data. CS supports high stability because several chemically similar ternary nitrides are on or near the hull, including La₃Ta₂N₆ and La₃Nb₂N₆ at 0.0 eV/atom and NaTa₃N₅/U₂CrN₃ near the hull (CS-1 to CS-5). SS/TS also support high stability because MoMnN₂ and La₃Cr₂N₆ are stable analogues (SS-1, SS-2, TS-1, TS-2). Remaining uncertainty is kinetic rather than thermodynamic because the dataset identifies EuReN₃ as theory-only, not experimentally synthesized.

**F2/F3 — Route 1 Flux Growth.** Flux synthesis is chemically well supported by rare-earth transition-metal nitride analogues using Ga, Li₃N, Na/Ga, NaN₃, and sealed Nb/Ta ampoules (CS-2, CS-5, SS-2, TS-2). F2 is reduced by sealed reactive/air-sensitive handling and multi-step isolation, while F3 is limited by Re’s refractory nature and by phase competition seen for La₃Cr₂N₆ (SS-2, TS-2). The main uncertainty is whether Re dissolves/reacts sufficiently in the chosen flux [Reasonable Inference].

**F2/F3 — Route 2 Ammonolysis.** Route 2 has the best weighted score because NH₃ ammonolysis achieves nitride formation at 625–700 °C under ambient pressure in close structural/thermodynamic analogues (SS-1, TS-1). F2 is favorable because no >1000 °C or high pressure is required, but NH₃ and repeated precursor processing impose deductions (SS-1, TS-1). F3 is only moderate because EuReN₃ formation from oxides requires complete reconstruction and Re redox control; CS provides insufficient direct signal for Eu–Re ammonolysis and is neutral 50 for that route-specific support.

**F2/F3 — Route 3 Solid-State Sintering.** Solid-state nitride sintering is supported by X₂YN₃ actinide nitrides made from mononitrides and transition metals at 1200–1600 °C in N₂ (CS-3, CS-4).
