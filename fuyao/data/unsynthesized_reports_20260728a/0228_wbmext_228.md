Condition severity is moderate because the route avoids >1000 °C and high pressure but requires vacuum sealing, volatile iodine/sulfur chemistry, and a multistep two-zone setup. [CS3; TS4] Kinetics are less favorable than CVT because Zr diffusion through sulfide product layers is expected to be slow by [Reasonable Inference], even though direct metal–sulfur routes are documented.
