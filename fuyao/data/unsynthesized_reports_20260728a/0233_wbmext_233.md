### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability**  
The target has a supplied E_hull of 0.0 eV/atom, so it receives a high thermodynamic score within the ≤0.05 eV/atom bin. (Target data) CS/SS/TS reinforce this because chemically and structurally close analogues include MnGa₂Se₄ near the hull and HgAl₂Se₄ at E_hull = 0.0. (CS, SS, TS) The remaining uncertainty is that the target is WBM theory-only and not experimentally reported in the supplied data. (Target data)

**F2 — Route 1**  
Route 1 is penalized for >1000 °C operation, vacuum sealing, volatile Se, multiple thermal steps, and selenium toxicity. (CS, TS; Target safety note) The score remains viable because analogous MnGa₂Se₄ was explicitly synthesized by sealed-ampoule melt/anneal rather than exotic equipment. (CS)

**F3 — Route 1**  
Elemental Mn/Al/Se can react successfully in analogous sealed systems, but elemental metals are less kinetically active than pre-reacted chalcogenides. (CS, TS; [Reasonable Inference]) The tetrahedral framework similarity to MnGa₂Se₄ and HgAl₂Se₄ reduces the structural-reconstruction penalty. (CS, SS, TS)

**F2 — Route 2**  
Route 2 avoids the >1000 °C penalty and avoids direct elemental Se as the main Se source, but it still requires vacuum/inert handling, multistep grinding/annealing, and toxic selenide handling. (CS, SS, TS) This gives the best severity score among the practical routes. (CS, SS, TS)

**F3 — Route 2**  
Binary selenides are chemically closer to the target than elemental metals, but solid-state diffusion and cation ordering remain moderate kinetic barriers. (CS, SS; [Reasonable Inference]) Long annealing in CoGa₂S₄ and solid-state Al-selenide analogues supports the need for extended equilibration. (CS, SS, TS)

**F2 — Route 3**  
CVT uses lower temperatures than the melt route but still needs sealed high-vacuum ampoules, volatile/corrosive transport agents, and a multistep feed-plus-growth sequence. (CS) Insufficient evidence from SS and TS for CVT of the closest I-4 Al-selenide analogues was scored neutral at 50 for route-specific support.

**F3 — Route 3**  
CS supports CVT for MnIn₂Se₄ and FeGa₂S₄, but not specifically MnAl₂Se₄. (CS) The main uncertainty is composition control because Mn, Al, and Se may transport at different rates under halide gradients. [Reasonable Inference based on CS]

**F2 — Route 4**  
High pressure is the dominant severity penalty because MnAl₂S₄ required up to 95 kb. (CS) Insufficient evidence from SS and TS for high-pressure Mn–Al–Se synthesis was scored neutral at 50 for route-specific support.

**F3 — Route 4**  
The high-pressure sulfide analogue shows that Mn–Al chalcogenide polymorphs can be accessed, but it also reports difficult phase control, multiple polymorphs, and decomposition behavior. (CS) This lowers the kinetics score because pressure screening and quenching introduce uncertainty not present in ambient solid-state routes. (CS; [Reasonable Inference])

**F4 — Amorphization Tendency**  
The target is penalized once for Mn/Al cation size mismatch; CS/SS/TS do not provide ionic radii, so that trigger is a [Reasonable Inference] and the radius-specific source signal is neutral 50. The framework itself is favorable because close analogues are crystalline corner-sharing tetrahedral networks and do not require mixed edge/face-sharing connectivity. (CS, SS, TS) The remaining uncertainty is whether Mn/Al ordering produces antisite disorder rather than amorphization. [Reasonable Inference based on CS/SS/TS]

**Contradictory Source Handling**  
The CuAlSe₂ SS/TS entry has useful structural/thermodynamic information for AlSe₄ tetrahedral stability, but its listed synthesis precursors describe an unrelated lithium zirconium phosphate procedure. (SS, TS) Therefore, that entry is used only for structural/thermodynamic context and treated neutral at 50 for synthesis-condition route scoring. (SS, TS)

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

MnAl₂Se₄ is most likely a thermodynamic ground-state or near-ground-state phase because the supplied target E_hull is 0.0 eV/atom and several close TS/SS analogues are also at or near the hull. (Target data; SS, TS) The analogue support is strong structurally because HgAl₂Se₄ has the same tetragonal I-4 corner-sharing tetrahedral AlSe₄ framework, and chemically strong because MnGa₂Se₄ contains MnSe₄ tetrahedra in a closely related AB₂Se₄ selenide. (CS, SS, TS) The most critical enabling factor is the existence of experimentally synthesized tetrahedral Mn–group-13–chalcogen and Al-selenide analogues under sealed high-temperature conditions. (CS, SS, TS) The most critical limiting factor is maintaining Mn:Al:Se stoichiometry while preventing Se loss and suppressing competing Al-selenide phases. (SS, TS; [Reasonable Inference]) Overall, the best first attempt is binary-selenide solid-state synthesis, with CVT reserved for crystal growth after phase confirmation. (CS, SS, TS; [Reasonable Inference])
