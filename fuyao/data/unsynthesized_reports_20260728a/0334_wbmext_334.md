### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability.**  
The target’s supplied E_hull is 0.0000 eV/atom, placing it in the 90–100 range, and TS/SS include zero-hull CaB2C2, GdB2C2, and SmB2C2 analogues (SS1, TS1, SS3, TS2, TS3). The exact Yb P4₂/mmc analogue has mixed thermodynamic metadata, with E_hull ~0.165 eV/atom but E_above_ground_state = 0.0, so I weighted the supplied target hull and zero-hull structural/thermodynamic analogues more heavily (CS1, SS2, TS1, TS2, TS3). Remaining uncertainty is that the target is a theory-only WBM candidate and not experimentally identified in the supplied record.

**F2 — Route 1.**  
Route 1 is penalized for >1000°C firing, controlled inert/sealed atmosphere, multi-step pellet/seal/fire processing, and Yb volatility/air-sensitive handling (SS1, TS1, SS3, TS2). It avoids 1900°C processing and avoids >5 GPa pressure, which keeps it less severe than the sintering and high-pressure routes (SS1, TS1, CS2–CS5, TS5). CS gives exact Yb chemistry but not this route, so **insufficient evidence from CS; neutral 50 route-condition signal**.

**F3 — Route 1.**  
Kinetics are favorable relative to oxide/boride sintering because the closest I4/mcm analogue forms at 1050°C after 48 h, and the target framework is structurally close to CaB2C2 (SS1, TS1). The main kinetic uncertainty is B–C network ordering with a volatile Yb component, which motivates sealed containment and possible reannealing **[Reasonable Inference]** supported by Yb vapor-pressure concerns in related routes (SS3, TS2).

**F2 — Route 2.**  
Route 2 is more severe because arc-melting exceeds 1400°C and requires Ar/vacuum/sealed annealing, multiple remelts, and Yb-loss control (CS1, SS2, SS3, TS2, TS3). The route has direct exact-composition support, but its severity score is reduced by volatility and multi-step annealing requirements (CS1, SS2, SS3, TS2).

**F3 — Route 2.**  
Arc-melting improves precursor contact and can form exact Yb(BC)2 chemistry, which helps kinetics (CS1, SS2). The structural pathway is uncertain because the reported exact Yb phase is P4₂/mmc, not target I4/mcm, and one related study reports YbB2C2 failure under its arc/anneal conditions (CS1, SS2, SS3, TS2).

**F2 — Route 3.**  
Route 3 is severe due to 1900°C processing, vacuum, 10 kbar tablet pressing, and multiple synthesis steps (CS2–CS5, SS4, SS5). It is not penalized for >5 GPa because the 10 kbar pressure is shaping pressure and below the high-pressure reaction threshold; **insufficient evidence from TS for this route; neutral 50 route-condition signal** (CS2–CS5, SS4, SS5).

**F3 — Route 3.**  
Kinetics are limited by refractory oxide/boride precursors and the high 1900°C temperature required for analogous rare-earth RB2C2 phases (CS2–CS5, SS4, SS5). The route is chemically transferable across rare-earth borocarbides, but polymorph transfer to target I4/mcm is uncertain because the cited rare-earth analogues are mainly P4₂/mmc (CS2–CS5, SS4, SS5, SS1, TS1).

**F2 — Route 4.**

**F3 — Route 4.**  
High pressure may help access metastable borocarbide polymorphs **[Reasonable Inference]**, but the only source-supported high-pressure analogue is MgB2C2 with a different orthorhombic structure (TS5). The uncertain transformation pathway and lack of Yb-specific high-pressure evidence keep the kinetics score low (TS5).

**F4 — Amorphization Tendency.**  
The target is a simple single-cation MB2C2 composition, and closely related analogues are crystalline ordered borocarbides, which supports low amorphization tendency (CS1–CS5, SS1–SS5, TS1–TS5). Yb(BC)2 is likely a thermodynamic ground-state or near-ground-state phase because the supplied target E_hull is 0.0000 eV/atom and multiple structurally/thermodynamically similar MB2C2 analogues sit at E_hull = 0.0 (SS1, TS1, SS3, TS2, TS3). The analogue support is strong at the family level: exact Yb(BC)2 chemistry is reported, and the closest I4/mcm topology is experimentally synthesized for CaB2C2 (CS1, SS2, SS1, TS1). The most important enabling factor is the existence of a simple crystalline MB2C2 framework accessible by sealed elemental reaction or arc/anneal processing (SS1, TS1, CS1, SS2). The main limiting factor is Yb-specific phase control, because Yb has volatility concerns and one arc/anneal study reports YbB2C2 failure, while the exact Yb analogue adopts P4₂/mmc rather than the target I4/mcm structure (SS3, TS2, CS1, SS2). Overall, the material is **moderately likely synthesizable**, but polymorph selection and Yb retention must be treated as critical variables.
