### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability.**  
Rb₂TiS₃ is predicted on the hull with E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom, which places it in the highest thermodynamic scoring bin [Target]. The chemically similar set is also mostly on-hull, with CS1–CS4 at 0 eV/atom and CS5 at 0.0476 eV/atom [CS1–CS5]. The structurally and thermodynamically similar sets are especially supportive because all five listed SS/TS analogues have E_hull = 0, including K₂TiS₃ [SS1–SS5, TS1–TS5]. The main uncertainty is that the target is theory-only and the source dataset reports no experimental synthesis [Target].

**F2/F3 — Route 1.**  
The severity score is reduced by vacuum sealing, sulfur volatility, air-sensitive alkali sulfide handling, and multi-step ampoule processing, all present in the K₂TiS₃ route [SS1/TS1]. Kinetically, Ti metal is relatively sluggish, but the exact K₂TiS₃ analogue demonstrates that Ti + S + alkali sulfide can form the TiS₅ network after long annealing [SS1/TS1]. This route is weighted most heavily because it directly transfers the structural motif, while Rb-specific solid-state sulfide precedents support replacing K with Rb [CS1, CS5]. Remaining uncertainty is the Rb/K size change, which may alter symmetry or phase stability [Reasonable Inference from Target and SS1/TS1].

**F2/F3 — Route 2.**  
Flux synthesis has lower temperature severity, but still requires Ar/vacuum handling, sealed tubes, excess sulfur flux, and post-growth washing [SS2/TS2, SS5/TS5]. Kinetics benefit from liquid/flux transport, but the provided flux analogues are Li–V/Nb/Ta or Ba–V sulfides rather than TiS₅ Rb₂TiS₃ [SS2–SS5, TS2–TS5]. Some flux series show variable yields and unsynthesized members, so phase-window sensitivity limits the score [SS2/TS2, SS3/TS3, SS4/TS4]. Insufficient evidence from CS for this flux pathway is treated as neutral = 50.

**F2/F3 — Route 3.**  
Reactive-gas sulfidation is supported by alkali carbonate + oxide/metal syntheses in H₂S or H₂/chalcogen streams [CS2, CS5]. Severity is dominated by toxic/reactive gas handling and, in CS2, temperature slightly above 1000 °C [CS2]. Kinetics are moderate because H₂S is a strong sulfidizer, but transforming Rb₂CO₃/TiO₂ into an ordered TiS₅ framework is a full reconstruction [CS2; Reasonable Inference]. Insufficient evidence from SS and TS for this route is treated as neutral = 50 for both sources.

**F2/F3 — Route 4.**  
The solvothermal route has mild temperature conditions, and CS4 shows that Rb thiometalates can crystallize in ethylenediamine at 393 K [CS4]. However, CS4 also reports Rb₂MoS₄ as a minor phase with Ag₂S as the main product, which is a negative phase-control signal [CS4]. The target requires an extended edge-sharing TiS₅ network, while CS4 concerns tetrahedral MoS₄ chemistry, so structural reconstruction is scored very low [CS4; Target]. Insufficient evidence from SS and TS for solvothermal target-framework formation is treated as neutral = 50.

**F4 — Amorphization Tendency.**  
Cation-size mismatch is triggered because Rb coordination distances are far larger than Ti–S distances in the target and in K₂TiS₃, consistent with a large alkali/Ti radius contrast [Target, SS1/TS1; Reasonable Inference]. Mixed face/edge/corner-sharing connectivity is also triggered because both target Rb₂TiS₃ and K₂TiS₃ show mixed connectivity around sulfur-centered octahedra [Target, SS1/TS1]. Unit-cell atom count is not provided, so C2 is not triggered and CS/SS/TS are neutral = 50 for that check. These two triggers give a medium amorphization/ordering risk and F4 = 60.

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

Rb₂TiS₃ is likely a ground-state or near-ground-state phase because its target E_hull is 0.0000 eV/atom and the closest K₂TiS₃ analogue is also on the hull [Target, SS1/TS1]. The strongest synthesis support comes from SS1/TS1 because K₂TiS₃ has the same TiS₅ edge-sharing trigonal-bipyramidal framework and was experimentally made by direct ampoule annealing [SS1/TS1]. CS data reinforce that Rb sulfides can be made by sealed-tube, gas-sulfidation, and solvothermal methods, but CS4 also warns that Rb sulfide phase selectivity can be poor in solvothermal chemistry [CS1, CS4, CS5]. The most critical enabling factor is the nearly isostructural K₂TiS₃ precedent [SS1/TS1]. The most critical limiting factor is controlling Rb/S volatility and air-sensitive sulfide stoichiometry during a long sealed-tube anneal [SS1/TS1, CS5].
