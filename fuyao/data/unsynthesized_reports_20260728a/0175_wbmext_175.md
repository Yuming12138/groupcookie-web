# SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

## F1 — Thermodynamic Stability

The direct target record reports E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom and a negative formation energy, which places Ba₂PI in the highest thermodynamic-stability category under the supplied rubric. [Target record] The analogues Ba₂PCl, Ba₃I₂Si, Ba₃GeI₂, Ca₂AsI, and Ba₅P₅I₃ are also reported with E_hull = 0.0 eV/atom, providing a consistent thermodynamic signal across CS, SS, and TS. [CS, SS, TS] The remaining uncertainty is that the target is identified as theory-only, with no experimental synthesis reported in the supplied validation data. [Target record]

## F2 — Route 1

Route 1 receives a moderate F2 score because the closest analogue requires inert handling, sealed ampoules, prolonged heating, grinding, and repeated tempering. [CS, SS, TS] The proposed maximum temperature is below 1000 °C, avoiding the highest temperature deduction, but iodine-containing charges still justify a sealed system and air-sensitive precursor penalty. [CS, SS, TS]; [Reasonable Inference]

## F2 — Route 2

Route 2 is penalized for vacuum/sealed operation and air-sensitive Ba-containing precursors, even though the reaction is nominally single-step. [CS, SS, TS] The Ba₃I₂Si precedent shows that the required temperature and sealed-ampoule operation are experimentally practical, but the use of a reactive halide-rich medium introduces additional containment and phase-control requirements. [CS, SS, TS]

## F2 — Route 3

Route 3 uses a preformed phosphide and volatile I₂, so it requires at least two synthetic stages and strict sealed handling. [SS, TS] Its moderate F2 score reflects the relatively accessible reaction temperature but also the need to prepare and preserve an air-sensitive Ba–P precursor before iodination. [SS, TS]

## F3 — Route 1

The precursor activity is limited because Ba metal and red phosphorus are relatively coarse elemental reactants, while BaI₂ is a conventional salt. [CS, SS, TS] Ion diffusion is favorable at the proposed temperature range, and structural reconstruction is especially favorable because Ba₂PCl has the same R-3m Caswellsilverite topology as the target. [SS] Repeated tempering reported for Ba₂PCl indicates that kinetics and homogenization may still be limiting. [CS, SS, TS]

## F3 — Route 2

The reactive-flux route benefits from the demonstrated Ba/BaI₂/p-block reaction at 1170 K and controlled cooling in sealed niobium ampoules. [CS, SS, TS] A mobile BaI₂-rich phase could improve diffusion, but this is an extrapolation because the provided Ba₃I₂Si record does not explicitly establish that BaI₂ is molten or that it acts as a true flux under the stated conditions. [CS, SS, TS]; [Reasonable Inference] The structural reconstruction score is lower than Route 1 because Ba₃I₂Si is orthorhombic rather than R-3m. [CS, SS, TS]

## F3 — Route 3

Route 3 has favorable precursor activity because a preformed Ba–P phase should react more readily with iodine than separate Ba and P powders. [SS, TS] The direct formation of Ba–P–I compounds at 1050 K strongly supports chemical feasibility, but the reported products are Ba₃P₃I₂/Ba₅P₅I₃-type phases rather than Ba₂PI. [SS, TS] Consequently, the main kinetic uncertainty is not elemental reaction but selective ordering into the target R-3m topology. [SS, TS]; [Reasonable Inference]

## F4 — Amorphization Tendency

The target has a simple three-element composition and a highly symmetric R-3m framework, which is favorable for crystallization by analogy with Ba₂PCl. [SS] The mixed edge- and corner-sharing connectivity is a supported amorphization-risk trigger, but no direct CS/SS/TS evidence is provided for the target unit-cell atom count or a multication radius mismatch; those checks are therefore neutral. [CS/SS/TS] The resulting score is 80/100, corresponding to low-to-medium amorphization risk.
