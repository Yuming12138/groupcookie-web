### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability**  
The target is reported at E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom, which places it in the 90–100 thermodynamic stability bin. [Target] Chemically, structurally, and thermodynamically similar fluorides are mostly on or very near the hull, with TS analogues all at E_hull = 0.0 eV/atom, so CS/SS/TS all provide useful positive thermodynamic signal rather than neutral evidence. [CS1–CS5, SS1–SS5, TS1–TS5] The main uncertainty is not the DFT hull value but whether the zero-hull cubic target is experimentally accessible before Si-F loss or competing fluoride formation occurs. [Target, Reasonable Inference]

**F2 — Route 1 Severity**  
Route 1 avoids >1000 °C and >5 GPa conditions because analogous sealed fluoride reactions occur around 850–860 °C in Pt ampoules/tubes. [CS1, CS5] The severity deductions come from volatile SiF4/SiF6 retention, dry sealed loading, and toxic/moisture-sensitive fluoride handling, with the SiF4-specific part labeled [Reasonable Inference]. [CS1, CS5, Reasonable Inference] Remaining uncertainty is that CS/SS/TS do not directly show CaSiF6 formation from SiF4, so the sealed-vapor pressure window is not validated. [Reasonable Inference]

**F3 — Route 1 Kinetics**  
Long holds of 96 h and 12 d in CaAlF5/CaFeF5 analogues indicate that dense fluoride solid-state kinetics can be slow. [CS1, CS5] SiF4 could improve reaction activity by providing a mobile Si-F species, but that improvement is a [Reasonable Inference] rather than a CS/SS/TS observation. [Reasonable Inference] The target requires ordering into an untilted corner-sharing CaF6–SiF6 network, so structural reconstruction is scored only moderate. [Target, CS1, CS5]

**F2 — Route 2 Severity**  
Flux synthesis operates below 1000 °C and at ambient or sealed-tube pressure, which keeps temperature and pressure severity moderate. [CS2, SS4, TS2, TS4] Deductions remain for multistep flux handling, volatile Si-F retention, and dry/toxic fluoride precautions. [CS2, TS4, Reasonable Inference] CS, SS, and TS all give useful flux-growth evidence, so none is neutral for this route’s condition-severity factor. [CS2, SS1, SS4, TS2, TS4]

**F3 — Route 2 Kinetics**
