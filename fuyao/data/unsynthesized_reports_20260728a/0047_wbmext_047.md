### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability**  
OsO3 receives a high F1 score because the target E_hull is 0.0000 eV/atom, and TS analogues include multiple Os/Ru oxides at E_hull = 0.0. [TS1, TS2, TS3, TS4] CS gives mixed thermodynamic support because some chemically similar oxides are near-stable, while WO5 is highly metastable. [CS1, CS4, CS5] The main uncertainty is that CS/SS/TS do not report experimental synthesis of binary OsO3, so direct experimental confirmation is neutral, score 50. [CS, SS, TS]

**F2 — Route 1**  
Route 1 is severe because CS/SS/TS high-pressure analogues require GPa pressure, sealed capsules, and controlled oxidizing chemistry. [CS1, CS3, SS2, TS2] The score is penalized mainly by >5 GPa pressure and multi-step capsule/quench processing. [CS1, CS3] The uncertainty is whether OsO3 needs the full 6–17 GPa window or could form at the lower ~3 GPa conditions used for KOs2O6. [CS1, CS3, SS2, TS2]

**F3 — Route 1**  
The kinetics are moderate-poor because OsO2 is a refractory oxide and the target requires oxygen insertion/reconstruction into an untilted ReO3 network. [CS1, SS1, TS1, Reasonable Inference] Existing OsO6 octahedra in analogues help structural transferability, but high-pressure analogues imply substantial activation barriers. [CS1, CS3, SS2, TS2] No CS/SS/TS source directly proves topotactic OsO2 → OsO3 conversion, so that sub-signal is neutral, score 50. [CS, SS, TS]

**F2 — Route 2**  
Route 2 avoids GPa pressure and >1000 °C temperatures, so its condition severity is lower than Route 1. [SS1, TS1] It is still penalized by sealed-tube oxygen control, multi-step grinding/heating, and toxic osmium chemistry. [SS1, SS3, SS5, TS1, TS4] CS gives insufficient route-specific evidence for sealed-tube OsO3, so CS is neutral, score 50, for this route. [CS]

**F3 — Route 2**  
The kinetic score is limited by OsO2’s low expected diffusivity and by the need to reorganize from OsO2-like motifs into a binary ReO3-type lattice. [SS1, SS5, TS1, TS4, Reasonable Inference] AgO-derived oxygen improves oxidizing activity and is directly supported by RbOs2O6 synthesis. [SS1, TS1] Repeated regrinding/reheating in Cd2Os2O7 synthesis suggests that dense osmium oxide reactions may need extended cycling. [SS5, TS4]

**F2 — Route 3**  
Route 3 avoids extreme GPa pressure and very high temperatures, but it uses sealed oxidizing hydrothermal/autoclave conditions. [CS4, SS4, TS3] The route is penalized for possible OsO4 formation in oxidizing aqueous environments and for multi-step dissolution/crystallization/annealing. [SS3, SS5, TS4, Reasonable Inference] Direct OsO3 hydrothermal evidence is absent from CS/SS/TS, so direct-source support is neutral, score 50. [CS, SS, TS]

**F3 — Route 3**  
Hydrothermal/peroxo W analogues support active low-temperature oxide chemistry, which improves precursor activity. [CS4, CS5] The kinetic uncertainty is high because the only hydrothermal structural analogue is a complex Ba–Ru oxide, not binary OsO3. [SS4, TS3] Complete reconstruction from solution into an anhydrous ReO3-type osmium oxide is therefore a substantial extrapolation. [Reasonable Inference]

**F4 — Amorphization Tendency**

[Target] SS/TS analogues show crystalline OsO6 frameworks with similar Os–O distances, supporting low amorphization risk for an octahedral network. ### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

OsO3 should be treated as a **predicted ground-state candidate**, not an experimentally validated phase, because the target E_hull is 0.0000 eV/atom while CS/SS/TS provide no direct experimental OsO3 synthesis. [Target, CS, SS, TS] The analogues strongly support crystalline OsO6 networks and high-valent osmium oxide chemistry, especially RbOs2O6, Hg2Os2O7, and Cd2Os2O7. [SS1, SS3, SS5, TS1, TS4] The most important enabling factor is the simple corner-sharing OsO6 framework, which resembles several Os-containing oxide frameworks. [SS1, SS3, SS5, TS1, TS4] The most important limiting factor is oxygen-potential control: Os must be oxidized beyond OsO2 but not lost as volatile OsO4. [SS1, SS3, SS5, TS1, TS4, Reasonable Inference] Overall, synthesis is plausible but specialized, with sealed elevated-O2 solid-state synthesis as the best first attempt. [SS1, TS1, SS3, SS5, TS4]
