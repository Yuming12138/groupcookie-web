### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability**  
The target E_hull is 0.0000 eV/atom, which places it in the 90–100 scoring bin, and TS analogues Y₂NCl₃, Nd₂NCl₃, Pr₂NCl₃, and Gd₂NCl₃ also report E_hull = 0.0 eV/atom. Evidence: Target, TS1–4. CS and SS also contain multiple synthesized Ln₂NCl₃ analogues with E_hull = 0.0, so the thermodynamic signal is consistently favorable. Evidence: CS1–4, SS1–4. Remaining uncertainty is that the target is theory-only and no experimental Sm₂NCl₃ synthesis is identified in the supplied target validation. Evidence: Target.

**F2 — Route 1**  
Route 1 avoids >1000 °C and high pressure, but it requires sealed ampoule handling, dry N₂, moisture-sensitive precursors, and precursor preparation. Evidence: CS1, SS1, TS1. The severity score is therefore limited by handling complexity rather than temperature. Evidence: CS1, SS1, TS1.

**F2 — Route 2**  
Route 2 uses the proven 850 °C azide route for La–Nd analogues, so temperature severity is moderate. Evidence: CS2–4, SS2–3, TS2–3. The deductions come from sealed-tube pressure risk, NaN₃ toxicity, anhydrous MCl₃ handling, and multi-step precursor preparation. Evidence: CS2–4; hazard interpretation is [Reasonable Inference].

**F2 — Route 3**  
Route 3 has moderate temperature but requires evacuated sealed ampoules, chloride flux containment, and controlled cooling. Evidence: CS4, CS5. SS and TS provide insufficient flux-specific evidence for Sm₂NCl₃, so their route-specific flux signal is neutral 50. Evidence: SS1–5, TS1–5.

**F2 — Route 4**  
Route 4 remains below 1000 °C but uses welded Nb/Ta ampoules, glovebox handling, staged annealing, and moisture-sensitive rare-earth reagents. Evidence: SS1, TS1. These requirements make the route challenging but not extreme because no GPa pressure or >1000 °C synthesis is supported. Evidence: SS1, TS1.

**F3 — Route 1**  
Direct SmN + SmCl₃ has strong precursor logic because GdN + GdCl₃ gives complete conversion to Gd₂NCl₃. Evidence: CS1. The kinetic uncertainty is polymorph selection, because the Gd analogue is Pbcn while the target is C2/m. Evidence: CS1, Target, SS1, TS1.

**F3 — Route 2**  
NaN₃ provides a reactive nitrogen source and has precedent for Ln₂NCl₃ formation from MCl₃ + M + NaN₃. Evidence: CS2–4, SS2–3, TS2–3. The lower structural-reconstruction score reflects that the reported La–Nd products are not the same C2/m topology as the target. Evidence: CS2–4, Target.

**F3 — Route 3**  
Flux growth should improve ion mobility and crystal growth because La₂NCl₃ and Sm₂SCl₄ analogues were grown using NaCl or SmCl₃ flux at 850 °C. Evidence: CS4, CS5. The uncertainty is that no SS/TS flux analogue directly demonstrates C2/m Sm₂NCl₃, so SS/TS are neutral 50 for flux-specific polymorph kinetics. Evidence: SS1–5, TS1–5.
