### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability**  
The target is calculated exactly on the hull, so it receives a high F1 score of 96. (Target data) CS/SS/TS analogues also show strong thermodynamic support because multiple close analogues have E_hull = 0 or near-zero values, including Cd2Os2O7, Tl2Ru2O7, Cd2Ru2O7, NaIrO3, and TS-listed oxides. (CS-1, CS-2, SS-1, SS-2, TS-1, TS-2) The main uncertainty is that CS/SS/TS provide insufficient direct experimental AgIrO4 synthesis evidence, so direct synthesis precedent is neutral 50.

**F2 — Condition Severity**  
Route 1 is penalized for controlled capsule chemistry, multistep handling, and oxidizer/reactive precursor handling, but the base NaIrO3-like route stays just below the >5 GPa penalty at 4.5 GPa. (SS-2, TS-2) Route 2 is favored because 650–700°C and 1500 atm–3 kbar hydrothermal conditions avoid GPa pressure while still preserving an oxidizing sealed environment. (SS-1, TS-1, TS-3) Route 3 has moderate severity because high oxygen pressure is specialized but below GPa scale, while Route 4 is operationally simple but still needs O2/oxidizer control and repeated processing. (TS-4, CS-4, SS-5, TS-5)

**F3 — Reaction Kinetics**  
Route 1 has moderate kinetics because high-pressure oxidizers can form Ir oxides at 800°C, but Ir/IrO2 persistence remains a risk. (SS-2, TS-2) Route 2 scores best because water/oxidizer hydrothermal conditions plausibly improve transport and reaction kinetics relative to dry solid-state diffusion. [Reasonable Inference] (SS-1, TS-1, TS-3) Route 3 is limited by oxide diffusion despite high oxygen pressure, and Route 4 is most limited because conventional solid-state analogues require long holds/regrinding and are less structurally relevant. [Reasonable Inference] (TS-4, CS-4, SS-5, TS-5)

**F4 — Amorphization Tendency**  
The target triggers only the cation-size-mismatch check because AgO5 and IrO6 imply substantially different cation environments. [Reasonable Inference] (Target data) The target does not trigger the >40 atom check or the vertex+edge+face coexistence check because the supplied structure is a small AgIrO4 P-1 framework with corner and edge sharing but no reported face sharing. (Target data) CS/SS/TS provide insufficient measured amorphization evidence, so empirical source evidence is neutral 50.
