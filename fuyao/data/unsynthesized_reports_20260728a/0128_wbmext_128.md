### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability**  
SmMoN₃ receives a high F1 because the supplied target E_hull is 0.0000 eV/atom, which is in the strongest stability band. (Target data) Chemically similar and thermodynamically similar nitrides are mostly hull-stable, especially CS1–CS4 and TS1–TS5. (CS1–CS4, TS1–TS5) The main uncertainty is that SS1 is a structurally similar but metastable W nitride at 0.340 eV/atom, so structural similarity alone is not uniformly stabilizing. (SS1)

**F2/F3 — Route 1: Li₃N / Ga Flux Growth**  
Route 1 has moderate-to-harsh F2 severity because close analogues require 900–1030 °C Li₃N flux or up to 1600 °C Ga flux, sealed ampoules, and inert handling. (CS1, CS3, CS4) Its F3 is moderate because Li₃N improves nitrogen activity and flux transport, but Mo is refractory and the target is a unique prototype, so ordering is not guaranteed. (CS1, CS3, CS4; Target data; [Reasonable Inference]) Remaining uncertainty is the correct Mo nitrogen chemical potential needed to form MoN₅ units rather than binary Mo nitrides. (CS1, CS3; [Reasonable Inference])

**F2/F3 — Route 2: Na/Ga/NaN₃ Reactive Flux**  
Route 2 has a similar F2 score to Route 1 because its lower 900 °C temperature is offset by Na volatility, NaN₃ hazard, sealed Nb-tube handling, and vacuum post-treatment. (CS2, TS5) Its F3 is slightly lower because the direct analogue La₃Cr₂N₆ formed only in small amounts with several competing phases, indicating a narrow kinetic/phase window. (CS2, TS5) SS gives insufficient evidence for this exact Na/Ga/NaN₃ route and is treated as neutral 50. (insufficient evidence from SS)

**F2/F3 — Route 3: Solid-State Nitride Sintering**  
Route 3 is condition-severe because SS/TS analogues require high temperature, long durations, sealed crucibles, controlled atmospheres, and air-sensitive binary nitrides. (SS2, SS3, SS5, TS1, TS2, TS4) Its F3 is weak because dense nitride precursors and refractory Mo imply slow diffusion, and the target unique prototype likely requires substantial reconstruction. (SS2, SS3, SS5; Target data; [Reasonable Inference]) The uncertainty is whether Li₃N can act sufficiently as a mineralizer rather than simply volatilizing or forming impurities. (SS3, TS2)

**F2/F3 — Route 4: HPHT Nitridation**  
Route 4 is condition-severe because the only direct HPHT analogue uses 5 GPa and high temperature in a multi-anvil press. (SS1) Its F3 is moderate-low because pressure can accelerate nitride formation, but CS and TS provide insufficient evidence for high-pressure ternary Sm–Mo nitride synthesis and are neutral 50. (SS1; insufficient evidence from CS; insufficient evidence from TS) The key uncertainty is whether HPHT stabilizes the desired SmMoN₃ framework or instead produces binary SmN/Mo nitride assemblages. (SS1; [Reasonable Inference])

**F4 — Amorphization Tendency**
