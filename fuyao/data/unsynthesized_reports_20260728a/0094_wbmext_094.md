### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability**  
The target has E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom, placing it in the 90–100 stability bin, and multiple CS/SS/TS analogues are also on hull [CS#2–3, SS#2–4, TS#1–5]. The near-hull Na5(CN2)3 analogue at 0.049 eV/atom further supports stable Na–C–N chemistry [CS#1, SS#1]. The uncertainty is structural validation: TS#5 shows Na2(CN2) as a reagent, but not the exact WBM I4/mmm polymorph [TS#5].

**F2 — Route 1**  
Route 1 avoids high pressure and >1000 °C conditions, matching the 600 °C ambient-Ar Li2CN2 synthesis [CS#2, SS#3, TS#2]. The penalties come from controlled Ar handling, multi-step precursor preparation, and air-sensitive alkali nitride/acetylide chemistry [Reasonable Inference from CS#2/SS#3/TS#2]. Overall severity is moderate rather than harsh [CS#2, SS#3, TS#2].

**F3 — Route 1**  
Kinetics are favorable because Li2CN2 is structurally identical in space group and cyanamide geometry, making reconstruction into Na2CN2 comparatively direct [CS#2, SS#3, TS#2]. Na-rich chemistry should aid cation transport [Reasonable Inference from CS#2/SS#3/TS#2]. The main kinetic uncertainty is removal or accommodation of excess Na metal in the Na analogue [Reasonable Inference].

**F2 — Route 2**  
Route 2 uses low/moderate temperatures but needs vacuum or Ar and atmosphere-sensitive Na(HNCN), as explicitly noted for Na(HNCN) chemistry [CS#1, SS#1]. Gas evolution and extra deprotonation steps add severity [Reasonable Inference from SS#4/TS#3]. The route is chemically close but less conditionally simple than Route 1 [CS#1, SS#1].

**F3 — Route 2**  
Na(HNCN) is a highly active precursor for sodium cyanamide-related phases [CS#1, SS#1]. However, the reported product is Na5(CN2)3/Na5H(CN2)3, so structural and stoichiometric reconstruction to Na2CN2 is uncertain [CS#1, SS#1]. This lowers the structural-reconstruction component relative to Route 1 [CS#1, SS#1].

**F2 — Route 3**  
Route 3 is penalized by pressurized N2/autoclave conditions, molten Na flux, air-sensitive reagents, and long multi-step processing [CS#3, SS#2, TS#1]. It does not exceed the rubric’s >5 GPa threshold unless adapted to a GPa route, but the equipment burden remains high [TS#4]. This makes it the most challenging route [CS#3, SS#2, TS#1, TS#4].

**F3 — Route 3**  
Molten Na flux should improve transport, but graphite/N2 incorporation and conversion to the target structure are slower and more reconstructive [CS#3, SS#2, TS#1]. The K2CN2 analogue requires 100 h and has a different monoclinic structure, so kinetics are only moderate [CS#3, SS#2, TS#1]. This route is better as a fallback if ambient solid-state synthesis fails [Reasonable Inference].

**F2 — Route 4**  
Route 4 benefits from low temperatures and the direct TS#5 indication that Na2(CN2) can be handled as a reagent [TS#5]. It still requires dry/vacuum handling, reactive sodium bases, and multiple isolation/annealing steps [Reasonable Inference from TS#5 and SS#4/TS#3]. Insufficient evidence from CS for solution deprotonation is treated as neutral source score = 50.

**F3 — Route 4**  
Salt/metathesis chemistry should have good precursor activity and short diffusion distances [Reasonable Inference from TS#5]. The weak point is crystallization of the exact target polymorph, because TS#5 uses Na2(CN2) to make ZnCN2 rather than proving Na2CN2 crystal growth [TS#5]. Insufficient evidence from SS for direct solution crystallization is treated as neutral source score = 50.

**F4 — Amorphization Tendency**  
The target is single-cation and structurally simple by comparison to Li2CN2, so cation-size disorder and large-cell amorphization risks are low [CS#2, SS#3, TS#2]. Mixed edge/corner NaN4 connectivity triggers one amorphization check, giving low–medium risk [CS#2, SS#3–4, TS#2–3]. The remaining uncertainty is that CS/SS/TS do not provide the exact target unit-cell atom count, so that source signal is neutral 50.

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

Na2CN2 is likely a ground-state rather than strongly metastable phase because the target E_hull is 0.0000 eV/atom and several close cyanamide/nitridoborate analogues are also on hull [CS#2–3, SS#2–4, TS#1–5]. The strongest synthesis support is the isostructural Li2CN2 analogue, which has the same I4/mmm framework and is experimentally made by a moderate solid-state route [CS#2, SS#3, TS#2]. TS#5 also supports formula-level feasibility because Na2(CN2) is used directly as a reagent, but that does not prove the exact WBM target polymorph [TS#5]. The key enabling factor is the robust linear CN2 unit shared across Li2CN2, K2CN2, Na5(CN2)3, and ZnCN2 analogues [CS#1–3, SS#1–3, TS#1–2/TS#5]. The key limiting factor is air-sensitive sodium precursor handling and avoidance of competing Na-rich cyanamide/dicyanamide phases [CS#1, SS#1, SS#5].
