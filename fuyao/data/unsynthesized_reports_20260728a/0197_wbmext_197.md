### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability**  
The supplied target E_hull is 0.0000 eV/atom, so the target scores near the top of the thermodynamic rubric. Target data. CS/SS/TS support this score because the closest YHSe and HoHSe P-6m2 analogues are also reported with E_hull = 0.0 eV/atom. CS#1, SS#1, SS#2, TS#1, TS#2. Remaining uncertainty is experimental, not thermodynamic, because the target is stated to be theory-only with no identified synthesis. Target data.

**F2 — Route 1**  
Route 1 is penalized for sealed Ta/vacuum-controlled processing, volatile Se containment, multistep hydride/flux handling, and toxic/air-sensitive reagents. CS#1, SS#2, TS#2; [Reasonable Inference]. It avoids >1000 °C and high pressure, which keeps severity moderate rather than extreme. CS#1, SS#2, TS#2.

**F3 — Route 1**  
Kinetics are favorable relative to bulk solid-state reaction because alkali chloride flux is explicitly reported to secure complete and fast reactions and single crystals in close hydride selenide analogues. SS#2, TS#2. Structural reconstruction is the strongest kinetic advantage because YHSe/HoHSe share the same P-6m2 topology, but H-content sensitivity remains a phase-control penalty. CS#1, SS#1, SS#2, TS#1, TS#2.

**F2 — Route 2**  
Route 2 has similar atmosphere and sealing penalties to Route 1 because ScHCl and Tb hydride-halide analogues require sealed Ta/Nb or Ta ampoules. CS#2, SS#3, TS#3. It avoids flux handling but also loses the flux-assisted reaction advantage, so condition severity remains challenging rather than easy. CS#2, SS#3, TS#3; [Reasonable Inference].

**F3 — Route 2**  
Bulk solid-state diffusion is less favorable because the closest no-flux analogues are hydride halides rather than the same P-6m2 hydride selenide structure. CS#2, SS#3, TS#3. The route is still plausible because sealed hydride chemistry at 750–900 °C is demonstrated for Sc-containing and rare-earth hydride materials. CS#2, SS#3, TS#3.

**F2 — Route 3**  
Route 3 has lower thermal severity because the mechanochemical analogue operates at room temperature with only mild H2 pressure. SS#5, TS#4. It still requires controlled Ar/H2 handling and air-sensitive/toxic salt reagents, so the severity score is not high. SS#5, TS#4; [Reasonable Inference].

**F3 — Route 3**  
Metathesis should give high local reactivity and avoid long cation diffusion, but no CS/SS/TS source demonstrates ordered ScHSe formation by this method. SS#5, TS#4; insufficient evidence from CS. The low yield and impurity formation in the rare-earth hydride metathesis analogue strongly penalize structural reconstruction and phase purity. SS#5, TS#4.

**F4 — Amorphization Tendency**  
Only the mixed face/edge/corner-sharing polyhedral criterion is triggered, because the target and close analogues contain mixed SeM6 connectivity. CS#1, SS#1, SS#2, TS#1, TS#2. The single-cation chemistry and small formula reduce amorphization risk, but CS/SS/TS do not independently prove the target unit-cell atom count or radius mismatch, so those source signals are neutral 50. Target data; insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS.

ScHSe is likely a ground-state or near-ground-state phase because the supplied target E_hull is 0.0000 eV/atom and the closest P-6m2 YHSe/HoHSe analogues are also zero-hull phases.
