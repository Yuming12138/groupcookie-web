| # | Route | Challenge | Severity | Mitigation Strategy |
|:--:|:--:|---|:--:|---|
| 1 | All | Thallium precursor/product toxicity | High | Use closed handling, fume hood, secondary containment, and dedicated Tl waste; all relevant routes use Tl salts or Tl compounds. **CS1–CS4; SS1–SS4; TS1–TS4; [Reasonable Inference]** |
| 2 | Route 2 / Route 4 | Tl loss or Tl-rich melt volatility at high temperature | High | Use slight Tl2CO3 excess, covered/lidded crucible, lower T, and short holds; Tl2TiO3 used 5% excess Tl2CO3. **CS4; SS1; TS1; [Reasonable Inference]** |
| 3 | Route 2 / Route 4 | Competing Tl(III) oxide or oxidation-state drift | Medium | Use N2 atmosphere for high-T work, following Tl4V2O7 synthesis that used N2 to avoid thallium(III) oxide. **CS2; SS3; TS3** |
| 4 | Route 1 | Nb(V) soluble precursor/speciation mismatch | Medium–High | Prepare fresh alkaline niobate/peroxoniobate and maintain pH ≈13; direct Nb evidence is insufficient from CS/SS/TS and is neutral 50. **CS1; SS2; TS2; insufficient Nb-aqueous evidence from CS/SS/TS; [Reasonable Inference]** |
| 5 | Route 2 | Slow ordering from Nb2O5 | Medium | Use fine Nb2O5, intimate grinding, regrinding, and repeated anneals; carbonate/oxide analogues validate the general approach but not Nb kinetics. **CS2; CS3; CS4; SS1; SS3; SS4; TS1; TS3; TS4; [Reasonable Inference]** |
| 6 | Route 1 / Route 4 | Crystal growth difficulty | Medium | Start with powder synthesis, then use slow cooling or flux only after phase formation; Tl3VO4 precipitation was homogeneous but single-crystal attempts were difficult. **CS1; SS2; TS2; [Reasonable Inference]** |
| 7 | Route 3 | Hydrothermal competing hydrated or alkali niobate phases | Medium | Tune pH, Tl:Nb ratio, fill fraction, and cooling rate; hydrothermal analogues show phase-selective crystallization but no direct NbTl3O4 case. **CS5; SS5; insufficient evidence from TS; [Reasonable Inference]** |
| 8 | Route 4 | Flux contamination and crucible compatibility | Medium | Prefer self-flux/Tl-rich growth after powder synthesis and use Pt/alumina only after compatibility checks; Pt/alumina are reported in high-T analogues. **CS3; SS4; TS4; TS5; [Reasonable Inference]** |
