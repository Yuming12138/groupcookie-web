### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability**

Similar CS/SS/TS nitrides include multiple E_hull = 0 analogues, especially La₃Ta₂N₆, La₃Nb₂N₆, La₃Cr₂N₆, and several TS entries. (Target data; CS1, CS3, SS1, SS2, TS1, TS2, TS3) The remaining uncertainty is that WBM stability is theoretical and the dataset does not identify experimental SmReN₃ synthesis. (Target data)

**F2/F3 — Route 1**  
Route 1 is severe because Li₃N flux analogues use sealed refractory ampoules, inert handling, ~1030 °C heating, and post-reaction flux removal. (CS1, CS3, SS2, TS2) Its kinetics are the best among routes because Li₃N provides reactive nitrogen and flux transport, but Re’s refractory nature and the target’s different R-3c ReN₆ topology remain [Reasonable Inference] kinetic risks. (CS1, CS3, TS3; Target data)

**F2/F3 — Route 2**

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

SmReN₃ is likely a thermodynamic ground-state candidate because the reported E_hull is 0.0000 eV/atom, but it remains theory-only with no experimental synthesis identified. (Target data) The best synthesis support comes from rare-earth transition-metal nitrides made by Li₃N/Ga flux and Na/Ga flux routes, especially La₃Ta₂N₆, La₃Nb₂N₆, and La₃Cr₂N₆. (CS1, CS3, SS1, SS2, TS1, TS2, TS3) The most important enabling factor is the availability of reactive nitride flux chemistry that can supply high nitrogen activity and improve transport below refractory-metal melting points. (CS1, CS3, SS1, TS1, TS2) The main limiting factor is phase control: the closest La₃Cr₂N₆ analogue had competing phases and low-yield crystals, and Re-specific chemistry is not directly demonstrated in CS/SS/TS. (SS1, TS1; [Reasonable Inference]) Overall, I would rate SmReN₃ as **moderately synthesizable but experimentally challenging**. (CS1, CS3, SS1, TS1, TS2)
