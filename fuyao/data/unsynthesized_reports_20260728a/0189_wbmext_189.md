This gives a moderate-good kinetic score of 74 [CS1, CS5, SS1, TS1].

MnTlSe₂ is most likely a **ground-state or near-ground-state phase** because the target is reported at 0.0000 eV/atom above hull, although it remains theory-only in the supplied WBM validation [Target]. CS/SS/TS analogues support practical synthesis because Tl–Se ternaries, Mn–Se octahedral phases, and related selenides are made by sealed-tube, solid-state, Bridgman, flux, and CVT methods [CS2–CS5, SS1–SS2, TS1–TS5]. The most important enabling factor is the strong thermodynamic signal combined with known sealed-ampoule Tl–Se and Mn–Se chemistry [Target, CS5, SS1, TS1]. The main limiting factor is not thermodynamics but controlling toxic/volatile Tl and Se while avoiding competing binary or halide/flux side phases [CS1, CS5, TS1, TS4]. Overall, I would attempt Route 1 first for phase-pure powder and only then use Route 2 or Route 4 for larger crystals [CS2, CS3, SS1, TS1; Reasonable Inference].

### SECTION 7 — Synthesis Challenges

| # | Route | Challenge | Severity | Mitigation Strategy |
|:--:|:--:|:--|:--:|:--|
| 1 | All | Tl toxicity and volatility during loading/heating | High | Use glovebox loading, sealed ampoules, secondary containment, and small batches [CS1, CS5, TS1]. |
| 2 | All | Selenium vapor pressure and possible Se loss | High | Use staged low-temperature prereaction and sealed evacuated tubes; slight Se excess may be screened [SS1, CS5; Reasonable Inference]. |
| 3 | Route 1 | Slow ordering / incomplete reaction | Medium | Use binary precursors, regrinding, repeated anneals, and slow cooling as in Mn–Se and Tl–Se analogues [SS1, CS1, TS1]. |
| 4 | Route 1 | Competing MnSe, TlSe/Tl₂Se, or Se-rich phases | Medium | Check phase purity after each anneal and adjust Tl/Se chemical potential [TS1, SS1; Reasonable Inference]. |
| 5 | Route 2 | Supercooling or incongruent melting | High | Use pre-reacted charge, controlled Bridgman gradient, slow translation, and composition screening [CS2, CS3]. |
| 6 | Route 3 | Flux inclusions and mixed-phase products | High | Use slow cooling, mechanical separation, solvent flux removal, and treat flux as exploratory only [TS4]. |
| 7 | Route 4 | Differential vapor transport of Tl/Mn/Se | High | Start from pre-synthesized charge and screen low transport-agent loadings [TS2; Reasonable Inference]. |
