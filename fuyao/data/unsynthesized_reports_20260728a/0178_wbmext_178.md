**F2 — Route 1**

[CS1, CS5] The main severity deduction is HF/SnF4 hazard rather than thermal or pressure complexity. [CS5, Reasonable Inference] SS and TS provide insufficient evidence for solution processing and are treated as neutral 50 for this route. [SS, TS]

**F3 — Route 1**  
Solution precursors should be highly active because the target ions can be assembled from dissolved Sr2+ and [SnF6]2− species. [CS5, Reasonable Inference] Kinetics are favorable because diffusion occurs in solution, but structural uncertainty remains because the closest CS analogues are trigonal BaF12 frameworks rather than the target cubic SrF6/SnF6 framework. [CS1, CS5, Target] This is why F3 is high but not near-perfect. [CS1, CS5, Reasonable Inference]

The sealed-tube route avoids >1000 °C conditions but requires inert/sealed Au or Pt tubes, as shown for Cs2SnF6 and related fluoride frameworks. [SS3, SS4, TS1, TS3] SnF4 volatility and moisture sensitivity justify deductions for sealed volatile precursor and hazardous precursor handling. [CS2] The route receives deductions for reactive atmosphere, multi-step gas treatment, and toxic precursor handling. [CS2] SS and TS provide insufficient evidence for F2 direct fluorination and are neutral 50 for this route. [SS, TS]

F2 provides high chemical activity, and CaPdF6 demonstrates that ABF6 fluorides can be made by direct fluorination at low temperature. [CS2, Reasonable Inference] Thus F3 is moderate-high but lower than solution or flux routes. [CS2, Reasonable Inference]

**F2 — Route 4**
