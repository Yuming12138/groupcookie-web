### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability**  
The target has E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom, which places it in the highest stability bin. [Target data] The analogous RZnSn compounds in CS/SS/TS also report E_hull = 0.0 eV/atom, supporting the interpretation that the RZnSn structure is thermodynamically plausible. [CS#1–CS#4, SS#1–SS#4, TS#1–TS#4] Remaining uncertainty comes from the target being a theory-only WBM candidate with no experimental synthesis identified. [Target data]

**F2/F3 — Route 1**  
Route 1 is penalized for >1000 °C melting, controlled Ar/vacuum handling, Zn containment, and multiple processing steps. [CS#3, CS#4, SS#1, TS#1] Its kinetics score is highest because induction melting, remelting, shaking, and long annealing directly address homogenization and ordering in the same P6₃/mmc RZnSn family. [CS#3, SS#1, TS#1]

**F2/F3 — Route 2**  
Route 2 has milder peak temperature than Route 1 but still requires evacuated ampoules, Zn containment, and long multi-step annealing. [CS#1, SS#2, TS#2] Its kinetics are lower than Route 1 because pellet sintering is less homogenizing than melt/remelt processing, even though TbZnSn is an excellent structural analogue. [CS#1, SS#2, TS#2]

**F2/F3 — Route 3**  
Route 3 has moderate condition severity because arc-melting and evacuated annealing are standard for intermetallics, but Zn loss is a route-specific concern. [CS#2; Reasonable Inference] Its kinetics are helped by melt formation, but structural confidence is reduced because DyCuSn is Cu-based and P6₃mc rather than Zn-based P6₃/mmc. [CS#2] Insufficient evidence from SS and TS for this arc-melting route is treated as neutral 50 for those source signals.

**F2/F3 — Route 4**  
Route 4 has similar severity to Route 1 except with a lower maximum temperature near 1047 °C and no explicit induction remelting. [SS#5, TS#5] Its kinetics are moderate because the sealed Ta-tube route works for Zn/Sn intermetallics, but EuZnSn is orthorhombic and therefore less structurally transferable. [SS#5, TS#5]

**F2/F3 — Route 5**  
Route 5 is heavily penalized for 8 GPa pressure, >1000 °C heating, pre-synthesis of a ternary precursor, and complex HPHT processing.
