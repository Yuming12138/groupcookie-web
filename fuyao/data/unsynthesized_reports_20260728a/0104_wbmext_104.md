CeNBr is likely a DFT ground-state candidate because the supplied target hull is 0.0000 eV/atom, but it remains experimentally unvalidated in the supplied dataset [Target]. The strongest support comes from exact Matlockite ThNBr/ThNCl analogues and chemically close HfNBr/ZrNBr nitride bromides [SS2; TS2; TS5; CS1; CS2; CS4]. The most important enabling factor is the existence of stable layered MNBr/MNX compounds made under accessible thermal conditions [CS1; SS2; TS2; TS5]. The most important limiting factor is Ce-specific redox and phase control, because no source reports direct CeNBr synthesis and Ce must be stabilized in a nitride-bromide environment [Target; Reasonable Inference]. Overall, the target is moderately high feasibility, with the sealed-tube NH4Br route recommended first and ammonolysis/flux routes as backup pathways [CS1; SS2; TS2; SS1; TS1; Reasonable Inference].
