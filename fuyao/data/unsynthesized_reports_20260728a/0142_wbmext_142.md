### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

TbMoN3 is **thermodynamically promising** because the target E_hull is 0.0000 eV/atom and thermodynamic neighbors are predominantly ground-state nitrides. (Target; TS1; TS2; TS3; TS4; TS5) It should be treated as **experimentally unproven** because the target is a theory-only WBM candidate with a unique relaxed prototype and no reported synthesis in the supplied target block. (Target) The CS/SS/TS analogues most strongly support a sealed flux-growth strategy, especially rare-earth transition-metal nitrides made from Ln/T metals with Li3N, Ga, Na, or NaN3. (CS1; CS2; CS3; SS1; SS2; TS1; TS2) The key enabling factor is high nitrogen chemical potential plus flux-assisted transport, which directly addresses nitride formation and refractory Mo diffusion. (CS1; CS2; CS3; [Reasonable Inference]) The key limiting factor is phase control, because La3Cr2N6 and MnWN2 analogues show small yields, impurity phases, and sensitivity to reaction conditions. (CS2; CS5)
