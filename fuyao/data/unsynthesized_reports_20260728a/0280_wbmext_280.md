### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability**  
The target’s E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom places it in the 90–100 thermodynamic score band from the required rubric, so 98/100 is assigned from target data. Multiple CS/SS/TS analogues are also hull-stable, especially LiPdH and Li₄RhH₄, which supports the plausibility of stable alkali/alkaline-earth–late-transition-metal hydrides. Evidence: target data; CS-1; SS-1; TS-1; TS-5. Remaining uncertainty is that WBM theory-only status does not prove experimental isolation, and CS/SS/TS do not directly report MgRhH.

**F2 — Route 1**  
Route 1 avoids >1000 °C and GPa pressure, but it requires H₂ pressure and air-sensitive hydride handling, giving deductions for controlled atmosphere, sealed/volatile Mg chemistry, and air-sensitive precursor use. Evidence for moderate H₂ solid-state routes comes from LiPdH at 340 °C/10 bar H₂ and NaPd₃H₂ at 280 °C/5 MPa H₂. Evidence: CS-1, SS-2, TS-1. The main uncertainty is whether MgH₂ supplies Mg and H cleanly without Mg metal loss or MgH₂ decomposition side products [Reasonable Inference].

**F3 — Route 1**  
Hydride-assisted kinetics are favorable relative to direct Mg/Rh metal diffusion because LiH + Pd/Rh analogues react at moderate temperatures, but Rh remains a refractory, slow-diffusing metal [Reasonable Inference]. Evidence: CS-1, TS-5. Structural reconstruction is only moderate because P4/mmm LiPdH is structurally close, while replacing Li/Pd with Mg/Rh changes coordination and charge balance [Reasonable Inference]. Evidence: SS-1, TS-1; target data.

**F2 — Route 2**  
Route 2 uses no extreme temperature by the rubric, but H₂ autoclave operation and Mg retention require controlled/sealed conditions. Evidence: SS-4. The direct Mg₂RuH₄ precedent shows feasibility under 20–160 bar H₂/D₂, but the same evidence shows long holds or high pressure may be needed. Evidence: SS-4. TS gives insufficient direct evidence for Mg + Rh high-pressure hydrogenation, so TS is neutral 50 for this route-specific condition assessment.

**F3 — Route 2**  
Direct Mg + Rh hydrogenation has slower kinetics because cation interdiffusion must occur between elemental Mg and refractory Rh [Reasonable Inference]. The Mg₂RuH₄ analogue supports that Mg–late-transition-metal hydrides can form, but also reports residual educts in at least one sample, implying phase-purity limitations. Evidence: SS-4. This justifies a lower F3 than Route 1.

**F2 — Route 3**  
Route 3 is penalized for multi-step processing, controlled atmospheres, and Mg volatility because it requires alloying plus hydrogenation. Evidence: CS-2, CS-4, SS-5, TS-3. Intermetallic hydrogenation itself is moderate, as SrAl₂H₂ and Ba(GaH)₂-type analogues use ~50 bar H₂ at 100–400 °C. Evidence: CS-2, CS-4. The uncertainty is that no source directly demonstrates MgRh intermetallic hydrogenating to MgRhH.

**F3 — Route 3**  
Pre-alloying should improve cation mixing before H insertion [Reasonable Inference], and intermetallic hydrogenation analogues show low-to-moderate-temperature hydrogen uptake. Evidence: CS-2, CS-4. However, Mg–Rh solid-state synthesis can show impurities and optimization needs, so precursor phase purity is a kinetic and thermodynamic bottleneck. Evidence: SS-5, TS-3. Insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS exists for exact MgRh → MgRhH topotactic conversion, so that subcomponent is neutral 50.

**F4 — Amorphization Tendency**  
Only one amorphization trigger is assigned: Mg/Rh cation size and bonding mismatch is a [Reasonable Inference], while the target’s small P4/mmm cell and absence of face-sharing in the target polyhedral description avoid C2 and C3. Evidence: target data; SS-1; TS-1. CS/SS/TS do not provide direct ionic radii, so they are neutral 50 for the numerical C1 radius criterion. The crystalline success of several related hydrides reduces concern that the target must amorphize. Evidence: CS-1, SS-1, TS-1, TS-5.

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

MgRhH is best treated as a likely ground-state candidate because its target E_hull is 0.0000 eV/atom, although it remains theory-only without reported experimental synthesis. Evidence: target data. The analogue support is strong: LiPdH is chemically, structurally, and thermodynamically close and P4/mmm, Mg₂RuH₄ supports Mg–late-transition-metal hydride chemistry, and Li₄RhH₄ supports Rh–H bonding near 1.70 Å. Evidence: CS-1, SS-1, TS-1, SS-4, TS-5. The most critical enabling factor is maintaining high hydrogen chemical potential while using reactive hydride or high-pressure H₂ routes. Evidence: CS-1, SS-2, SS-4. The most critical limiting factor is phase control under Mg volatility and refractory Rh diffusion, which is inferred from Mg/Ru hydride residual educts and Mg–Rh solid-state impurity issues. Evidence: SS-4, SS-5, TS-3; [Reasonable Inference].
