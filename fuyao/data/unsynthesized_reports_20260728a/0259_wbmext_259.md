### F1 — Thermodynamic Stability

[Target validation] Chemical and thermodynamic analogues also predominantly report zero energy above hull, including YbNiAl₂, Al₃Pt₂, TmPtAl, PtAl₂, and ErPtAl. [CS-1; TS-1–TS-5] The score is 95 rather than 100 because the target is a theory-only WBM candidate without supplied experimental synthesis confirmation. [Target validation]

### F2 — Route 1

Arc melting is not intrinsically penalized for temperature in the proposed annealing range, but the route uses a controlled Ar/vacuum environment and multiple operations including remelting, crushing/sealing, and annealing. [CS-3; SS-3, SS-5] Sc air sensitivity adds handling difficulty, although the supplied sources do not quantify oxidation kinetics. [Reasonable Inference] These factors support an F2 score of 65.

### F3 — Route 1

The elemental precursors are relatively low-activity compared with molecular or nanopowder precursors, and Pt/Sc diffusion is demanding because of their refractory character. [CS-1, CS-3; SS-2, SS-4] The liquid stage of arc melting substantially improves mixing, and the exact Cmcm connectivity is strongly supported by YbNiAl₂. [CS-1] These opposing effects yield a moderate-to-good F3 score of 58.

### F2 — Route 2

The solid-state route requires evacuated ampoules, extended homogenization, and several handling steps, while Sc introduces air-sensitivity concerns. [SS-2, SS-4; Reasonable Inference] There is no evidence for high pressure or a required temperature above 1400 °C in the proposed initial procedure, so those deductions are not applied. The resulting F2 score is 65.

### F3 — Route 2

Powdering improves precursor contact, but the reaction remains diffusion-limited because Pt and Sc are refractory and the Pt–Al references require prolonged homogenization. [SS-2, SS-4; Reasonable Inference] The Pt–Al binaries provide only partial structural guidance because they do not reproduce the target’s Sc-containing Cmcm framework. [SS-2, SS-4] Therefore this route receives the lowest F3 score, 50.

### F2 — Route 3

Melt growth requires a controlled atmosphere and additional premelting, homogenization, growth, and cooling operations. [CS-4] CS does not provide a target-specific temperature, atmosphere, or pressure, so those details are extrapolated and the corresponding evidence is limited. ### F3 — Route 3

A homogeneous liquid can improve compositional mixing relative to solid-state diffusion, but the refractory Pt/Sc combination remains a kinetic challenge. [SS-2, SS-4; Reasonable Inference] Czochralski growth of a related ternary intermetallic demonstrates feasibility of the growth concept, but not specifically for ScAl₂Pt. [CS-4] This supports an intermediate F3 score of 57.

### F4 — Amorphization Tendency

The target has a likely coordination-size mismatch between Sc and the smaller Al/Pt framework atoms, counted as one trigger only as a [Reasonable Inference]; formal ionic-radius evidence is unavailable for this metallic compound.
