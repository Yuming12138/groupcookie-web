### SECTION 1 — Possible Synthesis Routes

Direct peroxide-framework evidence is strongest for **K3TaO8**, which has the same tetragonal **I-42m A3MO8** topology and was crystallized from alkaline H2O2 at 0 °C [CS1/SS1/TS2]. High-temperature ordinary oxide routes exist in the reference set, but they are weaker for this target because they do not preserve an O–O peroxide motif [CS2/CS3/CS5]; I therefore weight the peroxide/low-temperature analogues more heavily.

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

Rb3ZrO8 is predicted to be a thermodynamic ground-state or near-ground-state phase by its reported E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom, and thermodynamic analogues include several stable peroxide/alkali oxide phases [SS2/SS3/TS1/TS2/TS3/TS4/TS5]. The synthesis case is strongly supported by the direct K3TaO8 analogue, which shares the I-42m A3MO8 peroxide framework and crystallizes under mild alkaline H2O2 cooling conditions [CS1/SS1/TS2]. The most important enabling factor is low-temperature peroxide solution chemistry, which can preserve O–O units and bypass solid-state diffusion [CS1/SS1/TS2]. The main limiting factor is the unusual Zr-centered charge/oxidation requirement implied by Rb3ZrO8 peroxide stoichiometry **[Reasonable Inference]**. Overall, the material is plausibly synthesizable, but the best attempt should prioritize low-temperature crystallization rather than high-temperature ceramic processing [CS1/SS1/TS2].
