### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability**

The target is reported on the hull, and the closest exact/near-exact analogues CaN2, SrN2, and BaN2 are also on-hull in CS/SS/TS, supporting a high F1 score (CS1, SS1–SS3, TS1–TS3). The NaN3 slow-evaporation entries are not useful thermodynamic evidence for CaN2 diazenide stability because they are alkali azides with high listed E_hull values; insufficient evidence from CS2–CS4, neutral 50 for CaN2 stability transfer. Remaining uncertainty is not the Ca–N thermodynamic basin but the target polymorph, because the exact CaN2 analogue is tetragonal while the target is C2/c (CS1, SS1, SS3, TS1, TS3).

**F2 — Route 1**

Route 1 is penalized heavily for 12 GPa pressure and hazardous/moisture-sensitive azide handling, both directly reported for alkaline-earth diazenide synthesis (CS1, SS1, TS1). It avoids >1000 °C and avoids a reactive gas atmosphere during the reaction, so temperature and gas-atmosphere penalties are not triggered (CS1, SS1, TS1). The source conflict with easy NaN3 slow evaporation is discounted because CS2–CS4 do not synthesize CaN2 or a diazenide framework; those entries remain neutral 50 for this factor.

**F3 — Route 1**

Kinetics are favorable because Ca(N3)2 is a reactive N-rich precursor and the reported CaN2 conversion occurs in only 20 min under HP/HT conditions (CS1, SS1, TS1). Structural reconstruction is moderate rather than severe because CaN2 and BaN2 analogues preserve the N2 dimer and high alkaline-earth coordination, but tetragonal-vs-C2/c polymorph selection remains uncertain (SS1, SS3, TS1, TS3). This supports the highest F3 among routes.

**F2 — Route 2**

Route 2 is less extreme than 12 GPa multianvil in the baseline version, but still requires sealed high-pressure N2 and long high-pressure reaction handling, as shown by SrN2 formation at 5500 bar and 920 K for 72 h (SS5, TS4). It receives penalties for controlled N2 atmosphere, sealed gas precursor, multistep nitride preparation, and air-sensitive dry nitride handling by [Reasonable Inference] from the Sr2N route and diazenide glovebox handling (SS5, TS4; CS1, SS1, TS1). Exact CaN2 gas nitridation is not demonstrated, so CS/SS/TS support is indirect.

**F3 — Route 2**

The Sr2N → SrN2 precedent shows that high N2 pressure can drive diazenide formation from an alkaline-earth nitride, which supports moderate kinetics (SS5, TS4). N2 activation and CaN2 polymorph selection are more uncertain than azide decomposition, so precursor activity and structural reconstruction scores are lower than Route 1 (SS5, TS4; CS1, SS1, TS1). The long 72 h analogue reaction suggests slower equilibration than the 20 min azide route (SS5, TS4; CS1, SS1).

**F2 — Route 3**

Route 3 avoids formal high pressure but uses hazardous azide/nitride chemistry and controlled N2/NH3 or sealed conditions, so safety and atmosphere penalties remain significant (CS1, SS1, TS1; SS4, TS5; CS5). The 750 °C / 36 h solid-state nitride precedent supports feasible furnace conditions, but not exact CaN2 phase stabilization (SS4, TS5). CS5 is treated as weak/neutral for exact CaN2 because its retrieved precursor chemistry is inconsistent with the target.

**F3 — Route 3**

The azide/nitride mixture should be kinetically active, but the absence of high pressure makes structural reconstruction and nitrogen retention uncertain for a binary diazenide (CS1, SS1, TS1; [Reasonable Inference]). Generic solid-state nitride sintering under N2 supports powder reaction feasibility but not the target C2/c CaN2 phase (SS4, TS5). Therefore F3 is lower than Route 1 despite potentially easier equipment access.

**F4 — Amorphization Tendency**

Amorphization risk is low because the target is a simple binary single-cation diazenide, and the closest analogues are also simple alkaline-earth diazenide frameworks rather than compositionally complex networks (CS1, SS1–SS3, TS1–TS3). The complex Li/Sr/Ba–Fe nitride entries contain mixed polyhedra but are not representative of CaN2; insufficient evidence from SS4/TS5 for target amorphization, neutral 50 for that sub-signal. The main structural risk is polymorph competition, not glass formation (SS1, SS3, TS1, TS3).

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

CaN2-wbmext_090 is likely a thermodynamic ground-state or near-ground-state phase because the target is reported at E_hull = 0 and the closest Ca/Sr/Ba diazenide analogues are also on-hull (CS1, SS1–SS3, TS1–TS3). The synthesis evidence is strong at the chemical-family level because exact CaN2 has already been made by high-pressure azide decomposition and C2/c BaN2 matches the target symmetry and N2-dimer topology (CS1, SS1, SS3, TS1, TS3). The most critical enabling factor is the reactive Ca(N3)2 precursor, which supplies a preorganized nitrogen-rich source for N2^2− diazenide formation under pressure (CS1, SS1, TS1). The most critical limiting factor is polymorph control under extreme pressure, because the known CaN2 analogue is tetragonal while the target is monoclinic C2/c (CS1, SS1, SS3, TS1, TS3). Overall, the material is plausibly synthesizable but should be treated as a specialized high-pressure diazenide target rather than a routine solid-state nitride (CS1, SS1–SS3, TS1–TS3).
