### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamics.** The target’s supplied E_hull is 0.0 eV/atom, and the closest exact analogue SrTiN₂ is also on hull in both SS and TS, supporting a high thermodynamic score. [SS1, TS1] Chemically and thermodynamically similar nitrides are mostly on hull or near-hull, with CS1 at 0.031 eV/atom and CS2–CS5 at 0.0 eV/atom. [CS1–CS5] The remaining uncertainty is that the target is theory-only and CS/SS/TS do not report experimental EuTiN₂ synthesis. [SS1, TS1, Reasonable Inference]

**F2/F3 — Route 1.** The solid-state route is strongly transferable because SrTiN₂ is the exact P4/nmm structural analogue made from Sr₂N + TiN. [SS1, TS1] Its condition score is low because the analogue requires 1030 °C, 5 days, sealed inert handling, and reports impurity/phase-purity difficulty. [SS1, TS1] Kinetics are moderate because TiN is refractory and long diffusion lengths are likely, though the final topology is directly validated by the analogue. [SS1, TS1, Reasonable Inference]

**F2/F3 — Route 2.** Li₃N flux has direct precedent for crystallizing rare-earth / transition-metal nitrides at 900–1030 °C, giving a better condition/kinetic balance than dry sintering. [CS3, CS4, TS4] Its F3 is limited by the lack of a direct EuTiN₂ flux analogue and possible Li-containing side phases reported in related nitride chemistry. [CS2, CS3, CS4, Reasonable Inference] SS gives insufficient route-specific evidence for Li₃N flux to this topology and is treated as neutral 50 for that route-specific transferability. [SS1–SS5]

**F2/F3 — Route 3.** Na/Ga flux is plausible because CS5 made La₃Cr₂N₆ from elements, Na/Ga, and NaN₃ at 900 °C in sealed Nb tubes. [CS5] The route is penalized because CS5 reports small amounts of desired product, competing phases, and unsuccessful attempts to improve yield or crystal size. [CS5] SS and TS provide insufficient evidence for Na/Ga flux synthesis of EuTiN₂, so they are neutral 50 for this route-specific factor. [SS1–SS5, TS1–TS5]

**F2/F3 — Route 4.** High-pressure NH₃ synthesis is plausible only as an exploratory route because SS4 shows a nitride can crystallize at 550 °C and 0.6 GPa NH₃. [SS4] Its lower temperature is attractive, but the structural reconstruction score is low because no CS/SS/TS source shows Eu–Ti–N or SrTiN₂-type formation under ammonothermal conditions. [SS4] CS and TS are therefore neutral 50 for this route-specific factor. [CS1–CS5, TS1–TS5]

**F4 — Amorphization.** The main amorphization trigger is the large Eu/Ti cation size mismatch, but exact SrTiN₂ shows that a large alkaline-earth/Ti size mismatch can still form an ordered P4/nmm nitride. [SS1, TS1, Reasonable Inference] The exact analogue has a small reported Sr₂Ti₂N₄ cell and corner/edge sharing without face-sharing, so C2 and C3 are not triggered. [SS1, TS1] CS is less useful for F4 because several CS structures are different prototypes, so CS is neutral 50 for target-specific amorphization. [CS1–CS5]
