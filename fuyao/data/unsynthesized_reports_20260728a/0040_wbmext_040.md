### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability**  
The supplied target E_hull is 0.0000 eV/atom, and the top CS, SS, and TS analogues all report E_hull = 0.0 eV/atom, supporting a high F1 score. (CS, SS, TS) The WBM candidate is therefore likely thermodynamically accessible rather than strongly metastable, although the dataset does not identify an experimental synthesis. (CS, SS, TS; [Reasonable Inference])

**F2/F3 — Route 1**  
Route 1 is conditionally severe because Rb oxide/suboxide handling requires dry Ar, sealed vessels, and moisture exclusion, which are repeatedly used in RbYO₂/RbLaO₂ and alkali indate analogues. (CS, SS, TS) Its kinetics score is high because NaInO₂ and RbYO₂/RbLaO₂ share octahedral alkali–trivalent-cation oxide frameworks, reducing structural-reconstruction burden. (CS, SS, TS) The main uncertainty is whether the target H-phase P6₃/mmc ordering is selected over the more common R-3m Caswellsilverite or Rb-rich indate phases. (SS, TS; [Reasonable Inference])

**F2/F3 — Route 2**  
Route 2 has similar condition severity to Route 1 because Rb-rich suboxides and closed Ag/Ni vessels are needed. (SS, TS) Kinetics may be favorable because alkali-rich media can act as self-fluxes, but SS/TS analogues show Rb₃GaO₃/Cs₃InO₃-type phases that may compete with 1:1 RbInO₂. (SS, TS; [Reasonable Inference]) This route is better for crystal growth exploration than first-pass phase-pure powder synthesis. (SS, TS; [Reasonable Inference])

**F2/F3 — Route 3**  
Route 3 is supported chemically by CsCoO₂ azide/nitrate metathesis, which uses ball milling, vacuum drying, Ar, and 833 K annealing. (CS) Its kinetic advantage is reactive in situ alkali oxide generation, but its structural relevance is weak because the analogue is CoO₄-based rather than InO₆-based. (CS; [Reasonable Inference]) SS and TS provide insufficient evidence for azide/nitrate Rb–In oxide synthesis and are therefore neutral 50 source signals for this route. (SS, TS)

**F2/F3 — Route 4**  
Route 4 benefits from lower-temperature open O₂ precedent in RbCuO₂/CsCuO₂, but this precedent is chemically redox-specific to Cu oxocuprates. (CS) For RbInO₂, O₂ is not obviously needed for In³⁺ stabilization, and CS/SS/TS more directly support dry Ar sealed routes for alkali indates and related octahedral oxides. (CS, SS, TS; [Reasonable Inference]) Its lower F3 reflects the structural mismatch between square-planar Cu–O analogues and octahedral InO₆ target chemistry. (CS, SS, TS)

**F4 — Amorphization Tendency**  
The target has large A/B cation size contrast and mixed polyhedral connectivity, both of which raise amorphization or disorder risk. (CS, SS, TS; [Reasonable Inference]) The risk is moderated because multiple crystalline alkali oxometallate analogues with edge/corner-sharing networks are experimentally reported. (CS, SS, TS) CS/SS/TS do not directly provide the target unit-cell atom count, so C2 is treated as neutral/uncertain rather than a triggered penalty. (CS, SS, TS)
