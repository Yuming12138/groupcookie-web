### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability**  
The target is scored high because its reported E_hull is 0.0000 eV/atom, which falls in the 90–100 range. [Target] The thermodynamic signal is reinforced by many CS/SS/TS analogues that are also on the hull, although CeCuIn and CeIrIn2 show that nearby synthesized analogues can tolerate small positive E_hull values. [CS1][CS2][CS3][SS4][TS1][TS2][TS4]

**Route 1 F2/F3 — Arc Melting + Anneal**  
Condition severity is moderate because arc melting, Ar atmosphere, remelting, and long annealing are standard for the analogous Ce–T–p-block intermetallics, with no high pressure required. [CS2][CS3][SS2][TS2] Kinetics are favorable relative to unmelted solid-state synthesis because melt homogenization and the C2/m Ce3Ru2In3 analogue reduce reconstruction uncertainty, but 700–720 h annealing in the closest analogues indicates slow ordering. [SS2][TS2]

**Route 2 F2/F3 — Sealed-Tube Direct Reaction**  
Condition severity is similar to Route 1, but sealed Ta/quartz handling is operationally more demanding than open arc melting under Ar. [SS4][CS4] Kinetics are slightly weaker because the elements start less homogenized than an arc-melted button, although high-temperature sealed reaction and liquid In should assist transport. [SS4][SS5][Reasonable Inference]

**Route 3 F2/F3 — Melt Growth / Controlled Cooling**  
Severity is moderate because reported melt-growth analogues use ~1097–1200 °C and sealed inert conditions without high pressure. [CS4][SS5] Kinetics benefit from a liquid pathway, but phase selection is penalized because CePdZn shows dimorphism and Ce–Pt–In shows measurable composition drift from nominal stoichiometry. [CS4][TS4]

**Route 4 F2/F3 — Vacuum Sintering**  
Severity is higher because the closest route uses high vacuum, slow cooling, and sealed ampoules. [SS5] Kinetics are the weakest of the four routes because dense Co/Ce solids must react through limited contact unless liquid In provides transport, and CS/TS give only indirect support for a no-arc vacuum-sintering route; **insufficient evidence from [CS]** and **insufficient evidence from [TS]** are treated as neutral 50 for method specificity. [SS5][CS5][CS3][Reasonable Inference]

One amorphization trigger is assigned because Ce–Co size mismatch is likely >15%, but the target formula and described sites do not indicate a large >40-atom cell. [Target][Reasonable Inference] The analogue sets mainly show that related intermetallics crystallize under bulk thermal routes, but they do not directly prove the target’s exact polyhedral sharing topology, so uncertainty remains neutral for the missing trigger data.
