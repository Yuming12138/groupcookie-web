# SECTION 1 — Possible Synthesis Routes

The target NaAuIn is strongly supported by close chemical and structural analogues. In particular, CaAuIn and SrAuIn have the same orthorhombic Pnma topology and nearly identical coordination descriptions, while NaAuSn has the same Na–Au–X structural framework and Pnma symmetry [CS-2, CS-4, SS-4]. The proposed routes below are therefore adaptations of demonstrated intermetallic syntheses; adaptations not directly demonstrated for NaAuIn are labeled **[Reasonable Inference]**.

# SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

NaAuIn is likely to be a calculated ground-state phase rather than an intrinsically metastable phase because the supplied energy above hull is 0.0000 eV atom⁻¹ [Target record]. The strongest structural support comes from CaAuIn and SrAuIn, which share the Pnma space group and nearly identical coordination topology [CS-4, SS-4, TS-4]. The strongest chemical-processing support comes from NaAuSn and the experimentally synthesized NaAuIn₂/Na₃AuIn₂ phases, demonstrating that Na, Au, and In can form crystalline ternary intermetallics under sealed inert conditions [CS-2, SS-1, SS-2, TS-1, TS-2]. The most important enabling factor is the close Pnma structural analogy combined with favorable calculated thermodynamics [CS-4, SS-4, Target record]. The principal limitation is phase competition and Na loss, especially because the known Na–Au–In phases have different compositions and structures [SS-1, SS-2, TS-1, TS-2].
