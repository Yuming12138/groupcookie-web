(SS1, SS2, CS1, CS2, TS1, TS3)

Route 2 avoids sealed high-pressure equipment while retaining oxidizing dry O₂ conditions around 600 °C. (CS3) The severity penalty is mainly for controlled dry O₂ and Na₂O₂ handling. (CS3; SS5, TS4)
