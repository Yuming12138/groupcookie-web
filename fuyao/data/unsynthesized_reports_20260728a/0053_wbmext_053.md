### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability**  
The target E_hull is supplied as 0.0000 eV/atom, placing it in the highest thermodynamic scoring band. [Target] Thermodynamic analogues Ba2SrWO6, Ba2CaWO6, and Sr2CaWO6 are also at or very near the hull, supporting oxide stability in this structure family. [TS1; TS2; TS3; TS4] The uncertainty is polymorph-specific because the exact known Ba3WO6 analogue in CS1/SS3 is cubic, while the target is monoclinic C2/m and theory-only. [CS1; SS3; Target]

**F2 — Route 1**  
Route 1 is penalized for >1000 °C synthesis and multiple firing/regrinding steps, both directly reported for exact Ba3WO6 ceramic synthesis. [CS1] It avoids penalties for pressure and controlled atmosphere because the exact and structural analogues are made in air at ambient pressure. [CS1; SS1; TS1] The remaining uncertainty is whether the direct route yields the monoclinic target or the cubic Ba3WO6 polymorph. [CS1; SS3; Target]

**F3 — Route 1**  
BaCO3/WO3 are reliable but kinetically ordinary ceramic precursors, so precursor activity is moderate rather than high. [CS1; SS1; TS1] W-containing oxide diffusion is likely slow, consistent with the repeated firing/regrinding used across Ba–W analogues. [CS1; SS1; TS1; Reasonable Inference] Structural reconstruction is favorable at the composition level but less favorable at the polymorph level because the reported exact-composition phase is cubic rather than monoclinic. [CS1; SS3; Target]

**F2 — Route 2**  
Route 2 has the same high-temperature and multi-step penalties as Route 1, but it remains an air/ambient-pressure route. [SS1; SS2; TS1; TS2] The stepwise heating schedule is directly supported by Ba2SrWO6 analogues and longer ceramic treatments in TS4. [SS1; TS1; TS4] No high-pressure or reactive-atmosphere penalty is applied because the closest tungstate analogues use air. [SS1; TS1; TS4]

**F3 — Route 2**  
Route 2 improves structural kinetics because the closest analogue Ba2SrWO6 is monoclinic C2/m with WO6 octahedra and W–O distances essentially matching the target. [Target; SS1; TS1] Long annealing and regrinding mitigate diffusion limitations but do not eliminate W-related sluggishness. [SS1; TS1; TS4; Reasonable Inference] The main uncertainty is whether Ba-only A/B-site chemistry stabilizes the same tilted monoclinic topology without Sr/Ca size tuning. [SS1; SS4; Target; Reasonable Inference]

**F2 — Route 3**  
Route 3 is penalized for high temperature, controlled atmosphere, multi-step handling, and BaO/BaO2 reagent risk. [CS2; CS3; SS4; TS3; Reasonable Inference] It is not penalized for high pressure because evacuated ampoules and vacuum/Ar reactions are not >5 GPa synthesis. [CS2; CS3; SS4; TS3] Its main uncertainty is that controlled atmosphere is indirectly motivated by analogues rather than required by exact Ba3WO6 evidence. [CS1; CS2; CS3; SS4; TS3]

**F3 — Route 3**  
BaO can improve precursor reactivity relative to BaCO3 by avoiding carbonate decomposition, but this benefit is an extrapolation. [SS4; SS5; TS3; Reasonable Inference] W-containing oxide diffusion and complete ceramic reconstruction remain limiting. [SS1; TS1; TS3; Reasonable Inference] Structural support is weaker than Route 2 because controlled-atmosphere analogues are less compositionally and structurally direct. [CS2; CS3; SS4; TS3; Target]

**F4 — Amorphization Tendency**
