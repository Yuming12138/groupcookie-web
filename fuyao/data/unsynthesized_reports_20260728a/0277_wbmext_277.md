### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability**  
Target ScRhAs is reported at E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom, so it scores near the top of the provided stability rubric. (Target) The closest ScRhGe analogue is also E_hull = 0 across CS/SS/TS, and other nearby analogues are mostly at or very near the hull. (CS-2, SS-1, TS-1; CS-3, SS-3) Remaining uncertainty is experimental, because the target is theory-only and not identified as previously synthesized. (Target)

**F2 — Route 1**  
Arc-melting is directly supported for the closest Pnma ScRhGe analogue under Ar, but controlled atmosphere, multi-step homogenization, and toxic As handling lower the score. (CS-2, SS-1, TS-1; Target) The sustained anneal temperatures in analogues are moderate, while transient arc temperatures are not explicitly quantified by CS/SS/TS and were not double-penalized. (CS-1, CS-3, TS-4)

**F2 — Route 2**  
Sealed-tube arsenide synthesis is supported by REPtAs analogues at 950–1100/1150 °C over 2–4 weeks with intermediate grinding. (SS-4, SS-5, TS-3, TS-5) The route is penalized for controlled atmosphere, sealed As containment, multi-step processing, and toxic As. (SS-4, TS-3; Target)

**F2 — Route 3**  
Pb-flux growth is supported by ScPtP and ScPdSb analogues at 1000 °C and 700 °C, respectively. (SS-2, TS-2; CS-5) The route avoids very high sustained temperature but is penalized for sealed-system As control, toxic Pb/As handling, flux removal, and multi-step crystal isolation. (SS-2, TS-2; CS-5; Target)

**F3 — Route 1**  
Reaction kinetics are favorable relative to dry solid-state synthesis because arc-melting creates strong mixing and repeated re-melting improves homogeneity. (CS-2, SS-1, TS-1) Structural reconstruction is the strongest kinetic factor because ScRhGe has the same Pnma topology and closely matching coordination environment. (CS-2, SS-1, TS-1; Target)

**F3 — Route 2**  
Direct solid-state kinetics are slower because dense Sc/Rh elemental precursors require long diffusion distances and analogues need multi-week anneals. (SS-4, SS-5, TS-3, TS-5; [Reasonable Inference]) Structural support is weaker than Route 1 because the arsenide analogues are mainly REPtAs hexagonal phases rather than exact ScRhAs/ScRhGe Pnma analogues. (SS-4, TS-3; CS-2, SS-1, TS-1)

**F3 — Route 3**  
Flux growth improves transport through a liquid medium, which supports better kinetics than dry solid-state reaction. (SS-2, TS-2; CS-5; [Reasonable Inference]) The score is capped because ScPtP powder preparations had impurity problems and because Rh–As flux solubility is extrapolated rather than directly evidenced. (SS-2, TS-2; [Reasonable Inference])

**F4 — Amorphization Tendency**  
The target is a small-cell ordered intermetallic/Pnictide-like phase with a close crystalline Pnma analogue, which supports low amorphization risk. (CS-2, SS-1, TS-1; Target) One weak radius-mismatch trigger is assigned by extrapolation, while CS/SS/TS provide insufficient direct ionic-radius evidence and therefore would be neutral on that sub-check alone. ([Reasonable Inference]; CS/SS/TS insufficient)
