### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

#### F1: Thermodynamic Stability

Ba₂AsCl is assigned F1 = 98 because the target record gives E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom, which falls in the highest stability band. **Target record.** The strongest analogues Ba₂PCl, Ba₃I₂Si, Ba₃GeI₂, Ca₂AsI, and Ba₂NCl are also reported at E_hull = 0 or near-zero, supporting a stable Ba-rich pnictide/halide structural family. **CS/SS/TS.** Remaining uncertainty is that WBM is theory-only and no experimental synthesis is identified for Ba₂AsCl. **Target record.**

#### F2: Route 1

Route 1 is penalized for controlled Ar atmosphere, sealed ampoule handling, multi-step heating/grinding/tempering, and air-sensitive/toxic reagents. **CS/SS/TS + target note + [Reasonable Inference].** It is not penalized for >1000 °C or high pressure because the Ba₂PCl analogue uses 500–1000 °C under Ar in ampoules and no high-pressure equipment. **CS/SS/TS.** Remaining uncertainty is the exact As vapor pressure and phase window relative to P. **[Reasonable Inference].**

#### F3: Route 1

Route 1 receives moderate-good kinetics because the closest analogue Ba₂PCl forms by solid-state sintering but requires slow heating, grinding, and repeated tempering. **CS/SS/TS.** Structural reconstruction is favorable because Ba₂PCl and Ba₂AsCl are isostructural Caswellsilverite R-3m Ba₂PnCl phases. **CS/SS/TS + [Reasonable Inference].** The main uncertainty is whether As ordering is slower or more volatile than P under the same conditions. **[Reasonable Inference].**

#### F2: Route 2

Route 2 has similar severity to Route 1 because Ba₂NCl synthesis uses Ar glovebox handling, dried halides, pelletization, Mo wrapping, sealed crucibles, and long firing. **SS/TS.** It avoids high pressure and >1000 °C penalties because the analogue fires at 998 K, below 1000 °C. **SS/TS.** The uncertainty is that Ba arsenide precursors are inferred rather than directly reported for Ba₂AsCl. **Insufficient direct evidence from CS/SS/TS; [Reasonable Inference].**

#### F3: Route 2

Route 2 receives slightly better precursor activity than Route 1 because a preformed Ba–As precursor could reduce the need to form Ba–As bonds from elemental As during the final chloride reaction. **SS/TS + [Reasonable Inference].** Diffusion remains moderate because Ba₂NCl requires 5 days and slow cooling, indicating nontrivial ordering kinetics even in the isostructural family. **SS/TS.** The structural match is favorable but less certain than Route 1 because N→As substitution is a larger chemical change than P→As. **SS/TS + [Reasonable Inference].**

#### F2: Route 3

Route 3 is penalized for vacuum/inert sealed conditions, volatile As containment, multi-step heating/cooling/separation, and toxic/air-sensitive constituents. **CS/SS/TS + target note + [Reasonable Inference].** It is not penalized for >1000 °C because the Ba₃I₂Si/Ba₃GeI₂ analogues are prepared at 1170 K, approximately 897 °C. **CS/SS/TS.** The uncertainty is whether BaCl₂ provides the same practical flux window as BaI₂. **Insufficient direct evidence from CS/SS/TS; [Reasonable Inference].**

#### F3: Route 3

Route 3 has the best kinetic score because flux-like Ba-halide analogues form after 8 h with controlled cooling, implying enhanced mass transport compared with repeated dry sintering. **CS/SS/TS.** Structural reconstruction is less direct than Route 1 because the flux analogues are orthorhombic Caswellsilverite-like iodides with Si/Ge rather than trigonal Ba₂AsCl. **CS/SS/TS.** The uncertainty is phase selectivity: the flux may favor Ba-rich iodide-like or competing arsenide/chloride phases rather than Ba₂AsCl. **[Reasonable Inference].**

#### F4: Amorphization Tendency

F4 is high because the target is a simple 3D periodic Caswellsilverite-type structure with one cation species and corner/edge-sharing octahedra, and the strongest analogues show crystalline products rather than amorphous outcomes. **Target record + CS/SS/TS.** The face-sharing H-phase CsPrS₂ analogue indicates that related layered chalcogenides can adopt competing connectivities, but it is not the closest target topology. **CS.** Remaining uncertainty is that amorphization risk is inferred from structural simplicity and analogue crystallinity rather than measured glass-forming behavior. **[Reasonable Inference].**

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

Ba₂AsCl is most likely a ground-state or very-near-ground-state phase because its supplied E_hull is 0.0000 eV/atom and multiple close analogues in CS/SS/TS are also stable or near-stable. **Target record + CS/SS/TS.** The synthesis case is strongly supported by Ba₂PCl, which matches the Ba₂PnCl Caswellsilverite R-3m structure and is experimentally made by sealed Ar solid-state sintering. **CS/SS/TS.** The most critical enabling factor is the direct structural and chemical transferability from Ba₂PCl to Ba₂AsCl. **CS/SS/TS + [Reasonable Inference].** The most critical limiting factor is safe and stoichiometric incorporation of arsenic under sealed, dry, Ba-rich chloride conditions. **Target note + CS/SS/TS + [Reasonable Inference].** Overall, the material appears synthetically plausible but will likely require careful sealed-ampoule optimization rather than open ceramic firing. **CS/SS/TS + [Reasonable Inference].**
