### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

Ta3Rh is best interpreted as a likely 0 K ground-state candidate rather than an obvious metastable target because the supplied hull distance is 0.0000 eV/atom and the formation energy is negative **[Target]**. The analogue support is strongest for high-temperature bulk metallurgy because Ta–noble-metal/intermetallic examples use arc melting, vacuum heat treatment, and elemental Ta feedstocks **[CS2; CS3; SS5]**. Structural support is especially useful from Pm-3n Nb3Pb, which shares the A3B cuboctahedral framework, although its Pb volatility and hot-press route are not chemically identical to Ta3Rh **[CS4]**. The most important enabling factor is the combination of zero-hull thermodynamics, binary composition, and small A15-like Pm-3n topology **[Target; CS4]**. The main limiting factor is kinetic: refractory Ta/Rh interdiffusion, oxygen/carbon contamination control, and possible ordering/phase-window constraints observed in related Ta-rich systems **[CS3; SS1/TS1; SS5; SS4/TS4; Reasonable Inference]**.
