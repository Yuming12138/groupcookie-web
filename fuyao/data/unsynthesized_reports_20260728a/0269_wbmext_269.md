BaRu₂Si₂ is best viewed as a **likely ground-state or near-ground-state intermetallic candidate** because the target E_hull is 0.000 eV/atom and the closest CS/SS/TS analogues are also on the hull [Target, CS1–CS5, SS1–SS5, TS1–TS5]. |:--:|:--:|:--|:--:|:--|
| 1 | All | Ba loss, oxidation, or off-stoichiometry during high-T synthesis | High | Use sealed Ar ampoule, inert handling, and slight Ba excess only if mass loss/impurities indicate need [CS1, SS1, SS2, SS3, TS1, TS2, TS3; Reasonable Inference]. |
| 2 | All | Competing Ba silicides, Ru silicides, or unidentified impurity phases | Medium | Follow analogue stoichiometry, verify by powder XRD, and regrind/reanneal if needed; impurity risk is suggested by some analogue phase-control notes [CS1, CS3, SS3, TS3]. |
| 3 | Route 1 / 2 | Slow Ru–Si ordering and solid-state diffusion | Medium | Use long 50–72 h anneals near 1100 °C and fine mixing/pelletizing [CS1, SS3, TS1, TS3; Reasonable Inference]. |
| 4 | Route 3 | Ba evaporation during arc melting | High | Use pre-alloyed Ba–Si pieces, low arc power, rapid melting, slight Ba excess, and sealed post-anneal [CS2, CS5, SS4, TS4; Reasonable Inference]. |
| 5 | Route 4 | Flux composition window and target solubility unknown | Medium–High | Screen Ba–Si-rich ratios and use slow cooling; treat direct flux evidence as neutral because insufficient evidence from [CS], [SS], and [TS] for BaRu₂Si₂ flux growth [CS5, SS3, SS4, TS3, TS4]. |
| 6 | Route 2 / 4 | Acid wash may attack target or alter surface composition | Medium | Use dilute/brief wash only for confirmed excess alkaline-earth residue, following BaRuGe₂ precedent [SS3, TS3; Reasonable Inference]. |
| 7 | All | Quartz ampoule compatibility with Ba vapor | Medium | Use corundum crucible plus possible Ta/Nb liner inside sealed quartz [CS1, SS2, TS2; Reasonable Inference]. |
