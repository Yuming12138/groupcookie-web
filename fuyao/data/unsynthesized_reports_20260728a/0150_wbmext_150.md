### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability.**  
The target is reported at E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom, which places it in the highest thermodynamic score band (Target data). The strongest structural and thermodynamic analogues La3InN, La3AlN, Pr3InN, Pr3AlN, and Ce3InC are also listed with E_hull = 0.0, supporting the plausibility of the cubic R3MN framework (CS2–CS5, SS1–SS5, TS1–TS5). The main uncertainty is that the closest Tl analogue Nd3TlN has a high listed E_hull despite synthesis evidence, so the CS thermodynamic signal is mixed (CS1).

**F2 — Route 1.**  
Route 1 avoids >1000 °C annealing and high pressure, but it requires evacuated/inert sealed ampoule work, multiple processing steps, and air-sensitive rare-earth nitride/alloy handling (CS3, SS1, TS1). The Tl-specific sealed-containment penalty is a [Reasonable Inference] because CS/SS/TS do not quantify Tl vapor pressure, while the target safety note flags thallium toxicity. The severity score is therefore limited by handling and containment rather than by extreme temperature or pressure (CS3, SS1, TS1).

**F3 — Route 1.**  
Kinetics are helped by the exact La3InN structural analogue and arc-fused precursor contact, which should reduce large-scale reconstruction (CS3, SS1, TS1). Kinetics are penalized because RN/R2In-type routes use dense precursors and long anneals of at least one week, so ordering is not instantaneous (CS3, SS1, TS1). The main uncertainty is whether Tl substitution changes liquid formation, vapor loss, or phase competition relative to In; that is a [Reasonable Inference] rather than directly proven by CS/SS/TS.

**F2 — Route 2.**  
Route 2 uses moderate temperatures of 900 °C and 700 °C and no high pressure, directly matching the closest Tl analogue (CS1). It still requires a controlled N2 atmosphere, multiple steps, and toxic Tl handling, so condition severity remains substantial (CS1; Target safety note). CS1 gives insufficient direct evidence on Tl loss control, so the volatile-component deduction is based on [Reasonable Inference] with source evidence treated neutral (50).

**F3 — Route 2.**  
The closest chemical analogue supports Tl incorporation into a rare-earth nitride perovskite-like phase, which helps structural transferability (CS1). However, CS1 reports non-homogeneous samples and undetermined nitrogen content, which directly lowers the phase-ordering and reproducibility score (CS1). Diffusion may benefit from Tl being liquid at synthesis temperature, but that kinetic advantage is a [Reasonable Inference] not directly measured by CS/SS/TS.

**F2 — Route 3.**  
Route 3 uses the mildest temperature window, 600–800 °C, and no high pressure, based on Pr3AlN-type analogues (SS4, TS4). It still requires evacuated sealed tubes, long annealing, multiple handling operations, and toxic/air-sensitive precursors, so its condition score is not easy despite lower temperature (SS4, TS4; Target safety note). CS/SS/TS provide insufficient direct evidence for Tl behavior in this low-temperature route, so Tl volatility control is neutral in source evidence and added by [Reasonable Inference].

**F3 — Route 3.**  
The low-temperature route is structurally plausible because R3AlN analogues form the same cubic Pm-3m family, but it relies on adapting Al chemistry to Tl chemistry (SS4, TS4; [Reasonable Inference]). The very long annealing times up to 800 h indicate slow diffusion and ordering kinetics (SS4, TS4). Its kinetic score is therefore lower than Route 1 because there is no directly demonstrated La–Tl–N low-temperature analogue in CS/SS/TS.

**F4 — Amorphization Tendency.**  
The target is a small-cell cubic Pm-3m phase and the closest analogues are also simple cubic R3MN-type structures, which lowers amorphization risk (Target data; CS3, SS1, TS1). The connectivity descriptions show corner- and face-sharing motifs but not simultaneous vertex/edge/face sharing, so C3 is not triggered (Target data; CS3, SS1, TS1). CS/SS/TS do not report cation radii, so C1 is treated with neutral source evidence and judged non-triggered by [Reasonable Inference].

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

La3TlN is likely a ground-state or near-ground-state phase because the target E_hull is 0.0000 eV/atom and several close La/R nitride perovskite analogues also have E_hull = 0.0 (Target data; CS3, SS1, TS1). The analogue support is strong structurally through La3InN and La3AlN, and strong chemically through Nd3TlN containing Tl in the same rare-earth nitride family (CS1, CS3, SS1, SS2, TS1, TS2). The most critical enabling factor is the existence of cubic Pm-3m R3MN analogues made by conventional solid-state sealed-ampoule or sintering routes (CS3, SS1, TS1). The most critical limiting factor is thallium handling: toxicity is explicitly flagged for the target, while Tl volatility/retention during annealing is a [Reasonable Inference] not resolved by CS/SS/TS (Target safety note). A secondary limiting factor is phase purity, because the closest Tl analogue reports non-homogeneous samples and uncertain nitrogen content (CS1).
