### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability**

**F2 — Route 1**  
Route 1 loses severity points for high-temperature arc melting and controlled Ar atmosphere, both common in the analogue set. **CS1, CS4, SS1, SS4, TS3, TS4** It also loses points for target-specific Sc/Os air/toxicity risk. [Reasonable Inference] It does not lose high-pressure points because all route analogues are ambient-pressure metallic syntheses. **CS1, CS4, SS1, SS4, TS3, TS4**
