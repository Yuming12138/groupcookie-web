The target has E_hull = 0.0 eV/atom, which places it in the highest thermodynamic score bin. (Target) The nearest thermodynamic and structural analogue TlPO4 is also E_hull = 0.0 eV/atom and shares the same Cmcm TlO6–XO4 framework. (SS2; TS1) CS evidence is mostly supportive because exact-composition CS1 is only 0.0289 eV/atom above hull, but CS2 gives a less favorable arsenate analogue at 0.269 eV/atom, so the score is high but not perfect. (CS1; CS2)

### F2/F3 — Route 1 Hydrothermal

Hydrothermal conditions are moderate because the best analogue uses 350–400 °C for a week or more in a hydrothermal bomb, not extreme pressure or >1000 °C temperatures. (SS2; TS1) Kinetics are favorable because solution-mediated hydrothermal transport is supported by TlPO4 and arsenate analogues, and the target is isostructural with TlPO4. (SS2; TS1; TS3; TS4; Target) The main uncertainty is whether Tl(III) arsenate remains stable against reduction or hydration under the chosen acidic conditions. (Target; CS1; SS1; [Reasonable Inference])

### F2/F3 — Route 2 Slow Evaporation

Slow evaporation is conditionally mild because CS1/SS1 report room-temperature solution growth from Tl2CO3 and arsenic acid. (CS1; SS1) The kinetic score is reduced because the reported chemistry likely favors a protonated TlH2AsO4-like or low-dimensional polymorph rather than the target anhydrous Cmcm framework. (CS1; SS1; Target) Insufficient evidence from TS for this specific slow-evaporation route, so TS is neutral 50 for this route. (TS)

### F2/F3 — Route 3 Solid-State

The solid-state route has moderate condition severity because related oxides are made by 600/900 °C or 1273 K firings with repeated regrinding, but the recommended Tl–As route should stay lower to limit volatilization. (CS5; TS5; Target; [Reasonable Inference]) Kinetics are weaker because dense oxide reactions require reconstruction and repeated heating, as shown by the ceramic analogues. (CS5; TS5) Insufficient evidence from SS for solid-state TlAsO4, so SS is neutral 50 for this route. (SS)

### F2/F3 — Route 4 Precipitation/Digestion

Precipitation/digestion is mild because BiAsO4 and related oxo-salts are prepared in steam-bath or aqueous acid conditions. (CS3; TS2) The kinetic score is moderate because solution precursors are reactive, but precipitation analogues show phase/digestion sensitivity and possible amorphous or broadened products. (CS3; TS2) The remaining uncertainty is whether ambient digestion can directly form the 3D Cmcm target rather than a hydrated or poorly crystalline arsenate. (CS3; TS2; SS2; TS1; [Reasonable Inference])

### F2/F3 — Route 5 CVT

CVT is penalized because the Hg arsenate analogue requires a sealed silica ampoule, halide transport agent, 650 → 550 °C gradient, and 18 d growth. (CS2) Vapor availability and composition control are uncertain for TlAsO4 because Tl/As halide transport may cause volatile side reactions. (CS2; Target; [Reasonable Inference]) Insufficient evidence from SS and TS for CVT of the target or close analogues, so SS and TS are neutral 50 for this route. (SS; TS)

### F4 — Amorphization Tendency

The target has one amorphization trigger because TlO6 and AsO4 imply substantially different cation sizes/coordination environments. (Target; [Reasonable Inference]) The small Cmcm analogue Tl2P2O8 and the target’s simple TlAsO4 formula argue against a >40-atom-cell trigger. (SS2; TS1; Target) The target has corner and edge sharing but no reported face sharing, so the mixed vertex+edge+face criterion is not triggered. (Target)

## SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

TlAsO4 is likely a **DFT ground-state or near-ground-state phase** because the supplied target E_hull is 0.0 eV/atom and the isostructural TlPO4 analogue is also thermodynamically stable. (Target; SS2; TS1) The CS/SS/TS evidence strongly supports a hydrothermal path because TlPO4 has the same Cmcm TlO6–XO4 topology and hydrothermal arsenates are repeatedly reported. (SS2; TS1; TS3; TS4) The most critical enabling factor is the direct structural match between target TlAsO4 and hydrothermal Cmcm TlPO4. (Target; SS2; TS1) The most critical limiting factor is phase/redox control: CS1/SS1 suggest related Tl–As–O solution chemistry may crystallize as protonated or alternate polymorphs rather than the target. (CS1; SS1; Target) Toxic Tl and As handling is a universal practical constraint. (Target)
