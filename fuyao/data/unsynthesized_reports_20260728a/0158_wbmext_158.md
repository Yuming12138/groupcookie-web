### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability.** The target is on the supplied hull, and CS/SS/TS analogues show many closely related A₂MCl₆ chloride frameworks are also on-hull, especially Cs₂TiCl₆, Cs₂GeCl₆, Cs₂MoCl₆, Rb₂PdCl₆, and Rb₂PtCl₆ (CS-3, CS-4, CS-5, SS-1, SS-4, TS-1, TS-3). This supports a high F1 score because the target belongs to a well-populated, thermodynamically plausible chloride structure family (CS-3, CS-4, CS-5, TS-1, TS-3). The remaining uncertainty is that CS/SS/TS do not provide direct experimental Cs₂NiCl₆ synthesis evidence, so direct-target validation is neutral 50 from CS/SS/TS.

**F2/F3 — Route 1.** Condition severity is moderate because the route avoids high temperature and pressure, but likely requires reactive oxidation/chlorination to reach formal Ni(IV) **[Reasonable Inference]**. Kinetics are favorable because precipitation analogues use dissolved chlorometallate species and form crystalline A₂MCl₆-type salts directly (CS-3, CS-4, SS-1, TS-1). The main uncertainty is redox, because CS/SS/TS have insufficient direct evidence for Ni(IV) chloride stabilization and are neutral 50 on that point.

**F2/F3 — Route 2.** Slow evaporation/cooling is mild in temperature and pressure, as shown by Fe/Rh/Au/Cu chloride analogues, but oxidation control and longer crystallization increase operational complexity **[Reasonable Inference]** (CS-2, SS-2, SS-3, TS-5). Kinetics remain strong because solution growth avoids solid diffusion, but phase selection may favor competing nickel chloride complexes if the Ni(IV) window is narrow **[Reasonable Inference]**. CS/SS/TS provide useful crystal-growth evidence but insufficient direct Ni-specific redox evidence, scored neutral 50 for that subfactor.

**F2/F3 — Route 3.** Closed-ampoule solvothermal synthesis is more severe than open precipitation because it combines corrosive volatile acid/halogen chemistry with autogenous pressure, even though temperatures are modest (SS-5, TS-4). Kinetics are good because solvothermal media enhance transport and crystallization at low temperature (SS-5, TS-4). The uncertainty is whether sealed oxidizing chloride media stabilize Ni(IV) instead of decomposing or reducing it **[Reasonable Inference]**.

**F2/F3 — Route 4.** CVT/oxidative chlorination is conditionally plausible because Cs₂MoCl₆ is an exact Fm-3m Cs₂MCl₆ analogue made in a sealed silica gradient, but that route required four weeks and gave only a small crystal yield (CS-5). Kinetics are weaker than solution routes because solid precursors and vapor transport still require long reaction times, and high-temperature redox stability of Ni(IV) chloride is uncertain **[Reasonable Inference]**. CS supports the method class, while SS/TS support the framework stability but give insufficient direct evidence for Ni CVT chemistry, neutral 50 for the Ni-specific transport window.

**F4 — Amorphization Tendency.** The main amorphization trigger is the large A-site/B-site cation size contrast, inferred from Cs–Cl versus M–Cl coordination distances in target-like analogues **[Reasonable Inference]** (CS-3, CS-4, CS-5). The structure is otherwise simple and high-symmetry, and the closest CS analogues do not require simultaneous vertex+edge+face connectivity, reducing amorphization risk (CS-3, CS-4, CS-5). SS/TS edge-sharing language in Cl-centered square pyramids is a minor conflicting signal, but closer exact Cs₂MCl₆ CS analogues are weighted more heavily (SS-1, SS-4, TS-1, TS-3).

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

Cs₂NiCl₆ is best interpreted as a computed ground-state candidate because the supplied target E_hull is 0.0000 eV/atom, and many CS/SS/TS A₂MCl₆ analogues are also on-hull or very near-hull (CS-3, CS-4, CS-5, SS-1, SS-4, TS-1, TS-3). The structural support is strong because exact Cs₂MCl₆ Fm-3m analogues exist for Ti, Ge, and Mo, while Rb₂PdCl₆/Rb₂PtCl₆ reinforce the same hexachlorometallate topology in SS/TS (CS-3, CS-4, CS-5, SS-1, SS-4, TS-1, TS-3). The most critical enabling factor is high chloride activity in acidic solution, which repeatedly enables direct precipitation/crystallization of related alkali metal chlorometallates (CS-3, CS-4, SS-1, TS-1). The most critical limiting factor is forming and preserving formal Ni(IV) in chloride coordination, because no CS/SS/TS source directly demonstrates a Ni analogue and this is treated as neutral 50 from each source **[Reasonable Inference]**. Overall, synthesis is plausible but should be attempted first by low-temperature oxidative precipitation rather than high-temperature sealed-tube methods **[Reasonable Inference]**.
