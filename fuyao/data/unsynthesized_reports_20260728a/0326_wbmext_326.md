### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability**  
The target is reported at E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom, which strongly supports ground-state thermodynamic accessibility. The CS/SS/TS analogues are also mostly at E_hull = 0, with the only listed nonzero CS analogue still low at 0.0379 eV/atom. Remaining uncertainty is that the WBM entry is theory-only and does not by itself prove experimental synthesis. (CS, SS, TS)

**F2 — Route 1**  
Route 1 uses moderate temperatures, Ar atmosphere, sealed quartz ampoules, and common crucibles, matching repeated CS/SS/TS solid-state protocols. The main severity penalties are Li air sensitivity, sealed containment, and the likely need for long anneal/regrind. This route is experimentally standard but not trivial because Li stoichiometry must be protected. (CS, SS, TS; [Reasonable Inference])

**F2 — Route 2**  
Route 2 is harsher because LiPd₂Sn/Pb analogues use 1000–1100 °C preheating before 700 °C annealing. The high-temperature step improves homogenization but increases Li volatility risk and ampoule/reactivity constraints. It remains feasible because no high pressure or exotic atmosphere is required. (CS, SS, TS; [Reasonable Inference])

**F2 — Route 3**  
Route 3 is lower-temperature but more operationally complex because flux separation and post-annealing are added. Direct LiInPd₂ flux evidence is insufficient from CS/SS/TS, so the route is penalized for uncertainty. It may still be useful for crystal growth or kinetic screening. [Reasonable Inference]

**F3 — Route 1**  
Elemental precursor routes are well supported, but Pd diffusion is the likely kinetic bottleneck. The closest structural analogue LiPd₂Tl required ~4 weeks and still noted minor unreacted Pd, supporting a moderate kinetics score rather than an easy one. The target’s matching Heusler topology improves the structural-reconstruction score. (SS, TS)

**F3 — Route 2**  
The high-temperature homogenization step should accelerate Pd incorporation relative to the 700 °C-only route. However, CS/SS/TS comments on homogeneity ranges and tetragonal/cubic modifications indicate that ordering and phase purity may be sensitive to thermal history. This route has better diffusion kinetics but worse condition severity. (CS, SS, TS)

**F3 — Route 3**  
A Li–In melt could improve precursor activity and diffusion, but this is extrapolated rather than directly demonstrated in CS/SS/TS. The ordered tetragonal Heusler product may not crystallize directly from flux without post-annealing. Therefore kinetics are potentially favorable, but structural selectivity is uncertain. [Reasonable Inference]

**F4 — Amorphization Tendency**  
The mixed corner/edge/face-sharing cuboctahedral connectivity triggers one amorphization-risk check, but the existence of crystalline Heusler analogues reduces concern. CS/SS/TS do not provide explicit ionic radii or unit-cell atom counts, so those checks are treated as insufficient evidence and neutral source signals. Overall amorphization risk is low–medium rather than high. (SS, TS)

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

LiInPd₂ is likely a ground-state or near-ground-state phase because the target E_hull is 0 and the closest CS/SS/TS analogues are predominantly hull-stable. The analogue support is strong: CS contains chemically close Li–Pd–In and Li–Pd–main-group Heuslers, while SS/TS contains tetragonal LiPd₂Tl with essentially the same structural motif. The most critical enabling factor is the demonstrated sealed-ampoule solid-state accessibility of Li–Pd–main-group Heuslers from elemental precursors. The main limiting factor is phase-pure ordering with Li retention, because analogue reports require sealed Ar conditions, long anneals, and sometimes show residual Pd or homogeneity-range issues. Overall, synthesis is promising but should be approached with careful stoichiometry and extended annealing. (CS, SS, TS)
