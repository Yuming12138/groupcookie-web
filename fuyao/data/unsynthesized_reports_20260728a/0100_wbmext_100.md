### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability**  
The target has E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom, which places it in the highest stability bracket. [Target data] The strongest thermodynamic analogue Ce₂SeN₂ and the listed TS analogues are also on hull, supporting the idea that this structure/chemistry family can be ground-state accessible. [TS1–TS5] Remaining uncertainty is that DFT hull stability does not prove experimental isolation, and the target is explicitly theory-only in the provided dataset. [Target data]

**Route 1 — F2/F3**  
The route is conditionally demanding because it requires dry azide chemistry, sealed Nb/silica containment, Ar/vacuum handling, and a 120 h 900 °C hold. [CS1/SS1/TS1] Kinetics are favorable because the route uses reactive flux chemistry and has an isostructural Ce₂SeN₂ analogue, so the main reconstruction is Se→S substitution rather than framework discovery. [CS1/SS1/TS1; **[Reasonable Inference]**]

**Route 2 — F2/F3**  
The NaBr/NaN₃ route has similar severity to Route 1 because it also uses azide, sulfur, evacuated ampoules, and week-long high-temperature flux treatment. [CS2/SS5/TS5] Kinetics are moderately favorable for Ce–S–N formation, but the known analogue Ce₃NS₃ has a different structure and composition, so structural selectivity toward Ce₂SN₂ is less certain. [CS2/SS5/TS5]

**Route 3 — F2/F3**  
The solid-state route is harsh because the exact Th₂N₂S analogue requires 1500–1700 °C under N₂, and Ce nitride solid-state synthesis can require repeated grinding/reaction cycles. [SS2/TS2; CS3/SS4/TS4] Kinetics are limited by dense binary CeN/CeS precursors, but the P-3m1 M₂N₂S analogue gives strong structural guidance if Ce valence and N chemical potential are controlled. [SS2/TS2; **[Reasonable Inference]**]

**Route 4 — F2/F3**  
The Li₃N route is attractive kinetically because Li₃N flux crystallizes Ce nitrides at 900 °C, but it is less directly supported for Ce–S–N chemistry. [CS4] SS and TS provide insufficient evidence for Li₃N flux transfer to Ce₂SN₂, so they are treated as neutral 50 source signals for this route. [insufficient evidence from SS/TS]

**F4 — Amorphization Tendency**  
The target has a simple single-cation P-3m1 framework and the closest analogues show ordered corner/edge-sharing tetrahedral–octahedral networks rather than large mixed-polyhedral face-sharing complexity. [Target data; CS1/SS1/TS1; SS2/TS2] This gives low amorphization risk, although air/moisture sensitivity and anion volatility can still reduce crystallinity experimentally. ### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

Ce₂SN₂ is likely a ground-state or near-ground-state phase because the target E_hull is 0.0000 eV/atom and thermodynamic analogues are also on hull. [Target data; TS1–TS5] The strongest support is the experimentally synthesized isostructural Ce₂SeN₂, which matches the target’s P-3m1 topology and Ce/N/chalcogen coordination. [CS1/SS1/TS1] The key enabling factor is reactive flux transport with high nitrogen activity from azide chemistry, which has already produced the closest analogue at 900 °C. [CS1/SS1/TS1] The main limiting factor is controlled incorporation of both N and S while maintaining Ce in the required oxidized nitride–sulfide framework; this is a valence/anion-activity risk inferred from the Ce₂SN₂ charge balance. **[Reasonable Inference]** Overall, synthesis looks feasible but not routine, with Route 1 clearly preferred. [CS1/SS1/TS1]
