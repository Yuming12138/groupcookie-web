### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability**  
The target’s supplied E_hull is 0.000 eV/atom, and the closest SrN₂/CaN₂/BaN₂ diazenide analogues in CS/SS/TS are also reported at E_hull = 0.0. [CS-1, CS-3, CS-5; SS-1, SS-2, SS-3; TS-1, TS-2, TS-3] The remaining uncertainty is polymorph-specific: the direct SrN₂ experimental analogue is tetragonal, while the target is monoclinic C2/c. [CS-1; SS-1; TS-1]

**F2 — Route 1**  
Route 1 is heavily penalized for >5 GPa pressure and hazardous/moisture-sensitive azide chemistry, even though temperature is only moderate. [CS-1, CS-3, CS-5; SS-1, SS-3; TS-1, TS-3] The short dwell and direct analogue support make it feasible but operationally challenging. [CS-1; SS-1; TS-1]

**F3 — Route 1**  
Azide decomposition provides high nitrogen activity and fast kinetics, with SrN₂ analogue formation reported in ~15 min. [CS-1; SS-1; TS-1] Structural reconstruction is favorable because the closest SrN₂ and BaN₂ analogues preserve N–N diazenide units and high alkaline-earth coordination, but the exact SrN₂-C2/c polymorph remains an extrapolation. [CS-1, CS-5; SS-1, SS-2; TS-1, TS-2] [Reasonable Inference]

**F2 — Route 2**  
Route 2 avoids >5 GPa multianvil pressure but still requires 5500 bar N₂, controlled gas loading, and a prepared Sr₂N precursor. [SS-4; TS-4] CS provides insufficient evidence for this Sr₂N + N₂ route and is treated as neutral source evidence (50). [insufficient evidence from CS]

**F3 — Route 2**  
The Sr₂N route has credible stoichiometric transfer because single-phase SrN₂ is reported at high N₂ pressure, but the 72 h dwell indicates slower kinetics than azide decomposition. [SS-4; TS-4] Phase selectivity is pressure-sensitive because lower N₂ pressure gives SrN rather than SrN₂. [SS-4; TS-4]

**F2 — Route 3**  
Route 3 uses much lower temperature but requires high-pressure NH₃/sealed systems and long-duration growth conditions. [CS-2, CS-4] SS and TS provide insufficient direct NH₃-route evidence for SrN₂ and are treated as neutral source evidence (50). [insufficient evidence from SS; insufficient evidence from TS]

**F3 — Route 3**  
Ammonia-mediated chemistry can enable low-temperature nitride/diazenide growth, but the BaN₂ analogue needs 3–4 months to obtain crystals. [CS-4] Transfer to SrN₂-C2/c is plausible but uncertain because the direct NH₃ evidence is Ba-based and the CS-2 precursor set is not compositionally aligned with SrN₂. [CS-2, CS-4] [Reasonable Inference]

**F4 — Amorphization Tendency**  
The target-like analogues are simple binary diazenides rather than large mixed-cation frameworks, so cation disorder and amorphization risk appear low. [CS-1, CS-3, CS-5; SS-1, SS-2, SS-3; TS-1, TS-2, TS-3] Mixed polyhedral connectivity is mainly seen in less relevant Li/Sr/Ba/Fe nitride analogues, not in the closest SrN₂/BaN₂ diazenide analogues.
