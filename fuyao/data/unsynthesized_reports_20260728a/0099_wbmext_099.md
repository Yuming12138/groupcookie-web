### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

YbGeN₂ is likely a thermodynamic ground-state or very near-ground-state phase because the supplied target E_hull is 0.0000 eV/atom and many CS/SS/TS analogues are also at or near the hull. The analogues support synthesis of related nitrides by solid-state, flux, and metathesis routes, but none provides exact Yb–Ge–N experimental validation, so the confidence is high for feasibility but moderate for a single-shot synthesis. The most critical enabling factor is the strong thermodynamic stability combined with known flux/solid-state nitride routes for divalent/group-14 nitride frameworks (CS:5, SS:2, TS:2). The most critical limiting factor is phase/valence control for Yb²⁺ with GeN₄ tetrahedra, because exact YbGeN₂ precedent is absent and related Li-nitride metathesis can produce heterogeneous side phases (SS:4). Overall, the best first experimental route is alkali-metal azide flux growth, followed by direct nitride sintering for bulk powder optimization.
