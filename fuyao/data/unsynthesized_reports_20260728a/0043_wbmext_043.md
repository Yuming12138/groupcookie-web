### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability**  
K₂HfO₃ has E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom in the target data, which maps to the 90–100 scoring bracket. (Target) All CS/SS/TS analogues listed here also have E_hull = 0.0, so the similarity set does not contradict the target hull stability. (CS1–CS5, SS1–SS5, TS1–TS5) The main uncertainty is experimental, because direct K₂HfO₃ synthesis evidence is insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS and is therefore treated as a neutral experimental sub-signal. (insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS)

**F2 — Route 1**  
Route 1 uses 1000 °C and dry N₂, so it is moderately severe but not high-pressure or >1000 °C by the scoring rule. (SS1, TS1) The main deductions come from controlled dry atmosphere and multi-step flux handling. (SS1, TS1) Volatile K₂O is a phase-control concern in the analogue, but because the reported route is not sealed, it is not double-counted as a sealed volatile-precursor deduction. (SS1, TS1)

**F3 — Route 1**  
KNO₃-derived K₂O gives high precursor activity because the K₂ZrO₃ analogue forms through dissolution of ZrO₂ in excess K₂O flux. (SS1, TS1) HfO₂ is expected to dissolve/react more slowly than ZrO₂ by [Reasonable Inference], so ion diffusion is scored moderate rather than high. (SS1, TS1) Structural reconstruction is favorable because K₂ZrO₃ is the closest K₂M O₃ analogue and shares the MO₅ trigonal-bipyramidal framework motif. (SS1, TS1)

**F2 — Route 2**  
Route 2 is less thermally severe because K₂TiO₃ forms at 600 °C from KOH melt. (SS5, TS5) Deductions are assigned for sealed silver-vessel handling and multi-step flux processing. (SS5, TS5) No H₂/NH₃/vacuum atmosphere or high-pressure condition is reported for the KOH analogue. (SS5, TS5)

**F3 — Route 2**  
The KOH melt route has strong precursor activation because TiO₂ reacts in molten KOH in the analogue. (SS5, TS5) The score is reduced because HfO₂ is expected to be less soluble/reactive than TiO₂ by [Reasonable Inference]. (SS5, TS5) Structural transfer is strong because K₂TiO₃ is Cmcm and has the same mixed OK₄M₂ connectivity motif, but Ti→Hf transfer is less direct than Zr→Hf. (SS5, TS5, SS1, TS1)

Route 3 is synthetically more severe because it uses sealed Ag/Ni containment, dry alkali oxides, controlled Ar/vacuum conditions, and multi-day anneals. (CS1, CS4, SS2, TS2, SS3, TS3) The route does not require >1000 °C or high pressure in the recommended version.
