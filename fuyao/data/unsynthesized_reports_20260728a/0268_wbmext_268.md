### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability**  
The target is supplied with E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom, and all CS/SS/TS analogues listed for comparison also have E_hull = 0.0 eV/atom, so the thermodynamic signal is strongly favorable. (CS1–CS5, SS1–SS5, TS1–TS5) The remaining uncertainty is that the target is a theory-only WBM candidate with no cited experimental synthesis, so I do not assign a perfect 100. (CS/SS/TS analogues provide stability signal; target novelty is from supplied target data.)

**F2 — Route 1**  
Arc melting is severe because it exceeds 1000 °C and effectively exceeds 1400 °C, and analogous borides require Ar/vacuum control plus repeated processing. (CS1, CS3, CS5, SS1, TS1) The route avoids volatile halides and high pressure, which prevents a lower severity score. (CS1, CS5)

**F3 — Route 1**  
Elemental Sc/Nb/B precursors are not highly reactive, but arc melting overcomes much of the diffusion barrier through liquid-phase mixing. (CS1, CS2, CS5) Structural reconstruction is manageable because omega-derived MB12 cuboctahedral boride frameworks are strongly represented in SS/TS analogues. (SS1, SS3, TS1, TS3)

**F2 — Route 2**  
The sealed-tube route has milder temperature severity because ScFeB4/DyCrB4 analogues use about 1070 K, but it still requires controlled Ar/sealed conditions and multiple processing steps. (CS4, CS5) It avoids high pressure and volatile halide precursors. (CS4, CS5)

**F3 — Route 2**  
Kinetics are the main weakness because 700–800 h anneals in analogues imply slow solid-state equilibration. (CS4, CS5) Nb substitution is expected to worsen diffusion by **[Reasonable Inference]** because Nb is refractory and no CS/SS/TS source directly demonstrates ScNbB4 ordering. (CS4, CS5)

**F2 — Route 3**  
Reactive sintering/SPS has high-temperature and controlled-atmosphere penalties because ZrB2 analogues use 1200–1550 °C under Ar/vacuum. (SS1, TS1) Insufficient evidence from CS for exact ScB2/NbB2 precursor use, so the CS signal for this route is neutral 50. (CS1–CS5)

**F3 — Route 3**  
Diboride precursors should reduce structural reconstruction because SS/TS analogues share omega/AlB2-like connectivity. (SS1, SS3, TS1, TS3) The uncertainty is whether Sc and Nb can order into Immm ScNbB4 without remaining as separate ScB2/NbB2 or disordered diboride phases, which is **[Reasonable Inference]**. (SS1, TS1)

**F2 — Route 4**  
The autoclave route has lower temperature severity because the TiB2 analogue forms at 650 °C, but sealed handling, volatile halides, and wash/dry steps add deductions. (SS3, TS3) Insufficient evidence from CS for halide/autoclave synthesis of ScMB4-type compounds, so CS route signal is neutral 50. (CS1–CS5)

**F3 — Route 4**  
Halide/Mg chemistry should be kinetically active, as demonstrated for TiB2 formation from TiCl4/MgB2/Mg. (SS3, TS3) The limiting uncertainty is ternary composition and Sc/Nb ordering, because SS/TS only demonstrate binary TiB2 by this route. (SS3, TS3)

**F4 — Amorphization Tendency**  
The target and closest omega analogues use compact edge/face-sharing cuboctahedral boride networks rather than mixed vertex/edge/face-sharing frameworks, so amorphization risk is low. (SS1, SS3, TS1, TS3) Direct ionic-radius and exact target unit-cell atom-count evidence are insufficient from CS/SS/TS and treated neutral 50 where required; the high F4 score relies on structural simplicity and chemically compatible boride analogues. (CS1, CS5, SS1, TS1)
