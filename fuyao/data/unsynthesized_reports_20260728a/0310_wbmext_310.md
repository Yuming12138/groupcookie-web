Route 1 receives 80/100 because MgPdSb was synthesized using closely related sealed-Ar direct solid-state conditions. (CS, SS, TS) The main penalties are Mg air sensitivity and the need for sealed containment, both [Reasonable Inference] from Mg chemistry and the ampoule-based analogue procedure. The elemental-metal precursor activity is scored low-to-moderate because dense Mg, Pd, and Sn require solid-state interdiffusion unless a liquid phase forms. (CS, SS, TS) Structural reconstruction is scored very highly because MgPdSb has the same Mg–Pd half-Heusler topology and similar bond distances. (CS, SS, TS)

### F3 — Route 2

Arc melting should accelerate homogenization compared with a wholly solid-state reaction, but prolonged annealing remains necessary for ordering and phase purification. (CS, SS) However, Cu→Pd substitution can change melt chemistry and crystallization behavior, and no direct MgPdSn growth experiment is supplied; therefore, Route 3 is better viewed as a secondary route or crystal-growth optimization rather than the first experiment.
