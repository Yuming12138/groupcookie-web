### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability**

CS strongly supports this because all five chemically similar fluorides are reported at E_hull = 0.0, including exact Rb-based hexafluoride analogues (CS). SS and TS also support thermodynamic plausibility because their nearest analogues are on or very near the hull, so there is no insufficient thermodynamic signal from CS/SS/TS (SS, TS).

**F2 — Route 1**  
Route 1 has mild temperature and no GPa pressure, but it requires F2, anhydrous HF, UV irradiation, and fluorine-compatible sealed equipment, which drives the F2 score down to 55 (CS). SS and TS do not provide useful photochemical-HF/F2 evidence, so insufficient evidence from SS and TS is scored neutral = 50 for this route-specific factor (SS, TS).

**F3 — Route 1**  
Kinetics are favorable because the closest RbCrF6 analogue forms at ambient temperature in reactive HF/F2 solution rather than by slow bulk diffusion (CS). The long 10–20 d exposure indicates crystallization/ordering is not instantaneous, so the structural-reconstruction component is reduced despite exact prototype transferability (CS). SS and TS provide insufficient route-specific photochemical kinetic evidence and are neutral = 50 for this route (SS, TS).

**F2 — Route 2**  
Route 2 avoids elemental F2 in the baseline version but requires sealed-tube fluoride handling, inert loading, volatile NbF5 management, and long thermal treatment, so it scores 55 for severity (SS, TS; NbF5 volatility is [Reasonable Inference]). CS also supports sealed fluoride sintering through BaCrF5, so no source is completely neutral for Route 2 (CS).

**F3 — Route 2**  
Binary fluorides can form related phases, but SS/TS analogues require 2 d to 4 weeks and slow cooling, indicating moderate diffusion/ordering barriers (SS, TS). The exact RbNbF6 topology is supported by CS rather than by the sealed-tube SS/TS structures, so structural reconstruction is only moderate (CS, SS, TS).

**F2 — Route 3**  
Route 3 uses low temperature and only autogenous pressure, but HF solvothermal chemistry still requires sealed corrosive equipment and toxic reagent handling, so the F2 score is 55 (SS). Gas-solid fluorination and reported impurity/phase-control issues in related chromium fluorides reduce the kinetic/structural score relative to the photochemical HF route (CS).

**F4 — Amorphization Tendency**
