### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability**  
Yb₃SbN is listed with E_hull = 0.000 eV/atom, which maps to a 90–100 thermodynamic score, and the closest CS/SS/TS analogues also include multiple E_hull = 0.0 anti-perovskite nitrides. (CS, SS, TS) The most relevant corroboration is Sr₃SbN because it shares Sb, N, cubic Pm-3m topology, and E_hull = 0.0. (SS, TS) The remaining uncertainty is that the target is theory-only in the provided data, so thermodynamic stability does not prove experimental accessibility. (CS, SS, TS)

**F2 — Route 1**  
Route 1 avoids >1000 °C and >5 GPa conditions because Sr₃SbN forms at 1070 K under ambient N₂. (SS, TS) The score is reduced for controlled N₂/Ar atmosphere, air-sensitive Yb handling, and likely need to manage Yb/Sb volatility. (SS, TS; [Reasonable Inference]) CS gives insufficient direct evidence for elemental Yb–Sb nitridation, so CS is neutral for this route-specific severity signal: 50. (CS)

**F3 — Route 1**  
Kinetics are favorable because SS/TS show the same Sb–N anti-perovskite can be obtained from pre-alloyed melt beads, which should reduce long-range cation diffusion. (SS, TS) Precursor activity is not rated higher because dense elemental beads are less reactive than molecular or sol-gel precursors. (SS, TS) The main uncertainty is whether Yb₃SbN nitrides as readily as Sr₃SbN, because Yb is smaller and more volatile than Sr. [Reasonable Inference] (SS, TS)

**F2 — Route 2**  
Route 2 uses moderate temperature but has more severe process complexity because CS analogues require RN/R₂In precursor preparation, sealed ampoules, and week-long annealing. (CS) The deductions for multi-step processing, controlled atmosphere, and air-sensitive precursors are directly supported by CS handling and synthesis descriptions. (CS) SS/TS provide insufficient evidence for a rare-earth RN + Yb₂Sb binary-precursor route, so SS and TS are neutral for this route-specific condition signal: 50. (SS, TS)

**F3 — Route 2**  
The binary-precursor route has good structural targeting because RN + R₂In gives rare-earth anti-perovskites in CS. (CS) Kinetics are only moderate because the route uses dense solid precursors and long annealing, and some CS analogues require repeated cycles for phase purity. (CS) The main uncertainty is the In → Sb substitution, which is chemically plausible but not directly demonstrated by CS. [Reasonable Inference] (CS)

**F2 — Route 3**  
Route 3 is below 1000 °C and below GPa pressures, but it uses pressurized N₂, reactive flux, sealed vessels, and post-growth flux removal. (SS, TS) The safety/process deductions are supported by SS/TS reports of MPa/bar-scale N₂, Na flux, glovebox loading, slow cooling, and liquid-NH₃ washing. (SS, TS) CS gives insufficient evidence for flux growth of rare-earth anti-perovskites, so CS is neutral for this route-specific condition signal: 50. (CS)

**F3 — Route 3**  
Flux improves ion transport and nitrogen incorporation, as shown by SS/TS nitride flux syntheses. (SS, TS) The structural score is lower because the flux analogues are not cubic Yb₃SbN-type anti-perovskites, so nucleation selectivity is uncertain. (SS, TS) This route is best viewed as a backup for crystal growth or kinetic activation, not the first powder-synthesis attempt. [Reasonable Inference] (SS, TS)

**F4 — Amorphization Tendency**  
The cubic anti-perovskite framework is simple and highly symmetric, and the closest CS/SS/TS anti-perovskites show ordered Pm-3m connectivity rather than large-cell disorder. (CS, SS, TS) There is insufficient CS/SS/TS evidence to calculate exact target cation radius mismatch, so that source-specific signal is neutral at 50 and the check is treated as not triggered because Yb is the only clear electropositive cation site. [Reasonable Inference] The absence of edge-sharing coexistence in the anti-perovskite analogues supports a low amorphization tendency. (CS, SS, TS)

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

Yb₃SbN is likely a ground-state candidate because the target E_hull is 0.000 eV/atom and multiple chemically, structurally, and thermodynamically similar anti-perovskites are also on the hull. (CS, SS, TS) The strongest synthesis support comes from Sr₃SbN, which has the same Sb–N cubic anti-perovskite motif and was obtained as single-phase powder from elemental melt beads in N₂. (SS, TS) The most important enabling factor is the existence of experimentally synthesized cubic anti-perovskite nitrides using both elemental nitridation and rare-earth nitride/intermetallic routes. (CS, SS, TS) The most important limiting factor is Yb/Sb stoichiometry control under high-temperature inert or nitrogen conditions, especially because sealed handling and air-sensitive precursors are repeatedly required in the analogues. (CS, SS, TS; [Reasonable Inference]) Overall, the material is plausibly synthesizable with moderate difficulty, and Route 1 should be attempted first. (SS, TS)
