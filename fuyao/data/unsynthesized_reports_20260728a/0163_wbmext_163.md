[Target data] Chemically, structurally, and thermodynamically similar references are also largely stable or near-stable, including Cs₄Ga₂Se₅, Cs₂GeSe₃, CsYbSe₂, Cs₂NbAgSe₄, K₃AlSe₃, and Rb₂Se. [CS1–CS5; SS1–SS5; TS1–TS5] The main uncertainty is that the target is theory-only and CS/SS/TS provide insufficient direct experimental evidence for Cs₂TiSe₃ itself. [insufficient direct target synthesis evidence from CS/SS/TS]

**F2 — Route 1**  
Route 1 is penalized for vacuum/protective-gas handling, sealed volatile Se/Cs–Se chemistry, multiple grinding/annealing steps, and toxic/air-sensitive materials. [CS1/SS1/TS1; SS2; Target safety note] The temperature is moderate rather than extreme because the closest solid-state analogue is made at 600–700 °C. [CS1/SS1/TS1]

**F3 — Route 1**  
Binary selenides should be more reactive than refractory oxides, but the long anneals and regrinding in the closest analogue indicate slow ordering kinetics. [CS1/SS1/TS1] The TiSe₅ target framework requires nontrivial reconstruction from Cs₂Se/TiSe₂, so kinetics are only moderate. [Target data; Reasonable Inference]

**F2 — Route 2**  
Route 2 uses high-temperature sealed flux conditions but avoids >1000 °C and high pressure. [CS5; CS4] The severity comes from evacuated ampoules, volatile Cs₂Se₃/Se flux, multi-step flux preparation, and air-sensitive/toxic components. [CS5; CS4; Target safety note]

**F3 — Route 2**  
The Cs₂Se₃ flux route has the best kinetic outlook because reactive flux growth directly produced alkali-rich selenide crystals at 1173 K. [CS5] Slow cooling in related flux routes supports crystallization rather than amorphous quenching. [CS5; CS4] The key uncertainty is that no CS/SS/TS example contains Ti in a Cs₂TiSe₃-like TiSe₅ framework. [insufficient direct Ti-framework evidence from CS/SS/TS]

**F2 — Route 3**  
Route 3 has low thermal severity because analogous A₂Q₃ flux reactions operate at 350 °C. [SS3/TS2] Its score is reduced by vacuum/glovebox handling, pre-synthesized polychalcogenide flux, washing steps, and toxic Se-rich media. [SS3/TS2; Target safety note]

**F3 — Route 3**  
Reactive polyselenide flux should help precursor activity, but Ti incorporation at 350–500 °C is uncertain. [SS3/TS2; Reasonable Inference] The analogue set explicitly notes some related compositions could not be made, which signals a narrower phase window. [TS2]

**F2 — Route 4**  
The solvothermal route benefits from low temperature and no high-pressure requirement beyond autogenous autoclave pressure. [CS2/TS5] It is still penalized for sealed volatile solvent conditions, multiple isolation steps, and toxic/air-sensitive reagents. [CS2/TS5; Reasonable Inference]

**F3 — Route 4**  
Solution-mediated chemistry reduces diffusion barriers, as shown by Cs₂GeSe₃ formation at 170 °C. [CS2/TS5] The route is kinetically uncertain for the target because GeSe₄ tetrahedra are not a close match to TiSe₅ trigonal bipyramids. [CS2/TS5; Target data]

**F2 — Route 5**  
The H₂/Se route is thermally moderate and direct, but it requires a reactive H₂/chalcogen atmosphere and volatile selenium control. [CS3] It also carries toxic/air-sensitive handling penalties. [CS3; Target safety note]

**F3 — Route 5**  
The Cs₂CoSe₂ precedent supports transition-metal alkali selenide formation under H₂/Se, but Ti chemistry is not directly demonstrated. [CS3; insufficient direct Ti evidence from CS/SS/TS] A reducing H₂ stream may conflict with the Ti(IV) charge state implied by Cs₂TiSe₃. [Reasonable Inference]

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

Cs₂TiSe₃ is most likely a thermodynamic ground-state candidate rather than a merely metastable phase because its supplied E_hull is 0.0000 eV/atom and many CS/SS/TS analogues are also on or near the hull. [Target data; CS1–CS5; SS1–SS5; TS1–TS5] The analogue support is strongest for alkali selenide sealed-ampoule and polyselenide flux chemistry, especially Cs₂Se/Cs₂Se₃-based routes. [CS1/SS1/TS1; CS5; CS4] The most critical enabling factor is a reactive Cs–Se flux or binary selenide precursor set that supplies high Se activity and improves Ti incorporation. [CS5; CS4; Reasonable Inference] The main limiting factor is the absence of a direct Ti-containing Cs₂TiSe₃ experimental analogue in CS/SS/TS, especially for the unusual TiSe₅ trigonal-bipyramidal framework. [insufficient direct Ti-framework evidence from CS/SS/TS; Target data] Overall, synthesis looks feasible but will likely require careful air-free flux or sealed-ampoule optimization. [CS1/SS1/TS1; CS5; CS4]
