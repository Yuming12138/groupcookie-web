### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

[CS2, SS4, CS5, TS2, TS3] The most critical limiting factor is the absence of any direct Tl-containing analogue in CS/SS/TS, combined with Tl toxicity from the target safety note and inferred volatility during high-temperature synthesis. [Target safety note; Reasonable Inference] Therefore, the best practical strategy is sealed Ta-tube induction melting plus annealing rather than open arc melting or high-pressure synthesis.
