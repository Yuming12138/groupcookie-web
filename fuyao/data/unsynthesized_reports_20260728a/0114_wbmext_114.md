### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability**  
NdReN₃ receives a high F1 score because the target E_hull is explicitly 0.0000 eV/atom and the MP2020 formation energy is negative. [Target] CS and TS reinforce this with multiple near-hull ternary nitride analogues, while SS gives mixed evidence because some structurally similar high-pressure nitrides are metastable. [CS1–CS5; SS1–SS5; TS1–TS5] The remaining uncertainty is that the target is theory-only and the dataset does not identify experimental synthesis. [Target]

**F2 — Route 1 Flux Severity**  
Route 1 is penalized heavily because analogues use sealed ampoules, inert atmospheres, air-sensitive flux/nitrogen sources, and multi-step heating/cooling/recovery procedures. [CS1, CS4; SS1; TS1–TS3] It avoids multi-GPa pressure and can be screened below 1400 °C, which prevents the most severe pressure and ultra-high-temperature deductions. [SS1; CS1, CS4; Reasonable Inference]

**F3 — Route 1 Flux Kinetics**

[CS1, CS4; SS1; TS1–TS3] The score remains only moderate because Re is refractory and the target ReN₆ R-3c framework is not the same structure type as the closest I4/mmm analogues. **F3 — Route 3 High-Pressure Kinetics**  
High P–T conditions can overcome barriers for dense nitride formation, as shown for W₂N₃, Zr₃N₄, and Hf₃N₄. [SS3–SS5] The kinetics remain uncertain because no SS analogue directly combines Nd and Re or the R-3c NdReN₃ topology. [SS insufficient direct Nd–Re evidence; Target; Reasonable Inference]

[TS5] The kinetic score is limited because CS/SS do not support an Nd/Re ammonothermal route, and Re precursor solubility/valence control is an extrapolation. ### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

NdReN₃ is best interpreted as a **DFT ground-state candidate** because its target E_hull is 0.0000 eV/atom, but it remains experimentally unvalidated because the WBM source identifies it as theory-only. [Target] The CS and TS analogues strongly support the existence of stable rare-earth / transition-metal nitrides, especially via flux methods, while SS gives additional but less chemically direct evidence from dense nitride synthesis. [CS1–CS5; SS1–SS5; TS1–TS5] The most important enabling factor is the combination of zero hull energy, negative formation energy, and a 3D corner-sharing ReN₆ framework with reasonable minimum bond distance. [Target] The most important limiting factor is the lack of a direct Nd–Re–N experimental analogue, especially because Re incorporation into a high-nitrogen ReN₆ environment is inferred rather than demonstrated. [CS insufficient direct Nd–Re evidence; SS insufficient direct Nd–Re evidence; TS insufficient direct Nd–Re evidence; Target; Reasonable Inference] Overall, synthesis appears **moderately feasible but experimentally challenging**, with sealed flux growth the most defensible first attempt. [CS1, CS4; SS1, SS2; TS1–TS3; Reasonable Inference]
