### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability**  
The target record gives E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom, placing EuZrN2 in the highest stability bin; TS supports this because several thermodynamic analogues are also at or near the hull, including ZrN at 0.0 eV/atom and SrZrN2 at 0.0073 eV/atom. **TS** The remaining uncertainty is that the target is WBM theory-only and not experimentally validated, so the zero-hull result supports feasibility but does not prove kinetic accessibility. **TS**

**F2 — Route 1**  
The solid-state route is moderately severe because SrZrN2/SrHfN2 require 1000 °C, 5 days, Mo wrapping, sealed stainless-steel crucibles, Ar glovebox handling, and controlled cooling. **CS/SS** The route avoids multi-GPa pressure and ultra-high temperatures, but air-sensitive nitrides and sealed containment justify condition penalties. **CS/SS**

**F3 — Route 1**  
Kinetics are limited by refractory ZrN and long annealing in SrZrN2/SrHfN2 syntheses, which indicates slow diffusion and nontrivial phase evolution. **SS/TS** Structural reconstruction is favorable because SrZrN2 and EuZrN2 share the same Caswellsilverite R-3m edge/corner-sharing octahedral framework, but impurity reports in the Sr analogues reduce confidence. **CS/SS**

**F2 — Route 2**  
The nitrogen-atmosphere route is less thermally severe because SrCeN2 forms at 1020 K and BaCeN2 at 850 °C, both under N2. **CS** Penalties remain for air-sensitive nitrides, multiple handling steps, and likely Li-containing by-products or volatile nitride species in reactive nitride chemistry. **CS**

**F3 — Route 2**  
Reactive nitride/metathesis chemistry can improve precursor activity versus dry refractory powders because Li2CeN2 and alkaline-earth nitrides react under N2 in the cited analogues. **CS** However, the exact Eu–Zr Caswellsilverite framework is not experimentally demonstrated by the Ce routes, so structural-transfer uncertainty remains. **CS/SS**

**F2 — Route 3**  
Flux synthesis is harsher because La3Nb2N6/La3Ta2N6 analogues use either 1030 °C Li3N flux in sealed Ta/Nb ampoules or Ga flux at 1600 °C. **CS/SS/TS** Sealed ampoules, reactive Li3N, high-temperature flux handling, and post-reaction flux removal make this route challenging. **CS/SS/TS**

**F3 — Route 3**  
Flux routes improve kinetics by providing liquid/flux-mediated transport, as shown by Li3N acting as flux/reactive nitrogen source and Ga acting as flux in La–Nb/Ta nitrides. **CS/SS/TS** The main uncertainty is structural: these flux analogues are not Caswellsilverite ABN2 phases, so successful EuZrN2 crystallization would rely on extrapolation. [Reasonable Inference]

**F4 — Amorphization Tendency**  
EuZrN2 has two distinct cation–N distances, Eu–N 2.55 Å and Zr–N 2.30 Å, so cation-size mismatch is a moderate amorphization/ordering risk. **CS/SS** The simple Caswellsilverite framework and lack of face-sharing in the target-like analogues reduce amorphization tendency compared with larger, mixed face/edge/corner-sharing structures. **CS/SS**

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

EuZrN2 is likely a thermodynamic ground-state or near-ground-state phase because the target E_hull is reported as 0.0000 eV/atom and TS contains near-hull nitride analogues. **TS** The strongest synthesis support comes from SrZrN2 and SrHfN2, which are experimentally synthesized Caswellsilverite R-3m nitrides with very similar Zr/HfN6 octahedral connectivity. **CS/SS** The most critical enabling factor is the close structural match to SrZrN2, including the same octahedral edge/corner-sharing ABN2 topology. **SS** The most critical limiting factor is Eu nitride chemistry: Eu-containing precursors are not directly demonstrated in the provided Caswellsilverite examples, so Eu oxidation state, air sensitivity, and competing Eu–N/Zr–N phases must be controlled. **CS/SS** A nitrogen-atmosphere reactive nitride route is the best first attempt because it balances lower temperature with proven rare-earth nitride reactivity. **CS**
