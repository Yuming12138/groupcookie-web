### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability:** The target’s supplied E_hull is 0.000 eV/atom, and all top CS/SS/TS analogues also have E_hull = 0.0, so a high score of 95 is justified. (Target data; CS1–CS5; SS1–SS5; TS1–TS5) The remaining uncertainty is that the target is theory-only, so the hull result does not prove kinetic accessibility. (Target data)

**F2 — Route 1:** Route 1 avoids high temperature and high pressure because the exact Cs₂HgO₂ analogue is made at 500/350 °C and the Cd analogue Rb₂CdO₂ is made at 500–750 °C under sealed conditions. (CS2; SS1; TS1; SS5; TS5) The deductions come from controlled Ar atmosphere, sealed volatile/air-sensitive alkali oxides, and toxic CdO. (CS2; SS1; TS1; SS5; TS5)

**F3 — Route 1:** Kinetics are favorable because the closest structure analogue is already the same I4/mmm topology with linear MO₂ units, reducing the required structural reconstruction. (CS2; SS1; TS1) The main kinetic uncertainty is that Cd in Rb₂CdO₂ forms CdO₄ rather than linear CdO₂, so stoichiometry and Cs size may be critical for selecting the target topology. (SS5; TS5)

**F2 — Route 2:** Route 2 remains below 1000 °C and ambient pressure, but it receives larger penalties because flux growth likely requires more operations, longer sealed anneals, and stricter precursor handling. (SS5; TS5; CS1; CS4) The long anneal and sealed Ag/Pt containment make this route more challenging than the direct bomb route. (SS5; TS5; CS1; CS4)

**F3 — Route 2:** Excess CsOₓ should improve mass transport [Reasonable Inference], and alkali-oxide flux behavior is consistent with long sealed syntheses of Rb₂CdO₂ and Cs-rich oxometallates. (SS5; TS5; CS1; CS4) The kinetics score is limited by analogue reaction times of 14–42 d or longer, which indicate slow ordering and narrow phase-control windows. (SS5; TS5; CS1; CS4)

**F4 — Amorphization:** Only the cation-size mismatch check is triggered because Cs–O and Cd/Hg–O distances differ strongly, while the target structure is simple and lacks face-sharing connectivity. (Target data; CS2; SS1; TS1) The exact target atom count is not explicitly supplied by CS/SS/TS, so those sources are neutral 50 for the C2 sub-check, but the provided I4/mmm target description supports no C2 trigger. (Target data; SS1; TS1)
