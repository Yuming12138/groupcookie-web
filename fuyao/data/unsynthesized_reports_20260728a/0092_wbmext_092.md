### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

Sr₂NF is likely a thermodynamic ground-state or very near-ground-state phase because the supplied target E_hull is 0.0000 eV/atom and multiple Caswellsilverite nitride-halide analogues are stable or near-stable. [Target data, CS1, SS2–SS4, TS1–TS4] The strongest synthesis support comes from the direct Ba₂NF analogue and the same-cation Sr₂NCl/Sr₂NBr analogues, all made by sealed Ar solid-state routes. [CS1, SS1–SS3, TS1–TS3] The most critical enabling factor is the pre-existing Caswellsilverite alkaline-earth nitride-halide structure family. [CS1, SS1–SS4, TS1–TS4] The most critical limiting factor is practical phase control under air-/moisture-free conditions, especially avoiding residual SrF₂/Sr₂N/SrO and maintaining N/F stoichiometry.
