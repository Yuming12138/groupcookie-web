### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability.** Target AlSbO4 is supplied at E_hull = 0.0 eV/atom, which strongly supports ground-state synthesizability [Target]. SS and TS analogues are mostly stable or nearly stable, including LiSb3O8, Sb2O5, Ca2Sb2O7, LiSbO3, and BiSbO4 [SS1–SS5/TS1–TS5]. CS is more mixed because CS1 is 0.241 eV/atom above hull, while CS2–CS5 are near-hull or stable; this conflict is weighted less than SS/TS because SS/TS are structurally and thermodynamically closer to the target [CS1–CS5; SS1–SS5; TS1–TS5].

**F2/F3 — Route 1.** Air solid-state synthesis receives a favorable severity score because close analogues use air, ambient pressure, and ≤900 °C conditions [SS1/TS1/TS5]. Kinetics are moderate rather than easy because repeated regrinding is needed in analogues and Al2O3 can be refractory [SS1/TS1/TS5; Reasonable Inference]. CS provides insufficient direct signal for this route and is treated as neutral 50.

**F2/F3 — Route 2.** Combustion synthesis has better precursor activity because acetate/nitrate/fuel chemistry gives intimate mixing [SS4/TS3]. Its severity is lower than Route 1 because it involves more processing steps and may need high finishing calcination [SS4/TS3]. CS provides insufficient direct combustion-route signal and is treated as neutral 50.

**F2/F3 — Route 3.** Hydrothermal synthesis is mild in temperature and pressure because CS hydrothermal analogues use 523 K and aqueous autoclaves [CS2/CS4]. Its kinetics score is limited by uncertain dense AlSbO4 nucleation and lack of SS/TS hydrothermal support, so SS and TS are neutral 50 for this route [CS2/CS4; Reasonable Inference]. This route is plausible for exploratory crystals but less reliable than solid-state synthesis [CS2/CS4; Reasonable Inference].

**F2/F3 — Route 4.** Flux growth benefits from a liquid transport mechanism and has a structurally relevant hydrophilite CuSb2O6 basis [SS3]. The severity score is reduced by evacuated ampoule handling, flux toxicity, multi-step workflow, and crystal-quality issues such as twinning [SS3]. CS and TS provide insufficient independent flux-growth signal and are neutral 50.

**F2/F3 — Route 5.** High-pO2 synthesis directly addresses Sb(V) stabilization because Sb2O5 formation is reported only under high oxygen pressure in the analogue source [SS2/TS2]. The route is penalized for specialized high-pressure oxygen conditions even though temperature is only moderate [SS2/TS2]. CS provides insufficient high-pO2 Al–Sb oxide signal and is neutral 50.
