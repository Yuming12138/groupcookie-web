### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability**  
The target’s reported E_hull is 0.000 eV/atom, which places it in the 90–100 range by the provided rubric. (Target data) The closest structural and thermodynamic analogue CaNiN is also E_hull = 0.0 and is experimentally synthesized, strengthening confidence that this topology is not intrinsically unstable. (SS1/TS1) Remaining uncertainty is that the target is theory-only, and CS/SS/TS do not provide direct experimental EuNiN validation. (Target data; insufficient direct EuNiN evidence from CS/SS/TS)

**F2 — Route 1**  
Route 1 avoids extreme temperature and GPa pressure because the CaNiN analogue is made at 800 °C under N₂. (SS1/TS1) The main penalties are controlled atmosphere, multi-step grinding/annealing, and air-sensitive Eu nitride handling. (SS1/TS1; SS3/TS3; [Reasonable Inference]) CS2–CS5 provide insufficient direct nitride-atmosphere evidence because they are phosphides, so they are neutral 50 for this subfactor. (CS2–CS5)

**F3 — Route 1**  
The exact CaNiN-type structure strongly helps structural reconstruction, and the Ni–N bond length/topology match is excellent. (SS1/TS1) Kinetics are still moderate because EuN/Ni are solid precursors and analogues require long annealing or repeated processing. (SS1/TS1; CS3–CS5) The main uncertainty is whether Eu valence and nitrogen activity stabilize EuNiN rather than Eu nitrides plus Ni. [Reasonable Inference]

**F2/F3 — Route 2**  
Flux/azide synthesis has better mass transport because BaCuN and Eu₂SiN₃ form through alkali flux or azide-assisted routes at 800–900 °C. (CS1; SS4/TS4) The severity score is lower because alkali metals, azides, sealed tubes, slow cooling, and N₂ pressure increase operational complexity. (CS1; SS4/TS4) Transferability is not exact because the best flux analogue is BaCuN rather than EuNiN, so this route is promising but less direct than Route 1. (CS1; [Reasonable Inference])

**F2/F3 — Route 3**  
The Ni-tube wall reaction is supported by Li₃Sr₃(NiN)₄, which has linear Ni–N units and related nitride octahedra at 800 °C. (SS2/TS2) Its kinetics benefit from reactive Li–nitride chemistry, but phase control is risky because the known analogue incorporates Li/Sr and the target is Li-free EuNiN. (SS2/TS2; [Reasonable Inference]) Evidence for direct Eu substitution is insufficient from SS2/TS2, so that transferability component is partly neutral. (SS2/TS2)

**F2/F3 — Route 4**  
NH₃ nitridation is operationally moderate and is proven for CdNi₃N at 743 K with regrinding and repeated NH₃ exposure. (SS5/TS5) However, CdNi₃N is structurally different from EuNiN, so the structural reconstruction score is lower. (SS5/TS5) CS data provide insufficient NH₃-route evidence and are neutral 50 for this route. (CS1–CS5)

**F2/F3 — Route 5**  
High-temperature sealed-ampoule Eu nitride chemistry is supported by LiEu₄(BN₂)₃ at 1950 K in sealed Nb ampoules. (SS3/TS3) The route receives severe F2 penalties because it may require >1400 °C, sealed reactive nitride mixtures, and specialized ampoules. (SS3/TS3) It is a fallback because SS3/TS3 proves Eu nitride accessibility but not the Eu–Ni–N CaNiN-type structure. (SS3/TS3; [Reasonable Inference])

**F4 — Amorphization Tendency**

(Target data; SS1/TS1; [Reasonable Inference]) The structure is compact and ordered, and the dominant polyhedral connectivity is edge/corner sharing rather than simultaneous vertex/edge/face sharing.
