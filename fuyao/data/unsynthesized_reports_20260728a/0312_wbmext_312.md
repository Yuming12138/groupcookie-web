### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

RuB is likely a DFT ground-state or near-ground-state phase because the target E_hull is 0.0000 eV/atom and the closest TS analogues are also on-hull. [Target record, TS1, TS2, TS3, TS4, TS5] Experimental realization is not proven because the target record identifies it as theory-only and no CS/SS/TS entry reports this exact WC-type RuB phase. [Target record, CS1, SS1, TS1] The analogue support is nevertheless strong because Ru₂B₃ is the closest chemical/structural/thermodynamic match and has nearly identical Ru–B bond lengths near 2.18–2.19 Å. [CS2, SS1, TS1] The key enabling factor is access to Ru–B bonding networks through elemental high-temperature synthesis under Ar or vacuum. [CS1, CS2, SS1, SS3, TS1, TS3] The key limiting factor is phase competition among RuB, Ru₂B₃, and RuB₂, which is directly suggested by multiphase Ru–B analogue synthesis and mono-phase RuB₂ stability. [CS2, SS1, SS3, TS1, TS3]
