**F2 — Route 1 Condition Severity**  
Route 1 uses standard high-temperature phosphide solid-state conditions: 900–1100 °C, Ar atmosphere, alumina crucible, sealed quartz ampoule, grinding, and extended annealing. [CS-2, CS-4, SS-1, SS-2, SS-5, TS-1, TS-2, TS-5] Deductions come from T >1000 °C, volatile P/sealed-tube handling, multi-step grinding/tempering, and air-sensitive Ba/P handling. [CS-4, SS-5, TS-5; Reasonable Inference] No high-pressure or >1400 °C deduction is applied because analogues do not require those conditions. [CS-2, CS-4, SS-5, TS-5]

**F3 — Route 1 Reaction Kinetics**  
The kinetic score is moderate because elemental Rh is diffusion-limited, but Ba–Rh–P and same-prototype BaMP analogues are successfully synthesized by long solid-state anneals. [SS-5, TS-5; CS-2, CS-4, SS-1, SS-2, TS-1, TS-2] Structural reconstruction is favorable because BaIrP and BaPtP match the target P2₁3 topology closely. [CS-2, CS-4, SS-1, SS-2, TS-1, TS-2] The remaining uncertainty is whether Rh substitutes cleanly into the BaMP P2₁3 field without forming BaRh₂P₂ or RhP impurities. [SS-5, TS-5; Reasonable Inference]

**F2 — Route 2 Condition Severity**  
Route 2 adds RhP pre-synthesis, so it is more operationally severe than Route 1 despite potentially better local Rh–P chemistry. [Reasonable Inference] The deductions come from >1000 °C annealing, sealed handling of volatile phosphorus, vacuum/sealed precursor preparation, multiple steps, and air-sensitive Ba. [SS-5, TS-5; Reasonable Inference] CS/SS/TS provide insufficient direct evidence for the exact RhP-assisted route, so unsupported route-specific details are treated as neutral source evidence. [insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS]

**F3 — Route 2 Reaction Kinetics**  
Preforming RhP should improve Rh–P contact and may reduce the burden of direct Rh + P reaction during BaRhP formation. [Reasonable Inference] The diffusion barrier remains moderate because the final phase still requires Ba incorporation and long-range ordering near 1000–1100 °C. [SS-5, TS-5; Reasonable Inference] Same-prototype BaIrP/BaPtP analogues keep the structural-reconstruction score favorable. [CS-2, CS-4, SS-1, SS-2, TS-1, TS-2]
