### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability:** The supplied target E_hull is 0.0000 eV/atom, so the target is scored near the top of the ≤0.05 eV/atom band. [Target] CS, SS, and TS analogues are mostly on-hull or near-hull, and TS includes on-hull sulvanite Cu₃VSe₄, strengthening the thermodynamic argument. [CS1–CS5; SS1–SS5; TS5] The main uncertainty is that the target is theory-only and not experimentally validated in the supplied record. [Target]

**F2/F3 — Route 1:** Elemental sealed-ampoule synthesis is well supported by Cu–M–S and Cu–M–Se analogues, but vacuum sealing, sulfur volatility, long anneals, and regrinding lower F2. [CS1; CS4; TS5] Kinetics are only moderate because elemental Mn/Cu diffusion and long-range sulvanite ordering likely require weeks, consistent with the long anneals in Ti–Cu–S and Cu₃VSe₄ analogues. [CS1; TS5; Reasonable Inference]

**F2/F3 — Route 2:** Binary sulfide precursors reduce condition severity relative to flux and should improve chemical activity while retaining standard sealed-ampoule processing. [CS5; SS2; TS2] F3 is higher than Route 1 because CuS/Cu₂S-type precursors already contain Cu–S bonding, but exact conversion to MnCu₃S₄ is not directly demonstrated by CS/SS/TS. [CS5; SS2; insufficient direct MnCu₃S₄ evidence from CS/SS/TS]

**F2/F3 — Route 3:** Flux growth has higher operational severity because it adds moisture-sensitive sulfide flux handling, slow cooling, and washing. [SS1; TS1] Its kinetic score is high because sulfur-rich flux can provide fast transport at ~500 °C, but phase selectivity is risky because NaCu₄S₄ itself required a specific Na/Ba/S flux. [SS1; TS1]

**F2/F3 — Route 4:** Thin-film synthesis has no direct support in CS/SS/TS, so CS, SS, and TS are neutral 50 for route evidence. [insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS] The route is plausible only as [Reasonable Inference] because sulfurization can overcome bulk diffusion, but ordered sulvanite crystallization and Mn:Cu:S control remain uncertain.

**F4 — Amorphization Tendency:** The sulvanite framework is compact and not a >40-atom complex cell, and the structural analogue shows edge/corner sharing rather than vertex + edge + face coexistence. [TS5] One amorphization trigger is assigned for likely cation-size/charge mismatch between Cu and high-valent tetrahedral Mn, but this is [Reasonable Inference] because CS/SS/TS do not provide ionic radii.

MnCu₃S₄ is best interpreted as a DFT ground-state candidate because the supplied target E_hull is 0.0000 eV/atom and the TS set includes stable sulvanite Cu₃VSe₄. [Target; TS5] The analogue support is strong for sealed-tube chalcogenide synthesis and moderate-to-strong for the sulvanite topology, but CS/SS/TS do not directly report MnCu₃S₄ synthesis. [CS1; CS4; CS5; SS2; TS5] The most critical enabling factor is maintaining sulfur chemical potential in a sealed ampoule while using reactive sulfide precursors. [CS5; SS2; TS5] The most critical limiting factor is stabilizing Mn on the tetrahedral high-valent sulvanite site without forming MnS/CuS/Cu₂S competing phases. [Reasonable Inference; insufficient direct Mn evidence from CS/SS/TS] Overall, the material is plausibly synthesizable but requires careful redox, stoichiometry, and phase-control screening. [CS1; CS5; TS5; Reasonable Inference]
