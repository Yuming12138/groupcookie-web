|:--:|:--:|:--|:--:|:--|
| 1 | All | Preserving peroxide O–O units during synthesis | High | Use low-temperature alkaline H2O2 crystallization at ~0 °C, following K3TaO8. **CS1 / SS1 / TS1** |
| 2 | All | Ti-for-Ta chemical transferability is unproven | High | Start from highly reactive hydrous TiO2·xH2O and screen peroxide-rich CsOH/H2O2 conditions; this is **[Reasonable Inference]** from Ta2O5·aq use. **CS1 / SS1 / TS1** |
| 3 | Route 2 | Air/moisture sensitivity of CsO2/Cs2O2 reagents | High | Load and seal in dry conditions using Au/Ag tubes, as in Cs2FeO4 and Rb2MnO4 analogues. **CS5 / SS4 / TS3** |
| 4 | Route 2 | Slow ordering kinetics in solid-state routes | High | Use fine grinding, reactive alkali oxidants, long anneals, and slow heating/cooling; long-duration precedent exists for Ru/Mn analogues. **CS3 / SS4 / TS5** |
| 5 | Route 4 | Competing ordinary titanate formation | High | Avoid very high-temperature Ba2TiO4-like conditions; if flux is used, keep oxidizing and Cs-rich by **[Reasonable Inference]**. **CS4 / CS5** |
| 6 | All | Stoichiometry control for volatile/reactive Cs/O species | Med–High | Use slight Cs/O excess and sealed systems where heated, following excess alkali and sealed-tube analogues. **CS5 / SS4 / TS5** |
| 7 | Route 3 | Peroxide decomposition and pressure buildup in autoclaves | Medium | Use low fill fractions, lower-temperature screens, and O2/peroxide control; hydrothermal and peroxide-aging precedents support controlled sealed/oxygen handling. **SS2 / SS3 / TS2** |
| 8 | Route 1 | Crystal growth and characterization sensitivity | Medium | Grow slowly at 0 °C and handle gently; K3TaO8 yields single crystals but notes sensitivity during characterization. **CS1 / SS1 / TS1** |
