NdWN₃ is most likely a computational ground-state candidate because the target E_hull is 0.0000 eV/atom, but it remains experimentally unvalidated in the supplied dataset. [Target] The strongest synthesis support comes from chemically, structurally, and thermodynamically similar rare-earth transition-metal nitrides made by Li₃N or Ga flux. [CS1, CS2, SS1, SS3, TS1, TS2] The most important enabling factor is a reactive nitride flux, especially Li₃N, because it supplies nitrogen and enhances transport below the extreme temperatures required for refractory W diffusion. [CS1, CS2; Reasonable Inference] The main limiting factor is phase control among NdN, WNₓ, W metal, and possible off-stoichiometric ternary nitrides. [CS5, TS5; Reasonable Inference] Overall, synthesis appears feasible but experimentally challenging, with Route 1 providing the best balance of thermodynamic support and practical transferability.
