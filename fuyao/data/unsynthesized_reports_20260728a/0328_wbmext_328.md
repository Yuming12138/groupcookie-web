### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability**  
NaAu receives a high F1 score because the target E_hull is 0.0000 eV/atom and the provided analogue sets all contain stable E_hull = 0.0 entries. [Target, CS1–CS5, SS1–SS5, TS1–TS5] The remaining uncertainty is experimental, because the target is described as a theory-only WBM DFT-relaxed candidate rather than a reported synthesis. [Target]

**Route 1 F2/F3 — Sealed Direct Elemental Alloying**  
F2 is limited by sealed/vacuum handling, air-sensitive Na, and a multi-step anneal/cool schedule seen in K₂Au₃, K₃Au₅In, and NaTl precedents. [SS2, TS2, SS4, TS4, CS3] F3 is moderate because molten Na should aid Au reaction, but long anneals and slow cooling in analogues indicate ordering and phase-control kinetics are nontrivial. [SS2, TS2, SS4, TS4, CS3, Reasonable Inference]

**Route 2 F2/F3 — NaN₃–Au Reactive Solid-State**  
F2 is moderate because the route is low-temperature but uses hazardous NaN₃ and sealed evacuated ampoules. [SS5, TS5] F3 is relatively favorable because NaAu₂ forms phase-pure at 400–600 °C, but the target NaAu stoichiometry/prototype differs, so phase selectivity remains uncertain. [SS5, TS5, Target]

**Route 3 F2/F3 — High-Pressure Elemental Route**  
F2 is the best route-specific score because the closest high-pressure precedents operate at 2–4 GPa and 700–805 °C, below the rubric’s >5 GPa and >1000 °C deduction thresholds, though specialized equipment remains a practical burden. [SS1, TS1, SS3, TS3] F3 is strongest because the closest analogues share P6₃/mmc symmetry and alkali–Au cuboctahedral connectivity with the target. [Target, SS1, TS1, SS3, TS3]

**Route 4 F2/F3 — Full Melt Growth**  
F2 is lowest because full melt growth likely requires >1000 °C while containing volatile Na in sealed hardware. [CS2, SS2, TS2, CS3, Reasonable Inference] F3 benefits from liquid-phase transport, but phase control is risky because related Na–Au/K–Au systems show multiple stable compositions and slow-cooling sensitivity. [SS2, TS2, SS5, TS5]
