### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

EuCoN3 is likely a **thermodynamic ground-state candidate** by DFT because its supplied E_hull is 0.0000 eV/atom, but it remains experimentally unvalidated. [Target] The strongest synthesis support comes from CS rare-earth transition-metal nitride flux analogues and the Eu-containing Eu2SiN3 flux synthesis. [CS1, CS3, CS4] TS supports the stability of related nitride/diazenide chemistry and shows Co-containing phases can be made under controlled high-temperature conditions. [TS1, TS3, TS4, TS5] SS supports short N–N/azide-like motifs but is chemically less transferable because many analogues are molecular azide/cyanide salts. [SS1, SS3, SS5] The key enabling factor is a reactive alkali/Ga flux with high nitrogen activity. [CS1, CS3, CS4] The key limiting factor is phase selectivity among Eu nitrides, Co-containing phases, and possible azide/diazenide side products. [CS1, TS3; competing-phase risk is **[Reasonable Inference]**]
