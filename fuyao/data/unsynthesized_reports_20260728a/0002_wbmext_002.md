### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

Mg(PdO₂)₃ is likely a thermodynamic ground-state or near-ground-state phase because its reported E_hull is 0.0000 eV/atom and many closest Pd oxide analogues are also on hull (SS1–SS5, TS1–TS5). The synthesis evidence is strongest from SS1/TS1 because Ba₂Hg₃(PdO₂)₇ shares PdO₂-rich square-planar/octahedral Pd chemistry and is made under high oxygen pressure. The most important enabling factor is high oxygen chemical potential to stabilize mixed Pd²⁺/Pd⁴⁺ character [Reasonable Inference from SS1/TS1, SS2/TS2, TS5]. The main limiting factor is phase selectivity: ambient routes may form PdO, Pd metal, MgO, or simpler Pd oxides rather than the ordered MgPd₃O₆ framework [Reasonable Inference from SS4/TS3 and SS1/TS1]. Overall, synthesis looks plausible but should be attempted first under high-O₂ pressure rather than ordinary ceramic conditions.
