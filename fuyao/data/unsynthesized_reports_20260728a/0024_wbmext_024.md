### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability**  
The target’s provided E_hull is 0.0000 eV/atom, and the closest TS analogues include multiple arsenates at E_hull = 0.0–0.0014 eV/atom, so the thermodynamic score is very high. [TS] SS reinforces this because the closest structural analogues Cd(AsO₃)₂ and SrAs₂O₆ are on-hull or nearly on-hull. [SS] CS is supportive overall but less decisive because one Mg-containing chemical analogue, MgSiO₆, is high in E_hull, so CS is not weighted as strongly for F1. [CS]

**F2 — Route 1**  
Solid-state synthesis uses moderate temperatures of 700–900°C, air, and standard furnaces in the analogues, so the condition severity is low. [SS, TS, CS] The main deduction is arsenic toxicity and volatility management through excess As₂O₅. [CS, SS, TS] Remaining uncertainty is whether Mg substitution narrows the phase window relative to Sr/Cd analogues. [Reasonable Inference from SS/TS]

**F3 — Route 1**  
Kinetics are favorable because carbonate/nitrate + As₂O₅ routes have produced related arsenates without extreme equipment. [SS, TS, CS] The same MAs₂O₆ structural family in Sr/Cd analogues reduces reconstruction burden, but Mg’s smaller radius could increase competing-phase risk. [SS, TS; Reasonable Inference] The 24–48 h hold is an extrapolated compromise between 36 h SrAs₂O₆ and shorter Ni/Co arsenate sintering. [SS, TS, CS; Reasonable Inference]

**F2 — Route 2**  
Hydrothermal synthesis is mild in temperature and avoids open high-temperature arsenic loss, so the severity score is high. [SS, TS] The sealed autoclave is standard but still imposes pressure and arsenic-safety constraints. [SS, TS] The route loses only the toxic-precursor deduction under the specified scoring rubric. [SS, TS]

**F3 — Route 2**  
Hydrothermal kinetics are strong because solution transport bypasses slow solid-state cation diffusion, and several AsO₆-containing arsenates crystallize hydrothermally. [SS, TS] The closest same-prototype Cd(AsO₃)₂ analogue makes structural reconstruction highly favorable. [SS, TS] Remaining uncertainty is that the Cd analogue’s listed precursor snippet is chemically inconsistent with Cd–As–O, so precursor selection for Mg is inferred rather than directly copied. [SS, TS; Reasonable Inference]

**F2 — Route 3**  
The kbar route is much more severe because it uses sealed high-pressure hydrothermal or oxidizing conditions. [CS, SS, TS] AsPO₅ evidence shows oxygen pressure may be needed to manage arsenic(V)-oxide decomposition pressure. [SS, TS] This route is therefore scored as challenging despite temperatures below 1000°C. [CS, SS, TS]

**F3 — Route 3**  
Kinetics benefit from hydrothermal transport and high temperature, but the pathway likely requires denser reconstruction than Route 2. [CS, SS, TS] CrAsO₄ shows a high-pressure hydrothermal arsenate can form as a single product, while AsPO₅ shows phase control can be difficult. [CS, SS, TS] The route is best treated as a backup if lower-pressure methods fail. [Reasonable Inference from CS/SS/TS]

**F2 — Route 4**  
Multi-anvil synthesis is penalized heavily because CS evidence uses 10 GPa, specialized equipment, long duration, and sealed capsules. [CS] SS and TS provide insufficient direct evidence that Mg(AsO₃)₂ needs or benefits from multi-GPa pressure, so they are neutral at 50 for this route. [insufficient evidence from SS/TS] The route is feasible only as a fallback. [CS; Reasonable Inference]

**F3 — Route 4**  
A gel precursor may improve kinetics, but long high-pressure holds indicate slow ordering. [CS] Structural reconstruction is uncertain because MgSiO₆ is chemically related through MgO₆ octahedra but not structurally equivalent to Hydrophilite-derived Mg(AsO₃)₂. [CS; SS, TS] This produces the lowest route score despite strong pressure-stabilization logic. [CS; Reasonable Inference]

**F4 — Amorphization Tendency**

[SS, TS] The main amorphization risk is the Mg–As octahedral size/charge mismatch, which may frustrate crystallization relative to Cd/Sr analogues. ### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

Mg(AsO₃)₂ is likely a genuine ground-state or near-ground-state phase because the target E_hull is 0.0 eV/atom and TS analogues are overwhelmingly on-hull or near-hull. [TS] The synthesis case is strong because SS and TS include very close MAs₂O₆ analogues, especially Hydrophilite-derived Cd(AsO₃)₂ and P-31m SrAs₂O₆. [SS, TS] The most critical enabling factor is the existence of hydrothermal and solid-state arsenate routes that already stabilize AsO₆ octahedral frameworks. [SS, TS, CS] The most critical limiting factor is arsenic volatility/toxicity and stoichiometry control, especially for high-temperature routes. [CS, SS, TS] Overall, the best first attempt is hydrothermal crystallization, followed by a controlled solid-state As₂O₅-buffered route if hydrothermal products are hydrated or multiphase. [SS, TS, CS; Reasonable Inference]
