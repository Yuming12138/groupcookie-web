### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability.**  
The target is exactly on the calculated hull at E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom, which supports ground-state thermodynamic accessibility. (Target data) The CS set contains five E_hull = 0 analogues, the SS set contains four E_hull = 0 analogues plus one near-hull Zr₂Al₃ at 0.007 eV/atom, and the TS set contains five E_hull = 0 analogues. (CS1–CS5; SS1–SS5; TS1–TS5) Remaining uncertainty is experimental rather than computational, because CS/SS/TS do not report direct Sc₂Al₃Ir synthesis; direct-target experimental evidence is insufficient from CS/SS/TS and neutral 50.

**F2 — Route 1.**  
Arc melting has moderate severity because it needs very high local temperature but no high pressure, reactive gas, or sealed vessel. (CS2; SS2, SS4; TS2) Exact arc temperatures are not given in CS/SS/TS, so the >1400 °C penalty is a [Reasonable Inference] from Ir-containing target chemistry rather than a reported source condition.

**F3 — Route 1.**  
Kinetics are moderate because elemental metals are less reactive than salt/solution precursors, but molten processing and repeated remelting improve homogenization. (SS2; SS4; TS2) Structurally similar aluminides crystallize from melt routes, supporting the structural reconstruction score. (CS2–CS4; SS2–SS3; TS2–TS3)

**F2 — Route 2.**  
The annealed route is more severe because it combines arc melting with sealed or evacuated annealing. (SS1/TS1; SS3/TS3; TS5) The extra severity is justified by reported reduced-pressure Ar tubes, evacuated quartz tubes, and long annealing schedules. (SS1/TS1; SS3/TS3; TS5)

**F3 — Route 2.**  
Kinetics improve after pre-melting, but long annealing in analogues indicates slow ordering or diffusion. (CS5; SS1/TS1; TS5) This route is valuable as a phase-purity correction rather than the fastest discovery route [Reasonable Inference]. (SS3/TS3)

**F2 — Route 3.**  
Solid-state powder sintering has high handling and atmosphere burden because SS1/TS1 uses purified Ar under reduced pressure, pellet pressing, and a multi-stage 340 h heat treatment. (SS1; TS1) Fine Sc/Al powder sensitivity is a [Reasonable Inference], not directly reported in CS/SS/TS.

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

Sc₂Al₃Ir is most likely a DFT ground-state candidate rather than a strongly metastable phase because its reported E_hull is 0.0000 eV/atom. (Target data) The synthesis support is strong but indirect: CS/SS/TS show many Al-rich intermetallic analogues made from elemental metals by arc melting, melt growth, high-frequency melting, or annealing, but none reports Sc₂Al₃Ir itself. (CS1–CS5; SS1–SS5; TS1–TS5) The key enabling factor is the broad success of elemental melt routes for chemically and structurally related aluminides. (CS2; SS2, SS4; TS2, TS5) The main limiting factor is kinetic and compositional control, especially Ir dissolution, Sc/Al oxidation or loss, and competing phases during cooling [Reasonable Inference]. (SS2/TS2; SS3/TS3; CS3) Overall, the target is a good synthesis candidate, with arc melting plus optional annealing as the most practical first attempt. (CS2; SS2–SS4; TS2–TS5)
