**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability.** The target E_hull is supplied as 0.0000 eV/atom, and the closest exact-composition Ba₂FeO₄ analogue plus the same-structure Sr₂FeO₄ analogue are also reported at E_hull = 0.0. (TS1–TS5) The remaining uncertainty is not hull stability but whether the predicted I4/mmm polymorph is experimentally selected over the known monoclinic Ba₂FeO₄ polymorph. (CS1 / SS1 / TS1; target data)

**F2 — Route 1.** Route 1 avoids >1000 °C and high-pressure equipment, which supports a high condition-severity score. (CS1 / SS1 / TS1) The deductions come from multi-step dry flux preparation/slow cooling and caustic or moisture-sensitive hydroxide handling. (CS1 / SS1 / TS1) The structural score is penalized because the flux-grown exact-composition phase is monoclinic with tetrahedral Fe, while the target is I4/mmm with FeO₆ octahedra. ### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

Ba₂FeO₄ is best viewed as a **DFT ground-state candidate with meaningful polymorph risk**, because the supplied target E_hull is 0.0 eV/atom while exact-composition Ba₂FeO₄ is already known experimentally in a different monoclinic/tetrahedral structure. (target data; CS1 / SS1 / TS1) The analogue support is unusually strong chemically because Ba₂FeO₄ itself has been synthesized, and unusually strong structurally because Sr₂FeO₄ has the same I4/mmm FeO₆ framework. (CS1 / SS1 / TS1; SS2 / TS2) The most important enabling factor is access to Fe(IV) Ba–Fe–O chemistry through hydroxide flux or high oxygen pressure. (CS1 / SS1 / TS1; SS2 / TS2) The most important limiting factor is phase selection between monoclinic tetrahedral Ba₂FeO₄ and target tetragonal octahedral Ba₂FeO₄. (CS1 / SS1 / TS1; target data) A practical synthesis campaign should start with Route 1 flux screening and then use Route 2 high-O₂ annealing if the target I4/mmm phase is not obtained.
