# SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

## F1: Thermodynamic Stability

The supplied target record reports \(E_\mathrm{hull}=0.0000\) eV/atom and a strongly negative formation energy, indicating a calculated ground-state position relative to the assessed chemical space [target validation record]. CS, SS, and TS analogues provide supporting but indirect evidence: TiRuP, ZrRhP, ZrRuP, NbRhP, and TiNiP are reported at zero hull energy, while the less favorable listed examples remain only slightly above hull [CS/SS/TS]. The main uncertainty is that the target is explicitly theory-only and has no direct experimental synthesis confirmation [target validation record].

## F2: Route 1

Route 1 receives a moderate F2 score because related Pnma phosphides use Ar arc melting, sealed quartz annealing at 1000 °C, phosphorus excess, and multistep processing [CS/SS/TS]. The main penalties arise from the controlled/sealed atmosphere, phosphorus volatility, and the need for melting followed by prolonged homogenization [CS/SS/TS]. No evidence from CS/SS/TS indicates high pressure or temperatures above 1400 °C, so those factors are neutral [CS/SS/TS].

## F2: Route 2

Route 2 is more operationally demanding because it requires a pre-reacted master alloy, a large Cu/P flux excess, sealed-capsule processing, controlled cooling, and acid flux removal [CS/SS/TS]. The reported flux method provides a credible precedent, but its analogue temperature and duration are incompletely specified, so the severity estimate contains uncertainty [CS/SS/TS]. The TiRh master-alloy substitution and proposed temperature window are [Reasonable Inference].

## F2: Route 3

Direct sealed-tube synthesis is consistent with the reported 800–1150 °C solid-state preparation of ternary phosphides [TS]. Its moderate-to-low F2 score reflects vacuum sealing, phosphorus volatility, and likely repeated homogenization steps, although no high-pressure operation is required [CS/SS/TS]. The exact number of grinding/annealing cycles is not specified, so that portion is [Reasonable Inference].

## F3: Route 1

Arc melting improves precursor contact by producing a reacted bulk alloy, but the elemental Ti/Rh precursors are relatively low-activity and Rh is refractory, limiting diffusion kinetics [CS/SS/TS; refractory-metal interpretation is a Reasonable Inference]. The 4-day annealing used for Pnma analogues indicates that homogenization and ordering are not instantaneous [CS/SS/TS]. Structural reconstruction is comparatively favorable because the target and NbRhP/ZrRhP analogues share Pnma topology and closely related coordination environments [SS/TS].

## F3: Route 2

The flux route receives the highest F3 score because the Cu/P-rich melt provides liquid-mediated mass transport, and related TiRuP/ZrRuP materials were synthesized by Cu-flux growth [CS/SS/TS]. Pnma ZrRuP is particularly relevant because it was obtained using the same broad method [CS]. The uncertainty is whether Cu flux stabilizes TiRhP rather than competing Cu-containing or binary phases; this is [Reasonable Inference].

## F3: Route 3

Direct solid-state reaction is kinetically less favorable because Ti and Rh must interdiffuse through a multiphase pellet, and no highly reactive molecular or solution precursors are reported [CS/SS/TS; kinetic interpretation is a Reasonable Inference]. The Pnma analogue structures reduce the structural-reconstruction penalty [CS/SS/TS]. Nevertheless, the need for long annealing or repeated homogenization is plausible from the related TiCrP and Pnma phosphide syntheses [CS/SS/TS].

## F4: Amorphization Tendency

The only activated check is the estimated difference between Ti and Rh effective cation/metallic sizes, which is [Reasonable Inference]. CS/SS/TS do not provide a unit-cell atom count above 40 or explicit mixed vertex/edge/face-sharing information, so both checks are scored neutral at 50 as required. The strong structural similarity to several ordered Pnma phosphides argues against an intrinsically high amorphization risk, but experimental crystallization behavior remains unverified [SS/TS; target validation record].

# SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

TiRhP is likely a calculated ground-state phase rather than an intentionally metastable phase because its supplied energy above hull is 0.0000 eV/atom [target validation record]. The analogue evidence is strong: NbRhP and ZrRhP reproduce the Pnma topology with nearly identical coordination environments, while TiRuP demonstrates that Ti can participate in a related ternary phosphide framework [CS/SS/TS]. The most important enabling factor is the close structural and chemical correspondence to established Rh-containing Pnma phosphides [SS/TS]. The most critical limitation is kinetic and compositional control, especially phosphorus loss, slow Ti–Rh interdiffusion, and competition from binary or Cu-containing phases [CS/SS/TS; competition statement is a Reasonable Inference]. A Cu/P-rich flux route is therefore the preferred first experiment, followed by arc melting and sealed annealing for bulk phase verification [CS/SS/TS; recommendation is expert judgment].
