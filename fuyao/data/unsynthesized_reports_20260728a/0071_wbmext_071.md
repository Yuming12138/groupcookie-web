### SECTION 1 — Possible Synthesis Routes

I weight **K3TaO8** most heavily because it is simultaneously chemically, structurally, and thermodynamically close: same tetragonal **I-42m** prototype, same alkali–high-valent-metal peroxide framework, and reported low-temperature crystallization from **KOH/H2O2** solution [CS1/SS1/TS2]. High-temperature ceramic/flux/floating-zone routes in other chemically similar oxides are **not retained as primary routes** because they describe ordinary refractory oxides at ~800–1503 K/°C and do not preserve peroxide-like O–O units [CS2/CS3/CS4/CS5]; insufficient target-specific peroxide-route evidence from those high-temperature CS entries is treated as neutral source signal **50**.

### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability**  
The target is supplied as E_hull = 0.000 eV/atom, which maps to the 90–100 range, and the closest analogues include stable or near-stable peroxide/oxide entries [CS1/SS1/TS2/SS2/SS5/TS1/TS3]. The thermodynamic evidence is internally supportive because K3TaO8 is near hull and the peroxide analogues SrO2/BaO2 are on hull [CS1/SS2/SS5/TS1/TS3]. Remaining uncertainty is that the target is theory-only and no CS/SS/TS entry directly validates experimental Rb3HfO8 [CS/SS/TS].

**F2 — Route 1**  
Route 1 is mild because the closest analogue crystallizes at 0 °C from KOH/H2O2 solution without high pressure or inert atmosphere [CS1/SS1/TS2]. The only deduction is multi-step handling of fresh hydrous precursor, dissolution, cooling, and isolation, which is **[Reasonable Inference]** from the reported Ta2O5·aq requirement [CS1/SS1/TS2].

**F3 — Route 1**  
The kinetics are favorable because solution crystallization bypasses refractory cation diffusion and the same I-42m framework is already experimentally known for K3TaO8 [CS1/SS1/TS2]. The kinetic score is not perfect because Hf substitution for Ta may not preserve the same peroxide charge chemistry **[Reasonable Inference]** [CS1/SS1/TS2].

**F2 — Route 2**  
Route 2 has low thermal severity because peroxide precipitation analogues are prepared at room temperature/low temperature [SS2/SS5/TS1/TS3]. It receives deductions for controlled O2/CO2-free handling and multi-step washing/drying because those controls are explicitly important for SrO2/BaO2 peroxide stability [SS2/SS5/TS1/TS3].

**F3 — Route 2**  
Precipitation gives good precursor mixing and direct peroxide formation, supported by alkaline-earth peroxide syntheses and K3TaO8 peroxide solution chemistry [CS1/SS2/SS5/TS1/TS3]. The structural-reconstruction score is lower because binary peroxide precipitation does not prove assembly of the Rb3HfO8 I-42m framework [SS2/SS5/TS1/TS3].

**F2 — Route 3**  
Hydrothermal conditions are moderate because SS3 uses 180–330 °C Teflon-bomb aging, but the adapted peroxide route should be lower temperature **[Reasonable Inference]** [SS3]. Deductions reflect sealed volatile peroxide handling and multiple preparation/isolation steps, while insufficient CS/TS evidence for hydrothermal peroxide-hafnate synthesis is treated as neutral **50** [CS/TS].

**F3 — Route 3**  
Hydrothermal solution transport should help Hf mobility relative to ceramic methods, based on oxide dissolution/crystal growth in concentrated base [SS3]. The main uncertainty is structural: SS3 supports alkaline hydrothermal growth generally, but not the A3MO8 peroxide framework, so CS/TS route-specific signal is insufficient and neutral **50** [SS3/CS/TS].

**F2 — Route 4**  
Route 4 is severe because TS analogues require oxygen pressure/flow, sealed ampoules/autoclaves, long heating, and air/moisture-sensitive alkali peroxide/superoxide reagents [TS4/TS5]. It avoids >1000 °C and >5 GPa penalties, but controlled oxidizing systems and reactive precursors dominate the severity [TS4/TS5].

**F3 — Route 4**  
Solid-state kinetics are poor because TS4/TS5 reactions require long times of 8–62 days even for reactive alkali oxidant systems [TS4/TS5]. The structural mismatch is also large because TS4/TS5 are alkali ruthenates rather than I-42m peroxide hafnates, so substantial reconstruction would be required [TS4/TS5].
