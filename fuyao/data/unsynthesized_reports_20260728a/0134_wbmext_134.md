### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability**  
TbWN₃ is predicted at E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom, so it scores in the highest thermodynamic-stability bin. [Target] TS analogues La₃Nb₂N₆ and La₃Ta₂N₆ are also reported at E_hull = 0.0 eV/atom, so thermodynamic similarity supports feasibility. [TS1, TS2] The uncertainty is that the target is a theory-only WBM-relaxed candidate with no provided experimental synthesis. [Target]

**F2/F3 — Route 1**  
Li₃N flux has the strongest precedent because CS/TS analogues use rare-earth metals, Nb/Ta metals, and Li₃N in sealed Ta/Nb ampoules. [CS1, CS4, TS1, TS2] The condition score is limited by >1000 °C operation, sealed inert handling, and air-sensitive Li₃N. [CS1, CS4, TS1, TS2] The kinetics are better than direct sintering because Li₃N acts as both nitrogen source and flux, but W remains a refractory cation and the target structure is not the same prototype as the analogues. [CS1, CS4, SS2, SS3, Target; Reasonable Inference]

**F2/F3 — Route 2**  
Ga flux is supported by the same La₃Nb₂N₆ / La₃Ta₂N₆ family, but it requires ~1600 °C and controlled Ar/N₂ processing. [CS1, CS4, TS1, TS2] The higher temperature improves W dissolution but increases severity and risk of competing phases. [CS1, CS4; Reasonable Inference] Kinetics are moderate because molten Ga improves transport, while N₂ is less chemically active than Li₃N. [CS1, CS4; Reasonable Inference]

**F2/F3 — Route 3**  
Solid-state synthesis is supported by U₂MnN₃ and U₂CrN₃ preparation from mononitride plus transition metal under N₂ at 1200–1600 °C. [CS3, CS5] The target transfer is weaker because no source directly demonstrates a rare-earth tungsten ternary nitride made this way. [insufficient evidence from SS and TS, neutral 50] Kinetics are poor because W is refractory and the route likely needs repeated regrinding and long anneals. [CS3, CS5; Reasonable Inference]

**F2/F3 — Route 4**  
High-pressure synthesis is supported primarily by SS4, where W₂N₃ is synthesized at ~5 GPa and high temperature.
