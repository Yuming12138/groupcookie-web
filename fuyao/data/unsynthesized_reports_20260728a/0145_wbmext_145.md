## SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

### F1 — Thermodynamic Stability

The target is reported at \(E_\mathrm{hull}=0.0000\) eV/atom, corresponding to the highest stability bracket in the supplied scoring rubric. The closest thermodynamic analogues La₃Nb₂N₆, La₃Cr₂N₆, La₃Ta₂N₆, Ce₂MnN₃, and Th₂MnN₃ also have \(E_\mathrm{hull}=0.0\) eV/atom, providing strong supporting precedent. **[TS]** CS and SS do not independently report the target’s thermodynamic value, so their direct source-specific scores are neutral at 50; the target record and TS analogue evidence determine F1. **[CS, SS, TS]**

### F2 — Route 1: Li₃N flux

The closest La₃Nb₂N₆ and La₃Ta₂N₆ syntheses use Li₃N flux at approximately 1030 °C for about 60 h in sealed Ta/Nb ampoules. **[CS, SS, TS]** The route therefore receives deductions for temperature above 1000 °C, sealed reactive-flux handling, multiple operations, and air-sensitive precursors. The absence of high pressure keeps the severity substantially below that of the high-pressure Hf₃N₄/Zr₃N₄ routes. **[SS]**

### F2 — Route 2: Ga flux

The Ga-flux routes require approximately 1600 °C, staged Ar/N₂ handling, long reaction periods, and specialized furnace equipment. **[CS, SS, TS]** The high temperature produces the principal penalty, with additional deductions for multistep operation and air-sensitive metallic precursors. No direct evidence indicates that Ga itself must be treated as a volatile sealed precursor under the scoring rubric, so that deduction is not applied. **[CS, SS, TS]**

### F2 — Route 3: Direct solid state

The related Ce₂MnN₃ synthesis uses 900 °C under N₂, while Th₂MnN₃ and related compounds are formed from binary nitrides and transition-metal powders under 1 atm N₂. **[TS]** This supports a lower thermal-severity score than the flux routes, but the target route still requires air-sensitive nitride precursors and likely repeated grinding or precursor preparation. **[TS] [Reasonable Inference]**

### F3 — Route 1: Li₃N flux

Li₃N provides a reactive nitrogen-containing flux and can enhance mass transport relative to direct reaction of dense refractory solids. **[CS, SS, TS]** The target’s framework is strongly supported by the exact I4/mmm topology of the La₃Nb₂N₆, La₃Cr₂N₆, and La₃Ta₂N₆ analogues, so structural reconstruction is scored highly. **[CS, SS, TS]** The principal uncertainty is whether V can be incorporated without producing VN or V-rich secondary phases; this is a **[Reasonable Inference]** from the absence of a direct Nd–V–N synthesis example.

### F3 — Route 2: Ga flux

Ga flux has a demonstrated ability to produce structurally related rare-earth transition-metal nitrides. **[CS, SS, TS]** Its liquid or flux-mediated transport improves kinetics, but the 1600 °C schedule may promote competing reactions and nitrogen stoichiometry changes. **[Reasonable Inference]** Structural reconstruction remains favorable because the target and the closest Ga-flux products share the same I4/mmm framework family. **[CS, SS, TS]**

### F3 — Route 3: Direct solid state

The Ce₂MnN₃ and Th₂MnN₃ examples establish that rare-earth or actinide nitrides can react with transition-metal sources under N₂ to form ternary nitrides. **[TS]** However, related synthesis may require multiple grinding/pressing cycles, indicating slow solid-state diffusion and incomplete conversion. **[TS]** The target’s larger I4/mmm framework and mixed polyhedral connectivity likely require more extensive reconstruction than the binary-nitride starting materials, which lowers the kinetics score. **[TS] [Reasonable Inference]**

### F4 — Amorphization tendency

The target contains two inequivalent Nd sites, VN₅ square pyramids, and mixed face-, edge-, and corner-sharing nitrogen-centered polyhedra. **[CS, SS, TS]** The mixed connectivity triggers C3. The large size difference between Nd and V is assessed as a second trigger, but this is an extrapolation rather than a reported amorphization measurement. **[Reasonable Inference]** The unit-cell atom-count criterion has insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS and is assigned a neutral source score of 50.
