Route 3 is intermediate because induction heating improves reaction kinetics, but the closest induction analogues are ternary rare-earth–Mg–Cu phases rather than exact binary LuCu [SS2, SS5, TS2, TS5].

LuCu is likely a DFT ground-state or near-ground-state phase because the supplied target E_hull is 0.0000 eV/atom and the close CS/SS/TS analogues are also on the hull [CS1–CS5, SS1–SS5, TS1–TS5].
