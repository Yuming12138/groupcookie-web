### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability**  
The target has E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom in the supplied target record, so it falls in the 90–100 stability band. CS, SS, and TS all support this high score because close analogues are either on the hull or only slightly above it: exact CdMoO4/ZnMoO4/CdWO4 entries are E_hull = 0, and the less stable Cu/Li analogues remain near the hull (CS1–CS5, SS1–SS5, TS1–TS5). The remaining uncertainty is that the target is theory-only and competes with an experimentally referenced tetragonal CdMoO4 polymorph (CS1, SS4, TS3, Target record).

**F2 — Route 1**  
Route 1 uses mild hydrothermal temperatures and common autoclave equipment, so no high-temperature, high-pressure >5 GPa, or special-atmosphere penalties apply (CS1, CS2, SS3, TS2). Deductions come from multi-step hydrothermal handling and cadmium precursor toxicity (CS1, CS2; target safety note). The main uncertainty is that exact CdMoO4 hydrothermal evidence favors tetragonal CdMoO4, not the target P2/c polymorph (CS1, SS4, TS3).

**F3 — Route 1**  
Hydrothermal dissolution gives favorable kinetics because P2/c ZnMoO4 crystallizes at 453 K for 48 h and exact CdMoO4 forms hydrothermally at 473 K (CS1, CS2, SS3, TS2). Structural reconstruction is only moderate because the exact CdMoO4 analogue is tetrahedral/tetragonal while the target requires octahedral P2/c ordering (CS1, SS4, TS3). This route is kinetically accessible but phase-selective rather than intrinsically difficult (CS1, CS2).

**F2 — Route 2**  
Route 2 is penalized for T > 1000 °C because CdWO4 flux growth uses 1050 °C for one week (SS1, TS1). It is also penalized for multi-step flux handling and cadmium-bearing feed toxicity, but not for high pressure or reactive atmosphere because the analogue route is ambient-pressure furnace growth (SS1, TS1). Insufficient evidence is not assigned for SS/TS because both directly support the route through CdWO4 flux growth (SS1, TS1).

**F3 — Route 2**  
Flux transport improves ion mobility and provides a strong structural template because CdWO4 is the closest P2/c Cd-containing octahedral analogue (SS1, TS1). The kinetics are not scored higher because pre-reacted feed and week-long growth imply slower equilibration than hydrothermal crystallization (SS1, TS1). The main uncertainty is whether Mo substitution will preserve the CdWO4-type P2/c lattice or revert to known tetragonal CdMoO4 chemistry (CS1, SS1, TS1).

**F2 — Route 3**  
Route 3 avoids >1000 °C if kept within the 700–950 °C analogue-derived range, but it receives penalties for volatile-component containment by [Reasonable Inference], multi-step grinding/firing, and toxic Cd precursors (SS5, TS4, CS1). Insufficient evidence from CS for direct solid-state P2/c CdMoO4 is scored neutral at 50, while SS/TS provide useful solid-state analogue evidence (SS5, TS4). The route is operationally standard but less phase-selective than hydrothermal/flux approaches (SS5, TS4).

**F3 — Route 3**  
Solid-state oxide kinetics are weakest because diffusion through CdO/MoO3-derived products requires repeated grinding and long annealing, as seen in related solid-state molybdate/tungstate routes (SS5, TS4). Structural reconstruction from binary oxides is complete and may favor tetragonal CdMoO4, reducing the structural score (CS1, SS4, TS3). ### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

The target CdMoO4 P2/c phase is likely thermodynamically viable because its supplied E_hull is 0.0000 eV/atom and many CS/SS/TS analogues are also on or near the hull (CS1–CS5, SS1–SS5, TS1–TS5, Target record). The analogue support is strong but not uniform: CdWO4 provides an excellent P2/c Cd–octahedral structural analogue, ZnMoO4 provides a P2/c Mo-based hydrothermal analogue, and exact CdMoO4 evidence points to a competing tetragonal tetrahedral phase (SS1, TS1, CS2, SS3, TS2, CS1, SS4, TS3). The most critical enabling factor is solution or flux transport, which can overcome slow oxide diffusion and promote crystallization under controlled cooling (CS1, CS2, SS1, TS1). The most critical limiting factor is polymorph selectivity against known tetragonal CdMoO4, especially because the target is theory-only in the supplied record (CS1, SS4, TS3, Target record). Overall, synthesis is feasible but should be approached as a polymorph-targeting experiment rather than a simple stoichiometric CdMoO4 preparation (CS1, CS4, CS5, SS1, TS1).
