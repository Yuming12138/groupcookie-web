### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability**  
Eu3Pb is predicted exactly on the hull at 0.0000 eV/atom, so the target receives a near-maximum thermodynamic score. [Target data] The CS set contains several on-hull Eu-rich intermetallics and an exact Pb-bearing same-prototype analogue, while SS/TS top analogues are all on-hull. [CS-1–CS-5; SS-1–SS-5; TS-1–TS-5] Remaining uncertainty is experimental, because the target is explicitly theory-only in the supplied validation note. [Target data]

**F2 — Route 1**  
Route 1 avoids >1000 °C and high pressure, matching Ce3Pb synthesis at 600–850 °C in sealed quartz ampoules. [CS-4] The main severity penalties come from controlled sealed atmosphere, Pb volatility/toxicity, and Eu air sensitivity. [CS-4; SS-1; TS-1; Reasonable Inference] The long duration is inconvenient but not an explicit F2 deduction unless treated as a kinetic penalty. [CS-4]

**F3 — Route 1**  
The kinetics are favorable relative to refractory oxides because the precursors are elemental metals and Pb can provide liquid-assisted contact. [CS-4; Reasonable Inference] The structural reconstruction score is high because Ce3Pb and Nd3Tl share the U3Si-type framework with the target. [CS-3; CS-4] The penalty is slow ordering/homogenization, since Ce3Pb required 500–1000 h. [CS-4]

**F2 — Route 2**  
Route 2 stays below ~900–1000 °C and avoids pressure, but melt processing adds controlled-atmosphere, sealed-system, and multi-step penalties. [CS-1; CS-5] Pb retention and Eu/Pb safety are more demanding in a melt than in a static sinter. [CS-4; Target data; Reasonable Inference] No CS/SS/TS evidence suggests high pressure is needed, so that deduction is not applied. [CS-1; CS-5; SS-5; TS-5]

**F3 — Route 2**  
Melt homogenization improves precursor activity and diffusion, as shown by Eu-Au and Eu-Ir sealed crucible analogues. [CS-1; CS-2; CS-5] Structural ordering is less certain than Route 1 because the exact Eu3Pb phase has no supplied experimental synthesis and the closest same-prototype Pb analogue was made by long sintering, not melt growth. [CS-4; Target data] Phase segregation/undercooling risk is supported by Eu-Au phase-control difficulty. [CS-2]

**F2 — Route 3**  
Route 3 uses moderate temperature and ambient pressure, but controlled sealed handling, molten Pb, decanting, and multi-step crystal growth lower the condition score. [SS-5; TS-5; Reasonable Inference] CS/SS/TS do not directly report Pb self-flux growth for Eu3Pb, so flux-specific evidence is neutral 50. [insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS] The route is therefore exploratory rather than preferred. [Reasonable Inference]

**F3 — Route 3**  
A liquid Pb flux should maximize transport and reduce diffusion limitations, which supports a high kinetic score. [Reasonable Inference] However, the Eu-rich 3:1 target may not crystallize from Pb-rich solution, and CS/SS/TS provide no direct flux-composition window. [Reasonable Inference; insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS] The structural support comes from same-prototype CS analogues, but the growth pathway remains uncertain. [CS-3; CS-4]
