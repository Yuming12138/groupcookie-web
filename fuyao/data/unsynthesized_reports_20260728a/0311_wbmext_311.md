### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability.** LiCdSb is reported at E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom with negative MP2020 formation energy, so the thermodynamic score is high [Target]. Similar retrieved compounds are also mostly hull-stable, including LiCdAs, NaMgSb, KCd₄As₃, NaCd₄As₃, RbCd₄As₃, and EuCd₂Sb₂ [CS1–CS4, SS1–SS5, TS1–TS5]. Remaining uncertainty is experimental rather than computational, because the target is a theory-only WBM candidate with no identified synthesis in the source dataset [Target].

**F2 — Route 1.** The Bridgman route avoids >1000 °C and high pressure, which lowers severity [CS1]. The penalties come from vacuum sealing, volatile/reactive Li/Cd handling, multiple thermal-control steps, and cadmium toxicity [CS1, Target, Reasonable Inference]. SS and TS give insufficient direct Bridgman evidence, so they are neutral 50 for this route’s condition transferability.

**F3 — Route 1.** Kinetics are favorable because the route is melt-based and the closest analogue LiCdAs is the same half-Heusler F-43m structure [CS1, Target]. Liquid-mediated transport should reduce diffusion barriers relative to ordinary powder solid-state reaction [CS1, Reasonable Inference]. The main uncertainty is whether Sb substitution changes the crystallization window enough to favor competing Li–Sb or Cd–Sb phases [Reasonable Inference].

**F2 — Route 2.** The direct sealed-tube route uses moderate high temperatures and ambient pressure, matching NaMgSb, TlCdSb/NaCdAs-type, and EuCd₂Sb₂ preparations [CS2, CS4, SS4, TS4]. Severity remains moderate because Ar sealing, volatile Cd/Li containment, and toxic/air-sensitive precursors are still required [CS2, CS4, Target, Reasonable Inference]. Exact one-step LiCdSb half-Heusler evidence is insufficient from SS/TS, so exact-prototype transfer is neutral 50.

**F3 — Route 2.** Elemental reactions are less activated than solution routes, but several related pnictides form directly from elements under sealed high-temperature conditions [CS2, CS4, SS4]. Low-melting components should help diffusion, but one-step annealing may not fully control half-Heusler ordering [Reasonable Inference]. Phase purity uncertainty is moderate because related Cd–pnictide syntheses sometimes report impurities or foreign interferences [SS4, TS4].

**F2 — Route 3.** Refractory-metal ampoule synthesis adds glovebox handling, arc-welding, jacketing, slow cooling, and possible annealing, so it is more operationally severe [SS1–SS3, TS1–TS3, CS5]. It still avoids high pressure and can stay below 1000 °C for the LiCdSb adaptation [Reasonable Inference]. CS evidence is insufficient for exact LiCdSb formation by this route, so CS exact-prototype transfer is neutral 50.

**F3 — Route 3.** Long anneals and slow cooling can improve ordering and crystallinity in related Cd–pnictide systems [SS1–SS3, TS1–TS3]. Kinetics should be acceptable because the components are low-melting and the route allows extended equilibration [Reasonable Inference]. The limiting kinetic factor is phase competition, since structurally similar Cd–pnictides reported side products and poor crystallinity before optimization [SS1–SS3, TS1–TS3].

**F4 — Amorphization Tendency.** LiCdSb triggers mixed polyhedral connectivity because the target description includes corner-, edge-, and face-sharing polyhedra [Target]. It also likely triggers cation size mismatch between Li and Cd, but this is an ionic-radius extrapolation rather than direct CS/SS/TS evidence [Reasonable Inference]. The simple half-Heusler prototype and crystalline LiCdAs analogue moderate the risk [CS1, Target].

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

LiCdSb is best interpreted as a likely predicted ground-state phase because its reported E_hull is 0.0000 eV/atom and its formation energy is negative [Target]. The strongest synthesis support is the chemically closest LiCdAs analogue, which is experimentally synthesized as the same half-Heusler F-43m prototype by Bridgman growth [CS1]. Broader CS/SS/TS analogues support sealed high-temperature routes for alkali/Cd/pnictide and Cd/Sb-containing phases, but many are not the same prototype [CS2, CS4, SS1–SS5, TS1–TS5]. The most critical enabling factor is liquid or melt-assisted transport under sealed ampoule conditions, which should help overcome diffusion and ordering barriers [CS1, Reasonable Inference]. The most critical limiting factor is controlling Li/Cd volatility and Cd toxicity while avoiding competing Li–Sb or Cd–Sb phases [Target, SS1–SS4, TS1–TS4, Reasonable Inference].
