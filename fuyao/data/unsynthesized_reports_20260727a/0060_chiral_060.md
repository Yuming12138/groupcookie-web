### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

InSe₂F is best interpreted as a **metastable but plausible** phase because its E_hull is 0.130 eV/atom, above the hull but inside the 0.05–0.20 eV/atom screening window, and it passes the reported dynamic-stability screen [Target]. The strongest support comes from structurally similar 2D In–Se sheet compounds synthesized in evacuated silica tubes and from Se–halide analogues containing Se–Se bonds near the target Se–Se distance [SS1, SS2, SS5, Target]. The most critical enabling factor is sealed-tube control of Se chemical potential combined with a low-complexity one-cation In framework [SS1, SS5, TS2; Reasonable Inference]. The most critical limiting factor is the lack of any direct experimental In–Se–F analogue in CS/SS/TS, especially because F incorporation may compete with InF₃ segregation or non-target In–Se phases [CS3, CS5, SS1–SS5; Reasonable Inference]. Overall, the material is worth experimental screening, with Route 1 as the most rational first attempt and flux/CVT as follow-up crystal-growth strategies [CS2, SS1, SS2, SS5, TS1].
