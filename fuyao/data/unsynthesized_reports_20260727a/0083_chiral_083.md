### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability**  
The target’s 0.180 eV/atom hull places it in the 65–85 rubric band, so 75/100 is appropriate because it is metastable but not far outside known recoverable windows. TS gives the strongest support: ZnP₂, AgTe₃I, and Pb₂BiP have similar high thermodynamic placement yet are associated with successful synthesis routes. (TS1, TS2, TS5) CS and SS analogues are mostly lower-hull phases, so they support chemical plausibility but not the target’s exact metastable energy. (CS1–CS5, SS1–SS5)

**F2 — Route 1 CVT**  
The route avoids >1000 °C and high pressure, but it requires evacuated sealed tubes, volatile P/I chemistry, multiple thermal stages, and hazardous iodine/phosphorus handling. These deductions are supported by CVT iodide analogues and sealed ZnP₂ pressure control. (SS1, SS2, SS5, TS1)

**F3 — Route 1 CVT**  
Kinetics are favorable relative to dry solid state because vapor transport reduces diffusion length, and SS analogues show Bi–I transport/crystal growth at moderate temperatures. The score is capped because the exact BiP₂I structure is not known experimentally and SS1 reports small amounts/twinning. (SS1, SS2, SS5, TS1)

**F2 — Route 2 Solid-State / Melt-Anneal**  
The route uses moderate temperatures but still requires vacuum sealing, volatile components, long anneal/cooling schedules, and hazardous P/I handling. CS4 and CS5 show sealed-tube solid-state feasibility but also long cooling/annealing requirements. (CS4, CS5, TS1)

**F3 — Route 2 Solid-State / Melt-Anneal**  
Reaction kinetics are weaker than CVT because dense elements must reconstruct into the target framework without a mobile transport medium. Long anneals, oxygen sensitivity, and phase-window issues in CS/TS analogues justify the lower kinetics score. (CS2, CS4, CS5, TS1, TS5)

**F2 — Route 3 Hydrothermal HI**  
Hydrothermal synthesis has low temperature and no GPa pressure penalty, but the sealed acidic HI medium, volatile iodine species, and toxic/corrosive reagents impose moderate severity. The route is directly supported for Te–I and Ag–Te–I analogues, not for Bi–P–I. (SS3, SS4, TS2)

**F3 — Route 3 Hydrothermal HI**
