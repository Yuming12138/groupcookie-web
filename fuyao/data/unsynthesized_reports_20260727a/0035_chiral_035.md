The target is best regarded as a **moderately metastable phase**, because its calculated \(E_\mathrm{hull}\) is 0.080 eV/atom rather than zero, although this value is within the range of several synthesized thermodynamic analogues [Target record; TS-1–TS-5]. The most critical limitation is iodine retention and selective formation of the P6₅22 mixed S/I framework rather than CoS₂, cobalt iodides, or other competing phases [Target structure; SS-4; TS-2, TS-3; Reasonable Inference].
