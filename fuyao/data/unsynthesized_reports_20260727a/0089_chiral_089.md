The target’s E_hull = 0.190 eV/atom places it in the 65–85 band, so 78/100 is appropriate. ### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

AgSe₂Cl₃ is most likely a metastable but accessible phase rather than a ground-state compound because its E_hull is 0.190 eV/atom, near the top of the moderate metastability window, while TS contains experimentally synthesized analogues in a similar energy range (TS1, TS4, TS5). The CS evidence supports mild chloride crystallization but is chemically indirect, so it is weighted less than SS and TS for this Ag–Se–Cl target (CS1–CS4, SS3, TS5). The most critical enabling factor is low-temperature halochalcogen transport, especially SeCl₄-assisted sealed-ampoule chemistry, because it can deliver Se and Cl without requiring high-temperature bulk diffusion (SS3). The most critical limiting factor is phase selectivity: close solvothermal and hydrothermal analogues often produce multiphase mixtures requiring manual separation (SS1, SS2, TS5). Overall, synthesis appears plausible but not routine, with Route 1 offering the best balance of chemical specificity and kinetic accessibility (SS3, TS5).
