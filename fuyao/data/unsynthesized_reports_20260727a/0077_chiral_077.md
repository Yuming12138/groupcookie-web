**F3 — Route 1:** Kinetics are better than direct elemental synthesis because LaI₃ provides a chemically activated iodide source and local La–I coordination. [CS-2] Structural reconstruction remains nontrivial because no exact LaP₂I phase is experimentally reported, although La–P/P–P and P–I-adjacent motifs are well represented in SS analogues. **F3 — Route 2:** Elemental La is less reactive than halide or flux feeds, so precursor activity is modest. [SS-2] The route is still plausible because single-cation diffusion is simpler than multi-cation ordering, but complete formation of the chiral La–P–I network is required. [Target]

**F2 — Route 3:** CVT has the harshest route score because La–P transport precedents use ~1000–1080 °C gradients, vacuum ampoules, I₂, and multi-stage growth. [CS-1, SS-2, TS-5] The method is better suited to crystal growth than rapid bulk synthesis. [SS-2, TS-5]

**F3 — Route 3:** Iodine transport strongly improves mass transport, which supports a high ion-diffusion score. [CS-1, SS-2] The kinetic uncertainty is stoichiometry drift because iodine is both transport agent and constituent in LaP₂I. [Target, SS-2]

**F2 — Route 4:** Flux growth lowers the effective crystallization temperature but adds multi-step handling, dry flux requirements, and sealed-system halide risk. [CS-5, TS-4] Insufficient evidence from SS for flux-specific LaP₂I behavior, so SS is neutral 50 for this route. [SS-1]

**F3 — Route 4:** Halide fluxes can accelerate diffusion and crystal growth, giving strong kinetic support. [CS-5, TS-3, TS-4] The limiting issue is phase selection because flux analogues report air sensitivity or difficult crystallization. [CS-5, TS-4]

**F2 — Route 5:** High-pressure synthesis is demanding even though the proposed 3–5 GPa does not trigger the rubric’s >5 GPa penalty. [SS-5] CS and TS provide insufficient evidence for high-pressure La–P–I synthesis, so they are neutral 50 for this route. [CS-1, TS-1]

**F3 — Route 5:** High pressure can stabilize La polyphosphide frameworks, but the iodine-containing target has no direct high-pressure precedent in the provided data. [SS-5, Target] The route is therefore a fallback for metastability, not the first choice. [SS-5]
