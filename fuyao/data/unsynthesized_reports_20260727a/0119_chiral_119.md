### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability:** The target’s E_hull of 0.172 eV/atom places it in the metastable but potentially accessible 65–85 scoring band (Target). CS/SS/TS analogues show mixed thermodynamic signals: several synthesized analogues are at 0 eV/atom, CS4 is near the target at 0.163 eV/atom, and SS5/TS1 is reported at 0.311 eV/atom yet has experimental synthesis evidence (CS4, SS5/TS1). Exact target E_hull evidence is insufficient from CS, insufficient from SS, and insufficient from TS, so those exact-target source contributions are neutral 50 while the score is driven by Target data.

**F2 — Route 1:** Route 1 is penalized heavily because arc melting and boride homogenization involve high temperature, controlled Ar, sealed annealing, multiple steps, and air/volatility risks (CS4–CS5, SS4, TS3–TS5). The route is still plausible because multiple rare-earth borides/borocarbides form by arc melting plus anneal (CS4–CS5, SS2, SS4). The main uncertainty is whether La₃B₂Sb survives the high-temperature melt without segregating to LaSb + La borides [Reasonable Inference] (Target, TS3, CS5).

**F2 — Route 2:** Route 2 avoids the >1400 °C arc-melt penalty but still requires sealed high-temperature treatment, controlled atmosphere, prereaction, grinding, and Sb containment (TS2–TS5, SS5). It is less severe than arc melting but more kinetically limited because B must diffuse through a solid-state La/LaSb matrix [Reasonable Inference] (CS5, SS4). CS gives useful boride-annealing evidence but insufficient evidence from CS for Sb volatility control, so CS is neutral 50 for that sub-factor.

**F2 — Route 3:** Route 3 has moderate condition severity because structurally similar flux analogues operate near 1050 °C in sealed Ta/silica systems, not at high pressure (SS1, SS3). It still receives deductions for controlled atmosphere, sealed Sb flux, multi-step cooling/decanting, and La/Sb handling risks [Reasonable Inference] (SS1, SS3, TS3, TS5). CS gives insufficient evidence from CS for flux-specific conditions, so CS is neutral 50 for Route 3 condition transferability.

**F3 — Route 1:** Arc melting improves precursor contact and may create liquid La–Sb regions [Reasonable Inference], but elemental B remains kinetically sluggish and analogues require repeated remelting or long annealing (CS5, SS4, TS4). Structural reconstruction is partial because SS La–B analogues share B-network motifs, but no exact La₃B₂Sb structure is reported in CS/SS/TS (SS1–SS4, Target). This supports a midrange kinetics score.

**F3 — Route 2:** Pre-reacted LaSb lowers Sb loss risk [Reasonable Inference] but reduces precursor activity because LaSb and B must interdiffuse in the solid state (TS3, CS5). Long annealing in boride/borocarbide analogues supports the expectation of slow ordering kinetics (CS5, SS4). This route has the weakest F3 score because it lacks a liquid/flux transport mechanism [Reasonable Inference].

**F3 — Route 3:** Flux growth receives the best kinetic score because SS analogues explicitly report best crystals from flux-like excess LaBr₃ conditions at 1050 °C for 5 days (SS1, SS3). Sb self-flux is an extrapolation, but TS confirms La–Sb/Pr–Sb chemistry is compatible with sealed high-temperature synthesis (TS3, TS5). The uncertainty is B solubility and whether Sb flux selects La₃B₂Sb rather than LaSb or La–B binaries [Reasonable Inference] (TS3, CS5).

**F4 — Amorphization Tendency:** The target has one cation type, an inferred unit cell below 40 atoms, and target-reported corner/edge but not face-sharing, so none of the three strict amorphization triggers applies (Target, [Reasonable Inference]). SS analogues do show complex edge/face-sharing La–B polyhedra, but those are analogue features rather than exact target triggers (SS1, SS3). Exact F4 trigger evidence is insufficient from CS, insufficient from SS, and insufficient from TS, so those source-specific contributions are neutral 50 and the F4 score is target-structure driven.

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

La₃B₂Sb is most likely a **metastable** rather than ground-state phase because its DFT E_hull is 0.172 eV/atom above the Materials Project hull (Target). Its reported no-imaginary-frequency screening improves confidence in dynamic stability but does not constitute experimental synthesis evidence (Target). CS/SS/TS analogues moderately support synthesis because rare-earth borides/borocarbides and La–B–halide frameworks are accessible by arc/anneal or flux methods, while La/Pr antimonides are accessible in sealed high-temperature reactions (CS4–CS5, SS1–SS4, TS3–TS5). The most critical enabling factor is a liquid/flux-assisted pathway that improves B transport and buffers Sb volatility [Reasonable Inference] (SS1, SS3, TS3, TS5). The most critical limiting factor is phase competition and stoichiometry control among LaSb, La–B binaries, and the metastable La₃B₂Sb target [Reasonable Inference] (Target, CS5, SS1, TS3).
