(Target) Thermodynamic analogues with E_hull values near this range have reported synthesis routes, which supports a score in the upper part of the 65–85 band. (TS-1, TS-3, TS-4, TS-5) Remaining uncertainty is that CS/SS/TS do not prove the exact B–Pd–Au phase, so exact-composition evidence is incomplete. (insufficient evidence from CS for exact target composition: neutral 50; insufficient evidence from SS for exact target composition: neutral 50; insufficient evidence from TS for exact target composition: neutral 50)

(TS-5, [Reasonable Inference]) The main kinetic uncertainty is crystallizing the exact chiral 1D BPd4Au2 ribbon rather than binary Pd–B or Au-rich impurities. (Target, SS-1, SS-2, TS-5, [Reasonable Inference])
