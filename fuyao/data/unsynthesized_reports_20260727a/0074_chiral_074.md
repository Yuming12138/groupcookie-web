### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

TiSBr₂ is most likely a **metastable but kinetically accessible** phase rather than a ground-state phase because its E_hull is 0.160 eV/atom while its phonon screen is dynamically stable. [Target] The CS set strongly supports chemical feasibility because Nb(SBr)₂ and related chalcogenide–halides are experimentally synthesized in sealed-tube or CVT conditions. [CS1, CS3, CS5] The SS set supports edge-sharing metal-bromide octahedral motifs, but it is less chemically direct because many SS examples are binary bromides or molecular/cluster bromides rather than Ti–S–Br sheets. [SS1, SS2, SS3] The most important enabling factor is volatile halide transport, which can overcome refractory-metal diffusion and promote low-dimensional crystal growth. [CS3, CS5] The most important limiting factor is Br chemical-potential control, because analogous systems show polymorphs, by-products, poor reproducibility, and mixed phases. [CS1, CS2, CS4, TS4]
