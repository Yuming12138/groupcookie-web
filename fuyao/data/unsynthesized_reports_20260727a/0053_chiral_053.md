**F2 — Route 1**

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

GaS2Cl is best interpreted as a **metastable but plausible** phase because its E_hull is 0.110 eV/atom rather than 0, while the target phonon screening passed dynamic stability (Target data). The CS/TS analogues support Ga–S framework synthesis through solid-state, flux, and CVT routes, and SS5 supports low-temperature sealed-tube chalcohalide chemistry (CS1, CS2, CS5, TS3, SS5). The strongest enabling factor is the combination of low-temperature sealed halide chemistry and flux/CVT mobility, which can help retain volatile Cl/S while allowing layered ordering [Reasonable Inference from SS5, CS1, CS2]. The strongest limiting factor is stoichiometry control of volatile GaCl3/S and avoidance of competing Ga2S3 or halide-rich phases [Reasonable Inference from SS5, CS1, TS3]. High-pressure evidence supports the S–S motif but is less transferable to the 2D Ga–S–Cl topology, so it is a backup rather than preferred route (SS1, SS2, TS1).
