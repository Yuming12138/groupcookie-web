### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability**  
The target’s 0.170 eV/atom hull energy places it in the metastable but potentially accessible 65–85 scoring band, and TS analogues show synthesized materials near this energy range, especially ZnP₂ at 0.1898 eV/atom and Fe₂GeSe₄ at 0.1731 eV/atom (**TS-1**, **TS-5**). Structural analogues include stable or near-stable phosphides/halophosphides, including Hg₂P₃Cl and Ag₃P₁₁, supporting that related P-network frameworks can be isolable (**SS-4**, **SS-5**). CS is contradictory for F1 because some chemically similar entries have very high listed hull energies despite reported synthesis, so CS is treated as mixed rather than decisive (**CS-1**, **CS-3**, **CS-5**).

**F2/F3 — Route 1**  
CVT has moderate condition severity because it avoids >1000 °C and high pressure but requires vacuum sealing, volatile P/Cl control, and hazardous chloride chemistry (**CS-5**, **SS-3 / TS-1**). Kinetics are favorable because halide/P vapor transport reduces diffusion limitations, and Cd₂P₃Cl is the closest direct precedent for metal–P–Cl growth (**CS-5**). The main uncertainty is whether the In analogue selects the target chiral P4₁ polymorph rather than another In–P–Cl phase [Reasonable Inference from polymorph/phase-control issues in **CS-4**, **CS-5**, **SS-5**].

**F2/F3 — Route 2**  
Direct sealed-tube annealing is chemically plausible because Hg₂P₃Cl forms from metal chloride, red P, and metal in sealed silica tubes (**SS-5**). Its severity is higher than CVT because repeated annealing/regrinding may be needed, and analogues show long annealing or impurity issues (**CS-2**, **SS-4**, **TS-5**). Kinetics are weaker than CVT because the target framework must form by solid-state reconstruction rather than vapor transport [Reasonable Inference from **CS-2**, **SS-4**, **SS-5**].

**F2/F3 — Route 3**  
High pressure is supported by structurally similar P-rich phosphides InP₃ and ZnP₄, which require 3–4 GPa and 1000–1200 °C (**SS-1**, **SS-2**). This route may help stabilize a metastable P-network phase, but CS provides insufficient high-pressure signal and is scored neutral for this route-specific evidence. The limiting uncertainty is chloride compatibility under pressure, because the high-pressure analogues do not contain Cl (**SS-1**, **SS-2**; insufficient direct chloride evidence from **CS/TS**).

**F2/F3 — Route 4**  
Flux growth could lower kinetic barriers because TS-4 demonstrates molten flux growth at 250–350 °C with slow cooling (**TS-4**). However, no CS/SS/TS source directly shows chloride-flux growth of In–P–Cl, so structural reconstruction and composition control are penalized and CS/SS are neutral for direct flux transfer. This route is best treated as a secondary crystal-growth strategy after CVT screening [Reasonable Inference from **TS-4**, **CS-5**, **SS-5**].

**F4 — Amorphization Tendency**  
The target has only one cation, so the cation-radius mismatch trigger is absent; CS/SS/TS do not add a mixed-cation warning and are neutral for that check. The target appears to have a modest P4₁ cell and no reported face-sharing coexistence, while close halophosphide analogues crystallize as ordered phases (**CS-5**, **SS-5**). Remaining uncertainty is the exact unit-cell atom count because CS/SS/TS do not provide that target-specific value, so C2 is treated using the target structure description plus [Reasonable Inference].
