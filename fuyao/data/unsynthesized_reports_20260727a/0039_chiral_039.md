The passed no-imaginary-frequency screening improves confidence that the calculated structure is dynamically viable, although it does not prove that the phase can be isolated experimentally [Source-paper].

### F2 — Route 1

Route 1 requires air-free phosphide handling, iodine containment, and a sealed controlled-atmosphere reaction, as in the Ba₅P₅I₃ synthesis [CS-2; SS-1]. There is insufficient evidence from TS for route-specific condition severity, so TS is treated as neutral, 50, for this factor [TS].

## SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

SrPI is most appropriately classified as a potentially isolable metastable phase rather than a confirmed ground-state phase because its calculated \(E_\mathrm{hull}\) is 0.0800 eV atom⁻¹, although its reported phonon screening is dynamically stable [Source-paper; Target]. The strongest support comes from Ba₅P₅I₃, which combines an alkaline-earth cation, P–P bonding, iodide coordination, and sealed-tube phosphide/I₂ synthesis [CS-2; SS-1]. The most critical enabling factor is the use of a preformed Sr–P precursor, potentially combined with an iodide-rich liquid or flux to accelerate diffusion [CS-2; SS-1; TS-4; Reasonable Inference]. The main limiting factor is precise control of iodine activity and Sr:P:I stoichiometry, because iodine loss or incomplete P–P framework formation could produce competing binary or iodine-deficient phases [CS-2; Target; Reasonable Inference]. The candidate’s predicted chirality and two inequivalent Sr sites also make slow cooling and phase identification important [Target; Reasonable Inference].
