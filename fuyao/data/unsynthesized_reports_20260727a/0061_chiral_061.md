The synthesis evidence is reasonably supportive because SS and TS include Se-rich halide phases made by hydrothermal, solvothermal, and CVT routes. [SS1][SS2][TS2][TS4]  
The strongest enabling factor is low-temperature Se–halide chemistry, which can form Se–Se bonded halide structures without high-temperature bulk diffusion. [SS2][TS2][TS4]  
The strongest limiting factor is phase control, because several close analogues report contamination, multiphase products, or difficulty obtaining larger/pure crystals. [SS2][TS2][TS4]  
Overall, the target is plausible but likely requires careful chloride/selenium chemical-potential control rather than a simple one-shot solid-state reaction. [Target][TS2][SS2][Reasonable Inference]
