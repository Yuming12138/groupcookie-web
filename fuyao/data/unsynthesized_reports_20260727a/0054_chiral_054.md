### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability.** The target’s 0.110 eV/atom hull places it in the metastable-but-plausible 65–85 range, and TS analogues include experimentally reported materials in the ~0.10–0.15 eV/atom range (TS1–TS5). CS is mixed because CS1/CS4/CS5 are low-hull sulfide analogues while CS2/CS3 are high-hull molecular chloride salts, so CS is useful but not decisive. SS analogues are mostly hull-stable and structurally similar, but SS does not prove the In–S–Cl composition.

**Route 1 F2/F3.** Severity is mainly from vacuum sealing, volatile sulfur/chloride chemistry, and likely regrind/reanneal cycles, all consistent with sealed sulfide syntheses in SS1 and TS3. Kinetics are only moderate because CS4 and TS3 show solid-state sulfides can need long anneals or repeated homogenization. The main uncertainty is whether InCl3 reacts cleanly before volatilizing or forming competing indium chlorides [Reasonable Inference].

**Route 2 F2/F3.** CVT severity is moderate because CS1/TS5 show no high pressure is needed, but evacuated ampoules and volatile iodine/halide transport are required. Kinetics are favorable because vapor transport bypasses bulk diffusion, but structure transfer is uncertain because neither CS1 nor TS5 contains the exact InS3Cl stoichiometry. SS provides insufficient direct CVT evidence and is treated neutral 50 for this route.

**Route 3 F2/F3.** The chalcogen-halide melt route has moderate severity because TS2 works at only 180 °C, but sealed ampoules, vacuum, and volatile/corrosive chalcogen halides are required. Kinetics score highest because TS2 demonstrates low-temperature formation of a metal chalcogen-halide with Ch–Ch bonding, directly relevant to the target’s S–S and In–Cl chemistry [TS2; Reasonable Inference]. The main uncertainty is replacing Pd/Se/SeCl4 chemistry with In/S/Cl chemistry without forming InCl3-rich or sulfur-rich side phases.

**Route 4 F2/F3.** Flux growth has moderate severity because SS4 uses 250–350 °C rather than extreme temperature, but it requires vacuum, glovebox/N2 handling, slow cooling, and flux removal. Kinetics are strong because molten polysulfide flux should enhance transport and crystallization, as in SS4. The limiting uncertainty is alkali incorporation or formation of alkali indium sulfides/chlorides instead of neutral InS3Cl [Reasonable Inference].

**Route 5 F2/F3.** Low-temperature CS2 crystallization is mild thermally because SS2/SS5 operate near 4–5 °C, but CS2 volatility/toxicity and dry handling impose practical deductions.
