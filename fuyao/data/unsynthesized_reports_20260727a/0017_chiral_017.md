### F2 — Route 1

Route 1 uses temperatures comparable to the 400–600 °C range established for related chalcogenide and chalcohalide solid-state reactions. **[CS-1] [TS-5]** Its main penalties arise from vacuum sealing, volatile sulfur/bromine species, toxic bromine handling, and likely regrinding or extended annealing. **[CS-1] [TS-5] [Reasonable Inference]** No supplied analogue uses high pressure or temperatures above 1000 °C, so those deductions are not applied. **[CS-1] [TS-5]**

### F3 — Route 1

The Ge–S analogue is made from simple elemental or binary-sulfide precursors, supporting moderate precursor accessibility. **[CS-1]** Diffusion is not expected to be prohibitive because the relevant reaction temperatures are below the melting point of Ge and related reactions can involve sulfur-rich liquid or vapor transport. **[CS-1] [Reasonable Inference]** The lower structural-reconstruction score reflects that the target is a one-dimensional chiral Ge–S–Br ribbon, while the closest Ge–S analogue is two-dimensional and the closest brominated analogue is Bi–Se based. **[CS-1] [TS-3]**

### F2 — Route 2

CVT requires the same sealed-vacuum environment as the direct route, but adds a thermal gradient and transport-agent handling. **[CS-3] [TS-3]** The route is not penalized for high pressure or extreme temperature because the precedents operate at approximately 623–823 K or 650–700 °C. **[CS-3] [TS-3]** Volatile sulfur, bromine, and iodine species justify deductions for sealed operation and precursor hazards. **[CS-3] [TS-3] [Reasonable Inference]**

### F3 — Route 2

Iodine-assisted CVT has produced crystalline Sn–P–Se materials, and a Bi–Se–Br analogue has been grown using a sealed ampoule and temperature gradient. **[CS-3] [TS-3]** These precedents support high mass-transfer kinetics and make Route 2 preferable for obtaining ordered crystals. **[CS-3] [TS-3]** The score is reduced because neither precedent establishes formation of the exact GeS₃Br₂ composition or the \(P4_3\) chiral space group. **[SS-1] [TS-3]**
