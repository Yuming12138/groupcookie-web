### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

#### F1 — Thermodynamic Stability

The target’s E_hull of 0.198 eV/atom places it in the 65–85 score bin but at the least favorable edge of that bin. (Target record) TS contains close thermodynamic analogues near 0.186 and 0.214 eV/atom, showing that phases in this energetic range can be structurally meaningful but are likely metastable. (TS-1/3) CS and SS include several 0 eV/atom Rh/Ir borides, so the chemical/structural family itself is stable even if this exact Tl compound is not. (CS-2/3/4/5; SS-2/3/4/5)

#### F2 — Route 1

Route 1 is penalized for >1000 °C conditions, sealed Ar/vacuum handling, volatility control, multiple operations, and Tl toxicity. (CS-1/2/5; SS-1/5; [Reasonable Inference]) The route is strongly precedent-backed by Zn–Rh/Ir–B and Rh–B direct reactions, but the Tl substitution creates a more severe containment problem than Zn-based analogues. (CS-1/2/5; SS-1; [Reasonable Inference])

#### F3 — Route 1

Kinetics are limited by refractory Rh and B, consistent with long high-temperature holds in related borides. (CS-1/2/5; SS-1/5) Tl may provide some liquid-phase assistance, but no CS/SS/TS source directly demonstrates Tl-assisted Rh boride formation, so that benefit is extrapolated. ([Reasonable Inference]) Structural reconstruction is moderate-to-large because the target is a novel Pmc2₁ ternary phase, not a directly reported analogue. (Target record; CS; SS; TS)

#### F2 — Route 2

Route 2 receives the harshest F2 score because it combines a high-temperature melt or precursor step with sealed Tl annealing. (CS-3; TS-3; [Reasonable Inference]) CS and TS support melt/anneal processing, while insufficient evidence from SS exists for the melt component specifically, so SS is neutral at 50 for that route evidence. (CS-3; TS-3; SS)

#### F3 — Route 2

Preforming Rh–B bonds can help overcome Rh/B reaction barriers, as RhB/Rh₂B binaries are known from SS/TS analogues. (SS-1; TS-3) The main kinetic uncertainty is whether Tl can diffuse into or reconstruct the binary network without forming competing Tl–Rh/Tl–B phases, which is not directly answered by CS/SS/TS. (CS; SS; TS; [Reasonable Inference])

#### F2 — Route 3

Route 3 still requires controlled sealed-ampoule work, volatile Tl excess, and toxic flux handling. (SS-2/3; [Reasonable Inference]) Its severity is lower than Route 2 because it avoids arc/local melting above 1400 °C. (CS-3; TS-3; SS-2/3)

#### F3 — Route 3

Flux growth receives the best kinetic score because SS analogues show Rh borides can be grown with excess liquid-metal flux at 1000–1100 °C. (SS-2/3) The uncertainty is that Tl self-flux is not directly demonstrated in CS or TS, so insufficient evidence from CS and TS for this route is scored neutral at 50. (CS; TS; [Reasonable Inference])

#### F4 — Amorphization Tendency

Only one clear amorphization trigger is assigned because Tl/Rh size and chemistry are disparate. ([Reasonable Inference]) The target has multiple inequivalent sites, but CS/SS/TS do not establish a >40-atom unit cell for the target, and the target description emphasizes corner-sharing rather than explicit mixed vertex/edge/face connectivity. (Target record; CS; SS; TS) Therefore, amorphization risk is low–medium rather than high. (CS-3/4; TS-3; [Reasonable Inference])

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

The target is more likely a metastable phase than a ground-state phase because its E_hull is 0.198 eV/atom, close to the 0.20 eV/atom cutoff. (Target record) The CS/SS/TS analogues support feasibility of Rh/Ir transition-metal boride synthesis by sealed high-temperature methods, but they do not directly validate Tl-containing Rh borides. (CS-1/2/5; SS-1/2/3/5; TS-1/3) The most critical enabling factor is liquid Tl or Tl-rich flux potentially improving transport and crystallization of a refractory Rh–B framework. (SS-2/3; [Reasonable Inference]) The most critical limiting factor is simultaneous control of Tl volatility/toxicity and narrow ternary phase stability. (CS-1/2/5; TS-1; [Reasonable Inference]) Overall, synthesis is plausible but would require careful sealed-ampoule screening rather than routine solid-state reaction. (CS; SS; TS; [Reasonable Inference])
