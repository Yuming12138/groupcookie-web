# SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

The target is unlikely to be a conventional ground-state phase because its reported \(E_\mathrm{hull}=0.0700\) eV atom⁻¹ places it above the convex hull, although this value is within the range of experimentally accessible metastable analogues [target record][SS][TS]. Structural support is strongest from ZnP₄, which reproduces the metal–P octahedral/P–P/tetrahedral motif, and from Bi₃Se₄Br, which supports Bi–halide framework chemistry [SS]. The most important enabling factor is the ability to use a sealed ampoule to maintain bromine chemical potential and suppress volatile-component loss [CS][SS][TS]. The main limiting factor is phase selectivity: the Bi–P–Br system may form BiP, BiBr₃-derived products, phosphorus-rich phases, or disordered material before the chiral P2₁ network develops [SS][TS][Reasonable Inference]. Overall, direct sealed-ampoule synthesis is plausible but requires careful phase screening and slow cooling.
