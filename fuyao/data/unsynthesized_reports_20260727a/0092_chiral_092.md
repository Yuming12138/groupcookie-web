| # | Route | Challenge | Severity | Mitigation Strategy (from CS/SS/TS) |
|:--:|:--:|---|:--:|---|
| 1 | All | Be toxicity from dust/fume exposure. [Target] | High | Use coarse Be, sealed systems, inert handling, and avoid open powder manipulation; Be–B analogues already rely on protective reaction environments. [SS1, TS1, Target, Reasonable Inference] |
| 2 | All | Be loss, Be sublimation, and BeO/impurity formation. [SS1, TS1] | High | Use sealed Ta/quartz containment, minimize peak temperature, check mass balance, and consider slight Be excess by **[Reasonable Inference]**. [CS5, SS1, TS1] |
| 3 | All | Competing Be–B, Al–B, or Al-rich phases. [SS1, SS2, SS3, TS1, TS2, TS3] | High | Use slow cooling for flux trials, long annealing/regrinding for solid-state trials, and quench/anneal screens to map phase stability. [SS2, SS3, TS2, TS3, CS1, CS4] |
| 4 | Route 1 | Slow solid-state ordering and incomplete reaction. [SS1, TS1] | Medium–High | Press pellets, use fine B, hold 48–96 h, and repeat grind/anneal if needed. [SS1, TS1, CS5] |
| 5 | Route 2 | Melt inhomogeneity, B dissolution difficulty, and volatile mass loss. [CS1, CS4, SS4, SS5] | High | Remelt several times, use getter/inert gas, measure mass loss, then homogenization anneal. [CS1, CS4, SS4, SS5] |
| 6 | Route 3 | Residual Al flux or Al-rich side products. [SS2, SS3, TS2, TS3] | Medium | Control Al excess, slow cool, decant/etch excess Al, and verify product by XRD/EDS before assuming phase purity. [SS2, SS3, TS2, TS3] |
| 7 | Route 3 | Phase/polymorph selection during slow cooling. [SS2, SS3, TS2, TS3] | Medium | Screen cooling rates around 1–5 K/h and compare quenched versus slow-cooled products. [SS2, SS3, TS2, TS3, Reasonable Inference] |
| 8 | Route 4 | Stoichiometry control in iodine transport. [TS4, Reasonable Inference] | High | Use CVT only as exploratory growth after bulk phase validation, with low iodine loading and post-growth composition checks. [TS4, Reasonable Inference] |
