### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability**  
The target is not predicted as a ground-state phase because E_hull = 0.180 eV/atom, but it remains in the rubric’s 65–85 metastability band. [Target] Dynamic stability was reported as passing the no-imaginary-frequency screen, which improves confidence that the relaxed structure is not an immediate vibrational artifact. [Target] CS and SS contain several E_hull = 0 analogues, while TS shows that thermodynamically similar metastable compounds can still be experimentally accessed. [CS#1–CS#5; SS#3–SS#4; TS#1–TS#5]

**F2 — Route 1**  
Route 1 is conditionally moderate because sealed ampoule equipment and 500–550 °C temperatures are standard for the Re–Te–Br analogue. [CS#4] The main severity deductions are vacuum sealing, volatile Se/I₂, and selenium/iodine toxicity. [CS#2; SS#1; Target] Insufficient evidence from TS for an elemental Re–Se–I ampoule route, so TS is neutral 50 for this route’s F2. [TS]

**F3 — Route 1**  
Elemental Re lowers the kinetic score because refractory metal incorporation is expected to be slow. [CS#4; Reasonable Inference] The score is partially rescued by evidence that sealed halogen/chalcogen chemistry can form related clusters at moderate temperatures. [CS#4; CS#2] The route still requires substantial reconstruction into a new tetrahedral ReSe₃I cluster, which is not directly demonstrated. [Target; CS#4; SS#3]

**F2 — Route 2**  
Route 2 has moderate severity because CVT uses sealed ampoules, controlled gradients, volatile iodine/halogen transport, and longer multistep growth periods. [CS#1; CS#3; SS#1] It avoids high pressure and >1000 °C operation in the proposed screen. [CS#1; SS#1; Reasonable Inference] Insufficient evidence from TS for CVT transferability to ReSe₃I, so TS is neutral 50 for Route 2 F2. [TS]

**F3 — Route 2**  
CVT improves kinetic feasibility by allowing gas-phase transport instead of relying only on solid-state diffusion. [CS#1; SS#1; Reasonable Inference] The presence of iodine in the target makes self-transport chemically plausible. [CS#2; SS#1; Target] The uncertainty is that the strongest CVT examples are not exact Re–Se–I cluster analogues. [CS#1; CS#3; SS#1]

**F2 — Route 3**  
Route 3 is thermally mild because ReCl₅-derived cluster analogues form at 350–400 °C. [SS#4] It is still condition-severe due to evacuated tube handling, volatile iodine chemistry, air-sensitive halide precursors, and possible purification. [SS#4; SS#1; Reasonable Inference] CS gives partial support through Re–Te–Br ampoule chemistry, while TS mainly signals air-sensitive halide-route complexity rather than direct target synthesis. [CS#4; TS#4]

**F3 — Route 3**  
Reactive rhenium halides provide the best kinetic path because they avoid starting solely from dense Re metal. [SS#4; Reasonable Inference] Structurally similar Re–chalcogen–halide clusters support partial transfer to ReSe₃I, although the target’s tetrahedral ReSe₃I cluster is not directly reported. [SS#4; CS#4; Target] The main uncertainty is whether iodine substitution stabilizes the desired ReSe₃I stoichiometry instead of iodine-rich or mixed-halide phases. [CS#2; SS#1; Reasonable Inference]

**F2 — Route 4**  
Route 4 uses low temperatures but involves sealed reactive melts/fluxes, volatile selenium halides/iodine, and post-synthesis flux removal. [SS#2; SS#5; TS#3] It avoids high pressure in the >5 GPa sense. [SS#5; Reasonable Inference] Insufficient evidence from CS for selenium-iodide melt/flux chemistry in Re systems, so CS is neutral 50 for Route 4 F2. [CS]

**F3 — Route 4**  
Flux or selenium-halide melt media should improve mass transport relative to dry powders. [SS#2; TS#3] However, the closest examples are Pd selenium-halide ribbons or alkali polychalcogenide phases rather than ReSe₃I molecular clusters. [SS#2; SS#5; TS#3] Re dissolution and alkali/flux contamination remain major uncertainties. [TS#3; Reasonable Inference]

**F4 — Amorphization Tendency**  
The target has low amorphization risk because it is a small zero-dimensional cluster compound with only one cation and no extended mixed polyhedral network. [Target] Structurally similar Re chalcogen-halide clusters crystallize as zero-dimensional molecular solids, supporting the crystallinity of related motifs. [CS#4; SS#3; SS#4] CS/SS/TS do not independently determine the target’s exact unit-cell atom count, so that sub-check relies on Target data and CS/SS/TS are neutral 50 for the exact C2 trigger. ### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

ReSe₃I is more likely a metastable phase than a ground-state phase because its E_hull is 0.180 eV/atom, despite passing dynamic-stability screening. [Target] The synthesis evidence is moderately supportive because Re–chalcogen–halide zero-dimensional clusters and Nb–Se–I sealed-tube chemistry are experimentally known. [CS#4; SS#3; SS#4; CS#2] The most critical enabling factor is the use of reactive halide or iodine-mediated chemistry to bypass slow refractory Re diffusion. [SS#4; CS#1; Reasonable Inference] The most critical limiting factor is controlling volatile Se/I chemical potentials while avoiding competing Re–Se, Re–I, or mixed-halide phases. [CS#2; SS#1; Target; Reasonable Inference] Overall, the target is feasible but not routine, with the best estimate around **70/100** synthesizability. [Target; CS; SS; TS]
