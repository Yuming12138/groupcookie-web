# SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

## F1 — Thermodynamic Stability

The target \(E_\mathrm{hull}=0.090\) eV/atom lies in the requested 0.05–0.20 eV/atom interval, corresponding to a moderately favorable thermodynamic score. **Target validation data.** This is less favorable than the close YSF analogues at 0 and 0.0265 eV/atom but more favorable than the TS YF\(_3\) reference at approximately 0.2005 eV/atom. **CS-3–4; SS-1–2; TS-1.** The main uncertainty is that the hull value is computed against reference phases and does not prove that the phase can be kinetically accessed experimentally. **Target validation data.**

## F2 — Route 1

Route 1 receives a high F2 score because its temperature is below 1000 °C and it does not require external high pressure. **CS-4; SS-1–2.** A 10-point deduction is applied for sulfur-containing chemistry requiring a sealed system at high temperature. **CS-4; SS-1–2; [Reasonable Inference].** No additional deductions are assigned for reactive atmosphere or multistep processing because the supplied route uses inert sealed ampoules and is described as a direct flux-growth reaction. **CS-4; SS-1–2.**

## F3 — Route 1

The flux provides a liquid-assisted pathway that can reduce diffusion limitations relative to an unfluxed reaction, as demonstrated by YSF and LuSF growth at 850 °C. **CS-4; SS-1–2.** Precursor activity remains only moderate because elemental Y and crystalline YF\(_3\) are not highly reactive solution-derived precursors. **[Reasonable Inference]** Structural reconstruction is scored as partial because the YSF family is chemically and topologically related, but the target’s monoclinic \(P2_1\) symmetry differs from the reported hexagonal/trigonal structures. **CS-4; SS-1–2; target structure.**

## F2 — Route 2

Route 2 uses the demonstrated 900 °C, 10-day, Ar/He sealed-Ta conditions for a related Y–S–F solid-state reaction. **CS-3.** The route therefore avoids the penalties for temperatures above 1000 °C and external high pressure. **CS-3.** Sulfur volatility and the need for sealed containment justify a 10-point deduction. **CS-3; [Reasonable Inference].**

## F3 — Route 2

The direct reaction is kinetically less favorable than the flux route because it depends more heavily on solid-state diffusion between elemental Y, YF\(_3\), and sulfur. **[Reasonable Inference]** The 10-day duration of the related Y–S–F reaction supports a moderate rather than high kinetics score. **CS-3.** The target structure is not the same as the reported direct-solid-state analogue, so substantial structural ordering or polymorph selection may be required. **CS-3; target structure.**

## F4 — Amorphization Tendency

The target has only one cation species, so the cation-radius mismatch criterion is not triggered. **Target structure.** The supplied data do not provide enough information to verify the full unit-cell atom count, and they do not establish face-sharing polyhedral connectivity for the target; therefore these checks are treated as unconfirmed rather than positive triggers. **Target structure; CS-4; SS-1–2; insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS.** The nominal F4 score is consequently high, although the uncertainty means that crystallization and polymorph selection should still be monitored experimentally. **CS-4; SS-1–2; [Reasonable Inference].**

# SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

The target is best regarded as a **metastable or weakly metastable phase**, rather than a ground-state phase, because its \(E_\mathrm{hull}\) is 0.090 eV/atom rather than zero, although its reported dynamic-stability screening was passed. **Target validation data.** Chemical and structural analogues provide strong support for formation of Y–S–F materials under sealed Ta flux or solid-state conditions, especially the YSF and LuSF examples. **CS-3–4; SS-1–2.** The most important enabling factor is the established rare-earth sulfur-fluoride flux chemistry with NaCl-assisted mass transport and crystal growth. **CS-4; SS-1–2.** The main limiting factor is competition from lower-energy Y–S–F polymorphs or compositions, combined with sulfur/fluorine stoichiometry control and the need to select the low-symmetry \(P2_1\) structure. **CS-3–4; SS-1–2; TS-1; [Reasonable Inference].**
