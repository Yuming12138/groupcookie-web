## SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

### F1 — Thermodynamic Stability

The target’s calculated hull distance is 0.0300 eV/atom, which falls in the high-stability ≤0.05 eV/atom range specified by the scoring rubric [Target validation]. Its reported dynamic stability and low hull distance support the possibility of isolating the phase, but the absence of experimental confirmation leaves uncertainty regarding competing bromide, sulfide, or elemental phases [Target validation][Reasonable Inference]. The low hull energies of Rh₂S₃, CuRh₂S₄, and several Rh-containing selenides provide additional support for Rh–chalcogen framework stability [SS][TS].

### F2 — Route 1

The direct solid-state route requires a sealed evacuated ampoule, which incurs the controlled-atmosphere deduction, and sulfur/bromine chemistry requires containment because of volatile components [SS][TS][Reasonable Inference]. Multiple firings or intermediate grinding are commonly used in related Rh-containing solid-state reactions, supporting the multi-step deduction [SS][TS]. The route avoids extreme pressure and temperatures above 1000 °C, so it is demanding but not as severe as high-pressure synthesis [SS][TS].

### F3 — Route 1

The main kinetic limitation is diffusion through refractory Rh-containing solids; Rh₂S₃ and CuRh₂S₄ require 800–900 °C, and related Rh selenides require up to 1000–1080 °C with grinding or repeated firing [SS][TS]. The target’s Rh octahedral environment is partially supported by Rh₂S₃ and CuRh₂S₄, but the mixed S/Br coordination and chiral connectivity require structural reconstruction [SS][Reasonable Inference]. Therefore, the route receives a moderate rather than high F3 score.

### F2 — Route 2

The hydrothermal route operates at much lower temperature than the dry solid-state analogues, and no gigapascal pressure is required [CS][SS]. Nevertheless, concentrated HBr, sealed autoclave operation, volatile sulfur/halogen chemistry, and multi-step loading and recovery justify deductions [SS][Reasonable Inference]. There is insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS for a need for a reactive gas atmosphere, so that deduction is not applied [CS][SS][TS].

### F3 — Route 2

The hydrothermal medium should improve precursor activity and ion transport relative to dense Rh metal powder [Reasonable Inference]. The Pd–Se–Br analogue demonstrates that a chalcogen–halide framework can crystallize from an acidic sealed ampoule at 453 K over 5 days [SS], and CS demonstrates related solvothermal chalcogenide crystallization in the 185–200 °C range [CS]. The score is limited because neither source demonstrates formation of RhS₂Br or the P6₅22 chiral topology directly [CS][SS][Reasonable Inference].

### F2 — Route 3

CVT requires an evacuated sealed ampoule and volatile halogen transport chemistry, so controlled-atmosphere, volatile-component, multi-step, and hazard deductions apply [Reasonable Inference]. Insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS supports a direct CVT precedent for this target [CS][SS][TS]. The route therefore receives a condition score similar to direct solid-state synthesis despite its potential crystal-growth advantage.

### F3 — Route 3

A transport agent could increase precursor mobility and facilitate crystal growth [Reasonable Inference]. However, the absence of a matching CVT example means vapor-phase precursor availability, crystallization pathway, and composition tolerance are all uncertain [CS][SS][TS]. The likely sensitivity of bromine incorporation to the temperature gradient and halogen chemical potential lowers the composition-control score [Reasonable Inference].

### F4 — Amorphization Tendency

The target has a single cation species and the supplied description reports corner- and edge-sharing octahedra but no face-sharing octahedra, so two of the three specified amorphization checks are not triggered [Target structure]. Insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS is available to determine whether the unit cell contains more than 40 atoms because Z is not supplied [CS][SS][TS]. The resulting F4 score is therefore favorable but should be regarded as somewhat uncertain.

## SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

The target is best classified as a **low-hull, dynamically stable predicted phase**, rather than a confirmed ground-state experimental material, because its calculated hull distance is 0.0300 eV/atom but its experimental status is theory-only [Target validation]. SS and TS provide meaningful support for Rh–chalcogen octahedral frameworks and for Rh-containing phases prepared by sealed high-temperature reactions [SS][TS]. The most enabling factor is the low calculated energy above hull combined with the availability of chemically related Rh–S and Rh–Se frameworks [Target validation][SS][TS]. The most important limitation is simultaneous control of bromine retention, S–S bonding, and chiral P6₅22 ordering, for which direct precedent is absent from CS/SS/TS [Reasonable Inference]. An acidic hydrothermal route is therefore the preferred first experiment, with sealed solid-state synthesis as an important backup route [CS][SS][Reasonable Inference].
