### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

PtN₃F is best interpreted as a **metastable but plausible** phase because its E_hull is 0.100 eV/atom rather than zero, while its dynamic-stability screen passed [Target]. The strongest synthesis support comes from SS/TS solution azide analogues and TS azide-halide chemistry, which show that low-dimensional metal–azide or azide-halide phases can crystallize under mild solution conditions [SS1, SS2, SS4, TS2, TS4].
