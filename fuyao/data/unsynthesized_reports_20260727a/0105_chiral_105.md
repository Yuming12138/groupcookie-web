(Target) TS analogues show that experimentally handled intermetallics/borides can appear with comparable or higher calculated hull energies, especially TS-1 at 0.136 eV/atom and TS-3 at 0.188 eV/atom. (TS-1; TS-3) Insufficient direct target thermodynamic evidence is available from CS and SS, so CS/SS are neutral 50 for source-specific F1 signal, while TS carries the useful analogue signal. **F2 — Route 1.** The direct sealed-ampoule route avoids >1000°C and high pressure by following the 900°C Li₂Pd₃B precedent. (SS-2) It still requires controlled Ar/vacuum sealing and a volatile/toxic Cd-containing charge, so the deductions are atmosphere, volatile sealed precursor, and toxicity. (SS-2; CS-3; Target)

**F3 — Route 1.** Kinetics are limited by elemental Pd/B solid-state reaction, but the structural reconstruction burden is low because Li₂Pd₃B already contains BPd₆ octahedra and similar cuboctahedral connectivity. (SS-2; Target) Long reaction times and occasional regrinding in structural analogues justify keeping the kinetics score moderate rather than high. (SS-1; SS-2)

**F2 — Route 2.** The flux route benefits from lower proposed temperature and no high pressure, but it is penalized for controlled sealed atmosphere, Cd volatility, Cd toxicity, and multi-step flux separation. (CS-2; CS-1; Target) Insufficient direct evidence from SS for Cd-flux growth of this target is treated as neutral 50 for that route-specific method signal. (SS)

**F3 — Route 2.** Flux chemistry improves precursor activity and diffusion compared with dry powders, as shown by the Zn/Rh/B flux precedent. (CS-2) The score is capped because CS analogues report side phases/narrow composition windows and because Cd–Pd–B flux solubility is an extrapolation. (CS-1; CS-2; [Reasonable Inference])

**F2 — Route 3.** The arc-melt/master-alloy route receives high severity deductions because arc melting exceeds both >1000°C and >1400°C thresholds, and the process is multi-step. **F3 — Route 3.** Arc melting improves Pd–B mixing, but Cd incorporation after master-alloy formation requires a separate diffusion/ordering step. (TS-1; TS-3; CS-3) The long annealing times in TS analogues and phase complexity in TS-1 keep the kinetics score below the flux route. (TS-1; TS-3)
