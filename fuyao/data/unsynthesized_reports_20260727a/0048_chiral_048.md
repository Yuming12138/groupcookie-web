### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability**  
The target E_hull is 0.100 eV/atom, placing it in the moderate metastability window rather than a clear ground-state window. [Target] CS1 and TS1 have similar E_hull values near 0.096–0.098 eV/atom, while SS1 is a stable Ba iodate analogue at E_hull = 0, supporting possible accessibility of nearby Ba/I/O chemistry. [CS1; SS1; TS1] Remaining uncertainty is phase competition because the target is reported as theory-only and absent from OQMD in the supplied validation data. [Target]

**F2/F3 — Route 1**  
Hydrothermal severity is moderate because the strongest analogues use ~180–200 °C, autogenous pressure, and standard Teflon-lined autoclaves rather than extreme pressure or temperature. [CS1; SS2; TS1] Kinetics are favorable because water-mediated transport and reactive iodate/iodide chemistry avoid refractory solid diffusion, and several iodate analogues crystallize in days. [SS1; SS4; TS1] The main uncertainty is whether the mixed iodate/iodide Ba(IO₃)I ordering can be selected instead of Ba(IO₃)₂ or BaI₂-rich phases. [Target; SS1; Reasonable Inference]

**F2/F3 — Route 2**  
Cooling crystallization has mild conditions and is supported only weakly by CS2, where a calcium iodide/oxyiodide phase crystallizes from saturated aqueous solution. [CS2] The kinetic barrier for ions in solution is low, but structural reconstruction is poorly supported because insufficient evidence from SS and TS exists for ambient-pressure crystallization of the Ba–iodate/iodide target, so those sources are neutral 50 for this pathway. [SS1; TS1] This route is experimentally attractive as a screen but less reliable than hydrothermal synthesis. [CS2; Reasonable Inference]

**F2/F3 — Route 3**  
Sealed-ampoule synthesis is more severe because it requires vacuum or inert sealing and careful iodine retention, as indicated by sealed oxyhalide/halide analogue syntheses. [TS2; TS3] Kinetics are weaker than hydrothermal because diffusion in a low-temperature solid/salt mixture is slower and no direct CS or SS solid-state Ba–I–O analogue is supplied, so CS and SS are neutral 50 for this route. [CS1; SS1; TS3] It remains plausible as a fallback for powder synthesis but is less likely to yield clean single crystals. [TS3; Reasonable Inference]

**F4 — Amorphization Tendency**  
The target does not appear to have a very large unit cell or explicit mixed vertex/edge/face-sharing complexity from the supplied target structure, lowering amorphization risk. [Target] A conservative C1 trigger is assigned because Ba²⁺ and iodate-centered I(V) represent strongly mismatched cationic centers, but CS/SS/TS do not provide ionic radii, so the exact threshold is inferred rather than directly sourced. [SS1; Target; Reasonable Inference] Overall amorphization risk is low–medium rather than high because multiple iodate analogues crystallize successfully under hydrothermal conditions.
