## SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

### F1 — Thermodynamic Stability

The target has a reported DFT energy above hull of 0.0000 eV/atom, corresponding to the highest stability band in the supplied rubric [target-provided DFT validation]. This is reinforced by CuBrSe₃ and CuSe₂Br, both reported at 0.0 eV/atom, and by near-ground-state SS/TS analogues [CS-1; CS-2; SS-1; SS-4; TS-1; TS-2]. The main uncertainty is that the target remains theory-only and has no direct experimental phase-confirmation evidence [target-provided validation; insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS for exact-target experimental confirmation].

### F2 — Route 1: CVT

CVT receives a moderate F2 score because the closest CuBrSe₃ precedent uses vacuum, a temperature gradient, and a sealed Vycor tube at 290–340 °C [CS-1; SS-1; TS-1]. Cd, Br, and Se volatility and toxicity add practical penalties [target safety note; Reasonable Inference]. The route avoids high pressure and very high temperature, but phase-selective transport may be sensitive to vapor composition [Reasonable Inference].

### F2 — Route 2: Direct Solid-State Reaction

The CuSe₂Br synthesis demonstrates that a metal bromide plus Se can react in an evacuated quartz ampoule at 550 °C, but the cooling rate is critical because different products form under different cooling conditions [CS-2; SS-4; TS-2]. The target route therefore receives deductions for vacuum, sealed volatile components, toxicity, and the proposed multi-step preparation [Reasonable Inference]. Its principal advantage is simple equipment and a straightforward precursor set [CS-2; SS-4; TS-2].

### F2 — Route 3: Acidic Hydrothermal Synthesis

The hydrothermal route operates at substantially lower temperature than the solid-state alternatives, with direct precedent for Se–Br and Se–halide frameworks in acidic media [CS-4; SS-2; SS-3; TS-5]. It nevertheless requires sealed pressure equipment, several days of reaction, slow cooling, and concentrated hydrogen halide handling [CS-4; SS-2; SS-3; TS-5]. The route is therefore less thermally severe but more demanding in vessel compatibility and process control [Reasonable Inference].

### F3 — Route 1: CVT

CVT benefits from vapor-phase transport, which can overcome slow solid-state diffusion; CuBrSe₃ single crystals were obtained in a single transport operation at moderate temperature [CS-1; SS-1; TS-1]. The target’s two Cd environments and face-sharing CdBr₆ units require more structural organization than the CuBrSe₃ analogue [target structure; CS-1; SS-1; Reasonable Inference]. The resulting F3 score is favorable but not maximal because the exact transport chemistry of CdSe₃Br₂ is untested [insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS for exact-target transport].

### F3 — Route 2: Direct Solid-State Reaction

The direct route uses simple reactants and has strong chemical precedent from CuBrSe₃ and CuSe₂Br [CS-1; CS-2; SS-1; SS-4; TS-1; TS-2]. However, the target contains two Cd sites with distinct tetrahedral and octahedral environments, so substantial ordering and reconstruction may be required [target structure; Reasonable Inference]. The CuSe₂Br analogue’s cooling-rate sensitivity also indicates a risk of competing phases or metastable products in this chemical family [CS-2; SS-4; TS-2].

### F3 — Route 3: Acidic Hydrothermal Synthesis

Hydrothermal conditions provide mobile ionic or molecular species and have produced crystalline Pd–Se–Br and Ag–Se–halide phases [SS-2; SS-3; TS-5; CS-4]. This supports favorable precursor activity and transport kinetics [Reasonable Inference]. The uncertainty is phase selectivity: the Ag analogue was obtained as a major component of a multiphase product, so pure CdSe₃Br₂ formation is not assured [CS-4; SS-3].

### F4 — Amorphization Tendency

The target has only one cation species, so the cation-radius mismatch criterion is not triggered [target structure]. The supplied data do not establish the unit-cell atom count or fully resolve whether vertex-, edge-, and face-sharing polyhedra coexist, so there is insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS to trigger C2 or C3. Ordered Se–Se motifs and well-defined metal coordination in the analogues support a crystalline product, although the F4 estimate remains provisional [CS-1; CS-2; SS-1; SS-2; TS-1].
