### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability**  
The target E_hull is 0.184 eV/atom, placing it near the upper end of the 65–85 scoring band. (Target data) The no-imaginary-frequency screening supports dynamic stability, but the theory-only status and OQMD novelty mean the phase is more likely metastable than a confirmed ground state. (Target data) CS/SS analogues in the same Zn–Rh–B chemical space are on-hull or nearly on-hull, while TS analogues with similar E_hull values demonstrate possible metastable accessibility but do not prove this target. (CS1/SS1; CS2/SS2; TS1–TS5)

**F2 — Route 1**  
Route 1 is penalized for >1000 °C operation, Ar-sealed ampoules, Zn volatility, and multi-step encapsulated synthesis. (CS1/SS1; CS2/SS2; SS4) It is not penalized for high pressure or highly toxic/air-sensitive target precursors because no high-pressure or exceptional target hazard is reported. (Target data; CS1/SS1)

**F3 — Route 1**  
Solid-state kinetics are limited by refractory Rh and B, even though Zn becomes a liquid/vapor transport component at the synthesis temperature. (CS1/SS1; SS4; [Reasonable Inference]) Structural reconstruction is only moderate because the target is related to known Zn–Rh–B borides but has a distinct tetragonal chiral structure. (CS1/SS1; CS2/SS2; Target data)

**F2 — Route 2**  
Route 2 has the same sealed-system and Zn-volatility penalties as Route 1 but benefits from a lower 1100 °C analogue route. (CS2/SS2; SS4) Optional Li/Mg flux is not included in the base penalty because the recommended adaptation is Zn self-flux to avoid foreign-cation contamination. (CS2/SS2; [Reasonable Inference])

**F3 — Route 2**  
Flux growth improves precursor activity and diffusion by providing a liquid-mediated pathway, which is why Route 2 receives the best kinetics score. (CS2/SS2; [Reasonable Inference]) Remaining uncertainty is phase selection because Zn–Rh–B sources report side phases and narrow compositional windows. (CS1/SS1; CS2/SS2)

**F2 — Route 3**  
The isopiestic route avoids >1000 °C during Zn uptake but requires vacuum sealing, a temperature gradient, controlled Zn pressure, and multiple handling steps. (SS4) CS and TS give insufficient direct evidence for Zn-vapor formation of a Rh–Zn–B boride, so they are neutral for this route. (insufficient evidence from CS/TS = 50)

**F3 — Route 3**  
Zn vapor is chemically active, but conversion of a Rh/B compact is likely diffusion-limited and may remain surface-rich or nanocrystalline. (SS4; [Reasonable Inference]) The route is therefore useful for Zn chemical-potential screening but less likely to produce bulk single-phase material on the first attempt. (SS4; [Reasonable Inference])

**F2 — Route 4**

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

Rh2Zn3B is more likely a metastable, dynamically stable phase than a confirmed ground-state phase because its E_hull is 0.184 eV/atom and it is theory-only with no reported experimental procedure. (Target data) The synthesis case is still credible because the closest chemical and structural analogues are real Zn–Rh–B borides made from elemental precursors in sealed high-temperature ampoules. (CS1/SS1; CS2/SS2) The most important enabling factor is liquid/vapor Zn-assisted transport in sealed Ta/BN systems, which can help overcome Rh/B diffusion barriers. (CS2/SS2; SS4; [Reasonable Inference]) The most important limiting factor is phase competition in the Zn–Rh–B system, since analogues report phase-purity issues and narrow compositional windows. (CS1/SS1; CS2/SS2) Overall, the target is experimentally plausible but should be approached as a screening synthesis rather than a guaranteed single-phase preparation. (Target data; CS1/SS1; CS2/SS2)
