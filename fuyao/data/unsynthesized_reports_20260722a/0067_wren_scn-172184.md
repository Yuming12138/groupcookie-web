### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

#### F1 — Thermodynamic Stability

The reported target E_hull is −0.133090676 eV/atom, placing it in the highest stability bin. Chemically similar TlMnF₄ and Tl₂TiF₆ are both reported on the hull with E_hull = 0.0 eV/atom (CS-1, CS-2), which supports the chemical plausibility of stable Tl fluorometallates. The remaining uncertainty is that negative hull values can reflect database/reference-energy artifacts, but the value is strongly favorable rather than marginal [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2/F3 — Route 1

Route 1 conditions are moderate because the closest analogue Tl₂TiF₆ is prepared at only 300 °C for 4 days in a Pt ampoule under Ar (CS-2). The main severity penalties are toxic Tl fluoride and sealed fluoride containment, both implied by the use of dry-box/Pt-ampoule handling in the analogue (CS-2). Kinetically, Route 1 is strong because it uses preformed fluorides and maps directly onto the same A₂MF₆/P-3m1 isolated-octahedron structure type (CS-2), although ReF₄ may be less volatile/reactive than TiF₄ [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2/F3 — Route 2

Route 2 has mild thermal severity because TlMnF₄ crystallizes from HF at ~50 °C over several days in polyethylene equipment (CS-1). The penalties come from HF volatility/corrosivity and Tl toxicity, not from high temperature or high pressure (CS-1 + Reasonable Inference). Kinetically, solution-mediated HF chemistry should be favorable because ion transport occurs through dissolved fluoro complexes, but topology transfer is weaker because CS-1 forms TlMnF₄ with corner-sharing MnF₆ octahedra rather than isolated A₂MF₆ clusters (CS-1).

#### F2/F3 — Route 3

Route 3 is supported by sealed fluoride sintering precedents at 500–650 °C in Pt/Ag/Au containers (CS-3, CS-4, CS-5). Its condition severity is higher than Route 1 because longer high-temperature sealed treatment increases TlF volatility and phase-competition risk [Reasonable Inference]. Kinetically, flux/sintering improves mobility relative to dry low-temperature reaction, but the analogues often need many days and specialized sealed metal ampoules (CS-3 to CS-5), so the route is less attractive than Route 1.

#### F4 — Amorphization Tendency

The target is structurally simple: isolated ReF₆ octahedra plus Tl sites in a zero-dimensional P-3m1 structure, with no mixed edge/face/vertex-sharing network described in the target. The only triggered amorphization check is the large cation-size mismatch between Tl⁺ and Re⁴⁺ [Reasonable Inference]. The small formula size and isolated-octahedron topology reduce glass-forming complexity relative to extended mixed-connectivity networks.

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

Tl₂ReF₆ is likely a thermodynamically stable or near-ground-state phase because its reported E_hull is strongly favorable at −0.133 eV/atom, and chemically related Tl fluorides include stable hull phases (CS-1, CS-2). The strongest synthesis support is the exact structural and stoichiometric analogue Tl₂TiF₆, made by direct reaction of TlF and TiF₄ in a Pt ampoule at 300 °C under Ar (CS-2). The most important enabling factor is preorganization of the target chemistry through binary fluorides, especially a TlF + ReF₄ route that directly builds isolated [ReF₆]²⁻ units [Reasonable Inference based on CS-2]. The main limiting factor is maintaining Re in the +4 oxidation state while excluding moisture/oxygen and preventing Tl/F volatilization [Reasonable Inference]. SS and TS analogues do not materially strengthen the chemical synthesis recommendation because they are not fluoride/Tl/Re-related and are treated as neutral evidence.
