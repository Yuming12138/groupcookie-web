### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

The main uncertainty is that a negative hull for a chlorine-rich molecular adduct may reflect computational/reference-state issues rather than guaranteed ambient stability [Reasonable Inference].

**F2 — Route 1 condition severity.**  
Route 1 uses moderate temperature but requires dry Cl₂ and a sealed vessel, both of which add severity; direct dry Cl₂ use is supported by FeCl₃ CVT synthesis (CS-1). MoCl₅ ampoule synthesis shows that sealed chlorinated environments and glove-box handling are realistic but demanding (CS-2). No high-temperature >1000 °C or high-pressure >5 GPa penalty is assigned because the inferred route is low-temperature sealed-tube chemistry [Reasonable Inference]. The uncertainty is the required Cl₂ pressure, which is not given by CS for LiCl₈ specifically.

**F3 — Route 1 kinetics.**  
Dry Cl₂ provides high chemical activity, as demonstrated by Fe + Cl₂ formation/transport of FeCl₃ (CS-1). Kinetics are helped by gas/solid chlorine access and the lack of multiple cation diffusion, but LiCl itself is a solid and may be kinetically inert at low temperature [Reasonable Inference]. Structural reconstruction is nontrivial because the target contains separated Cl₂ molecules and LiCl₄ clusters, not ordinary rock-salt LiCl [Reasonable Inference]. CS-2 supports the existence of zero-dimensional chloride clusters, but insufficient evidence from SS and TS directly addresses LiCl₈ reconstruction.

**F2 — Route 2 condition severity.**

CS-2 shows multiple MoCl₅ polymorphs and phase-control complexity in chloride systems, supporting a lower composition/phase-control score. **F2 — Route 3 condition severity.**
