### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

#### F1 — Thermodynamic stability

The target E_hull is −0.004333 eV/atom, effectively a convex-hull phase, so the thermodynamic score is very high. TS analogues RbCd13, CsCd13, TbCd3, DyCd3, and HoCd3 are all E_hull = 0.0 eV/atom, showing that nearby Cd-rich intermetallics are thermodynamically robust (TS1–TS5). CS and SS provide corroboration through stable or near-stable Cd/indide analogues (CS1, CS4, CS5, SS1–SS5). Remaining uncertainty is that DFT hull stability does not guarantee that the exact BaCd4 ordering is the experimentally selected phase under all thermal histories [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2 — Route 1 direct sealed solid-state

Route 1 is not temperature-severe because analogous Cd intermetallic syntheses use 870–1170 K rather than >1000 °C (SS1, SS2, TS1, TS2). The main penalties are sealed handling of volatile Cd, multi-step loading/sealing/heating/cooling, and toxic/air-sensitive elemental precursors [Reasonable Inference from SS1, TS1]. CS gives supporting but less direct signal through sealed Ta/quartz use for Cd and indide intermetallics (CS1–CS3). Insufficient evidence from CS for exact BaCd4 formation by this route is treated neutrally for phase-specific severity.

#### F3 — Route 1 direct sealed solid-state

Elemental Ba/Cd precursors are lower-activity than salts or sol-gel precursors, but direct elemental synthesis is repeatedly successful in related Cd systems (SS1, SS2, TS1, TS2). Diffusion should be favorable for low-melting metals and Cd-rich intermetallics [Reasonable Inference], consistent with moderate-temperature sealed syntheses (SS1, SS2, TS1, TS2). Structural reconstruction is only partial because RbCd13/CsCd13 are not BaCd4 I4/mmm, so this route receives a moderate-high kinetics score rather than an excellent one. Insufficient evidence from CS for BaCd4 direct formation is treated neutrally.

#### F2 — Route 2 low-temperature pre-reaction/annealing

Route 2 benefits from low temperatures, since RECd3 analogues are pre-reacted at 400 °C and annealed at 600 °C (CS1, CS5, SS3–SS5, TS3–TS5). Its severity is reduced by the explicit use of vacuum, pellet pressing, Ta/quartz sealing, and annealing, all of which make it a multi-step controlled-atmosphere route (CS1, CS5, SS3–SS5, TS3–TS5). Cd volatility and Ba air sensitivity are not directly quantified in the sources but are practical concerns [Reasonable Inference]. Insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS for exact BaCd4 phase purity in this route is treated neutrally.

#### F3 — Route 2 low-temperature pre-reaction/annealing

Metal filings improve contact area, and filings are directly reported for RECd3 preparations (CS1, CS5, SS3–SS5, TS3–TS5). Diffusion is likely adequate because analogous Cd-rich phases form after moderate-temperature annealing (CS1, CS5, SS3–SS5, TS3–TS5). The structural reconstruction score is lower than for flux because the nearest sintering analogues are RECd3 Cmcm phases rather than the I4/mmm BaCd4/SrIn4 structure (CS1, CS5, SS3–SS5). The uncertainty is whether BaCd4 competes with other Ba–Cd phases under a long anneal [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2 — Route 3 Cd self-flux

Route 3 avoids high temperature and high pressure; the exact SrIn4 analogue uses 700 °C followed by 300–450 °C in an Ar glovebox/ampule flux setup (CS4). The penalties are mainly Cd volatility, multi-step flux growth/decanting, and hazardous/air-sensitive precursors [Reasonable Inference from CS4]. CS provides strong structural-method evidence, while SS/TS provide only indirect support for Cd intermetallic sealed handling; insufficient evidence from SS/TS for flux growth of BaCd4 is neutral. Overall severity is moderate rather than easy because Cd flux decanting is operationally demanding [Reasonable Inference].

#### F3 — Route 3 Cd self-flux

The flux route has the best kinetics because a liquid Cd-rich medium should enhance transport and composition equilibration [Reasonable Inference], and CS4 explicitly notes that self-flux is favorable for crystal growth. Structural reconstruction is strongly supported because SrIn4 has the same tetragonal I4/mmm framework and very similar coordination motif to the target (CS4). Composition control remains uncertain because the Ba–Cd phase diagram is not supplied, and excess Cd may stabilize other Cd-rich phases [Reasonable Inference]. SS/TS provide insufficient direct flux-kinetics evidence for BaCd4, so they are treated neutrally for this route-specific F3 component.

#### F4 — Amorphization tendency

The large Ba–Cd size mismatch triggers one amorphization/complexity check [Reasonable Inference], although the target’s ordered intermetallic coordination is compatible with large Ba coordination. Mixed polyhedral connectivity is inferred from the exact SrIn4 I4/mmm analogue, which reports edge-, corner-, and face-sharing polyhedra (CS4). Unit-cell-size evidence is insufficient from CS/SS/TS for the exact target, so that check is treated as neutral/not triggered. The resulting F4 score is medium because metallic flux or annealing routes should still crystallize well, as shown for related intermetallics (CS4, SS1, TS1).

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

BaCd4 is likely to be a ground-state or near-ground-state phase because its calculated E_hull is essentially zero, and many thermodynamically similar Cd-rich intermetallics are reported at E_hull = 0.0 (TS1–TS5). The strongest synthetic support comes from the exact structure-type analogue SrIn4 in tetragonal I4/mmm, which was grown by self-flux (CS4), plus broad evidence that Cd intermetallics can be made from elements in sealed Ta/Ar systems (SS1, SS2, TS1, TS2). The most critical enabling factor is the low-melting Cd-rich reaction medium, which should promote diffusion and ordering during flux growth [Reasonable Inference from CS4]. The most critical limiting factor is Cd volatility/toxicity combined with Ba air sensitivity, requiring excellent sealed-tube or ampoule practice [Reasonable Inference from CS4, SS1, TS1]. Phase competition with other Ba–Cd compounds remains the main scientific uncertainty because no direct BaCd4 experimental analogue is provided.
