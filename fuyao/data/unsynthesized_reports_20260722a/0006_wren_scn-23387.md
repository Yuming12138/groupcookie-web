### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

#### F1 — Thermodynamic stability

The target E_hull is −0.0089 eV/atom, which is within numerical tolerance of a ground-state phase and receives a high stability score (target data). Thermodynamic analogues K3Pd2O4, KPd2O3, and Na2Pd3O4 are all reported at E_hull = 0.0 eV/atom, supporting stable alkali palladate chemistry (TS-1; TS-2; TS-4). The main uncertainty is that Rb2Pd3O4 itself lacks direct experimental synthesis evidence in the provided CS/SS/TS set; however, the thermodynamic signal is strongly favorable (TS-1; TS-2; TS-4).

#### F2 — Route 1

Route 1 is penalized for controlled O2 pressure, sealed-tube handling, air-sensitive superoxide/peroxide precursors, and multi-step annealing/optimization because K3Pd2O4/KPd2O3 analogues used glovebox handling, sealed silica tubes, and controlled O2 pressures (CS-2; SS-1; SS-2; TS-1; TS-2). It avoids high temperature above 1000 °C and high pressure, which improves feasibility (CS-2; SS-1; SS-2; TS-1; TS-2). Remaining uncertainty is whether Rb analogues are more volatile or more reactive than K analogues under identical O2 conditions [Reasonable Inference].

#### F3 — Route 1

The KO2/PdO route has good precursor activity because reactive alkali superoxide and PdO gave K3Pd2O4/KPd2O3 at 600–700 °C (CS-2; SS-1; SS-2; TS-1; TS-2). Structural reconstruction is favorable because K3Pd2O4 has similar square-planar PdO4 units and high structural similarity to the target (SS-1; TS-1). The kinetic uncertainty is phase competition among Rb–Pd–O compositions under controlled pO2 [Reasonable Inference], as K–Pd–O analogues required optimization (CS-2; SS-1; SS-2; TS-1; TS-2).

#### F2 — Route 2

Route 2 has the best condition severity score because the Na2Pd3O4 analogue uses a relatively simple dry-Ar heating/quench protocol at 900 °C without high pressure (SS-4; TS-4). Deductions are mainly for air-sensitive Rb oxide/peroxide precursors and likely need for sealed or protected handling at high temperature [Reasonable Inference]. Insufficient evidence from CS for the exact Na2Pd3O4-style dry-Ar route, but SS/TS provide strong direct structural/thermodynamic support (SS-4; TS-4).

#### F3 — Route 2

Kinetics are moderate because 850–900 °C improves diffusion and the Na2Pd3O4 analogue shares A2Pd3O4 stoichiometry and PdO4 square-planar motifs (SS-4; TS-4). Precursor activity is lower than metathesis because oxide/peroxide + PdO requires solid-state interdiffusion [Reasonable Inference]. The main uncertainty is whether large Rb+ stabilizes the same framework or promotes alternate Rb-rich palladates [Reasonable Inference], since the direct analogue is Na rather than Rb (SS-4; TS-4).

#### F2 — Route 3

Route 3 is lower temperature but operationally hazardous because CsCoO2 azide/nitrate synthesis uses CsN3/CsNO3, ball milling, pellet pressing, vacuum drying, Ar atmosphere, and a closed vessel (CS-1). The route is penalized for controlled atmosphere, multi-step processing, sealed gas-generating chemistry, and azide hazard (CS-1). Insufficient evidence from SS and TS supports azide/nitrate synthesis for palladates specifically, so severity remains high despite low temperature.

#### F3 — Route 3

Azide/nitrate metathesis has high precursor activity because it generates reactive oxide/oxygen species and successfully produced CsCoO2 at 833 K (CS-1). However, structural reconstruction is uncertain because CsCoO2 uses CoO4 tetrahedra, unlike the square-planar PdO4 units in the target and K/Na palladate analogues (CS-1; SS-1; SS-4). Insufficient evidence from SS/TS for this method in Pd square-planar oxides lowers confidence.

#### F2 — Route 4

Route 4 uses moderate temperatures similar to alkali stannate and K/Pt/Ni oxide capsule syntheses, so it avoids high-temperature penalties (CS-4; CS-5; SS-3; TS-3). It is penalized for sealed inert handling, air-sensitive alkali oxide flux, multi-step capsule processing, and possible volatile Rb oxide loss [Reasonable Inference]. Direct flux evidence for Rb–Pd–O is insufficient from CS/SS/TS, so this route is exploratory.

#### F3 — Route 4

Flux/self-flux conditions can improve ion transport and crystal growth at lower temperature [Reasonable Inference], consistent with alkali oxide single-crystal/capsule syntheses over days at 480–550 °C (CS-4; CS-5; SS-3; TS-3). Structural reconstruction is less certain because the strongest flux precedents are Sn, Ni, or Pt systems rather than Rb–Pd–O (CS-4; CS-5; SS-3; TS-3). Related routes report inhomogeneity, impurities, and narrow phase windows, which limits the kinetic score (CS-4; CS-5; SS-3; TS-3).

#### F4 — Amorphization tendency

The target has a large cation size/coordination contrast between Rb–O and Pd–O environments, which triggers the ionic-radius mismatch criterion [Reasonable Inference] and is reflected by long Rb–O versus shorter Pd–O distances (target data; CS-2; SS-4). The closest structural analogue K3Pd2O4 has mixed face-, edge-, and corner-sharing OK4Pd2 octahedra, so mixed connectivity is likely for the Rb analogue [Reasonable Inference] (SS-1; TS-1). The unit-cell atom count criterion is not triggered because the provided target formula has only 9 atoms per formula and CS/SS/TS do not establish a >40 atom target cell.

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

Rb2Pd3O4 is likely a thermodynamic ground-state or near-ground-state phase because its reported E_hull is −0.0089 eV/atom and several K/Na alkali palladate analogues are at E_hull = 0.0 eV/atom (target data; TS-1; TS-2; TS-4). The synthesis evidence is strong in the sense that square-planar alkali palladates K3Pd2O4, KPd2O3, and Na2Pd3O4 have all been synthesized by accessible solid-state routes (CS-2; SS-1; SS-2; SS-4; TS-1; TS-2; TS-4). The most critical enabling factor is the robust PdO4 square-planar chemistry across Na/K/Pd and K/Pt analogues (SS-1; SS-3; SS-4; TS-1; TS-3; TS-4). The most critical limiting factor is Rb chemical-potential control, because larger and more reactive alkali oxide species may volatilize, react with containers, or stabilize competing Rb-rich phases [Reasonable Inference] while related alkali oxide syntheses require sealed/dry handling and optimized stoichiometry (CS-2; CS-4; CS-5; SS-1; SS-2; TS-1; TS-2). Overall, the best practical route is a Na2Pd3O4-inspired dry-Ar high-temperature reaction with slight Rb excess and quenching, followed by Route 1 O2 sealed-tube screening if Pd oxidation state control is problematic (SS-4; TS-4; CS-2; SS-1; TS-1).
