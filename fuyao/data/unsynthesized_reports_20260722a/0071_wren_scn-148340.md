### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic stability.**  
The target E_hull is reported as −0.028317 eV/atom, which falls in the ≤0.05 eV/atom stability bin and is treated as effectively thermodynamically stable [Reasonable Inference]. CS analogues Cs₂IrF₆, Rb₂IrF₆, Cs₂SnF₆, and RbAuF₄ have E_hull = 0.0 eV/atom or near-zero values, supporting the stability of related alkali fluorometallates (CS). SS and TS are not chemically related to Cs₂BiF₆ and provide insufficient evidence for fluoride thermodynamic chemistry; they are neutral, score 50.

**F2 — Route 1 condition severity.**  
The Cs₂IrF₆ and Rb₂IrF₆ analogues crystallize from aqueous solution at room temperature by slow evaporation, so Route 1 avoids high temperature, high pressure, and reactive gas flow (CS). The deductions come from the expected need to prepare or stabilize H₂[BiF₆] and to use HF/oxidizing fluoride media, which are [Reasonable Inference] rather than directly shown in CS. SS and TS give insufficient evidence for this route and are neutral.

**F3 — Route 1 kinetics.**  
Solution crystallization has favorable kinetics because Cs₂IrF₆ formed directly from Cs₂CO₃ and H₂[IrF₆] in aqueous solution (CS). Structural reconstruction should be minimal because Cs₂IrF₆ is isostructural with the target P-3m1 phase and has the same 18° octahedral tilt (CS). The uncertainty is whether Bi(IV) fluoro-complexes persist long enough in solution to crystallize Cs₂BiF₆ [Reasonable Inference].

**F2 — Route 2 condition severity.**  
Direct fluorination is substantially harsher because Cs₂PtF₆ required diluted F₂/N₂ at 400 °C for 10 d and subsequent 800 °C heating in a sealed Pt tube (CS). RbAuF₄ also required F₂ at 300–400 °C and was noted as moisture/air sensitive in the detailed reasoning (CS). SS and TS do not provide useful evidence for oxidative fluoride synthesis, so they are neutral.

**F3 — Route 2 kinetics.**  
F₂ provides high fluorination activity, but the Cs₂PtF₆ analogue required very long heating, suggesting slow ordering or nucleation of the desired A₂MF₆ phase (CS). The target’s trigonal P-3m1 form is more directly matched by Cs₂IrF₆ and Cs₂SnF₆ than by cubic Cs₂PtF₆, so structural transfer from Route 2 is good but not perfect (CS). The Bi oxidation step may either help by increasing fluoride activity or hurt by producing competing Bi fluoride phases [Reasonable Inference].

**F2 — Route 3 condition severity.**  
The sealed fluoride solid-state route is less severe than F₂ gas fluorination because Cs₂SnF₆ was synthesized at 873 K for 12 h under Ar in a gold tube (CS). It still requires dry inert handling and a sealed noble-metal tube, and an oxidizing fluoride additive may introduce pressure or reactivity hazards [Reasonable Inference]. SS and TS are neutral for this factor due to insufficient chemical relevance.

**F3 — Route 3 kinetics.**  
Cs₂SnF₆ is isostructural with the target and forms under moderate solid-state fluoride conditions, which supports the kinetic accessibility of the P-3m1 Cs₂MF₆ framework (CS). However, the Cs₂SnF₆ report notes that pure samples were never realized and that the desired phase was at least 75 mass%, indicating competing phases or incomplete reaction (CS). Bi(IV) stabilization adds an additional kinetic/chemical bottleneck not present for Sn(IV) [Reasonable Inference].

**F4 — Amorphization tendency.**  
The target has a large size/coordination mismatch between 12-coordinate Cs and octahedral Bi, so one amorphization-risk trigger is assigned [Reasonable Inference from target structure]. The cell is small and the reported connectivity includes corner and face sharing but not edge sharing, so the atom-count and full mixed-connectivity triggers are not assigned from the target description. CS analogues with the same P-3m1 framework crystallize successfully, especially Cs₂IrF₆ and Cs₂SnF₆, reducing concern about intrinsic glass formation (CS).

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

Cs₂BiF₆ is likely to be a thermodynamically stable or near-ground-state phase because its reported E_hull is negative and within the stable range [Reasonable Inference]. The strongest synthesis support comes from CS: Cs₂IrF₆ is essentially isostructural with the target and crystallizes by mild aqueous slow evaporation, while Cs₂SnF₆ is also isostructural and forms by sealed fluoride solid-state reaction. The most critical enabling factor is the robust A₂MF₆ framework chemistry of Cs⁺ with isolated MF₆ octahedra, which is repeatedly demonstrated in Cs₂IrF₆, Rb₂IrF₆, Cs₂SnF₆, and Cs₂PtF₆ (CS). The most critical limiting factor is maintaining Bi in the unusual +4 oxidation state under fluoride-rich conditions, which is not directly demonstrated by CS, SS, or TS and is therefore a [Reasonable Inference]. SS and TS provide little chemically relevant evidence for this target and are treated as neutral rather than contradictory.
