Route 1 receives a moderate-low severity score because its temperature window is not extreme: related SrNiF₆ forms at 280 °C (CS-2), and Ba/Ni fluoride growth occurs at 450–600 °C (CS-4). #### F3 — Route 1 reaction kinetics

SrF₂ is a chemically appropriate but not highly labile precursor, so its activity is only moderate; SrF₂ use is supported in CS-5. The main kinetic uncertainty is structural reconstruction: forming SrF₆ from SrF₂ likely requires insertion/stabilization of additional fluorine or F–F units, and CS-2/CS-4 both indicate phase-control difficulties for high-fluorine phases.

#### F2 — Route 2 condition severity

Route 2 avoids high temperature because the close SrF₂/HF precedent operates at 573 K, about 300 °C (CS-5). ### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

The target appears thermodynamically favorable or at least strongly predicted to be stable, because its reported E_hull is negative and falls in the best stability bin. CS analogues support the broader feasibility of high-fluorine Sr/Ba/Ca fluoride chemistry: SrNiF₆ forms under high-pressure F₂ (CS-2), CaPdF₆ forms under direct F₂ fluorination (CS-3), and SrF(HF₂)/SrF₃ forms from SrF₂/HF at 3 GPa (CS-5). SS and TS do not provide useful Sr–F chemical guidance, so their contribution is neutral rather than strongly supportive. The most critical enabling factor is very high fluorine chemical potential supplied by HF/F₂ media or pressurized F₂, supported by CS-1, CS-2, CS-4, and CS-5. The most critical limiting factor is the unusual SrF₆ stoichiometry and likely need to stabilize F–F/polyfluoride-like units without forming SrF₂ or SrF(HF₂)-type competing phases; this limitation is a [Reasonable Inference] from the target formula and the SrF(HF₂) precedent in CS-5.
