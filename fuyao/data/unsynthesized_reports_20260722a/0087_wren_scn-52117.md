**F2/F3 — Route 1, high-pressure F₂ fluorination.**  
The severity score is reduced because CS analogues require F₂, pressure-compatible Monel autoclaves, and toxic/reactive fluorination conditions (CS-1, CS-4). The temperature is not extreme because CS-1 and CS-4 operate at 280–600 °C, and pressure is below the rubric’s >5 GPa penalty even for >3500 atm F₂ in CS-1. **F2/F3 — Route 2, high-pressure HF hydrothermal synthesis.**  
F2 is penalized for HF toxicity, sealed high-pressure handling, and multi-operation synthesis, all directly supported by the 3 GPa HF route in CS-3. F3 is high because HF provides a fluid-mediated fluoride environment, and CS-3 reports single crystals of SrF(HF₂) and BaF(HF₂) at only 573 K. Structurally, CS-3 is the best analogue because SrF₃ is monoclinic P2₁/m with 9-coordinate alkaline-earth cation geometry, close to the target’s P2₁/m and 9-coordinate Ba environment. The remaining uncertainty is whether BaF(HF₂)-type chemistry converts to the exact binary BaF₆ stoichiometry rather than retaining hydrogen-containing HF₂⁻ species [Reasonable Inference].

**F2/F3 — Route 3, aHF/F₂ solvolysis.**  
The route is mild in temperature because CS-2 uses room-temperature aHF/F₂ fluorination, but F2 is reduced for F₂/HF toxicity, volatility, sealed/reactive handling, and multiple steps.
