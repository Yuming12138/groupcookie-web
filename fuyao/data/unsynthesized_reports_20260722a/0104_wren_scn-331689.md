Remaining uncertainty is whether the negative hull value reflects a true ground state or database/DFT correction artifacts [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2 — Route 1 condition severity

Route 1 is moderate severity because the analogue syntheses operate at only ~600 °C and ambient sealed-ampoule pressure, not at >1000 °C or high pressure (CS1, CS4, CS5). The penalties come from volatile Se requiring sealed containment, multistep heating/cooling/tempering, and toxic/air-sensitive Cs/Tl handling [Reasonable Inference; sealed-ampoule and Ar handling basis from CS1/CS4/CS5]. #### F3 — Route 1 kinetics
