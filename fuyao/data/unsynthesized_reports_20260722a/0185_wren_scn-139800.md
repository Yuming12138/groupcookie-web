### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

#### F1 — Thermodynamic stability

The target has E_hull = −0.002831865 eV/atom, which is effectively 0 eV/atom and receives a high stability score of 98 (target data). The TS set provides strong positive evidence because all top thermodynamic analogues, including Rb4Au7Sn2 and K4Au7Ge2, are listed at E_hull = 0.0 eV/atom (TS-1–TS-5). CS and SS also support this conclusion because their nearest analogues are reported on the hull (CS-1–CS-5; SS-1–SS-5). Remaining uncertainty is mostly DFT/numerical tolerance and possible finite-temperature phase competition [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2 — Route 1 condition severity

Route 1 is moderate-severity because the exact Rb analogue is synthesized at only 500 °C for 10 days under Ar, with no high pressure or extreme temperature (CS-3 / SS-1 / TS-1). The main deductions are for sealed handling of volatile/reactive K and air-sensitive alkali-metal precursors, consistent with the use of sealed Ta/steel or Ta ampoule methods in related systems (CS-3 / SS-1 / TS-1; SS-5 / TS-5; [Reasonable Inference]). CS/SS/TS do not indicate any need for high pressure, so that factor is not penalized. The remaining uncertainty is whether K4Au7Sn2 needs tighter K activity control than Rb4Au7Sn2 [Reasonable Inference].

#### F3 — Route 1 reaction kinetics

The strongest kinetic evidence is that Rb4Au7Sn2 forms directly from stoichiometric elements in a single annealing step at 500 °C (CS-3 / SS-1 / TS-1). The long 10-day anneal indicates slow ordering or diffusion, but the presence of molten K/Sn at the reaction temperature likely assists transport [Reasonable Inference]. The structural reconstruction burden is low because the target and Rb analogue share the same R-3m layered Au–Sn framework (CS-3 / SS-1 / TS-1). Insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS remains on the exact K/Rb substitution phase width, so the score is high but not near-perfect.

#### F2 — Route 2 condition severity

Route 2 uses a reported K4Au7Ge2 protocol at 550 °C for 3 days under Ar in evacuated ampoules (CS-1 / SS-2 / TS-2). It is penalized for evacuated/sealed conditions, multiple handling steps, and KN3 hazards, since the source explicitly reports drying, Ar grinding, ampoule loading, and sealed heating (CS-1 / SS-2 / TS-2). KN3 safety risks are not quantified in CS/SS/TS, but are a standard chemical hazard extrapolation [Reasonable Inference]. Insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS exists for direct KN3 + Sn + Au synthesis of K4Au7Sn2, so transferability is less certain than Route 1.

#### F3 — Route 2 reaction kinetics

KN3 provides a more reactive K source than bulk K metal and was successful for K4Au7Ge2 at 550 °C (CS-1 / SS-2 / TS-2). The structural match is good because K4Au7Ge2 has the same K4Au7Tt2 stoichiometry and R-3m Au framework, while Rb4Au7Sn2 confirms Sn compatibility with the same framework (CS-1 / SS-2 / TS-2; CS-3 / SS-1 / TS-1). However, Ge → Sn substitution changes bond lengths and liquid formation behavior, and no source directly reports K4Au7Sn2 from KN3, so that claim is an extrapolation [Reasonable Inference]. The 3-day hold suggests kinetics are manageable but not instantaneous (CS-1 / SS-2 / TS-2).

#### F2 — Route 3 condition severity

Route 3 is more severe because related K/Rb–Au trielides use Ta containers, evacuated silica jackets, controlled cooling, and long equilibration at 450 °C after 650 °C heating (CS-2 / SS-3 / TS-3; CS-4 / SS-4 / TS-4). K2Au3 also requires sealed Ta ampoules and a long staged cooling process (SS-5 / TS-5). No high pressure is used in these references, so high-pressure penalties do not apply. The main uncertainties are whether higher temperature improves homogenization or instead destabilizes the target composition [Reasonable Inference].

#### F3 — Route 3 reaction kinetics

The use of 650 °C and controlled cooling should improve intermetallic diffusion compared with a lower-temperature anneal, as related K/Rb–Au phases form under such schedules (CS-2 / SS-3 / TS-3; CS-4 / SS-4 / TS-4). However, the structural analogues in Route 3 are less exact than Rb4Au7Sn2 or K4Au7Ge2, so structural reconstruction and phase selection are less favorable (CS-2 / SS-3 / TS-3; SS-5 / TS-5). The evidence also reports unsuccessful analogue attempts in related trielide chemistry, implying stoichiometry and composition sensitivity (CS-2 / SS-3 / TS-3; CS-4 / SS-4 / TS-4). Therefore kinetics are moderate, but phase selectivity is the main limitation.

#### F4 — Amorphization tendency

The target is an ordered intermetallic with large alkali-metal versus Au/Sn size mismatch, which triggers one amorphization-risk check [Reasonable Inference]; long K/Rb–Au contacts and short Au–Au/Au–Sn bonds in the analogues support strong size contrast in the coordination network (CS-3 / SS-1 / TS-1; CS-1 / SS-2 / TS-2). The closest analogue describes edge-sharing cuboctahedra rather than mixed vertex/edge/face connectivity, so the connectivity check is not triggered for the target-like structure (CS-3 / SS-1 / TS-1). Insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS exists for the exact target unit-cell atom count, but the formula and described three-sheet motif suggest the cell is likely not far above 40 atoms [Reasonable Inference]. The resulting amorphization risk is low–medium rather than high.

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

K4Au7Sn2 is likely a thermodynamic ground-state or near-ground-state phase because its reported E_hull is −0.002831865 eV/atom, effectively on the hull (target data), and the closest TS analogues are also on the hull (TS-1–TS-5). The synthesis case is strongly supported by the exact Sn-containing Rb analogue Rb4Au7Sn2 in the same R-3m layered structure and by the K-containing Ge analogue K4Au7Ge2 in the same K4Au7Tt2 framework (CS-3 / SS-1 / TS-1; CS-1 / SS-2 / TS-2). The most critical enabling factor is the demonstrated stability of alkali–Au–group-14 layered frameworks at moderate temperatures using sealed inert solid-state methods (CS-1 / SS-2 / TS-2; CS-3 / SS-1 / TS-1). The main limiting factor is chemical handling and stoichiometry control for K, because related K–Au phases require sealed Ta ampoules and slow cooling to avoid loss or competing phase formation (SS-5 / TS-5; [Reasonable Inference]). Overall, the target should be synthetically accessible, with Route 1 being the most direct and best-supported approach.
