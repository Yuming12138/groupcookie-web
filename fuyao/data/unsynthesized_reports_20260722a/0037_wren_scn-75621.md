### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic stability.**  
The reported target E_hull is −0.134367262 eV/atom, placing it well inside the ≤0.05 eV/atom “highly synthesizable” thermodynamic window. Chemically similar nitrides such as CsNbN₂, Ba₂VN₃, Ba₂NbN₃, Sr₂NbN₃, and Ba₃MnN₃ have E_hull values of 0–0.0078 eV/atom, supporting the plausibility of stable alkali/alkaline-earth transition-metal nitrides (CS-1 to CS-5). SS and TS entries are mostly unrelated amides, borides, phosphates, bromides, or intermetallic nitrides, so they give insufficient evidence for CsCrN₂ thermodynamic chemistry and are scored neutral 50 as source signals. The remaining uncertainty is that the negative hull value may reflect calculation/reference-state artifacts rather than a directly realized experimental ground state [Reasonable Inference].

**F2 — Route 1 condition severity.**  
Route 1 avoids very high temperature because CsNbN₂ was made at 600 °C, but it requires high-pressure autoclaves and reactive moisture-sensitive CsNH₂ (CS-1). The scoring deducts for sealed/volatile component management and air-sensitive precursor handling, while not deducting for >5 GPa because CS-1 specifies high-pressure autoclaves but not multi-GPa conditions. SS/TS give insufficient route-specific Cs–Cr autoclave evidence and are neutral 50 for this condition factor. Uncertainty lies in whether Cr requires a different nitrogen chemical potential than Nb to form CsCrN₂ [Reasonable Inference].

**F3 — Route 1 kinetics.**  
CsNH₂ is a strong reactive medium and successfully produced CsNbN₂ from NbN, suggesting good precursor activity and enhanced transport (CS-1). However, CrN is likely refractory and the target Cmce edge/corner-sharing CrN₄ network is not identical to cubic CsNbN₂, so partial structural reconstruction is expected [Reasonable Inference]. The 9-day hold for CsNbN₂ indicates kinetics may be slow even in the favorable amide/autoclave medium (CS-1). SS/TS do not improve the kinetic estimate for this specific route and are neutral 50.

**F2 — Route 2 condition severity.**  
Solid-state analogues use 900–1030 °C, Ar or N₂ atmospheres, sealed crucibles, gloveboxes, and slow cooling (CS-2 to CS-4). Route 2 is penalized for multi-step handling, air-sensitive nitride precursors, and sealed Cs/N stoichiometry control, but not for NH₃/H₂/vacuum because the closest analogues use Ar/N₂ (CS-2 to CS-4). SS/TS are insufficient for Cs–Cr solid-state nitride transfer and neutral 50. The main uncertainty is whether a sub-1000 °C window is enough for CsCrN₂ or whether the Ba₂VN₃-like 1030 °C condition becomes necessary (CS-2; [Reasonable Inference]).

**F3 — Route 2 kinetics.**  
Binary nitride solid-state routes work for Ba₂VN₃, Ba₂NbN₃, and Sr₂NbN₃, but they require long holds and sometimes grinding/slow cooling, indicating moderate diffusion barriers (CS-2 to CS-4). Structural transfer is promising because Ba₂VN₃ is also Cmce and contains transition-metal N₄ tetrahedra with M–N distances close to the target Cr–N distances (CS-2). The target-specific challenge is coupling volatile Cs chemistry with refractory CrN in a sealed environment [Reasonable Inference]. SS/TS are neutral 50 due to insufficient directly relevant evidence.

**F2 — Route 3 condition severity.**  
Ammonolysis uses reactive flowing NH₃ at 725–950 °C for 12–72 h in the W₃Ni₂N analogue (SS-5, TS-5), so the route is penalized for reactive atmosphere and multi-step processing. It avoids high pressure and can remain below 1000 °C, but NH₃ toxicity/reactivity and Cr precursor hazards add handling severity [Reasonable Inference]. CS only partially supports controlled nitride atmospheres and gives no direct ammonolysis route for CsCrN₂, so CS is weaker than SS/TS for this route. The main uncertainty is Cs retention under flowing NH₃ at high temperature [Reasonable Inference].

**F3 — Route 3 kinetics.**  
Salt/oxide-derived precursor routes can offer high mixing and high precursor activity, and W₃Ni₂N ammonolysis demonstrates nitride formation from precursor oxides under flowing NH₃ (SS-5, TS-5). However, forming ordered CsCrN₂ from oxide/salt precursors requires complete anion exchange and framework reconstruction, which lowers the structural-reconstruction score [Reasonable Inference]. W₃Ni₂N also shows narrow temperature/time windows and impurity/decomposition risk, supporting caution for phase control (SS-5, TS-5). CS provides insufficient direct evidence for oxide/salt ammonolysis of CsCrN₂ and is neutral 50.

**F4 — Amorphization tendency.**  
The target combines very large Cs coordination distances with short Cr–N tetrahedral bonds, implying a large cation size mismatch that triggers C1 [Reasonable Inference]. The target also explicitly contains mixed edge- and corner-sharing CrN₄ tetrahedra, triggering C3 and raising medium amorphization/kinetic-trapping risk. No provided source proves a >40-atom unit cell for the target, so C2 is not triggered; CS/SS/TS are insufficient for this target-specific cell-count factor and score neutral 50. Ba₂VN₃ demonstrates that related mixed-connectivity nitride frameworks can crystallize, partially mitigating the risk (CS-2).

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

CsCrN₂ is likely a thermodynamic ground-state or near-ground-state phase because its reported hull energy is negative, and chemically similar nitrides in CS are on or near the hull (CS-1 to CS-5). The strongest synthesis support comes from CsNbN₂, which directly demonstrates that a Cs–transition-metal nitride with MN₄ tetrahedra can be synthesized from CsNH₂ and a transition-metal nitride in an autoclave (CS-1). Additional support comes from Ba₂VN₃, a Cmce nitridometalate with VN₄ tetrahedra and V–N distances close to the target Cr–N distances (CS-2). The most critical enabling factor is the use of a reactive CsNH₂-rich medium that can provide Cs, nitrogen activity, and transport at moderate temperature (CS-1). The most critical limiting factor is phase/oxidation-state control for chromium, since the target formally requires high-valent tetrahedral Cr nitride chemistry and no direct Cs–Cr analogue is given [Reasonable Inference].
