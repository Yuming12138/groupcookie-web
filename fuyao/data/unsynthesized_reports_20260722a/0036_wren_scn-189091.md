### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic stability.**  
The target’s reported E_hull is −0.13467763 eV/atom, so by the supplied rubric it is in the ≤0.05 eV/atom bin and receives 100/100. CS1–CS4 are all reported with E_hull = 0.0 eV/atom and are experimentally synthesized, supporting that low-hull alkali hexafluorides can be made. SS and TS provide insufficient chemistry-specific evidence for Cs–N–F stability and are treated as neutral chemistry signals, although TS entries are generally low-hull reported phases.

**F2 — Route 1.**  
Route 1 is penalized for reactive fluorination because CS4 uses fluorine gas at 200–300 °C, and the target route would require similarly aggressive fluorination. It is also penalized for volatile/toxic nitrogen-fluoride precursors by [Reasonable Inference]. The temperature itself is not severe because CS4 operates at 270 °C and CS1/CS3 show related fluoride syntheses below 1000 °C.

**F3 — Route 1.**  
Kinetics benefit from the same Fm-3m A2MF6 topology in CS4 and from reactive F2 chemistry in CS4. Kinetics are limited because CsF is a stable fluoride precursor as in CS1/CS3, and conversion to an NF6 octahedron is not directly evidenced. Remaining uncertainty is high because no CS/SS/TS entry demonstrates nitrogen-centered hexafluoride octahedra.

**F2 — Route 2.**  
Route 2 avoids flowing F2 but requires sealed handling of volatile/reactive nitrogen-fluoride donors, which triggers severity penalties by [Reasonable Inference]. CS1 and CS3 support sealed Au/Pt-tube fluoride synthesis at moderate temperature, so no high-temperature or high-pressure deduction is applied. If NF4 salts require prior synthesis, the route becomes multi-step.

**F3 — Route 2.**  
Solid-state kinetics are moderate because CS1 and CS3 show Cs-containing fluorides form under sealed heating at 600–873 K/600 °C. The precursor activity is lower than in solution routes because CsF and other binary fluorides are relatively stable, consistent with CS1/CS3. Structural reconstruction is only partially supported because CS1/CS4 contain A2MF6-like frameworks but not N-centered octahedra.

**F2 — Route 3.**  
CS2 provides a mild precedent: room-temperature reaction of Rb2CO3 with H2[IrF6] followed by slow evaporation. The formal condition severity is therefore low, but the need for a preformed H2[NF6]/[NF6]²⁻ reagent adds a multi-step and toxic/air-sensitive precursor penalty by [Reasonable Inference]. SS and TS give insufficient evidence for this solution chemistry.

**F3 — Route 3.**  
Solution crystallization has favorable kinetics because CS2 forms Rb2IrF6 crystals by slow evaporation without high-temperature diffusion. If a discrete [NF6]²⁻ unit exists, structural reconstruction into Cs2NF6 would be limited, which supports a moderate-to-high kinetics score. The main uncertainty is precursor existence and stability, not ion diffusion.

**F4 — Amorphization tendency.**  
The target has a simple A2MF6-type description with a single Cs site, single N site, and single F site, which reduces configurational complexity compared with many SS/TS structures. The target description includes corner and face sharing but no edge sharing, so the mixed-connectivity amorphization trigger is not fully met. The main amorphization/defect risk is the extreme size and bonding mismatch between Cs and the N-centered octahedral site, which is a [Reasonable Inference].

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

The target is predicted as a thermodynamically stable or even below-hull phase by the supplied E_hull value, so the thermodynamic metric strongly favors synthesizability. The chemically similar CS data strongly support the general Cs/Rb A2MF6 structural family, especially CS4 because it has the same cubic Fm-3m topology, and CS1/CS3 because they show sealed-tube alkali fluoride synthesis is practical. However, the support for an N-centered NF6 octahedron is weak; no CS/SS/TS analogue directly demonstrates Cs2NF6 or a stable [NF6]²⁻ precursor. The most critical enabling factor is the strong A2MF6 structural analogy and favorable computed hull. The most critical limiting factor is chemical plausibility and kinetic stabilization of a highly fluorinated nitrogen octahedron under conditions that avoid volatile nitrogen-fluoride loss or decomposition.
