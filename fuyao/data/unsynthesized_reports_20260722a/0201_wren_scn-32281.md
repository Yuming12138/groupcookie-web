### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic stability.**  
The target has E_hull = −0.082685 eV/atom, which is in the most favorable ≤0.05 eV/atom bin and is scored as highly stable by the supplied target data. Chemically similar CaP and Ca2N are on the hull, and Ca2Si is essentially on the hull at 0.0011 eV/atom, showing that related Ca-rich binary pnictide/silicide phases can be thermodynamic phases (CS). TS analogues are also mostly near hull, but they are chemically unrelated to Ca–P, so they provide insufficient chemical transfer evidence from TS and are treated as neutral signal = 50.

**F2 — Route 1 condition severity.**  
The direct elemental route inherits moderate severity from CaP synthesis, which used sealed evacuated tubes, red phosphorus, reactive metal turnings, pressing, and several-day high-temperature annealing (CS). The key deductions are controlled/vacuum atmosphere, volatile P in a sealed ampoule, multistep processing, and air-sensitive Ca/Ca–P handling (CS; [Reasonable Inference]). The uncertainty is whether Ca5P3 forms below 900 °C like CaP or requires hotter Ca-rich equilibration [Reasonable Inference].

**F3 — Route 1 reaction kinetics.**  
Elemental Ca and P are reactive enough to form CaP under solid-state conditions, but the CaP analogue required prolonged annealing and pressing, indicating nontrivial kinetics (CS). The diffusion burden is moderate-to-low because Ca and P are not refractory, but the structural conversion from elemental mixture to the tetragonal Ca5P3 network is a full reconstruction [Reasonable Inference]. This supports a mid-range F3 score.

**F2 — Route 2 condition severity.**  
The Ca-rich self-flux route is more severe because it likely uses excess molten Ca and may require temperatures near or above 1000 °C [Reasonable Inference from Ca2Si staged high-temperature Ca-rich synthesis in CS]. It also requires sealed inert equipment, volatile phosphorus containment, and air-sensitive handling, as seen in CaP, K4P3, and Ca2Si synthesis descriptions (CS). The route is penalized despite good transport because container compatibility and excess-Ca removal are challenging [Reasonable Inference].

**F3 — Route 2 reaction kinetics.**  
Excess Ca should improve mass transport through a flux or partially molten environment [Reasonable Inference], and analogous excess-metal strategies were essential for K4P3 and Ca2Si synthesis (CS). The main kinetic advantage is improved diffusion, but nucleation of the exact Ca5P3 structure is not evidenced by SS or TS analogues; insufficient structural transfer evidence from SS/TS, neutral signal = 50. Phase selectivity remains the kinetic/thermodynamic bottleneck [Reasonable Inference].

**F2 — Route 3 condition severity.**  
The binary-phosphide precursor route avoids the most violent direct Ca–P reaction but adds precursor preparation and repeated inert handling [Reasonable Inference]. Its severity is supported by Ca2N synthesis from Ca3N2 requiring sealed purified-Ar high-temperature treatment and glovebox manipulation (CS). It is less temperature-severe than the Ca-rich flux route if kept in the 700–950 °C range inferred from CaP synthesis (CS; [Reasonable Inference]).

**F3 — Route 3 reaction kinetics.**  
Starting from Ca–P binary precursors should provide shorter chemical rearrangement distances than elemental Ca + P [Reasonable Inference]. Ca2N demonstrates that calcium pnictide precursor stoichiometry can be adjusted under sealed inert high-temperature conditions, although this is a nitride rather than phosphide analogue (CS). The remaining uncertainty is that no provided source directly reports Ca3P2/CaP conversion to Ca5P3, so the structural reconstruction score is moderate.

**F4 — Amorphization tendency.**  
The target has only one cation, Ca, so the cation-radius mismatch criterion is not triggered by the target composition. The target description reports corner-sharing CaP6 octahedra but not simultaneous vertex/edge/face-sharing coexistence, so the mixed-connectivity amorphization check is not triggered. The atom-count check is uncertain because the full crystallographic Z is not provided; based on the 8-atom formula and no evidence for a >40-atom cell, it is treated as not triggered [Reasonable Inference].

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

Ca5P3 is likely a thermodynamically stable or near-ground-state phase because the supplied E_hull is negative, which places it in the most favorable stability category (target). The strongest synthesis support comes from the chemically similar CaP route, where binary Ca–P material was made from elements in sealed evacuated tubes with several-day annealing (CS). The enabling factor is the availability of highly reactive Ca and P chemistry under sealed inert conditions, plus the target’s favorable calculated stability (CS; target). The limiting factor is phase selectivity in a Ca-rich Ca–P phase field, since K4P3 and Ca2Si analogues show that excess-metal stoichiometry and staged annealing strongly affect product purity (CS). SS and TS analogues provide little direct chemical guidance for Ca5P3 because they are largely chemically unrelated, so their route-transfer value is neutral rather than strongly supportive.
