### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

#### F1 — Thermodynamic stability

The target’s reported E_hull of −0.0117 eV/atom places it effectively on the convex hull, so the thermodynamic score is very high. CS analogues YFe₆Ga₆, TbFe₆Ga₆, DyFe₆Ga₆, ErFe₆Ga₆, and TmFe₆Ga₆ all have E_hull = 0.0 eV/atom and the same Immm structural family, reinforcing that this Fe/Ga framework is thermodynamically credible (CS). TS entries are stable or near-stable, but they are chemically unrelated, so TS gives insufficient useful target-specific thermodynamic signal beyond neutrality.

#### F2 — Route 1 condition severity

Route 1 inherits moderate-to-high procedural severity from the CS arc-melting route: high-temperature melting, Ar atmosphere, sealed evacuated annealing, 800 °C/120 h heat treatment, and quenching are all reported for the CS analogues (CS). Mg introduces added volatility and air-sensitivity risks not present in the rare-earth analogues, so Mg excess and sealed annealing are recommended [Reasonable Inference]. SS and TS do not provide useful Mg–Fe–Ga-specific condition evidence, so they are neutral for this factor.

#### F3 — Route 1 reaction kinetics

The CS analogues show that the Immm REFe₆Ga₆ structure can be reached by arc melting plus long annealing, which supports feasible ordering kinetics for a closely related Fe/Ga intermetallic framework (CS). The main kinetic penalty is that Fe is refractory and dense elemental metals require melt homogenization or long annealing [Reasonable Inference]. The isostructural match to CS reduces structural-reconstruction difficulty, but the 120 h anneal indicates nontrivial ordering kinetics (CS).

#### F2 — Route 2 condition severity

Route 2 avoids full arc melting but still requires evacuated or inert sealed containment and long annealing near 800 °C, consistent with the anneal conditions in the CS analogues (CS). Mg volatility makes a sealed system necessary, adding severity and safety risk [Reasonable Inference]. SS/TS examples use very different chemistries, including hydrothermal amides, phosphates, nitrides, and halides, so they provide insufficient direct signal for Mg–Fe–Ga sealed-tube synthesis.

#### F3 — Route 2 reaction kinetics

Without arc melting, Fe/Ga/Mg homogenization and long-range ordering are likely slower than in the CS method, because the CS analogues first use arc melting and then annealing for 120 h (CS; Reasonable Inference). Liquid Ga and Mg at the annealing temperature may improve wetting and diffusion, but Fe remains the slow-diffusing component [Reasonable Inference]. The same Immm framework as CS analogues lowers the structural-reconstruction burden, but phase purity may require repeated grinding/annealing [CS; Reasonable Inference].

#### F4 — Amorphization tendency

The target has a complex intermetallic connectivity with corner-, edge-, and face-sharing polyhedra, matching the mixed connectivity seen in the CS Immm analogues (CS). The Mg vs Fe/Ga size mismatch triggers an amorphization/disorder risk, although this is a metallic-radius extrapolation rather than a directly reported CS/SS/TS observation [Reasonable Inference]. The unit-cell-size trigger is not clearly met from the provided formula/structure description, and CS does not indicate exceptionally large cells, so this check is not triggered.

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

MgFe₆Ga₆ is likely close to a ground-state phase because its reported E_hull is slightly negative and the isostructural REFe₆Ga₆ analogues are calculated on the hull (CS). The CS evidence is strong because the analogues share the same orthorhombic Immm structure and nearly identical Fe/Ga polyhedral framework, whereas SS and TS provide insufficient direct chemical support. The most critical enabling factor is the demonstrated stability and synthesizability of the Immm Fe₆Ga₆ framework by arc melting plus 800 °C annealing (CS). The main limiting factor is Mg volatility and stoichiometry control during high-temperature alloying, which is a target-specific concern not directly resolved by the rare-earth CS analogues [Reasonable Inference]. A secondary limitation is slow Fe/Ga/Mg ordering, suggested by the long 120 h anneals used for the CS phases (CS).
