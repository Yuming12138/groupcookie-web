### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

#### F1 — Thermodynamic Stability

The target E_hull is −0.009126663 eV/atom, so it is effectively thermodynamically stable and receives 98/100 under the provided rubric. TS provides the strongest thermodynamic support because Cs₂ReCl₆ is a stable exact Cs–Re–Cl analogue with E_hull = 0.0 (TS-5). CS and SS also show many stable alkali/transition-metal chlorides, including Rb₂PtCl₆, K₂MoCl₆, and CsAuCl₄, but they do not directly confirm CsReCl₆ stoichiometry (CS-4/CS-5/SS-2/TS-2/TS-3). Remaining uncertainty is mainly chemical, not thermodynamic: CsReCl₆ implies a different Re oxidation state than Cs₂ReCl₆ [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2/F3 — Route 1: Acidic Gel Growth

The low-temperature gel route has mild conditions because Cs₂ReCl₆ was grown at 305 K using sodium metasilicate gel, HCl, K₂ReCl₆, and CsCl in ordinary U-tube/test-tube glassware (TS-5). F2 is reduced for multi-step gel preparation and possible use of moisture-sensitive Re(V) chloride [Reasonable Inference], but it avoids high temperature, high pressure, and sealed chlorine. Kinetics are favorable because solution/gel diffusion bypasses solid-state diffusion barriers, and TS-5 shows that Cs–Re–Cl crystals can form by this mechanism. The main uncertainty is whether CsReCl₆ can be selected over the known Cs₂ReCl₆ phase (TS-5).

#### F2/F3 — Route 2: Sealed-Tube Chloride Metathesis

The sealed-tube metathesis route is moderately severe because K₂MoCl₆ analogues require sealed-tube, air-free conditions with ICl promoter and reactive MoCl₅ (SS-2/TS-2). F2 deductions arise from controlled atmosphere, volatile ICl/sealed operation, and toxic/air-sensitive chloride reagents (SS-2/TS-2). F3 is moderate because the reactive chloride/ICl environment enables low-temperature hexachlorometallate formation at 150 °C in the Mo system (SS-2/TS-2), but Re-containing CsReCl₆ has a different stoichiometry than the directly evidenced A₂MCl₆ analogues (TS-5). The largest uncertainty is phase competition with Cs₂ReCl₆ and Re binary chlorides [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2/F3 — Route 3: Direct Chlorination

Direct chlorination is supported by binary high-valent chloride analogues: WCl₆ forms from W + Cl₂ at 800 °C in a sealed tube (SS-4), and OsCl₄ forms from Os + liquid chlorine at 525 °C for 5 days under 6–8 atm (SS-5/TS-4). F2 is penalized because sealed chlorine chemistry involves reactive/volatile precursors and pressure hazards (SS-4, SS-5/TS-4). F3 is relatively low because starting from refractory Re metal likely slows reaction, and ternary Cs–Re–Cl assembly requires more reconstruction than binary WCl₆/OsCl₄ formation [Reasonable Inference]. The route is plausible but less attractive than gel growth or metathesis because exact ternary Cs–Re–Cl control is not directly demonstrated by SS-4/SS-5/TS-4.

#### F4 — Amorphization Tendency

The target contains a large size contrast between Cs and Re coordination environments, as reflected by CsCl₁₂ cuboctahedra and ReCl₆ octahedra in the target and similar alkali/metal chloride frameworks in Cs₂ReCl₆, K₂MoCl₆, and Rb₂PtCl₆ (TS-5, SS-2/TS-2, CS-4/SS-3/TS-3). The target also has mixed corner/edge/face sharing connectivity, which can increase structural frustration and defect trapping; analogous mixed sharing is present in K₂MoCl₆ and Rb₂PtCl₆ (SS-2/TS-2, CS-4/SS-3/TS-3). Unit-cell atom count is not explicitly reported in CS/SS/TS, so that check is treated as insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS and not triggered. The resulting F4 score of 60 reflects medium amorphization/disorder risk.

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

CsReCl₆ is likely close to a ground-state phase because its reported E_hull is slightly negative/effectively zero, and stable Cs–Re–Cl and alkali hexachlorometallate analogues are present in the reference set (TS-5, SS-2/TS-2). The strongest synthesis support comes from Cs₂ReCl₆ gel growth, which directly demonstrates that Cs–Re–Cl octahedral chloride crystals can be grown under mild acidic gel conditions (TS-5). The most critical enabling factor is high chloride activity in acidic or sealed chloride media, which stabilizes isolated ReCl₆-type octahedra as seen in Cs₂ReCl₆ and K₂MoCl₆ analogues (TS-5, SS-2/TS-2). The most critical limiting factor is stoichiometry/oxidation-state selectivity because the known exact-system analogue is Cs₂ReCl₆, whereas the target is CsReCl₆ and likely requires Re(V) rather than Re(IV) chemistry [Reasonable Inference]. Overall, the target is plausibly synthesizable, with the best chance via low-temperature gel or diffusion crystallization rather than direct high-temperature chlorination.
