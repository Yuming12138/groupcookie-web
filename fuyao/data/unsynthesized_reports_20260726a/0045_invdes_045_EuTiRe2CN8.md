### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability**  
The target lacks a reported candidate-specific E_hull, so direct hull-based scoring is impossible and the placeholder 0 eV/atom is not counted. CS and TS contain many E_hull = 0 analogues, which supports general accessibility of related rare-earth and N-rich phases, but SS contains metastable structural analogues up to ~0.20–0.34 eV/atom, lowering confidence. The negative predicted formation energy supports non-obvious feasibility, but without hull or phonons the target is treated as potentially metastable rather than proven ground state.

**Route 1 — F2/F3**  
Route 1 has moderate condition severity because SS/TS show C–N flux/metathesis synthesis at 400–650°C without high pressure, but dry Ar/sealed ampoule handling, toxic/air-sensitive reagents, and multi-step flux work still apply. Kinetics are the best among routes because SS/TS demonstrate active LiCl/KCl or Li2(CN2)-based chemistry, while the Re/Ti diffusion and P1 mixed-cation ordering penalties are [Reasonable Inference]. CS gives insufficient evidence from CS for direct C/N metathesis and is scored neutral 50 for that route-specific chemical transfer.

**Route 2 — F2/F3**  
Route 2 is strongly penalized by high pressure because TS diazenides require 3–12 GPa and SS W2N3 uses 5 GPa. Kinetics benefit from azide N activity shown in TS, but Re/Ti ordering and C incorporation are not directly demonstrated, so those penalties are [Reasonable Inference]. CS gives insufficient evidence from CS for high-pressure nitride/carbonitride synthesis and is scored neutral 50.

**Route 3 — F2/F3**  
Route 3 is supported by CS high-temperature sealed-ampoule solid-state synthesis and TS nitride equilibration under N2, but it likely requires >1000°C, controlled nitrogen activity, and repeated grinding. Kinetics are weak because dense nitrides/metals and refractory Re/Ti create slow diffusion, and SS provides insufficient evidence from SS for direct Eu–Ti–Re bulk carbonitride sintering, scored neutral 50. The route is plausible but less attractive than flux/metathesis.

**Route 4 — F2/F3**  
Route 4 is conditionally supported by SS Nb nitride CVD using metal chloride + NH3/H2 at 950–1000°C. It scores poorly in kinetics because Eu, Ti, and Re vapor delivery plus C/N incorporation require tight composition control, which is [Reasonable Inference] beyond SS. CS and TS provide insufficient evidence from CS/TS for vapor-depositing this multication target and are scored neutral 50.

**Route 5 — F2/F3**  
Route 5 is supported only by CS CVT of rare-earth thiosilicate bromides in evacuated ampoules with a thermal gradient. SS and TS provide insufficient evidence from SS/TS for CVT preserving N-rich carbonitride motifs, scored neutral 50. It is therefore best viewed as secondary crystal growth after Route 1 or 2, not as a primary discovery route.

**F4 — Amorphization Tendency**
