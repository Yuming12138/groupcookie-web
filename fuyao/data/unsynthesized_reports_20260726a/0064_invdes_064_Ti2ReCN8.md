### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

Ti₂ReCN₈ is best treated as a **potentially metastable but chemically plausible** N-rich carbonitride/nitridocarbon framework because no candidate-specific E_hull or phonon validation is reported, despite the supplied negative predicted formation energy. The CS analogues support transition-metal nitride-halide formation involving Re/W/Ta under sealed conditions, while TS analogues strongly support crystalline C–N framework formation by solid-state metathesis. [CS2, CS4, CS5; TS1–TS5] SS analogues support N–N-rich azide/dicyanamide chemistry but are less transferable because they are mostly solution-grown coordination salts. [SS1–SS5] The key enabling factor is using reactive halide + Li₂CN₂/azide metathesis in a salt matrix to lower the effective crystallization temperature. [TS1–TS4; SS1, SS3] The key limiting factor is retaining the high nitrogen content and N–N motifs while ordering refractory Ti/Re cations into a halogen-free P1 phase. [CS4, CS5; SS1, SS3, SS4]
