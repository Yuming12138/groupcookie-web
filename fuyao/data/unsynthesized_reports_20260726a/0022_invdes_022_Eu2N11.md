### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

Eu2N11 is more likely a **metastable isolable phase** than a proven ground-state phase because the target lacks a real E_hull and phonon validation despite having a favorable predicted formation energy. [Target] The CS and SS data support synthesis of nitrogen-rich N–N bonded salts under mild conditions, but several close azide analogues have high reported hull energies, so synthesizability is not equivalent to ground-state stability. [CS1–CS3; SS1–SS5] The TS data are encouraging because thermodynamically similar N-containing materials sit on the hull, but they are mostly cyanide/carbodiimide/nitride chemistries rather than Eu polynitrides. [TS1–TS5; Reasonable Inference] The most critical enabling factor is **low-temperature solution mobility**, which can form N–N bonded crystalline materials without high-temperature nitrogen loss. [CS1–CS3; SS1, SS5] The most critical limiting factor is **phase and redox control of Eu in an explosive azide-rich environment**, because no source directly demonstrates Eu2N11 formation. [Target; CS1–CS3; SS1; insufficient direct Eu2N11 evidence from CS/SS/TS]
