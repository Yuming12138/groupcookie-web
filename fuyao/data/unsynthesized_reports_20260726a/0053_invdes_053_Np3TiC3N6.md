### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability**  
The target lacks a reported convex-hull energy, so the placeholder 0.000000 eV/atom is not used as stability evidence. Thermodynamic analogues are favorable because TS-1 through TS-5 all report E_hull = 0.0, and structural analogues include low-hull or stable actinide carbide/nitride phases such as Np₂C₃ and U₂MnN₃, but SS-2 shows that a structurally similar U carbide can also sit high above hull. **TS-1–TS-5, SS-1, SS-2, SS-4** Remaining uncertainty is high because negative formation energy alone does not prove target convex-hull or phonon stability.

**F2 — Route 1**  
Route 1 is penalized for high temperature, controlled N₂/Ar atmosphere, multi-step firing, and Np-containing air-sensitive/radioactive precursors. These deductions follow 1200–1600 °C nitride synthesis and 1773 K carbide sintering analogues. **SS-4, TS-2, TS-5** Insufficient evidence from CS supports a direct high-T Np–Ti–C–N ceramic route, so CS is neutral for this route beyond general carbide/nitride precedent.

**F3 — Route 1**  
Kinetics are limited because refractory nitride/carbide powders and graphite have low precursor activity, and actinide carbide/nitride analogues require high-T equilibration. **SS-4, TS-5** Structural reconstruction is difficult because none of the SS/TS analogues matches the target’s P1 Np₃Ti(CN₂)₃ topology. **SS-1–SS-4, TS-1–TS-5**

**F2 — Route 2**  
Route 2 is severe because arc melting/melt growth requires very high local temperatures, purified inert gas, multiple remelts, and post-annealing. **SS-1, SS-3, TS-1, CS-5** It avoids high pressure, but nitrogen retention is not demonstrated by the carbide-focused arc-melt analogues. **SS-1, SS-3, TS-1; [Reasonable Inference]**

**F3 — Route 2**  
The melt path improves cation mixing, as shown by successful actinide carbide arc-melt syntheses. **SS-1, SS-3, TS-1** The kinetic score remains moderate-low because the target requires N-rich anion ordering, while the closest melt analogues are mainly carbides and do not show preservation of CN₂-like motifs. **SS-1, SS-3, TS-1; insufficient evidence from CS for melt-preserved CN₂ units**
