### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

B₄C₂N₃ should be treated as a **possibly metastable, not confirmed ground-state** phase because no target E_hull or phonon validation is reported, despite a negative predicted formation energy (Target note). The strongest analogue support is that B–C borocarbides and B–N networks are experimentally accessible under high-T, HPHT, shock, and sealed/inert conditions (**CS-2; CS-3 / TS-5; SS-1; SS-5 / TS-1**). The most important enabling factor is the small covalent network cell with local B–C and B–N distances comparable to related borocarbide/BN motifs [Reasonable Inference from **CS-2; SS-5 / TS-1; SS-1**]. The most important limiting factor is **phase and stoichiometry control**, especially incorporation of both C–C and N–N motifs that are not directly validated by CS/SS/TS; that specific motif evidence is insufficient and neutral **50**. Overall, synthesis is plausible but should be pursued first as a thin-film or HPHT metastable screening target rather than assumed as an equilibrium bulk ceramic [Reasonable Inference from **CS-4; CS-3 / TS-5**].
