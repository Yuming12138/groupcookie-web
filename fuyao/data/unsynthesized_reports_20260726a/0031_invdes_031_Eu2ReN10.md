### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

Eu₂ReN₁₀ is more likely a metastable or conditionally stable nitrogen-rich phase than a proven ground-state material, because the target has negative predicted formation energy but no reported convex-hull or phonon validation (Target).
