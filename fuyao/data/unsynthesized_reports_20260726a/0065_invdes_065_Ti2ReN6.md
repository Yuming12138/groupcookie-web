### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

Ti₂ReN₆ is more likely a **metastable or conditionally stable nitride** than a confidently established ground-state phase because the target lacks a reported E_hull and phonon validation. The analogues support synthesis in the broad sense that transition-metal nitrides form by NH₃ ammonolysis, HPHT routes, CVD, and nitride sintering, but no CS/SS/TS entry directly validates Ti–Re–N with N–N units. (SS1–SS5, TS1–TS5, CS1–CS3) The most critical enabling factor is maintaining high nitrogen chemical potential while enabling Ti/Re mixing, best achieved by reactive deposition or chemically mixed ammonolysis. (SS4, SS5, TS3) The most critical limiting factor is phase control: SS1 and SS5 show impurity/narrow-window behavior, while the target has no hull-based stability confirmation. (SS1, SS5, TS3) Overall, the target is **moderately synthesizable** with a best-score route of reactive thin-film deposition and a bulk backup route of mixed-precursor ammonolysis.

|:--:|:--|:--|:--:|:--|
| 1 | All | No target-specific E_hull or phonon validation | High | Treat as metastability screen; explore kinetic routes and compare products against TiN/Re/ReN competitors. (SS2, SS3, TS1–TS5; [Reasonable Inference]) |
| 2 | All | Competing nitride/metal phases | High | Use calibrated N chemical potential and iterative XRD screening; SS1 and SS5 show impurity/phase-window sensitivity. (SS1, SS5, TS3) |
| 3 | Route 5 | Ti:Re:N composition control in film | High | Calibrate Ti/Re deposition rates, use N-rich plasma, and map substrate temperature/gas ratio. (SS5, TS3; [Reasonable Inference]) |
| 4 | Route 1 / 3 | Slow Ti/Re diffusion and ordering | Medium–High | Use molecularly mixed precursors, nanopowders, intermediate grinding, and longer anneals. (SS1, SS4, TS1) |
| 5 | Route 2 | Chloride retention or volatile halide loss | High | Use glovebox ampoule loading, controlled slow heating, and NH₃/H₂ dechlorination after nitridochloride formation. (CS1, CS2, CS3; [Reasonable Inference]) |
| 6 | Route 4 | High-pressure equipment and phase-window risk | High | Start with 5 GPa/1000–1300°C screens and quench rapidly, following HP nitride analogue practice. (SS2, SS3, TS4) |
| 7 | All | Air/moisture sensitivity of precursors/products | Medium | Handle nitrides/halides under Ar or dry N₂; CS2/CS3 and TS5 indicate moisture-sensitive or inert handling. (CS2, CS3, TS5) |
| 8 | All | Crystal growth difficulty for low-symmetry P1 phase | Medium | Use slow cooling for ampoule routes or post-deposition annealing for films; CS1/CS3 rely on slow cooling/crystal isolation. (CS1, CS3; [Reasonable Inference]) |
