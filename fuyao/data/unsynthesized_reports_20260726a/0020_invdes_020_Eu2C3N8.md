### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

Eu₂C₃N₈ should be treated as **possibly metastable rather than confirmed ground-state**, because the target lacks a reported E_hull and only has a negative predicted formation energy. [Target] The CS and TS analogues strongly support rare-earth cyanamide/carbodiimide chemistry under sealed, moderate-temperature metathesis conditions. [CS1, CS2, TS5, TS4] The SS analogues support accessible C–N coordination frameworks, but they are less directly transferable to Eu₂C₃N₈ because they are dicyanamides rather than rare-earth carbodiimides. [SS1–SS5; Target] The key enabling factor is the proven RECl₃ + Li₂(CN₂) metathesis platform in halide flux. [CS1, CS2, TS5] The key limiting factor is the target’s molecular N₂ clusters, which are not demonstrated in the closest CS/TS analogues and likely require tight nitrogen chemical-potential control. [Target; CS1, CS2, TS5; Reasonable Inference]
