### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability:**  
The target has a negative predicted formation energy, but no candidate-specific convex-hull or phonon result, so it cannot be treated as a confirmed ground-state phase. [Target] TS analogues provide the strongest thermodynamic signal because several B–C-related compounds are reported at or near the hull. [TS-1, TS-3, TS-4, TS-5] SS analogues support accessible metastability in the ~0.09–0.26 eV/atom range, while CS analogues are contradictory because some are moderately metastable and CS-1 is very high in listed E_hull. [SS-1–SS-5, CS-1–CS-5] The 68/100 score reflects favorable formation-energy direction but substantial uncertainty from missing target hull validation. [Target]

**F2/F3 — Route 1:**  
Route 1 has moderate condition severity because CS-5 demonstrates LiCl/KCl flux synthesis at only 400 °C under Ar, but the target likely needs higher condensation temperature and sealed handling. [CS-5, Reasonable Inference] Its kinetics are the best among routes because reactive C–N/cyanoborate precursors reduce diffusion limitations relative to elemental B/C solids. [CS-2, CS-3, CS-5] The main uncertainty is whether molecular CN/NCN motifs can reorganize into the target’s B–C/B–N/C–C/N–N ribbon without metal templating. [Target, CS-2, CS-3, Reasonable Inference]

**F2/F3 — Route 2:**  
Route 2 is conditionally plausible because TS-1 shows CaB₂C₂ formation from elements at 1050 °C in sealed Ta tubes, and TS-4 shows week-long annealing can produce borocarbide crystals. [TS-1, TS-4] The kinetic score is low because elemental B and C are refractory and the target needs complete reconstruction into a low-symmetry 1D ribbon. [TS-1, Target, Reasonable Inference] CS gives insufficient direct evidence for a metal-free B–C–N solid-state route, so that part of the evidence is treated as neutral for this route. [CS]

**F2/F3 — Route 3:**  
Route 3 has relatively mild thermal severity because CS-4 supports reactive sputtering under Ar/N₂ without extreme temperature or pressure. [CS-4] Its kinetics are only moderate because thin-film methods can tune composition but often yield amorphous metastable B–C–N unless crystallization is strongly templated. [CS-4, Target, Reasonable Inference] SS and TS give insufficient evidence for thin-film growth of the target, so they are neutral for this route. [SS, TS]

**F2/F3 — Route 4:**  
Route 4 is strongly penalized by pressure because TS-5 uses 9 GPa and 1300 °C to access MgB₂C₂, and SS-1 uses shock compression for BN. [TS-5, SS-1] HPHT may help crystallization, but it may also favor dense BN-like or borocarbide phases instead of the target’s 1D ribbon.
