### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

The target has a favorable predicted formation energy of −0.771 eV/atom, but the source explicitly does not provide a valid E_hull or phonon validation, so it cannot be treated as a proven ground-state phase. [Target] Chemically, structurally, and thermodynamically similar nitrides are mostly reported at E_hull = 0 or near-zero, including LiSr₂ReN₄, LiBa₂ReN₄, Eu₂SiN₃, La₃Ta₂N₆, and La₃Nb₂N₆. [CS2, CS3, CS4, SS1, SS3, TS1, TS2] The score is therefore moderately favorable but capped at 76 because candidate-specific convex-hull evidence is insufficient from CS/SS/TS. [insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS]

**F2/F3 — Route 1**  
Direct nitridation is supported most strongly by ReN₄ analogues made from metals and N₂ at 850–900 °C, so its condition severity is not extreme in temperature but does require controlled atmosphere, dry handling, and long thermal treatment. [SS1, SS3, TS1, TS2] Kinetics are limiting because Re is refractory and the route lacks a reactive nitrogen flux, even though the ReN₄ analogues prove that Re nitridation can occur under these conditions. [SS1, SS3; Reasonable Inference]

**F2/F3 — Route 2**  
The Li/Li₃N/LiN₃ flux route has more severe handling requirements, but CS evidence shows Eu nitride and rare-earth transition-metal nitrides can form in Li-containing reactive fluxes at 900–1030 °C. [CS4, CS2, CS3, TS4] Its kinetic score is highest because flux and reactive nitrogen sources should improve Re transport, Eu/N incorporation, and crystallization of the N-rich target. [CS4, CS2, CS3, SS1, SS3; Reasonable Inference]

**F2/F3 — Route 3**  
High-temperature Ga/metal flux is supported by rare-earth Ta/Nb nitride flux growth at 1600 °C, but the severity is high due to T >1400 °C and the need for controlled atmosphere and sealed containment. [CS2, CS3] Kinetics are better than direct solid-state synthesis but worse than Li₃N/LiN₃ flux because Ga does not itself provide as high a nitrogen chemical potential. [CS2, CS3; Reasonable Inference]

**F2/F3 — Route 4**  
Topochemical conversion is only weakly supported because CS1 demonstrates topotactic Na insertion into Ta₃N₅, not Eu/Re/N framework conversion. [CS1] SS and TS provide insufficient evidence for a topochemical Eu–Re–N parent, so those source families are neutral 50 for this route and the kinetic score is low. [insufficient evidence from SS/TS]
