### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

#### F1 — Thermodynamic Stability

F1 is moderate-high rather than high because no candidate-specific E_hull or phonon validation is reported for B5N3, and CS/SS/TS therefore provide insufficient direct target stability evidence. (CS, SS, TS) The thermodynamic analogue set is encouraging because the top TS entries are mostly on-hull or near-hull, including MgNB9, CaB2C2, MgB2C2, MgOs3B4, and KB6. (TS) The CS set is mixed, with BN moderately metastable at 0.0947 eV/atom, SiS2 near/on the hull, and Si2N3 much higher at 0.3559 eV/atom, so the score is capped at 68. (CS)

#### F2/F3 — Route 1

Route 1 receives a moderate F2 because it requires ~1600 °C, pressure equipment, controlled cooling, Mg volatility management, and post-treatment, but it does not require >5 GPa in the closest analogue. (SS, TS) Its F3 is the best of the bulk routes because molten Mg and amorphous B improve transport, and MgNB9 provides direct B-rich B/N network precedent. (SS, TS; [Reasonable Inference]) The main uncertainty is whether Mg can be excluded from the final structure rather than stabilizing Mg–B–N competing phases. (SS, TS; [Reasonable Inference])

#### F2/F3 — Route 2

Route 2’s F2 is penalized primarily for 55 GPa shock pressure and multi-step recovery, while temperature cannot be scored directly because the CS/SS shock analogue does not report a conventional furnace temperature. (CS, SS) Its F3 is low because shock compression is rapid and non-equilibrium, making B/N redistribution from BN + B into ordered B5N3 difficult. (CS, SS; [Reasonable Inference]) TS gives insufficient evidence for shock synthesis, so TS is neutral 50 for this route’s condition/kinetic signal. (TS)

#### F2/F3 — Route 3

Route 3 has moderate F2 because 600 °C is relatively mild, but 6 kbar NH3, KNH2, and long autoclave operation are severe handling constraints. (CS) Its F3 is moderate because ammonothermal chemistry can improve transport, but the known analogue supports nitridation/crystallization rather than B-rich B–B network preservation. (CS; [Reasonable Inference]) SS and TS provide insufficient ammonothermal-route evidence and are neutral 50 for this route-specific signal. (SS, TS)

#### F2/F3 — Route 4

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

B5N3 should be treated as a likely metastable or near-metastable target rather than a proven ground-state phase, because its E_hull and phonons are not reported and CS/SS/TS provide only analogue stability. (CS, SS, TS) The analogue support is meaningful: MgNB9 is both structurally and thermodynamically close and demonstrates that B-rich B/N networks can crystallize under HPHT conditions. (SS, TS) The strongest enabling factor is the availability of Mg-assisted high-temperature transport and controlled cooling, which gives a plausible pathway around slow B/BN diffusion. (SS, TS; [Reasonable Inference]) The strongest limiting factor is phase selectivity, especially avoiding BN, elemental B, and Mg–B–N competing phases while targeting a Mg-free B5N3 ribbon. (CS, SS, TS; [Reasonable Inference]) Overall synthesizability is moderate: feasible for a specialized HPHT/flux lab, but not a routine ambient-pressure solid-state target. (CS, SS, TS; [Reasonable Inference])
