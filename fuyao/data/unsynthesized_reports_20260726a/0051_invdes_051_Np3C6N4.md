**F2 — Route 3.** Melt growth is supported by CS but uses high temperature near or above 1500 °C and therefore carries both temperature penalties. 【CS】 Nitrogen buffering/sealing and flux handling are extrapolated because CS/SS/TS do not show N-bearing melt growth, lowering the condition score **[Reasonable Inference]**.

**F3 — Route 1.** Arc melting improves transport and homogenization, as seen across actinide/rare-earth carbide analogues. 【CS】【SS】【TS】 The kinetic penalty remains substantial because the target requires C–N ordering and a low-symmetry P1 framework not directly demonstrated in CS/SS/TS.

**F3 — Route 3.** Melt/self-flux conditions should improve diffusion and crystal growth kinetics, consistent with melt-based CS analogues. 【CS】 Composition control is the main kinetic/processing risk because nitrogen activity and C/N ordering are not demonstrated in CS/SS/TS **[Reasonable Inference]**.

**F4 — Amorphization Tendency.** The rule-based checks give no triggers: the target has one cation element, a small P1 formula count, and corner/edge but not face-sharing coexistence **[Reasonable Inference]**.
