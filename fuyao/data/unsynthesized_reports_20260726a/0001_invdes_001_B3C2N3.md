### SECTION 7 — Synthesis Challenges

| # | Route | Challenge | Severity | Mitigation Strategy |
|:--:|:--:|:--|:--:|:--|
| 1 | All | No target E_hull/phonon validation, so phase may be metastable. [Target] | High | Treat synthesis as metastable screening; prioritize low-diffusion flux route and HPHT backup. [CS-5; TS-5] |
| 2 | All | Competing BN, graphite/B4C, cyano residues, or amorphous BCN are likely. [Target; TS-5; Reasonable Inference] | High | Use stoichiometric control, repeated characterization, and slow cooling/annealing as in borocarbide workflows. [SS-4; TS-1; TS-4] |
| 3 | Route 1 | Slow BN/C/B diffusion and incomplete reconstruction. [SS-1; SS-4; TS-1] | High | Use nanoscale/amorphous precursors, ball milling, regrinding, and 12–48 h high-T holds. [SS-4; TS-1; Reasonable Inference] |
| 4 | Route 2 | Volatile CN/N loss during pyrolysis. [CS-5; Reasonable Inference] | High | Use sealed ampoules, N-rich precursor charge, and controlled ramp/degas steps. [CS-5; Reasonable Inference] |
| 5 | Route 2 | Flux route may yield amorphous BCN rather than single crystals. [CS-5; Target; Reasonable Inference] | Med–High | Slow cool from LiCl/KCl flux and screen flux ratios/hold temperatures. [CS-5] |
| 6 | Route 3 | HPHT phase window may be narrow and impurity-prone. [TS-5] | High | Screen 5–9 GPa and 1300–1600 °C; quench and decompress slowly as in HP MgB2C2. [TS-5] |
| 7 | Route 4 | B:C:N vapor composition control is likely narrow. [CS-1; Reasonable Inference] | High | Calibrate precursor flows and use post-deposition annealing; SS/TS provide insufficient direct guidance, neutral 50. [CS-1; insufficient evidence from SS/TS] |
| 8 | All | Low-symmetry 1D ribbon crystal growth may be difficult. [Target] | Med | Use slow cooling/evaporation/annealing logic from single-crystal cyano and borocarbide analogues. [CS-3; CS-4; TS-4] |
