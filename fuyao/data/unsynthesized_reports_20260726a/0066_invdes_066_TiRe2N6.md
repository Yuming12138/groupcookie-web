### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

TiRe₂N₆ is more likely a metastable or low-lying nitride candidate than a confirmed ground-state phase because no candidate-specific E_hull or phonon validation is reported. (Target data) The strongest support comes from SS/TS nitrides showing that W/Mo/Nb/Ta transition-metal nitride frameworks can be synthesized by NH₃ ammonolysis, CVD, autoclave nitridation, or HPHT routes. (SS1–SS5; TS1–TS5) The most critical enabling factor is high nitrogen chemical potential delivered by flowing NH₃, NaN₃, or HPHT nitrogen sources. (SS1, SS3, TS1, TS3, SS2) The most critical limiting factor is phase control in a mixed Ti/Re, N-rich, low-symmetry structure, because analogous ammonolysis systems report impurities, narrow temperature windows, or pressure-dependent ordering. (SS3, TS3, TS4, TS5) Overall, synthesis is plausible but not routine, with Route 1 as the best first experimental screen. (SS1, SS3, TS3; [Reasonable Inference])
