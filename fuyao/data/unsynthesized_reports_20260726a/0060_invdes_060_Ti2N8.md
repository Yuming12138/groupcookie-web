### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

TiN4 should be treated as a **metastable or pressure-stabilized candidate**, not a confirmed ground-state phase, because its E_hull and phonons are not reported despite a favorable predicted formation energy. [Target] The strongest analogue support comes from TS/SS high-pressure diazenides, which demonstrate stable N–N-containing nitrides under HPHT conditions. [SS-2, SS-3, TS-1, TS-2, TS-3] Chemical analogues support high nitrogen activity and azide-based crystallization, but they also show that N-rich azide materials can be hazardous, phase-sensitive, or high in E_hull. [CS-1, CS-2, SS-4, SS-5] The key enabling factor is maintaining high nitrogen chemical potential during crystallization, best achieved by high-pressure azide decomposition. [SS-2, TS-1, TS-3] The key limiting factor is uncertainty over whether TiN4 can beat competing Ti–N phases and retain the required polynitrogen framework after quench. [Target, Reasonable Inference]
