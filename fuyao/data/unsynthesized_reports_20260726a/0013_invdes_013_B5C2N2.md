### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

B5(CN)2 is best viewed as a plausible metastable or near-hull candidate rather than a proven ground-state phase, because the target has negative predicted formation energy but no reported E_hull or phonon validation. Thermodynamic analogues are encouraging, especially TS entries with E_hull = 0 or near-zero, and structural analogues show related B–C networks with similar bond lengths. The strongest enabling factor is HPHT access to dense B–C/B–N network formation, supported by MgB2C2 and MgNB9 analogues. The main limiting factor is the absence of direct metal-free crystalline B–C–N synthesis evidence in CS/SS/TS, especially for exact B:C:N = 5:2:2 ordering. Overall, synthesis is feasible but challenging, with HPHT bulk conversion the most defensible route and reactive sputtering a plausible secondary route for thin films.
