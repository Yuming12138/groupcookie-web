### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability:** Close analogues are strongly favorable: K₂LiAlH₆ is reported at E_hull = 0.0, Na₂LiAlH₆ at ≈0.004 eV/atom, and several transition-metal hydrides are at E_hull = 0.0 (CS1, CS4, CS2, CS3, CS5; SS1, SS2; TS1, TS2, TS3). The target itself has no reported convex-hull energy, so assigning a 90–100 ground-state score would overstate the evidence (Target entry). The resulting 78/100 treats the phase as analogue-supported but not thermodynamically confirmed [Reasonable Inference].

**F2 — Route 1:** Conditions are moderate in temperature and pressure compared with ceramic synthesis, but they require H₂/D₂, Ar glovebox work, and air-sensitive hydrides (CS4, CS5). The deduction is driven by controlled atmosphere, multiple operations, and air-sensitive precursors rather than extreme temperature or >5 GPa pressure (CS4, CS5). Insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS for a fully ambient-pressure route, so ambient synthesis feasibility is neutral 50 and not used as the preferred score.

**F3 — Route 1:** Mechanochemistry is kinetically favorable because Na₂LiAlD₆ and Na₂Mg₂NiH₆ both form from milled hydride/metal mixtures at ≤315 °C (CS4, CS5). Structural reconstruction is relatively favorable because Na₂LiAlH₆ is the same Fm-3m double-perovskite hydride topology (SS1, TS2). The kinetic uncertainty is Cu/Ga ordering, because no direct Cu–Ga hydride analogue appears in CS/SS/TS [Reasonable Inference].

**F2 — Route 2:** The autoclave route avoids >1000 °C and >5 GPa penalties, but it is operationally severe because K₂LiAlH₆ required up to 700 bar H₂ and strict inert handling (CS1, SS2, TS1). M–Mg–Ni analogues also require 120–140 bar H₂ and multi-day reactions, supporting the controlled-atmosphere and multi-step deductions (CS2, CS3). The F2 rubric does not separately penalize 700 bar unless >5 GPa, so equipment severity is captured mainly in the qualitative difficulty [Reasonable Inference].

**F3 — Route 2:** K₂LiAlH₆ gives strong chemical transferability for K-containing perovskite hydrides, but its narrow pressure window and R-3m distortion lower the kinetic score relative to Route 1 (CS1, SS2, TS1). Transition-metal hydride analogues form under high H₂, but their long 6–12 day schedules imply slow diffusion/order kinetics (CS2, CS3). Cu/Ga incorporation remains an extrapolation beyond the provided analogues [Reasonable Inference].

**F2 — Route 3:** Flux-assisted synthesis still requires H₂/D₂ autoclave conditions, glovebox handling, and multiple steps, as seen for BaMg₂FeH₈ and SrMgH₄ (TS4, TS5). Temperature and pressure remain below the explicit high-temperature and >5 GPa penalties (TS4, TS5). Insufficient evidence from CS for a flux analogue and insufficient evidence from SS for a flux analogue; both are neutral 50 for flux-specific feasibility.

**F3 — Route 3:** Flux can improve cation transport and ordering, as suggested by LiH/LiD-assisted BaMg₂FeH₈ synthesis (TS4). The structural match is weaker because TS4/TS5 are not the same Fm-3m double-perovskite framework, and 6–14 day holds suggest slow kinetics (TS4, TS5).
