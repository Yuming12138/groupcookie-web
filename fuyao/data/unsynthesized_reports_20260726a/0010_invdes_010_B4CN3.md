### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability:**  
The target E_hull is not reported, and the placeholder 0 eV/atom must be ignored, so direct stability evidence is incomplete. (target note) Thermodynamic analogues are encouraging because TS entries are mostly on or near the hull, including MgNB₉, MgB₁₂C₂, CaB₂C₂, MgB₂C₂, and BeB₂C₂. (TS) Structural and chemical analogues include both stable and metastable phases, so F1 is raised above neutral but not into the 90–100 range. (CS, SS, TS)

**F2/F3 — Route 1:**  
The high-pressure route is conditionally severe but avoids the >5 GPa deduction if started exactly at 5 GPa, consistent with the target validation pressure and CS high-pressure analogue range. (CS, SS, TS; target note) Kinetics remain difficult because B, C, and BN are refractory, but pressure-assisted analogues show that dense B–N/B–C frameworks can be accessed when bulk ambient routes are unfavorable. (SS, TS) The main uncertainty is whether the required N–N-containing B₄CN₃ sheet orders before competing BN/B₄C phases form. (SS, TS; [Reasonable Inference])

**F2/F3 — Route 2:**  
Ambient direct synthesis benefits from simple precursors and avoids pressure equipment, consistent with direct BeB₂C₂ and CaB₂C₂ analogues. (SS, TS) Its kinetic score is low because dry B/C/BN diffusion is slow at practical temperatures and the target is reported ambient-dynamically unstable. (SS, TS; target note) This route is therefore a screening route rather than the preferred route. (CS, SS, TS)

**F2/F3 — Route 3:**  
Flux growth is supported by MgB₁₂C₂ metallic-melt synthesis and sealed-ampoule B–C chemistry, which can lower diffusion barriers. (TS) The penalty is phase contamination, because a metal flux may stabilize metal borides/carbides/nitrides instead of metal-free B₄CN₃. (TS; [Reasonable Inference]) CS provides insufficient direct flux evidence for this route and is treated as neutral 50 for that route-specific signal. (CS)

**F2/F3 — Route 4:**  
Reactive sputtering has milder thermal severity and may stabilize metastable thin films, which is attractive for a 2D target. (CS; [Reasonable Inference]) However, SS and TS provide insufficient direct thin-film evidence and are neutral 50, while CS indicates that sputtered nitride phases can require careful gas-pressure and post-processing control. (CS; SS, TS) The limiting kinetic factor is B:C:N composition control and crystallization into the specific Pmm2 sheet rather than amorphous BCN. (CS; [Reasonable Inference])

**F2/F3 — Route 5:**  
Halide copyrolysis/CVD benefits from volatile precursors and low diffusion length, supported by CS boron-halide copyrolysis evidence. (CS) The route is penalized for toxic/corrosive volatile reagents, controlled atmosphere, and product-distribution sensitivity with temperature and reactant ratio. (CS) SS and TS provide insufficient direct evidence for this molecular route and are neutral 50 for route transferability. (SS, TS)

**F4 — Amorphization Tendency:**  
The target lacks a large mixed-cation sublattice and does not appear to exceed the >40-atom trigger, reducing intrinsic amorphization risk. (target note; [Reasonable Inference]) The target and close analogues emphasize corner-sharing tetrahedral motifs rather than mixed edge/face/vertex sharing, which supports a low F4 risk score. (CS, SS) Process-induced amorphization remains possible in thin-film or CVD routes even though the structural trigger count is low. (CS; [Reasonable Inference])

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

B₄CN₃ is more likely a pressure-stabilized or metastable phase than a robust ambient-pressure ground state because E_hull is absent and ambient-pressure dynamical instability is reported. (target note) The strongest support comes from SS/TS analogues where BN, MgB₂C₂, and MgNB₉-type covalent networks require pressure, high temperature, or controlled crystallization. (SS, TS) The most critical enabling factor is **pressure-assisted reconstruction and quenching**, because it directly matches the target’s 5 GPa validation and analogous high-pressure routes. (CS, SS, TS; target note) The most critical limiting factor is **ordering the exact B:C:N/N–N sheet without forming BN, B₄C, graphitic carbon, or amorphous BCN**. (CS, SS, TS; [Reasonable Inference]) Overall, the target is moderately synthesizable under high-pressure screening, but ambient bulk synthesis is significantly less credible. (CS, SS, TS; target note)
