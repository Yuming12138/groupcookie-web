### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability**  
The target lacks a real reported convex-hull energy, so direct ground-state assessment is insufficient from CS, SS, and TS and each direct-source signal is neutral at 50 for candidate-specific hull validation [CS; SS; TS]. TS/SS provide a positive analogue signal because the closest SrN2, CaN2, and BaN2 entries are reported on the hull, but CS is contradictory because some chemically similar entries show high E_hull values [SS; TS; CS]. The score is therefore capped at 60 rather than placed in the ≤0.05 eV/atom 90–100 range [CS; SS; TS; Reasonable Inference].

**F2 — Route 1**  
Route 1 is penalized mainly for >5 GPa high pressure, azide containment, multistep handling, and air-sensitive/toxic precursors [SS; TS]. It avoids >1000 °C operation, which partially offsets the pressure penalty [SS; TS]. Insufficient direct Eu4N9 pressure-window evidence remains from CS/SS/TS, so the pressure choice is an analogue-based inference [CS; SS; TS; Reasonable Inference].

**F3 — Route 1**  
Azide decomposition gives high precursor activity and is directly successful for alkaline-earth diazenide analogues [SS; TS]. Kinetics are helped by a single cation and relatively low synthesis temperature compared with refractory nitride ceramics [Reasonable Inference]. Structural reconstruction is still nontrivial because Eu4N9 is lower symmetry and more N-rich than the Sr/Ca/BaN2 analogues [SS; TS; Reasonable Inference].

**F2 — Route 2**  
Route 2 avoids GPa pressure and high temperature but requires reactive NH3 under very high autoclave pressure, sealed conditions, and reactive Eu/K handling [CS]. SS and TS provide insufficient route-specific ammonothermal evidence and are neutral at 50 for this route [SS; TS]. The condition severity is therefore challenging but less extreme than multianvil azide decomposition [CS; Reasonable Inference].

**F3 — Route 2**  
NH3 chemistry is chemically active and directly supports Eu/Ba nitride formation, but BaN2 crystal growth over months indicates slow mass transport and difficult crystallization [CS]. SS/TS do not improve this kinetic confidence for the ammonothermal route, so they remain neutral for this route-specific claim [SS; TS].

**F2 — Route 3**  
Route 3 benefits from moderate temperature and no external GPa pressure, but it still requires sealed/inert flux handling and reactive Li/LiN3/Li3N precursors [TS]. ### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

Eu4N9 should be treated as a plausible metastable or pressure-stabilized nitrogen-rich phase rather than a confirmed ground-state compound because no candidate-specific convex-hull or phonon validation is reported [CS; SS; TS; Reasonable Inference]. The CS/SS/TS analogue set supports synthesis most strongly through high-pressure diazenide/polynitride chemistry, especially SrN2/CaN2/BaN2 analogues with short N–N units [SS; TS; CS]. The key enabling factor is high nitrogen chemical potential under pressure, best delivered by azide decomposition in a sealed multianvil environment [SS; TS]. The key limiting factor is phase selectivity against simpler Eu nitrides, ammonides, or Li-containing/ternary side phases [CS; TS; Reasonable Inference]. Overall, synthesis is moderately feasible but requires specialized high-pressure or sealed reactive-nitrogen methods [CS; SS; TS].
