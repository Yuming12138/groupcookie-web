### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic stability.**  
The target is predicted exactly on the convex hull with E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom, which justifies a near-maximum thermodynamic score. The exact GaTeI₇ analogue has E_hull = 0.0 and E_above_ground_state = 0.0, and the close AlTeI₇/GaI₃ analogues are also on the hull (CS1, CS2, CS5; SS1, SS2, SS5; TS1, TS2, TS3). Remaining uncertainty is polymorphism: the known GaTeI₇ analogue is Pc with edge-sharing GaI₄–TeI₅ contact, whereas the target is Cm with separated GaI₄ and TeI₃ clusters [Reasonable Inference from target description and CS1/SS1/TS1].

**F2 — Route 1.**  
Route 1 is mild in temperature because the exact analogue forms at only 300–350 °C, with no high pressure (CS1/SS1/TS1). The penalties come from vacuum/Ar sealed ampoule operation, volatile iodine, multistep sealing/heating, and air-sensitive crystals (CS1/SS1/TS1). Insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS exists for exact internal iodine vapor pressure, so that specific pressure risk is treated qualitatively rather than over-penalized.

**F3 — Route 1.**  
Kinetics are favorable because the exact GaTeI₇ compound forms from elemental precursors under iodine-rich ampoule conditions in 48 h (CS1/SS1/TS1). The likely iodine vapor/transport chemistry avoids refractory diffusion [Reasonable Inference supported by CVT conditions in CS1/SS1/TS1]. The main uncertainty is whether the Cm target polymorph rather than Pc GaTeI₇ is obtained.

**F2 — Route 2.**  
Route 2 uses lower temperatures, supported by AlTeI₇-like closed-ampoule gradient crystallization at 150→120 °C and TlGaI₄-type halide synthesis at 200 °C (CS2/SS2/TS2; TS5). It still requires inert/vacuum ampoule handling and air/moisture-sensitive iodides, especially GaI₃ (CS5/SS5/TS3). Insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS exists for a direct GaI₃ + TeI₄ → GaTeI₇ protocol, so the route is an extrapolation.

**F3 — Route 2.**  
Preformed iodides should be highly reactive and structurally preorganized toward GaI₄/Te–I molecular units [Reasonable Inference], consistent with molecular halide analogues crystallizing readily in ampoules (CS2/SS2/TS2; CS5/SS5/TS3). Low-temperature gradients imply transport/crystallization rather than slow bulk diffusion (CS2/SS2/TS2). The uncertainty is possible formation of the known Pc phase or other tellurium iodides.

**F2 — Route 3.**  
Sublimation growth is low-temperature and uses standard ampoule equipment, as shown for GaI₃ crystal growth and GaTeI₇ ampoule synthesis (CS5/SS5/TS3; CS1/SS1/TS1). It is penalized for vacuum operation, volatile iodine/iodides, multistep pre-reaction plus recrystallization, and air-sensitive handling (CS5/SS5/TS3; CS1/SS1/TS1). Insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS establishes congruent sublimation of GaTeI₇, so this is a significant uncertainty.

**F3 — Route 3.**  
Vapor recrystallization should be kinetically favorable for molecular iodides because GaI₃ sublimes/grows under small gradients (CS5/SS5/TS3). However, the exact GaTeI₇ target may decompose or recrystallize as the known Pc polymorph during transport [Reasonable Inference]. Reported twinning and crystal-selection issues for related trihalides lower the structural/crystal-quality score (CS5/SS5/TS3).

**F2 — Route 4.**  
Route 4 benefits from mild 200–260 °C sealed-ampoule conditions used for TlGaI₄ and Hg–Se–I analogues (TS5; TS4). It is less operationally complex than CVT if binary iodides are used directly, but it still requires glovebox/vacuum handling and volatile/air-sensitive iodides (TS5; TS4; CS5/SS5/TS3). Insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS confirms this exact binary-iodide route for GaTeI₇, so the condition score is not raised above moderate.

**F3 — Route 4.**  
Binary iodides are reactive, and analogous halide salts form at low temperatures in sealed ampoules, indicating favorable kinetics (TS5; TS4). GaI₃ already supplies GaI₄-like tetrahedral units, reducing reconstruction requirements (CS5/SS5/TS3). The limiting kinetic issue is ordering of separate GaI₄ and TeI₃ clusters into the Cm polymorph rather than competing molecular iodide phases [Reasonable Inference].

**F4 — Amorphization tendency.**  
The target is zero-dimensional and has a small described cell of two GaI₄ plus two TeI₃ clusters, so network-driven amorphization risk is low (target structure description). One risk trigger is the chemically distinct Ga and Te coordination environments, but CS/SS/TS do not provide explicit ionic radii, so the radius criterion relies on [Reasonable Inference] and neutral source scores for that subcheck. ### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

The target is likely a thermodynamic ground-state or very near-ground-state phase because its reported E_hull is 0.0000 eV/atom, and the exact-composition GaTeI₇ analogue is also on the hull (CS1/SS1/TS1). The analogue support is unusually strong chemically: an experimentally synthesized GaTeI₇ phase exists from Te, Ga, and I₂ in sealed ampoules, and structurally/thermodynamically similar AlTeI₇ and GaI₃ iodide molecular solids are also stable and accessible (CS1, CS2, CS5; SS1, SS2, SS5; TS1, TS2, TS3). The most critical enabling factor is the molecular iodide chemistry: volatile iodine and soft Ga–I/Te–I bonding permit low-temperature transport and crystallization rather than high-temperature diffusion [Reasonable Inference supported by CS1/SS1/TS1]. The most critical limiting factor is polymorph control, because the target Cm structure differs from the known Pc GaTeI₇ analogue in cluster connectivity and Te coordination (target description vs CS1/SS1/TS1). Air/moisture sensitivity and iodine volatility are practical challenges across all routes (CS1/SS1/TS1; CS5/SS5/TS3).
