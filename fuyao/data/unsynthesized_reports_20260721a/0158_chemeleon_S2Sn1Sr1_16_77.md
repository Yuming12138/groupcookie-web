#### F2 — Route 1 condition severity

A controlled sealed or vacuum atmosphere is recommended because multiple related sulfide syntheses use vacuum ampoules or sealed tubes to control sulfur volatility (CS-1, CS-4, SS-5 / TS-3, TS-5), although the BaSnS₂ source itself gives insufficient atmosphere evidence (CS-2 / SS-1 / TS-1). #### F3 — Route 1 reaction kinetics

Binary sulfides are appropriate reactive precursors because the closest BaSnS₂ analogue was made directly from BaS and SnS (CS-2 / SS-1 / TS-1). Kinetic accessibility is supported by moderate-temperature formation at 750 °C, but the two-week duration indicates slow solid-state diffusion/order development (CS-2 / SS-1 / TS-1). Structural reconstruction should be limited because the target and BaSnS₂ share A–Sn–S₂ chemistry and similar Sn–S coordination environments (CS-2 / SS-1 / TS-1). #### F2 — Route 2 condition severity

Elemental sealed-ampoule synthesis is supported by related sulfides made from elements under vacuum with long high-temperature holds (SS-5 / TS-3), and by preheating protocols in sulfide syntheses (CS-1, CS-4). SnS-like local coordination is chemically compatible with the target’s T-shaped SnS₃ environment (SS-4 / TS-2; target description), and BaSnS₂ provides the closest ternary framework reference (CS-2 / SS-1 / TS-1).
