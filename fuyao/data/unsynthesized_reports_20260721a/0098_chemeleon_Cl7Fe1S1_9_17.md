### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

#### F1 — Thermodynamic stability

The target has E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom, which maps to a 90–100 thermodynamic score under the provided rubric. Several thermodynamic analogues are also reported at E_hull = 0.0 eV/atom, including RuS3Cl8, Zr(SeCl6)2, Hf(SeCl6)2, TaTeBr9, and ReTeSeCl4 (TS-1 to TS-5). The main uncertainty is that DFT hull stability for volatile molecular chlorides may not fully capture finite-temperature decomposition or vapor pressure effects [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2 — Route 1 condition severity

Route 1 is low-temperature because CVT analogues crystallize at 140–155 °C in closed ampoules (CS-1, CS-2; SS-1, SS-4, SS-5). The deductions come from vacuum/sealed-ampoule requirements, volatile chlorides, and air-sensitive precursors, all of which are evident in the analogous halide syntheses (CS-1, CS-2; SS-5). There is insufficient direct FeSCl7-specific condition evidence from CS/SS/TS, so the Fe-specific transferability component is neutral and treated as an extrapolation.

#### F3 — Route 1 kinetics

Molecular halide precursors are kinetically favorable because analogous chalcogen/metal chlorides react at only 140–155 °C over days (CS-1, CS-2; TS-2, TS-3). The target is zero-dimensional, and structurally similar zero-dimensional chloride clusters are abundant in SS (SS-1, SS-4, SS-5). The uncertainty is that the target contains separated FeCl4 and SCl3 clusters, while many CVT analogues contain MCl6/SeClx frameworks, so structural transfer is not exact.

#### F2 — Route 2 condition severity

Route 2 uses mild temperature, since RuS3Cl8 was made at 140 °C for 7 days (CS-5; SS-2; TS-1). Deductions are assigned for volatile SCl2 in a sealed ampoule, multiple handling/crystallization steps, and air/moisture-sensitive reagents/products, all reported for the Ru–S–Cl analogue (CS-5; SS-2; TS-1). It avoids the high-temperature and >5 GPa penalties because the analogue uses only an autoclave for containment, not extreme pressure.

#### F3 — Route 2 kinetics

The sulfur-chloride solvent route should have good kinetics because SCl2 is both reagent and reactive medium in the closest S–Cl analogue (CS-5; SS-2; TS-1). Low reaction temperature implies no severe bulk diffusion limitation, and S–Cl bond lengths in the analogue are close to those in the target (CS-5; target description). The main uncertainty is phase selectivity: the Ru–S–Cl route formed multiple ternary products requiring separation (CS-5; SS-2; TS-1).

#### F2 — Route 3 condition severity

Route 3 is least severe because the Sn/P chloride molecular-salt analogue forms by solution mixing at room temperature or reflux under dry conditions (CS-3). Deductions arise from drybox/Schlenk handling and hydrolyzable/toxic chloride precursors, as documented for CS-3 and supported by sulfur-chloride handling issues in CS-5. SS and TS do not provide useful direct evidence for solution crystallization of FeSCl7, so that source-specific route evidence is insufficient from SS and TS and scored neutral for this route.

#### F3 — Route 3 kinetics

Solution assembly should be kinetically favorable because the CS-3 chloride salt forms by mixing molecular chlorides in solution over minutes to hours. The target is molecular and zero-dimensional, so crystallization likely requires ion-pair assembly rather than slow solid-state reconstruction [Reasonable Inference from target structure and CS-3]. The main uncertainty is chemical stability of an SCl3-containing species in solution, which is not directly demonstrated by CS/SS/TS for FeSCl7.

#### F4 — Amorphization tendency

The target has only one small formula unit described as one FeCl4 cluster plus one SCl3 cluster, so the atom-count amorphization check is not triggered. The target also has no reported vertex-, edge-, or face-sharing polyhedral network, unlike some analogues with edge-sharing or face-sharing units (CS-5; TS-4), so mixed-connectivity amorphization risk is low. The only trigger is the Fe/S effective cation size mismatch [Reasonable Inference]; CS/SS/TS provide insufficient direct ionic-radius evidence and are neutral for that specific check.
