Route 1 inherits a high-temperature evacuated-ampoule protocol at 1350 K, i.e., just above 1000 °C, so it receives deductions for T >1000 °C and vacuum atmosphere (CS1; SS1). ### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

The target is predicted as a ground-state phase by its E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom, and TS contains several stable telluride analogues, so the thermodynamic case is strong (TS1–TS5). The synthesis evidence is also favorable because exact-composition CeTlTe2 has been made by evacuated-ampoule solid-state reaction, although in a different tetragonal polymorph (CS1; SS1).
