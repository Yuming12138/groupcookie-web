### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

#### F1 — Thermodynamic stability

The target is directly reported as E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom, which places it in the highest thermodynamic-stability bracket and supports a score of 98. Chemically similar LiNbS₂ and LiCrS₂ are also reported at E_hull = 0, and LiTiS₂ is nearly on hull at ~0.003 eV/atom, supporting the stability of layered LiMS₂ sulfides (CS/SS). TS analogues are stable binary sulfides, but they do not directly address the Li-Zr-S phase diagram; therefore there is insufficient evidence from TS for LiZrS₂-specific competing phases, and TS alone would be neutral at 50 for that sub-question. The remaining uncertainty is whether the calculated ground state corresponds to an experimentally accessible Zr redox state and ordering pattern [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2 — Route 1 severity

Route 1 is moderate because the closest LiMS₂ analogues are synthesized at ~800 °C, not >1000 °C, which avoids the largest thermal penalties (CS/SS). The route still requires evacuated sealed tubes, and Li/S-containing reagents introduce volatility and air-sensitivity concerns, consistent with sealed evacuated silica/quartz synthesis and Li₂S use in LiTiS₂/LiNbS₂-type preparations (CS/SS). SS gives useful support through LiTiS₂, but most other SS entries are rock-salt binary sulfides and give insufficient evidence from SS for ternary LiZrS₂ synthesis; those are treated as neutral 50 where not LiMS₂-relevant. TS provides insufficient evidence for this specific sealed LiMS₂ route and is neutral 50 for Route 1 severity.

#### F3 — Route 1 kinetics

The kinetic score is moderate because A + MX₂ and Li₂S + metal + S routes are experimentally demonstrated for closely related LiMS₂ compounds (CS/SS). The structural reconstruction should be relatively small when starting from ZrS₂-like layered sulfide plus Li source, analogous to alkali intercalation into MX₂ dichalcogenides (CS) [Reasonable Inference]. However, LiTiS₂ required a long 800 °C treatment and post-treatments to improve purity, indicating slow ordering/phase-control issues in some LiMS₂ systems (CS/SS). TS provides insufficient LiMS₂ kinetic information, so TS is neutral 50 for this route’s kinetics.

#### F2 — Route 2 severity

Route 2 benefits from lower temperatures, because the LiCrS₂/LiVS₂ sulfurization analogue uses 500 °C and 700 °C H₂S steps (CS). The major severity penalties are the toxic/reactive H₂S atmosphere and the multi-step sequence with intermediate powdering and slow cooling, both explicitly present in the analogue route (CS). SS and TS provide insufficient evidence for oxide-to-LiZrS₂ sulfurization specifically, so they are neutral 50 for this route’s severity. The uncertainty is whether the lower temperature is sufficient for refractory Zr precursor conversion [Reasonable Inference].

#### F3 — Route 2 kinetics

Kinetics are less favorable than Route 1 because oxide/carbonate precursors require substantial chemical reconstruction into a layered sulfide [Reasonable Inference]. Li₂CO₃/Cr₂O₃ or V₂O₃ conversion in H₂S is demonstrated for related LiMS₂ phases (CS), but ZrO₂ is expected to be more refractory and kinetically sluggish [Reasonable Inference]. The two-step H₂S route and intermediate powdering show that reaction completion requires enhanced gas-solid contact (CS). SS/TS do not provide direct Zr oxide sulfurization evidence and are neutral 50 for this route’s kinetics.

#### F2 — Route 3 severity

Route 3 is more severe because LiCrS₂ transport/growth reaches 800–1100 °C under Ar/CS₂ and slow cooling (CS), and CVT analogues use sealed silica/quartz tubes with I₂ at ~950–1000 °C gradients (SS/TS). CS₂ and I₂ are volatile and hazardous transport reagents, adding handling and containment risk supported by their explicit use in CS/SS/TS. The route does not require high external pressure, because all cited transport analogues use gas flow or sealed ampoules rather than high-pressure apparatus (CS/SS/TS). The uncertainty is whether Li and Zr can be co-transported without off-stoichiometry [Reasonable Inference].

#### F3 — Route 3 kinetics

CVT can improve kinetics by using gas-phase transport rather than only solid-state diffusion, as seen for LiCrS₂ and other sulfide crystal-growth examples (CS/SS/TS). Structural transferability is strongest from LiCrS₂ because it shares the target’s P-3m1 Caswellsilverite-like motif with similar LiS₆/MS₆ connectivity (CS). However, SS/TS transport examples are mostly binary or non-Li sulfides, so they provide insufficient direct evidence for LiZrS₂ co-transport stoichiometry and are partially neutral. The main kinetic uncertainty is whether transport favors LiZrS₂ rather than ZrS₂, Li₂S, or halide/carbon-containing byproducts [Reasonable Inference].

#### F4 — Amorphization tendency

The target is a simple layered LiZrS₂ phase with a small crystallographic description and not a large >40 atom cell, which lowers amorphization risk (target description; CS analogues). It does have mixed corner-, edge-, and face-sharing octahedral connectivity, which triggers one amorphization-risk criterion (target description; CS). Cation radius mismatch is not expected to exceed 15% for octahedral Li/Zr in this framework [Reasonable Inference], but CS/SS/TS provide insufficient direct ionic-radius evidence and are neutral for that specific check. Overall the amorphization tendency is low–medium rather than high.

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

LiZrS₂ is predicted to be a ground-state phase because its reported E_hull is 0.0000 eV/atom. The strongest synthesis support comes from chemically similar layered LiMS₂ sulfides—LiNbS₂, LiTiS₂, LiCrS₂, and LiVS₂—which are reported with closely related Caswellsilverite-like LiS₆/MS₆ frameworks and experimentally demonstrated solid-state or sulfurization routes (CS/SS). The most critical enabling factor is the clear precedent for forming layered LiMS₂ sulfides near 800 °C in evacuated tubes or sulfurizing atmospheres (CS/SS). The most critical limiting factor is Zr redox/phase competition, because common zirconium sulfide chemistry may favor ZrS₂ or other Zr sulfides rather than Li-stabilized Zr-centered LiZrS₂ [Reasonable Inference]. Overall, the material appears realistically synthesizable, with sealed-tube sulfide solid-state synthesis as the best first attempt.
