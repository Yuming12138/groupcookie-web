### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

#### F1 — Thermodynamic stability

The target is predicted exactly on the convex hull, E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom, which places it in the highest thermodynamic stability bin. TS provides multiple hull-stable thermodynamic analogues, including AgGaSe₂, Mn₂SiSe₄, MnCu₂SiSe₄, HgIn₂Se₄, and Na₂In₂GeSe₆, supporting the plausibility of stable multication selenides. CS and SS include MnIn₂Se₄ and MnGa₂Se₄ with low E_hull values, but they do not directly prove Mn₂In₂Se₅ stability; insufficient direct target thermodynamic evidence from CS/SS beyond analogue support.

#### F2 — Route 1: sealed-tube solid-state

The solid-state route uses common sealed-ampoule conditions seen across CS/SS/TS, but vacuum sealing, Se volatility, multiple heat/grind/anneal steps, and Se toxicity impose substantial deductions. No high pressure is supported by CS/SS/TS, and temperatures can likely remain below 1000 °C based on TS and CS solid-state analogues. The main uncertainty is whether Mn₂In₂Se₅ equilibrates cleanly or instead forms MnIn₂Se₄ plus MnSe/In₂Se₃ secondary phases [Reasonable Inference].

#### F3 — Route 1: sealed-tube solid-state

Kinetics are moderate because elemental Mn is less reactive than molecular or solution precursors, while In/Se can form low-melting intermediates that may improve contact [Reasonable Inference]. TS and SS analogues frequently require long anneals or multi-step processing, indicating slow cation ordering and diffusion. Structural reconstruction is not extreme because the target and analogues share Mn–Se/In–Se chalcogenide coordination motifs, but the mixed MnSe₄/MnSe₅/InSe₄ framework likely requires equilibration [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2 — Route 2: melt-and-anneal

The melt route is penalized for T >1000 °C because MnGa₂Se₄ used 1150 °C melting (CS, SS). Vacuum sealing, volatile Se, multi-step melt/anneal/cool processing, and toxic Se handling further reduce the score. TS and SS show that long anneals may be needed for equilibrium, making the route feasible but operationally more severe than CVT.

#### F3 — Route 2: melt-and-anneal

Kinetics benefit from partial or complete melting, which improves mixing and diffusion compared with dry solid-state reaction [Reasonable Inference]. However, structural ordering after solidification can be slow, as AgGaSe₂-type melt-and-anneal samples required long annealing for equilibrium (SS, TS). The remaining uncertainty is whether the P1 target orders during cooling or requires a narrow annealing window [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2 — Route 3: CVT

CVT avoids >1000 °C conditions because MnIn₂Se₄ crystals were grown at 840→820 °C (CS, SS). The route still requires high vacuum, volatile/corrosive transport agents, sealed ampoules, and a multi-step feed/transport process, giving moderate severity. TS does not provide a direct CVT analogue among the listed thermodynamic matches, so TS is neutral for CVT-specific condition severity.

#### F3 — Route 3: CVT

The closest analogue, MnIn₂Se₄, was successfully grown as large single crystals by AlCl₃ CVT (CS, SS), strongly supporting vapor availability and crystallization. CrGaSe₃ iodine CVT and iodine-assisted MnCu₂SiSe₄ synthesis further support halogen-assisted chalcogenide transport/reactivity (CS, TS). Composition control is the limiting kinetic/process factor because Mn₂In₂Se₅ has a different Mn:In:Se ratio than MnIn₂Se₄ and transport equilibria may fractionate Mn/In/Se species [Reasonable Inference].

#### F4 — amorphization tendency

CS/SS/TS do not directly report amorphization for Mn₂In₂Se₅, so there is insufficient evidence from CS, SS, and TS for this factor and they are treated as neutral. The target has mixed coordination and low symmetry P1, which can increase configurational complexity [Reasonable Inference]. However, the listed structure is relatively small by formula count and lacks face-sharing polyhedral connectivity, so the amorphization risk is low-to-medium rather than high.

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

Mn₂In₂Se₅ is likely a ground-state or near-ground-state phase because the target E_hull is 0.0000 eV/atom, and TS contains several hull-stable multication selenide analogues. The strongest support comes from MnIn₂Se₄, which is both chemically and structurally close and has been experimentally grown by AlCl₃ CVT (CS, SS). The most critical enabling factor is the demonstrated compatibility of Mn–In–Se chemistry with sealed-ampoule/CVT processing at accessible temperatures (CS, SS). The most critical limiting factor is phase and stoichiometry control, especially avoiding MnIn₂Se₄, MnSe, In₂Se₃, or Se-loss-derived impurity phases [Reasonable Inference from CS/SS/TS]. Overall, synthesis is plausible, with CVT recommended for crystalline material and sealed-tube solid-state reaction recommended for initial phase discovery.
