**F2 — Route 1.**

The severity deductions come from vacuum/dry-atmosphere handling, volatile sulfur in sealed tubes, multi-step annealing, and air/moisture-sensitive rubidium sulfide chemistry (CS1, SS1, TS1). However, the reported synthesis required long annealing up to 400 h, implying slow crystallization or ordering (CS1, SS1, TS1). Polymorph conversion from corner-sharing to edge-sharing BS₄ connectivity adds uncertainty (CS1, SS1, TS1; target).
