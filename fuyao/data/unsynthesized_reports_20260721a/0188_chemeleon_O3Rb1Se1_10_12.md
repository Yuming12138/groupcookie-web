The target is likely a ground-state or near-ground-state phase because its reported E_hull is 0.0000 eV/atom, and many Rb/K/Cs selenium oxo-salt analogues are also on or very near the hull (TS-1, TS-2, TS-3, TS-5). The strongest synthesis support comes from the exact RbSeO₃ chemical/structural analogue grown by cooling crystallization and from low-temperature Rb₂Se₂O₇ solid-state synthesis (CS-1, SS-1, CS-2, TS-1). The most important enabling factor is the high mobility/reactivity of aqueous or acid-mediated Rb–Se–O species, which allows crystallization at room temperature to ~300 °C rather than requiring refractory diffusion (CS-1, CS-2, CS-5, TS-1). The main limiting factor is phase/redox selectivity among RbSeO₃, Rb₂SeO₄, and Rb₂Se₂O₇, because stable related phases with different Se coordination are documented (CS-2, TS-1, TS-3). Overall, synthesis is favorable, with solution crystallization as the preferred first attempt.
