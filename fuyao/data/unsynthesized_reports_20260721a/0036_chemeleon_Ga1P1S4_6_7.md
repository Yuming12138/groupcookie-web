### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

#### F1 — Thermodynamic Stability

The target is calculated at E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom, which directly supports a high thermodynamic score under the rubric (target data). Exact-composition GaPS4 mp-30979 is also E_hull = 0.0 and was experimentally grown by CVT, supporting chemical feasibility (CS, TS). Structurally related AlPS4 1D phases are E_hull = 0.0 or near-zero, supporting the stability of SiS2-derived MPS4 ribbon/sulfide frameworks (SS, TS). Remaining uncertainty is polymorphic: the hull calculation favors the submitted C2 structure, but the experimentally known GaPS4 analogue in the reference set is a different 2D P2₁/c polymorph (CS, SS).

#### F2 — Route 1 CVT

CVT conditions are moderate in temperature, 600–650 °C, and no high pressure is reported (CS, SS, TS). The main severity penalties are the sealed volatile transport environment and iodine/phosphorus/sulfur handling risks [Reasonable Inference], with iodine transport directly reported (CS, SS, TS). Atmosphere and exact duration are not specified for the GaPS4 reference, so those aspects have insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS and were not over-penalized. The resulting F2 = 80 reflects manageable but nontrivial sealed-tube CVT practice.

#### F3 — Route 1 CVT

Kinetically, the exact GaPS4 composition is transportable and crystallizable by iodine CVT, which is strong evidence for precursor vapor availability (CS, SS, TS). The crystallization-pathway score is reduced because the observed GaPS4 phase is 2D P2₁/c, whereas the target is 1D C2, so CVT may not select the target topology without optimization (SS; [Reasonable Inference]). Composition control is uncertain because volatile P and S can shift stoichiometry in a gradient [Reasonable Inference], and CS/SS/TS provide insufficient evidence for tolerance windows. This gives a good but not excellent F3 = 72.

#### F2 — Route 2 High-pressure

The high-pressure route uses 1023 K / 750 °C, which is not especially high by the rubric, but it requires 5 GPa belt-press synthesis in the analogue evidence (CS, SS, TS). Strictly, the high-pressure deduction is defined as >5 GPa, so the 5 GPa condition does not trigger that specific deduction, although it remains experimentally challenging (CS, SS, TS). The route is penalized for multiple operations such as ball milling, precompaction, and pressure-temperature treatment, which are directly reported for AlPS4 analogues (CS, SS, TS). Toxic/volatile P/S handling adds another penalty [Reasonable Inference], giving F2 = 80 under the strict deduction scheme.

#### F3 — Route 2 High-pressure

The strongest kinetic advantage is structural: the AlPS4 references are 1D SiS2-derived ribbon phases very close to the target topology (SS, TS). High-pressure synthesis can promote dense or specific polymorphic arrangements and suppress volatile loss inside a capsule [Reasonable Inference], while the reported 10 h dwell suggests the 1D structure can form on practical timescales (CS, SS, TS). Precursor activity is only moderate because elemental mixtures require ball milling/precompaction in the references (CS, SS, TS). Overall F3 = 80 because this route best targets the desired polymorph.

#### F2 — Route 3 Sealed-tube Solid-state

Sealed-tube solid-state analogues use accessible temperatures, including 500–600 °C for KInP2S7-related synthesis and 623 K for Cu thiophosphate synthesis, with no external high pressure (CS, SS, TS). The route is penalized for vacuum-sealed tubes, volatile P/S/P2S5 chemistry, multiple handling/heating steps, and moisture-sensitive or toxic precursors (CS, SS, TS; [Reasonable Inference]). Some analogues require long dwell times or controlled heating/cooling, indicating moderate operational burden (CS, SS, TS). Thus F2 = 55, lower than CVT or high pressure despite simpler equipment.

#### F3 — Route 3 Sealed-tube Solid-state

P2S5-based thiophosphate chemistry is well precedented in sealed tubes, supporting moderate-to-good precursor activity (CS, SS, TS). Diffusion is acceptable because analogous thiophosphates form at 500–750 °C rather than requiring refractory temperatures (CS, SS, TS). The structural-reconstruction score is reduced because binary sulfides/P2S5 must assemble the GaPS4 framework and analogues report impurities or non-homogeneity in some cases (CS, SS, TS). F3 = 70 reflects plausible kinetics but significant phase-selection uncertainty.

#### F4 — Amorphization Tendency

The target has chemically dissimilar cations Ga3+ and P5+ in tetrahedral sulfide environments, which triggers the ionic-radius/size-mismatch check [Reasonable Inference]. The target description does not provide unit-cell atom count, so there is insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS to trigger the >40 atom criterion; this source contribution is neutral 50. The target description also does not state simultaneous vertex/edge/face-sharing coexistence, so there is insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS to trigger mixed-connectivity amorphization. ### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

The target GaPS4 C2 ribbon phase is likely a thermodynamic ground-state or near-ground-state phase because its computed E_hull is 0.0000 eV/atom (target data), and multiple related thiophosphate phases are also hull-zero or near-hull (TS). The analogue support is strong chemically because exact GaPS4 has been synthesized by iodine CVT (CS, SS, TS), but structurally mixed because the exact GaPS4 reference is 2D while the target is 1D (SS). The most critical enabling factor is the robust GaS4/PS4 tetrahedral thiophosphate chemistry, shown by matching Ga–S and P–S bond lengths in exact GaPS4 and related MPS4 frameworks (CS, SS). The most critical limiting factor is polymorph control: ambient CVT may favor the known 2D GaPS4 polymorph, while the target 1D topology is better supported by high-pressure AlPS4 analogues (SS, TS). Overall, synthesis is feasible, with the high-pressure route being the best strategy for selectively accessing the target 1D C2 structure.
