### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

#### F1 — Thermodynamic stability

The target is predicted on the convex hull with E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom from the supplied target data, so it receives a high F1 score. Multiple close analogues are also calculated or reported at E_hull = 0.0, including Rb₂MoS₄ (CS1; SS4; TS4), K₂MoS₄ (CS2), Rb₂VAgS₄ (SS1/TS1), and Rb₂LiVS₄ (SS2/TS2). The remaining uncertainty is that CS/SS/TS provide no direct Cr-containing Rb₂CrS₄ experimental precedent, so the Cr-specific phase competition evidence is insufficient from CS/SS/TS and treated as neutral = 50.

#### F2 — Route 1 solvothermal

The solvothermal route is mild in temperature because Rb₂MoS₄ forms at 393 K for 7 d (CS1) and K₂MoS₄ forms at 443 K for 2 d (CS2). It still incurs deductions for sealed volatile solvent/sulfur conditions, multi-step handling, and likely toxic/air-sensitive sulfide/thiochromate chemistry [Reasonable Inference from CS1/CS2]. There is no evidence for high pressure >5 GPa or >1000 °C conditions from CS1/CS2, so those penalties do not apply.

#### F3 — Route 1 solvothermal

Kinetics are favorable because solvothermal media provide high precursor mobility and chemical activation, as demonstrated by low-temperature formation of Rb₂MoS₄ and K₂MoS₄ (CS1/CS2). The target’s tetrahedral CrS₄ motif matches the tetrahedral MoS₄ motif in Rb₂MoS₄/K₂MoS₄ by structural analogy [Reasonable Inference based on CS1/CS2 and target description]. The key uncertainty is whether a chromium thiometallate precursor can remain stable and convert cleanly to Rb₂CrS₄; CS/SS/TS give insufficient Cr-specific evidence, neutral = 50.

#### F2 — Route 2 polysulfide flux

Flux growth analogues use moderate temperatures of 350 °C to 773 K and sealed evacuated ampoules with slow cooling (SS1/SS2; TS1/TS2). The severity score is reduced by vacuum/inert handling, sealed sulfur/polysulfide volatility, multi-step flux preparation/workup, and air-sensitive alkali sulfides (SS1/SS2/SS3; TS1/TS2/TS3). No analogue requires high pressure or >1000 °C conditions (SS1/SS2; TS1/TS2).

#### F3 — Route 2 polysulfide flux

Reactive polysulfide fluxes improve mass transport and enable formation of structurally related Rb–transition-metal sulfides at relatively low temperatures (SS1/SS2; TS1/TS2). However, using Cr metal or Cr sulfide is less directly supported than V/Nb/Ta analogues, and some related flux series show phase-window sensitivity and failed members (SS2/SS3; TS2/TS3). Therefore the kinetics are moderate rather than high.

#### F2 — Route 3 direct sealed-tube solid-state

The direct solid-state route is supported by Rb₂GeS₃ synthesis in evacuated sealed silica at 1073 K for 12 h (CS4) and Rb₂CoS₂ high-temperature synthesis at 1030 K (SS5). It is penalized for vacuum/sealed-tube requirements, sulfur volatility, multi-step precursor preparation such as Rb₂S preparation, and air-sensitive sulfide handling (CS4; SS5). It avoids extreme temperature >1000 °C in the proposed 650–800 °C window [Reasonable Inference based on CS4].

#### F3 — Route 3 direct sealed-tube solid-state

Solid-state kinetics are least favorable because Cr metal is refractory and dense [Reasonable Inference], and diffusion through chromium sulfides may be slow. CS4 shows that Rb₂S + element + S can produce an alkali sulfide framework, but the Ge analogue does not establish Cr(VI) thiometallate formation. The route therefore has substantial uncertainty and a higher risk of forming binary/ternary lower-valent chromium sulfides [Reasonable Inference; insufficient direct CS/SS/TS evidence = neutral 50].

#### F4 — Amorphization tendency

The target has a large cation size/coordination contrast between 8-coordinate Rb and tetrahedral Cr, so C1 is triggered [Reasonable Inference from target structure]. The supplied target description does not indicate a unit cell atom count >40, nor does it report mixed edge/face/vertex-sharing polyhedral connectivity; evidence from CS/SS/TS is insufficient for these checks and treated as neutral = 50. With one trigger, amorphization/structural-disorder risk is low-to-medium rather than severe.

Overall, the material appears synthetically plausible, with solvothermal thiometallate metathesis as the best first attempt.
