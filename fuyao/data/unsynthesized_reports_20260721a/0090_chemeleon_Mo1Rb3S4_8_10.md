### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

#### F1 — Thermodynamic stability

The target has E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom, which places it in the 90–100 range and supports a ground-state prediction. The closest chemical, structural, and thermodynamic analogues Rb₂MoS₄ and Cs₂MoS₄ are also reported at E_hull = 0.0 eV/atom, reinforcing that alkali molybdenum sulfides with tetrahedral MoS₄ units are stable families (CS; SS; TS). Remaining uncertainty is that the target has Rb₃MoS₄ stoichiometry rather than the demonstrated A₂MoS₄ stoichiometry, so phase competition with Rb₂MoS₄ or Rb₂S-rich phases remains plausible [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2 — Route 1 solvothermal severity

Route 1 is mild in temperature because the direct Rb₂MoS₄ analogue forms at 393 K for 7 d, and no high-temperature or GPa-pressure conditions are indicated (CS; SS; TS). The deductions come from use of a sealed solvothermal system and potentially toxic/moisture-sensitive sulfide/amine chemistry [Reasonable Inference]. Insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS indicates whether inert loading is strictly required, so that aspect is treated conservatively rather than as a formal controlled-atmosphere deduction.

#### F3 — Route 1 solvothermal kinetics

The kinetics are favorable because `(NH4)2MoS4` supplies preformed MoS₄ tetrahedra and the closest analogue Rb₂MoS₄ crystallizes at low temperature in solution (CS; SS; TS). Liquid-phase transport largely avoids refractory Mo solid-state diffusion [Reasonable Inference]. The main uncertainty is structural/compositional reconstruction from Rb₂MoS₄-type chemistry to Rb₃MoS₄, which lowers the structural-reconstruction score [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2 — Route 2 hydrothermal severity

Route 2 is also mild because Cs₂MoS₄ is synthesized at 423 K for 24 h in a sealed quartz ampoule, with no high-temperature or GPa conditions (CS; SS; TS). Deductions are assigned for sealed hydrothermal pressure and sulfide/polysulfide precursor handling [Reasonable Inference]. Insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS confirms the exact Rb hydrothermal recipe, so the score is not higher than the demonstrated Cs analogue.

#### F3 — Route 2 hydrothermal kinetics

Hydrothermal/polysulfide media provide good transport and enable Cs₂MoS₄ single crystals at low temperature, indicating favorable kinetics for related alkali molybdenum sulfides (CS; SS; TS). Mo powder may dissolve/react more slowly than preformed thiomolybdate, but polysulfide chemistry can activate Mo–S formation [Reasonable Inference]. The uncertainty is whether Rb₃MoS₄, rather than Rb₂MoS₄ or Rb sulfides, nucleates under aqueous conditions [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2 — Route 3 solid-state severity

Route 3 requires evacuated sealed ampoules analogous to Rb₂GeS₃ and Rb₂S syntheses, so vacuum/sealing and air-sensitive Rb sulfide handling reduce the F2 score (CS; SS). The proposed temperature range remains below 1000 °C, so no high-temperature penalty is applied (CS). The route is less attractive because sulfur volatility and Rb₂S air sensitivity complicate safe handling [Reasonable Inference].

#### F3 — Route 3 solid-state kinetics

Solid-state kinetics are relatively poor because Mo powder or MoS₂ is refractory, and no direct CS/SS/TS evidence shows Rb₃MoS₄ forming by simple solid-state reaction. Rb₂S and S are chemically active, but long-range diffusion and full structure assembly are still required [Reasonable Inference]. Insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS supports direct solid-state Rb–Mo–S phase formation at high yield, so the F3 score is kept low.

#### F2 — Route 4 flux severity

Flux growth is moderate in temperature because the Rb₂LiVS₄ flux analogue is synthesized at 773 K, but it requires Ar glovebox loading, vacuum sealing, and post-reaction washing (TS). Deductions are assigned for controlled atmosphere/vacuum, sealed sulfur-containing flux, multistep processing, and air-sensitive alkali sulfide flux [Reasonable Inference; TS]. Insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS demonstrates a direct Rb₃MoS₄ flux synthesis, so severity is not reduced further by assumed simplicity.

#### F3 — Route 4 flux kinetics

Flux chemistry should improve kinetics by providing mobile alkali sulfide/polysulfide species, and Rb₂LiVS₄ crystallizes from such a flux at 773 K with slow cooling (TS). This supports good diffusion/transport even for transition-metal sulfides [Reasonable Inference]. The uncertainty is structural selectivity, because Rb₃MoS₄ has not been shown in CS/SS/TS to crystallize from molten sulfide flux.

#### F4 — Amorphization tendency

The target has a large cation size/coordination contrast between Rb and Mo, which triggers one amorphization-risk criterion [Reasonable Inference]. The target structure contains distorted edge- and corner-sharing sulfur-centered polyhedra, but no face-sharing connectivity is reported, so the mixed-connectivity criterion is not fully triggered. Insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS gives the full unit-cell atom count, so C2 is treated as neutral and not triggered.

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

Rb₃MoS₄ is likely a thermodynamic ground-state candidate because its computed E_hull is 0.0000 eV/atom, and multiple related alkali molybdenum sulfides are also on the hull (CS; SS; TS). The analogue support is strong for the MoS₄ tetrahedral chemistry and Rb/Cs/K alkali sulfide environments, especially from Rb₂MoS₄ and Cs₂MoS₄ (CS; SS; TS). The most important enabling factor is that low-temperature solution-mediated routes already crystallize closely related A₂MoS₄ phases, avoiding refractory Mo diffusion (CS; SS; TS). The main limiting factor is phase control: the closest Rb₂MoS₄ solvothermal route produced only a minor amount of product with Ag₂S as the major phase, and the target requires Rb-rich stoichiometry relative to A₂MoS₄ (CS; SS; TS; [Reasonable Inference]). Overall, synthesis is plausible, with the best chance coming from Rb-rich solvothermal or hydrothermal thiomolybdate chemistry.
