### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

#### F1 — Thermodynamic Stability

The target is reported at E_hull = 0.0 eV/atom, which places it in the highest thermodynamic-stability scoring bracket. Multiple high-similarity analogues are also calculated on or essentially on the hull: Cs3BS3, Cs4In2S5, Cs2MoS4, Cs2AgAsS4, Rb2GeS3, and Cs2AgSbS4 (CS1–CS3, SS1–SS5, TS1–TS5). This strongly supports a ground-state or near-ground-state phase assignment, although computational hull stability does not guarantee kinetic accessibility [Reasonable Inference]. There is insufficient destabilizing evidence from CS/SS/TS.

#### F2/F3 — Route 1: Sealed-Tube Solid-State Sulfidation

Condition severity is moderate because analogous Cs3BS3 requires 720 °C, Ar atmosphere, carbon-coated sealed silica tubes, and slow cooling (CS1), while Rb2GeS3 requires evacuated sealed silica tubes and ~800 °C treatment (SS3, TS3). The main penalties are sealed/vacuum handling, volatile sulfur, and air-sensitive alkali sulfides, all reflected in the analogue syntheses (CS1, SS3, TS3). Kinetically, the route benefits from reactive sulfide precursors and no refractory cations, but bulk diffusion and possible regrinding remain concerns [Reasonable Inference]. The structural motif is plausible because AsS4 tetrahedra are present in Cs2AgAsS4 and target As–S distances match closely (SS1, TS1).

#### F2/F3 — Route 2: Supercritical Ammonia / Ammonothermal Route

Condition severity is high even at low temperature because Cs2AgAsS4 synthesis uses supercritical NH3, fused silica tubes, vacuum sealing, drybox handling, and an autoclave counter-pressured to 3200 psi Ar (SS1, TS1). Kinetically, this is the strongest route because the closest As-containing analogue crystallizes directly at 135–160 °C over 3–6 days (SS1, TS1). The same AsS4 tetrahedral unit with As–S = 2.17–2.22 Å is present in Cs2AgAsS4, matching the target’s As–S = 2.17–2.23 Å (SS1, TS1). The key uncertainty is that removing Ag changes charge balance/topology and may redirect crystallization to other Cs–As–S phases [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2/F3 — Route 3: Alkali Azide Route

The azide route has moderate-to-high severity because Cs4In2S5 synthesis requires CsN3 preparation/handling, decomposition at 270–350 °C, sealed ampoules, inert/vacuum conditions, and long anneals up to 15 days (CS2, SS2, TS2). It offers good kinetic activity because azide decomposition supplies reactive alkali metal species without using elemental Cs (CS2, SS2, TS2). However, no provided azide-route analogue contains arsenic, so AsS4 formation under these conditions is inferred rather than demonstrated [Reasonable Inference; insufficient direct As evidence from CS2/SS2/TS2]. Phase control is likely moderate because long anneals and possible rehomogenization were required in the analogue route (CS2, SS2, TS2).

#### F2/F3 — Route 4: Low-Temperature Hydrothermal / Solvothermal Route

The hydrothermal route has the mildest nominal conditions because Cs2MoS4 single crystals formed at 423 K for 24 h in a sealed quartz ampoule (CS3). Kinetics are favorable for solution-mediated crystallization, but precursor speciation is uncertain because CS3 supports MoS4 chemistry, not AsS4 chemistry. Using sulfide/polysulfide mineralizers to dissolve As2S3 is therefore a [Reasonable Inference], not directly proven by CS3. The route receives an expert penalty because the closest As-containing evidence comes from supercritical NH3 routes, not mild hydrothermal water-based conditions (SS1, TS1).

#### F4 — Amorphization Tendency

The target has a large contrast between very large Cs+ coordination environments and small tetrahedral AsS4 units, which triggers the cation-size mismatch check [Reasonable Inference supported by analogous alkali thiometallate structural motifs in CS1, SS1, TS1]. The described cell contains only eight crystallographic atoms, so the >40 atom-count check is not triggered by the target description. The target description reports corner-sharing SCs5As octahedra but not simultaneous vertex/edge/face sharing coexistence, so the mixed-connectivity check is not triggered. Uncertainty remains because P1 symmetry can sometimes reflect subtle ordering, but the low atom count and simple AsS4 unit reduce amorphization risk [Reasonable Inference].

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

Cs3AsS4 is likely a ground-state or very near-ground-state phase because the target E_hull is 0.0 eV/atom and many high-similarity Cs/Rb thiometallate analogues are also on the hull (CS1–CS3, SS1–SS5, TS1–TS5). The best direct structural support comes from Cs2AgAsS4, which contains Cs, As, S and nearly identical AsS4 tetrahedra, though it requires supercritical NH3 synthesis and includes Ag absent from the target (SS1, TS1). The most critical enabling factor is the strong chemical plausibility of isolated AsS4^3− tetrahedra charge-balanced by three Cs+ ions [Reasonable Inference supported by AsS4 in SS1/TS1 and Cs-rich thiometallates in CS1]. The most critical limiting factor is phase selection: related supercritical NH3 syntheses produced multiple Cs–Ag–M–S phases and mixed products, indicating that small changes in stoichiometry can redirect crystallization (SS1, TS1, SS5, TS5). Overall, synthesis is feasible but will likely require strict Cs:S:As stoichiometry control, sulfur chemical potential control, and air-free handling.
