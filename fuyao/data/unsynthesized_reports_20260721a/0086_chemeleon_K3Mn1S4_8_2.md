### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

#### F1 — Thermodynamic stability

The target has E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom, so it receives the maximum thermodynamic score under the required rubric. CS gives strong support because CS1, CS2, CS3, and CS5 are all hull-stable K chalcogenides, and CS4 is essentially stable at 0.00125 eV/atom above hull. TS also gives strong support because TS1–TS5 are all hull-stable; SS is somewhat less supportive because SS1–SS5 lie 0.020–0.051 eV/atom above hull, but those values are still within common synthesizable metastability windows. The remaining uncertainty is that thermodynamic stability does not guarantee redox accessibility of Mn in K₃MnS₄ [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2/F3 — Route 1

Route 1 is penalized in F2 because CS1 uses evacuated sealed ampoules at high temperature and requires controlled slow cooling, and K₂S handling is nontrivial (CS1, CS3). Kinetically, K₂S is a reactive sulfide precursor supported by CS1/CS3, but Mn powder may react slowly and require long annealing [Reasonable Inference]. The tetrahedral MS₄ motif is supported by CS3 and SS1, but no exact K₃MnS₄ structural analogue is provided, so structural reconstruction remains uncertain.

#### F2/F3 — Route 2

Route 2 is supported by elemental K–M–chalcogen ampoule syntheses for K₃GaS₃ and K₃AlSe₃ (CS2, CS5). F2 is limited by evacuated ampoule requirements and reactive/volatile K and S [Reasonable Inference]. F3 is moderate because elemental synthesis can work for K chalcogenides (CS2/CS5), but Mn metal is less kinetically favorable than molten/low-melting components and requires complete reconstruction [Reasonable Inference]. The largest uncertainty is safe, homogeneous pre-reaction of K and S without off-stoichiometry [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2/F3 — Route 3

Route 3 benefits from many analogues made under chalcogen-loaded H₂ streams: K/Rb/Cs cobalt chalcogenides in SS1–SS5 and alkali pnictogen selenides in TS2/TS3/TS5. F2 is mainly reduced by the reactive H₂/chalcogen atmosphere, while no high-pressure or >1000 °C penalty applies. F3 is relatively favorable because gas-phase sulfur transport and in situ alkali sulfide formation accelerate reaction, as implied by 9–10 h syntheses in SS1–SS5 and TS2/TS5. The uncertainty is that H₂ may over-reduce Mn relative to the high formal Mn oxidation state required in K₃MnS₄ [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2/F3 — Route 4

Route 4 has excellent kinetic potential because CS3 forms crystalline K₂MoS₄ at only 443 K in sealed methanol, demonstrating high activity of solvothermal K₂S chemistry. F2 is reduced by sealed-tube solvent pressure, air-sensitive K₂S, and multi-step loading/sealing operations (CS3). The main uncertainty is chemical: Mo(VI) readily forms thiomolybdate, while high-valent Mn sulfide speciation may not be stable under the same solvothermal conditions [Reasonable Inference]. Therefore F3 is high, but the route is chemically riskier than its kinetic score alone suggests.

#### F2/F3 — Route 5

Route 5 receives the best F2 because TS4 shows low-temperature sealed Ar annealing of alkali sulfide + metal chalcogenide mixtures at 360–380 °C without H₂, vacuum, or high pressure. F3 is moderate-to-good because preformed sulfides reduce reconstruction and are directly supported by TS4, but 7–10 d annealing indicates slow ordering/diffusion. K₂S use is further supported by CS1/CS3.
