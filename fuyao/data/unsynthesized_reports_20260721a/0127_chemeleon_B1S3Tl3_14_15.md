### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

#### F1 — Thermodynamic stability

The target is calculated at E_hull = 0.0 eV/atom, which is strong evidence for ground-state thermodynamic stability. The closest chemical/structural/thermodynamic analogue Tl3BS3 is also reported with E_hull = 0.0 eV/atom (CS1, SS1, TS1), and related Tl–B–Se, Tl–Si–S, and Tl–Ge–S compounds are also hull-stable (CS2–CS4, SS2–SS3, TS2–TS4). The main uncertainty is polymorphism: the exact-composition analogue is 0D while the target is 2D, so the computed 2D phase may require specific annealing conditions [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2 — Route 1

Route 1 is penalized for vacuum sealing, volatile sulfur in a sealed ampoule, multiple thermal steps, and Tl toxicity, all consistent with the exact Tl3BS3 synthesis conditions in CS1/SS1/TS1. It is not penalized for very high temperature or high pressure because the reported route uses 800 °C and evacuated tubes rather than >1000 °C or GPa pressure (CS1, SS1, TS1). Remaining uncertainty is whether the 2D polymorph can be isolated rather than the known 0D polymorph [Reasonable Inference].

#### F3 — Route 1

The exact analogue proves that Tl2S+B+S can form Tl3BS3 at 800 °C, supporting moderate kinetics (CS1, SS1, TS1). B is refractory and likely limits solid-state reaction rate [Reasonable Inference], but sulfur excess and sealed-tube conditions likely enhance transport or liquid/vapor-assisted reaction [Reasonable Inference] based on CS1/SS1/TS1. Structural kinetics are favorable because formula and space group match, but dimensionality differs, implying polymorph reconstruction/selection uncertainty [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2 — Route 2

Route 2 uses evacuated ampoule conditions analogous to Tl4SiS4 synthesis from elements (CS2, SS3, TS3), so deductions apply for controlled atmosphere, volatile components, multistep heat treatment, and Tl toxicity. The route avoids high pressure and likely avoids >1000 °C because both the elemental analogue and exact Tl3BS3 route operate below that threshold (CS1, CS2, SS3, TS1, TS3). The uncertainty is greater than Route 1 because no elemental Tl–B–S synthesis is directly reported in the provided data.

#### F3 — Route 2

Direct elemental synthesis is kinetically less favorable because B must be converted into B–S units from a refractory solid [Reasonable Inference], whereas CS2/SS3/TS3 only confirm this approach for Tl–Si–S. The very long hold time reported for Tl4SiS4, 83 days, indicates possible slow ordering or diffusion in related Tl main-group sulfides (CS2, SS3, TS3). This route is plausible but less efficient and less controlled than the exact Tl2S+B+S route.

#### F2 — Route 3

The flux route is based on Tl sulfide framework growth in molten sulfide/polythiophosphate flux at 550 °C under vacuum with slow cooling (TS5). It is penalized for vacuum sealing, volatile sulfur-containing species, multiple operations, and toxic/air-sensitive sulfide handling [Reasonable Inference] supported by glovebox/vacuum details in TS5. It is not penalized for >1000 °C or high pressure because the analogue flux route operates at 550 °C and sealed-tube pressure (TS5).

#### F3 — Route 3

Flux growth should improve kinetics by providing a molten medium and enhanced ion mobility [Reasonable Inference], and TS5 confirms such a pathway for Tl sulfide frameworks. However, TS5 contains P/Au chemistry, not B, so there is insufficient direct evidence from TS5 that B dissolves or incorporates efficiently into Tl3BS3. The route may be especially useful for crystal growth or polymorph selection [Reasonable Inference], but composition control is less certain than in Route 1.

#### F4 — Amorphization tendency

The target combines very large Tl cations with small trigonal-planar B centers, giving one amorphization-risk trigger for cation size mismatch [Reasonable Inference] supported by the structural motif descriptions in the target and CS1/SS1/TS1. The provided data do not show a unit cell atom count >40, and CS/SS/TS do not establish mixed vertex/edge/face-sharing connectivity, so those checks are not triggered. The remaining uncertainty is that the target is 2D, and layered sulfides can sometimes form glassy or poorly ordered products if cooled too fast [Reasonable Inference].

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

Tl3BS3 is likely a thermodynamic ground-state or near-ground-state phase because the target has E_hull = 0.0 eV/atom and the closest Tl3BS3 analogue is also hull-stable (CS1, SS1, TS1). The evidence is unusually strong chemically because an exact-composition Tl3BS3 synthesis is reported, but structurally it is not perfectly identical because the reported analogue is 0D whereas the target is a 2D P2_1/m sheet polymorph (SS1 vs target). The most critical enabling factor is the demonstrated sealed-tube Tl2S+B+S route with sulfur/B excess and long annealing, which directly accesses the Tl–B–S composition (CS1, SS1, TS1). The main limiting factor is polymorph/phase control: the target’s 2D sheet may compete with the known 0D cluster form, requiring careful thermal history [Reasonable Inference]. Secondary limitations are Tl toxicity, sulfur volatility, and boron chalcogenide/container reactivity, all addressed by evacuated sealed tubes and carbon/BN liners (CS1, SS1, TS1; CS4, SS2, TS2).
