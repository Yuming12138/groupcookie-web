### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

The remaining uncertainty is that computed hull stability does not guarantee kinetic accessibility or absence of polymorphic competition [Reasonable Inference].

**F2 — Route 1.**  
Route 1 avoids >1000 °C, high pressure, and exotic reactive gases because close analogues are synthesized at 783–975 K under Ar in sealed crucibles/ampoules (SS #2, SS #5; TS #2, TS #5). The main severity penalty is air/moisture sensitivity of alkali sulfides, consistent with hygroscopic analogue behavior and inert sealed handling (CS #1, CS #2; SS #2, SS #3). Insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS exists for direct Cs₂S volatility under the exact proposed conditions, so no separate volatility deduction was applied for Route 1.

**F3 — Route 1.**  
Binary sulfides are reasonably reactive solid-state precursors, and Li₂S + Rb₂S/K₂S successfully produce Matlockite RbLiS/KLiS (SS #2, SS #5; TS #2, TS #5). The target is structurally very close to RbLiS/KLiS and CsNaS, lowering reconstruction difficulty (CS #1; SS #1, SS #2, SS #5). Long analogue anneals of 6 days to 3 weeks indicate ordering/diffusion may still be slow (SS #2, SS #5).

**F2 — Route 2.**  
Route 2 introduces elemental Cs/Li/S, which increases safety and sealing demands relative to preformed sulfides [Reasonable Inference]. The approach is partially supported by RbLiSe, where binary alkali selenides were prepared from elements before the ternary reaction (CS #3). The final anneal remains moderate in temperature by analogy to RbLiS/KLiS/CsNaS (CS #1; SS #2, SS #5).

**F3 — Route 2.**  
Elemental precursors are highly reactive and should form binary sulfides readily [Reasonable Inference], but the route adds a precursor-formation step before Matlockite ordering. The final structural target is strongly supported by isostructural analogues (CS #1; SS #2, SS #5; TS #1, TS #2, TS #5). Uncertainty remains in controlling exothermic sulfide formation and sulfur/Cs volatility [Reasonable Inference].

**F2 — Route 3.**  
The self-flux route uses similar sealed inert conditions and temperatures to solid-state analogues (SS #2–#5; TS #2–#5). It receives added severity penalties because excess Cs₂S/Cs-containing flux may create volatility and sealed-tube containment risks [Reasonable Inference]. **F3 — Route 3.**  
Flux growth should improve mass transport and crystal growth relative to a dry powder route [Reasonable Inference]. **F4 — Amorphization tendency.**
