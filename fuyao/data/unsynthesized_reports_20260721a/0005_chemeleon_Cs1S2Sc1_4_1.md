### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

#### F1 — Thermodynamic stability

The target has E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom from the provided target data, so it falls in the highest stability bin. Chemically similar RbLnS₂ analogues are all reported at E_hull = 0.0 eV/atom (CS1–CS5), and structurally/thermodynamically similar CsLnS₂ analogues are on-hull or within ~0.003 eV/atom of the hull (SS1, SS3–SS5, TS1–TS3). Insufficient direct Sc-specific experimental thermodynamic evidence is provided by CS/SS/TS, but the consistency of the ABX₂ sulfide family supports a high F1 score.

#### F2 — Route 1

Route 1 uses the same 1000–1025 °C H₂S solid-state sulfidation conditions reported for RbLnS₂/CsLnS₂ analogues (CS1–CS5, SS1–SS5, TS1–TS5). The main deductions are for reactive/toxic H₂S and atmosphere switching/washing steps, which are explicitly described for RbLaS₂ and CsLnS₂ protocols (CS2, SS4, TS3). No high pressure or ultra-high temperature is required according to the analogue data (CS1–CS5, SS1–SS5, TS1–TS5).

#### F3 — Route 1

The route benefits from strong structural precedent: Caswellsilverite R-3m analogues with the same A⁺M³⁺S₂ framework are reported for RbLnS₂ and CsLnS₂ (CS1–CS5, SS1, SS3–SS5, TS2, TS4–TS5). Kinetic uncertainty comes from replacing Ln₂O₃ with Sc₂O₃, because Sc₂O₃ is refractory and Sc³⁺ is smaller than the lanthanides [Reasonable Inference]. The use of large alkali carbonate excess and H₂S in analogues suggests enhanced mass transport and sulfidation activity (CS1, CS3–CS5, SS2–SS3, TS3–TS4).

#### F2 — Route 2

Route 2 avoids flowing H₂S but introduces sealed-ampoule sulfur vapor and air-sensitive sulfide precursors [Reasonable Inference]. Closed-system chalcogenide synthesis and evacuated quartz ampoules are reported for related compounds (SS1–SS3, SS5, TS1–TS3), but direct sealed sulfide synthesis of CsScS₂ is not reported; therefore the evidence is indirect. Severity is scored lower than Route 1 because volatile sulfur pressure, precursor sensitivity, and multistep loading/annealing add risk [Reasonable Inference].

#### F3 — Route 2

Using Cs/Sc sulfides should reduce the oxide-to-sulfide conversion barrier [Reasonable Inference], giving slightly better intrinsic reaction kinetics than Sc₂O₃ sulfidation. However, SS/TS data show that related chalcogenides can require closed systems, long heating, or multiple treatments, and phase control may be difficult because CsLnS₂ can exhibit multiple modifications (SS1–SS5, TS1–TS3). Insufficient direct evidence from CS/SS/TS exists for Cs₂S + Sc₂S₃ → CsScS₂ kinetics, so the score remains moderate.

#### F4 — Amorphization tendency

The target has a large A-site/B-site cation size contrast between Cs⁺ and Sc³⁺ [Reasonable Inference], triggering one amorphization-risk check. The structure is otherwise simple and crystalline Caswellsilverite R-3m, and analogues with large alkali/lanthanide size contrast crystallize successfully (SS1, SS3–SS5). The target/analogue octahedra are edge/corner-sharing rather than mixed edge/corner/face-sharing in the target Caswellsilverite description, reducing amorphization risk relative to more topologically complex structures (CS1–CS5, SS1, SS3–SS5).

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

CsScS₂ is likely a thermodynamic ground-state or very strongly stable phase because the target E_hull is 0.0000 eV/atom and many chemically/structurally similar A⁺M³⁺S₂ sulfides are on-hull or near-hull (CS1–CS5, SS1, SS3–SS5, TS1–TS5). The strongest support comes from CsLnS₂ Caswellsilverite analogues synthesized by alkali carbonate plus rare-earth oxide sulfidation in H₂S at 1000 °C (SS1, SS3–SS5, TS2–TS3). The most critical enabling factor is the close structural match to known CsLnS₂/RbLnS₂ layered sulfides with edge-sharing MS₆ octahedra (CS1–CS5, SS1, SS3–SS5). The most critical limiting factor is phase control, because SS/TS sources report narrow temperature windows and multiple CsLnS₂ modifications (SS1–SS5, TS1–TS3). A secondary uncertainty is the replacement of lanthanides by smaller Sc³⁺, for which the provided CS/SS/TS data give insufficient direct experimental precedent.
