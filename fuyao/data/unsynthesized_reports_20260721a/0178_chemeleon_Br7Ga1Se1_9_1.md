Insufficient evidence from **CS/SS/TS** exists for a directly demonstrated Ga + Se + Br₂ route to GaSeBr₇, so this route is less preferred.

**F2/F3 — Route 3.**  
Route 3 has the best formal score because solution crystallization lowers temperature and avoids sealed-tube pressure, while exact GaSeBr₇ is listed under a solution mixing method (**CS-1; SS-2**).
