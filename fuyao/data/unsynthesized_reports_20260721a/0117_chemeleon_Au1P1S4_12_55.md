### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

#### F1 — Thermodynamic Stability

The target has E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom, which places it in the highest thermodynamic-stability scoring bin. The closest analogue mp-30938 is also on the hull and has the same AuPS4 composition and closely matching 1D ribbon structure (CS1, SS1, TS1). The main uncertainty is computational/structural degeneracy: the target may be a symmetry-equivalent or slightly distorted variant of known AuPS4 rather than a distinct new polymorph [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2 — Route 1

Route 1 uses moderate temperature, 600 °C, and a 24 h dwell, which is not severe by solid-state standards (CS1, SS1, TS1). The key deductions are for evacuated ampoule operation and volatile P/S retention, because the reported synthesis explicitly uses an evacuated quartz ampoule (CS1, SS1, TS1). CS2–CS3 provide insufficient evidence for this sulfide route because they are chloride precipitation systems, so they are treated as neutral signals.

#### F3 — Route 1

The strongest kinetic evidence is that the exact AuPS4 analogue forms directly from the elements in only 24 h at 600 °C (CS1, SS1, TS1). Elemental Au is relatively inert and dense [Reasonable Inference], but sulfur/phosphorus volatility or melt/vapor transport likely improves contact and reaction kinetics [Reasonable Inference consistent with ampoule use in CS1/SS1/TS1]. Structural reconstruction is minimal because the target and reference share square-planar AuS4 and tetrahedral PS4 ribbon motifs (CS1, SS1, TS1).

#### F2 — Route 2

Flux growth is supported by related Pd thiophosphate structures synthesized at 480–650 °C in sealed tubes with controlled heating/cooling and flux removal (SS2–SS3, TS2–TS3). Severity is higher than Route 1 because flux methods involve vacuum sealing, moisture-sensitive sulfide/thiophosphate reagents, multiple processing steps, and post-reaction flux removal (SS2–SS3, TS2–TS3). The uncertainty is whether alkali flux components can be excluded from the final AuPS4 lattice [Reasonable Inference].

#### F3 — Route 2

Flux should improve kinetics by dissolving/reacting metal, phosphorus, and sulfur species in a molten sulfide medium, as demonstrated for structurally related square-planar Pd thiophosphates (SS2–SS3, TS2–TS3). The Pd analogues contain PdS4 square planes and PS4 tetrahedra with bond lengths close to the AuPS4 target, supporting structural compatibility (SS2–SS3, TS2–TS3). However, phase control is less certain because several flux analogues require ratio optimization or form minor/disordered phases (SS2–SS3, TS2–TS3).

#### F2 — Route 3

Reactive sulfide/thiophosphate ampoule annealing is less directly supported than Route 1 but has precedent from Hg2P2S7, which was made from HgS, red P, and S in an evacuated ampoule (CS5, SS4, TS4). Its severity comes from sealed-tube operation, volatile S/P species, and possible need to presynthesize Au sulfide [Reasonable Inference]. The AuPS4 target itself is known from elemental ampoule synthesis, so Route 3 is an alternative rather than the primary route (CS1, SS1, TS1).

#### F3 — Route 3

Using Au sulfide or P2S5 could increase precursor activity relative to elemental Au/P/S [Reasonable Inference], and metal-sulfide-based thiophosphate formation is demonstrated for Hg2P2S7 (CS5, SS4, TS4). The kinetic uncertainty is that Au sulfide chemistry is not directly evidenced in the provided references, so competing Au metal, Au sulfide, or phosphorus sulfide phases may intervene [Reasonable Inference]. Structural reconstruction is plausible because the final AuPS4 motif is already demonstrated as stable and synthesizable (CS1, SS1, TS1).

#### F4 — Amorphization Tendency

The closest AuPS4 analogue has a relatively simple 1D ribbon structure with square-planar AuS4 units and tetrahedral PS4 units linked through two-coordinate sulfur, which does not suggest high topological frustration (CS1, SS1, TS1). One amorphization trigger is assigned for the strong Au/P cation-size and coordination mismatch [Reasonable Inference], but no evidence indicates a >40 atom cell or mixed edge/face/vertex-sharing complexity for the target-like structure (CS1, SS1, TS1). Remaining uncertainty is low because the exact analogue crystallizes under standard ampoule cooling (CS1, SS1, TS1).

The target is very likely a ground-state or near-ground-state phase because its reported E_hull is 0.0 eV/atom and the closest AuPS4 reference is also on-hull (CS1, SS1, TS1). The CS/SS/TS evidence is unusually strong: the top hit in all three categories is effectively the same composition and structural motif, and it has a direct reported synthesis from Au, P, and S (CS1, SS1, TS1).
