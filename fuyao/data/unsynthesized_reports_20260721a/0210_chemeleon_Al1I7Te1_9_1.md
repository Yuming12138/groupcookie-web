### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

#### F1 — Thermodynamic Stability

The target has E_hull = 0.0 eV/atom, so it falls in the highest stability scoring range. The closest AlTeI7 analogue is also E_hull = 0.0 eV/atom and is present in chemical, structural, and thermodynamic nearest-neighbor lists (CS1, SS1, TS1). GaTeI7 and GaI3 analogues also sit on the hull, reinforcing that low-dimensional group-13 iodide cluster chemistry is thermodynamically plausible (CS2, CS3, SS2, SS5, TS2, TS3). The remaining uncertainty is polymorph-level rather than composition-level stability [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2 — Route 1

Route 1 is mild because the closest AlTeI7 analogue is synthesized at only 150 °C with a 150 → 120 °C gradient and no high pressure (CS1, SS1, TS1). The main penalties are sealed volatile iodide chemistry and moisture-sensitive handling, both indicated by the ampoule/I2/AlI3 route and moisture-sensitive crystal description (CS1, SS1, TS1). Insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS indicates any need for high pressure or >1000 °C processing, so those factors are scored as non-applicable. The uncertainty is whether the same route yields the target P1 polymorph or the known Pc packing [Reasonable Inference].

#### F3 — Route 1

The kinetic score is high because the exact-composition analogue crystallizes directly in a temperature gradient, implying that vapor transport and cluster assembly are accessible at low temperature (CS1, SS1, TS1). The zero-dimensional cluster nature reduces long-range diffusion requirements compared with dense extended solids [Reasonable Inference from target and CS1/SS1 descriptions]. Composition control is not perfect because AlI3 and I2 volatility can shift stoichiometry, although the analogue reports quantitative/pure crystals (CS1, SS1, TS1). The remaining uncertainty is polymorphic packing control [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2 — Route 2

Route 2 avoids high temperature and high pressure because GaTeI7/InTeI7 analogues form at 300–350 °C in sealed ampoules (CS2, SS2, TS2). It is penalized more strongly than Route 1 because vacuum sealing, liquid-N2 cooling/sealing, volatile I2, and air-sensitive products are reported (CS2, SS2, TS2). The slow temperature ramp is explicitly stated to be essential for high-quality single crystals (CS2, SS2, TS2). Insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS indicates any benefit from open-atmosphere or ambient-pressure unsealed synthesis, so sealed conditions are assumed necessary.

#### F3 — Route 2

The kinetic pathway is plausible because Te, I2, and group-13 metal elements form [TeI3][MI4]-type crystals in sealed ampoules (CS2, SS2, TS2). However, substitution of Al for Ga/In may change metal iodination kinetics and volatility through AlI3 formation [Reasonable Inference]. The analogue’s requirement for slow ramping suggests crystallization is kinetically sensitive rather than completely robust (CS2, SS2, TS2). The composition window is likely narrower because iodine stoichiometry and vapor pressure affect cluster formation [Reasonable Inference from CS2/SS2/TS2].

#### F2 — Route 3

Route 3 is mild in temperature because related mixed-halide cluster compounds are made at 150–200 °C or 573 K in sealed ampoules (TS4, CS4). It is penalized for vacuum/inert handling, volatile iodide species, and air-sensitive halides, which are common to the referenced sealed-ampoule iodide syntheses (TS4, CS4). Insufficient evidence from SS directly supports this exact preformed-halide route for AlTeI7, so SS is neutral for this route. The route is less proven for Al–Te–I than Route 1, despite favorable conditions [Reasonable Inference].

#### F3 — Route 3

Preformed iodide salts should be more reactive than refractory oxide/metal powders, and related TlMX4 iodides form by direct halide mixing in sealed ampoules (TS4). Low-temperature halide mobility, sublimation, or partial melt behavior can assist transport and ordering [Reasonable Inference from TS4/CS4]. AlI4-like tetrahedral units are present in AlI3 and related group-13 iodide clusters, supporting partial structural inheritance (CS5, SS5, TS3). The key uncertainty is whether TeI4/AlI3 reaction produces the desired mixed Al–Te cluster rather than separate binary iodides [Reasonable Inference].

#### F4 — Amorphization Tendency

Only one amorphization check is triggered: Al and Te have strongly different coordination environments and effective ionic sizes in iodide clusters [Reasonable Inference], consistent with AlI4 tetrahedra and TeI5/TeI4 distorted units seen in the analogues (CS1, CS2, SS1, SS2, TS1, TS2). The target has only 9 atoms per formula/cluster and is zero-dimensional, so it does not meet the >40 atom criterion based on the provided target structure. The target also does not show simultaneous vertex-, edge-, and face-sharing network connectivity; the closest analogue has edge-sharing AlI4/TeI5 units but not the full mixed-connectivity criterion (CS1, SS1, TS1). The remaining uncertainty is whether molecular packing disorder could produce glassy or poorly crystalline products during rapid cooling [Reasonable Inference].

The target is likely thermodynamically stable or very near a ground-state phase because its calculated E_hull is 0.0 eV/atom, and the closest AlTeI7 analogue is also on the hull (CS1, SS1, TS1).
