### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

#### F1 — Thermodynamic stability

The target’s provided E_hull is 0.0000 eV/atom, so it receives the maximum thermodynamic score. Chemically close LiScS₂ is also reported on the hull (CS1), and structurally/thermodynamically similar ScS, YS, TmS, DyS, and LaS examples are on or near their ground states in SS/TS lists (SS1–SS4, TS1–TS5). The uncertainty is that hull stability does not guarantee kinetic accessibility or phase purity, as LiTiS₂ shows near-hull stability but difficult phase-pure synthesis (CS2).

#### F2/F3 — Route 1: Stoichiometric binary-sulfide solid-state synthesis

The condition severity is high because the closest LiScS₂ synthesis uses 1150 °C, H₂S, controlled cooling, washing, and annealing (CS1). The route avoids high pressure, consistent with CS/SS/TS analogues, but Li₂S/H₂S handling and possible sealed conditions justify deductions [Reasonable Inference; CS1, CS2]. Kinetically, binary sulfides are plausible and structurally matched to the target’s octahedral sulfide framework, but Sc-rich ordering and possible ScS nonstoichiometry add uncertainty (SS1/TS1).

#### F2/F3 — Route 2: Alkali-sulfide flux / Li₂S-rich mineralizer synthesis

This is the best-supported route because LiScS₂ was prepared with a large excess of Li₂S or Na₂S, high temperature, H₂S, slow cooling, washing, and annealing (CS1). The flux should improve cation mobility and phase formation relative to a purely stoichiometric dry mixture [Reasonable Inference], giving a higher F3 than Route 1. The main uncertainty is that target LiSc₃S₄ is more Sc-rich than LiScS₂, so the Li₂S flux must promote Li incorporation without driving formation of LiScS₂ or residual ScS/Sc₂S₃ [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2/F3 — Route 3: Chemical vapor transport

CVT is credible for Sc-containing sulfides because ScS and ZnSc₂S₄ analogues are grown by I₂/HCl transport near 950–1000 °C (SS1/TS1, CS3). Its F2 is somewhat better than Routes 1/2 because the operating temperature can be ≤1000 °C, but sealed halogen transport and multistep handling remain demanding (SS1/TS1, CS3, SS5). The key uncertainty is Li stoichiometry during vapor transport; CS/SS/TS provide insufficient direct evidence for retaining LiSc₃S₄ composition during CVT, so this route is best treated as recrystallization after powder synthesis rather than primary synthesis.

#### F2/F3 — Route 4: Oxide/carbonate sulfidation

LiCrS₂ demonstrates that alkali transition-metal sulfides can be made from Li₂CO₃ and metal oxide under Ar/CS₂ at 800–1100 °C (CS4). However, Sc₂O₃ sulfidation is not directly supported in CS/SS/TS, and rare-earth/Sc sulfides often require stringent high-temperature or transport conditions (SS1/TS1, SS2/TS2). Therefore, this route has the lowest kinetic score because complete oxide-to-sulfide conversion and target cation ordering are both challenging [Reasonable Inference].

#### F4 — Amorphization tendency

The target is built from LiS₆ and ScS₆ octahedra with corner/edge sharing, matching the robust octahedral sulfide motif seen in LiScS₂ and ScS analogues (CS1, SS1/TS1). No face-sharing coexistence is reported in the target, and LiScS₂ similarly shows corner/edge-sharing octahedral connectivity (CS1). The remaining uncertainty is exact cell-size complexity because CS/SS/TS do not directly provide the target’s conventional-cell atom count; this is treated as insufficient evidence and not a strong amorphization penalty.
