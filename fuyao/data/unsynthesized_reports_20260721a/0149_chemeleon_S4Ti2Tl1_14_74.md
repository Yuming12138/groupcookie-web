### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

Ti₂TlS₄ is likely a thermodynamic ground-state or very near-ground-state phase because the supplied target E_hull is 0.0 eV/atom. The synthesis case is supported by chemically similar Tl–transition-metal sulfide formation in sealed ampoules (CS-1), structurally similar TiS₆ sulfide networks synthesized by evacuated-tube or flux methods (SS-1, SS-2), and thermodynamically similar stable Ti sulfides accessible by flux or solid-state methods (TS-1, TS-2). The most critical enabling factor is the strong precedent for TiS₆ sulfide frameworks forming under sealed-tube/flux conditions at 750–950 °C (SS-1, SS-2, TS-1). The most critical limiting factor is Tl volatility/toxicity and possible off-stoichiometry during heating [Reasonable Inference], with direct Tl–Ti–S precedent absent from CS/SS/TS. Overall, a halide-flux sealed-tube route is the best first attempt because it balances lower temperature, improved transport, and crystallization control.
