### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic stability.**  
The target has E_hull = 0.0 eV/atom, which directly supports a maximum thermodynamic score. The closest analogue mp-28868 is also E_hull = 0.0 eV/atom and is chemically, structurally, and thermodynamically the top match (CS1, SS1, TS1). Remaining uncertainty is mainly computational/experimental: a 0 K hull point does not guarantee easy kinetic access [Reasonable Inference].

**F2 — Route 1 direct solid-state.**  
The route is moderate severity because the closest analogue uses 700–900 °C and 1–4 weeks, with no high pressure or exotic apparatus (CS1, SS1, TS1). The main deductions come from the recommended sealed/vacuum sulfide ampoule and volatile sulfur control [Reasonable Inference]; the original close analogue does not specify atmosphere, so atmosphere evidence is insufficient from CS1/SS1/TS1 and treated neutrally before applying practical synthesis judgment.

**F3 — Route 1 direct solid-state.**  
The kinetics are moderate because TX₂ + M reactions are known to form target-like phases, but they require week-scale annealing (CS1, SS1, TS1). Nb-containing solids have slow diffusion compared with molecular or solution precursors [Reasonable Inference]. The structural match is excellent, which offsets diffusion difficulty (CS1, SS1, TS1).

**F2 — Route 2 CVT.**  
CVT analogues use evacuated quartz ampoules, halogen transport agents, and week-scale gradients, which increases severity relative to direct sintering (CS5, SS5, TS5). Temperatures remain below 1000 °C in the cited examples, so no >1000 °C penalty is applied (CS5, SS5, TS5). Toxic/corrosive halogens or Pb chloride are a practical hazard [Reasonable Inference].

**F3 — Route 2 CVT.**  
Vapor transport improves mass transport and can yield crystalline sulfides, as shown for SnTaS₂ and NbS₂-related materials (CS5, SS5, TS5). However, no direct Bi–Nb–S CVT synthesis is provided, so composition control is uncertain [Reasonable Inference]. Bi halide volatility could either help transport or destabilize stoichiometry [Reasonable Inference].

**F2 — Route 3 intercalation-style.**  
The route inherits manageable temperatures from TX₂ + M and intercalation analogues, generally 700–1000 °C (CS1, CS4, SS3, TS3). It requires sealed evacuated tubes, but no high pressure or toxic halogen if implemented without CVT (CS4). Sulfur/Bi volatility remains a practical sealed-tube concern [Reasonable Inference].

**F3 — Route 3 intercalation-style.**  
This route scores best kinetically because NbS₂ provides a preassembled Nb–S framework, reducing the need for full reconstruction [Reasonable Inference]. The transferability is supported by structurally similar edge-sharing NbS₆ motifs and post-transition-metal sulfide analogues (CS1, SS1, SS3, SS5, TS1, TS3, TS5). The main uncertainty is whether the exact Bi content and ordering are accessible through a topotactic pathway rather than requiring bulk equilibration [Reasonable Inference].

**F2 — Route 4 oxide/H₂S.**  
The oxide route is more severe because a similar Nb sulfide synthesis used 1000 °C air firing, 1150 °C H₂S reduction, 800 °C annealing, and slow cooling (SS4, TS4). H₂S is a reactive and toxic atmosphere [Reasonable Inference], and the process is clearly multi-step (SS4, TS4). No high pressure is indicated (SS4, TS4).

**F3 — Route 4 oxide/H₂S.**  
The kinetics are limited by oxide-to-sulfide conversion and strong Nb–O bonding [Reasonable Inference]. The structural analogue shows that such conversion can work for Nb sulfides, but the target contains Bi and may form Bi oxides/oxysulfides or Bi₂S₃ side phases [Reasonable Inference; route basis from SS4/TS4]. Therefore this is plausible but not preferred.

**F4 — Amorphization tendency.**  
The major amorphization trigger is the large cation size/coordination mismatch between Nb and Bi [Reasonable Inference], reflected in NbS₆ versus linear Bi–S bonding (CS1, SS1, TS1). The target is not a large complex cell by formula count, and the supplied target description does not report simultaneous vertex/edge/face-sharing connectivity. The existence of crystalline target-like analogues strongly lowers the practical amorphization concern (CS1, SS1, TS1).
