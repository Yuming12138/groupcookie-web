### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

#### F1 — Thermodynamic stability

The target E_hull is 0.0000 eV/atom, placing it in the highest thermodynamic-stability scoring bin. TS analogues are also on-hull or very near on-hull, including K3NaSn3Se8, Na4SnSe4, K2HgSnSe4, Rb3AgSn3Se8, and Na2GeSe3 (TS1–TS5). CS analogues show that chemically close Na–Sn–Se phases are at 0.0127 and 0.0070 eV/atom, supporting a robust Na–Sn–Se stability field (CS1, CS2). Remaining uncertainty is polymorph-specific: the target Amm2 phase is on the computed hull, but the experimentally referenced exact-composition phase is monoclinic P2₁/c (CS1, SS1).

#### F2 — Route 1

Route 1 is moderately severe because it requires evacuated sealed ampoules and volatile/reactive elemental Na/Se handling, as shown by direct Na–Sn–Se syntheses in evacuated silica or graphitized silica ampoules (CS1, CS2, SS1, SS2). It does not require high pressure or >1000 °C temperatures, since the closest analogues use 973–1173 K (CS1, CS2). Insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS exists for exact Amm2 polymorph-specific thermal windows, so the condition score is penalized mainly for atmosphere and volatility rather than extreme temperature.

#### F3 — Route 1

Kinetics are favorable because exact-composition Na2SnSe3 has been made directly from elements, demonstrating that the Na–Sn–Se network can form without elaborate precursor chemistry (CS1, SS1). The target and analogue share SnSe4 tetrahedra and Na–Se coordination motifs, reducing the structural-reconstruction penalty (CS1, SS1). The main uncertainty is whether the Amm2 polymorph forms under the same thermal schedule or requires different cooling/annealing [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2 — Route 2

Route 2 is more operationally complex because Na2Se preparation and handling are required, and related routes use vacuum-sealed ampoules, stepwise heating, and controlled cooling (SS4, TS2, CS4). The temperatures are moderate for solid-state chalcogenides, 850 K to 800 °C, and no high pressure is reported (SS4, TS2, CS4). Insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS exists for whether Na2Se specifically favors the Amm2 Na2SnSe3 polymorph, so the score reflects procedural complexity rather than proven phase selectivity.

#### F3 — Route 2

Na2Se is a chemically active Na–Se precursor and should reduce the need to form Na–Se bonds from elemental Na during the ternary reaction [Reasonable Inference], while Na4SnSe4 shows successful formation of Na–Sn–Se polyhedral networks from Na2Se/Sn/Se mixtures (SS4, TS2). The local motifs in Na4SnSe4—NaSe5 trigonal bipyramids and SnSe4 tetrahedra—match key target motifs (SS4, TS2). The stoichiometric difference between Na4SnSe4 and Na2SnSe3 leaves uncertainty about competing phase formation.

#### F2 — Route 3

Route 3 uses a lower temperature, 773 K, but introduces flux-handling, sealed-tube, inert-atmosphere, and post-growth washing steps (SS3, TS1). The flux route is not high-pressure and not ultra-high-temperature, but alkali polyselenide fluxes are chemically aggressive and air-sensitive [Reasonable Inference], consistent with glovebox/vacuum sealed-tube handling (SS3, TS1). Insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS exists for a Na-only flux recipe that suppresses K-containing byproducts.

#### F3 — Route 3

Flux growth is kinetically attractive because molten alkali polychalcogenides can transport Sn/Se species at lower temperature [Reasonable Inference], and K3NaSn3Se8 crystallized at 773 K over 6 days (SS3, TS1). The presence of SnSe4 and alkali–selenide coordination motifs supports structural compatibility (SS3, TS1). However, the cited flux product was obtained unexpectedly and later showed minor impurity issues, implying moderate phase-control risk (SS3, TS1).

#### F2 — Route 4

Route 4 has mild temperature conditions because TS4 reports 180 °C solvothermal synthesis. No high pressure >5 GPa or high-temperature sealed ampoule is involved; only autogenous solvothermal pressure is used (TS4). However, atmosphere evidence is insufficient from TS4, and the method is less directly supported for Na2SnSe3 than the solid-state routes.

#### F3 — Route 4

Solvothermal transport may improve kinetics at low temperature [Reasonable Inference], and TS4 demonstrates crystallization of an alkali–Sn–Se compound from Sn/Se/alkali carbonate precursors at 180 °C. The structural pathway is uncertain because TS4 includes Ag and Rb and forms a quaternary framework, not ternary Na2SnSe3. CS and SS provide insufficient evidence for solvothermal formation of either monoclinic or Amm2 Na2SnSe3, so structural-reconstruction and phase-selectivity scores are reduced.

#### F4 — Amorphization tendency

The target contains cations with substantially different size/coordination preferences, Na in high-coordinate Na–Se polyhedra and Sn in SnSe4 tetrahedra, which triggers the radius/polyhedral mismatch criterion [Reasonable Inference] supported by analogous Na–Sn–Se structures (CS1, SS1). The target has mixed corner- and edge-sharing polyhedral connectivity, and similar mixed connectivity is present in Na2SnSe3, Na6Sn2Se7, and Na4SnSe4 analogues (CS1, CS2, SS4). Unit-cell atom-count evidence for the target Amm2 cell is insufficient from CS/SS/TS, so that check is treated as neutral/not triggered. The resulting amorphization risk is medium, mostly due to polymorphism and connectivity complexity rather than thermodynamic instability.

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

Na2SnSe3 is likely a thermodynamically accessible phase because the target is reported at E_hull = 0 and multiple chemically or thermodynamically similar alkali tin selenides are on or near the hull (CS1, CS2, TS1–TS5). The strongest support is the exact-composition Na2SnSe3 precedent synthesized from elements in evacuated silica ampoules, although that reference is monoclinic P2₁/c rather than the target Amm2 polymorph (CS1, SS1). The most critical enabling factor is the robust Na–Sn–Se chemistry built from stable SnSe4 tetrahedra and Na–Se coordination environments, which recur across CS/SS/TS analogues (CS1, CS2, SS4, TS2). The most critical limiting factor is polymorph control between known monoclinic Na2SnSe3 and the predicted orthorhombic Amm2 phase (CS1, SS1). Air/moisture sensitivity and Na/Se volatility further require sealed, carefully loaded ampoule synthesis (CS2, CS5, SS4).
