### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

#### F1 — Thermodynamic stability

The target has E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom, placing it on the computed convex hull and giving the maximum thermodynamic score. Several closely related analogues are also hull-stable, including Rb₂GeS₃, Cs₂AgAsS₄, Rb₂AgPS₄, Rb₃SbSe₃, and Rb₃BiSe₃ (CS-1; SS-1–5; TS-1–5). The remaining uncertainty is that CS/SS/TS do not give a full competing-phase list for Rb–As–S, so finite-temperature decomposition or polymorphism remains insufficiently evidenced from CS/SS/TS and is treated neutrally where not specified.

#### F2 — Route 1: sealed-tube direct solid-state synthesis

The route avoids extreme temperature and high pressure, because the most relevant Rb sulfide analogues were synthesized in sealed ampoules at 650 °C to 1073 K, not above 1000 °C, and without high-pressure apparatus (CS-1; SS-2; SS-5; TS-2; TS-5). The score is reduced for vacuum/inert atmosphere and sealed-tube handling, because Rb₂GeS₃ required an evacuated silica tube and Rb₂AgPS₄ required a sealed N₂ ampoule (CS-1; SS-2; SS-5; TS-2; TS-5). Additional deductions come from toxic As chemistry and air-sensitive Rb₂S [Reasonable Inference], while exact toxicity/air-sensitivity of the target is insufficiently evidenced from CS/SS/TS.

#### F3 — Route 1: sealed-tube direct solid-state synthesis

Kinetics should be reasonably favorable because Rb₂GeS₃ formed single crystals after only 12 h at 1073 K, indicating that Rb sulfide/chalcogenide reactions can proceed without prolonged ceramic sintering (CS-1; SS-5; TS-5). Structural reconstruction is moderate-low because the target and Rb₂GeS₃ both contain Rb–S polyhedra and tetrahedral MS₄ units, although As(V) and Ge(IV) differ in charge and stoichiometry [Reasonable Inference from CS-1/SS-5/TS-5]. The main kinetic uncertainty is whether As₂S₃/S fully converts to isolated AsS₄³⁻ without forming lower-valent arsenic sulfides [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2 — Route 2: polysulfide flux

The flux route uses lower temperature than sealed solid-state synthesis, as K₂TeS₃ crystallized at 573 K for 6 days in potassium polysulfide flux (CS-3). The score is lowered by the need for Ar/vacuum handling, sealed ampoules, multistep flux preparation/removal, and toxic/air-sensitive sulfide/arsenic chemistry [Reasonable Inference; Ar/vacuum and washing steps from CS-3]. Insufficient evidence from SS and TS directly supports Rb polysulfide flux for Rb₃AsS₄, so their route-specific signal is neutral for exact flux transferability.

#### F3 — Route 2: polysulfide flux

A liquid or molten polysulfide flux should improve kinetics by dissolving sulfide species and enabling crystal growth at lower temperature [Reasonable Inference], consistent with K₂TeS₃ flux growth at 573 K and slow cooling (CS-3). The route has strong ion-transport advantages but only partial structural match, because K₂TeS₃ contains TeS₃ units rather than AsS₄ tetrahedra (CS-3). The largest uncertainty is phase selectivity under sulfur-rich conditions, especially possible formation of Rb polysulfide salts or lower-valent arsenic sulfides [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2 — Route 3: solvothermal / supercritical ammonia

This route benefits from low temperature, since Cs₂AgAsS₄ was synthesized at 135–160 °C in supercritical NH₃ and Rb₂MoS₄ at 393 K in ethylenediamine (SS-1; TS-1; CS-2). The severity score is reduced because the supercritical ammonia synthesis required high-pressure autoclave counter-pressure to 3200 psi, fused silica tubes, drybox handling, and inert/vacuum procedures (SS-1; TS-1). Although this is not GPa-level pressure, the specialized equipment and toxic/air-sensitive arsenic sulfide chemistry make the route challenging [Reasonable Inference].

#### F3 — Route 3: solvothermal / supercritical ammonia

Solution-mediated crystallization should provide high precursor mobility and avoid long-range solid-state diffusion [Reasonable Inference], consistent with low-temperature formation of Cs₂AgAsS₄ and Rb₂MoS₄ (SS-1; TS-1; CS-2). The AsS₄ tetrahedron is directly supported by Cs₂AgAsS₄, but the extra Ag cation and different framework topology reduce certainty for direct formation of Rb₃AsS₄ (SS-1; TS-1). Rb₂MoS₄ was only a minor phase with Ag₂S as the main product, indicating phase-control risk in solvothermal Rb thiometallate chemistry (CS-2).

#### F4 — Amorphization tendency

The target combines large Rb coordination polyhedra with compact AsS₄ tetrahedra, so the cation-size/bonding mismatch check is triggered [Reasonable Inference from the target structure description]. The target description includes corner and edge sharing but no face-sharing coexistence, so the mixed-connectivity amorphization check is not triggered. The unit-cell atom count is not explicitly given by CS/SS/TS; based on the supplied formula and identifier it is likely not >40, but this remains a minor uncertainty.

Rb₃AsS₄ is likely a thermodynamic ground-state phase because the target is reported with E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom, and multiple close Rb/chalcogenide and AsS₄-containing analogues are also hull-stable (CS-1; SS-1–5; TS-1–5).
