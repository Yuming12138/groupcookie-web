The main severity penalty is the H₂ atmosphere, explicitly reported for Rb₂Ni₃Se₄ synthesis (CS1, SS1, TS1), plus Se handling risk [Reasonable Inference]. #### F3 — Route 1

The reaction is kinetically plausible because the exact Rb₂Ni₃Se₄ composition is reported after only 4 h at 850 °C (CS1, SS1, TS1). Ni metal is less reactive than molecular or salt precursors, which lowers precursor-activity scoring [Reasonable Inference], but the carbonate/chalcogen environment likely enhances transport [Reasonable Inference]. Structural reconstruction is favorable because the analogue contains the same square-planar Ni–Se units and Rb–Se coordination motifs (CS1, SS1, TS1), although Fddd-versus-Fmmm polymorph selection remains uncertain.

#### F2 — Route 2

Route 2 avoids H₂ and vacuum, using Ar at 800–850 °C as reported for A₂M₃X₄ fusion reactions (CS3, CS5, SS2–SS5, TS2–TS5).
