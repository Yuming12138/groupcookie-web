### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

#### F1 — Thermodynamic stability

The target is calculated on the convex hull with E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom, which maps to the 90–100 range and justifies a high F1 score. The closest RbSH and KSH analogues are also E_hull = 0.0, and RbSH is the top chemical, structural, and thermodynamic match (CS1, SS1, TS1; SS2, TS2). Remaining uncertainty is that computed hull stability does not guarantee kinetic accessibility or room-temperature polymorph stability, and alkali hydrogensulfides can exhibit multiple modifications requiring phase control (CS1, SS1, TS1).

#### F2 — Route 1 condition severity

Route 1 avoids high temperature and high pressure by using the 50–150 °C autoclave conditions reported for RbSH/KSH analogues (CS1, SS1, SS2, TS1, TS2). The severity deductions come from controlled Ar handling, volatile/toxic H₂S, sealed autoclave operation, and multi-step gas/glovebox handling (CS1, SS1, SS2, TS1, TS2). The main uncertainty is how Cs changes vapor pressure/autogenous pressure and whether CsHS has narrower phase windows than RbSH/KSH [Reasonable Inference].

#### F3 — Route 1 kinetics

Kinetics are favorable because RbSH/KSH form at only 50–150 °C from reactive alkali metals and H₂S, implying that bulk refractory diffusion is not the controlling barrier (CS1, SS1, SS2, TS1, TS2). The target structure closely matches the RbSH/KSH MSH motif, including single H–S bonding, so little structural complexity is expected during nucleation/growth (CS1, SS1, SS2, TS1, TS2). Remaining uncertainty is polymorph selection, since the analogue hydrogensulfides have multiple crystalline modifications and narrow temperature windows (CS1, SS1, TS1).

#### F2 — Route 2 condition severity

Route 2 is penalized primarily for supercritical NH₃ at 2000–3000 bar, controlled high-pressure autoclave conditions, slow cooling, and air-sensitive/hygroscopic products (SS4, TS4). It does not trigger the >5 GPa penalty because 2000–3000 bar is far below 5 GPa, and it does not trigger >1000 °C because the route uses 300–400 °C (SS4, TS4). The uncertainty is whether adding an intentional hydrogen source such as H₂S/NH₄HS can be done safely and reproducibly under those conditions [Reasonable Inference].

#### F3 — Route 2 kinetics

Transport and crystallization can be good under supercritical NH₃, and the reference route uses slow cooling from 300–400 °C to grow Cs–S products (SS4, TS4). However, the reported main phase is Cs₂S/Cs–S chemistry, while CsSH is a side product, so the kinetic pathway may favor sulfide/polysulfide products unless hydrogen chemical potential is controlled (SS4, TS4). This uncertainty lowers the structural-reconstruction and phase-selectivity components.

#### F2 — Route 3 condition severity

Route 3 inherits sealed-system and toxic/air-sensitive precursor penalties because it would likely require CsH/Cs₂S and H₂S or sulfur under inert conditions [Reasonable Inference]. CS2–CS5 show sealed hydride-halide syntheses at high temperature, but they are not direct hydrosulfide evidence; therefore they are treated as neutral, 50, for direct applicability to CsHS. A lower-temperature adaptation is chosen because the direct RbSH/KSH hydrogensulfide syntheses occur at 50–150 °C (CS1, SS1, SS2, TS1, TS2).

#### F3 — Route 3 kinetics

Kinetics may be acceptable if H₂S participates as a reactive gas, but the lack of direct evidence for CsH + S or Cs₂S + H₂S forming CsHS reduces confidence [Reasonable Inference]. The target structure is simple and similar to RbSH/KSH, which supports feasible ordering once the correct stoichiometry is reached (CS1, SS1, SS2, TS1, TS2). The main uncertainty is competing Cs₂S or polysulfide formation and whether solid-state hydrosulfidation can proceed without melting/decomposition.

#### F4 — Amorphization tendency

The target has only one cation, so cation-size disorder is not expected, and the closest RbSH/KSH analogues are simple ordered MSH phases rather than compositionally complex networks (CS1, SS1, SS2, TS1, TS2; [Reasonable Inference]). The target description and analogue descriptions emphasize single H–S bonding with alkali coordination, not mixed vertex/edge/face-sharing polyhedral networks (CS1, SS1, SS2, TS1, TS2). Remaining uncertainty is the exact unit-cell size of the specific Cm target setting, but no CS/SS/TS evidence suggests a large >40-atom amorphization-prone cell.
