### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

#### F1 — Thermodynamic Stability

The target has E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom, placing it in the 90–100 range by the provided rubric. Thermodynamic analogues K₂Ag₄S₃, CsAg₃S₂, CsAg₃Se₂, RbAg₃Se₂, and K₂Ag₄Se₃ are also on the hull with E_hull = 0.0 eV/atom, supporting stability of nearby alkali silver chalcogenide phases (TS-1 to TS-5). Remaining uncertainty is mainly computational/temperature dependence, because E_hull does not guarantee ease of nucleation or phase purity [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2/F3 — Route 1: Direct alkali-carbonate / Ag / S fusion growth

The preferred route uses 850 °C, pure Ar, corundum crucible, and 4 h heating, which are moderate and standard solid-state/flux conditions rather than extreme conditions (CS-1, SS-2, TS-1). The route receives a high F2 score because it avoids high pressure and specialized autoclaves, though it has multiple handling/washing/drying steps (CS-1, SS-2, TS-1). Kinetically, carbonate/sulfur flux fusion improves transport relative to dry metal diffusion [Reasonable Inference], and the exact Rb₂Ag₄S₃ family is reported as synthesizable by this method (CS-1, SS-2, TS-1). The main uncertainty is phase purity, because traces of a second phase and crystal isolation difficulty are noted for the closely related materials (CS-1, SS-2, TS-1).

#### F2/F3 — Route 2: Sealed-tube alkali polysulfide / reactive sulfide flux growth

The sealed polysulfide flux route is lower-temperature than the preferred route, based on a Rb-containing sulfide flux analogue at 550 °C for 3 days (SS-5). Its F2 score is reduced because evacuated tubes, volatile sulfur, air-sensitive Rb₂S/Rb₂Sₓ, controlled cooling, and inert isolation add operational severity (SS-5; [Reasonable Inference]). Kinetically, a molten/reactive sulfide flux should promote ion transport and crystal growth [Reasonable Inference], consistent with the RbAuCdS₂ flux synthesis (SS-5). The uncertainty is significant because CS and TS provide insufficient direct evidence for Rb₂Ag₄S₃ formation from Rb₂Sₓ flux specifically, so those sources are neutral for transferability.

#### F2/F3 — Route 3: Supercritical ethylenediamine solvothermal synthesis

The supercritical ethylenediamine route operates at only 300 °C, but it requires quartz-tube/autoclave handling, Ar counterpressure, and six days of heating (CS-4, SS-1). Kinetics can be favorable because supercritical ethylenediamine enabled crystalline KAg₃S₂ formation at low temperature (CS-4, SS-1). However, subcritical reactions failed and yielded amorphous products in the analogue, so pathway control is a major uncertainty (CS-4, SS-1). Transferability to Rb₂Ag₄S₃ is plausible but weaker than Route 1 because the analogue has different stoichiometry and the direct Rb₂Ag₄S₃ evidence uses high-temperature fusion (CS-1, SS-2, TS-1; CS-4, SS-1).

#### F4 — Amorphization Tendency

The target has large cation-size/coordination contrast between Rb and Ag, which triggers one amorphization-risk check [Reasonable Inference], and analogous alkali silver sulfides show complex mixed coordination environments (CS-1, SS-2, TS-1). The target description includes corner and edge sharing but not face sharing, so the mixed-connectivity check requiring vertex + edge + face sharing is not triggered. The unit-cell atom count is not explicitly available; insufficient evidence from CS, SS, and TS for this check, so it is treated neutrally rather than as a trigger. Overall amorphization risk is low–medium, consistent with the existence of crystalline analogues but with some sensitivity to crystallization/isolation (CS-1, SS-2, TS-1).
