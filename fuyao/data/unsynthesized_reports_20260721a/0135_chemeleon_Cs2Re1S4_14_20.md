### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

#### F1 — Thermodynamic stability

The target has E_hull = 0.0 eV/atom, so it is scored near the top of the ≤0.05 eV/atom stability range. This is reinforced by chemically similar Cs₂WS₄, Rb₂WS₄, Cs₂MoS₄, and Rb₂MoS₄, all reported with E_hull = 0.0 eV/atom (CS-1, CS-2, CS-3, SS-1, SS-2, TS-1, TS-2). The remaining uncertainty is that Re-specific competing redox sulfides are not represented directly in the reference set; this uncertainty is a [Reasonable Inference], not directly provided by CS/SS/TS.

#### F2 — Route 1 condition severity

Route 1 is very mild because the closest chemical analogue Cs₂WS₄ was crystallized from dilute aqueous solution in air with simple filtration and washing (CS-1). There is insufficient evidence from SS for an aqueous Cs/Re-specific route, so SS is neutral for this route-specific condition factor, score 50. There is insufficient evidence from TS for an aqueous Cs/Re-specific route beyond the Mo analogue’s stability, so TS is neutral for this route-specific condition factor, score 50. The only deduction is a small expert penalty for handling Re thiometallate/sulfide precursor chemistry [Reasonable Inference].

#### F3 — Route 1 kinetics

The kinetics are favorable because the likely precursor already contains the tetrahedral MS₄ unit, analogous to `(NH4)2[WS4]` used for Cs₂WS₄ (CS-1). Solution crystallization bypasses slow cation diffusion, and Cs₂WS₄ formed directly from solution rather than by high-temperature diffusion (CS-1). Insufficient evidence from SS/TS exists for Re-specific aqueous nucleation behavior, so SS and TS are neutral for that sub-aspect, score 50, while the W/Mo structural match supports the general isolated-tetrahedron pathway (SS-1, TS-1).

#### F2 — Route 2 condition severity

Route 2 uses mild hydrothermal conditions because Cs₂MoS₄ was obtained at 423 K for 24 h in a sealed quartz ampoule (CS-3, SS-1, TS-1). The conditions are more severe than Route 1 due to sealed-vessel pressure and polysulfide handling, but they are much milder than high-temperature flux routes (CS-3, SS-1, TS-1). The main uncertainty is whether Re requires stricter redox/sulfide control than Mo [Reasonable Inference].

#### F3 — Route 2 kinetics

Hydrothermal media provide high precursor mobility and can crystallize Cs₂MoS₄ directly, which supports good kinetic feasibility for Cs₂ReS₄ (CS-3, SS-1, TS-1). The target’s Re–S bond lengths of ~2.17–2.21 Å are close to Mo–S/W–S values of ~2.18–2.20 Å in the analogues, supporting low structural reconstruction penalty (CS-1, CS-3, SS-1). The uncertainty is the Re oxidation-state pathway from Re metal or Re sulfide if a preformed tetrathioperrhenate is not used [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2 — Route 3 condition severity

Route 3 is more demanding because Rb₂WS₄ flux synthesis used sealed fused-silica tubes, Ar/vacuum, 923 K, and slow cooling (CS-2). Related Cs sulfide flux syntheses also require glovebox loading, sealed tubes, vacuum or Ar, and post-washing (SS-4, SS-5, TS-3, TS-4). These requirements justify deductions for controlled atmosphere, sealed volatile sulfur, multi-step flux preparation, and air-sensitive precursors.

#### F3 — Route 3 kinetics

Reactive fluxes can dissolve/activate refractory transition metals, as shown by W metal conversion to Rb₂WS₄ at 923 K and by V/Nb/Ta sulfide flux syntheses (CS-2, SS-4, SS-5, TS-3, TS-4). However, Re metal is refractory and may react slowly without a preformed ReS₄ species [Reasonable Inference]. Slow cooling improves ordering/crystal growth, but the target’s low-symmetry P1 structure may require careful nucleation control [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2 — Route 4 condition severity

Route 4 has mild temperature because Rb₂MoS₄ was synthesized solvothermally at 393 K for 7 d (SS-2, TS-2). The main severity arises from ethylenediamine handling, sealed autoclaves, and sulfide chemistry rather than temperature or pressure (SS-2, TS-2). There is insufficient evidence from CS for this exact solvothermal amine route in Cs/Re chemistry, so CS is neutral for this route-specific condition factor, score 50.

#### F3 — Route 4 kinetics

Solvothermal conditions provide good mass transport and low diffusion barriers, as shown by Rb₂MoS₄ formation at 393 K (SS-2, TS-2). However, the same analogue was only a minor phase with Ag₂S as the main product, indicating poor phase selectivity and lowering the structural/nucleation score (SS-2, TS-2). The route is plausible but less reliable than the aqueous or hydrothermal routes.

#### F4 — Amorphization tendency

The target has a large Cs/Re cation size mismatch, so one amorphization-risk trigger is assigned [Reasonable Inference]. The target does not exceed 40 atoms in the described cell and lacks explicit mixed vertex/edge/face-sharing polyhedral connectivity, so C2 and C3 are not triggered. The resulting F4 score is low–medium risk rather than high risk, because the structure is dominated by discrete ReS₄ tetrahedra in a Cs–S lattice.
