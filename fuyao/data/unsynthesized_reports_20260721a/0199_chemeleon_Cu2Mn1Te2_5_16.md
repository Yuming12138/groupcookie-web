### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

#### F1 — Thermodynamic stability

The target is predicted exactly on the convex hull with E_hull = 0.0 eV/atom, which places it in the 90–100 thermodynamic score range by the required rubric. Thermodynamic analogues include several stable tellurides with E_hull = 0.0, especially Cu₂TiTe₃, CuInTe₂, CuGaTe₂, AgInTe₂, and AgGaTe₂ (TS1–TS5). Chemical analogues Cu₂TiTe₃ and Cu₂ZrTe₃ are also hull-stable and synthesized (CS1, CS5). The main uncertainty is that DFT hull stability does not guarantee kinetic accessibility or absence of low-temperature competing Mn–Cu–Te orderings [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2/F3 — Route 1: Cu/Te flux

Condition severity is moderate because flux analogues use sealed evacuated quartz tubes, multi-day heating, controlled cooling, and volatile Te, but no high pressure or >1000 °C temperature (CS1, CS5, TS1). Kinetics are aided by a molten Cu/Te flux, as Cu₂TiTe₃ and Cu₂ZrTe₃ formed at 650–900 °C with slow cooling (CS1, CS5). The limiting kinetic issue is that the closest flux analogues are monoclinic C2/m rather than target R3m, requiring different cation ordering [Reasonable Inference from CS1/CS5 versus target structure]. Insufficient evidence from SS for this exact flux route except that SS5 repeats Cu₂TiTe₃ as a structural analogue.

#### F2/F3 — Route 2: Direct solid-state reaction

Direct solid-state synthesis uses standard evacuated ampoules and furnaces, consistent with ErCu₃Te₃/TmCu₃Te₃ and NaCu₃Te₂ syntheses (SS1, SS2, SS4). Severity is penalized by vacuum sealing, Te volatility, multistep processing, and Mn/Te handling risks [Reasonable Inference]. Kinetics are less favorable than flux because dense elemental Mn/Cu require interdiffusion; long anneals in SS1/SS2 indicate slow ordering can matter. Structural support is strong from NaCu₃Te₂, which is also R3m and has similar high-coordinate Cu/Te environments, but Na→Mn is a chemically significant substitution (SS4; [Reasonable Inference]).

#### F2/F3 — Route 3: Binary chalcogenide route

The binary route has similar condition severity to direct solid state because it still requires vacuum sealing, heating, and multistep annealing [Reasonable Inference from TS3/SS4]. Its kinetic score is highest because MnTe + Cu₂Te preorganizes the required metal–tellurium bonding and reduces the need to form binary tellurides in situ [Reasonable Inference].
