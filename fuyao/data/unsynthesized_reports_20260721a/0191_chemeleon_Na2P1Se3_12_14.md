### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic stability.**  
The target is reported at E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom, which places it in the 90–100 stability band. The closest exact Na2PSe3 analogue is also E_hull = 0.0 in chemical, structural, and thermodynamic lists (CS-1, SS-1, TS-1). Additional P–Se framework analogues such as K2Mg(PSe3)2 and Rb2Cd(PSe3)2 are also hull-stable (SS-2, TS-2, SS-4, TS-4), so the thermodynamic evidence is strong. The remaining uncertainty is polymorph-specific: the known exact analogue is orthorhombic Cmce, whereas the target is monoclinic C2/m [Reasonable Inference; CS-1/SS-1/TS-1].

**F2/F3 — Route 1.**  
The direct route uses moderate-to-high temperatures but avoids high pressure, consistent with Na2GeSe3, Na6Si2Se7, and Na2TeSe3 syntheses in sealed ampoules (CS-2, CS-3, CS-5). The main F2 penalties are inert/vacuum handling, sealed volatile P/Se chemistry, and air-sensitive Na, all consistent with glovebox and sealed ampoule requirements in CS-2 and CS-5. Kinetically, elemental Na/P/Se should react more readily than oxide precursors [Reasonable Inference], and exact Na2PSe3 stability reduces the reconstruction penalty (CS-1, SS-1, TS-1). The uncertainty is whether direct equilibration selects the C2/m polymorph rather than the known Cmce polymorph [Reasonable Inference].

**F2/F3 — Route 2.**  
Flux growth is directly supported by the exact Na2PSe3 analogue synthesized at 725 °C for 150 h with slow cooling and DMF washing (CS-1, SS-1, TS-1). Its condition severity is higher because it requires precursor preparation, vacuum sealing, slow cooling, and solvent washing, as also seen in P–Se flux analogues (SS-4, TS-4, TS-5). Its kinetics score is high because molten polyselenide/polyselenophosphate flux promotes diffusion and crystal growth [Reasonable Inference; SS-4/TS-4]. The limiting uncertainty is whether the flux stabilizes the target C2/m polymorph or preferentially reproduces the known orthorhombic Cmce Na2PSe3 [Reasonable Inference; CS-1/SS-1/TS-1].

**F2/F3 — Route 3.**  
Low-temperature annealing is inferred from Na2TeSe3 polymorph control, where different temperature/time schedules give different Na2TeSe3 modifications (CS-5, SS-5, TS-3). F2 remains penalized because this is a multi-step sealed-ampoule route involving air-sensitive/volatile components [Reasonable Inference; CS-5/SS-5]. Kinetically, the correct composition is already formed, but cation/anion rearrangement at 350–500 °C may be slow, consistent with the 10-day anneal used for Na2TeSe3-mP24 (CS-5, SS-5, TS-3). The main uncertainty is whether C2/m Na2PSe3 is a low-temperature polymorph or only a computed ground-state ordering [Reasonable Inference].

**F4 — Amorphization tendency.**  
The target combines large Na-centered octahedral environments with compact PPSe3 tetrahedra, implying a large cation-size/coordination mismatch [Reasonable Inference from target structure]. The target also has mixed corner- and edge-sharing polyhedral connectivity, which increases the chance of disorder or glassy intermediates during rapid cooling [Reasonable Inference; mixed connectivity explicit in target description and comparable in SS-1/TS-1]. Insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS confirms whether the target unit cell contains >40 atoms, so that check is treated as neutral. The resulting medium amorphization risk favors slow cooling/annealing rather than quenching [Reasonable Inference].

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

Na2PSe3 is likely thermodynamically synthesizable because the target is calculated on the hull and an exact Na2PSe3 analogue is already known on the hull (CS-1, SS-1, TS-1). The main caveat is polymorphism: CS/SS/TS report orthorhombic Cmce Na2PSe3, while the target is monoclinic C2/m, so the composition is well supported but the specific structure requires phase-selection control [Reasonable Inference; CS-1/SS-1/TS-1]. The strongest enabling factor is the direct chemical match to a synthesized Na2PSe3 phase and the existence of multiple related Na–chalcogenide and P–Se frameworks made in sealed ampoules or fluxes (CS-1 to CS-5, SS-1 to SS-5, TS-1 to TS-5). The strongest limiting factor is control of Na/P/Se volatility and air sensitivity during sealed-tube synthesis [Reasonable Inference; inert/vacuum handling in CS-2, CS-5, SS-3, TS-3]. A secondary limiting factor is polymorph competition with the known Cmce phase [Reasonable Inference; CS-1/SS-1/TS-1]. Overall, the material is feasible but should be approached as a polymorph-targeting synthesis rather than a simple new-composition synthesis.
