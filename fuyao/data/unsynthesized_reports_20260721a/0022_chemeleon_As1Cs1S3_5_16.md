### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

#### F1 — Thermodynamic stability

The target is reported at E_hull = 0.0 eV/atom, which places it in the 90–100 scoring bin. This is reinforced by all five chemically similar materials being at E_hull = 0.0 eV/atom (CS-1 to CS-5), and by structurally/thermodynamically similar polysulfides and thioantimonites being mostly on the hull (SS-1 to SS-5, TS-1 to TS-5). Remaining uncertainty is that computed ground-state stability does not guarantee kinetic accessibility or absence of hydrated/solvated competing products during solution synthesis [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2 — Route 1

Route 1 is penalized for vacuum/sealed-tube operation because CS-1/CS-3 used evacuated carbon-coated tubes and SS-1/TS-1 used dynamic vacuum/flame-sealed ampoules. It is also penalized for volatile sulfur/arsenic sulfide and toxic/air-sensitive reagents [Reasonable Inference], consistent with air/moisture sensitivity and sealed handling described for related chalcogenides in CS-1, CS-3, CS-5, and SS-1. The temperature is not extreme because analogous Cs polysulfides and sulfides form at 250–803 K/°C-scale conditions rather than >1000 °C (CS-5, SS-1/TS-1).

#### F3 — Route 1

Kinetics are moderate because alkali sulfides and sulfur are chemically active, but a solid-state route still requires reconstruction into a low-symmetry P1 Cs–As–S framework [Reasonable Inference]. Cs4S12 and CsSnS3 show that Cs–S/S–S frameworks can crystallize under sealed/flux conditions (SS-1/TS-1, CS-5). Uncertainty remains because no CS/SS/TS entry demonstrates the exact CsAsS3 framework by direct solid-state reaction.

#### F2 — Route 2

Route 2 has the mildest condition severity because SS-5 uses only 145–160 °C solvothermal heating for 5 days in sealed tubes/autoclaves. It is penalized for sealed-vessel vapor pressure and arsenic toxicity because As2S3 is listed in SS-5 and arsenic sulfides are toxic [Reasonable Inference]. SS-5 reports air sealing, so no controlled-atmosphere deduction is applied, although inert loading is recommended [Reasonable Inference].

#### F3 — Route 2

Kinetics are favorable because solvothermal media provide transport and crystallization at low temperature, as demonstrated by SS-5. As2S3, S, and Cs2CO3 are already represented in the SS-5 precursor set, making this the strongest transfer to CsAsS3. The main uncertainty is whether the solution speciation favors the target CsAsS3 stoichiometry rather than other thioarsenates/polysulfides [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2 — Route 3

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

CsAsS3 is likely a thermodynamic ground-state phase because its supplied E_hull is 0.0 eV/atom, and many chemically, structurally, and thermodynamically similar alkali chalcogenides are also on the hull (CS-1 to CS-5, SS-1 to SS-5, TS-1 to TS-5). The most critical limiting factor is phase selectivity among Cs polysulfides, As2S3-derived phases, and possible hydrated/solvated products [Reasonable Inference; phase-control issues are noted in CS-5, SS-1, and SS-5]. Overall, solvothermal synthesis is the most attractive first attempt because it provides low temperature, high precursor mobility, and direct precedent for Cs/As/S-containing reagent sets (SS-5).
