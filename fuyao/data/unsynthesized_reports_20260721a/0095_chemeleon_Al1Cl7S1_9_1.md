### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

#### F1 — Thermodynamic stability

The target has E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom, placing it in the most favorable thermodynamic bin. CS provides supporting evidence because several chemically similar chlorides are hull-stable or near-hull: CS2, CS3, CS4, and CS5 have E_hull = 0.0, and CS1 has E_hull ~0.0099 eV/atom. SS/TS also support stability because Hf and Zr selenium chloride analogues are hull-stable in SS2/TS1 and SS3/TS2, while other structurally similar salts are near hull in SS1/SS5. The remaining uncertainty is that DFT hull stability does not guarantee kinetic accessibility or resistance to hydrolysis; this limitation is not directly resolved by CS/SS/TS.

#### F2 — Route 1 condition severity

Route 1 benefits from low temperatures because related sealed-ampoule halide syntheses occur at 140–250 °C or ~473 K in CS1, CS3, CS4, SS2–SS4, and TS1–TS2. The score is reduced because the route requires vacuum or sealed ampoules, volatile chloride precursors, and moisture-sensitive/toxic halides, all reflected in CS1–CS4 and SS/TS chalcogen chloride syntheses. Insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS gives no exact safe pressure window for SCl₂/Cl₂/SCl₄ mixtures, so pressure management is an uncertainty and is partly [Reasonable Inference].

#### F3 — Route 1 kinetics

Kinetics are favorable because molecular chlorides react and crystallize at low temperature by vapor/liquid transport rather than refractory solid-state diffusion, as shown by CS1, CS3, CS4, SS2–SS4, and TS1–TS2. The isolated-cluster target resembles molecular salt analogues such as SnCl₅PCl₄ in CS2/TS3 and RuS₃Cl₈ in CS5/TS4. The main uncertainty is whether SCl₃⁺ and AlCl₄⁻ form selectively under the same chemical potential; that exact chemistry is [Reasonable Inference], not directly shown in CS/SS/TS.

#### F2 — Route 2 condition severity

Route 2 uses mild temperatures because CS2/TS3 reports room-temperature or reflux solution mixing for a molecular chloride salt. The severity score is still limited by required drybox/drying-tube handling, hydrolyzable chlorides, volatile solvents/reagents, and multiple operations such as mixing, filtering, crystallization, and drying, all described in CS2/TS3. Insufficient evidence from SS for solution routes means SS is neutral for this sub-factor.

#### F3 — Route 2 kinetics

Solution kinetics are strong because CS2/TS3 shows molecular chloride products can form within 15 minutes to several hours. The target’s zero-dimensional ion-pair character makes a solution crystallization pathway plausible [Reasonable Inference], especially because no long-range cation diffusion is needed. Remaining uncertainty is phase selectivity: sulfur chloride cations may disproportionate or generate competing salts [Reasonable Inference], and CS/SS/TS do not directly document AlSCl₇ solution crystallization.

#### F2 — Route 3 condition severity

Route 3 is low-temperature, using the 140 °C SCl₂ closed-ampoule synthesis in CS5/TS4 as the closest sulfur-chloride precedent. The score is reduced by autogenous pressure, closed ampoule/autoclave containment, N₂ handling, reactive SCl₂, multiple post-reaction operations, and air/hydrolysis sensitivity, all explicitly noted in CS5/TS4. Insufficient evidence from SS for SCl₂ solvothermal processing makes SS neutral for this route.

#### F3 — Route 3 kinetics

SCl₂ is a highly reactive sulfur-chloride medium and forms Ru–S–Cl clusters at 140 °C in CS5/TS4, so precursor activity and diffusion are favorable. However, CS5/TS4 reports multiple ternary Ru–S–Cl products crystallizing over weeks and requiring microscopic separation, indicating phase-selectivity challenges. Applying this to AlSCl₇ is [Reasonable Inference], so the F3 score is lower than Routes 1–2.

#### F4 — Amorphization tendency

The target has only small isolated molecular clusters and no extended mixed connectivity, so C2 and C3 are not triggered by the target structure. C1 is conservatively triggered because Al and S represent very different coordination/bonding environments; this is [Reasonable Inference] because SCl₃⁺ is molecular rather than a conventional cation. CS2/CS5 and SS2–SS4/TS1–TS2 support molecular crystallization rather than network glass formation, but CS/SS/TS provide insufficient direct amorphization data and are neutral for direct glass-forming tendency.
