### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

#### F1 — Thermodynamic stability

The target is calculated at E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom, which gives the maximum thermodynamic score under the provided rubric. This is supported by multiple TS analogues with E_hull = 0.0, including ZnAl₂S₄, NaInS₂, MgAl₂S₄, AgAlS₂, and HgAl₂S₄ (TS-1–TS-5). CS also supports the broader stability of sodium sulfides because Na₂PS₃ and Na₃PS₄ are on the hull, while NaVS₂ analogues are close enough to hull to be experimentally accessible (CS-1, CS-2, CS-4, CS-5). Remaining uncertainty is that calculated zero hull does not guarantee kinetic accessibility or absence of competing polymorphs [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2 — Route 1 condition severity

Route 1 inherits sealed-ampoule, sulfur-containing, Na-containing reaction constraints from NaVS₂ synthesis, which used evacuated quartz tubes, staged heating, 600 °C holding for 4 days, and quenching (CS-4, CS-5). Al sulfide analogues often require evacuated ampoules and controlled heating, with MgAl₂S₄ using a low-temperature pre-hold to avoid explosive sulfur reaction before high-T treatment (SS-4; TS-3). The score is limited mainly by vacuum/sealed-tube operation, volatile sulfur/sodium control, and air-sensitive handling [Reasonable Inference; CS-2, CS-4, CS-5]. Insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS exists for an exact NaAlS₂ temperature window, so the 650–850 °C proposal is an extrapolation from NaVS₂ and Al sulfides [Reasonable Inference].

#### F3 — Route 1 kinetics

Kinetically, the target benefits from a simple four-atom formula and known layered NaMS₂ analogues, especially NaVS₂ and NaInS₂ with NaS₆/MS₆ octahedra (CS-5, SS-3; TS-2). The main kinetic drawback is the use of elemental Al, whose oxide skin may impede direct sulfidation [Reasonable Inference], although Al metal is successfully used in MgAl₂S₄ and ZnAl₂S₄ preparations (SS-1, SS-4; TS-1, TS-3). Long holds in NaVS₂ and Al sulfide syntheses indicate that ordering and diffusion are not instantaneous (CS-4, CS-5; SS-1, SS-4). Overall kinetics are favorable but not trivial.

#### F2 — Route 2 condition severity

Route 2 avoids external high pressure and can likely remain below 1000 °C using a sulfide flux [Reasonable Inference], whereas direct copying of NaInS₂ oxide/flux conditions could reach 1350 °C and become more severe (SS-3; TS-2). The route still requires sealed/inert handling because sodium sulfides and sulfide products are moisture sensitive, as seen in sodium thiophosphate synthesis (CS-2). Flux handling introduces multiple steps: loading, melting/reaction, slow cooling, and flux removal [Reasonable Inference; SS-3, TS-2]. Insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS exists for a demonstrated NaAlS₂ flux composition, so the exact flux choice is extrapolated.

#### F3 — Route 2 kinetics

Flux growth receives the best F3 score because liquid/reactive sulfide flux should reduce diffusion distances and promote layered ordering [Reasonable Inference]. This is supported by NaInS₂, a close Caswellsilverite NaMS₂ analogue, being associated with flux methods (SS-3; TS-2). AlS₆ octahedra are compatible with sulfide frameworks in ZnAl₂S₄ and MgAl₂S₄, supporting the structural reconstruction score (SS-1, SS-4; TS-1, TS-3). The remaining uncertainty is whether Al³⁺ prefers octahedral NaAlS₂ over competing Al₂S₃/spinel-like or tetrahedral Al sulfide environments under the chosen flux chemistry [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2 — Route 3 condition severity

Route 3 uses conditions similar to ZnAl₂S₄ CVT, which required iodine, quartz ampoules, and a controlled 800→740 °C gradient for 90 h (SS-2; TS-1). It also resembles chemically similar sealed-tube vapor-transport/annealing sulfide work at 948 K (CS-1). Severity deductions arise from vacuum/sealed-tube operation, volatile iodine/sulfur species, multi-step precursor formation plus transport, and hazardous iodine/air-sensitive sodium sulfide handling [Reasonable Inference; CS-2, SS-2]. Insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS demonstrates successful CVT of a sodium aluminum sulfide specifically, so this route is less certain.

#### F3 — Route 3 kinetics

CVT bypasses long-range solid-state diffusion during crystal growth and is successful for ZnAl₂S₄ single crystals (SS-2; TS-1). However, the transport chemistry of NaAlS₂ is more uncertain than ZnAl₂S₄ because Na can form stable/volatile halides and disturb stoichiometry [Reasonable Inference]. Structural reconstruction should be modest if a phase-pure NaAlS₂ feed is first prepared [Reasonable Inference]. The route is therefore attractive for crystals but not the best first synthesis attempt.

#### F4 — Amorphization tendency

The target has a large cation size/charge contrast between Na⁺ and Al³⁺ in octahedral sulfide coordination [Reasonable Inference], triggering C1. The structural model is simple NaAlS₂ in a trigonal Caswellsilverite-like framework, and there is insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS that the unit cell exceeds 40 atoms, so C2 is not triggered. The target description emphasizes edge-sharing octahedra, while analogues may include edge and corner sharing but not face-sharing coexistence (CS-4, CS-5, SS-3; TS-2), so C3 is not triggered. The resulting amorphization risk is low–medium rather than high.

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

NaAlS₂ is likely a thermodynamic ground-state or near-ground-state phase because the target has E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom and several thermodynamic analogues are also on the hull (TS-1–TS-5). The synthesis case is strong because the target’s Caswellsilverite-like NaS₆/AlS₆ motif is closely supported by NaVS₂ and NaInS₂ layered NaMS₂ analogues (CS-5, SS-3; TS-2). The most important enabling factor is the close structural match to known NaMS₂ octahedral layered sulfides combined with AlS₆ stability in related Al sulfides (CS-5, SS-1, SS-3, SS-4; TS-1–TS-3). The main limiting factor is controlling Na and S volatility/moisture sensitivity during sealed-tube or flux synthesis, as sodium sulfides and several sulfide analogues require dry/vacuum handling (CS-2, CS-4, CS-5; SS-2, SS-4). Overall, the material should be synthetically accessible, with reactive sulfide flux or carefully staged sealed-ampoule synthesis as the most credible routes.
