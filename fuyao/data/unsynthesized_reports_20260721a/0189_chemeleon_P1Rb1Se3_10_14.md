### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic stability.**  
The target has E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom, giving a high thermodynamic score. Close analogues RbPSe₃/Rb₂P₂Se₆ and Rb₄P₂Se₉ are also hull-stable or essentially hull-stable, supporting the stability of Rb–P–Se frameworks (CS1, CS4; SS1, SS2; TS1, TS2). Remaining uncertainty is that hull stability does not guarantee the predicted C2/m polymorph is the experimentally favored polymorph over the known trigonal RbPSe₃ analogue [Reasonable Inference] from the polymorph difference (CS1, SS2, TS2).

**F2 — Route 1 condition severity.**  
Route 1 avoids very high temperature and high pressure because the exact analogue is made at 450 °C in sealed silica (CS1, SS2, TS2). The penalties arise from vacuum sealing, volatile Se/P species, multi-step precursor handling, and air-sensitive Rb₂Se-type precursors (CS1, SS2, TS2; CS3). The severity is therefore moderate rather than easy.

**F3 — Route 1 kinetics.**  
Kinetics are favorable because Rb₂Se/P/Se has direct precedent and likely forms reactive selenophosphate intermediates [Reasonable Inference] from the exact analogue synthesis (CS1, SS2, TS2). The main kinetic uncertainty is structural reconstruction into C2/m edge-sharing PSe₄ rather than the known trigonal PPSe₃-related polymorph (CS1, SS2, TS2). The 3 d hold suggests ordering is not instantaneous (CS1, SS2, TS2).

**F2 — Route 2 condition severity.**  
Route 2 uses moderate temperatures of 500–750 °C and sealed tubes, not high pressure or >1000 °C conditions (CS4, SS1, TS1; SS5, TS5). It still requires vacuum sealing, volatile Se-rich fluxes, inert handling, precursor preparation, and washing steps (CS4, SS1, TS1; SS5, TS5). Thus it scores similarly to Route 1 in severity.

**F3 — Route 2 kinetics.**  
Flux growth receives the best kinetic score because molten polyselenide/polyselenophosphate fluxes improve mass transport and crystal growth [Reasonable Inference] from successful growth of Rb₄P₂Se₉ and Rb₂Cd(PSe₃)₂ under flux conditions (CS4, SS1, TS1; SS5, TS5). Structurally similar analogues contain Rb–Se coordination and PSe₄ motifs, supporting transferability (SS1–SS5, TS1–TS5). The uncertainty is phase selectivity, since different Rb/Cs polyselenophosphates form under related chemical potentials (CS1, CS4; SS1, SS4, SS5; TS1, TS4, TS5).

**F2 — Route 3 condition severity.**  
The direct elemental route has precedent from Rb₂P₂S₆/RbPS₃ at 800 °C in evacuated quartz with strict Ar handling (CS3). Although still below 1000 °C and not high-pressure, it involves Rb metal, sealed tubes, chalcogen vapor pressure, and a prereaction step (CS3). Its severity is therefore challenging in practice despite the simple precursor list.

**F3 — Route 3 kinetics.**  
Elemental Rb/P/Se should be reactive, but it lacks the preorganization of Rb₂Se/P₂Se₅/Se flux routes [Reasonable Inference] from the analogue precursor choices (CS1, SS2, TS2; SS5, TS5). The high-temperature sulfide analogue demonstrates feasibility for Rb–P–chalcogen network formation (CS3). The major uncertainty is that direct elemental reaction may equilibrate to the known trigonal polymorph or other Rb–P–Se phases rather than the target C2/m structure [Reasonable Inference] from the range of Rb–P–Se phases in CS/SS/TS.

**F4 — Amorphization tendency.**  
The target has a large contrast between Rb coordination and PSe₄ covalent tetrahedra, which can promote local structural disorder [Reasonable Inference] from Rb–Se high coordination and PSe₄ motifs in target/analogues (CS1, SS2, TS2). However, many close analogues crystallize successfully as ordered phases under sealed-tube or flux conditions, reducing amorphization concern (CS1, CS4; SS1–SS5; TS1–TS5). Insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS is available to confirm a >40 atom unit cell for the target.
