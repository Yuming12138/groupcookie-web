### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic stability.**  
The target is predicted exactly on the convex hull with E_hull = 0.0 eV/atom from the provided target data. Closely related thermodynamic analogues such as CdSc2S4, CdTm2S4, CdY2S4, CdHo2S4, and MnSc2S4 are also at or very near E_hull = 0.0 eV/atom (TS1–TS5). Chemically similar analogues also show near-hull stability, including Lu2ZnS4 and CdLn2S4 examples (CS3–CS5). The remaining uncertainty is that no Hg-containing analogue is reported in CS/SS/TS, so the thermodynamic prediction must carry some chemical-risk uncertainty [Reasonable Inference].

**F2/F3 — Route 1, sealed binary sulfide synthesis.**  
Condition severity is moderate because the closest CdSc2S4 analogue forms at 900 °C in evacuated sealed silica ampoules, with long firing and refiring but no high pressure (SS1, TS1). Kinetics are also moderate because binary sulfide diffusion is slow, and CdSc2S4 required 10-day sintering plus homogenization/refiring (SS1, TS1). The strong structural match to CdSc2S4 improves feasibility because both contain ScS6 octahedra and tetrahedral divalent-metal sulfide units in cubic Fd-3m spinel (SS1, TS1). The main uncertainty is Hg volatility and lack of direct Hg-spinel precedent in CS/SS/TS [Reasonable Inference].

**F2/F3 — Route 2, H2S sulfiding synthesis.**  
This route is more severe because H2S or sulfurizing atmosphere is explicitly used in ZnLn2S4 and MnSc2S4 syntheses, and such atmospheres are reactive/toxic (CS3, TS5). Kinetics may improve relative to dry sulfide sintering because sulfur chemical potential is maintained and high-temperature sulfiding is demonstrated for spinel-related sulfides (CS3, TS5). However, the closest CdSc2S4 analogue does not require H2S, making this a backup route rather than the primary approach (SS1, TS1). The uncertainty is whether H2S at high temperature would worsen Hg volatility or transport losses [Reasonable Inference].

**F2/F3 — Route 3, CVT/vapor-assisted annealing.**  
CVT is supported for ZnSc2S4 and MnSc2S4, using closed tubes and I2/HCl transport near 1000–1050 °C for crystal growth (SS2, TS5). The route is condition-severe because it is multi-step, requires transport agents, and may run for weeks (SS2, TS5). Kinetic crystal growth can be favorable once powder exists, but composition control is risky because ZnSc2S4 CVT showed quartz reaction/Si impurity issues, and Hg volatility adds additional uncertainty (SS2; Hg issue [Reasonable Inference]). This route is best viewed as a crystal-growth method after bulk phase formation.

**F2/F3 — Route 4, halide-flux-assisted sealed synthesis.**  
KBr-assisted heating improved crystal size for chemically similar Tm2ZnS4 after sealed-ampoule synthesis (CS1). The temperature is attractive because the reported KBr step is 1120 K, about 847 °C, which is close to the 900 °C range used for CdSc2S4/CdLn2S4 spinels (CS1, SS1, CS4, TS1). Flux can improve mass transport and reduce the kinetic penalty of dense sulfide diffusion [Reasonable Inference], giving the best F3 among the proposed routes. The key uncertainty is that SS/TS do not directly report flux synthesis for CdSc2S4 or any Hg analogue, so halide compatibility with Hg sulfide chemistry is not proven.

**F4 — Amorphization tendency.**  
The target has distinct ScS6 octahedra and HgS4 tetrahedra, indicating significant cation size/coordination contrast and triggering the radius-difference criterion [Reasonable Inference from target structure]. A conventional cubic Fd-3m spinel cell contains more than 40 atoms, triggering the unit-cell-size criterion [Reasonable Inference from target spinel symmetry and analogous spinel descriptions in SS1/TS1]. The target shows corner and edge sharing but no face sharing, so the mixed vertex+edge+face sharing criterion is not fully triggered. The resulting amorphization risk is medium, mainly relevant for low-temperature or thin-film routes rather than high-temperature annealed bulk synthesis [Reasonable Inference].

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

Sc2HgS4 is likely a thermodynamic ground-state or near-ground-state phase because the target E_hull is 0.0 eV/atom and thermodynamic analogues in the Sc/Cd/Ln sulfide spinel family are also on or near the hull (TS1–TS5). The strongest support comes from CdSc2S4, which has the same cubic Fd-3m spinel topology, identical Sc–S bond length of 2.59 Å, and a tetrahedral divalent metal sulfide site analogous to HgS4 (SS1, TS1). The most critical enabling factor is the excellent structural match to known Sc-based thiospinels and the existence of routine sealed-ampoule sulfide synthesis protocols (SS1, TS1). The most critical limiting factor is the absence of any direct Hg-containing analogue in CS/SS/TS, combined with likely Hg/S volatility and Hg toxicity [Reasonable Inference]. Overall, synthesis appears plausible but requires careful sealed-ampoule stoichiometry and volatility control.
