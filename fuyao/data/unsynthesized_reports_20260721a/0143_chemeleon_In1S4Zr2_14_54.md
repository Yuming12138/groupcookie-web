### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic stability.**  
The target is calculated on the convex hull with E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom, which strongly supports ground-state accessibility from a thermodynamic standpoint. Thermodynamically similar sulfides in TS1–TS5 are also mostly on the hull, including SrZrS₃ in TS4 and CeScS₃ in TS1. The remaining uncertainty is that DFT hull stability does not guarantee a wide experimental phase field or fast crystallization kinetics [Reasonable Inference].

**F2/F3 — Route 1.**  
Route 1 uses standard sealed-tube solid-state sulfide conditions, supported by Nb₂PdS₅ at 850 °C in evacuated quartz (CS4) and SrZrS₃ at 850–1000 °C under vacuum (SS5/TS4). Its condition severity is moderate because vacuum sealing, volatile sulfur, and regrinding are needed (CS4, SS5/TS4). Kinetics are less favorable because elemental Zr is refractory and oxophilic [Reasonable Inference], and analogous sulfides often require long annealing or repeated grinding (CS1, SS5/TS4).

**F2/F3 — Route 2.**  
Route 2 is supported by binary-sulfide syntheses such as CeScS₃ from binary sulfides in H₂S at 1473 K (SS1/TS1) and BaLn₂FeS₅ from sulfide precursors in evacuated tubes (SS4). Severity is higher if H₂S is used because it is toxic and reactive, and high temperatures near 1000–1100 °C may be required (SS1/TS1). Kinetics improve relative to elements because Zr–S and In–S bonds are preformed [Reasonable Inference], but significant structural reconstruction and cation diffusion remain likely.

**F2/F3 — Route 3.**  
Route 3 is based on iodine CVT syntheses of MnNb₃S₆ and FeNb₃S₆-type sulfides at 950–1050 °C gradients (CS3, CS5). Its severity is high because it requires evacuated sealed ampoules, iodine vapor, a temperature gradient, and often a pre-reacted powder precursor (CS3, CS5). Kinetically, CVT can bypass bulk diffusion and improve crystallinity [Reasonable Inference], but phase and composition control are uncertain because CS3 and CS5 note nonstoichiometry, biphasic regions, intergrowths, or impurity-related issues.

**F2/F3 — Route 4.**  
Route 4 receives the best score because CS2 directly supports reactions of transition-metal dichalcogenides with In/Sn/Pb/Bi at 700–900 °C for 1–4 weeks. The lower temperature reduces severity relative to high-T binary-sulfide firing, while evacuated ampoules and sulfur volatility still impose penalties (CS2, CS4, SS5/TS4). Kinetics are favored by molten In acting as a reactive medium [Reasonable Inference], but the target’s exact Zr₂InS₄ stoichiometry may be sensitive to excess In and sulfur activity [Reasonable Inference].

**F4 — Amorphization tendency.**  
The target contains markedly different Zr and In coordination environments, with ZrS₆ octahedra at 2.55–2.62 Å and low-coordinate In–S contacts at 3.06–3.15 Å, so cation-size/coordination mismatch is a plausible amorphization or disorder driver [Reasonable Inference from target structure]. However, the structure is not described as having simultaneous vertex-, edge-, and face-sharing polyhedral networks, and the unit-cell atom count is not shown to exceed 40. Therefore the amorphization risk is low–medium rather than high.

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

Zr₂InS₄ is likely a thermodynamic ground-state phase because the target E_hull is 0.0000 eV/atom, and multiple thermodynamically similar sulfides are also reported on the hull (TS1–TS5). The strongest synthesis support comes from SrZrS₃, which contains edge-sharing ZrS₆ octahedra with comparable Zr–S distances (SS5/TS4), and from chemically similar In-containing dichalcogenide reactions at 700–900 °C (CS2). The most critical enabling factor is the availability of sealed-tube sulfide chemistry that can maintain sulfur chemical potential while allowing slow cation ordering (CS2, CS4, SS5/TS4). The main limiting factor is likely phase and stoichiometry control among ZrS₂, In₂S₃, metallic In, and the target Zr₂InS₄ [Reasonable Inference]. Overall, the target appears experimentally plausible, with the In/S flux or In-rich sulfide route being the most promising first attempt.
