### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic stability.**  
The target is predicted at E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom, giving a strong thermodynamic score by the required rubric. This is reinforced by several structurally and thermodynamically similar Pt–S compounds also lying on the hull, including Tl₂Pt₅S₆, KPt₂S₃, Pt₂TlS₃, and Rb₂U(PtS₂)₃ (SS/TS: mp-28805, mp-30533, mp-9272, mp-1191149). Chemically similar Tl–Ln sulfides also include stable or near-stable examples (CS: mp-16472, mp-3664, mp-557655, mp-1007900). The main uncertainty is that computed hull stability does not guarantee accessibility of the specific monoclinic BaPt₂S₃ ordering [Reasonable Inference].

**F2 — Route 1 condition severity.**  
Route 1 uses sealed vacuum or Ar ampoules, consistent with direct Pt–S reactions in evacuated silica tubes and sulfide solid-state synthesis in sealed quartz ampoules (SS/TS: mp-28805; CS: mp-557655). The route is penalized for controlled atmosphere, sulfur volatility, multistep staged heating/regrinding, and BaS air sensitivity [Reasonable Inference]. It avoids high pressure and does not require temperatures above 1000 °C based on Pt–S analogues operating around 950–1000 °C (SS/TS: mp-28805, mp-9272). Insufficient direct Ba synthesis evidence from CS/SS/TS leaves Ba handling scored neutral 50 at the source-evidence level.

**F3 — Route 1 kinetics.**  
Direct solid-state reaction is kinetically limited by refractory Pt metal [Reasonable Inference], even though Pt powder is a successful precursor in Pt–S analogues (SS/TS: mp-28805, mp-9272). Sealed-tube melt/slow-cooling protocols improve transport, but the absence of an exact BaPt₂S₃ structural analogue means the framework must form by substantial reconstruction [Reasonable Inference]. Repeated grinding/annealing in chemically similar sulfides signals that sluggish completion can occur (CS: mp-557655, mp-16472, mp-3664).

**F2 — Route 2 condition severity.**  
Route 2 is the least severe because the closest A–Pt₂–S₃ analogue KPt₂S₃ forms in flux at 800–900 °C without reported high pressure or exotic equipment (CS/SS/TS: mp-30533). It is still penalized for sulfur volatility, multistep flux recovery, and moisture/air sensitivity of BaS or sulfide fluxes [Reasonable Inference]. The reference implies open/air melting, but for Ba-containing sulfides sealed/inert adaptation is safer [Reasonable Inference]. No CS/SS/TS source directly validates Ba in this flux, so Ba-specific transferability remains neutral 50 from the evidence base.

**F3 — Route 2 kinetics.**  
Flux growth should significantly improve Pt transport compared with dry reaction, supported by KPt₂S₃ formation from a high-temperature melt and slow cooling (CS/SS/TS: mp-30533). The target and KPt₂S₃ share A₁Pt₂S₃ stoichiometry and similar Pt–S distances, which supports partial structural transferability (CS/SS/TS: mp-30533). The principal kinetic uncertainty is whether Ba²⁺ can enter the same Pt–S framework without instead forming BaS, PtS₂, or other Ba–Pt–S competing phases [Reasonable Inference].

**F2 — Route 3 condition severity.**  
Route 3 inherits vacuum sealing, glovebox handling, multistep flux processing, and volatile sulfur control from ACl/A₂Qₓ Pt–S flux growth (SS/TS: mp-1191149). The temperature is near 1000 °C, not above the >1000 °C penalty threshold if held at or below 1273 K/1000 °C (SS/TS: mp-1191149). Equipment such as carbon-coated fused-silica tubes and a high-vacuum line is specialized but standard for air-sensitive flux synthesis (SS/TS: mp-1191149). Chemical transfer from U/Rb/Pt/S to Ba/Pt/S is indirect, so CS evidence for this specific route is insufficient and neutral 50.

**F3 — Route 3 kinetics.**  
Chalcogenide/halide fluxes provide liquid-mediated transport and can grow Pt–S compounds with square-planar PtS₄ units (SS/TS: mp-1191149). The Pt–S distances in the flux-grown analogue, 2.37–2.38 Å, are close to the target’s 2.33–2.34 Å, supporting local bonding transferability (SS/TS: mp-1191149). The route is less compositionally direct than Route 2 because the analogue contains U and has a different stoichiometry, so phase selection remains uncertain [Reasonable Inference].

**F4 — Amorphization tendency.**  
The large mismatch between Ba coordination/bond distances and Pt square-planar coordination indicates one amorphization/structural-frustration trigger [Reasonable Inference], using the target’s Ba–S = 3.17–3.51 Å and Pt–S = 2.33–2.34 Å.
