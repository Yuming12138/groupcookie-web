### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic stability.**  
The target has E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom, so it receives a very high thermodynamic score under the provided rubric. Similar I-4 Ga2Te4 framework compounds CdGa2Te4, ZnGa2Te4, and HgGa2Te4 are also reported at E_hull = 0.0 eV/atom (SS3; SS4; TS1; TS2; TS3). The remaining uncertainty is polymorphism: exact MnGa2Te4 evidence in CS/SS is for a Pnma phase close to the hull rather than the target I-4 phase (CS1; SS1).

**F2 — Route 1.**  
Ambient sealed-ampoule solid-state synthesis is penalized for vacuum/sealed-tube operation, volatile Te, multi-step processing, and toxicity/handling risk [Reasonable Inference], all consistent with long evacuated ampoule syntheses of Zn/Cd/Hg Ga tellurides (CS5; SS3; TS1; TS2; TS3). The route avoids >1000 °C conditions and high pressure, which improves severity relative to CVT or pressure synthesis (CS5; SS4; TS2). The main uncertainty is whether Mn stabilizes the I-4 framework at ambient pressure rather than the known Pnma MnGa2Te4 polymorph (CS1; SS1).

**F3 — Route 1.**  
Kinetics are moderate because binary tellurides and molten Ga/Te can assist reaction [Reasonable Inference], while elemental Mn may remain a slower-diffusing component. I-4 analogues are structurally close, supporting a relatively direct crystallization pathway (CS5; SS3; SS4; TS1; TS2; TS3). Long annealing times of weeks to hundreds of days in related systems indicate slow ordering and phase-equilibration kinetics (SS3; CS5; TS1; TS2).

**F2 — Route 2.**  
High-pressure synthesis receives no formal deduction for >5 GPa because the cited pressures are 1.5–3 GPa, below the rubric threshold (CS1; SS1; CS3; SS5). It is still practically challenging due to belt-type high-pressure apparatus, BN capsules, and quenching requirements (CS1; SS1; CS3; SS5). Insufficient evidence from TS supports high-pressure MnGa2Te4 synthesis, so TS is treated as neutral for this route.

**F3 — Route 2.**  
Using prereacted MnGa2Te4 gives good chemical activity and compositional homogeneity, supported by exact-composition pressure transformation evidence (CS1; SS1). The key kinetic penalty is structural reconstruction: the known exact-composition high-pressure MnGa2Te4 reference has MnTe6 octahedra in Pnma, unlike the tetrahedral I-4 target (CS1; SS1). Therefore, pressure may enable polymorph access, but it does not guarantee the desired tetrahedral ordering [Reasonable Inference].

**F2 — Route 3.**  
CVT is penalized for >1000 °C operation, evacuated sealed silica tubes, volatile species, multi-step prereaction/growth, and corrosive/hygroscopic transport-agent handling [Reasonable Inference], consistent with the MnIn2Te4 CVT analogue using AlCl3 at about 1050 °C (CS2; SS2). It avoids high pressure but requires strong thermal-gradient and vapor-transport control (CS2; SS2). Insufficient evidence from TS supports CVT specifically, so TS is neutral for this route.

**F3 — Route 3.**  
Vapor transport can improve crystallinity and overcome slow solid-state diffusion, as MnIn2Te4 required CVT to obtain single-phase crystals (CS2; SS2). The crystallization pathway is plausible because MnIn2Te4 has tetrahedral MnTe4 and III–Te tetrahedra similar to the target (CS2; SS2). Composition control remains uncertain because transport rates of Mn, Ga, and Te halide species may differ [Reasonable Inference].

**F4 — Amorphization tendency.**  
The target is an ordered corner-sharing tetrahedral framework, not a mixed edge/face-sharing network, which lowers amorphization risk (target description). The only triggered risk is cation radius mismatch between Mn and Ga in tetrahedral coordination [Reasonable Inference]. Similar crystalline I-4 Ga2Te4 analogues are known, supporting low-to-medium rather than high amorphization risk (CS5; SS3; SS4; TS1; TS2; TS3).

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

The target is predicted to be a ground-state phase because its E_hull is 0.0000 eV/atom, and several thermodynamic analogues in the Ga2Te4 framework are also on the hull (TS1; TS2; TS3; TS4). The synthesis is strongly supported structurally by ZnGa2Te4, CdGa2Te4, and HgGa2Te4 in the same tetragonal I-4 tetrahedral framework (CS5; SS3; SS4; TS1; TS2; TS3). It is also chemically supported by exact MnGa2Te4 existence, although the cited exact-composition phase is Pnma rather than I-4 (CS1; SS1). The most critical enabling factor is the strong thermodynamic stability combined with an established I-4 Ga2Te4 structural family (TS1; TS2; TS3). The most critical limiting factor is polymorph control between tetrahedral I-4 MnGa2Te4 and the known Pnma MnGa2Te4 modification (CS1; SS1).
