### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

#### F1 — Thermodynamic Stability

Similar Rb sulfides and multinary sulfides are also frequently reported on or very near the hull, including Rb₂MoS₄, K₂MoS₄, RbCeS₂, BaCe₂MnS₅, CeScS₃, and RbLaS₂ (CS-1; CS-2; SS-1; SS-2; TS-1; TS-3; TS-5). The main uncertainty is not thermodynamic stability but whether the predicted monoclinic ordering is kinetically accessible [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2/F3 — Route 1

Route 1 is supported by RbLnS₂/RbLaS₂ syntheses from Rb₂CO₃ and oxides under flowing H₂S at ~1000–1025 °C (SS-1; TS-1; TS-5). Its severity is moderate because H₂S is toxic/reactive and the route operates near or slightly above 1000 °C (SS-1; TS-5). Kinetically, the main weakness is conversion of refractory ZrO₂ into an ordered Zr sulfide framework, for which CS/SS/TS provide insufficient direct evidence; therefore F3 is only moderate [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2/F3 — Route 2

Route 2 is supported by multiple sealed-ampoule binary-sulfide syntheses of structurally similar quaternary sulfides at 900–1273 K with regrinding (SS-2; SS-3; SS-4; TS-2). Its condition severity is reduced relative to H₂S flow but includes vacuum sealing, air-sensitive alkali sulfides, possible sulfur vapor pressure, and multi-step handling (SS-4; TS-2; [Reasonable Inference]). Kinetics are better than oxide sulfidation because sulfide precursors are chemically closer to the product, but Zr sulfide diffusion and long-range ordering remain limiting [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2/F3 — Route 3

Route 3 is supported by K₂S₂ molten flux synthesis of complex sulfides in sealed ampoules and by flux growth of sulfide crystals in CS data (TS-4; CS-3). Its severity is moderate because it requires glovebox loading, sealed ampoules, air-sensitive alkali polysulfide flux, and slow cooling (TS-4; [Reasonable Inference]). Kinetically, it is the strongest route because a molten sulfide flux can provide high sulfur activity and liquid-mediated transport for otherwise sluggish Zr-containing solids [Reasonable Inference from TS-4].

#### F2/F3 — Route 4

Route 4 is supported chemically by solvothermal Rb₂MoS₄/K₂MoS₄ syntheses at low temperature (CS-1; CS-2). Its F2 score is high because conditions are mild compared with high-temperature H₂S or ampoule synthesis (CS-1; CS-2). However, F3 is penalized because CS solvothermal analogues are tetrahedral thiometallates, while the target requires an extended edge-sharing ZrS₆ octahedral framework; insufficient evidence from SS/TS supports solvothermal crystallization of this exact motif.

#### F4 — Amorphization Tendency

The target has a large cation size mismatch between Rb and Zr, reflected by Rb–S distances of 3.27–3.62 Å versus Zr–S distances of 2.51–2.74 Å (target). That triggers one amorphization-risk criterion, but the target description does not indicate a >40 atom unit cell or coexistence of vertex/edge/face-sharing polyhedra (target). Thus the amorphization tendency is low–medium rather than high, with the main uncertainty being possible disorder in Rb layers/channels [Reasonable Inference].
