#### F2/F3 — Route 1: sealed-tube elemental solid-state

Condition severity is moderate because the closest spinel sulfide analogues use evacuated quartz tubes and 750–850 °C heating, not extreme temperature or high pressure (CS, SS, TS). The major deductions are vacuum/sealed-tube control, volatile sulfur, and likely multistep firing, all supported by CuRh₂S₄ and FeRh₂S₄ synthesis descriptions (CS, SS, TS). Kinetics are limited by dense Co/Pd metal diffusion [Reasonable Inference], but sulfurization and the demonstrated formation of Rh thiospinels at 800 °C improve confidence (CS, SS, TS). Remaining uncertainty is whether Pd prefers tetrahedral spinel coordination in Co₂PdS₄ rather than forming binary PdSₓ phases [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2/F3 — Route 2: binary sulfide solid-state

This route has similar severity to Route 1 because CoGa₂S₄ binary-sulfide synthesis used evacuated silica ampoules at 800 °C for 10 days (CS). Binary sulfides should reduce the initial sulfurization barrier relative to metals [Reasonable Inference], but repeated grinding and annealing are still expected because NiRh₂S₄ examples require recycling until single phase (CS, SS, TS). The structural argument is good but not perfect: the target is a spinel, while the direct binary-sulfide CoGa₂S₄ analogue is tetragonal (CS). Insufficient evidence from TS on binary-sulfide Co–Pd reaction pathways is treated as neutral.

#### F2/F3 — Route 3: hydrothermal sulfide synthesis

The hydrothermal route has mild temperature severity because Ni₃S₄ forms at 120 °C for 12 h in a Teflon-lined autoclave (SS, TS). Its kinetic score benefits from soluble metal salts and Na₂S, which provide high precursor activity (SS, TS). The main penalty is structural/phase uncertainty: Ni₃S₄ is a close Fd-3m structural/thermodynamic analogue, but no provided source shows Pd-bearing spinel formation hydrothermally, so cation ordering into Co₂PdS₄ is uncertain [Reasonable Inference]. Therefore, this route is numerically attractive for exploratory synthesis but less reliable for bulk, phase-pure material.

#### F2/F3 — Route 4: flux / H₂S-assisted growth

Flux growth is supported for Pd sulfide chemistry by BaPd₂S₄ in KSCN melt under dry H₂S at 730 K (TS). Severity is increased by H₂S toxicity and multistep flux handling (TS). Kinetically, flux growth can enhance mass transport and crystal growth [Reasonable Inference], but the BaPd₂S₄ structural motif is not spinel and contains non-tetrahedral Pd coordination (TS). Thus this route is most useful as a secondary crystal-growth or phase-screening route rather than the primary bulk route.

#### F2/F3 — Route 5: CVT

CVT is supported by FeGa₂S₄ crystal growth using iodine or chlorine in sealed silica ampoules at 950–1000 °C for 15 days (CS). The severity score is lower because halogen transport agents are volatile/toxic and require sealed-tube gradients (CS). Kinetically, vapor transport can improve crystallinity, but no CS/SS/TS source establishes Co–Pd–S transport chemistry or spinel Co₂PdS₄ crystallization by CVT; this missing signal is treated as neutral-to-negative. The route is therefore best used only after a bulk source phase has been obtained.

#### F4 — Amorphization tendency

The target is a relatively ordered cubic spinel, and analogous Fd-3m sulfides form crystalline phases by solid-state or hydrothermal routes (CS, SS, TS). One amorphization trigger is assigned because the conventional spinel cell exceeds 40 atoms [Reasonable Inference from Fd-3m spinel crystallography and CS/SS/TS spinel analogues]. The target has corner and edge sharing but no face-sharing coexistence in the description, so the mixed-connectivity trigger requiring vertex + edge + face sharing is not applied. Ionic-radius mismatch is not directly available from CS/SS/TS; bond-length mismatch is modest, so it is not triggered [Reasonable Inference].

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

Co₂PdS₄ is likely a ground-state or near-ground-state phase because the target E_hull is 0.0000 eV/atom, and several close sulfide spinel analogues also have E_hull = 0 or are reported synthesized despite small positive hull energies (CS, SS, TS). The strongest support comes from Fd-3m thiospinels such as CuRh₂S₄, FeRh₂S₄, NiRh₂S₄, and Ni₃S₄, which demonstrate that related tetrahedral/octahedral sulfide frameworks are experimentally accessible (CS, SS, TS). The most critical enabling factor is the robust spinel sulfide topology combined with favorable calculated thermodynamics (target data, CS, SS, TS). The most critical limiting factor is phase control of Pd coordination, since Pd often stabilizes other sulfide environments such as square-planar/see-saw coordination in BaPd₂S₄ (TS), while the target requires tetrahedral PdS₄ [Reasonable Inference]. Overall, synthesis is feasible, with sealed-tube solid-state or binary-sulfide annealing the most credible bulk routes and hydrothermal synthesis a useful exploratory nanoparticle route.
