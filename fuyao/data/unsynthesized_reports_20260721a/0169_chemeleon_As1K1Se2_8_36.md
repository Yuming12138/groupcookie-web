### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

#### F1 — Thermodynamic stability

The target has E_hull = 0.0 eV/atom, placing it on the calculated convex hull. The exact KAsSe2 analogue mp-14659 is also reported as E_hull = 0.0 and appears as the top chemical, structural, and thermodynamic match (CS, SS, TS). This strongly supports ground-state accessibility, although the target P-1 cell may represent a symmetry-modified polymorph relative to the reported P1 analogue [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2 — Route 1 condition severity

The exact analogue is synthesized at only 130 °C, which is very mild thermally (CS, SS, TS). Deductions arise from inert-gas/sealed-ampoule handling, volatile methanol in a sealed system, multiple operations, and toxic As2Se3 (CS, SS, TS; toxicity is [Reasonable Inference]). The source evidence is strong for temperature, atmosphere, time, and equipment; there is insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS on exact ampoule internal pressure, so pressure risk is treated qualitatively rather than as high-pressure synthesis.

#### F3 — Route 1 kinetics

The methanolothermal route scores high because the exact KAsSe2 analogue forms at low temperature over 7 d from K2CO3 and As2Se3 (CS, SS, TS). Solvent-mediated reaction should reduce diffusion limitations compared with solid-state routes [Reasonable Inference]. Remaining uncertainty is whether the P-1 target polymorph rather than the P1 analogue is obtained directly or appears as a closely related symmetry variant [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2 — Route 2 condition severity

Related K–Se compounds K2GeSe3 and K2TeSe3 are synthesized in evacuated graphitized ampoules at 973 K and 900 °C, respectively, showing that sealed high-temperature methods are feasible for K chalcogenides (CS #2, CS #3, SS #4, TS #4). The route is penalized for vacuum, volatile/reactive K/Se/As species, multistep sealing/heating, and toxic/air-sensitive reagents [Reasonable Inference]. There is insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS that exact KAsSe2 forms by this route, so the route is less favored than Route 1.

#### F3 — Route 2 kinetics

K and Se are reactive, and high-temperature ampoule conditions can form related K–Se frameworks (CS #2, CS #3, SS #4, TS #4). However, direct solid-state synthesis likely requires more structural reconstruction than the exact methanolothermal route because no CS/SS/TS entry reports elemental synthesis of KAsSe2 itself. Kinetic uncertainty is therefore moderate, especially due to competing K–Se and As–Se binaries [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2 — Route 3 condition severity

Flux analogues are made at moderate temperatures of 450–550 °C in evacuated tubes with slow cooling (SS #2, SS #3, SS #5, TS #2, TS #3, TS #5). The route is penalized for vacuum sealing, selenium volatility, multistep operations, toxic arsenic selenide, and air-sensitive potassium selenide/polyselenide precursors (SS #5, TS #5; hazards are [Reasonable Inference]). The evidence supports feasibility for related selenoarsenide crystal growth but not exact KAsSe2.

#### F3 — Route 3 kinetics

Flux synthesis offers favorable mass transport and can stabilize complex alkali selenides at lower temperatures than direct ceramic routes (SS #2, SS #3, SS #5, TS #2, TS #3, TS #5). Related structures contain AsSe3 trigonal units and alkali–selenide coordination, supporting transferability to KAsSe2 (SS #2, SS #5, TS #2, TS #5). The main uncertainty is phase selectivity, since K3BiAs6Se12 had reported purity/yield difficulties and nonstoichiometry (SS #5, TS #5).

#### F4 — Amorphization tendency

The target has a large K/As cation size contrast by standard ionic-radius reasoning [Reasonable Inference], which triggers one amorphization-risk check. The structure description reports mixed edge- and corner-sharing KSe6 octahedra and SeK4As square pyramids, but no face-sharing, so the most severe mixed-connectivity criterion is not fully triggered by the target description. CS/SS/TS do not provide direct amorphization data; therefore, the F4 score is moderately favorable but uncertain.

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

KAsSe2 is very likely synthesizable and likely close to a ground-state phase because the target has E_hull = 0.0 eV/atom and the exact KAsSe2 analogue mp-14659 is also on the hull (CS, SS, TS). The CS/SS/TS support is unusually strong: the top chemical, structural, and thermodynamic reference is the same composition and has an established methanolothermal synthesis (CS, SS, TS). The most critical enabling factor is the existence of a mild, demonstrated K2CO3 + As2Se3 methanolothermal route at 130 °C (CS, SS, TS). The most important limitation is polymorph/phase control, because the reported analogue is triclinic P1 while the target is P-1, so obtaining the exact target symmetry may require careful crystallization or may reflect computational symmetrization [Reasonable Inference]. Additional limiting factors are toxicity of As2Se3 and sealed-ampoule handling [Reasonable Inference based on precursors and conditions in CS/SS/TS].
