### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

#### F1 — Thermodynamic stability

The target is reported with E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom, placing it in the highest thermodynamic-stability bin. The exact-composition analogue mp-30222, Cs₃BS₃, also has E_hull = 0.0 in CS, SS, and TS, strongly supporting thermodynamic feasibility (CS1; SS1; TS1). The remaining uncertainty is polymorph selection: the reference phase is monoclinic P2₁/c whereas the target is monoclinic Cm, so both may be closely related or competing polymorphs [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2 — Route 1 condition severity

Route 1 uses 720 °C for 6 h, which is moderate and below the >1000 °C penalty threshold (CS1; SS1; TS1). The score is reduced because the source route uses sealed low-pressure/inert tubes, sulfur volatility, carbon-coated silica, and moisture-sensitive alkali sulfides (CS1; SS1; TS1). Insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS suggests any need for high pressure or >1000 °C, so those penalties are not applied.

#### F3 — Route 1 reaction kinetics

The kinetics are favorable relative to many boron-containing solids because the exact Cs₃BS₃ phase forms in only 6 h at 720 °C from Cs₂S, B, and S (CS1; SS1; TS1). The local BS₃ bonding in the analogue is nearly identical to the target, with B–S distances of 1.83–1.84 Å versus 1.84–1.85 Å in the target description (CS1; SS1; TS1). The main kinetic concern is that boron is refractory and may require intimate mixing and reactive sulfur/alkali sulfide chemistry [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2 — Route 2 condition severity

Route 2 avoids pre-made Cs₂S but uses metallic Cs and sulfur in sealed evacuated ampoules, which is supported for Cs₂S synthesis at 623 K for 48 h (SS5; TS4). It receives penalties for vacuum/sealed operation, volatile S/Cs chemistry, and air-sensitive metallic Cs [Reasonable Inference supported by SS5/TS4]. Insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS directly demonstrates elemental Cs + B + S to Cs₃BS₃, so the route is less secure than Route 1.

#### F3 — Route 2 reaction kinetics

Elemental Cs and S are reactive enough to form Cs₂S in an ampoule (SS5; TS4), but direct incorporation of B into a crystalline thioborate requires more extensive reconstruction [Reasonable Inference]. Cs₃BP₂ can be made from elements or binary precursors in a Nb ampoule at 900 K, showing that Cs–B–p-block frameworks are synthetically accessible (CS5). However, insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS confirms that direct elemental Cs–B–S avoids competing Cs₂S or boron sulfide phases, so F3 is modest.

#### F2 — Route 3 condition severity

The azide route uses controlled decomposition of CsN₃ and annealing in sealed ampoules, with reported Cs-containing sulfides requiring 500 °C and 8–15 days (CS2). Penalties arise from sealed/vacuum handling, evolved gases, multi-step azide preparation/decomposition, and hazardous azide chemistry (CS2). Insufficient direct evidence from CS2 for B-containing sulfides adds uncertainty but does not change the rubric deductions.

#### F3 — Route 3 reaction kinetics

Azide chemistry can generate reactive heavy alkali species without handling pure Cs metal, which is the key kinetic advantage reported for Cs-containing sulfides (CS2). This should improve mixing and alkali incorporation [Reasonable Inference], but CS2 contains In sulfides rather than B thioborates. Therefore, structural reconstruction and B–S framework formation remain only moderately supported, and insufficient direct B evidence from CS2 limits the score.

#### F2 — Route 4 condition severity

Ammonothermal Cs–S synthesis occurs at 300–400 °C but under 2000–3000 bar NH₃, requiring specialized steel autoclaves (SS2; TS2). The rubric does not penalize this as >5 GPa, but controlled NH₃ atmosphere, sealed volatile conditions, multi-step handling, and air-sensitive Cs species all apply (SS2; SS3; TS2). Operationally this is challenging even if the formal F2 deductions are similar to Route 1 [Reasonable Inference].

#### F3 — Route 4 reaction kinetics

Supercritical NH₃ can promote Cs–S transport and crystallization at relatively low temperatures, as shown for Cs–S and Cs–Se phases (SS2; SS3; TS2). This supports good ion mobility and crystal growth for alkali chalcogenides, but no CS/SS/TS entry demonstrates B incorporation into a Cs borosulfide under ammonothermal conditions. The F3 score is therefore held moderate despite potentially favorable transport kinetics.

#### F4 — Amorphization tendency

The exact Cs₃BS₃ analogue crystallizes successfully and contains trigonal-planar BS₃ units, which argues against a high amorphization tendency for the chemistry (CS1; SS1; TS1). A size/bonding mismatch between Cs and B is substantial [Reasonable Inference], triggering one amorphization-risk check, but the analogue shows this mismatch is crystallographically manageable (CS1; SS1; TS1). Insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS indicates a >40-atom target unit cell or mixed vertex/edge/face polyhedral connectivity, so those checks are treated as non-triggered/neutral.
