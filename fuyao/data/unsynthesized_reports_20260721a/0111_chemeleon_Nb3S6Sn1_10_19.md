### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

#### F1 — Thermodynamic stability

The target has E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom, so it scores near the top of the ≤0.05 eV/atom stability bin (target data). The closest chemically, structurally, and thermodynamically similar Nb₃SnS₆ analogue is also reported at E_hull = 0.0 eV/atom, reinforcing the ground-state plausibility (CS1, SS1, TS1). Remaining uncertainty is polymorph-specific: the supplied analogue is P6₃22 while the target is R32, so the computed hull stability supports composition stability but not necessarily unique polymorph isolation [Reasonable Inference from target data and CS1/SS1/TS1].

#### F2 — Route 1

The closest Nb₃SnS₆ analogue uses 700–900 °C for 1–4 weeks with only furnace heating and no reported high pressure or strict atmosphere, so condition severity is low-to-moderate (CS1, SS1, TS1). A deduction is applied for a sealed system because sulfur and Sn-containing sulfides can be volatile at these temperatures [Reasonable Inference]. Insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS exists for exact ramp/cooling dependence, so those details are treated as neutral rather than penalized beyond the volatility/sealing concern.

#### F3 — Route 1

Kinetics are aided by use of NbS₂-like precursors that already contain Nb–S bonding motifs relevant to the target (CS1, SS1, TS1). However, the 1–4 week anneal reported for the closest analogue indicates slow diffusion/order development, so the kinetic score is moderate rather than high (CS1, SS1, TS1). The main uncertainty is whether the R32 polymorph can be selected over the known P6₃22 form [Reasonable Inference from target vs CS1/SS1/TS1].

#### F2 — Route 2

CVT analogues require sealed silica/quartz tubes, iodine vapor, vacuum or controlled transport conditions, and hot/cold gradients around 950–800 °C or 1050–900 °C (CS4, CS5, SS4, TS4). These conditions are standard but more severe than direct sintering because I₂ is volatile/corrosive and composition transport must be controlled [Reasonable Inference]. The data provide useful evidence for Nb₃S₆-framework transport growth but insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS for Sn-specific iodine transport equilibria, so that uncertainty is penalized in difficulty rather than assumed favorable.

#### F3 — Route 2

Iodine transport can improve mass transfer and single-crystal growth in related MNb₃S₆ compounds (CS4, CS5, SS4, TS4). The closest Sn analogue is not reported by CVT in the supplied data, so transport of Sn relative to Nb and S is uncertain [Reasonable Inference]. This route may help crystal quality but may worsen stoichiometry control [Reasonable Inference based on phase-control issues noted for CVT analogues in CS4/CS5].

#### F2 — Route 3

The oxide/H₂S route uses 1000 °C air treatment, 1150 °C H₂S reduction, and 800 °C annealing in a related Nb–S framework, which triggers high-temperature, reactive-atmosphere, toxic-gas, and multistep penalties (CS3, SS3, TS3). No high-pressure penalty applies because no high pressure is reported (CS3, SS3, TS3). Insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS supports direct Sn oxide sulfurization to Nb₃SnS₆, so transferability is lower.

#### F3 — Route 3

The oxide-to-sulfide pathway demands major anion exchange and reduction, which generally slows kinetics relative to sulfide precursor routes [Reasonable Inference]. The related Mn(NbS₂)₄ synthesis requires multiple high-temperature steps and long annealing, consistent with moderate-to-poor kinetics (CS3, SS3, TS3). The route may still work because single-phase related products were obtained in the reference, but Sn redox and volatility add uncertainty [Reasonable Inference].

#### F4 — Amorphization tendency

The target’s Nb–S and Sn–S bond lengths differ substantially, indicating a size/coordination mismatch that can increase disorder tendency [Reasonable Inference from target data; similar local environments in CS1/SS1/TS1]. The target description does not prove a >40-atom cell, so insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS supports triggering the unit-cell-size check. The closest analogue reports mixed face/edge/corner-sharing motifs, but the target description explicitly emphasizes edge-sharing NbS₆ units rather than full mixed connectivity, so only one amorphization trigger is used (CS1, SS1, TS1; target data).
