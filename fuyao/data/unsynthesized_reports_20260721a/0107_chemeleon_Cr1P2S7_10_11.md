### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

#### F1 — Thermodynamic Stability

The target has E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom, which is direct strong evidence for ground-state thermodynamic stability. Similar thiophosphates TiP₂S₆, KCrP₂S₇, CsCrP₂S₇, CrAg(PS₃)₂, and AgScP₂S₆ are also reported at or near E_hull = 0 in the reference set (CS1, SS4, SS5, TS1, TS2, TS3, TS4, TS5). CrPS₄ is experimentally synthesized despite E_hull = 0.1289 eV/atom, indicating Cr–P–S chemistry can tolerate some metastability (CS2, SS1). Remaining uncertainty is that thermodynamic stability does not guarantee kinetic accessibility or phase selectivity [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2/F3 — Route 1: I₂-CVT

The condition severity is moderate because the route uses evacuated sealed ampoules and volatile P/S/I₂ chemistry but avoids high pressure and >1000 °C temperatures (CS2, SS1). Kinetically, the I₂ transport agent is beneficial because CrPS₄ single crystals were obtained by chemical transport in a temperature gradient (CS2, SS1). The structural transferability is strong because CrPS₄ is a 2D CrS₆–PS₄ layered phase with nearly matching Cr–S/P–S bond lengths and octahedral tilt angles to the target (CS2, SS1). The main uncertainty is whether CrP₂S₇, with higher P/S content than CrPS₄, will transport congruently or instead crystallize CrPS₄ plus phosphorus sulfides [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2/F3 — Route 2: Direct Sealed-Tube Solid-State

The condition severity is moderate: comparable thiophosphates form in evacuated or sealed silica/quartz tubes at 600–700 °C for one to two weeks (SS2, SS5, TS3, TS5). Kinetics are less favorable than flux/CVT because elemental Cr is refractory and long diffusion paths remain [Reasonable Inference], which is consistent with 1–2 week hold times in several analogues (SS2, SS4, TS5). Structural reconstruction is plausible because the product family of layered thiophosphates is well represented in SS and TS, including Cr-containing layered phases (SS1, SS4, SS5, TS2, TS3). The key uncertainty is phase purity, since sealed-tube reactions may generate competing CrPS₄, K/Cs-free P–S binaries, or sulfur-deficient phases [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2/F3 — Route 3: Alkali-Halide Flux Growth

The flux route has moderate condition severity due to vacuum sealing, multi-step heating/cooling/washing, and volatile P/S handling, but it avoids high pressure and extreme temperatures (TS4). Its kinetics are the best among the routes because a molten/reactive halide flux can enhance mass transport and crystal growth [Reasonable Inference], and CsCrP₂S₇ was successfully prepared by CsCl/LiCl flux at 923 K (TS4). Structural transferability is strong because KCrP₂S₇ and CsCrP₂S₇ contain CrS₆ octahedra and PS₄ tetrahedra with corner/edge sharing very similar to the target (SS4, TS2, TS4). The limiting uncertainty is that the flux may stabilize alkali-containing ACrP₂S₇ rather than alkali-free CrP₂S₇ [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2/F3 — Route 4: High-Pressure Fallback

High-pressure synthesis is scored as challenging because AlPS₄ analogues require 5 GPa, a belt-type press, ball milling, and capsule handling (CS4, CS5). Kinetically, ball milling can improve contact, but there is insufficient direct evidence from SS or TS that high pressure is needed or beneficial for CrP₂S₇, so SS/TS are neutral for this route-specific benefit. The chemical similarity to P–S frameworks is useful, but the structural similarity is weaker than CrPS₄/KCrP₂S₇/CsCrP₂S₇ analogues (CS4/CS5 vs SS1/TS4). The main uncertainty is whether pressure stabilizes the desired 2D CrP₂S₇ sheet or instead drives denser competing sulfides [Reasonable Inference].

#### F4 — Amorphization Tendency

The target contains mixed CrS₆ octahedra and PS₄ tetrahedra with both corner and edge sharing, but no face sharing is reported, so only one amorphization check is triggered [target structure description]. Close analogues crystallize as layered or low-dimensional thiophosphate networks rather than amorphous products, including CrPS₄, TiP₂S₆, KCrP₂S₇, CsCrP₂S₇, and AgVP₂S₆ (CS1, CS2, SS1, SS2, SS3, SS4, TS1, TS2, TS4). Long synthesis times and controlled cooling indicate crystallization may be slow but not prohibitively difficult (SS2, SS4, TS5). The remaining uncertainty is the triclinic P1 low-symmetry target may have a narrow ordering window [Reasonable Inference].

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

CrP₂S₇ is likely a ground-state phase because the supplied target E_hull is exactly 0.0000 eV/atom, and several close thiophosphate analogues are also hull-stable (TS1, TS2, TS4). The analogue support is strong: CrPS₄ directly supports Cr–P–S layered CrS₆/PS₄ chemistry (CS2, SS1), while KCrP₂S₇ and CsCrP₂S₇ support very similar CrP₂S₇ framework motifs (SS4, TS2, TS4). The most critical limiting factor is phase selectivity: the system may prefer known alkali-containing ACrP₂S₇ phases, CrPS₄, or binary P–S/Cr–S impurities unless sulfur fugacity and stoichiometry are carefully controlled [Reasonable Inference based on SS4/TS2 phase-control comments and TS4 flux chemistry]. Overall, the target is highly plausible but should be approached with careful sealed-ampoule or flux optimization rather than open solid-state firing.
