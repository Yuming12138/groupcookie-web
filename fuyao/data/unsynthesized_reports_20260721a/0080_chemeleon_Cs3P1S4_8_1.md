### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

#### F1 — Thermodynamic stability

The target has E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom, so the thermodynamic score is maximal by the provided rubric. This is reinforced by the fact that all top chemically, structurally, and thermodynamically similar examples listed are also hull-stable with E_hull = 0.0 eV/atom (CS1–CS5, SS1–SS5, TS1–TS5). The remaining uncertainty is that computed hull stability does not guarantee kinetic accessibility or absence of hydrate/solvate side phases [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2/F3 — Route 1, sealed-tube solid-state

The condition severity is moderate because related Cs-rich sulfides require inert handling, sealed ampoules, and 500–720 °C heating, but not >1000 °C or high pressure (CS2, CS3, SS2, SS3, TS1–TS3). Kinetics are favorable compared with oxide ceramics because sulfide precursors are reactive and analogues crystallize at moderate temperatures (CS2, SS2, TS2). The main uncertainty is whether Cs3PS4 forms directly or whether competing Cs2S/P2S5-derived thiophosphates appear [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2/F3 — Route 2, solution/solvothermal metathesis

This route has the lowest severity because CS4 demonstrates direct cesium tetrathiometalate crystallization from solution under very mild conditions, and CS1 shows mild hydrothermal access to a Cs thioanion salt. Kinetics are strong because preformed PS4^3− eliminates P–S bond formation and solution transport eliminates long-range diffusion [Reasonable Inference]. The largest uncertainty is hydrolysis of PS4^3−, since CS4 uses [WS4]^2−, which is not necessarily identical in aqueous stability to [PS4]^3− [Reasonable Inference]; insufficient direct evidence from CS/SS/TS exists for aqueous Cs3PS4 crystallization.

#### F2/F3 — Route 3, azide route

The azide route has significant condition penalties because CS3/SS1/TS1 require CsN3 handling, vacuum/inert processing, sealed quartz ampoules, and long anneals. Kinetically, it is promising because the azide route produces highly reactive heavy alkali-metal sulfide environments and successfully forms Cs-rich sulfides at 500 °C (CS3, SS1, TS1). Its uncertainty is pressure control during azide decomposition and formation of volatile P/S species [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2/F3 — Route 4, supercritical ammonia

Supercritical NH3 routes are kinetically attractive because SS5/TS5 and SS4/TS4 show fluid-mediated formation of Cs chalcogenides at relatively low temperatures. The severity score is reduced by NH3 pressure, sealed-tube/autoclave requirements, and multi-step loading/sealing protocols (SS4, SS5, TS4, TS5). The largest uncertainty is phase selectivity: SS5/TS5 report multiple Cs–Ag–M–S phases and contamination, so a simple Cs–P–S phase may still require careful optimization.

#### F4 — Amorphization tendency

The target has one amorphization trigger from the large Cs/P cation size and bonding mismatch [Reasonable Inference]. The target structure description indicates ordered PS4 tetrahedra and primarily corner-sharing Cs/S/P environments rather than multiple edge/face/vertex-sharing networks, lowering amorphization risk. Exact unit-cell atom count is not provided by CS/SS/TS, so the C2 check has insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS and is treated as non-triggering based on the small formula and simple Cm description [Reasonable Inference].

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

Cs3PS4 is likely a ground-state phase because the target has E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom, and many close Cs-rich sulfide analogues are also hull-stable and experimentally synthesized (CS1–CS4, SS1–SS5, TS1–TS5). The analogue support is strong for Cs–S frameworks and tetrahedral thiometalate/thiophosphate chemistry: CS4 supports cesium tetrathioanion solution crystallization, while SS2/TS2 directly supports PS4 tetrahedra in an alkali sulfide environment. The most critical enabling factor is the preformation or easy formation of PS4 tetrahedra, because the target P–S distances match known thiophosphate motifs (SS2, TS2). The most critical limiting factor is moisture/volatility control: PS4^3− and P2S5-derived mixtures are hydrolysis-sensitive, while sulfur/P-bearing reagents can volatilize in high-temperature sealed tubes [Reasonable Inference]. Overall, the target appears highly synthesizable, with the best practical route likely being dry metathesis/solvothermal crystallization followed by sealed-tube synthesis as a robust backup.
