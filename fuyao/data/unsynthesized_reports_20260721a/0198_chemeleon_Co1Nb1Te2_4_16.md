### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

#### F1 — Thermodynamic stability

The target is reported at E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom, which is a strong ground-state thermodynamic signal. Exact NbCoTe₂ analogues are also thermodynamically stable or essentially stable, with monoclinic NbCoTe₂ at E_hull = 0.0 eV/atom and orthorhombic NbCoTe₂ at ~0.00024 eV/atom (CS-1, CS-2, SS-1, SS-2, TS-1, TS-2). The remaining uncertainty is polymorph-specific: the target P3m1 polymorph is predicted stable, but the experimentally referenced polymorphs are Cmce/P2₁/c rather than P3m1 (CS-1, CS-2, SS-1, SS-2).

#### F2 — Route 1

Route 1 avoids high pressure and avoids temperatures above 1000 °C, since exact NbCoTe₂ is reported at 900–925 °C (CS-2, SS-2, TS-2). The main severity penalties are vacuum sealing, Te volatility management, and Te toxicity [Reasonable Inference], all consistent with the sealed evacuated-tube telluride protocols (CS-1, CS-2, SS-1, SS-2, TS-1, TS-2). CS/SS/TS provide strong route evidence; there is no insufficient-evidence issue for temperature, atmosphere, or precursor identity.

#### F3 — Route 1

Kinetics are limited by elemental Nb and Co powders, especially refractory Nb [Reasonable Inference], even though exact NbCoTe₂ formation at 900–925 °C shows that the chemistry is accessible (CS-2, SS-2, TS-2). The target’s P3m1 structure differs from reported NbCoTe₂ Cmce/P2₁/c analogues, so structural selection may require careful thermal history [Reasonable Inference] (CS-1, CS-2, SS-1, SS-2). TS supports thermodynamic accessibility but gives no unique kinetic mechanism beyond the duplicated synthesis evidence, so kinetic extrapolation from TS alone is limited.

#### F2 — Route 2

CVT/transport-assisted synthesis uses moderate temperatures but requires vacuum-sealed tubes, a transport agent, and often a two-zone furnace (CS-1, SS-5, TS-1, TS-4). TeCl₄ and TeBr₄ introduce toxicity/corrosivity/moisture sensitivity [Reasonable Inference], which increases severity relative to simple direct reaction. The source evidence supports CVT-like conditions for layered tellurides, but exact P3m1 NbCoTe₂ is not demonstrated by CS/SS/TS.

#### F3 — Route 2

Halide transport should improve mass transport compared with direct solid-state diffusion [Reasonable Inference], and transport agents are used in layered NbCoTe₂/NbMTe₂ and WTe₂ syntheses (CS-1, SS-5, TS-1, TS-4). This gives Route 2 a higher kinetic score than Route 1. The uncertainty is that transport growth may stabilize or select a known monoclinic/orthorhombic polymorph rather than the target trigonal P3m1 polymorph [Reasonable Inference] (CS-1, CS-2, SS-1, SS-2).

#### F2 — Route 3

Route 3 inherits vacuum, Te volatility, and Te toxicity penalties from sealed telluride synthesis (CS-2, SS-2, TS-2; SS-3, TS-3). It also receives a multistep penalty because NbIrTe₄ synthesis explicitly uses step heating, regrinding, and reheating (SS-3, TS-3). Its temperature can likely remain near the 900–925 °C NbCoTe₂ window rather than the full 1000 °C NbIrTe₄ condition [Reasonable Inference] (CS-2, SS-2, TS-2; SS-3, TS-3).

#### F3 — Route 3

The Te-rich/regrind strategy addresses slow diffusion and compositional inhomogeneity, as related NbIrTe₄ required excess Te and repeated annealing to obtain the desired phase (SS-3, TS-3). However, repeated processing signals that kinetics and phase control are not trivial (SS-3, TS-3). The method is best viewed as a troubleshooting/optimization route if direct reaction yields mixed NbCoTe₂ polymorphs [Reasonable Inference] (CS-1, CS-2, SS-1, SS-2).

#### F4 — Amorphization tendency

The target is a simple crystalline layered telluride rather than a large, complex framework, and exact NbCoTe₂ analogues have relatively compact Nb₄Co₄Te₈ descriptions (CS-1, CS-2, SS-1, SS-2). One amorphization-risk trigger is assigned for Nb/Co size mismatch [Reasonable Inference], while insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS prevents a firm assignment of mixed vertex/edge/face-sharing connectivity. The low-to-medium risk score is consistent with multiple crystalline layered telluride analogues being synthesized successfully (CS-1, CS-2, SS-1, SS-2, TS-1, TS-2).
