### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

#### F1: Thermodynamic Stability

The target E_hull is 0.0290 eV/atom, which is within the high-synthesizability ≤0.05 eV/atom range. This is consistent with thermodynamically similar halobismuth/tellurohalide phases near 0.018–0.029 eV/atom (TS-1; TS-2; TS-3) and structurally similar bismuth bromides near the hull (SS-3; SS-5). The exact BiBr3 analogue is reported at a higher E_hull of 0.0617 eV/atom (CS-1; SS-1; TS-4), so the target may be a lower-energy polymorph or may reflect computational polymorph uncertainty. Remaining uncertainty is mainly whether Pmma-BiBr3 is experimentally stable at room temperature or only metastable.

#### F2/F3 Route 1: Direct elemental halogenation

The condition severity is moderate because the exact route uses simple precursors Bi and Br2 in a closed Ar-filled glass tube and standard sublimation glassware, without high pressure or ultra-high temperature (CS-1; SS-1; TS-4). The main penalties are Br2 volatility/toxicity and sealed-tube requirements, which are implied by the use of a closed tube and Br2 precursor (CS-1; SS-1; TS-4). Kinetics should be favorable because Br2 is chemically reactive and the exact BiBr3 phase is directly obtained from the elements (CS-1; SS-1; TS-4). The uncertainty is polymorph selection, since the reported same-composition structure is monoclinic rather than Pmma (CS-1; SS-1; TS-4).

#### F2/F3 Route 2: Sublimation / vapor transport

This route has higher condition penalties because it requires vacuum-sealed ampoules, volatile halide transport, and usually multiple handling/purification/growth steps, as shown for metal trihalide and Bi–Te–Br vapor growth (CS-3; CS-5; SS-2; TS-1). Kinetically, it is attractive because BiBr3 is explicitly sublimable in the exact-composition reference (CS-1; SS-1; TS-4), and related trihalides grow crystals by slow sublimation (CS-3; CS-5). Composition control is simpler than multicomponent vapor transport because the source can be single-component BiBr3 [Reasonable Inference]. The uncertainty is whether vapor deposition favors the Pmma polymorph rather than the known monoclinic form.

#### F2/F3 Route 3: Annealing / polymorph conversion

The severity score is reduced by vacuum sealing, dry glove-box handling, volatile BiBr3, and multi-step purification/annealing, all supported by BiBr3-containing bromide ampoule syntheses (SS-3; SS-5; TS-2). Kinetically, this route is strong because the starting material already has the target composition and only needs rearrangement among BiBr3 polymorphs [Reasonable Inference]. The 523 K slow-cooling precedent for BiBr3-containing bromides supports moderate-temperature mobility and annealing feasibility (SS-3; SS-5; TS-2). The key uncertainty is that the target Pmma polymorph is not directly reported as an annealing product in CS/SS/TS.

#### F2/F3 Route 4: Dry-solution cooling crystallization

The low-temperature conditions are mild, and the closest structural analogue SbBr3 is crystallized by cooling a warm CS2 solution in a dry closed vessel (SS-4). The route is penalized for toxic/volatile solvent and dry handling needs, as explicitly noted for SbBr3 crystallization (SS-4). Kinetics are favorable because solution crystallization avoids solid-state diffusion [Reasonable Inference from SS-4]. However, CS and TS provide insufficient direct evidence for BiBr3 solution growth, and the SbBr3 analogue is 0D/molecular rather than the target’s 2D octahedral sheet, so polymorph transferability is uncertain (SS-4).

#### F4: Amorphization Tendency

The target has only one cation, so cation-size disorder is not a concern [Reasonable Inference from BiBr3 formula]. The structure is chemically simple, and no CS/SS/TS source indicates a large target cell; however, the exact atom count is not provided, so this check retains some uncertainty. Mixed corner/edge-sharing octahedral connectivity is present in the target and in related Bi–Br networks (SS-3; SS-5; TS-2), which slightly raises amorphization or disorder risk. Overall, the risk is low–medium rather than high because the chemistry is binary and near the hull.

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

The target Pmma-BiBr3 is likely a **near-ground-state or low-energy metastable polymorph**, because its E_hull is only 0.0290 eV/atom and comparable to other synthesized thermodynamic analogues (TS-1; TS-2; TS-3). The CS/SS/TS analogues strongly support making **BiBr3 as a compound**, especially because exact BiBr3 is synthesized directly from Bi and Br2 and purified by sublimation (CS-1; SS-1; TS-4). The most critical enabling factor is the high volatility/reactivity of bromide chemistry, which allows direct halogenation and sublimation-based purification/crystallization (CS-1; CS-3; CS-5). The most critical limiting factor is **polymorph control**, because the closest exact BiBr3 reference is monoclinic P2₁/c and notes phase/polymorph sensitivity rather than directly demonstrating the target Pmma phase (CS-1; SS-1; TS-4). Overall, synthesis is feasible, but isolating the specific orthorhombic Pmma polymorph may require temperature-gradient, annealing, or seeding optimization.
