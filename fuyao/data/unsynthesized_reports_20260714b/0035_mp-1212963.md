### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability.** The target E_hull of 0.0500 eV/atom sits exactly at the ≤0.05 eV/atom high-synthesizability threshold [Target]. The strongest analogues are experimentally crystallized despite similar positive hull energies, especially NH4NH3SO4 at ~0.0428 eV/atom and NH4H2AsO4 at ~0.0376 eV/atom [CS1, CS2, SS1, SS2, TS1, TS3]. The main uncertainty is that the target may be borderline metastable rather than a strict ground state [Target; Reasonable Inference].

**F2 — Route 1.** Slow evaporation has very mild condition severity because analogous ammonium acid oxyanion crystals grow from saturated aqueous solution without high temperature, pressure, or controlled atmosphere [CS1, CS3, SS1, SS5, TS3]. The only deduction is selenium precursor toxicity/oxidizing character, which is not covered by CS/SS/TS and is therefore a [Reasonable Inference] with neutral direct source evidence.

**F3 — Route 1.** Kinetics are favorable because the route is solution-mediated and avoids solid-state diffusion [CS1, CS3; Reasonable Inference]. Structural transfer is good but not perfect because PO4/AsO4 analogues share the acid oxyanion motif, while the target requires SeO4/HSeO4 proton ordering [Target, CS1, CS3; Reasonable Inference].

**F2 — Route 2.** Cooling crystallization has the strongest condition evidence: the closest Indium-like P-1 zero-dimensional sulfate analogue crystallizes after dissolution at 45 °C and cooling to room temperature or 7 °C [CS2, SS2, TS1]. No high pressure, high temperature, or special atmosphere is needed [CS2, SS2, TS1]. The selenium toxicity deduction remains a [Reasonable Inference].

**F3 — Route 2.** Kinetics are rated highest because soluble salt dissolution and cooling crystallization are directly demonstrated in the closest structural/thermodynamic analogue [CS2, SS2, TS1]. The target and analogue share Indium-like, triclinic P-1, zero-dimensional descriptions, so structural reconstruction should be minimal [Target, CS2, SS2, TS1]. The remaining uncertainty is precise proton/ammonium ordering in the selenate analogue [Target; Reasonable Inference].

**F2 — Route 3.** Precipitation/antisolvent crystallization remains mild in temperature and pressure because precipitation analogues use aqueous solutions, filtration, washing, or standing at low temperature [CS5, SS3, SS4, TS2, TS4]. It is penalized for being multi-step and for selenium precursor risk [Reasonable Inference]. CS5 also warns that some ammonium oxyanion precipitates can decompose or contain impurity phases [CS5].

**F3 — Route 3.** Precursor activity is good because precipitation analogues form crystals quickly from solution [CS5, SS3, TS4]. The kinetic risk is poorer ordering under rapid supersaturation, so recrystallization is likely needed for the target hydrogenselenate phase [CS5, SS4; Reasonable Inference]. This makes Route 3 less preferred than cooling crystallization [CS2, SS2, TS1].

**F4 — Amorphization Tendency.** Direct amorphization evidence is absent; insufficient evidence from CS, SS, and TS, so direct source signal is neutral 50. The target’s zero-dimensional cluster structure avoids mixed edge/face/vertex-sharing network frustration [Target], but the large structural contents trigger the >40 atom check [Target]. Readily crystallized molecular analogues support a low-to-medium amorphization risk rather than a high risk [CS1, CS2, CS3, SS1, SS2, TS1, TS3].

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

The target is likely a borderline metastable but accessible crystalline salt because its E_hull is 0.0500 eV/atom, exactly at the high-synthesizability cutoff [Target]. The CS/SS/TS evidence strongly supports solution synthesis because the closest Indium-like P-1 zero-dimensional sulfate analogue crystallizes by mild aqueous cooling [CS2, SS2, TS1], and related ammonium acid oxyanion salts crystallize by slow evaporation [CS1, CS3, SS1, SS5, TS3]. The most critical enabling factor is solution-mediated crystallization, which bypasses solid-state diffusion and allows hydrogen-bond/proton ordering at low temperature [CS2, SS2, TS1; Reasonable Inference]. The most critical limiting factor is control of NH4+/HSeO4−/H+ stoichiometry, because the target contains multiple protonated selenate and ammonium environments [Target; Reasonable Inference]. A secondary limiting factor is selenium oxyacid toxicity/oxidizing character, which is not addressed in CS/SS/TS and is therefore a practical extrapolation [Reasonable Inference].
