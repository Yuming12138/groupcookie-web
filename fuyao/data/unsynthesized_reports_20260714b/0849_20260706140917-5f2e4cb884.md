### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic stability.**  
The target is reported at E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom, so the direct thermodynamic score is very high. Similar hydride-containing rare-earth compounds are also on or near the hull: Eu₂H₃Br is at 0 eV/atom (CS2), Eu₂H₃Cl is ≈0.0049 eV/atom (CS1), LaH₃ is at 0 eV/atom (SS2/TS2), and La₂H₅ is ≈0.0003 eV/atom (SS1/TS1). The remaining uncertainty is that hull stability does not guarantee kinetic accessibility or correct N/H ordering [Reasonable Inference].

**F2 — Route 1.**  
The sealed-ampoule route inherits moderate severity from CS1 and CS4, which use 850–900 °C sealed tantalum ampoules for rare-earth hydride/halide chemistry. It avoids >1000 °C and high pressure, but requires inert/vacuum sealing and air-sensitive hydride/nitride handling [Reasonable Inference; CS1 uses glove boxes, Ta capsules, and silica ampoules]. The major uncertainty is H retention and whether LaN + LaH₃ reacts before disproportionation [Reasonable Inference].

**F3 — Route 1.**  
Kinetically, the route benefits from strong structural similarity to Eu₂H₃Cl in P-3m1 and La hydride polyhedral motifs in LaH₃/La₂H₅ (CS1; SS1/SS2; TS1/TS2). However, the reaction uses solid LaN/LaH₃-type powders, so diffusion and anion ordering may be slower than in a flux or metathesis process [Reasonable Inference]. CS1 and CS4 show successful solid-state synthesis but still require sealed high-temperature holds.

**F2 — Route 2.**  
The mechanochemical route has mild temperature but requires controlled Ar/H₂, pressure-capable milling, and air-sensitive reagents. SS3–SS5 and TS3–TS5 explicitly use high-energy ball milling under Ar and 1–15 bar H₂, with glovebox/Schlenk handling. The severity is reduced relative to high-temperature ampoule synthesis but increased by metathesis byproducts and precursor sensitivity [Reasonable Inference].

**F3 — Route 2.**  
Mechanochemical metathesis has high precursor activity because rare-earth chlorides and hydride reagents react at room temperature in analogous REAlH₆ syntheses (SS3–SS5; TS3–TS5). The main kinetic penalty is structural selectivity: REAlH₆ analogues form in low yield with NaCl/Al impurities and partial decomposition, indicating difficult phase control (SS3–SS5; TS3–TS5). For La₂NH₃, the nitride donor adds another anion-ordering requirement not directly demonstrated by CS/SS/TS [Reasonable Inference].

**F2 — Route 3.**  
The ammonolysis/hydrogenation route requires reactive NH₃/H₂ atmospheres, gas handling, and likely multiple steps, so it scores lower on severity [Reasonable Inference]. CS5 supports the feasibility of rare-earth hydride hydrogenation under H₂ up to 10 MPa, but it does not directly support NH₃ nitridation. Toxic/volatile NH₃ and H stoichiometry control are the limiting condition issues [Reasonable Inference].

**F3 — Route 3.**  
Gas-solid N/H exchange could be kinetically active, especially with fine LaHₓ powders [Reasonable Inference]. However, CS5 reports long hydrogenation times and multiple competing hydride phases, so phase selection is likely difficult. No CS/SS/TS example confirms that ammonolysis yields the ordered La₂NH₃ structure, making this route more speculative.

**F4 — Amorphization tendency.**  
The target contains one cation, La, so cation size mismatch does not promote amorphization [Reasonable Inference]. The target structure does contain mixed corner-, edge-, and face-sharing NLa₆/HLa₆/HLa₄ polyhedra, which triggers one amorphization-risk criterion from the target description. Overall amorphization risk is low–medium rather than high.

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

La₂NH₃ is likely a ground-state or very near-ground-state phase because the target E_hull is 0.0000 eV/atom, and several chemically or thermodynamically related rare-earth hydrides are also on or near the hull (CS1/CS2; SS1/SS2; TS1/TS2). The strongest analogue support is structural: Eu₂H₃Cl has the same P-3m1 rare-earth hydride framework topology (CS1), while LaH₃ and La₂H₅ provide chemically relevant La–H coordination motifs (SS1/SS2; TS1/TS2). The most critical enabling factor is the apparent thermodynamic stability of the La–H framework and the close match of HLa₄/HLa₆ polyhedra (SS1/SS2; TS1/TS2). The most critical limiting factor is controlled incorporation and ordering of nitride plus hydride anions, because no CS/SS/TS route directly demonstrates synthesis of a La nitride-hydride with this exact composition. The best practical route is mechanochemical metathesis followed by mild annealing, because it maximizes precursor activity while limiting hydrogen loss [Reasonable Inference].
