### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

#### F1 — Thermodynamic Stability

The target has E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom, so it is predicted to be a ground-state phase. This is supported by several stable analogues: Ta₃In₂Cl₉ is on the hull (CS-1, SS-3, TS-3), Nb₃TeCl₇ is on the hull (SS-4, TS-4), and Cs₂PbTa₆Cl₁₈ is on the hull (SS-2, TS-2). The closest Ta₃QX₇ analogue, Ta₃SBr₇, is slightly metastable at only 0.0049 eV/atom but experimentally accessible (CS-2, SS-1, TS-1). Remaining uncertainty is that no direct Ta-Se-Cl experimental synthesis is provided; direct evidence from CS/SS/TS for Ta₃SeCl₇ itself is insufficient.

#### F2 — Route 1

Route 1 uses moderate high temperature, sealed ampoule handling, and air-sensitive TaCl₅. The temperature is supported by Nb₃TeCl₇ at 1090 K for one week (SS-4, TS-4) and Ta chloride cluster phases at 750–800 °C for several days (CS-1, SS-3, TS-3). The main deductions arise from dry/inert handling and volatile TaCl₅/Se in a sealed ampoule. There is no evidence from CS/SS/TS that high pressure or >1000 °C treatment is required.

#### F3 — Route 1

Kinetics are moderately favorable because TaCl₅/NbCl₅-type halides provide reactive chloride transport, as shown by Nb₃TeCl₇ and Ta chloride analogues (SS-4, TS-4; CS-1). The limiting kinetic issue is refractory Ta metal, which can react slowly [Reasonable Inference]. The structural reconstruction burden is reduced because Nb₃TeCl₇ has the same M₃QCl₇ layered motif (SS-4, TS-4). Remaining uncertainty is whether Se gives the same high-yield window as Te.

#### F2 — Route 2

Route 2 is more severe because it involves direct halogen use, likely Cl₂ or a volatile chlorine donor [Reasonable Inference]. Ta₃SBr₇ synthesis from Ta + S + Br₂ requires evacuated ampoules, long reaction time, purification, and further crystal-growth processing (CS-2, SS-1, TS-1). Although the temperature can be lower than Route 1, the sealed volatile halogen chemistry and toxic/corrosive precursor handling justify a lower F2 score. There is insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS that direct Cl₂ chemistry has been demonstrated for Ta₃SeCl₇.

#### F3 — Route 2

The elemental route starts from refractory Ta, so precursor activity and diffusion are less favorable. Ta₃SBr₇ formation at 550 °C proves that a Ta₃QX₇-like sheet can form from elements under halogen transport conditions (CS-2, SS-1, TS-1). However, the same source reports mixtures, TaBr₅ byproduct, and small crystal yields, indicating difficult phase control and slow kinetics (CS-2, SS-1, TS-1). Chlorine may be more reactive than bromine, but this is an inference rather than direct evidence.

#### F2 — Route 3

CVT/gradient growth avoids very high temperatures but requires pre-synthesis, transport agent loading, sealed evacuated ampoules, and long gradients. Ta₃SBr₇ required a 505–495 °C gradient for 6 weeks for crystal growth (CS-2, SS-1, TS-1), and TaCl₄ crystals can be produced from Ta + TaCl₅ in sealed evacuated silica at 623 K (SS-5). The volatile and air-sensitive transport chemistry drives the severity score down. High pressure is not supported or required by CS/SS/TS.

#### F3 — Route 3

Kinetics are better than direct elemental synthesis if preformed Ta₃SeCl₇ is used, because vapor transport redistributes an already-formed phase [Reasonable Inference]. Ta chloride volatility is supported by TaCl₄ CVT from Ta + TaCl₅ (SS-5). Crystal growth may still be slow: Ta₃SBr₇ required 6 weeks in a temperature gradient and yielded small crystals (CS-2, SS-1, TS-1). Composition control remains uncertain because excess TaCl₅ may create competing Ta chloride phases [Reasonable Inference supported by impurity issues in SS-1 and SS-2/TS-2].

#### F4 — Amorphization Tendency

The target has only one cation, so cation-radius mismatch is not a concern; CS/SS/TS do not provide a contrary signal. The formula unit contains 11 atoms, and no evidence indicates a >40-atom unit cell; therefore C2 is not triggered. The target description specifies edge-sharing TaSeCl₅ octahedra, not mixed vertex/edge/face-sharing coexistence, so C3 is not triggered. Remaining uncertainty is crystallite disorder or stacking faults in the 2D layered structure, which is not captured by the F4 checks.

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

Ta₃SeCl₇ is likely a true ground-state or near-ground-state phase because its calculated energy above hull is 0.0000 eV/atom. The analogues support synthesis well: Nb₃TeCl₇ is a very close M₃QCl₇ layered chloride analogue synthesized in quantitative yield from NbCl₅ + Nb + Te (SS-4, TS-4), and Ta₃SBr₇ demonstrates that Ta₃QX₇ sheets are experimentally accessible (CS-2, SS-1, TS-1). The most important enabling factor is the availability of volatile/reactive TaCl₅ chemistry, which can overcome the low diffusivity of refractory Ta metal. The main limiting factor is stoichiometry and volatility control for both chlorine and selenium in sealed ampoules. Overall, the target should be synthesizable in a competent solid-state/halide chemistry laboratory, with Route 1 as the preferred bulk route and Route 3 as a follow-up for crystal growth.
