### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability.** The target E_hull is 0.0500 eV/atom, exactly at the high-score threshold, so it is near-hull but not unambiguously a ground-state phase [Target]. CS analogues include stable or very low-E_hull hydroxide/oxyhydroxide phases, which supports the chemical viability of hydrated metal oxyhydroxides [CS]. TS analogues near ~0.055–0.075 eV/atom are synthesized but often require specialized conditions, so the thermodynamic signal is favorable but not risk-free [TS].

**F2 — Route 1.** Hydrothermal conditions are mild in temperature relative to solid-state oxide synthesis, with GaOOH at 448 K and Fe hydrated sulfate at 140 °C [CS-1, SS-3]. The only deduction is for multiple operational steps, while there is no GPa pressure, >1000 °C firing, or required toxic/air-sensitive precursor in the recommended version [CS-1, SS-3, Reasonable Inference]. Insufficient evidence from TS for hydrothermal route severity is treated as neutral 50 rather than negative [TS].

**F3 — Route 1.** Aqueous Fe salts and base provide high precursor activity, and hydrothermal crystallization reduces solid-state diffusion limitations [CS-1, SS-3, Reasonable Inference]. Structural reconstruction is moderate-low because the target FeO6 corner/edge-sharing network resembles the GaOOH MOOH motif and Fe-containing hydrothermal octahedral analogues [Target, CS-1, SS-3]. The remaining uncertainty is exact P2_12_12_1 polymorph selection rather than formation of FeOOH chemistry itself [Target, Reasonable Inference].

**F2 — Route 2.** Precipitation analogues are very mild, with Ni(OH)2 at room temperature and Co(OH)2 from aqueous nitrate/KOH chemistry [CS-2, CS-3, CS-4]. The deduction is for precipitation plus aging plus drying steps, not for temperature, pressure, or specialized equipment [CS-2, CS-5, Reasonable Inference]. Insufficient evidence from SS/TS for direct FeOOH precipitation is treated as neutral 50 [SS, TS].

**F3 — Route 2.** Precursor activity and ion mobility are excellent because precipitation occurs from aqueous ions [CS-2, CS-3, CS-4]. The structural-reconstruction score is lower than Route 1 because precipitation can produce poorly crystalline or competing FeOOH polymorphs, and no source directly proves the target P2_12_12_1 FeOOH by this route [CS, Target, Reasonable Inference]. Aging, seeding, and mild heating mitigate this uncertainty [CS-5, Reasonable Inference].

**F2 — Route 3.** HP analogues show that Fe-containing oxide polymorphs may be accessible under 7–15 GPa, but this imposes a large condition-severity penalty [SS-2, TS-3, TS-4, TS-5]. The proposed hydrous adaptation avoids >1000 °C to preserve OH, but still requires sealed capsules, multiple steps, and specialized apparatus [TS-3, TS-4, Reasonable Inference]. Insufficient evidence from CS for high-pressure FeOOH is treated as neutral 50 [CS].

**F3 — Route 3.** Dense Fe oxide or FeOOH precursors are less reactive than solution precursors, so kinetics are slower than Routes 1–2 [SS-2, Reasonable Inference]. HP analogues demonstrate FeO6 network stabilization but also show narrow phase windows and impurity risks, lowering structural-reconstruction confidence [SS-2, TS-3, TS-4, TS-5]. This route is best considered a backup if ambient/hydrothermal synthesis fails [SS-2, TS-3, Reasonable Inference].

**F4 — Amorphization Tendency.** The target has a single Fe cation, so cation-radius mismatch does not trigger amorphization risk [Target, Reasonable Inference]. The target structure uses FeO6 octahedra with corner and edge sharing but no reported face-sharing, so the strict mixed vertex+edge+face criterion is not triggered [Target]. Exact unit-cell atom count is not provided by CS/SS/TS, so that source-specific signal is neutral 50, while the target description supports a low-complexity FeOOH cell [CS, SS, TS, Target, Reasonable Inference].

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

The target is best viewed as a near-hull metastable FeOOH polymorph rather than a guaranteed ground-state phase because E_hull = 0.0500 eV/atom [Target]. CS data strongly supports low-temperature synthesis of chemically similar hydroxide/oxyhydroxide phases, especially hydrothermal GaOOH and precipitation-derived transition-metal hydroxides [CS]. SS data adds Fe-specific support for hydrothermal hydrous Fe frameworks and FeO6 structural motifs, while TS data mainly warns that some near-hull Fe oxides require specialized routes [SS, TS]. The most critical enabling factor is solution-mediated Fe3+ hydrolysis/crystallization, which avoids high-temperature dehydration and long-range diffusion [CS-1, SS-3, Reasonable Inference]. The most critical limiting factor is polymorph selectivity, because the exact P2_12_12_1 FeOOH phase is not directly demonstrated in the provided references [Target, CS, SS, TS].
