**Data caveats:** CS1 is structurally/chemically useful for sealed-tube practice, excess chalcogen, and liner choice, but its synthesis snippet describes a sulfide despite the listed selenide formula, so I do not use it for exact Tl–Si–Se stoichiometry [CS1]. SS5/TS3 support melt-growth conditions, but their precursor lists appear mismatched to the listed Tl–Pb/Sn–Se formulas, so I use their evidence snippets for ampoule/melt-growth conditions only [SS5; TS3].

### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability:** The target is at E_hull = 0.0500 eV/atom, which sits exactly on the ≤0.05 eV/atom high-score boundary [Target]. CS analogues are all reported as ground-state hull phases, SS analogues include stable Tl2SiSe3/Tl2GeSe3, and TS analogues show experimentally referenced phases can exist near 0.04–0.08 eV/atom [CS1–CS5; SS1–SS2; TS1–TS5]. The remaining uncertainty is that the target is not computed at 0.0 eV/atom, so it is better viewed as a low-energy metastable candidate rather than an unambiguous ground state [Target].

**F2/F3 — Route 1:** The direct elemental route is well supported by Tl–Si–Se/Tl–Ge–Se analogues synthesized from elements in evacuated ampoules at moderate temperatures [CS2; CS4; SS1; SS2]. Its condition score is penalized by vacuum sealing, volatile elemental Se/Tl containment, multi-step thermal treatment, and Tl/Se toxicity [CS2; SS1; TS3; Reasonable Inference]. Kinetics are moderate because liquid/vapor chalcogen chemistry helps reaction below Si’s melting point, but the target’s complex Tl-rich C2/c ordering likely requires long annealing [Target; CS2; SS1; Reasonable Inference].

**F2/F3 — Route 2:** The binary precursor route scores best because CS3 provides a direct Tl4GeS4-style binary chalcogenide pathway, and the target maps cleanly as 2 Tl2Se + SiSe2 → Tl4SiSe4 [CS3; Reasonable Inference]. It avoids free elemental Se/Tl during most of the reaction, so the condition severity is lower than Route 1, although vacuum sealing and toxic/moisture-sensitive chalcogenides still penalize the route [CS3; Reasonable Inference]. The main uncertainty is that CS3 is a sulfide/germanium analogue rather than the exact selenosilicate target [CS3; Target].

**F2/F3 — Route 3:** Melt growth is supported by Tl-rich selenide analogues grown from molten stoichiometric mixtures and by a Si-containing Tl–Hg–Si–Se ampoule synthesis [SS5; TS3; SS4]. The route benefits kinetically from a liquid phase, but the condition score is reduced by sealed ampoules, volatile Tl/Se chemistry, and possible air-sensitive products [SS5; TS3; Reasonable Inference]. The main uncertainty is whether Tl4SiSe4 melts congruently or crystallizes before competing Tl–Si–Se phases form [Reasonable Inference from SS5/TS3 and Target].

**F2/F3 — Route 4:** CVT is supported only thermodynamically/operationally by TS analogues using iodine gradients at 800–900 °C, while CS and SS provide insufficient evidence for Tl–Si–Se CVT and are neutral 50 [TS1; TS5; insufficient evidence from CS; insufficient evidence from SS]. Its condition score is similar to other sealed-tube routes, but the kinetic score is lower because Si transport, Tl/Se fractionation, and iodine side chemistry are uncertain [TS1; TS5; Reasonable Inference]. This route is better for crystal growth after bulk phase formation than for initial synthesis [TS1; TS5; Reasonable Inference].

**F4 — Amorphization Tendency:** The radius/charge contrast between Tl and tetrahedral Si and the likely large C2/c cell trigger medium amorphization/ordering risk [Target; Reasonable Inference]. The target description does not prove simultaneous vertex-, edge-, and face-sharing connectivity, and CS/SS/TS give insufficient direct amorphization evidence, so each source category is neutral 50 for C3 [insufficient evidence from CS; insufficient evidence from SS; insufficient evidence from TS]. The F4 score is therefore medium rather than high [Target; Reasonable Inference].

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

Tl4SiSe4 is likely a low-energy metastable or borderline ground-state-accessible phase because its E_hull is exactly 0.0500 eV/atom rather than 0.0 eV/atom [Target]. The CS and SS analogues strongly support synthesis because Tl2SiSe3/Tl2GeSe3 are known from sealed-ampoule routes and Tl4GeS4 provides a close Tl4MCh4 stoichiometric template [CS2; SS1; CS3]. The most critical enabling factor is the availability of sealed-ampoule chalcogenide chemistry with slow cooling/annealing, which is repeatedly successful across Tl–Si/Ge–Se/S analogues [CS2; SS1; CS3]. The main limiting factor is control of volatile/toxic Tl–Se species and slow ordering into the complex monoclinic C2/c structure [Target; TS3; Reasonable Inference]. Overall, the target is reasonably synthesizable, with the binary Tl2Se + SiSe2 annealing route being the most defensible starting point [CS3; CS2; SS1; Reasonable Inference].
