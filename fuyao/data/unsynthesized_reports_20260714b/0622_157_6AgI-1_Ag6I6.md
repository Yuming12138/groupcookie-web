### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

#### F1: Thermodynamic stability

The target E_hull is 0.0260 eV/atom, which falls in the ≤0.05 eV/atom range assigned 90–100. TS strongly supports accessibility of such low-above-hull iodides: AgTl₂I₃ has E_hull = 0.0272 eV/atom and was synthesized hydrothermally, while CuHgSI and CuHgSBr at similar E_hull values were synthesized by sealed-tube or hydrothermal routes. CS supports that layered iodides such as CdI₂ polytypes can be near the hull and experimentally accessible, but CS does not directly prove binary AgI P3m1 stability. SS provides mixed evidence: some Ag–I analogues are on-hull, but Ag₃SI polymorphs above the hull show that metastable Ag–I phases may require careful phase control.

#### F2: Route 1 condition severity

Route 1 uses standard sealed-tube conditions, supported by CS CuSe/ZnSe and SS AgI-containing solid-state syntheses. The severity score is reduced because vacuum sealing is required, iodine/AgI volatility must be managed, and multiple grind/anneal/cool steps may be needed, as seen in SS Ag₃SI. The temperature is not severe relative to oxide solid-state synthesis, and no high pressure is needed; insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS suggests any need for >1000 °C conditions for binary AgI.

#### F3: Route 1 kinetics

Kinetically, AgI is a favorable precursor because SS Ag₂HgSI₂, Ag₃SI, and AgHgSI all use AgI successfully in solid-state reactions. Ag mobility in iodides is expected to be high by [Reasonable Inference], and SS examples show Ag–I analogues forming at 120–473 K to 973 K. The main kinetic uncertainty is structural reconstruction into the specific 2D P3m1 sheet; CS CdI₂ and SS Ag₃SI indicate polytype and phase-selection problems, so the structural-reconstruction score is only moderate.

#### F2: Route 2 condition severity

Route 2 is mild in temperature, directly supported by SS/TS AgTl₂I₃ at 155–175 °C in aqueous HI. It avoids vacuum and high temperature, but uses corrosive/toxic HI in a sealed pressure vessel, so deductions are applied for sealed volatile medium, toxicity, and multistep hydrothermal handling. Insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS indicates any need for high pressure >5 GPa or reactive gas atmospheres.

#### F3: Route 2 kinetics

Hydrothermal solution transport gives the best kinetic score because it bypasses slow solid-state diffusion; SS/TS AgTl₂I₃ demonstrates that AgI can participate in HI-mediated crystallization at low temperature. High iodide activity should enhance AgI dissolution and recrystallization by [Reasonable Inference]. The uncertainty is crystal quality and polymorph selectivity: SS/TS AgTl₂I₃ reports twinning and low untwinned yield, and CS CdI₂ shows layered iodides may form multiple polytypes.

#### F2: Route 3 condition severity

Route 3 requires sealed ampoules, vacuum or inert handling, and careful thermal gradients, consistent with CS CdI₂ vapor growth, SS Ag₃SI Bridgman growth, and TS Bridgman iodides. Although temperatures remain below 1000 °C, volatile iodine/AgI species and ampoule pressure are significant hazards. Insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS indicates any need for high-pressure synthesis, so no high-pressure deduction is applied.

#### F3: Route 3 kinetics

Vapor/melt growth benefits from direct transport of AgI and possible crystal growth, supported by CS CdI₂ vapor-grown layered iodides and SS/TS Bridgman iodide examples. However, the target binary P3m1 AgI phase is not directly demonstrated by any CS/SS/TS vapor route. Composition control is likely sensitive because iodine chemical potential and cooling rate can select different AgI polymorphs; this concern is reinforced by CS CdI₂ polytypes and SS Ag₃SI phase mixtures.

#### F4: Amorphization tendency

The target has only one cation, Ag, so there is no cation-radius mismatch trigger; this is based on the target composition, while CS/SS/TS provide insufficient evidence for cation disorder in binary AgI. The described structure is a small AgI sheet with two inequivalent Ag and two inequivalent I sites, not a >40-atom complex cell. The target connectivity is primarily corner-sharing tetrahedral/trigonal coordination, with no reported coexistence of vertex-, edge-, and face-sharing networks, so amorphization tendency is low.

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

The target AgI P3m1 phase is likely a low-energy metastable phase rather than an unequivocal ground state, because its E_hull is 0.0260 eV/atom rather than 0.0. The CS/SS/TS analogues support synthesis reasonably well: TS shows iodides with nearly identical E_hull values can be synthesized, SS shows AgI-containing compounds form under mild sealed or hydrothermal conditions, and CS shows layered iodide polytypes are experimentally accessible. The most critical enabling factor is the high mobility and solution/vapor processability of iodides, especially under HI hydrothermal conditions supported by SS/TS AgTl₂I₃. The most critical limiting factor is polymorph control, since CS CdI₂ and SS Ag₃SI show polytype intergrowth, phase mixtures, twinning, and long annealing requirements. Overall, hydrothermal iodide-complex recrystallization is the most plausible first route, with sealed-tube annealing and vapor growth as backup polymorph-screening methods.
