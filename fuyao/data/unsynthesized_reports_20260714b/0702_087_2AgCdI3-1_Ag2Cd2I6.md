### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic stability.**  
The target E_hull is 0.0280 eV/atom, which falls in the high-stability range specified by the prompt. This is close to AgTl₂I₃ at 0.0272 eV/atom (TS-2) and TlCdI₃ at 0.0167 eV/atom (CS-1; TS-1), so thermodynamic analogues support accessibility. The remaining uncertainty is that E_hull near 30 meV/atom may still indicate metastability relative to AgI/CdI₂ decomposition, but it is within the commonly synthesizable metastability window [Reasonable Inference].

**F2/F3 — Route 1.**  
The mild conditions are strongly supported by CdI₂ analogues grown by room-temperature evaporation from aqueous solution (CS-4; SS-1; SS-4), so severity is low except for Cd toxicity. Kinetics are favorable because the process is solution-mediated rather than diffusion-limited, but SS data alone gives insufficient evidence for Ag/Cd ordering in AgCdI₃. The main uncertainty is whether Ag remains soluble long enough to co-crystallize rather than precipitating AgI [Reasonable Inference from AgI chemistry and need for HI in CS-2/TS-2].

**F2/F3 — Route 2.**  
Hydrothermal HI synthesis is directly supported for Ag-containing iodide systems by CS-2 and TS-2, with temperatures of 155–220 °C and day-scale holds. The route has moderate severity due to sealed acidic HI and toxic Cd/Ag iodide handling. Kinetics should be good due to solution transport, but CS-2 reports multiphase/intergrown products and TS-2 reports twinning/low untwinned yield, so phase control is a significant uncertainty.

**F2/F3 — Route 3.**  
Bridgman/melt growth is supported by the chemically/thermodynamically similar TlCdI₃ analogue (CS-1; TS-1) and by mixed iodide melt/annealing procedures in TS-4. The route is penalized for sealed ampoule/vacuum or Ar handling, volatile iodides, and multistep crystal growth. Kinetics are good in the melt, but structural transferability is imperfect because CS-1 is a 1D orthorhombic TlCdI₃ rather than the target trigonal 2D AgCdI₃.

**F2/F3 — Route 4.**  
Sealed-tube reaction is supported by TS-3 and TS-4, where related heavy-metal iodide/chalcogenide systems form at moderate temperatures over long times. Severity is moderate because evacuated sealed tubes and toxic/volatile iodides are involved. Kinetics are less favorable than in solution or melt, and TS-4 shows that repeated annealing may be needed, implying slow ordering or competing phase issues.

**F2/F3 — Route 5.**  
Silica-gel growth is supported for a CdI₂ structural analogue at room temperature under ordinary conditions (SS-5), giving very mild severity. Kinetics are favorable through diffusion, but the gel route has insufficient direct evidence from SS for incorporation of Ag into the CdI₂-like structure. The likely difficulty is avoiding immediate AgI precipitation while maintaining the required Ag:Cd:I stoichiometry [Reasonable Inference].

**F4 — Amorphization tendency.**  
The target is a small, crystalline layered iodide with only corner-sharing Ag/Cd contacts and edge-sharing CdI₆ octahedra described; no face-sharing coexistence is reported in the target structure. CdI₂ analogues form crystalline layered materials readily by evaporation (CS-3–CS-5; SS-1–SS-4), supporting low amorphization risk. The uncertainty is that mixed Ag/Cd ordering could introduce stacking disorder, consistent with polytypism and stacking faults in CdI₂ analogues (CS-3–CS-5; SS-3–SS-5).

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

AgCdI₃ is likely a **near-ground-state or mildly metastable** phase because its E_hull is only 0.0280 eV/atom, comparable to synthesizable thermodynamic analogues (TS-1; TS-2). The CS/SS/TS data strongly support formation of Cd–I layered frameworks by mild solution growth (CS-3–CS-5; SS-1–SS-4) and support Ag iodide chemistry in HI/hydrothermal media (CS-2; TS-2). The most important enabling factor is the structural match between the target CdI₆ octahedral sheet and the many CdI₂ layered analogues with similar Cd–I distances (CS-3–CS-5; SS-1–SS-4). The most important limiting factor is phase selectivity: CdI₂ polytypes, AgI, or Ag/Cd disorder may compete, and similar systems show polytypism, twinning, intergrowth, or multiphase products (CS-2; SS-3–SS-5; TS-2). Overall, synthesis is feasible, with the best initial attempt being iodide-rich slow solution crystallization followed by hydrothermal HI optimization if Ag incorporation is poor.
