## SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

### F1 — Thermodynamic Stability

The target E_hull of 0.0500 eV/atom lies at the boundary of the highest scoring bin, so the score is high but not maximal. [Target] The direct CaMn₂O₄ analogue is reported with E_hull = 0.0, while thermodynamically similar Mn oxides near ~0.05–0.06 eV/atom are reported as experimentally accessible, supporting practical synthesizability. [CS1, SS1, TS1, TS3, TS4] The conflict is that the target entry is slightly metastable while the exact analogue entry is ground-state; I weight the direct synthesis evidence more heavily for feasibility, but keep uncertainty in the score. [CS1, SS1, TS1]

### F2 — Condition Severity

Route 1 scores 80 because it requires high temperature above 1000 °C and multiple regrind/fire cycles, but only standard furnace equipment and no special atmosphere or pressure are reported. [CS1, SS1] Route 2 scores 90 because the related Ca–Mn–O air sintering route uses only 800 °C and ambient pressure, but still requires repeated regrinding. [SS4] Route 3 scores 55 because high-pressure analogues require 4.5–6 GPa-class equipment, capsules, and controlled quench/release operations. [CS3, SS2, TS2, TS4] Route 4 scores 75 because hydrothermal temperatures are lower, but sealed pressure operation and possible high oxygen pressure increase severity. [CS2, CS5, TS5]

### F3 — Reaction Kinetics

Route 1 has moderate kinetics because CaCO₃/MnO₂ powders are not highly reactive, and the direct report requires repeated 20 h firings, indicating slow diffusion or phase completion. [CS1, SS1] Route 2 has weaker kinetics because lower temperature improves condition severity but may slow cation diffusion and is not directly demonstrated for CaMn₂O₄. [SS4, CS1] Route 3 has moderate kinetics under high temperature and pressure, but it risks forming other dense Mn oxide frameworks rather than the target Pbcm CaMn₂O₄. [CS3, SS2, TS2] Route 4 benefits from solution/mineralizer transport, but the exact target phase is not demonstrated hydrothermally, so structural reconstruction is the largest uncertainty. [CS2, CS5, TS5]

### F4 — Amorphization Tendency

The main amorphization risk is the large Ca/Mn cation-size contrast, because Ca is high-coordinate and Mn is octahedral in the target-like structure. [CS1, SS1, Reasonable Inference] The unit cell is not large enough to trigger the >40 atom-count penalty, and the strict vertex+edge+face-sharing criterion is not triggered because face sharing is not reported for Pbcm CaMn₂O₄. [CS1, SS1] Remaining uncertainty is moderate because distorted MnO₆ octahedra and mixed corner/edge sharing can still slow crystallization even without the formal C3 trigger. [CS1, SS1]
