### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

#### F1 — Thermodynamic stability

The target has E_hull = 0.0400 eV/atom, placing it in the ≤0.05 eV/atom range, so it receives a high stability score. Thermodynamically similar materials near this range include Cu₂As₄S₃Cl₂ at 0.0286 eV/atom (TS1), MnIn₂Se₄ at 0.0316 eV/atom (TS2), and Cu₃SbSe₃ at 0.0346 eV/atom (TS3), all of which have reported syntheses. The remaining uncertainty is that 0.040 eV/atom suggests possible metastability rather than a guaranteed ground state, so kinetic trapping or careful chemical potential control may matter [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2 — Route 1

Route 1 benefits from ambient-temperature synthesis, directly supported by Cu₂As₄S₃Cl₂ solution diffusion at ambient temperature for 12 h (CS3/SS4/TS1). Deductions arise because the analogue required N₂ atmosphere, Schlenk/diffusion-tube handling, and specialized As₄S₃·M(CO)₅ precursors (CS3/SS4/TS1). The main uncertainty is whether Ag/I reagents can be kept soluble long enough to assemble the framework rather than precipitating AgI [Reasonable Inference].

#### F3 — Route 1

The kinetics are favorable because solution diffusion provides high precursor activity and avoids long-range solid diffusion, as shown by ambient-temperature crystallization of the Cu analogue (CS3/SS4/TS1). Structural reconstruction is also favorable because the target’s As₄S₃ sheet motif closely matches the Cu₂As₄S₃Cl₂ framework (CS3/SS4/TS1). The score is not higher because Ag⁺/I⁻ chemistry differs from Cu⁺/Cl⁻ chemistry, and AgI solubility may limit nucleation control [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2 — Route 2

The sealed-ampoule route uses moderate temperatures similar to 400–430 °C solid-state chalcohalide syntheses (CS1/CS2) and 400–600 °C chalcogenide-halide annealing (CS4). It is penalized for vacuum sealing, volatile/toxic As–S–I components, and multiple steps including grinding, sealing, annealing, and possibly regrinding (CS1/CS2/CS4; Reasonable Inference). The uncertainty is phase selectivity, because the closest same-framework analogue was not made by bulk solid-state reaction (CS3/SS4/TS1).

#### F3 — Route 2

Diffusion is manageable because no refractory cations are present and related chalcohalides form at moderate temperature in evacuated tubes (CS1/CS2/CS4). However, converting AgI + As₄S₃ into a specific 2D sheet likely requires substantial rearrangement, and related solid-state reactions require multi-day to multi-week annealing (CS1/CS2/CS4). The route is therefore kinetically plausible but less efficient than solution assembly.

#### F2 — Route 3

Hydrothermal synthesis is moderate in temperature because AgSe₃I was made at 220 °C for 5 days in concentrated HI (SS3). The route is penalized for sealed-vessel acid pressure, multi-step loading/heating/cooling/separation, and hazardous HI/arsenic sulfide handling (SS3; Reasonable Inference). There is insufficient evidence from CS/TS for hydrothermal Ag–As–S–I specifically, so the score remains cautious.

#### F3 — Route 3

Solution-mediated hydrothermal transport can overcome diffusion limitations, and Ag–I–chalcogen crystals are directly supported by AgSe₃I hydrothermal formation (SS3). The weakness is structural reconstruction: SS3 supports Ag–I–chalcogen chemistry but not preservation of an As₄S₃ cluster sheet under strongly acidic HI. The route may be excellent for crystal growth if the target is stable in HI, but this stability is uncertain [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2 — Route 4

CVT uses standard sealed silica ampoules and high vacuum, as shown for MnIn₂Se₄ with AlCl₃ transport (TS2). It is penalized for vacuum, volatile halide transport agents, multistep feed preparation/transport, and toxic As-containing material [Reasonable Inference from TS2]. Direct support for Ag–As–S–I CVT is insufficient from CS/SS/TS, so this route is treated as a secondary recrystallization option.

#### F3 — Route 4

Transport kinetics can be good because vapor transport reduces reliance on bulk diffusion, as demonstrated in TS2. However, the target contains volatile iodide and arsenic sulfide components, so the vapor phase may decompose or fractionate the material [Reasonable Inference]. Structural reconstruction is uncertain because the closest same-framework analogue used solution diffusion rather than CVT (CS3/SS4/TS1).

#### F4 — Amorphization tendency

The target has a large effective cation-size/coordination mismatch between Ag⁺ and As-centered units, so one amorphization-risk trigger is assigned [Reasonable Inference]. The target formula has only 11 atoms per formula unit, and no evidence is provided that the crystallographic cell exceeds 40 atoms; insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS supports triggering C2. The target description does not explicitly show simultaneous vertex-, edge-, and face-sharing polyhedral connectivity, so C3 is not triggered; uncertainty remains because related Cu₂As₄S₃Cl₂ includes edge/corner-sharing tetrahedra (CS3/SS4/TS1).

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

Ag₂As₄S₃I₂ is likely a mildly metastable but accessible phase because its E_hull is 0.040 eV/atom, below the common ~0.05 eV/atom practical metastability threshold. The strongest support comes from Cu₂As₄S₃Cl₂, which has nearly the same As₄S₃-based 2D framework and is synthesized by solution diffusion (CS3/SS4/TS1). Additional support comes from AgSe₃I, which confirms that Ag–I–chalcogen coordination can crystallize hydrothermally (SS3), and from several sealed-tube chalcohalides made at moderate temperature (CS1/CS2/CS4). The key enabling factor is the preorganized As₄S₃ cluster precursor used in the closest analogue, which can lower reconstruction barriers (CS3/SS4/TS1). The key limiting factor is AgI chemistry: AgI is poorly soluble and may form a competing binary phase unless iodide activity, concentration, and diffusion rate are carefully controlled [Reasonable Inference].

| 1 | All | Arsenic-containing precursors are toxic and require controlled handling. | High | Use sealed tubes or Schlenk/glovebox-style handling; related syntheses loaded reactants in drybox or sealed ampoules (CS1/CS2/CS3/SS4/TS1). |
