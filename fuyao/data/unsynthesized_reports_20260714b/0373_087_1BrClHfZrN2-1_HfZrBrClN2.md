### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic stability.**  
The target E_hull of 0.0090 eV/atom places it well within the highly synthesizable thermodynamic window, so F1 is high. This is reinforced by structurally and thermodynamically close analogues: α-HfNBr is on the hull (SS1; TS3), β-HfNBr is only ≈0.0044 eV/atom above hull (SS2; TS1), ZrNCl is essentially on the hull (SS5), and Zr2N2S/Hf2N2S are low-energy metastable phases (SS4; TS2; TS4). CS analogues are also stable or near-stable, but because they are lanthanide sulfide nitride chlorides rather than Hf/Zr nitride halides, CS gives only general support.

**F2/F3 — Route 1 direct sealed-tube route.**  
The severity is moderate because HfNBr forms at 760 °C in a fused silica tube from Hf + NH4Br (SS1; TS3), but volatile NH4X and sealed-tube conditions impose deductions. Kinetics are less favorable because Hf/Zr metals are refractory and cation ordering may be slow [Reasonable Inference], although the HfNBr structural match is excellent (SS1; TS3). The largest uncertainty is whether Zr–Cl can be incorporated in the same α-like Pmm2 sheet rather than segregating to ZrNCl or ZrNBr-like domains.

**F2/F3 — Route 2 ammonolysis route.**  
CS2 directly supports low-temperature conversion of metal tetrahalides with NH3 into MNX nitride halides, so the kinetic score is relatively high. The route is penalized for NH3, volatile/moisture-sensitive tetrahalides, and purification/sublimation requirements (CS2). The remaining uncertainty is structural: CS2 supports MNX formation chemically, but SS/TS provide insufficient direct evidence that ammonolysis yields the ordered mixed Hf/Zr/Br/Cl Pmm2 phase.

**F2/F3 — Route 3 high-pressure route.**  
High-pressure nitride halide growth is strongly supported for HfNBr, ZrNBr, and ZrNCl using NH4X fluxes at 3–5 GPa and 900–1200 °C (SS2, SS3, SS5; TS1, TS5). The route receives a moderate F2 under the provided rubric because pressure is not above the >5 GPa deduction threshold, but practically it remains equipment-intensive. The kinetic/structural score is lower because the high-pressure analogues often form β/R-3m 7-coordinate phases, not the target’s orthorhombic 6-coordinate α-like motif.

**F2/F3 — Route 4 topochemical route.**  
Topochemical conversion of α-HfNBr to Hf2N2S at 750 °C preserves layered Hf–N connectivity but is phase-sensitive (SS4; TS2), and ZrNCl metathesis to Zr2N2S at 800 °C/72 h demonstrates related Zr nitride chloride reactivity (TS4). Conditions are moderate, but the route is multi-step and requires preformed layered precursors. Kinetically, preserving the framework is favorable, but Hf/Zr interdiffusion and long-range cation/halide ordering are likely slow [Reasonable Inference].

**F4 — Amorphization tendency.**  
The target has closely size-matched Hf and Zr cations, supported by very similar Hf–N and Zr–N distances in analogues (SS1, SS3, SS5), so radius-mismatch amorphization risk is low. The structure is a small 2D nitride halide sheet rather than a large complex cell, and the target description reports edge/corner sharing but not face-sharing. Thus F4 is high, with the main uncertainty being disorder between Hf/Zr and Br/Cl rather than true amorphization.

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

HfZrN2BrCl is likely a very low-energy metastable or near-ground-state ordered derivative, since its E_hull is only 0.0090 eV/atom and many Hf/Zr nitride halide analogues lie on or very near the hull (SS1, SS5; TS1, TS3, TS5). The strongest structural support comes from α-HfNBr, which shares the 2D sheet motif, 6-coordinate Hf, and L-shaped Br environment with the target (SS1; TS3). The most critical enabling factor is the known stability and accessibility of layered group-IV nitride halide frameworks by direct, ammonolysis, topochemical, and high-pressure methods (CS2; SS1–SS5; TS1–TS5). The most critical limiting factor is not basic phase stability but long-range ordering of Hf/Zr and Br/Cl without segregation into HfNBr, ZrNCl, ZrNBr, or β-polymorph domains [Reasonable Inference from SS1/SS3/SS5]. Overall, the target appears synthetically plausible, with ammonolysis or direct sealed-tube methods being preferable starting points.
