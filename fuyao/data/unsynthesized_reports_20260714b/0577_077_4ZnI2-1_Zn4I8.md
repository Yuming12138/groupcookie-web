### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

#### F1 — Thermodynamic Stability

The target has E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom, so it is predicted to be on the convex hull and receives a very high F1 score. CS, SS, and TS all show near-hull layered CdI₂ analogues with E_hull values of roughly 10⁻⁴–10⁻³ eV/atom (CS1–CS5; SS1–SS5; TS1–TS5), which supports that related binary iodide layered phases are thermodynamically accessible. Remaining uncertainty is that the analogues are CdI₂ octahedral structures, not tetrahedral ZnI₂ Pmc2₁, so they support binary iodide stability more than exact polymorph stability.

#### F2 — Route 1

Route 1 receives F2 = 100 because analogous CdI₂ crystals are grown at room temperature or ambient conditions by slow/spontaneous evaporation without high pressure, special atmosphere, or complex equipment (CS1–CS5; SS1–SS4; TS1–TS4). CS3 explicitly mentions room temperature in a Petri dish, and SS1/TS1 mention spontaneous evaporation of a saturated solution at room temperature. The main uncertainty is ZnI₂ hygroscopicity/hydrolysis, which is a **[Reasonable Inference]** not directly addressed by CS/SS/TS.

#### F3 — Route 1

Route 1 receives F3 = 88 because solution-phase ion mobility and direct crystallization give favorable kinetics, as shown by repeated room-temperature CdI₂ crystallization (CS1–CS5; SS1–SS4; TS1–TS4). The structural reconstruction burden is low because ions assemble directly during crystallization, but not perfect because the target has tetrahedral ZnI₄ sheets whereas the analogues have octahedral CdI₆ sheets. Polytypism and phase-selection concerns in CS1–CS5, SS2–SS4, and TS2–TS4 reduce the kinetics/phase-control score.

#### F2 — Route 2

Route 2 receives F2 = 90 because SS5/TS5 report room-temperature silica-gel crystal growth in a test tube under ordinary room conditions over 5–6 days, avoiding high temperature, high pressure, and controlled atmosphere. The deduction is for procedural complexity because gel preparation, aging, diffusion, and crystal recovery constitute a multi-operation process. A direct Cd-to-Zn transfer is not explicitly demonstrated, so this is partly **[Reasonable Inference]**.

#### F3 — Route 2

Route 2 receives F3 = 83 because soluble salts and gel diffusion provide high precursor activity and avoid refractory diffusion, supported by SS5/TS5. Gel growth can improve crystal size by slowing nucleation, but the target ZnI₂ phase is not directly demonstrated by SS5/TS5. The remaining uncertainty is whether gel pH and silicate chemistry will generate Zn hydrolysis/silicate impurities, which is a **[Reasonable Inference]**.

#### F2 — Route 3

Route 3 receives F2 = 55 because sealed-ampoule synthesis requires vacuum or dry inert loading and containment of volatile iodine/ZnI₂, all of which are **[Reasonable Inference]** rather than direct CS/SS/TS evidence. CS/SS/TS provide insufficient evidence for sealed-ampoule synthesis conditions. The route avoids extreme temperature and high pressure, but it is operationally more severe than solution evaporation.

#### F3 — Route 3

Route 3 receives F3 = 78 because elemental iodination and melt/vapor recrystallization should be kinetically feasible for a binary iodide **[Reasonable Inference]**. However, CS/SS/TS do not confirm this pathway, and the exact Pmc2₁ ZnI₂ polymorph may not crystallize without careful temperature-gradient or slow-cooling control. Polytype/phase selection concerns in CS1–CS5, SS2–SS4, and TS2–TS4 justify a moderate penalty.

#### F4 — Amorphization Tendency

The target has only one cation, no indicated large unit cell, and only corner-sharing tetrahedral connectivity, so none of the three amorphization triggers are activated. CS/SS/TS show that related layered iodides crystallize readily under mild conditions (CS1–CS5; SS1–SS5; TS1–TS5), which indirectly supports low amorphization tendency. The uncertainty is that the target’s tetrahedral ZnI₂ framework is not the same as the octahedral CdI₂ analogue framework, so the final F4 score relies mainly on the target structure.

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

The target ZnI₂ phase is likely a ground-state or very near-ground-state phase because its reported energy above hull is 0.0000 eV/atom. The CS/SS/TS analogues strongly support that layered binary iodides can be crystallized under mild conditions, especially by room-temperature slow evaporation from concentrated aqueous solutions (CS1–CS5; SS1–SS4; TS1–TS4). The most important enabling factor is the availability of a direct, soluble binary iodide precursor and a low-temperature crystallization route, as shown for CdI₂ analogues. The main limiting factor is polymorph/phase control: CS/SS/TS repeatedly discuss CdI₂ polytypes and stacking variants, while the target is a different tetrahedral ZnI₂ Pmc2₁ structure. Aqueous processing may additionally risk hydration or hydrolysis for ZnI₂, which is a **[Reasonable Inference]** and should be controlled by dry handling or iodide-rich mildly acidic conditions.
