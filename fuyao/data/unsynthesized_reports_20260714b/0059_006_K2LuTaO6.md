### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability**  
The target E_hull is 0.0500 eV/atom, exactly at the high-score threshold, so it is plausibly synthesizable but not a deep ground state [Target]. CS and SS analogues are mostly on or near the hull, supporting oxide stability in related K/Ta and Ln/Ta chemistries [CS][SS]. TS analogues with similar metastability, including ~0.054–0.067 eV/atom cases, have reported syntheses, supporting a 90/100 rather than a lower metastability score [TS].

**F2 — Route 1**  
Route 1 is penalized for >1000 °C firing, multi-step ceramic processing, and alkali volatility control [CS][TS]. The air/ambient-pressure setup is favorable because CS and TS analogues do not require high pressure or inert gas [CS][TS]. Insufficient evidence from [SS] for ceramic-route severity; SS source score neutral = 50.

**F3 — Route 1**  
K₂CO₃ is reactive, but Lu₂O₃ and Ta₂O₅ are refractory, so precursor activity and ion diffusion are weak [CS][SS][TS][Reasonable Inference]. The target’s P1 structure, TaO₅ units, and O–O bond are not well matched to the high-temperature ceramic analogues, so reconstruction is difficult [Target][CS][TS]. This route is feasible as a screening route but risks forming simpler tantalates instead of K₂LuTaO₆ [CS][TS][Reasonable Inference].

**F2 — Route 2**  
Route 2 avoids >1000 °C growth because SS analogues crystallize at 600 °C in sealed hydroxide flux [SS]. It is penalized for sealed caustic flux handling, multi-step processing, and optional peroxide/oxidizer use [SS][Target][Reasonable Inference]. Insufficient evidence from [CS] and insufficient evidence from [TS] for flux-route severity; both source scores neutral = 50.

**F3 — Route 2**  
The flux route has the best kinetics because SS demonstrates low-temperature crystallization of K–Ln–Ta–O phases containing TaO₅ trigonal bipyramids [SS]. The remaining kinetic penalty comes from refractory Lu/Ta chemistry and the need to shift from KNaLnTaO₅ to Na-free K₂LuTaO₆ [SS][Target][Reasonable Inference]. The SS lanthanide size limit makes Lu incorporation uncertain, so the route is promising but not straightforward [SS].

**F2 — Route 3**  
Route 3 is low-temperature, but controlled peroxide/oxidizing chemistry, sealed handling, and reactive precursors make it condition-severe [Target][Reasonable Inference]. No CS/SS/TS source directly demonstrates peroxide-assisted K₂LuTaO₆ synthesis, so the peroxide-specific evidence from CS, SS, and TS is neutral = 50. Its advantage is avoiding high-temperature O–O loss, inferred from the target O–O bond [Target][Reasonable Inference].

**F3 — Route 3**  
Solution or molten-salt precursors should improve precursor activity compared with dense oxides [Reasonable Inference]. The route still faces difficult crystallization because the desired P1/peroxide-like structure is not directly reported among CS/SS/TS analogues [Target]. This route is experimentally attractive for screening but carries high uncertainty because direct source evidence is absent [CS][SS][TS].

**F4 — Amorphization Tendency**  
The radius/coordination mismatch among K, Lu, and Ta triggers one amorphization-risk check [Target][Reasonable Inference]. The unit cell description does not indicate >40 atoms, and the target connectivity is mainly corner-sharing rather than coexisting vertex/edge/face sharing [Target]. CS/SS/TS sources do not report amorphization behavior for K₂LuTaO₆, so empirical amorphization evidence from all three sources is neutral = 50.

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

K₂LuTaO₆ is best viewed as a **low-lying metastable or borderline ground-state-accessible phase**, because its E_hull is exactly 0.0500 eV/atom [Target]. CS and TS analogues support that alkali tantalates/niobates can be made by conventional high-temperature routes with alkali excess [CS][TS], while SS analogues more strongly support a low-temperature hydroxide flux pathway for K–Ln–Ta–O structures with TaO₅ trigonal bipyramids [SS]. The most important enabling factor is the SS-style hydroxide flux, which can reduce Lu/Ta diffusion barriers and avoid excessive K loss at high temperature [SS][CS]. The main limiting factor is that the SS lanthanide series shows size sensitivity and does not include Lu, while the target also has an O–O unit not directly demonstrated in CS/SS/TS data [SS][Target]. Overall, synthesis is plausible but will likely require careful flux composition, K activity control, and phase screening [SS][CS][TS][Reasonable Inference].
