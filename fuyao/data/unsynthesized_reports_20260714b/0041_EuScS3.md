### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability.** EuScS3 lies at 0.0500 eV/atom above hull, which is exactly at the high-synthesizability threshold [Target]. Exact AScS3 analogues GdScS3 and CeScS3 are stable at E_hull = 0.0 [CS1, CS2, SS3], while TS analogues near 0.05–0.085 eV/atom show that sulfides in this metastability range are still experimentally accessible [TS1, TS3, TS4, TS5]. The remaining uncertainty is that EuScS3 may be slightly metastable rather than a strict ground state [Target; Reasonable Inference].

**F2/F3 — Route 1.** The Sb2S3 flux route has moderate condition severity because it avoids >1000 °C and high pressure, but requires sealed ampoules, inert loading, volatile sulfur, and multi-step slow cooling [CS1, SS3]. Its kinetics are favorable because the closest analogue GdScS3 uses the same ScS6 Pnma framework and flux-mediated transport [CS1, SS3]. TS gives insufficient Sb2S3-specific evidence and is treated as neutral 50 for this route.

**F2/F3 — Route 2.** The alkali-halide flux route has similar severity to Route 1 because it still requires sealed inert conditions and volatile sulfur control, although the temperature can be lower [CS3, SS1, TS4]. Kinetics are moderately good because Eu sulfides crystallize in NaCl/KCl flux [CS3, SS1], but structural transfer to EuScS3 is less direct than the Sb2S3 GdScS3 analogue [CS1, SS3]. This route is useful as a lower-temperature screen but is not the primary route [Reasonable Inference].

**F2/F3 — Route 3.** Binary-sulfide sintering is strongly supported structurally because CeScS3 was made by firing binary sulfides in H2S [CS2]. Its severity is higher than flux growth because 1200 °C, flowing H2S, and multi-day firing are required [CS2]. Kinetics are moderate because binary sulfides are chemically matched, but solid-state diffusion and Eu redox control remain limiting [CS2; Reasonable Inference].

**F2/F3 — Route 4.** Direct elemental sealed-tube synthesis is plausible because related Sc and rare-earth sulfides form from elements in evacuated ampoules [CS5, TS1]. It scores lower because Sc metal is refractory, sulfur is volatile, Eu is air-sensitive, and no direct EuScS3 elemental synthesis is reported [CS5, TS1, CS3; Reasonable Inference]. SS gives insufficient direct elemental EuScS3 evidence and is treated as neutral 50.

**F2/F3 — Route 5.** High-pressure synthesis is a fallback because high pressure stabilizes some rare-earth sulfides and LnCrS3-type phases [CS4, SS5, TS5]. It scores poorly on condition severity due to specialized pressure equipment and possible >5 GPa screening [SS5, TS5]. It is not preferred because exact AScS3 analogues are already synthesized under ambient-pressure flux or H2S conditions [CS1, CS2, SS3].

**F4 — Amorphization Tendency.** Only C1 is triggered because Eu and Sc occupy very different coordination environments and likely have >15% ionic-radius mismatch [Target; Reasonable Inference]. C2 is not triggered because AScS3 Pnma analogues have 20 atoms per conventional cell [CS1, CS2, SS3], and C3 is not triggered because the target and closest analogues are dominated by corner-sharing ScS6 octahedra rather than mixed vertex/edge/face sharing [Target, CS1, CS2, SS3]. TS provides insufficient structure-specific amorphization signal and is neutral 50 for this factor.

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

EuScS3 is likely a borderline near-ground-state or mildly metastable sulfide because its E_hull is 0.0500 eV/atom [Target]. The CS and SS evidence is strong because GdScS3 and CeScS3 share the same Pnma AScS3 structural type and are experimentally synthesized [CS1, CS2, SS3]. The most critical enabling factor is flux-assisted mass transport, especially Sb2S3 flux, which directly produced the closest GdScS3 analogue [CS1, SS3]. The most critical limiting factor is Eu redox/stoichiometry control, since Eu sulfides can favor Eu2+ chemistry while EuScS3 charge balance implies Eu3+ with Sc3+ and S2− [Reasonable Inference]. Overall, EuScS3 should be synthetically accessible, with Sb2S3 flux growth as the best first attempt [CS1, SS3].
