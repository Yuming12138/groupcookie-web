### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

#### F1 — Thermodynamic stability

The target has E_hull = 0.028 eV/atom, placing it in the high-synthesizability thermodynamic window below 0.05 eV/atom [Target]. TS analogues with similar E_hull values around 0.02–0.033 eV/atom are experimentally synthesized by sealed-tube, hydrothermal, or high-pressure routes [TS1–TS5], supporting the idea that this energy range is accessible. CS and SS contain mixed stability examples and do not directly address binary AgCl Pmma stability; therefore, **insufficient evidence from CS and mixed/insufficient evidence from SS are treated as neutral 50 source signals**.

#### F2 — Route 1

Route 1 uses mild solution crystallization under ambient pressure, matching chloride analogues crystallized from aqueous solution [SS1, SS5]. The only significant severity penalty is the use of Ag salts, which are chemically hazardous and photosensitive in product form [Reasonable Inference]. CS provides partial support for mild wet-chemical processing through CBD at 80 °C in air [CS1], while TS gives insufficient direct evidence for this specific route and is neutral.

#### F3 — Route 1

Kinetics are favorable because Ag⁺ and Cl⁻ react rapidly in solution and diffusion limitations are minimal [Reasonable Inference], consistent with solution crystallization of chloride compounds [SS1, SS5]. The main kinetic uncertainty is polymorph selection: ordinary AgCl precipitation is easy, but formation of a 1D Pmma ribbon requires controlled nucleation or templating [Target; Reasonable Inference]. Thus F3 is high but not near-perfect.

#### F2 — Route 2

Sealed-ampoule melt processing is more severe because it requires an evacuated/sealed tube, elevated temperature, volatile halide management, and a multi-step thermal program [SS2, TS5]. The temperature itself is not extreme for AgCl-adapted synthesis, but the sealed halide environment adds safety and stoichiometry-control burdens [Reasonable Inference]. CS provides insufficient direct evidence for AgCl melt synthesis and is neutral.

#### F3 — Route 2

Melt growth offers good ion mobility and bypasses solid diffusion, analogous to Ag₃SI Bridgman growth from sealed ampoules [SS2]. However, crystallization from a melt will likely favor thermodynamically stable AgCl unless cooling rate, gradient, or confinement favors the metastable Pmma ribbon [Target; Reasonable Inference]. This limits the structural-reconstruction component.

#### F2 — Route 3

Chemical bath deposition is mild in temperature and pressure, as shown for CdS CBD at 80 °C in air [CS1]. It incurs penalties for multiple practical steps and hazardous Ag/complexing/acid reagents [Reasonable Inference]. SS supports solution crystallization of chloride networks [SS1, SS5], while TS gives insufficient direct thin-film evidence and is neutral.

#### F3 — Route 3

Thin-film or surface-confined precipitation has good precursor availability and fast reaction kinetics [CS1; Reasonable Inference]. The moderate score reflects uncertainty in direct crystallization of the Pmma polymorph rather than amorphous or rocksalt AgCl [Target; Reasonable Inference]. Composition control is easier than multication systems, but rapid AgCl precipitation can create nonuniform films [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2 — Route 4

Vapor transport requires sealed ampoules, vacuum or halide atmosphere, and volatile transport species, similar to WOI₃ CVT and Ag₃SI sealed thermal-gradient growth [TS4, SS2]. It avoids very high temperatures for AgCl but still has sealed-tube and halogen-handling hazards [Reasonable Inference]. CS vapor ZnS analogues require much higher temperatures and show polytype issues [CS3–CS5], so CS support is indirect and cautious.

#### F3 — Route 4

Vapor transport can produce low-dimensional crystals, as in ribbon-like WOI₃ [TS4], and sealed ampoule gradients are transferable from Ag₃SI [SS2]. The uncertain part is whether AgCl vapor recrystallizes into the target Pmma chain rather than the stable rocksalt polymorph [Target; Reasonable Inference]. Volatile halide stoichiometry also lowers the composition-control score [Reasonable Inference].

#### F4 — Amorphization tendency

The target contains only Ag and Cl with one cation species, so there is no cation radius mismatch trigger [Target]. The described structure is simple and one-dimensional, with no evidence for >40 atoms per cell or mixed polyhedral sharing [Target]. Therefore the intrinsic amorphization tendency is low, though metastable polymorph selection remains a separate issue [Target; Reasonable Inference].

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

The target AgCl Pmma ribbon is likely a low-energy metastable phase rather than a ground-state phase because its E_hull is 0.028 eV/atom [Target]. Thermodynamic analogues near this energy are experimentally accessible [TS1–TS5], and structurally similar low-dimensional chloride materials can crystallize from solution under mild conditions [SS1, SS5]. The strongest enabling factor is the simple binary Ag–Cl chemistry and very low E_hull [Target; SS1/SS5]. The main limiting factor is polymorph competition with ordinary AgCl, because the target is an unusual 1D ribbon rather than the common dense AgCl arrangement [Target; Reasonable Inference]. Solution or surface-confined crystallization is therefore preferred over unconstrained bulk melt growth.
