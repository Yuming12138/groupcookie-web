### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic stability.**  
The target E_hull is 0.029 eV/atom, placing it in the 90–100 scoring band. The exact-composition Tl₂O analogue is essentially hull-stable in CS-1/SS-1/TS-5, which supports the chemical feasibility of Tl₂O, but the target’s hexagonal P-6m2 polymorph is not the same as the reported monoclinic C2/m analogue, so polymorph stability remains uncertain. TS also includes several Tl-containing phases with low E_hull around 0.01–0.04 eV/atom, suggesting small metastabilities are experimentally accessible (TS-1, TS-3, TS-4).

**F2 — Route 1 condition severity.**  
Route 1 is penalized for controlled Ar/vacuum atmosphere, sealed handling, toxic Tl precursors, and multistep processing, all directly supported by the exact Tl₂O analogue synthesis (CS-1/SS-1/TS-5). It is not penalized for high temperature or high pressure because the reference uses 600 °C and ambient sealed-vessel conditions (CS-1/SS-1/TS-5). The remaining uncertainty is whether the P-6m2 polymorph needs different thermal history than the reported C2/m polymorph.

**F3 — Route 1 reaction kinetics.**  
The route benefits from low-melting Tl chemistry and the close structural match to known Tl₂O sheets (CS-1/SS-1), but long 14-day annealing indicates slow ordering or phase equilibration (CS-1/SS-1/TS-5). Structural reconstruction is moderate rather than easy because the target is a different stacking/symmetry variant from the known Tl₂O analogue (SS-1). SS-3 and SS-4 show related Tl compounds can form at moderate temperatures, supporting manageable diffusion, but those are not binary Tl₂O.

**F2 — Route 2 condition severity.**  
Hydrothermal/solvothermal analogues use mild temperatures and standard autogenous pressure rather than extreme pressure (CS-3, TS-2, TS-4). The main severity penalty is Tl toxicity and potential sensitivity, supported by Tl₂O handling concerns in CS-1/SS-1/TS-5. There is insufficient direct SS evidence for hydrothermal formation of the target-like Tl₂O sheet, so SS is neutral for the route conditions.

**F3 — Route 2 reaction kinetics.**  
Solution-mediated chemistry gives high precursor activity and low diffusion barriers, supported by Bi₂O₃ hydrothermal crystallization and Tl oxo-compound hydrothermal synthesis (CS-3, TS-2). However, structural reconstruction is poorly supported because no CS/SS/TS source shows binary Tl₂O crystallizing hydrothermally; alkali incorporation or hydroxide/carbonate impurities are plausible [Reasonable Inference], and KTlO formation from Tl₂O plus alkali oxide shows Tl–alkali oxide phases can compete (SS-3). Thus the route has good nominal kinetics but uncertain phase selectivity.

**F2 — Route 3 condition severity.**  
Thin-film synthesis avoids high bulk temperatures and high pressures, but requires vacuum, controlled oxygen potential, and toxic/volatile Tl sources [Reasonable Inference]. The need for controlled atmosphere is consistent with exact Tl₂O synthesis under Ar/vacuum and phase sensitivity (CS-1/SS-1/TS-5). There is insufficient direct CS/SS/TS evidence for Tl₂O thin films, so CS/SS/TS are neutral regarding actual deposition feasibility.

**F3 — Route 3 reaction kinetics.**  
Thin-film routes can kinetically trap metastable layered phases [Reasonable Inference], which is relevant because the target is only 0.029 eV/atom above hull. But no CS/SS/TS analogue confirms direct thin-film crystallization, and Tl volatility makes composition control difficult, inferred from the sealed/inert processing needs of Tl₂O (CS-1/SS-1/TS-5). Therefore F3 is the lowest among the three routes.

**F4 — Amorphization tendency.**  
The target has one cation, a small Tl₂O formula basis, and a simple edge-sharing 2D connectivity, so none of the three amorphization checks are triggered. The exact Tl₂O analogue also has layered edge-sharing Tl–O polyhedra rather than complex mixed connectivity (CS-1/SS-1). The uncertainty is minor because unit-cell details beyond the structural description are not fully provided, but the simple formula strongly suggests low amorphization tendency.

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

The target is likely a low-energy metastable polymorph rather than the established ground-state Tl₂O structure, because its E_hull is 0.029 eV/atom while the known Tl₂O analogue in CS-1/SS-1/TS-5 is essentially on the hull. The CS and SS data support synthesis strongly at the chemical and local-structure level because an exact Tl₂O composition with similar 2D T-shaped Tl–O coordination is known, but they do not directly prove the hexagonal P-6m2 stacking. The most important enabling factor is the low thermodynamic penalty combined with direct Tl₂O solid-state precedent (CS-1/SS-1/TS-5). The most important limiting factor is phase control under a narrow Tl/O chemical-potential window, since the exact analogue reports oxidation/disproportionation/impurity sensitivity (CS-1/SS-1/TS-5). Hydrothermal and thin-film routes may help access the hexagonal polymorph, but those are less directly supported and should be treated as exploratory.
