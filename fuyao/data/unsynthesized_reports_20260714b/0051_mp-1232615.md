### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability.** The target E_hull of 0.0500 eV/atom places it exactly at the favorable ≤0.05 eV/atom threshold, so F1 is scored 90 rather than higher because it is near the boundary [Target]. Exact BaMn₂Bi₂ has already been synthesized as a tetragonal polymorph even with E_hull = 0.1835 eV/atom, supporting the idea that Ba–Mn–Bi metastable phases are experimentally accessible [CS1; SS1]. Thermodynamic analogues near 0.05–0.12 eV/atom are also reported as synthesizable Mn pnictides, strengthening the feasibility signal [TS1; TS2; TS3; TS4; TS5]. The main uncertainty is polymorph competition between the target monoclinic C2/m phase and the known tetragonal I4/mmm phase [Target; CS1; SS1].

**F2 — Route 1.** Bi self-flux requires only 1000 °C, standard furnace growth, and no high pressure, but it requires vacuum sealing, controlled slow cooling, and centrifugation [CS1; SS1]. The deductions come from vacuum atmosphere, sealed volatile flux, multi-step processing, and Ba reactivity/air sensitivity inferred from related inert-handling practice [CS1; SS1; SS2; Reasonable Inference]. The remaining uncertainty is whether the same program yields the monoclinic distortion rather than the reported tetragonal polymorph [Target; CS1; SS1].

**F3 — Route 1.** Kinetics are favorable because excess molten Bi provides a liquid transport medium and the exact BaMn₂Bi₂ framework already forms under these conditions [CS1; SS1]. The precursor activity is not maximal because elemental Mn is not as reactive as molecular or salt precursors, but flux transport compensates substantially [CS1; SS1; Reasonable Inference]. Structural reconstruction is low-to-moderate because the target is a distorted version of a known BaMn₂Bi₂ motif rather than a chemically unrelated framework [Target; CS1; SS1; Reasonable Inference].

**F2 — Route 2.** Direct solid-state synthesis is more severe because related reactions require sealed Ar/vacuum conditions and can run for 1–5 days or longer [CS2; CS3; CS4; TS3; TS5]. The recommended route may touch >1000 °C, and the process needs sealed containment for Bi/Ba mixtures plus inert handling [CS3; CS4; SS2; Reasonable Inference]. The highest-temperature analogues are not adopted as the preferred condition, so the >1400 °C deduction is not applied to the recommended route [TS4; SS5; Reasonable Inference].

**F3 — Route 2.** Kinetics are moderate because direct elemental reactions can form AMnBi/AMnSb phases, but elemental Mn diffusion and long annealing remain limiting [CS3; CS4; TS3; TS5]. Structural assembly from elements is more demanding than flux growth, and related systems report impurities, non-uniform products, or failed compositions in some cases [CS2; TS2; TS5]. This route is plausible but less selective for the target polymorph than Bi self-flux [CS1; CS2; Reasonable Inference].

**F2 — Route 3.** Sn/In flux growth has similar severity to Bi self-flux because it uses ~1000 °C, Ar-sealed ampoules, slow cooling, and centrifugation [SS2; SS4; TS1]. CS provides insufficient direct evidence for Sn/In flux in Ba–Mn–Bi, so the CS route-specific signal is neutral at 50 [insufficient evidence from CS]. The main severity comes from sealed flux handling and the need for inert containment [SS2; SS4].

**F3 — Route 3.** Sn/In flux should improve mass transport relative to direct sintering, and AB₂Pn₂ Mn-pnictide structural analogues have been grown by Sn/In flux [SS2; SS4; TS1]. The kinetics score is lower than Bi self-flux because the flux chemistry differs from the target anion chemistry and may introduce Sn/In incorporation [SS2; SS4; CS1; Reasonable Inference]. The route is best treated as an alternative screen if Bi self-flux does not isolate the monoclinic polymorph [CS1; SS4; Reasonable Inference].

**F4 — Amorphization Tendency.** One amorphization trigger is assigned because Ba and Mn occupy very different coordination environments and have a large effective size contrast [Target; CS1; SS1; Reasonable Inference]. No trigger is assigned for >40 atoms per unit cell because the provided target and exact analogue descriptions are chemically simple, although exact target unit-cell atom count is not provided [Target; CS1; SS1]. No trigger is assigned for vertex + edge + face coexistence because the target/exact analogue reports edge and corner sharing but not face sharing [Target; CS1; SS1].

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

BaMn₂Bi₂ in the target monoclinic C2/m form is likely a near-hull metastable phase rather than an unequivocal 0 K ground state because its E_hull is positive at 0.0500 eV/atom [Target]. The synthesis outlook is strong because exact-composition BaMn₂Bi₂ has already been grown by Bi self-flux with nearly identical local coordination, even though that reference polymorph is tetragonal I4/mmm [CS1; SS1; Target]. The most critical enabling factor is molten Bi self-flux, which supplies both the Bi chemical reservoir and liquid-mediated mass transport for MnBi₄ framework formation [CS1; SS1]. The most critical limiting factor is polymorph and impurity control, especially competition with tetragonal BaMn₂Bi₂ and MnBi-type secondary phases [CS1; SS1; Target]. Overall, the best route is a tuned Bi self-flux growth followed by careful cooling/annealing screens to bias the monoclinic distortion [CS1; SS1; Reasonable Inference].
