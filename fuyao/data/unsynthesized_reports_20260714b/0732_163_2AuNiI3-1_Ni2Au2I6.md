### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

#### F1 — Thermodynamic Stability

The target E_hull is 0.045 eV/atom, which lies within the ≤0.05 eV/atom high-synthesizability bin, so the numerical score is high. CS analogues include both stable iodides and metastable iodides as high as 0.147 eV/atom, showing that halide phases can be experimentally accessible above hull [CS1–CS5]. TS analogues with E_hull values near 0.021–0.067 eV/atom are experimentally made, supporting accessibility of phases in this metastability range [TS2, TS5]. Remaining uncertainty is that the target may be metastable rather than a true ground state, and competing AuI/NiI2/I2 phases are not enumerated in the references.

Source signal scores: CS = 75, SS = 70, TS = 85. No source is fully silent on thermodynamic accessibility because all provide E_hull values for related phases [CS/SS/TS].

#### F2 — Route 1

The sealed-ampoule solid-state route uses moderate temperatures comparable to TlFeI3/TlGaI4/CuHgSI analogues [CS1, CS2, TS2], so no high-temperature penalty is applied. The penalties arise from vacuum/Ar sealing, volatile iodides, moisture-sensitive handling, and likely regrinding/reannealing, all supported by iodide analogue procedures [CS1, CS2, CS4, TS2]. SS provides general sealed-ampoule support but insufficient evidence from SS for exact Au–Ni iodide conditions. The main uncertainty is whether AuI survives long anneals without disproportionation [Reasonable Inference].

Source signal scores: CS = 80, SS = 60, TS = 75.

#### F3 — Route 1

Binary iodides are reactive compared with oxides, and many iodide analogues form at 150–350 °C [CS1, CS2, CS4]. However, the target requires reconstructing separate Au cluster and NiI3 sheet motifs, and no analogue directly shows Au–Au cluster ordering in this chemistry; insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS for that specific motif. Long annealing and repeated cycles in TlFeI3 and CuHgSI analogues suggest kinetic/phase-purity challenges [CS1, TS2]. The resulting kinetic score is moderate.

Source signal scores: CS = 75, SS = 50 neutral-to-weak, TS = 70.

#### F2 — Route 2

Melt/Bridgman growth is supported for chemically similar iodide frameworks [CS1, CS3, CS4]. Conditions remain below 1000 °C, but sealed ampoules, volatile iodide components, moisture exclusion, and slow cooling are required [CS4]. Repeated annealing may be necessary, as reported for TlFeI3 [CS1]. TS gives insufficient evidence for melt growth specifically, so it is neutral for this route.

Source signal scores: CS = 85, SS = 50 neutral, TS = 50 neutral.

#### F3 — Route 2

Molten iodide flux strongly improves mass transport, supported by CuGaI4 slow-cooling from a CuI/GaI3 melt [CS4]. Bridgman growth of TlFeI3/TlCdI3 indicates that extended iodide frameworks can crystallize from melts [CS1, CS3]. The target’s Au cluster motif remains unsupported directly, so the crystallization pathway is not fully certain. The kinetic score is high relative to solid-state annealing but below ideal.

Source signal scores: CS = 85, SS = 50 neutral, TS = 50 neutral.

#### F2 — Route 3

Hydrothermal synthesis in concentrated HI is mild in temperature and avoids vacuum/high-pressure solid-state requirements [CS5]. Autogenous pressure is present but not high-pressure synthesis in the >5 GPa sense [CS5, TS1]. The main severity penalties are corrosive/volatile HI and toxic/air-sensitive iodide/Au chemistry [CS5, TS1, Reasonable Inference]. SS gives insufficient evidence for hydrothermal synthesis of structurally similar compounds, so it is neutral.

Source signal scores: CS = 85, SS = 50 neutral, TS = 80.

#### F3 — Route 3

Solution-mediated iodide crystallization gives high precursor activity and low diffusion barriers, as demonstrated by CuTl2I3 from TlI/CuI in HI [CS5]. Concentrated hydrohalic solution also works for thermodynamic analogue CuHgSBr [TS1]. The uncertainty is structural: no source proves that the 2D NiI3 sheet with Au clusters nucleates from HI, and Au redox chemistry may divert the reaction [Reasonable Inference]. Thus kinetics are favorable, but phase selectivity is not guaranteed.

Source signal scores: CS = 80, SS = 50 neutral, TS = 80.

#### F2 — Route 4

CVT/sealed-tube iodine routes operate at moderate temperatures, as in GaTeI7/AlTeI7 [SS1, SS2]. The penalties are for vacuum/Ar sealed ampoules, volatile I2, multi-step tube preparation, and moisture-sensitive iodide products [SS1, SS2]. CS and TS give insufficient evidence for CVT synthesis of Au–Ni iodides, so both are neutral for route severity. The route is experimentally feasible but operationally less attractive than hydrothermal crystallization.

Source signal scores: CS = 50 neutral, SS = 80, TS = 50 neutral.

#### F3 — Route 4

I2 transport is proven for structurally similar iodide materials [SS1, SS2], but Ni transport is likely less efficient than Ga/In/Te iodide transport [Reasonable Inference]. Composition control is expected to be narrow because Au, Ni, and I have very different volatility/redox behavior [Reasonable Inference]. CS and TS provide insufficient evidence for vapor transport of Au–Ni iodides. Therefore, kinetics and composition control are the weakest among proposed routes.

Source signal scores: CS = 50 neutral, SS = 65, TS = 50 neutral.

#### F4 — Amorphization Tendency

The target has a large Au/Ni cation size mismatch, so one amorphization-risk trigger is assigned [Reasonable Inference]. The reported P-31m target description appears structurally simple with a NiI3 sheet and Au cluster, so no >40-atom-cell trigger is assigned [Target]. Mixed connectivity is limited because the target mainly contains edge-sharing NiI6 octahedra and separate Au–Au bonding rather than multiple simultaneous vertex/edge/face polyhedral sharing modes [Target]. CS/SS/TS do not provide direct amorphization data, so source-specific evidence is insufficient from CS/SS/TS and treated as neutral for this factor.

Source signal scores: CS = 50 neutral, SS = 50 neutral, TS = 50 neutral.

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

AuNiI3 is likely a low-energy metastable phase rather than an unequivocal ground-state compound because its E_hull is 0.045 eV/atom, just below the common 0.05 eV/atom practical metastability threshold. The chemical analogues strongly support iodide synthesis under sealed, moisture-free, moderate-temperature conditions, especially for edge-sharing MI6 iodide frameworks [CS1, CS3, CS4]. Thermodynamic analogues show that halide/chalcogenide phases with comparable or higher E_hull values can be synthesized [TS1–TS5]. The most critical enabling factor is high iodide mobility/chemical potential, best provided by concentrated HI solution or molten/sealed iodide media [CS5, CS4]. The most critical limiting factor is control of Au oxidation state and Au–Au cluster formation, for which the provided CS/SS/TS sources give no direct demonstration.
