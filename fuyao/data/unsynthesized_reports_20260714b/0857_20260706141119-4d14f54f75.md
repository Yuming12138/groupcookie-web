### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

#### F1 — Thermodynamic stability

The target is reported at E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom, so the assigned score is near the top of the ≤0.05 eV/atom range. The closest structural/thermodynamic analogue, Cs₂PbN₂(O₃Cl)₂, is also on the hull and has the same tetragonal I4₁/amd structure type (SS1, TS1). Other relevant oxosalt/oxychloride analogues are also stable or near-stable, including KNO₃ at E_hull = 0.0 eV/atom (SS2, TS2) and RbMoOPO₄Cl/KMoOPO₄Cl at E_hull = 0.0 eV/atom (CS4, CS5). CS is somewhat contradictory because one highly chemically similar Pd nitrito/chloro compound is 0.225 eV/atom above hull (CS1), but the target’s own hull stability and the isostructural stable analogue dominate the score.

#### F2 — Route 1 hydrothermal

Route 1 receives no severity deductions because the same-structure hydrothermal analogue uses only 240 °C, water, and an autoclave rather than extreme temperature, GPa pressure, or inert/reactive atmosphere (SS1, TS1). The precursors proposed for the Ca/Rb target are simple nitrates/chlorides rather than toxic Pb salts, so toxicity is lower than the Pb analogue [Reasonable Inference]. The main uncertainty is autogenous pressure and possible solubility/complexation differences for Ca²⁺ versus Pb²⁺ [Reasonable Inference].

#### F3 — Route 1 hydrothermal

Kinetics should be favorable because soluble salts in water provide high precursor activity, as demonstrated by Pb(NO₃)₂/CsCl hydrothermal synthesis of the same framework (SS1, TS1). Solution transport avoids solid-state diffusion limits, and slow cooling directly supports crystallization of the ordered I4₁/amd phase (SS1, TS1). The main kinetic uncertainty is whether smaller Ca²⁺ nucleates the same MCl₄O₄ framework as Pb²⁺ or instead forms hydrated calcium nitrate/chloride phases [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2 — Route 2 ambient aqueous crystallization

Route 2 is extremely mild because KNO₃ crystals were obtained by slow cooling or evaporation of saturated aqueous solution at room temperature (SS2, SS3, TS2), and KNO₂ was grown by desiccator evaporation (TS3). No high pressure, high temperature, inert gas, or sealed system is required in the analogue evidence (SS2, SS3, TS2, TS3). However, this route lacks direct evidence for the full Rb–Ca nitrate chloride framework, so the low severity does not guarantee phase selectivity; insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS for exact ambient synthesis of Rb₂CaN₂(O₃Cl)₂.

#### F3 — Route 2 ambient aqueous crystallization

Precursor activity and ion mobility are high because all ions are in aqueous solution, consistent with nitrate salt crystallization analogues (SS2, SS3, TS2). Rb-containing chloro/nitrito crystalline compounds can also form from aqueous precipitation (CS2, SS4), supporting general Rb–Cl–N–O solution compatibility. The structural reconstruction score is lower because the closest same-structure analogue required hydrothermal treatment rather than ambient evaporation (SS1, TS1), and separate crystallization of RbNO₃ or calcium chloride hydrates is plausible [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2 — Route 3 sealed nitrate/chloride flux

Route 3 is penalized for sealed or controlled atmosphere processing because Rb/K oxychloride analogues were prepared in evacuated silica ampoules (CS4, CS5), and dry/inert handling is used for hygroscopic oxosalts (TS5). It is also penalized for possible volatile or decomposition products requiring containment, since nitrates can evolve NOₓ if overheated [Reasonable Inference]. The temperature is far below 1000 °C, so no high-temperature deduction is applied [Reasonable Inference].

#### F3 — Route 3 sealed nitrate/chloride flux

A molten nitrate/chloride medium should give good ionic mobility, by analogy to oxosalt melt/slow-cooling synthesis (TS5), and nitrate/chloride salts are chemically active [Reasonable Inference]. Nevertheless, the route is less directly supported than hydrothermal synthesis because the exact I4₁/amd analogue was made hydrothermally (SS1, TS1), not by flux. CS4/CS5 also report poor phase control for sealed Rb/K Mo oxychlorides, including biphasic samples and manual crystal selection, so structural/phase reconstruction is assigned only moderate-high confidence.

#### F4 — Amorphization tendency

The target contains very different cation sizes, Rb⁺ and Ca²⁺, which triggers the radius-mismatch criterion [Reasonable Inference]. The I4₁/amd framework likely contains more than 40 atoms per conventional unit cell based on the 13-atom formula unit and the same-structure analogue (SS1, TS1), triggering the unit-cell-size criterion [Reasonable Inference]. The target description does not explicitly state simultaneous edge/face/corner polyhedral sharing, so C3 is not triggered; the Cs₂Pb analogue does show mixed face/corner/edge Cl coordination (SS1, TS1), but transferring that to the target is uncertain.

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

Rb₂CaN₂(O₃Cl)₂ is likely a ground-state or very near-ground-state phase because its reported energy above hull is 0.0000 eV/atom, and the closest isostructural Cs₂PbN₂(O₃Cl)₂ analogue is also hull-stable (SS1, TS1). The strongest synthesis support comes from SS1/TS1, which demonstrate hydrothermal formation of the same I4₁/amd nitrate chloride framework from simple nitrate/chloride salts in water. CS provides useful but less direct support: Rb-containing chloro/nitrito compounds form in aqueous routes (CS2), while Rb/K oxychlorides can be made in sealed high-temperature routes but with phase-purity issues (CS4, CS5). The most critical enabling factor is solution-mediated transport and slow hydrothermal cooling, which should allow ordering of Rb, Ca, nitrate, and chloride into the target framework (SS1, TS1). The most critical limiting factor is phase competition from highly soluble simple salts or hydrates, especially because no CS/SS/TS entry directly proves the Ca analogue and Ca²⁺ differs chemically from Pb²⁺ [Reasonable Inference].
