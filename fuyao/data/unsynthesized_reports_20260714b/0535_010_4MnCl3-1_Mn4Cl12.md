### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

#### F1 — Thermodynamic Stability

The target E_hull is 0.0260 eV/atom, which lies in the high-synthesizability range ≤0.05 eV/atom. CS supports that chemically related transition-metal chlorides can be synthesized even when slightly above hull, e.g., MoCl3 at 0.034 eV/atom and TcCl3 at 0.071 eV/atom (CS-1, CS-3). TS strongly supports this thermodynamic range because several thermodynamically similar chlorides have E_hull ≈0.024–0.036 eV/atom and documented sealed-ampoule/CVT syntheses (TS-1 to TS-4). Remaining uncertainty is that E_hull does not guarantee kinetic accessibility or resistance to decomposition under chlorine loss **[Reasonable Inference]**.

#### F2 — Route 1

Route 1 is penalized for vacuum/sealed conditions, volatile Cl2/transport chemistry, multiple operational steps, and toxic/air-sensitive reagents; similar requirements are explicitly reported for MoCl3 CVT and chloride ampoule syntheses (CS-1, TS-3, SS-3/SS-4). It avoids very high temperature and high pressure, unlike high-pressure sulfide synthesis in CS-4, so the severity is not extreme. The main uncertainty is the chlorine chemical potential needed for MnCl3, which is not directly provided by CS/SS/TS.

#### F3 — Route 1

Kinetics are favorable because volatile chloride transport can bypass slow bulk diffusion, as shown by MoCl3 CVT and many chloride ampoule syntheses (CS-1, TS-3, SS-3/SS-4). Structural reconstruction is moderate because MoCl3 and TcCl3 are layered MCl6-octahedral trichlorides (CS-1, CS-3), close to the target’s 2D MnCl6 sheet. However, CS reports multiple Mo chloride phases and TcCl3 polymorph/metastability issues, so nucleation of the desired Pc polymorph is uncertain (CS-1, CS-3).

#### F2 — Route 2

Route 2 has the simplest operational basis: metal + Cl2 in a sealed tube, directly supported by TcCl3 synthesis (CS-3). It still requires vacuum/sealed handling, toxic volatile Cl2, and anhydrous conditions, giving significant deductions. It avoids high pressure, autoclave solvents, and complex multi-step transport optimization, so it scores higher in condition severity than Routes 1 and 3.

#### F3 — Route 2

Direct chlorination has high precursor activity because Cl2 is a strong chlorinating agent and successfully forms TcCl3 from Tc metal (CS-3). The kinetic limitation is possible passivating MnCl2/MnCl3 surface formation and incomplete conversion **[Reasonable Inference]**. Structural formation is plausible because chemically similar binary trichlorides form layered octahedral networks (CS-1, CS-3), but the exact monoclinic Pc target may require careful annealing or quenching **[Reasonable Inference]**.

#### F2 — Route 3

Route 3 uses lower temperatures supported by MoCl5 solvothermal and TS chloride ampoule syntheses (CS-2/SS-1, TS-3, TS-4), but the severity remains substantial because SOCl2/SbCl5/Cl2 are volatile, toxic, and pressure-generating in sealed ampoules. CS-2/SS-1 explicitly requires glovebox loading, sealed ampoules, controlled cooling, and sometimes counterpressure/autoclave safety. Thus the lower temperature does not fully offset the handling complexity.

#### F3 — Route 3

Kinetics are strong because reactive chloride media can dissolve/recrystallize metal chlorides at low temperature, as shown by MoCl5 solvothermal growth and TS chloride syntheses over days (CS-2/SS-1, TS-3, TS-4). Diffusion limitations are minimal compared with solid-state routes because solution/vapor transport dominates **[Reasonable Inference]**. The uncertainty is structural: the closest solvothermal analogue is cluster-like MoCl5, not a 2D MnCl3 sheet, so the route may favor molecular complexes or other chloride phases (CS-2/SS-1).

#### F4 — Amorphization Tendency

The target is a simple binary chloride with one cation, so the cation-radius mismatch criterion is not triggered **[Reasonable Inference]**. The target description reports edge- and corner-sharing MnCl6 octahedra but no face-sharing, so the mixed vertex+edge+face criterion is not triggered. CS/SS/TS do not provide direct target unit-cell atom count evidence, so that specific source signal is insufficient and treated neutrally, but the simple MnCl3 composition suggests low amorphization risk **[Reasonable Inference]**.

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

The target is likely a slightly metastable but accessible phase rather than a deep ground-state compound, because its E_hull is 0.0260 eV/atom and similar above-hull chloride analogues are synthesized (CS-1, TS-1 to TS-4). CS provides the strongest support: MoCl3 and TcCl3 are binary layered trichlorides built from MCl6 octahedra and are synthesized by sealed chlorination/CVT routes (CS-1, CS-3). TS supports that low-energy chloride materials are commonly accessible in sealed ampoules at modest temperatures (TS-1 to TS-4), while SS gives mixed evidence because some structural analogues are not binary trichlorides (SS-2 to SS-5). The most critical enabling factor is volatile chloride transport/chlorine chemical potential, which can promote crystallization at temperatures far below refractory solid-state regimes (CS-1, CS-3, TS-3). The most critical limiting factor is phase control of Mn oxidation state and polymorph, since Mo and Tc chloride analogues show multiple phases, polymorphs, and metastability (CS-1, CS-3).
