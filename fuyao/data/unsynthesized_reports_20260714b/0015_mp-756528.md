[Target] CS analogues are synthesized and mostly at 0 eV/atom or near-hull, while SS/TS analogues include synthesized reduced Fe oxides around 0.03–0.088 eV/atom, so the target’s thermodynamic position is plausible but not deeply stable. [Reasonable Inference]
