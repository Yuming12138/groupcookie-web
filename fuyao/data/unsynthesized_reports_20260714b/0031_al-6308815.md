### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability:** The target E_hull is 0.0500 eV/atom, exactly at the high-feasibility threshold, so the score is 90 rather than a mid-range metastability score [Target]. TS analogues at comparable E_hull values are experimentally accessible, including hydrothermal Pb(H₂PO₄)₂ near 0.052 eV/atom and solution-grown K₂CoH₂(SeO₅)₂ near 0.058 eV/atom [TS-1][TS-5]. Remaining uncertainty is that the target may be metastable rather than a strict ground state because it lies just above the hull [Target][Reasonable Inference].

**Route 1 F2/F3:** Route 1 has mild severity because CS/SS analogues crystallize at room temperature to 65 °C under air or exsiccator conditions [CS-1][SS-1]. The only F2 penalty is toxic arsenic acid, inferred from the arsenate precursor requirement [CS-2][Reasonable Inference]. Kinetics are favorable because ions are solvated in acidic aqueous solution, but phase control is not perfect because CS-1 reports failed compositions/mixtures and CS-5 notes twinning or accompanying phases [CS-1][CS-5].

**Route 2 F2/F3:** Hydrothermal conditions are moderate but not severe because CS-2 and TS-1 use ~200–220 °C autoclaves rather than extreme temperature or multi-GPa pressure [CS-2][TS-1]. Insufficient evidence from SS supports hydrothermal conditions directly, so SS is neutral for this route signal (50) [SS]. Kinetics are strong in hydrothermal fluid, but the score is reduced because CS-4 reports impurities while CS-2 reports high-yield arsenate synthesis, giving contradictory phase-control evidence [CS-2][CS-4].

**Route 3 F2/F3:** Cooling crystallization has low condition severity because SS-4 and TS-3 use saturated aqueous solutions and slow cooling rather than high-temperature solid-state reaction [SS-4][TS-3]. Insufficient evidence from CS supports cooling-specific crystallization directly, so CS is neutral for this route signal (50) [CS]. Kinetics are favorable in solution, but structural transferability is slightly weaker than Route 1 because the strongest cooling analogue is a Cs hydrogen selenate rather than a Rb/K arsenate [SS-4][Target].

**Route 4 F2/F3:** Gel/solvent diffusion is mild in temperature and pressure, but it receives a multi-step penalty because SS-3 requires gel preparation and long diffusion growth [SS-3]. Insufficient evidence from CS supports gel/diffusion routes, so CS is neutral for this route signal (50) [CS]. Kinetics are intentionally slow, which helps crystal quality but lowers throughput and increases uncertainty over weeks to months [SS-3][TS-2].

**F4 — Amorphization Tendency:** CS/SS/TS do not directly report amorphization tendency, so all three source categories are neutral for this factor at 50 [CS][SS][TS]. The medium-risk F4 score comes from target-based triggers: likely large unit cell and conservative cation-size mismatch, while edge/face-sharing coexistence is absent [Target][Reasonable Inference]. The risk is moderated by the fact that multiple related hydrated oxysalts crystallize successfully from solution [CS-1][SS-4][TS-5].

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

Rb₃KAs₄(H₂O₇)₂ is best viewed as a near-hull, likely metastable-to-marginally-stable phase rather than an unequivocal ground-state phase because its E_hull is exactly 0.0500 eV/atom [Target]. The CS and SS data strongly support aqueous crystallization of related alkali hydrogen oxoanion salts, while TS data supports accessibility of similarly metastable hydrated oxysalts [CS-1][SS-1][SS-4][TS-1][TS-5]. The most critical enabling factor is the high solution mobility and acid-base reactivity of alkali carbonates/hydroxides with arsenic acid, which should allow low-temperature assembly of the protonated As–O framework [CS-1][CS-2][Reasonable Inference]. The most critical limiting factor is phase selectivity among competing Rb/K hydrogen arsenates or condensed arsenates, because related CS analogues show mixtures, twinning, and accompanying phases under nearby conditions [CS-1][CS-5]. Overall, the target is **reasonably synthesizable**, with best prospects from slow acidic evaporation or closely controlled cooling crystallization [CS-1][SS-4][Reasonable Inference].
