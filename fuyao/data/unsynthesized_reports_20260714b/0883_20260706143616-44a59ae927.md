### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

#### F1 — Thermodynamic stability

The target has **E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom**, giving a high F1 score of 98 under the required scoring rubric. Chemically related sulfides and sulfide chlorides are also reported as hull-stable, including Pb₂GeS₄, InBi₂S₄Cl, SiPb₂S₄, and SiPb₂Se₄ (CS-1, CS-2, CS-4, CS-5). TS analogues are mostly hull-stable but are chemically dominated by Cs/alkali compounds, so they provide only generic stability support; SS analogues provide insufficient target-specific thermodynamic evidence and are treated as neutral 50.

#### F2 — Route 1 severity

Route 1 uses temperatures comparable to CS solid-state syntheses, typically 600–800 °C in evacuated sealed tubes (CS-1, CS-4, CS-5), so no high-temperature penalty above 1000 °C applies. Vacuum sealing and volatile sulfur/chlorine components create moderate severity, as sealed ampoules are explicitly required in CS-1, CS-4, and CS-5. Toxic Pb/Sb reagents add a penalty [Reasonable Inference], while high-pressure equipment is not supported or required by CS/SS/TS.

#### F3 — Route 1 kinetics

Solid-state reaction from sulfides/halides should have moderate precursor activity; CS-1 shows appropriate metal sulfides and elemental sulfur can form complex Pb sulfides. Diffusion is not expected to be refractory because Pb and Sb are low-melting heavy p-block elements [Reasonable Inference], and CS analogues form at 600–800 °C (CS-1, CS-4, CS-5). The main kinetic penalty is structural reconstruction into a complex Pb/Sb/S/Cl ordered framework, and CS-1 reports iterative firing/XRD verification for phase control.

#### F2 — Route 2 severity

CVT conditions are moderate in temperature: CS-2 uses 600–550 °C for InBi₂S₄Cl, and CS-3 uses 430/350 °C for Sn₂SbS₂I₃. The severity comes from evacuated sealed ampoules and volatile/corrosive transport agents such as S₂Cl₂ or I₂, both supported by CS-2 and CS-3. Toxic Pb/Sb/halide reagents add risk [Reasonable Inference]; SS/TS provide insufficient useful signal for this route and are neutral 50 for route-specific severity.

#### F3 — Route 2 kinetics

CVT has favorable crystal-growth kinetics because CS-2 directly demonstrates transport growth of a sulfide chloride, and CS-3 demonstrates transport growth of an Sb sulfohalide. However, composition control is only moderate because CS-2 states that chlorine excess changes transport rate and that related bromide/iodide analogues encountered impurity or synthesis failure. The uncertainty is whether Pb and Sb transport at compatible rates under the same chlorine/sulfur chemical potentials [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2 — Route 3 severity

The flux/self-flux route is estimated at 500–650/700 °C, consistent with CS-2 and CS solid-state temperature windows, so it avoids extreme temperature penalties. It still requires sealed evacuated ampoules due to sulfur/chloride volatility, as supported by CS-1, CS-2, CS-4, and CS-5. Pb/Sb chlorides and sulfides are toxic and potentially volatile [Reasonable Inference], so the route remains moderate rather than easy.

#### F3 — Route 3 kinetics

A molten chloride/sulfide flux should improve ion mobility and reduce reliance on solid-state diffusion [Reasonable Inference], which is why Route 3 receives the highest F3 score. CS-2 supports chlorine-assisted growth of a related sulfide chloride, and CS-1/4/5 show heavy-metal chalcogenides form in sealed tubes at comparable temperatures. The limitation is that excess flux can shift stoichiometry or stabilize competing Pb/Sb chloride/sulfide phases [Reasonable Inference from CS-2 phase-control concerns].

#### F4 — Amorphization tendency

The target has substantially different Pb and Sb coordination environments and likely a large crystallographic cell, both of which increase configurational and topological complexity [Reasonable Inference from the target structure]. The target framework includes corner- and edge-sharing polyhedra, but the description does not indicate face-sharing, so only two of the three amorphization checks are triggered. CS/SS/TS do not provide direct amorphization data; therefore, their signal for F4 is insufficient and treated as neutral 50, with the score based mainly on structural complexity criteria.

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

Pb₄Sb₈S₁₀Cl is likely a **ground-state phase** because its reported energy above hull is 0.0000 eV/atom. The strongest synthesis support comes from chemically similar CS examples: Pb chalcogenides form by sealed-tube solid-state reactions, and a related sulfide chloride, InBi₂S₄Cl, forms by CVT using S₂Cl₂ (CS-1, CS-2, CS-4, CS-5).
