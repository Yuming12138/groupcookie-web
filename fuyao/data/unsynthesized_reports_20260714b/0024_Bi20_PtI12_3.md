### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability**  
The target’s E_hull of 0.0500 eV/atom places it exactly at the high-score cutoff, so it is likely a low-lying metastable phase rather than a clear ground-state compound. [Target] Synthesized TS analogues span similar energies, including BiBr₃ at ~0.0617 eV/atom and Bi–I/Bi–Br phases near 0.027–0.039 eV/atom, so this level of metastability is experimentally plausible. [TS] CS and SS analogues reinforce that low-dimensional heavy iodides can be made near or slightly above the hull. [CS][SS]

**F2 — Route 1**  
Route 1 avoids extreme temperature and high pressure, matching CS/SS/TS sealed-ampoule halide practice. [CS][SS][TS] Its deductions come from vacuum/Ar control, volatile iodide handling, and toxic/moisture-sensitive halides. [CS][SS][TS][Reasonable Inference] The main uncertainty is whether PtI₂ or Pt metal reacts fast enough at 280–350 °C, which is not directly answered by CS/SS/TS. [CS][SS][TS]

**F2 — Route 2**  
Route 2 uses moderate CVT temperatures but requires evacuated tubes, volatile iodine species, a temperature gradient, and multi-step crystal-growth handling. [SS][TS] The severity is higher than Route 1 because Bi₂TeI CVT analogues show small yields and multiphase outcomes. [SS][TS] CS has insufficient evidence for CVT transferability and is treated as neutral source score 50 for this route. [CS]

**F2 — Route 3**  
Route 3 is supported by CS Bridgman growth of TlPbI₃, but the target would require sealed high-temperature iodide melt handling. [CS] The route receives deductions for vacuum, volatile iodides, multistep directional growth, and toxic iodide handling. [CS][Reasonable Inference] SS and TS provide insufficient Bridgman-specific support and are neutral source score 50 for this route. [SS][TS]

**F2 — Route 4**  
Route 4 stays at moderate temperature, but flux loading, sealed iodine-rich growth, slow cooling, and flux removal make it operationally more complex. [CS][SS][TS][Reasonable Inference] The route is plausible because halide-rich media are compatible with low-dimensional iodides, but it lacks direct CS/SS/TS flux precedent for Pt–Bi–I. [Reasonable Inference] Direct flux evidence from CS/SS/TS is therefore neutral source score 50. [CS][SS][TS]

**F3 — Route 1**  
Halide precursors are reactive because CS/SS/TS analogues form iodides/bismuth halides at relatively low temperatures. [CS][SS][TS] Kinetics are limited by Pt incorporation and by the need to assemble a complex 59-atom cluster. [Target][Reasonable Inference] The score is moderate because structural motifs are partly supported by CS zero-dimensional iodides and SS/TS Bi–I polyhedra, but PtBi₆ units are not directly demonstrated. [CS][SS][TS][Target]

**F3 — Route 2**  
CVT improves transport through volatile iodide species and can bypass slow bulk diffusion. [SS][TS][Reasonable Inference] The penalty is structural and compositional control because the Bi₂TeI CVT analogue produced multiple phases, small crystals, and twinning. [SS][TS] Pt transport remains the largest kinetic unknown because no CS/SS/TS source demonstrates Pt–iodide transport into a Bi–I cluster. [CS][SS][TS]

**F3 — Route 3**  
Melt growth improves macroscopic transport and is supported for TlPbI₃-type heavy iodides. [CS] The Pt source may remain poorly soluble or sluggish unless PtI₂ is used. [Reasonable Inference] The target’s low-lying cluster phase may not survive the melt or may crystallize competing Bi–I phases. [SS][TS][Target][Reasonable Inference]

**F3 — Route 4**  
A BiI₃-rich flux should improve precursor activity and shorten diffusion distances. [Reasonable Inference] The score is higher kinetically than Route 1 because liquid/flux transport can help Pt incorporation. [Reasonable Inference] The uncertainty is phase selectivity because no direct CS/SS/TS flux example exists for Pt–Bi–I clusters. [CS][SS][TS]

**F4 — Amorphization Tendency**

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

Bi₂₀(PtI₁₂)₃ is most likely a **low-lying metastable phase**, not a strict ground-state phase, because its E_hull is positive at 0.0500 eV/atom. [Target] The CS data strongly supports low-temperature sealed iodide synthesis, while SS and TS data support Bi–halide ampoule and vapor-transport chemistry with known phase-control challenges. [CS][SS][TS] The most important enabling factor is the availability of reactive iodide media such as BiI₃/I₂ that can form low-dimensional heavy-halide structures at modest temperature. [CS][SS][TS][Reasonable Inference] The most important limiting factor is selective formation of the PtBi₆-containing cluster instead of competing BiI/BiI₃ or other Bi-rich iodides. [SS][TS][Target][Reasonable Inference] Overall, synthesis is plausible but should be approached as a phase-screening problem rather than a guaranteed single-shot solid-state reaction. [CS][SS][TS][Reasonable Inference]
