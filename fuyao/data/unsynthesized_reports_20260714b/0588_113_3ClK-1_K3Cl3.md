### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic stability.**  
The target E_hull is 0.0440 eV/atom, which is inside the ≤0.05 eV/atom high-synthesizability bin (target). RbBr with E_hull ≈0.0316 eV/atom is reported as accessible by low-temperature evaporation, showing that alkali-halide phases at comparable metastability can be experimentally observed (CS1, TS3). SS provides insufficient direct evidence for this exact KCl polymorph’s thermodynamic stability, so SS is treated as neutral for direct F1 support.

**F2 — Route 1 condition severity.**  
Route 1 requires vacuum and low-temperature control, which are controlled but not extreme conditions (CS1, CS4, TS3). It avoids high pressure, high-temperature ceramic processing, and toxic reagents, so its main deduction is the vacuum requirement (CS1, CS4, TS3). The uncertainty is that the equipment is specialized and low-temperature substrate control is essential, but the operation itself is simpler than sealed high-temperature chloride chemistry.

**F3 — Route 1 reaction kinetics.**  
Vapor availability is favorable because alkali halides are directly evaporated in the references (CS1, CS4, TS3). The crystallization pathway is the limiting kinetic factor because CS1 and TS3 explicitly note abnormal alkali-halide phase formation is temperature-sensitive and metastable. Composition control is relatively good because single-source KCl evaporation should maintain 1:1 stoichiometry [Reasonable Inference], but phase selection may be narrow (CS1, TS3).

**F2 — Route 2 condition severity.**  
Sealed-ampoule chloride synthesis uses dry Ar or vacuum, drying, sealing, and controlled heating/cooling (SS1, SS2, SS4, SS5, TS5). The temperature is moderate relative to oxide ceramics, but the sealed system and multi-step handling justify deductions (SS2, SS4, TS5). The main uncertainty is whether quench or slow cooling would favor the target polymorph [Reasonable Inference].

**F3 — Route 2 reaction kinetics.**  
The precursor is already KCl, so chemical diffusion is easy relative to multi-cation refractory systems [Reasonable Inference], and analogous chlorides are made at 300–400 °C or modest temperatures (SS1, SS2, SS4, SS5). However, structural reconstruction into the 2D tetragonal P4/mmm polymorph is not directly demonstrated by SS and is likely difficult because bulk annealing tends to favor equilibrium phases [Reasonable Inference]. SS5 and SS4 show that halide phase purity can require repeated annealing or careful cooling, reducing the structural-reconstruction score.

**F2 — Route 3 condition severity.**  
Flux growth uses controlled dry atmospheres and sealed tubes, as shown for chloride systems and molten-salt syntheses (SS4, SS5, CS5). A chloride eutectic can keep temperatures below 1000 °C [Reasonable Inference from SS4], but flux removal and dry handling add steps. CS5’s higher-temperature flux route is less preferred for KCl because it increases volatility and equilibrium-phase risk [Reasonable Inference].

**F3 — Route 3 reaction kinetics.**  
Flux/liquid media improve ion mobility, supported by molten-salt and slow-cooling flux analogues (CS5, SS4). The kinetic weakness is not ion transport but nucleating the desired metastable 2D KCl phase rather than normal KCl [Reasonable Inference]. Phase-control issues in SS4 and SS5 support a moderate-to-high risk of competing phases.

**F4 — Amorphization tendency.**  
The target has a single cation species, so the >15% cation-radius mismatch criterion does not apply (target). The described P4/mmm structure has two inequivalent K and two inequivalent Cl sites, giving insufficient evidence for a >40-atom cell and likely indicating a small cell [Reasonable Inference]. The target contains edge and corner sharing but no face-sharing coexistence, so the specified C3 trigger is not met (target).

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

The target is most likely a metastable rather than true ground-state KCl polymorph because its E_hull is 0.0440 eV/atom above the hull (target). The strongest synthesis support comes from CS1/CS4/TS3, where alkali halides are deposited at low temperature and abnormal metastable structures are accessed under vacuum. Structural analogues support dry chloride handling, sealed ampoules, and slow cooling, but they do not directly demonstrate this pure KCl layered tetragonal structure, so SS support is indirect. The most critical enabling factor is kinetic trapping by low-temperature deposition before the film can relax to the ordinary KCl phase (CS1, CS4, TS3). The most critical limiting factor is phase competition with conventional KCl polymorphs and the narrow temperature/deposition-rate window reported for abnormal alkali-halide structures (CS1, TS3).
