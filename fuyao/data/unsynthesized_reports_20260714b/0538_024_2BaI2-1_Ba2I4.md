### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

#### F1 — Thermodynamic stability

The target E_hull of 0.043 eV/atom lies within the ≤0.05 eV/atom high-synthesizability range, so the thermodynamic score is high. This is comparable to the thermodynamic analogue BaBrCl at 0.0484 eV/atom in TS1, while exact BaI₂ polymorphs in CS1/SS3 and CS2/SS2 are at 0.0032 and 0.0 eV/atom, respectively. The remaining uncertainty is that the target is not the computed ground-state BaI₂ polymorph, so it is likely metastable rather than equilibrium-accessible; that inference is supported by quench-retained BaI₂ polymorph behavior in CS1/SS3 and CS2/SS2.

#### F2 — Route 1 condition severity

Route 1 is severe because it requires GPa-scale pressure and quenching, even though the rubric does not deduct for pressure below 5 GPa; the need for a belt-type or modified belt apparatus is explicit in CS1/SS3 and CS2/SS2. The temperature is high but not clearly above the >1000 °C deduction threshold, because CS2/SS2 uses 800–900 °C and CS1/SS3 uses 1273 K, approximately 1000 °C. The main deductions come from volatile/dry iodide handling, multi-step pressure-temperature-quench control, and air-sensitive/protective handling, all supported by CS1/SS3 and CS2/SS2.

#### F3 — Route 1 kinetics

Route 1 has favorable kinetics because BaI₂ is already the correct composition, as in CS1/SS3 and CS2/SS2. Ionic halides can reconstruct more readily than refractory oxides, and exact BaI₂ polymorphs were obtained in hours to days in CS1/SS3 and CS2/SS2. The kinetic penalty is structural: the target is a layered Pmmn phase with 6-coordinate Ba, while the BaI₂ analogues in CS1/SS3 and CS2/SS2 are denser 9-coordinate polymorphs, so the correct pressure/cooling path is uncertain.

#### F2 — Route 2 condition severity

Route 2 avoids high pressure, which is favorable relative to CS1/SS3 and CS2/SS2. However, evacuated or sealed halide ampoules are supported by CS3/SS4 and TS3, slow cooling is supported by CS3/SS4 and TS1, and dry inert handling is supported by CS2/SS2 and TS1, giving multiple severity deductions. The remaining uncertainty is whether molten BaI₂ in a sealed tube can form the target at all, because exact BaI₂ polymorph data in CS1/SS3 and CS2/SS2 points toward pressure control.

#### F3 — Route 2 kinetics

Route 2 has good diffusion kinetics because melt or near-melt halide recrystallization reduces solid-state diffusion limitations, consistent with halide melt/slow-cooling examples in CS3/SS4 and TS1. The direct precursor BaI₂ also makes composition control simple, as shown by BaI₂ precursor use in CS1/SS3, CS2/SS2, SS1, and TS2. The structural-reconstruction score is lower because the desired layered Pmmn polymorph is not demonstrated by any CS/SS/TS ambient-pressure BaI₂ analogue.

#### F2 — Route 3 condition severity

Route 3 uses lower temperatures, supported by 300–600 °C in CS5 and 350–620 °C in TS5, so thermal severity is moderate. The route is penalized for vacuum sealing, volatile HgI₂, toxic Hg compounds, multi-step sublimation/annealing, and inert handling, all reported in CS5 and TS5. SS gives insufficient evidence for Ba-specific reactive iodination/metathesis and is neutral for the condition-transfer claim.

#### F3 — Route 3 kinetics

Route 3 benefits from reactive iodination and vapor/transport chemistry, as HgI₂-assisted synthesis is documented in CS5 and TS5. It is structurally attractive because CS5 reports a 2D MI₂ sheet with T-shaped iodide coordination, similar to the target’s 2D BaI₂ sheet and T-shaped iodide environment. The uncertainty is chemical transferability: TmI₂ stabilization in CS5 involves rare-earth divalency, whereas BaI₂ is an alkaline-earth iodide, so the route may simply yield a known BaI₂ polymorph.

#### F4 — Amorphization tendency

The amorphization risk is low because the target contains only one cation, so the cation-radius-mismatch criterion is not triggered by the target composition. The structure is simple and layered with edge-sharing BaI₆ units according to the target description, and no simultaneous vertex/edge/face-sharing network is reported. CS/SS/TS provide insufficient evidence for a >40-atom target unit cell, so that check is treated as not triggered rather than penalized.

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

The target is likely a metastable BaI₂ polymorph rather than the ground state, because its E_hull is 0.043 eV/atom and exact BaI₂ analogues in CS1/SS3 and CS2/SS2 include lower-energy BaI₂ polymorphs. The strongest support for synthesis comes from CS1/SS3 and CS2/SS2, which show that BaI₂ polymorphs can be made by high-pressure treatment and retained by quenching. The most critical enabling factor is the use of anhydrous BaI₂ as a direct same-composition precursor, minimizing compositional complexity, as shown in CS1/SS3 and CS2/SS2. The most critical limiting factor is phase selectivity: the target layered Pmmn structure is not the same as the high-pressure BaI₂ phases reported in CS1/SS3 and CS2/SS2, so pressure, temperature, and quench schedule must be screened. Overall, synthesis is feasible but most credible as a quench-trapped polymorph rather than a straightforward equilibrium product.
