### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

#### F1 — Thermodynamic Stability

The target has E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom, so it is scored near the top of the ≤0.05 eV/atom thermodynamic-stability bracket (Target data). The closest thermodynamic analogues include Nb3TeI7 with E_hull = 0 or ~0.0009 eV/atom and Nb3I8 with E_hull = 0, supporting the stability of related layered niobium iodide frameworks (TS1, TS2, TS3). The remaining uncertainty is that CS/SS/TS do not contain a direct experimental Nb3SI7 synthesis, so sulfur-specific stability against Nb sulfides/iodides is not directly established.

#### F2 — Route 1

Route 1 uses moderate temperatures because Nb3TeI7 analogues form at 600–800 °C, below the >1000 °C penalty threshold (CS3, CS4; SS1, SS2; TS1, TS2). The main severity penalties come from vacuum sealing and volatile/toxic iodine, since all close analogues use evacuated sealed ampoules or tubes with iodine (CS1–CS5; SS1–SS5; TS1–TS5). No high pressure is needed, which is favorable (CS3, CS4; SS1, SS2; TS1, TS2).

#### F3 — Route 1

Kinetics are limited by refractory Nb metal, but the successful formation of Nb3TeI7 at 600–800 °C indicates iodine-assisted reaction chemistry can overcome some diffusion barriers (CS3, CS4; SS1, SS2; TS1, TS2). The structural reconstruction score is relatively good because the target is isostructural or nearly isostructural with P3m1 Nb3TeI7 except for S replacing Te (CS3; SS2; TS2). The uncertainty is that smaller S may alter phase stability and may favor alternative Nb–S–I frameworks, for which direct evidence is insufficient from CS/SS/TS.

#### F2 — Route 2

Route 2 is less thermally severe because Ta3QI7 analogues were made at 450 °C for about one week (CS1, CS2). It still requires evacuated sealed ampoules and volatile iodine, so the same vacuum/volatile/toxic precursor deductions apply (CS1, CS2). This route is not penalized for high pressure or >1000 °C conditions because the references use standard evacuated fused silica ampoules at moderate temperature (CS1, CS2).

#### F3 — Route 2

The kinetic score is lower because Nb3TeI7 analogues require 600–800 °C, suggesting 450 °C may be too slow for Nb incorporation even though Ta3QI7 forms at that temperature (CS1–CS4). The M3QI7 layered framework is supported chemically by Ta3SeI7/Ta3TeI7 (CS1, CS2), but those syntheses had persistent side products, which lowers the structural/phase-control component (CS1, CS2). Thus, Route 2 is plausible but more suited as a low-temperature screening experiment than as the primary route.

#### F2 — Route 3

Route 3 adds complexity because chemical vapor transport typically requires a prereaction or purification stage plus a calibrated temperature gradient, unlike single-step direct solid-state synthesis (SS3; TS3). It remains below the >1000 °C threshold, since Nb3I8 CVT and related halide transport routes operate in the 500–800 °C range or around 823–923 K (SS3, SS5; TS3, TS5). Penalties are assigned for vacuum sealing, volatile/toxic iodine, and multi-step processing (SS3; TS3).

#### F3 — Route 3

CVT receives the best kinetics score because transport through volatile iodide species can reduce the need for long-range solid-state diffusion [Reasonable Inference], and Nb3I8 was synthesized/purified by chemical transport under similar halide conditions (SS3; TS3). The target’s Nb–I sheet chemistry resembles both Nb3TeI7 and Nb3I8, supporting structural transferability (SS2, SS3; TS2, TS3). The key uncertainty is that no source directly proves sulfur retention during Nb–S–I CVT, so the gradient and iodine chemical potential must be optimized.

#### F4 — Amorphization Tendency

The target contains only one cation, Nb, so the cation-radius-mismatch amorphization trigger is not activated [Reasonable Inference from target formula]. The primitive formula Nb3SI7 is small, and the reported layered crystal structure is ordered rather than compositionally complex [Reasonable Inference from target description]. CS/SS/TS provide insufficient direct information on mixed polyhedral connectivity for Nb3SI7, so that check is not triggered; overall amorphization risk is low.

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

Nb3SI7 is likely a ground-state or near-ground-state phase because its reported energy above hull is 0.0000 eV/atom, and thermodynamically similar layered niobium iodides also lie at or near the hull (TS1–TS3). The analogue support is strong for the Nb3QI7 framework because Nb3TeI7 exists in both P3m1 and P6₃mc-related layered forms and is synthesized directly from elements (CS3, CS4; SS1, SS2; TS1, TS2). The most important enabling factor is iodine-assisted sealed-tube chemistry, which allows formation or transport of niobium iodide frameworks at 500–800 °C rather than requiring temperatures near the melting point of Nb (CS3, CS4; SS3; TS3). The most important limiting factor is phase selectivity: related Ta3QI7 and Nb3TeI7 systems show side products or polytype competition (CS1, CS2; CS3, CS4; SS1, SS2; TS1, TS2). Overall, synthesis is feasible but likely requires careful stoichiometry, ampoule design, and temperature-gradient optimization.
