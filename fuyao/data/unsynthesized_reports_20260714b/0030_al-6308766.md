**Not carried forward:** high-temperature flux growth is not treated as a plausible primary route because the CS5 synthesis record is compositionally mismatched: it reports ZnO/Sb2O3 flux growth for a RbHSeO4 entry, so there is **insufficient evidence from CS** for transferring that flux route to K2As2H2O7; source score = neutral 50 for flux feasibility [CS5]. Thin-film/vapor deposition is also not carried forward because no CS/SS/TS source reports vapor deposition for analogous protonated oxyanion salts; **insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS**, source score = neutral 50.
