### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability**  
The target E_hull of 0.0500 eV/atom is at the favorable ≤0.05 eV/atom threshold, so the base thermodynamic score is high [Target]. Synthesized TS analogues with E_hull values around 0.050–0.075 eV/atom support the idea that low-positive-hull Mn pnictides can be experimentally accessible [TS1, TS2, TS3, TS4]. The main uncertainty is polymorph competition because exact BaMn₂As₂ is reported as a tetragonal E_hull = 0 phase, while the target is monoclinic and slightly metastable [CS1, SS1, Target]. No CS/SS/TS source directly reports monoclinic C2/m BaMn₂As₂ synthesis, so that polymorph-specific evidence is insufficient and neutral 50 [CS1–CS5, SS1–SS5, TS1–TS5].

**F2 — Route 1**  
Route 1 is penalized for >1000 °C heating, Ar controlled atmosphere, sealed volatile As handling, and toxic/air-sensitive precursor risk [CS1, SS1, Reasonable Inference]. It avoids >1400 °C heating, high pressure, and complex multistep processing, which keeps the severity moderate rather than severe [CS1, SS1]. TS gives insufficient direct evidence for this exact route and is neutral 50 for Route 1 condition severity [TS1–TS5].

**F3 — Route 1**  
The kinetics are moderate because elemental Ba/Mn/As are simple but not highly reactive molecular precursors, and Mn diffusion remains a solid-state limitation [CS1, SS1, Reasonable Inference]. Structural reconstruction is favorable because exact BaMn₂As₂ has the same local Ba–As and MnAs₄ motifs, but unfavorable polymorph selection is required to reach monoclinic C2/m rather than tetragonal I4/mmm [CS1, SS1, Target]. This yields a mid-high F3 score of 65 rather than an easy-kinetics score [CS1, SS1, Target].

**F2 — Route 2**  
Route 2 avoids >1000 °C in Celsius because 1200–1250 K is ~927–977 °C, but it requires vacuum tubes, volatile As containment, multiple handling/annealing steps, and toxic/air-sensitive precursor precautions [CS4, SS2, TS1, TS3, Reasonable Inference]. The multistep regrind/reseal workflow is the main severity penalty beyond atmosphere and precursor risk [CS4, SS2, TS1, TS3]. CS, SS, and TS all provide useful evidence for this route through Eu/Yb Mn–As analogues [CS4, SS2, TS1, TS3].

**F3 — Route 2**  
The long anneal and regrinding indicate slower ordering kinetics than Route 1 despite lower thermal severity [CS4, SS2, TS1, TS3]. MnAs₄ frameworks in Eu/Yb analogues support structural transfer, but the A-site and space group differ from BaMn₂As₂, so reconstruction is only moderate [CS4, SS2, TS1, TS3, Target]. The resulting F3 score is 58 because kinetics are diffusion-limited and polymorph transfer is uncertain [CS4, SS2, TS1, TS3].

**F2 — Route 3**  
Flux growth is penalized for high-temperature target adaptation, controlled Ar/vacuum handling, volatile As containment, multistep slow-cooling/decanting, and toxic/air-sensitive precursor risk [CS1, CS2, CS3, SS4, Reasonable Inference]. It does not require >1400 °C or >5 GPa pressure based on the supplied flux precedents [CS2, CS3, SS4]. TS provides only limited exact-condition support for Ba–Mn–As flux growth, so that exact-route TS evidence is neutral 50 [TS2].

**F3 — Route 3**  
Flux improves kinetics through liquid-mediated transport and can produce single crystals in related Mn pnictides [CS2, CS3, SS4, TS2]. BaMn₂Sb₂ is a close BaMn₂Pn₂ structural analogue, which supports transfer of the tetrahedral MnPn₄ framework, but Sb→As substitution and target polymorph control remain uncertain [CS2, SS4, Target]. The F3 score is highest among the routes at 72, but the route is not the best overall because F2 severity is worse than direct solid-state synthesis [CS2, CS3, SS4].

**F2 — Route 4**  
Melt/Bridgman synthesis is penalized for high-temperature target adaptation, Ar pressure/control, volatile As sealed-vessel requirements, multiple processing steps, and toxic/air-sensitive precursor risk [SS5, CS1, Reasonable Inference]. The 20 atm Ar pressure used in the precedent is not high pressure by the >5 GPa rubric, so no high-pressure deduction is applied [SS5]. CS and TS provide insufficient evidence for this route and are neutral 50 for route-specific condition severity [CS1–CS5, TS1–TS5].

**F3 — Route 4**  
The melt pathway improves precursor contact and diffusion compared with dry powder, but it introduces volatility and stoichiometry risks for Ba–Mn–As [SS5, Reasonable Inference].
