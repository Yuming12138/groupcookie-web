### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic stability.**  
The target E above hull is 0.042 eV/atom, which lies in the ≤0.05 eV/atom high-score range [Target]. This is consistent with chemically similar chlorides that are stable or nearly stable, including OsCl₄ Cmmm at 0 eV/atom and TaCl₄ at 0.025 eV/atom [CS1; CS4]. Thermodynamic analogues around 0.024–0.036 eV/atom are experimentally synthesized in sealed ampoules or CVT conditions, supporting accessibility of slightly metastable chlorides [TS1–TS5]. Remaining uncertainty is that the known OsCl₄ ground-state-like analogue is a different Cmmm ribbon polymorph, not the target P2₁/c sheet [CS1; SS1; Target].

**F2 — Route 1 severity.**  
Direct OsCl₄ synthesis uses only 525 °C, so the thermal severity is moderate, not extreme [CS1; SS1]. The major deductions come from reactive/toxic Cl₂, the need for a sealed pressurized chlorine setup, and the reported 6–8 atm pressure used to prevent dissociation [CS1; SS1]. There is insufficient evidence from TS for direct Os + Cl₂ synthesis of OsCl₄, so TS is neutral for this specific route.

**F3 — Route 1 kinetics.**  
The direct route suffers from low Os metal reactivity because Os is a refractory dense metal [Reasonable Inference], although chlorination by liquid/gaseous Cl₂ enables reaction at 525 °C [CS1; SS1]. The required 5-day hold implies slow reaction and/or ordering kinetics [CS1; SS1]. Structural reconstruction is significant because the known OsCl₄ product is 1D edge-sharing, while the target requires a 2D corner-sharing sheet [CS1; SS1; Target].

**F2 — Route 2 severity.**  
Sealed-ampoule chloride recrystallization avoids very high temperatures and is supported by IrCl₃, HfCl₄, TaCl₄, PdCl₂, and other chloride analogues [CS2; CS3; CS4; TS5; TS4]. The severity remains moderate because vacuum or chlorine chemical-potential control, volatile chlorides, and sealed-tube pressure are required [CS1; CS2; CS4; TS4]. The main uncertainty is the correct Cl₂ pressure: OsCl₄ specifically requires chlorine pressure to avoid dissociation, whereas several analogues use evacuated ampoules [CS1 versus CS2/CS4/TS4].

**F3 — Route 2 kinetics.**  
Vapor transport is kinetically favorable compared with direct solid-state diffusion because volatile chloride species can migrate and recrystallize, as seen in HfCl₄ sublimation, IrCl₃ CVT, PdCl₂ sublimation, and chloride gradient growth [CS3; CS2; TS5; TS4]. Composition control is only moderate because chlorine loss can shift OsCl₄ toward OsCl₃ [CS1; SS1]. Polymorph formation remains uncertain because no CS/SS/TS source reports this exact P2₁/c 2D OsCl₄ polymorph.

**F2 — Route 3 severity.**  
Solvothermal chloride routes operate at relatively low temperatures, with examples at 150 °C and 140–155 °C in sealed ampoules [TS3; TS4], and MoCl₅ polymorphs at 473–573 K [SS4]. However, the route requires volatile/toxic chloride solvents, sealed ampoules, inert or evacuated handling, and controlled cooling, so the condition severity is not low [SS4; TS3]. There is insufficient direct evidence from CS for solvothermal OsCl₄, so CS is neutral for this route.

**F3 — Route 3 kinetics.**  
Chloride-rich liquids or solvothermal media can greatly improve transport and crystallization kinetics compared with Os metal diffusion, as indicated by low-temperature chloride ampoule syntheses [TS3; TS4] and solvothermal polymorph control in MoCl₅ [SS4]. This is the strongest kinetic route for exploring metastable polymorphs, because solvent and cooling rate can alter polymorph outcome [SS4]. Its weakness is the lack of direct OsCl₄ demonstration, making the score partly inferential.

**F4 — Amorphization tendency.**  
The target contains only one cation and a simple OsCl₄ composition, so cation-size disorder is not expected [Target]. The target connectivity is described as corner-sharing OsCl₆ octahedra, so mixed connectivity does not trigger the amorphization penalty [Target]. The unit-cell atom count is not explicitly given; based on the simple P2₁/c OsCl₄ structure it is unlikely to exceed 40 atoms, but this remains a [Reasonable Inference] with insufficient direct evidence from CS/SS/TS for the target cell size.

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

The target P2₁/c OsCl₄ phase is likely a low-energy metastable polymorph rather than the known ground-state analogue, because its E above hull is 0.042 eV/atom while the closest reported OsCl₄ Cmmm polymorph is at 0 eV/atom [Target; CS1; SS1]. The CS and SS data strongly support that OsCl₄ itself is synthesizable, but they also show that the known OsCl₄ structure is 1D edge-sharing rather than the target 2D corner-sharing sheet [CS1; SS1; Target]. TS data support the broader idea that chlorides with similar modest hull energies can be synthesized in sealed ampoules, CVT, or solvothermal chloride media [TS1–TS5]. The most critical enabling factor is maintaining a sufficiently high chlorine chemical potential to prevent OsCl₄ decomposition [CS1; SS1]. The most critical limiting factor is polymorph selectivity: the target requires corner-sharing 2D sheets, while the directly documented OsCl₄ phase forms edge-sharing ribbons [CS1; SS1; Target].
