### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic stability.**  
The target has E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom, which places it in the highest stability bin. Closely related Cs₂AgCl₃ and CsAgCl₂ are also on the hull, and Rb₂AgBr₃ is on the hull, supporting stable alkali–Ag–halide chemistry (CS2, CS4, TS1, TS2, TS4). Rb₂CuCl₃ is only 0.0078 eV/atom above hull, showing that the Pnma A₂MCl₃ framework is nearly thermodynamically competitive even with Cu substitution (CS1, SS2). Remaining uncertainty is mainly experimental phase competition rather than thermodynamic instability.

**F2 — Route 1 severity.**  
The solid-state route uses moderate temperatures of 373–583 K and does not require high pressure, which keeps severity low (CS1, SS2, TS4). The score is reduced because evacuated ampoules and multiple grinding/annealing cycles are explicitly reported (CS1, SS2, TS4). There is insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS that RbCl or AgCl volatility alone requires a penalty, so no separate volatility deduction was applied.

**F3 — Route 1 kinetics.**  
Binary chloride precursors are reactive relative to refractory oxides, and analogue formation at ≤583 K indicates favorable halide diffusion/reaction kinetics (CS1, SS2, TS4). The target’s Pnma structure is strongly supported by Rb₂CuCl₃ and Rb₂AgBr₃ analogues, reducing the structural-reconstruction penalty (CS1, SS2, TS4). The main kinetic uncertainty is the need for long annealing and repeated grinding to achieve single phase, which the sources explicitly note (CS1, TS4).

**F2 — Route 2 severity.**  
Cooling crystallization uses warm saturated solution and cooling, so no high-temperature or high-pressure penalty applies (SS1, SS5, TS1). CO₂ flushing is reported but does not meet the specified H₂/NH₃/vacuum penalty category (SS1, SS5, TS1). The source reports simple dissolution and cooling, so severity is low, but direct Rb₂AgCl₃ evidence is absent.

**F3 — Route 2 kinetics.**  
Solution crystallization bypasses solid-state diffusion and should be kinetically fast once soluble species are present, consistent with K₂CuCl₃ needles forming on cooling (SS5, TS1). However, AgCl solubility is a significant uncertainty [Reasonable Inference], and the source solution example is K–Cu–Cl rather than Rb–Ag–Cl (SS5). Therefore, the kinetic score is moderate rather than high.

**F2 — Route 3 severity.**  
Melt growth of Rb₂CdCl₄ occurs at 450 °C, so the temperature is still moderate by solid-state standards (CS3, SS4). The route is penalized for drying/loading/melting/slow cooling complexity and for the source’s emphasis on severe drying and precise crystallization (CS3, SS4). High pressure is not reported (CS3, SS4).

**F3 — Route 3 kinetics.**  
Melt growth provides excellent mass transport, as shown by crystallization after reaching fusion temperature in the Rb₂CdCl₄ reference (CS3, SS4). The weakness is structural transferability: Rb₂CdCl₄ is tetragonal and octahedral, not Pnma tetrahedral Rb₂AgCl₃ (CS3, SS4). The source also reports phase-control difficulty, so the structural component is reduced (CS3, SS4).

**F2 — Route 4 severity.**  
Slow evaporation from aqueous solution is mild and uses no specialized atmosphere or high temperature in the Rb₂ZnCl₄ reference (CS5). The source does not report high pressure, vacuum, or complex equipment (CS5). The absence of SS/TS support for this exact route is a transferability limitation, not a condition-severity penalty.

**F3 — Route 4 kinetics.**  
Solution crystallization generally minimizes diffusion barriers, and Rb₂ZnCl₄ single crystals were grown by slow evaporation (CS5). However, the structural and chemical match to Rb₂AgCl₃ is weak, and AgCl solubility/complexation is uncertain [Reasonable Inference]. Insufficient evidence from SS/TS for Rb₂AgCl₃ slow evaporation keeps the score moderate.

**F4 — Amorphization tendency.**  
The target contains large Rb⁺ and much smaller Ag⁺ coordination environments, reflected by long Rb–Cl distances and shorter Ag–Cl bonds in the target description; this triggers the cation-size contrast check [Reasonable Inference]. The target also has mixed face-, edge-, and corner-sharing polyhedral connectivity, directly triggering C3; similar mixed connectivity appears in Rb₂CuCl₃ and Rb₂AgBr₃ analogues (CS1, SS2, TS4). The unit-cell atom count trigger is not applied because explicit >40-atom evidence is insufficient from CS/SS/TS.
