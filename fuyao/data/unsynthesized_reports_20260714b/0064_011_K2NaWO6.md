### SECTION 1 — Possible Synthesis Routes

Citation key: **CS1–CS5**, **SS1–SS5**, and **TS1–TS5** refer to the numbered records in the provided Chemical, Structural, and Thermodynamic Similarity sets. **Target** refers to the supplied K2NaWO6 structure and E_hull data.

> Note: **SS1 is structurally useful but synthetically inconsistent**, because its synthesis snippet describes Cu12Sb4S13 rather than K3Na(MoO4)2; therefore SS1 is used only as structural evidence, and its synthesis conditions are treated as insufficient synthesis evidence / neutral 50 where relevant.  

#### Route 1: Ambient-Pressure Ceramic / Solid-State Oxide Route

| Field | Details |
|:--|:--|
| Reference basis | Ceramic tungstate/perovskite oxides are made from carbonates + WO3 in air with repeated grinding and slow cooling in CS1/CS2; K-containing orthovanadates are made from K2CO3-based solid-state reactions in air in CS3; Na molybdate analogues form by annealing Na2MoO4/MoO3 at 530 °C in SS2/TS1; K–Na–W oxide formation from Na2WO4/K2O at 725–750 °C is directly supported by SS4. |

| Atmosphere | Air or flowing O2; air is directly supported for CS1/CS2/CS3, while O2 is a [Reasonable Inference] because the target contains an O–O unit with 1.35 Å bond length. |

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

K2NaWO6 is best interpreted as a **near-threshold metastable or marginally stable phase**, because its E_hull is exactly 0.0500 eV/atom rather than clearly zero. The CS/SS/TS data strongly support that alkali tungstate/molybdate frameworks can be synthesized, especially via SS4 K–Na–W oxide chemistry, SS2/TS1 molybdate chemistry, and TS5 K–Na–W hydrothermal chemistry. The most critical enabling factor is **using a reactive, oxygen-rich route that can preserve or generate the target O–O unit**, which is inferred from the Target O–O bond and supported indirectly by CS5 KO2 chemistry. The most critical limiting factor is **phase selectivity versus competing ordinary tungstates, hydrated species, or oxygen-deficient alkali tungstates**, as indicated by impurities or byproducts in CS1/CS2, SS4/SS5, TS2, and TS3. Overall, the material appears **moderately synthesizable**, with the best first attempt being low-temperature oxidizing hydrothermal/peroxide screening followed by sealed alkali-superoxide flux as the strongest anhydrous backup route.
