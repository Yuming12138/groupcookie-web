The remaining uncertainty is that computed hull stability does not guarantee kinetic accessibility or hydration-free crystallization [Reasonable Inference].

#### Route 1 F2 — Hydrothermal condition severity

The direct Pb analogue uses moderate hydrothermal conditions: 240 °C, 4 days, and slow cooling in a Teflon-lined autoclave (CS-2 / SS-1 / TS-1). No high temperature, high pressure above 5 GPa, vacuum, inert gas, or toxic/air-sensitive precursor is reported for that analogue (CS-2 / SS-1 / TS-1). The main severity deduction is procedural: mixing, sealing/heating, holding, slow cooling, and filtration constitute a multi-operation synthesis. Uncertainty is low because the synthesis protocol is directly transferable in solvent, anions, alkali cation, and structure type [Reasonable Inference].

#### Route 1 F3 — Hydrothermal reaction kinetics

The hydrothermal route uses soluble salts, so precursor activity is high, consistent with Pb(NO₃)₂ and CsCl dissolving in water for the analogue synthesis (CS-2 / SS-1 / TS-1). Solution-mediated transport avoids slow solid-state diffusion, and the analogue crystallizes at 240 °C, indicating efficient ion mobility under hydrothermal conditions (CS-2 / SS-1 / TS-1). Structural reconstruction is favorable because the target is the Ca analogue of the same Cs₂MCl₂(NO₃)₂ framework [Reasonable Inference based on CS-2 / SS-1 / TS-1]. The main uncertainty is whether smaller Ca²⁺ stabilizes exactly the same coordination geometry as Pb²⁺ under identical conditions [Reasonable Inference].

#### Route 2 F2 — Aqueous evaporation/cooling condition severity

Aqueous evaporation and cooling are very mild; KNO₃ and CsNO₂ analogues crystallize from saturated aqueous solutions at room temperature or low temperature (SS-2, SS-3, TS-2, TS-3, TS-4). These sources do not indicate high pressure, high temperature, vacuum, or special atmosphere for synthesis (SS-2, SS-3, TS-2, TS-3, TS-4). The deduction is for multiple practical steps such as dissolution, filtration, slow evaporation, and crystal selection. The key uncertainty is that no source directly demonstrates the target mixed Cs–Ca nitrate chloride double salt by ambient evaporation; therefore this route is less certain than hydrothermal synthesis [Reasonable Inference].

#### Route 2 F3 — Aqueous evaporation/cooling kinetics

Precursor activity is excellent because all relevant salts are expected to be soluble and solution-crystallization analogues are well represented for nitrate/nitrite systems (SS-2, SS-3, TS-2, TS-3, TS-4; [Reasonable Inference]). The uncertainty is competition from simple salts or hydrates during evaporation [Reasonable Inference].

#### Route 3 F2 — Molten-salt / nitrate–chloride flux condition severity

For the nitrate target, the proposed flux temperature must be reduced to ~250–350 °C to avoid nitrate decomposition, which is a [Reasonable Inference] rather than directly supported by CS-4/CS-5. #### Route 3 F3 — Molten-salt / nitrate–chloride flux kinetics
