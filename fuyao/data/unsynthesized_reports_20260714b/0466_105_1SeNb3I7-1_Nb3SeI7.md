Remaining uncertainty is mostly experimental rather than thermodynamic: a hull-stable compound can still be hard to isolate if multiple low-energy polytypes or iodine-rich side phases compete [CS1, CS2, CS5].

#### F2 — Route 1

Route 1 uses moderate temperatures of 600–800 °C and no high pressure, which is favorable [CS1, CS2, SS1, TS1]. The deductions come from evacuated sealed-tube operation and volatile/toxic iodine, both directly present in the analogue syntheses [CS1–CS5, SS1–SS4, TS1–TS4]. The uncertainty is ampoule pressure and iodine activity, especially after replacing Te by more volatile/reactive Se [Reasonable Inference from Se/I use in CS4/CS5].

#### F3 — Route 1

Kinetics are limited by refractory Nb metal, but Nb₃TeI₇ forms from elements at 600–800 °C, implying iodine-assisted transport or reaction pathways are effective [CS1, CS2, SS1, TS1]. Structural reconstruction is highly favorable because trigonal Nb₃TeI₇ is essentially the Te analogue of the target P3m1 Nb₃SeI₇ [Target, CS1, SS2, TS2]. The main uncertainty is whether Se substitution changes phase selection toward NbSeI₃-type chains/ribbons [CS5, SS4, TS4].

#### F2 — Route 2

Route 2 is milder thermally because Ta₃QI₇ analogues were synthesized at 450 °C [CS3, CS4]. It still requires evacuated sealed ampoules and volatile iodine, so the severity score remains reduced [CS3, CS4]. Its practical difficulty is not temperature severity but low-temperature reaction completeness and side products [CS3, CS4].

#### F3 — Route 2

The CS/SS/TS evidence is strong for a sealed-ampoule elemental route because Nb₃TeI₇ with the same Nb₃QI₇ sheet chemistry is synthesized directly at 600–800 °C [CS1, CS2, SS1, TS1]. The most critical enabling factor is iodine-mediated sealed-tube chemistry, which allows refractory Nb to react at temperatures far below its melting point [Reasonable Inference from CS1/CS2/SS3/TS3]. The most critical limiting factor is phase control in the Nb–Se–I system, because NbSeI₃-type phases and related Ta₃QI₇ systems show difficulty achieving phase purity [CS3–CS5, SS4, TS4]. Overall, the target is reasonably synthesizable, but phase-pure bulk isolation will likely require careful tuning of temperature, iodine activity, and cooling rate [CS1–CS5, SS1–SS4, TS1–TS4].
