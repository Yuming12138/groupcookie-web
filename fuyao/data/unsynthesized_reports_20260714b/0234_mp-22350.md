### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

#### F1: Thermodynamic Stability

The target E_hull is 0.0500 eV/atom, which lies at the boundary of the highest stability bin in the requested rubric. Chemically similar R₃InN analogues are predicted/recorded as hull-stable with E_hull = 0.0 [CS1, CS2, CS3], supporting feasibility of the rare-earth nitride perovskite motif. However, structurally close Nd₃TlN and Nd₃AlN entries have large database E_hull values [CS4, SS2, SS3], creating a conflict between experimental analogue existence and computed thermodynamic instability. I weight the target’s own low E_hull and chemically close stable nitrides more strongly, while treating TS as insufficiently nitride-specific and neutral, score 50.

#### F2: Route 1

Route 1 avoids >1000 °C processing because the analogous Nd₃MN nitride route uses 700–900 °C [CS4, SS2, SS3]. It still requires controlled nitrogen atmosphere, prior NdN formation, long sintering, and air-sensitive Nd/NdN handling [CS4, SS2, SS3]. No high-pressure apparatus is indicated, which keeps severity moderate [CS4, SS2, SS3]. Remaining uncertainty is Sn retention and whether the exact Nd₃SnN phase forms cleanly rather than multiphase material [Reasonable Inference from non-homogeneity in CS4/SS2/SS3].

#### F3: Route 1

The kinetic score is limited because NdN and metallic Nd/Sn are dense solid-state precursors [CS4, SS2, SS3]. The liquid or softened Sn component at 900 °C may aid mixing, but Nd diffusion and nitride reorganization still require long holds [Reasonable Inference; CS4/SS2/SS3]. Structural reconstruction is favorable because Nd₃TlN and Nd₃AlN share the same cubic Pm-3m Nd₃M nitride framework [CS4, SS2, SS3]. The main uncertainty is incomplete ordering and nitrogen non-stoichiometry, both suggested by reported non-homogeneous samples [CS4, SS2, SS3].

#### F2: Route 2

Route 2 operates below 1000 °C after arc fusion and uses the established sealed-ampoule anneal conditions for R₃InN analogues [CS1, CS2, CS3]. It is penalized for vacuum/sealed conditions, multi-step alloy and nitride preparation, and air-sensitive rare-earth precursors [CS1, CS2, CS3]. It also likely needs sealed containment to limit Sn loss at high temperature [Reasonable Inference]. TS gives insufficient evidence for this specific nitride route and is treated neutrally, score 50.

#### F3: Route 2

The preformed Nd₂Sn alloy should improve cation homogeneity relative to separate Nd and Sn powders [Reasonable Inference from R₂In use in CS1–CS3]. R₃InN analogues form in the same cubic Pm-3m family after arc melting and one-week annealing [CS1, CS2, CS3], which supports the structural pathway. Long annealing and possible repeated grinding imply slow kinetics and moderate phase-purity challenges [CS1, CS2, CS3]. The Sn substitution is chemically plausible but not directly proven in this exact arc/ampoule format [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2: Route 3

Route 3 is a proposed metastable thin-film route, not directly supported by CS/SS/TS; therefore CS, SS, and TS each provide insufficient direct evidence and are neutral, score 50. It avoids high bulk temperatures and high GPa pressures [Reasonable Inference]. It still requires reactive N₂ plasma, careful oxygen exclusion, and post-deposition annealing [Reasonable Inference]. Severity is moderate, but uncertainty is high due to lack of analogue deposition data.

#### F3: Route 3

Nd and Sn are feasible as sputtering targets, but nitrogen incorporation requires reactive deposition and may be difficult in a rare-earth-rich intermetallic nitride [Reasonable Inference]. No CS/SS/TS analogue confirms direct thin-film crystallization, so the crystallization pathway is uncertain and likely requires annealing [insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS]. Composition control is likely narrow because related bulk Nd₃MN nitrides had non-homogeneous products and uncertain nitrogen content [CS4, SS2, SS3].
