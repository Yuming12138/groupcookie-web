### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability.** The target E_hull is 0.0500 eV/atom, which places it at the favorable edge of the ≤0.05 eV/atom scoring bin. [Target] CS, SS, and TS all include synthesized analogues with comparable low-to-moderate E_hull values, including SS/TS-1 near 0.053 eV/atom and TS-5 near 0.078 eV/atom, so the thermodynamic evidence supports synthesizability but not guaranteed ground-state stability. [CS-1, CS-3, CS-4, SS-1, TS-1, TS-5]

**F2/F3 — Route 1.** Solid-state synthesis has strong CS and SS support because Co/Ni oxyanion analogues are made at 900 °C and a GeO2-based germanate is made by ceramic sintering near 1000 °C. [CS-1, CS-3, SS-4] The kinetic penalty is moderate because dry oxide/germanate diffusion and repeated firings are likely needed, while TS provides insufficient direct evidence for this route and is neutral 50. [SS-4, TS]

**F2/F3 — Route 2.** The Pechini route scores best because SS-5 demonstrates a Co oxyanion framework from nitrate/citric acid chemistry at 350–600 °C, and CS supports nitrate-based Co precursor reactivity. [SS-5, CS-1, CS-3] The main uncertainty is that the target likely requires higher Co oxidation than the analogues, so flowing O2 and careful final annealing are inferred rather than directly demonstrated. [Target, Reasonable Inference]

**F2/F3 — Route 3.** Flux growth has good kinetic logic because SS-1/TS-1 support flux-grown complex oxide crystals and liquid-mediated transport reduces diffusion barriers. [SS-1, TS-1] The score is slightly below Pechini because the SS/TS flux source lacks exact temperature/time details and is not a direct Co–Ge analogue, so those missing specifics are neutral 50 evidence. [SS-1, TS-1]

**F2/F3 — Route 4.** CVT is feasible for some chemically similar arsenates because CS-4/CS-5 report single-crystal growth in chlorine-containing sealed ampoules. [CS-4, CS-5] The score is reduced because CS-2 reports poor reproducibility and secondary phases in Cl2/O2 gas-solid synthesis, and Co–Ge–O volatility/redox control is inferred to be difficult. ### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

Co(GeO3)2 is best interpreted as a near-hull metastable phase rather than a clearly robust ground-state phase because its E_hull is exactly 0.0500 eV/atom. [Target] The analogue set supports synthesis reasonably well: CS supports high-temperature Co oxyanion formation, SS supports Co Pechini chemistry and GeO2 ceramic germanate formation, and TS supports the general accessibility of phases in this metastability range. [CS-1, CS-3, SS-4, SS-5, TS-1] The most critical enabling factor is homogeneous Co–Ge mixing before crystallization, which favors the Pechini route. [SS-5, Reasonable Inference] The most critical limiting factor is oxidation-state control for cobalt in the CoGe2O6 stoichiometry, because direct Co(IV)-germanate synthesis is not demonstrated in CS/SS/TS. [Target, Reasonable Inference] Overall, the target is moderately likely to be synthesizable, with the best chance coming from sol-gel precursor control plus oxidizing anneal. [SS-5, CS-1, CS-3, Reasonable Inference]
