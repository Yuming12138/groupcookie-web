### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability**  
The target E_hull is 0.0500 eV/atom, placing it at the boundary of the highest stability scoring bracket. [Target] Synthesized TS analogues appear at comparable or slightly higher E_hull values, so metastability at this level is not prohibitive. [TS1, TS2] The remaining uncertainty is whether CaLaMoCrO6 has a narrow competing-phase window involving Ca/La molybdates or chromites. [Reasonable Inference]

**F2 — Route 1**  
Route 1 is moderately severe because it uses reducing gas and multiple processing steps, matching the closest soft-chemistry analogues. [CS5, SS1, SS5] It avoids high pressure and >1000 °C temperatures, which keeps the condition score higher than ceramic or flux routes. [CS5, SS1, SS5]

**F2 — Route 2**  
Route 2 is severe because ceramic analogues use up to 1200 °C, long firing, regrinding, and controlled atmospheres. [CS4, SS3, TS5] Sealed/vacuum Mo oxide chemistry adds volatility/redox control burdens. [TS1, TS5]

**F2 — Route 3**  
Route 3 is severe because flux/electrolysis routes require long holds, controlled atmospheres, flux drying, slow cooling, and post-growth washing. [TS2, SS2] The fused-salt La–Mo analogue reaches 1070 °C, so a high-temperature penalty is included. [SS2]

**F2 — Route 4**  
Route 4 has a high F2 score by the rubric because the hydrothermal analogue uses only 580 °C and 1.2 kbar, below the high-pressure penalty threshold. [CS3] The practical difficulty remains high because silver ampoules, caustic mineralizer, and autoclave operation are specialized. [CS3]

**F3 — Route 1**  
Soft-chemistry has the best kinetics because citrate resin processing provides intimate cation mixing before crystallization. [CS5, SS1, SS5] Mo/Cr diffusion and ordering remain limiting because the target likely needs a reduced Mo/Cr charge distribution. [Reasonable Inference]

**F3 — Route 2**  
Solid-state kinetics are weakest because dense oxides/carbonates require long-range diffusion and repeated firing. [CS4, SS3] This route is most vulnerable to incomplete B-site ordering and residual binary/ternary oxides. [Reasonable Inference]

**F3 — Route 3**  
Flux transport improves local mobility and can grow crystals, as shown by TS and SS analogues. [TS2, SS2] The uncertainty is whether the molten salt oxygen activity stabilizes the desired Mo/Cr perovskite instead of Mo6+-rich phases. [SS2, TS2, Reasonable Inference]

**F3 — Route 4**  
Hydrothermal mineralizers improve dissolution and transport, but the only direct hydrothermal evidence is for a different transition-metal vanadate family. [CS3] Because no SS/TS hydrothermal perovskite-derived Mo/Cr analogue is provided, structural reconstruction is scored conservatively; insufficient evidence from SS and TS, neutral 50 source signal. [SS, TS]

**F4 — Amorphization Tendency**  
One amorphization trigger is counted because the full cation set contains large A-site and smaller B-site ions with >15% radius contrast. [Target, Reasonable Inference] The risk is moderated because perovskites intentionally separate A and B cations onto different coordination sites, and structurally similar double perovskites crystallize successfully. [SS1, SS5] No clear evidence indicates a >40-atom cell or mixed edge/face/vertex octahedral network in the target. [Target]

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

CaLaMoCrO6 is best viewed as a **near-hull metastable or borderline-stable oxide**, not a clear ground-state phase, because E_hull = 0.0500 eV/atom. [Target] The strongest synthesis support comes from structurally similar NaLaWFeO6 and NaNdWFeO6, which use soft-chemistry precursors and reducing anneals to form ordered perovskite-derived phases. [CS5, SS1, SS5] The most important enabling factor is homogeneous cation mixing before crystallization, which reduces diffusion barriers and improves ordering. [CS5, SS1, SS5] The most important limiting factor is redox control, because the target likely requires a Mo/Cr valence combination compatible with O6 stoichiometry. [TS4, Reasonable Inference] Overall, synthesis is feasible but likely requires deliberate oxygen-potential control and phase-purity screening. [CS4, SS3, TS1, TS5, Reasonable Inference]
