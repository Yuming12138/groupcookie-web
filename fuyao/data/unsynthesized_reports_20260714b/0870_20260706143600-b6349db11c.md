The remaining uncertainty is experimental polymorph selection, because the target is specified as hexagonal P6₃/mmc while the closest RbCdCl3 analogue is orthorhombic Pnma (CS-2; SS-1; TS-1).

#### F2 — Route 1 condition severity

Route 1 is mild because RbCdCl3 is grown at room temperature from aqueous RbCl/CdCl2 without special atmosphere or pressure (CS-2; SS-1; TS-1). The only major severity deduction is Cd toxicity, because CdCl2 is required for the analogous Cd chloride chemistry [Reasonable Inference]. Insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS exists for the exact evaporation time and target polymorph selectivity, but the physical conditions themselves are low severity.

#### F3 — Route 1 kinetics

The solution route has very favorable kinetics because the cations are dissolved and RbCdCl3 crystallizes directly from aqueous solution (CS-2; SS-1; TS-1). This bypasses slow solid-state diffusion and should allow CsCdCl3 nucleation under near-equilibrium conditions [Reasonable Inference]. The uncertainty is that CsCdCl3 may prefer a different hydrate or polymorph under aqueous conditions [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2 — Route 2 condition severity

Hydrothermal/acidic routes operate at only 120–150 °C in HCl media for Cs chloride perovskite-derived analogues (SS-3; TS-2; TS-4). The route requires an autoclave or hot acid handling, and post-reaction filtering/washing/drying introduces multiple steps (SS-3; TS-2; TS-4). CdCl2 toxicity adds another severity penalty [Reasonable Inference].

#### F3 — Route 2 kinetics

Hydrothermal HCl synthesis has good kinetics because HCl maintains high chloride activity and promotes dissolution–recrystallization, as shown for Cs2AgInCl6 and Cs2AgBiCl6 analogues (SS-3; TS-2; TS-4). The pathway should be suitable for chloride perovskite-like crystallization [Reasonable Inference]. However, exact CsCdCl3 hydrothermal evidence is absent, so phase selectivity remains uncertain.

#### F2 — Route 3 condition severity

Melt growth is more demanding than solution growth but still moderate because Rb2CdCl4 was crystallized from a melt at 450 °C, not at extreme temperature (SS-2). Sealed ampoules and rigorous drying are recommended due to hygroscopic and potentially volatile chloride precursors [Reasonable Inference], and Rb2CdCl4 work specifically notes severe drying conditions (SS-2). CdCl2 toxicity is the main chemical hazard [Reasonable Inference].

#### F3 — Route 3 kinetics

Once molten, ion transport is rapid, and Rb2CdCl4 crystallizes by slow cooling from the melt (SS-2). This supports favorable diffusion kinetics for Cd chloride salts [Reasonable Inference]. The main kinetic/phase issue is crystallizing the desired ABCl3 hexagonal phase rather than other cesium cadmium chlorides or glassy/defective material; Rb2CdCl4 itself had phase-control complications (SS-2).

#### F2 — Route 4 condition severity

The sealed solid-state route is supported by CsFeCl3 synthesis at 750 °C in sealed Ta capsules under Ar (CS-1). This temperature is moderate, but sealed containment is recommended for CdCl2 volatility and chloride stoichiometry control [Reasonable Inference]. The route is less mild than solution synthesis and carries CdCl2 toxicity risk [Reasonable Inference].
