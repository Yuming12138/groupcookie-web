**F1 — Thermodynamic stability.**  
The target has E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom, so it is predicted to be a ground-state compound [Target]. Thermodynamic analogues Tl₂SnF₆, CsTlF₃, Cs₂FeTlF₆, RbAuF₄, and K₂NaTlF₆ are also reported with E_hull = 0.0, supporting stability of related fluoride phases [TS1–TS5]. CS gives insufficient useful evidence because all CS entries are Pt oxides, not Tl fluorides, so CS is neutral = 50 for this factor. Remaining uncertainty is the redox stability of isolated binary Tl₃F₅ versus decomposition into TlF/TlF₃ under real fluorine chemical potentials [Reasonable Inference].

**F2 — Route 1 condition severity.**  
Route 1 uses moderate temperatures, based on Tl₂SnF₆ at 300 °C [SS1, TS1], CsTlF₃ at 500 °C [SS2, TS2], and related fluorides at 400–700 °C [SS5, TS5]. It is penalized for controlled Ar/vacuum atmosphere, sealed ampoule use, multistep drying/grinding/sealing/heating, and toxic/moisture-sensitive Tl fluorides [SS1, SS2, SS5, TS1, TS2, TS5]. CS gives insufficient evidence for these fluoride conditions and is neutral. The main uncertainty is whether TlF₃ remains stable and nonvolatile during the chosen anneal [Reasonable Inference].

**F2 — Route 2 condition severity.**  
Route 2 avoids high temperature and high pressure because related F₂ fluorinations occur at 300–550 °C and ambient pressure [SS3, SS4, TS3, TS4]. It is penalized for the reactive F₂ atmosphere, controlled fluorination equipment, multistep fluorination/annealing, and toxic Tl/F₂ handling [SS3, SS4, TS3, TS4]. CS high-pressure Pt oxide routes are not transferred because SS/TS fluoride analogues are more chemically relevant; CS is neutral. The key uncertainty is the safe and reproducible control of fluorine chemical potential [Reasonable Inference].

**F3 — Route 1 kinetics.**  
Binary fluoride precursors are reasonably reactive because similar thallium fluorides form at 300–500 °C under Ar/vacuum [SS1, SS2, TS1, TS2]. Diffusion should be manageable because only Tl and F are involved, unlike multication perovskite analogues [Reasonable Inference]. However, the target’s 1D ribbon arrangement requires ordering of octahedral TlF₆ units and bent Tl–F units, so structural reconstruction is nontrivial [Target]. Long anneals in analogues indicate possible slow ordering [SS1, SS5, TS1, TS5].

**F3 — Route 2 kinetics.**  
F₂ provides high chemical activity, and related fluorides are made by slow fluorination followed by annealing [SS3, SS4, TS3, TS4]. This route may generate reactive intermediate fluorides and improve conversion relative to dry binary salt diffusion [Reasonable Inference]. The limitation is that over-fluorination to TlF₃ or incomplete oxidation to TlF could compete with the desired Tl₃F₅ composition [Reasonable Inference]. Moisture sensitivity and phase-control issues in related fluorides add uncertainty [SS4, SS5, TS4, TS5].

**F4 — Amorphization tendency.**  
The target has two distinct Tl environments: octahedral TlF₆ and bent two-coordinate Tl–F, indicating a large effective coordination/ionic-radius mismatch even for the same element [Target; Reasonable Inference]. The reported structure contains corner-sharing octahedra but no coexistence of vertex-, edge-, and face-sharing polyhedra, so the connectivity criterion is not triggered [Target]. No evidence indicates a >40 atom unit cell, so that criterion is not triggered [Target]. Overall amorphization risk is low–medium rather than high.
