### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

#### F1 — Thermodynamic stability

The target has E_hull = 0.0140 eV/atom, which falls well inside the ≤0.05 eV/atom high-synthesizability range. Thermodynamic analogues with similar small positive hull energies are experimentally reported, including K6CoS4 at 0.0278 eV/atom (CS-1, TS-2), K6CoSe4 at 0.0203 eV/atom (CS-2, TS-1), Rb2S at 0.0138 eV/atom (TS-4), and RbKS at 0.0081 eV/atom (TS-5). The main uncertainty is that low hull energy does not guarantee kinetic accessibility or correct Sc oxidation state [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2/F3 — Route 1

Route 1 is strongly supported because K6CoS4 has the same K6MS4 stoichiometric family and P6₃mc structure, and it is synthesized at 1030 K in H₂/chalcogen flow (CS-1, TS-2). The severity score is reduced for H₂ and sulfur vapor handling, both explicitly present in Co/Mn analogue syntheses (CS-1, CS-3). Kinetically, the same structure family gives a high reconstruction score, but Sc metal may react more slowly than Co/Mn because of oxide passivation and different redox chemistry [Reasonable Inference]. SS data provide insufficient direct signal for this exact route and are treated as neutral 50 for route-specific support.

#### F2/F3 — Route 2

Direct sealed-ampoule synthesis is supported by K3GaS3 from elements at 1025 K in evacuated graphitized quartz ampoules (CS-4), Rb2S from Rb/S in evacuated quartz (TS-4), and K3AuSe2 in evacuated silica (SS-4). The route is penalized for vacuum/sealed handling, volatile K/S, and air-sensitive K [Reasonable Inference]. Kinetics are less favorable because Sc metal must sulfidize and the target structure must nucleate from elements rather than from an already analogous K6MS4 framework [Reasonable Inference]. Evidence from CS/TS/SS is real but less chemically exact than Route 1.

#### F2/F3 — Route 3

Sulfide-flux annealing is supported by RbKS formation from K2S/Rb2S at 820 K for 7 days in sealed silver crucibles under Ar (TS-5) and K3AuSe2 formation from K2Se/Au/Se at 823 K with slow cooling (SS-4). This route has lower atmosphere severity than H₂ but uses air-sensitive sulfides and sealed crucibles (TS-5). Kinetics may benefit from a molten or highly mobile alkali sulfide/polysulfide flux [Reasonable Inference], but structural reconstruction into isolated K6ScS4 clusters remains uncertain because the closest flux analogues are not the same P6₃mc K6MS4 structure (TS-5, SS-4). CS evidence supports alkali-rich chalcogenide formation generally but not this exact flux route, so route-specific CS signal is partial.

#### F4 — Amorphization tendency

Only the cation-size/coordination mismatch check is clearly triggered because K and Sc occupy very different bonding environments in the target, with long K–S and shorter tetrahedral Sc–S distances from the target structure [Reasonable Inference based on target structure]. The described unit contains two K6ScS4 clusters, i.e., 22 atoms, so the >40 atom-count check is not triggered. The structure is zero-dimensional rather than a mixed extended edge/vertex/face-sharing framework, so C3 is not triggered; CS/SS/TS provide insufficient direct amorphization evidence and are neutral for this factor. The resulting amorphization risk is low–medium.

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

K6ScS4 is likely a **near-ground-state metastable or marginally stable phase**, because its E_hull is only 0.014 eV/atom and analogous small-positive-hull K-rich chalcogenides are experimentally accessible (CS-1, CS-2, TS-1, TS-2, TS-4, TS-5). The strongest support comes from chemically and thermodynamically similar K6CoS4/K6CoSe4/K6MnSe4 materials, especially K6CoS4 with the same P6₃mc K6MS4 family (CS-1, CS-3, TS-2). The most critical enabling factor is maintaining high sulfur activity under a reducing H₂/chalcogen atmosphere, which is exactly how the Co/Mn analogues are made (CS-1, CS-3). The most critical limiting factor is the absence of a demonstrated Sc analogue and possible Sc redox/phase-competition issues [Reasonable Inference]. Overall, synthesis appears plausible, with Route 1 preferred and Route 3 a practical backup if H₂ processing is undesirable.
