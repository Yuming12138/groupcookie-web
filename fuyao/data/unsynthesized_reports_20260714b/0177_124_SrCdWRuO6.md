### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic stability.**  
The target E_hull is 0.0500 eV/atom, which falls at the high end of the ≤0.05 eV/atom rubric and justifies a score of 90 [Target data]. Chemically similar W double perovskites have E_hull ≈ 0 and are experimentally synthesized [CS1, CS2, CS5], while thermodynamic analogues near 0.055–0.115 eV/atom are also reported as synthesizable [TS2, TS3, TS5]. The remaining uncertainty is that the target’s exact Sr–Cd–Ru–W composition is not directly represented in CS/SS/TS [insufficient direct evidence from CS/SS/TS].

**F2/F3 — Route 1.**  
Route 1 benefits from the strongest chemical precedent: CS1/CS2 use citrate soft chemistry and final reducing anneals at 850–900 °C for W-containing ordered oxides [CS1, CS2]. The F2 score is reduced by the need for H2-containing atmosphere, multiple processing steps, and Cd toxicity/volatility risk [CS1, CS2; Reasonable Inference]. The F3 score is moderate because precursor activity is high, but W/Ru diffusion and the target’s unusual low-symmetry structure remain kinetically challenging [CS1, CS2; insufficient direct evidence from SS/TS].

**F2/F3 — Route 2.**  
Route 2 is supported by ceramic synthesis of W oxides at 1050 °C in air and 800–1200 °C in air/forming gas [CS3, CS5]. Its F2 score is lower because high temperature, forming gas, repeated annealing, and Cd volatility all increase severity [CS3, CS5; Reasonable Inference]. Its F3 score is poor because dense oxides/carbonates and refractory W/Ru cations imply slow diffusion and likely incomplete ordering without many regrinds [CS5; Reasonable Inference].

**F2/F3 — Route 3.**  
Route 3 is supported by TS/SS examples of evacuated silica or metal-tube synthesis for reduced Mo/W oxides at 700–1100 °C [TS1, TS3, TS4, SS1]. Its F2 score is moderate because vacuum sealing is less severe than high pressure but adds safety and volatility constraints [TS1, TS4, SS1]. Its F3 score remains low because sealed-tube oxide/metal reactions usually rely on slow solid-state diffusion, and no TS/SS source demonstrates the target cation set or structure [insufficient direct evidence from TS/SS].

**F4 — Amorphization tendency.**  
The large cation size/charge contrast among Sr, Cd, W, and Ru triggers the ionic-radius mismatch check [Reasonable Inference]. The target formula and P1 description do not establish a >40 atom cell, and the bonding description does not clearly show simultaneous vertex/edge/face-sharing networks, so C2 and C3 are not triggered [insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS]. The resulting amorphization risk is low–medium rather than high.

SrWRuCdO6 is likely a marginally stable or mildly metastable phase because its E_hull is exactly 0.0500 eV/atom [Target data].
