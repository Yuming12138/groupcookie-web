### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

#### F1 — Thermodynamic stability

The target’s E_hull of 0.035 eV/atom is within the ≤0.05 eV/atom high-synthesizability range, so the base thermodynamic score is high. Similar experimentally synthesized iodides/chalcogenides include stable Nb3I8 and TiI3 [CS1, CS2], near-hull TlMnI3 and TlCdI3 [SS1, SS3], and thermodynamic analogues with E_hull ≈0.017–0.031 eV/atom [TS1, TS2, TS5]. The remaining uncertainty is that 0.035 eV/atom implies the exact Pmmn MnI2 polymorph may be metastable rather than the ground-state MnI2 form. CS/SS/TS provide positive thermodynamic precedent and no direct contradiction.

#### F2 — Route 1

Route 1 is penalized because evacuated sealed ampoules and iodine vapor are required, as in Nb iodide and TiI3 syntheses [CS1, CS2]. Temperatures are not extreme because analogous iodide routes operate below 1000 °C [CS1, CS2], but volatile iodine and sealed-tube handling introduce meaningful severity. Multi-step transport/growth resembles the CVT workflows described for Nb and Mo compounds [CS1, CS5]. The main uncertainty is the appropriate iodine pressure for stabilizing the target Pmmn polymorph.

#### F3 — Route 1

The kinetics benefit from iodine vapor transport, which can bypass slow solid-state diffusion and is demonstrated for iodide compounds [CS1, CS2]. However, starting from Mn metal requires chemical conversion before crystallization, so precursor activity is lower than solution or melt routes. Structural reconstruction is moderate because the product forms directly from vapor/solid reaction, but the exact Pmmn sheet topology is not confirmed by CS/SS/TS. Phase competition with other MnI2 polytypes remains the main kinetic uncertainty.

#### F2 — Route 2

Route 2 requires dry glovebox/sealed ampoule handling and likely multiple annealing steps, consistent with TlMnI3/TlFeI3 preparation [SS1, TS1]. It avoids high temperature above 1000 °C and avoids high pressure, so its severity is moderate rather than extreme. Volatile iodide chemistry still requires sealed conditions, supported by sealed iodide syntheses [CS1, CS2]. The severity score is limited mainly by air/moisture sensitivity and long annealing.

#### F3 — Route 2

Kinetics are favorable because the precursor can be anhydrous MnI2, i.e., the same composition as the target, and MnI2 is already used in the closest structural analogue [SS1, TS1]. Melt/Bridgman processing gives high mobility, as used for TlMnI3 and TlCdI3-type iodides [SS1, SS3, TS1, TS3]. The penalty comes from possible polymorph ordering and the precedent that repeated annealing/powdering may be needed for phase purity [SS1, TS1]. Overall, this is the most chemically robust route despite not having the highest numerical F2.

#### F2 — Route 3

Solution evaporation is mild because CdI2 analogues crystallize at room temperature with no special equipment [SS2, SS4, SS5]. The only severity penalty is for handling anhydrous MnI2 or acidic iodide media, which is inferred from dry Mn iodide analogue handling [SS1, TS1]. There is insufficient direct CS evidence that MnI2 itself can be grown anhydrously by evaporation; CS is therefore neutral for this route severity where not involving solution growth. The low thermal burden makes this route attractive for metastable polymorph screening.

#### F3 — Route 3

Solution growth has excellent ionic mobility, and layered CdI2 analogues crystallize readily by evaporation [SS2, SS4, SS5]. However, the same CdI2 sources note polytypism or imply multiple stacking variants [SS4, SS5], which reduces structural-selectivity confidence. For MnI2 specifically, hydrate/solvate formation is a likely limitation [Reasonable Inference], and no CS/TS source directly proves anhydrous MnI2 solution growth. Thus F3 remains good but not near-perfect.

#### F2 — Route 4

Hydrothermal HI synthesis uses moderate temperatures and pressure vessels, not high-pressure conditions >5 GPa [TS2]. The penalties arise from sealed corrosive HI/I2 media, multistep loading/heating/cooling/isolation, and toxic/corrosive reagent handling [TS2, TS5]. There is insufficient SS evidence for hydrothermal MnI2 specifically, so SS is neutral for this route’s severity beyond general iodide handling. The route is moderate in severity but chemically more aggressive than simple evaporation.

#### F3 — Route 4

HI hydrothermal media provide high precursor activity and solution-mediated transport, as shown by AgTl2I3 synthesis in aqueous HI [TS2]. Slow cooling and pressure-vessel crystallization can produce iodide crystals [TS2], but TS2 reports twinning and low yield of untwinned crystals, indicating phase/crystal-quality challenges. Hydration or solvated Mn iodide formation is a plausible additional risk [Reasonable Inference]. Kinetics are favorable, but selectivity is uncertain.

#### F4 — Amorphization tendency

The target has only one cation, so cation-radius disorder is not relevant; CS/SS/TS provide insufficient additional signal and are neutral for C1. The target description does not indicate a >40 atom cell, so C2 is not triggered; CS/SS/TS provide insufficient direct unit-cell-size signal and are neutral. C3 is triggered because the target contains face-, edge-, and corner-sharing MnI6 octahedra, which can increase structural complexity; analogous Mn iodide synthesis requires long annealing for phase purity [SS1, TS1]. The final F4 score is therefore low–medium risk rather than high risk.

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

The target is likely a near-hull metastable polymorph rather than an unequivocal ground-state phase because E_hull = 0.035 eV/atom. The synthesis outlook is favorable because experimentally realized iodide analogues exist by sealed-tube CVT [CS1, CS2], dry melt/anneal routes using MnI2 [SS1, TS1], and low-temperature iodide crystallization [SS2, TS2]. The most critical enabling factor is the high mobility of iodides under melt, vapor-transport, or solution conditions, which reduces the need for refractory solid-state diffusion [CS1, CS2, SS1, SS2]. The most critical limiting factor is control of the exact MnI2 polymorph/stacking and avoidance of hydrate or competing iodide phases, especially in solution routes [SS4, SS5] and by [Reasonable Inference]. Practically, dry sealed MnI2 annealing/Bridgman is the safest primary route, while solution and hydrothermal methods are useful for metastable-polymorph screening.
