### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

#### F1 — Thermodynamic stability

The target E_hull is 0.0500 eV/atom, which falls at the upper edge of the “≤0.05 eV/atom” high-score bin specified in the instructions. Similar LnCu(WO4)2 analogues are reported with E_hull values near 0.033–0.042 eV/atom and have documented syntheses, supporting that this thermodynamic window is accessible (CS1–CS5, SS1–SS5, TS1/TS3–TS5). The remaining uncertainty is that the target is likely near-degenerate with other polymorphs, so thermodynamic accessibility does not guarantee the desired P-1 structure.

#### F2 — Route 1

Route 1 is penalized because it requires sealed copper ampoules, Cu+ redox control, multi-step Cu2WO4 preparation, and controlled cooling (CS1, SS1, TS3). It is not penalized for >1000 °C or >5 GPa because the reported temperature is 950 °C and the 5 t/cm² sealing pressure is not a >5 GPa synthesis pressure (CS1, SS1, TS3). Insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS supports volatile precursor loss as the reason for sealing, so that deduction was not applied.

#### F3 — Route 1

The kinetics are moderate because Dy2O3 and WO3 are refractory oxide precursors, while Cu2WO4 provides a more reactive pre-combined Cu–W oxide source (CS1, SS1, TS3). The structure is strongly transferable because the closest DyCu(WO4)2 analogue has the same formula and similar DyO6/WO6/Cu–O connectivity, but the 40 h hold and polymorph formation indicate sluggish ordering and phase competition (CS1, SS1, TS3). The main uncertainty is whether the P-1 target can be isolated rather than a P1 or monoclinic polymorph.

#### F2 — Route 2

Route 2 has the least severe conditions because Nd/Sm/Y analogues use 950 °C, N2 or Ar, and flux-assisted growth without explicit high mechanical sealing pressure (SS2, SS4, SS5, TS5). A controlled atmosphere deduction is still appropriate because oxygen exclusion is repeatedly emphasized for Cu+ tungstates (SS4, SS5, CS1). Insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS indicates volatile precursor retention as a central issue, so no sealed-volatility penalty was applied.

#### F3 — Route 2

Excess Cu2WO4 improves kinetics by acting as flux, and Sm analogues explicitly used excess Cu2WO4 to obtain X-ray-quality crystals (SS5). Similar Nd/Y/Sm/LnCu(WO4)2 frameworks form at 950 °C, showing that a liquid/flux-assisted pathway can overcome refractory W–O diffusion limitations (SS2, SS4, SS5). The uncertainty is that lanthanide size affects polymorph stability, so the cooling rate and Cu2WO4 excess may need optimization for Dy.

#### F2 — Route 3

Route 3 is the most severe because Gd analogue synthesis involved multiple oxide/metal precursors, closed copper tubes, Ar, temperatures including above 1000 °C, and long treatments up to days (CS4, TS4). It is not a >5 GPa process, and there is insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS that volatile precursor retention is the primary reason for sealed tubes. The route is therefore feasible but comparatively cumbersome.

#### F3 — Route 3

The reaction kinetics are poorer because the route starts from less preorganized CuO/Cu/Cu2O/WO3/Dy2O3 mixtures and must build Cu2WO4/CuWO4 intermediates before the target framework (CS4, TS4). The Gd analogue route also reports heterogeneous mixtures and mechanical isolation of crystals, indicating incomplete bulk conversion and slow ordering (CS4, TS4). It remains plausible because GdCu(WO4)2 is chemically close, but it is less direct than using preformed Cu2WO4.

#### F4 — Amorphization tendency

The target combines DyO6 octahedra, distorted WO6 octahedra, and linearly coordinated Cu–O units, implying significant cation-size and coordination mismatch [Reasonable Inference from target structure]. However, the target framework is a crystalline oxide built from corner- and edge-sharing polyhedra, and no face-sharing coexistence is reported in the target description. The amorphization risk is therefore low-to-medium rather than high; the bigger practical issue is polymorph/phase competition rather than glass formation.

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

DyCu(WO4)2 with E_hull = 0.0500 eV/atom is best viewed as a near-threshold metastable or marginally stable phase rather than a robust deep ground state. The analogue support is strong: chemically identical or nearly identical Dy/Er/Y/Nd/Sm/Gd copper tungstates have been synthesized under 950 °C Cu2WO4-rich, oxygen-excluding conditions (CS1, SS1–SS5, TS1/TS3–TS5). The most critical enabling factor is the use of Cu2WO4 as both Cu+ source and tungstate flux, which stabilizes Cu+ and improves mass transport (CS1, SS4, SS5). The most critical limiting factor is polymorph control, because the references report triclinic/monoclinic dimorphism and difficulty obtaining high-quality triclinic crystals (CS1, CS2, CS3, SS1, TS3). Overall, synthesis is likely feasible in a well-equipped solid-state/crystal-growth lab, but phase-pure bulk material may require optimization of cooling rate, Cu2WO4 excess, and oxygen fugacity.
