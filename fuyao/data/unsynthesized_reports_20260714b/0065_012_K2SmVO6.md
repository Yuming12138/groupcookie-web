### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability.** The target lies at E_hull = 0.0500 eV/atom, which is exactly at the favorable cutoff and supports moderate-to-high synthesizability [Target]. Similar synthesized materials in TS include phases at ~0.060–0.099 eV/atom, so a 0.0500 eV/atom phase is not outside experimentally accessible metastability ranges [TS1; TS2; TS3; TS5]. Remaining uncertainty is that E_hull does not resolve the unusual K2SmVO6 redox/oxygen-balance issue [Target; Reasonable Inference].

**F2 — Route 1.** The solid-state route uses mild-to-moderate ceramic temperatures comparable to K3RE(VO4)2, with no high pressure or inert/reducing atmosphere [CS2; SS2]. Deductions come from multi-step calcination/regrinding and toxic vanadium handling [CS5; Reasonable Inference]. Insufficient evidence from TS for a direct solid-state K–Sm–V oxide route is treated as neutral, TS = 50.

**F2 — Route 2.** Flux growth is penalized for possible T >1000 °C, sealed/covered K-rich flux handling, washing/isolation, and vanadium/caustic reagent hazards [CS4; SS4; Reasonable Inference]. The flux evidence is strong for K/Na vanadate crystal growth but not exact for K2SmVO6 [CS4; SS4; Target]. Insufficient evidence from TS for this flux route is treated as neutral, TS = 50.

**F2 — Route 3.** Hydrothermal synthesis avoids >1000 °C and >5 GPa by the formal rubric, but still requires specialized autoclave/silver ampule conditions [SS5]. Deductions are assigned for the multi-step SmVO4 precursor route and vanadium precursor toxicity [SS5; Reasonable Inference]. Insufficient evidence from CS for this hydrothermal target route is treated as neutral, CS = 50.

**F2 — Route 4.** The peroxide/metathesis route avoids very high temperature but is penalized for sealed reactive oxidizing conditions, air-sensitive peroxide/oxide precursors, and multi-step sealed-tube handling [TS4; Reasonable Inference]. It is chemically motivated by target oxygen balance, but not directly demonstrated for K–Sm–V chemistry [Target; TS4]. Insufficient evidence from CS and SS is treated as neutral, CS = 50 and SS = 50.

**F3 — Route 1.** K2CO3/NH4VO3/RE2O3 precursor chemistry is proven for K3RE(VO4)2, but Sm2O3 and dry ceramic diffusion lower the kinetics score [CS2; SS2; Reasonable Inference]. Structural reconstruction is nontrivial because the target contains SmO6–VO5 connectivity rather than the VO4-dominated analogue connectivity [Target; CS2; SS2].

**F3 — Route 2.** Flux conditions improve precursor activity and ion transport, supported by KBaVO4 and Na3ErV2O8 crystal growth [CS4; SS4]. The kinetics score is not higher because composition control for a specific K2SmVO6 phase in a K-rich flux is uncertain [Target; Reasonable Inference].

**F3 — Route 3.** Hydrothermal mineralizers improve transport, but the REVO4 starting material is comparatively inert and SS5 reports failures for larger lanthanides [SS5]. Because Sm is closer to the larger-lanthanide side than Ho/Sc, this route has significant phase-selection uncertainty [SS5; Reasonable Inference].

**F3 — Route 4.** Peroxide/metathesis reagents are highly reactive, but TS4 requires very long annealing, indicating slow ordering or diffusion [TS4]. The structural pathway from SmVO4 to K2SmVO6 is plausible but unconfirmed, so the reconstruction score is moderate [Target; TS4; Reasonable Inference].

**F4 — Amorphization Tendency.** The target has a large cation-size/coordination mismatch among K, Sm, and V environments, triggering C1 [Target; Reasonable Inference]. C2 and C3 are not triggered because the supplied P1 description corresponds to a small 10-atom formula-unit description and only corner-sharing SmO6/VO5 connectivity is specified [Target]. CS/SS/TS provide insufficient direct amorphization data for K2SmVO6, so source-specific amorphization evidence is neutral = 50, while the F4 score is assigned from the target structure [Target].

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

K2SmVO6 is best interpreted as a near-threshold metastable phase rather than a clear ground-state phase because its E_hull is 0.0500 eV/atom, exactly above the hull boundary used here [Target]. The CS/SS evidence supports synthesis of K–rare-earth vanadates by standard solid-state and hydrothermal/flux methods, especially K3RE(VO4)2-type compositions [CS2; SS2; SS5]. The TS evidence shows that slightly metastable oxides can be experimentally realized and that sealed oxidizing alkali chemistry is available if high oxygen activity is needed [TS1; TS4; TS5]. The most important enabling factor is the strong precedent for K2CO3/NH4VO3/RE2O3 chemistry in air [CS2; SS2]. The most important limiting factor is the target’s unusual oxygen/redox requirement relative to common RE3+/V5+ vanadates, which may force O2-rich or peroxide-assisted synthesis control [Target; CS2; TS4; Reasonable Inference].
