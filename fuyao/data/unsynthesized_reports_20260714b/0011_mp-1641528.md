## SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

CaLaFeAgO₆ is most likely a **borderline metastable but accessible** oxide rather than a robust ground-state phase, because its E_hull is 0.0500 eV/atom and its charge balance implies unusually oxidized Fe/Ag chemistry [Target, Reasonable Inference]. The most critical limiting factor is **simultaneously retaining Ag and stabilizing the required high B-site valence without forming competing Fe/Ca/La oxides or metallic Ag** [Target, SS1, SS5, Reasonable Inference].
