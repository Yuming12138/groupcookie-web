### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

#### F2 — Route 1

Route 1 is only moderately severe because the closest analogues form at 500–700 °C, ambient pressure, and standard evacuated sealed tubes [CS1, CS2, SS1]. #### F3 — Route 1

Kinetics are moderate because elemental Si is relatively refractory and covalent **[Reasonable Inference]**, while Mn and Te can form binary tellurides at 550 °C [SS1].
