### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic stability.**  
The target E_hull is 0.0500 eV/atom, placing it at the favorable boundary of the 90–100 category. TS provides useful evidence that similar alkali chalcogenides with E_hull ≈0.075–0.088 eV/atom have been synthesized, specifically Cs₂Se-II and Rb₂S (TS-1, TS-2). CS/SS add that the closest Pnma A₂Te analogue, Cs₂Te, is a stable phase at E_hull = 0 (CS-1, SS-2). Remaining uncertainty is whether the reported Rb₂Te Pnma polymorph competes with lower-energy Rb₂Te polymorphs near room temperature.

**F2 — Route 1 condition severity.**  
Route 1 uses moderate temperature but requires vacuum ampoule containment, slow heating, and air-free handling, all supported by Rb₂Se/Cs₂Se/Rb₂S analogues (CS-2, CS-4, SS-5, TS-2). The main deductions come from vacuum, volatile/air-sensitive alkali metal, sealed system, and multi-step handling. CS/SS/TS do not show a direct Rb₂Te low-temperature synthesis, so transferability is strong chemically but not proven structurally.

**F3 — Route 1 kinetics.**  
Direct reaction from elements is kinetically plausible because Rb is highly reactive and analogous Rb₂S/Rb₂Se-type binaries form at 300–350 °C over 2–7 days (TS-2, CS-2/SS-5). However, the target Pnma structure must assemble from elements, so structural reconstruction is not trivial. The long anneals reported for analogues justify a moderate rather than high kinetics score (CS-2, CS-4, SS-5).

**F2 — Route 2 condition severity.**  
Route 2 is not ultra-high-temperature or high-pressure, but it is more demanding experimentally because it involves high-temperature sealed-tube work with volatile Rb and Te. Cs₂Te-related synthesis uses 650–900 °C evacuated tubes and controlled cooling (CS-1, SS-2), and alkali tellurides in Nb tubes require glovebox loading and sealed containment (SS-4). The severity score is limited by air sensitivity and pressure risk inside sealed ampoules.

**F3 — Route 2 kinetics.**  
This route has the best kinetics score because melting/homogenization reduces diffusion limitations, as explicitly used for Cs₂Te-related synthesis (CS-1, SS-2). The structural match to Pnma Cs₂Te is strong, so the target Rb₂Te Pnma phase should require less reconstruction than unrelated structures (CS-1, SS-2). The uncertainty is phase selection: Rb₂Se is reported as fluorite rather than Pnma in one analogue (CS-2, SS-5), implying cation/anion size can shift the preferred structure.

**F2 — Route 3 condition severity.**  
Ammonothermal synthesis avoids high temperature but introduces NH₃, 300–2000 bar pressure, autoclaves, and high-vacuum post-treatment, all directly reported for Cs₂Se-II (CS-5, TS-1). Because the scoring rubric only penalizes pressure above 5 GPa, the formal F2 score is not as low as the practical difficulty might suggest. The source evidence is strong for Cs₂Se but insufficient for direct Rb₂Te ammonothermal transfer.

**F3 — Route 3 kinetics.**  
Solution-mediated transport should reduce diffusion barriers, and Cs₂Se-II forms at 100–300 °C under NH₃ pressure (CS-5, TS-1). However, phase control is challenging because the analogue only forms above 1000 bar and with increasing reaction time, and it is sensitive to moisture/light/X-rays (CS-5, TS-1). For Rb₂Te, Te solubility and the tendency to form the Pnma rather than another polymorph are uncertain, so the kinetics score remains moderate.

**F4 — Amorphization tendency.**  
The target has one cation species, no large cation-radius mismatch, and a likely small Pnma cell; the structure description mentions corner- and edge-sharing but not face-sharing. Therefore none of the three amorphization triggers is activated by the target structural data. CS/SS/TS provide insufficient direct amorphization evidence, so they are neutral for this factor, but the crystalline analogues throughout CS/SS/TS suggest crystalline alkali chalcogenides are accessible.

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

Rb₂Te in the described Pnma structure is likely a near-threshold metastable phase rather than a deep ground state, because its E_hull is 0.0500 eV/atom. The analogue support is strong: Cs₂Te has the same A₂Te chemistry and Pnma/cotunnite-related structure (CS-1, SS-2), while Rb₂S/Rb₂Se analogues show that rubidium chalcogenides can be made by sealed-ampoule reactions (TS-2, CS-2/SS-5). The most critical enabling factor is sealed-tube melt/anneal processing with slow cooling, which promotes homogenization and structural ordering as shown for Cs₂Te-related synthesis (CS-1, SS-2). The most critical limiting factor is phase control among possible Rb₂Te polymorphs, since related A₂Ch compounds can adopt different structures such as fluorite Rb₂Se and Fdd2/Cotunnite Cs₂Se variants (CS-2, CS-4, CS-5, SS-5). Air/moisture sensitivity is also a major practical limitation by analogy to Rb₂S and Cs₂Se (TS-2, CS-4/CS-5).
