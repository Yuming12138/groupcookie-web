### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

#### F1: Thermodynamic Stability

The target E_hull is 0.0360 eV/atom, placing it in the ≤0.05 eV/atom high-synthesizability bin. Thermodynamic analogues with similar E_hull values, including CuHgSBr at 0.0309 eV/atom and WOI₃ at 0.0332 eV/atom, were experimentally synthesized (TS-1, TS-4). Chemically and structurally close PtI₂ and PdBr₂ analogues are reported as E_hull = 0 and experimentally accessible (CS-1, SS-3). Remaining uncertainty is that PdI₂ at 0.036 eV/atom may be slightly metastable relative to competing Pd/I phases, so iodine chemical potential must be controlled [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2: Route 1 — CVT / Iodine Transport

The severity is moderate because structurally similar PdBr₂ requires 700 °C in a sealed quartz ampoule with halogen transport agent (SS-3), while iodide CVT analogues can operate around 400–440 °C under vacuum in sealed tubes (TS-4). The route requires vacuum/sealing and volatile iodine, directly supported by sealed-tube halide transport analogues (SS-3, TS-4). There is no evidence for high pressure or >1000 °C conditions from SS/TS, so those penalties are not applied. The main uncertainty is whether PdI₂ transports cleanly without disproportionation or iodine-rich phase formation [Reasonable Inference; phase competition concerns from CS-4].

#### F3: Route 1 — CVT / Iodine Transport

Reaction kinetics should be favorable for crystal growth because PdBr₂ single crystals were obtained by halogen-assisted CVT (SS-3), and iodine transport works for WOI₃ (TS-4). Crystallization is not guaranteed to reproduce the target layered PdI₂ polymorph because the closest Pd analogue in SS is 1D PdBr₂ rather than 2D PdI₂ (SS-3). Composition control is the largest kinetic/process risk because Pt iodides show narrow phase regions and adjacent iodide impurities (CS-4, SS-2). Insufficient direct evidence from CS for PdI₂ CVT specifically, so that part is treated cautiously.

#### F2: Route 2 — Hydrothermal Iodide-Rich Growth

The hydrothermal route avoids >1000 °C and high-pressure GPa conditions, but PtI₂ required 420 °C, 2–3 weeks, closed quartz ampoules, and CO₂ counter-pressure (CS-1, SS-1). Volatile iodine and closed pressure systems are clear severity factors because I₂/KI/water sealed systems are used in PtI₂/PtI₃ synthesis (CS-1, CS-4). Equipment demands are high compared with simple solid-state synthesis because thick-walled ampoules, autoclaves, and counter-pressure were used for PtI₂ (CS-1). The score remains relatively high because the absolute temperatures are moderate and no extreme pressure is reported from CS/SS.

#### F3: Route 2 — Hydrothermal Iodide-Rich Growth

Kinetically, this is the best-supported route because PtI₂, the closest layered monoclinic MI₂ analogue, forms hydrothermally from iodide-rich solution (CS-1, SS-1). Solution-mediated iodometalate chemistry reduces diffusion limitations compared with reacting dense Pd metal directly [Reasonable Inference supported by hydrothermal crystal formation in CS-1/CS-4]. Structural reconstruction is favorable because the target and PtI₂ share the same general layered P2₁/c square-planar iodide framework (CS-1, SS-1). The main uncertainty is phase purity: PtI₂ synthesis was reported to produce unclear mixtures, defects, and twinning (CS-1).

#### F2: Route 3 — Direct Sealed-Tube Iodination / Annealing

This route uses moderate temperature sealed-tube conditions analogous to CuHgSI at 553 K for 2 weeks and CuHgSCl at 573 K for 2 weeks (TS-2, TS-3). It requires vacuum and volatile iodine containment, similar to sealed ampoule halide syntheses (TS-2, TS-3, TS-4). There is no evidence from SS/TS that such reactions need high pressure or >1000 °C. The uncertainty is that direct Pd + I₂ may generate pressure spikes or surface passivation, neither directly addressed in CS/SS/TS and therefore inferred cautiously.

#### F3: Route 3 — Direct Sealed-Tube Iodination / Annealing

The kinetics are weaker than the hydrothermal and CVT routes because Pd metal is dense and noble, making direct diffusion/reaction slower [Reasonable Inference]. TS analogues show sealed-tube halides can form at modest temperatures, but they often use already-formed binary precursors such as CuI and HgS and require 2 weeks (TS-2, TS-3). Structural reconstruction from elements is complete rather than topotactic, lowering the structural kinetics score [Reasonable Inference]. Insufficient direct evidence from CS/SS/TS confirms that PdI₂ forms phase-pure by simple solid-state iodination, so this route is less preferred.

#### F4: Amorphization Tendency

The target is a simple binary PdI₂ phase with one cation type and square-planar Pd–I sheets (Target structure), so the cation-size mismatch trigger is absent. The described structure is not a large, chemically complex framework, and no mixed vertex/edge/face-sharing polyhedral network is reported (Target structure). CS/SS/TS provide insufficient direct evidence on amorphization tendencies, so their source contribution is neutral. Physicochemically, layered binary halides often crystallize readily if halogen chemical potential is controlled [Reasonable Inference].

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

PdI₂ is likely a **slightly metastable but experimentally accessible** phase because its E_hull is only 0.0360 eV/atom, within the commonly synthesizable metastability window, and TS analogues with similar hull energies were synthesized (TS-1, TS-4). The strongest support comes from PtI₂, which has a closely matching monoclinic layered MI₂ structure and was obtained hydrothermally from iodide-rich media (CS-1, SS-1). The most critical enabling factor is maintaining a high and controlled iodine chemical potential, as iodide-rich sealed systems are common across PtI₂/PtI₃ hydrothermal synthesis and halide CVT analogues (CS-1, CS-4, SS-3, TS-4). The most critical limiting factor is phase competition/stoichiometry control among Pd metal, PdI₂, iodine-rich complexes, and possible polymorphs, inferred from narrow Pt iodide phase regions and impurity issues (CS-1, CS-4). Overall, the target should be synthesizable with careful sealed-system handling and slow equilibration.
