### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

#### F1: Thermodynamic stability

The target E_hull of 0.0330 eV/atom places it in the ≤0.05 eV/atom class, so it receives a high thermodynamic score. This is strongly supported by chemically similar high-pressure CaF₂ with E_hull = 0.0346 eV/atom being experimentally synthesized [CS4]. SS and TS analogues also show many fluoride phases at or near the hull, including KLiBeF₄, Rb₂NaAlF₆, Rb₂KGaF₆, K₂NaYF₆, and SrZnF₄ [SS1, SS4, SS5, TS1, TS5]. The uncertainty is that the target is a polymorph of LiF rather than a distinct multicomponent fluoride, so the competing rocksalt LiF phase may dominate [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2/F3 Route 1: Direct sealed-tube melt/quench

Condition severity is moderate because fluoride analogues are often synthesized at 700–950 °C in sealed tubes or closed bombs without GPa pressure [SS2, SS5, TS5]. Na₂LiAlF₆ is made by melting/sintering from LiF-containing binary fluoride mixtures [SS3, TS3], which supports the use of LiF melt processing. Kinetics are favorable for Li/F diffusion but unfavorable for structural reconstruction because no CS/SS/TS source demonstrates conversion of ordinary LiF into the target tetragonal 2D phase. The main uncertainty is that this route may simply recrystallize rocksalt LiF [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2/F3 Route 2: High-pressure/high-temperature

Condition severity is harsh because high-pressure analogues require 6–8.6 GPa, high temperature, noble-metal capsules, and quenching [CS3, CS4]. The kinetic score is high because high pressure, high temperature, and flux/melt conditions can enable structural rearrangement; CaF₂ in a metastable high-pressure polymorph was grown at 8.6 GPa in hydroxide flux [CS4]. This route is better supported for metastable polymorph access than Route 1, but the high-pressure equipment penalty is severe [CS3, CS4]. The remaining uncertainty is whether the target P4/mmm LiF sheet is pressure-stabilized or strain-stabilized rather than bulk-pressure stabilized [Reasonable Inference].

#### F2/F3 Route 3: Thin-film deposition

Condition severity is moderate because the route avoids high temperature and GPa pressure but requires vacuum deposition and strict composition control [Reasonable Inference]. Kinetics are mixed: nonequilibrium deposition can stabilize metastable layered polymorphs, but CS/SS/TS provide no direct thin-film precedent, so the crystallization-pathway score is limited. Binary LiF composition control is simpler than multication fluorides reported in SS/TS [SS2–SS5, TS1–TS5], but Li/F re-evaporation or amorphous film formation is possible [Reasonable Inference]. The uncertainty is high because the method is extrapolated rather than directly evidenced.

#### F4: Amorphization tendency

The target has only one cation species, so cation radius mismatch is not an amorphization driver [Target, Reasonable Inference]. There is no evidence that the unit cell contains >40 atoms, so C2 is not triggered; CS/SS/TS do not provide useful direct evidence on the target cell size, so this is neutral. Mixed coordination and corner/edge-sharing connectivity are present in the target [Target], and similar complex fluoride frameworks are reported in SS/TS analogues [SS2, SS3, TS4]. The amorphization risk is therefore low–medium rather than high.

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

The target is likely a low-energy metastable LiF polymorph rather than the ambient ground-state LiF phase, because its E_hull is low but nonzero at 0.0330 eV/atom [Target]. CS evidence strongly suggests that similarly low-above-hull fluoride polymorphs can be synthesized when high-pressure or quench conditions are used, especially the CaF₂ polymorph at E_hull = 0.0346 eV/atom [CS4]. SS and TS analogues support the general feasibility of crystalline fluorides from binary fluoride precursors, sealed tubes, melts, and controlled cooling [SS2, SS3, TS3, TS5]. The most critical enabling factor is nonequilibrium stabilization, either by epitaxial strain in thin films [Reasonable Inference] or by pressure/quench [CS3, CS4]. The most critical limiting factor is competition with ordinary rocksalt LiF and the lack of direct analogue evidence for this exact binary tetragonal LiF sheet [Reasonable Inference; insufficient direct evidence from CS/SS/TS].
