### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability**  
The target’s E_hull of 0.0500 eV/atom is exactly at the favorable metastability threshold, so it receives 90 rather than a mid-range metastable score [Target]. SS/TS analogues show experimentally reported cobalt oxides at E_hull values around 0.0316–0.0875 eV/atom, supporting plausibility for a near-hull metastable oxide [SS1, SS3, TS1, TS2, TS3]. CS analogues are mostly stable but not chemically close to Li–Mg–Co–O redox chemistry, so CS gives insufficient target-specific stability evidence and is treated neutral at 50 [CS1–CS5].

**F2/F3 — Route 1 Flux**  
The flux route benefits from the closest structural analogue, P2/m Li₂Co₄O₈, synthesized with LiCoO₂, Li₂O₂, and LiCl in sealed Au at ~900 °C [SS1, TS2]. F2 is penalized for sealed handling, Li₂O₂ reactivity, and multi-step flux recovery [SS1, SS4]. F3 is high because flux transport and structural similarity reduce reconstruction barriers, but Mg incorporation and Co⁴⁺ stabilization remain inferred rather than demonstrated [SS1, TS2, Reasonable Inference].

**F2/F3 — Route 2 Solid-State**  
CS and TS show oxide/carbonate solid-state routes are feasible at high temperature with regrinding [CS2, CS3, CS5, TS1]. F2 is moderate because oxidizing atmosphere, Li volatility, and repeated anneals are needed [SS3, TS1, Reasonable Inference]. F3 is lower because MgO and Co oxides require long-range diffusion and full reconstruction into ordered P2/m Li₂MgCo₃O₈ [CS3, SS3, Reasonable Inference].

**F2/F3 — Route 3 High Pressure**  
SS4 directly supports Li-rich octahedral oxide synthesis from Li₂O₂ under high pressure, and TS5 supports high-pressure stabilization of metastable oxides [SS4, TS5]. F2 is low because >5 GPa operation, sealed capsules, oxygen-rich conditions, and Li₂O₂ handling are severe [SS4, TS5]. F3 remains moderate because high pressure may stabilize the dense oxidized phase, but cation ordering still depends on solid-state diffusion [SS4, Reasonable Inference].

**F2/F3 — Route 4 Topochemical/Electrochemical Oxidation**  
TS3 provides the strongest evidence for creating fully oxidized Co⁴⁺ oxide at ambient pressure through electrochemical oxidation, and SS2 supports chemical oxidation/deintercalation in cobaltates [TS3, SS2]. F2 is favorable because the oxidation step is mild, but multi-step precursor preparation and oxidant hazards reduce the score [TS3, SS2]. F3 is limited by slow oxidation kinetics and uncertainty that a Li–Mg–Co precursor can retain Li/Mg and convert cleanly to the target [TS3, SS2, Reasonable Inference].

**F2/F3 — Route 5 Sol–Gel**  
TS3 supports nitrate-based precursor chemistry, and sol–gel extrapolation is reasonable for improving Li/Mg/Co homogeneity [TS3, Reasonable Inference]. F2 is moderate because O₂ annealing, Li volatility, and multi-step gel/calcine/anneal processing are needed [TS3, Reasonable Inference]. F3 is relatively strong for precursor activity but uncertain for crystallizing the ordered P2/m target; direct evidence from CS/SS/TS is insufficient for this specific phase [insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS; Reasonable Inference].

**F4 — Amorphization Tendency**  
The target contains multiple octahedral cations with significant size/charge mismatch, which triggers C1 [Target, SS1, Reasonable Inference]. The low-symmetry P2/m cell with many inequivalent Li/Mg/Co/O sites likely exceeds 40 atoms per cell, triggering C2 [Target, Reasonable Inference]. Target connectivity shows corner and edge sharing but no face sharing, so C3 is not triggered [Target].

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

Li₂MgCo₃O₈ is best viewed as a near-hull metastable phase rather than a clear ground-state phase, because its E_hull is 0.0500 eV/atom [Target]. The strongest structural support comes from P2/m Li₂Co₄O₈, which has a closely related Li–Co octahedral network and a reported flux synthesis [SS1, TS2]. The strongest redox support comes from Co⁴⁺-type SrCoO₃ analogues prepared by sealed oxidizing and electrochemical routes [TS1, TS3]. The most important enabling factor is maintaining high oxygen chemical potential while preserving Li/Mg/Co octahedral ordering [TS1, TS3, SS1, Reasonable Inference]. The main limiting factor is simultaneous control of Li volatility, Mg incorporation, and average Co oxidation near +4 [SS1, TS3, Reasonable Inference].
