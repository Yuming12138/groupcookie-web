### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability**  
The target E_hull is 0.0500 eV/atom, exactly at the favorable metastability cutoff, so F1 is scored 90 rather than higher [Target]. Structurally/thermodynamically similar cobalt oxides are reported as synthesized at E_hull values from 0 to ~0.09 eV/atom, which supports experimental accessibility of near-hull cobalt oxides [SS1–SS3, TS1–TS3]. The remaining uncertainty is that the target’s average Co oxidation state is high, and CS analogues are mostly chemically remote non-Li cobalt-free oxides, so CS support is weaker than SS/TS support [Target, CS1–CS5, SS1, TS1, Reasonable Inference].

**Route 1 F2/F3 — Solid-State Sintering**  
Condition severity is moderate because the proposed route avoids >1000 °C and high pressure, but it needs controlled oxidizing conditions, Li-loss control, and multiple grinding/firing steps [CS2, CS3, CS5, SS3, TS2, Reasonable Inference]. Kinetics are only moderate because MgO and Co oxides require solid-state diffusion and the ordered Li/Mg/Co C2/m phase requires substantial reconstruction from binary precursors [Target, CS3, SS3, TS2]. CS provides useful evidence for Mg oxide ceramic processing, while SS/TS provide the stronger cobalt oxide oxygen-control evidence [CS3, SS3, TS2].

**Route 2 F2/F3 — Flux Route**  
Condition severity is moderate because the Li–Co analogue uses ~900 °C rather than extreme temperature or pressure, but sealed noble-metal tubes, Li₂O₂, LiCl, and washing steps add risk [SS1, TS1]. Kinetics are favorable because molten LiCl flux and Li₂O₂ increase transport, and the closest Li–Co analogue already contains a related LiO₆/CoO₆ octahedral network [SS1, TS1]. The main uncertainty is Mg incorporation, because CS supports Mg oxide chemistry but not Mg insertion into this flux-grown Li–Co framework [CS3, SS1, TS1, Reasonable Inference].

**Route 3 F2/F3 — High-Pressure Route**  
Condition severity is challenging in practice because specialized pressure hardware is required, even though the preferred 3–5 GPa window avoids the rubric’s >5 GPa deduction [SS4, TS5]. Kinetics are moderate because high pressure stabilizes dense Li-rich frameworks but short high-pressure runs and MgO diffusion can limit phase completion [SS4, CS3, TS5, Reasonable Inference]. This route is a strong backup if Co⁴⁺ or dense C2/m ordering cannot be stabilized at ambient pressure [Target, SS4, TS5, Reasonable Inference].

**Route 4 F2/F3 — Topochemical/Electrochemical Oxidation**  
Condition severity is relatively mild after precursor synthesis because electrochemical oxidation can occur at room temperature and ambient pressure [TS3]. Kinetics are uncertain because the SrCoO₃ precedent required 180 h, and no source demonstrates a lower-valent Li–Mg–Co precursor that topotactically converts to Li₂MgCo₃O₈ [TS3, SS2, Reasonable Inference]. Insufficient evidence from CS for this oxidation route is treated as neutral source score 50 [CS1–CS5].

**Route 5 F2/F3 — Citrate/Nitrate Route**  
Condition severity is moderate because it avoids high pressure and >1000 °C but uses multistep nitrate/organic processing and O₂ annealing [TS3, SS5, Reasonable Inference].
