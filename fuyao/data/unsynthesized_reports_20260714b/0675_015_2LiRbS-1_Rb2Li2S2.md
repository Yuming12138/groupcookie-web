### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

#### F1 — Thermodynamic stability

The target E_hull is 0.036 eV/atom, placing it in the highly synthesizable ≤0.05 eV/atom category. The exact RbLiS analogue in CS/SS has E_hull = 0.0 eV/atom [CS1, SS1], which strongly supports accessibility of the composition. The uncertainty is that the target description differs slightly from the reported RbLiS coordination environment, so it may be a metastable polymorph rather than the fully relaxed ground-state structure [target, CS1].

#### F2 — Route 1

Route 1 uses moderate temperature, 793 K / 520 °C, under Ar, with sealed Ag crucibles in Duran ampoules [CS1, SS1]. There is no evidence for high pressure, H₂, NH₃, or >1000 °C conditions [CS1, SS1]. Deductions arise from sealed handling and air-sensitive alkali sulfide precursors/products [CS1, CS5, SS1]. CS and SS provide strong positive evidence; TS is not needed for this route and gives insufficient route-specific signal beyond general alkali sulfide sensitivity [insufficient evidence from TS except TS5 for Rb₂S handling].

#### F3 — Route 1

The kinetics are favorable because the precursors Li₂S and Rb₂S already contain the target ions and anion, and this exact precursor pair produces RbLiS [CS1, SS1]. The need for three weeks of annealing indicates slow long-range ordering or crystal growth [CS1, SS1]. Structurally, the target and reference share the same P4/nmm matlockite family [CS1, SS1], so the reconstruction penalty is modest.

#### F2 — Route 2

Route 2 is penalized for vacuum/sealed-ampoule handling, volatile sulfur, and air-sensitive alkali metals [TS5; Reasonable Inference]. The Rb₂S reference shows Rb + S can be reacted at only 623 K in evacuated quartz [TS5], so the temperature severity is not high. However, CS/SS do not report direct elemental synthesis of RbLiS, so their evidence for this route is insufficient and scored neutral for route-specific reliability [insufficient evidence from CS/SS].

#### F3 — Route 2

Elemental Rb and sulfur are reactive enough to form Rb₂S under moderate sealed conditions [TS5], which supports initial binary formation. The ternary P4/nmm phase still requires cation ordering into the matlockite-like RbLiS structure demonstrated from binary sulfides [CS1, SS1]. This adds uncertainty and lowers the structural reconstruction score relative to Route 1.

#### F2 — Route 3

TS analogues show carbonate/chalcogen/H₂ routes at 925–1030 K and ambient pressure [TS1, TS2, TS4]. The controlled H₂ atmosphere and volatile sulfur/chalcogen transport increase severity [TS1, TS2, TS4]. CS/SS give insufficient direct evidence that carbonate/H₂ chemistry yields RbLiS, so this route is less reliable despite moderate temperatures [insufficient evidence from CS/SS].

#### F3 — Route 3

The reactive gas/flux environment can accelerate sulfide formation, as TS analogues crystallize within 6–10 h [TS1, TS2, TS4]. However, those compounds are transition-metal thiometallates rather than P4/nmm RbLiS, so structure selection is uncertain [TS1, TS2, TS4]. The route may produce competing Rb₂S, Li₂S, oxides, or carbonate residues unless sulfur chemical potential and reducing conditions are well controlled [Reasonable Inference].

#### F4 — Amorphization tendency

The Li⁺/Rb⁺ cation size mismatch triggers one amorphization-risk criterion [Reasonable Inference], but crystallization of exact and close matlockite analogues shows the structure can accommodate this mismatch [CS1, SS1–SS4]. The cell is small and the reported connectivity is mainly corner/edge-sharing tetrahedral rather than a highly frustrated vertex/edge/face-sharing network [target, CS1, SS1]. Remaining uncertainty comes from the target being a 2D metastable variant at 0.036 eV/atom, which may require careful annealing/cooling to avoid relaxation to the known RbLiS polymorph [target, CS1].

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

The target is likely a **low-energy metastable polymorph** rather than an obviously unstable phase, because its E_hull is only 0.036 eV/atom [target], while a closely related/exact-composition RbLiS P4/nmm phase is reported as stable [CS1, SS1]. The CS and SS evidence are very strong: the same composition and closely matching matlockite structure have been synthesized from Li₂S and Rb₂S under moderate sealed solid-state conditions [CS1, SS1]. TS evidence is supportive mainly for general alkali sulfide feasibility and Rb₂S precursor formation, but it is less structurally/chemically specific [TS5; insufficient evidence from TS for exact RbLiS]. The key enabling factor is the availability of a direct binary-sulfide route using precursors that already contain the desired ions and anion [CS1, SS1]. The key limiting factor is phase selection between the target 4-coordinate/2D variant and the reported 5-coordinate matlockite-like RbLiS ground-state analogue [target, CS1].
