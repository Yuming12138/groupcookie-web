### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic stability.**  
The target has E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom, which places it in the highest thermodynamic score band (target data). The closest enargite-like Zn₂PN₃ analogue is also on the hull and experimentally synthesized phase-pure under HP/HT conditions (CS-1, SS-1, TS-1). The uncertainty is that computed hull stability does not guarantee kinetic accessibility or resistance to Zn/N loss [Reasonable Inference].

**F2/F3 — Route 1: High-pressure route.**  
The severe F2 score is driven by direct analogue conditions of 1200 °C and 8 GPa for Zn₂PN₃ (CS-1, SS-1, TS-1). The strong structural match improves F3 because Zn₂PN₃ is enargite-like with corner-sharing tetrahedra, similar to MoZn₃N₄ (CS-1/SS-1/TS-1; target description). The main uncertainty is whether Mo can be oxidized/ordered into tetrahedral MoN₄ under the same HP conditions [Reasonable Inference].

**F2/F3 — Route 2: Sealed nitride solid-state route.**  
F2 is moderate because analogous nitrides are made at 673 K–930 °C without GPa pressure but require inert/sealed handling (SS-2/TS-2; SS-5/TS-5; CS-4/SS-3/TS-3). F3 is limited by Mo’s refractory character and by long diffusion times reported for solid-state analogues such as 4 d for Li₅ReN₄ and 7 d for Mg₂PN₃ (SS-2/TS-2; SS-5/TS-5). This route is favored overall because it avoids high pressure while still using nitride chemistry supported by several analogues.

**F2/F3 — Route 3: Ammonolysis/nitridation.**  
F2 is penalized by the NH₃ atmosphere, multistep processing, and air-sensitive nitride handling, all consistent with the Zn₄P₆N₁₂O NH₃ route (CS-4/SS-3/TS-3). F3 is lower because NH₃ can aid nitridation but CS/SS/TS do not demonstrate MoZn₃N₄ or an enargite Mo–Zn nitride by ammonolysis; insufficient direct evidence from CS/SS/TS, neutral = 50. The risk is oxygen/chloride contamination if MoO₃ or MoCl₅ is used [Reasonable Inference].

**F2/F3 — Route 4: Flux route.**  
F2 is penalized by NaN₃/Na handling, sealed Nb/Ta containers, vacuum encapsulation, and multistep flux removal, as shown for LiSrGaN₂ flux synthesis (SS-4/TS-4). F3 benefits from enhanced mass transport in flux, but the structural transfer is weak because LiSrGaN₂ is not enargite and contains different cations (SS-4/TS-4). The remaining uncertainty is Mo solubility and Zn volatility in the chosen flux [Reasonable Inference].

**F4 — Amorphization tendency.**  
The target has corner-sharing tetrahedral connectivity only, which is favorable against amorphization because mixed edge/face/vertex connectivity is not reported (target description). One risk is the large Zn–Mo cation size/charge mismatch, inferred from Zn²⁺ and Mo⁶⁺ charge balance and known tetrahedral radius trends [Reasonable Inference]. No CS/SS/TS source provides direct amorphization data for MoZn₃N₄, so that specific evidence is insufficient and neutral = 50.
