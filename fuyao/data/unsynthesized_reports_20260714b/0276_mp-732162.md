### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic stability.**  
The target has E_hull = 0.0500 eV/atom, which falls at the favorable ≤0.05 eV/atom threshold in the required rubric. TS analogues are supportive because TS-1 to TS-5 include synthesized thermodynamically similar molecular halides with E_hull values near the same range, especially TS-3 at 0.0339 eV/atom and TS-2 at 0.0767 eV/atom. SS is also supportive because SS-2 and SS-5 are zero-dimensional halogen-rich structures with low hull energies. CS is less diagnostic for stability because CS entries include high-E_hull hydrated/molecular crystals, so insufficient evidence from CS for thermodynamic stability; CS scored neutral, 50, for this sub-factor.

**F2/F3 — Route 1.**  
CS-1, CS-2, CS-4, CS-5 and SS-1 strongly support mild solution crystallization of chloride/oxygen/halide analogues under ambient or desiccator conditions, which gives Route 1 a high F2 score. Kinetically, solution diffusion is favorable, but the target’s discrete O₂ clusters and iodine chloride chemistry are not directly demonstrated in CS/SS/TS; therefore structural assembly is uncertain. The main uncertainty is hydrolysis or disproportionation of ICl₃/ICl₅ in water **[Reasonable Inference]**.

**F2/F3 — Route 2.**  
SS-2/TS-3 directly supports sealed-ampoule vapor/transport growth of zero-dimensional chlorides at 140–155 °C under vacuum, and SS-5/TS-2 supports sealed growth of related halogen-rich molecular crystals. This route has harsher handling requirements, because volatile/toxic halogen precursors and sealed ampoules are required **[Reasonable Inference]**, which lowers F2. F3 is high because vapor transport avoids solid-state diffusion and better matches zero-dimensional molecular crystallization, although direct evidence for trapping O₂ clusters remains insufficient from CS/SS/TS.

**F2/F3 — Route 3.**  
SS-5/TS-2 and TS-1/TS-4/TS-5 support sealed autoclave/solvothermal or hydrothermal routes for thermodynamically similar molecular halides, so pressure-assisted crystallization is plausible. The F2 penalty is significant because sealed autoclave work, reactive halogens, and toxic/volatile precursors are required. F3 is moderate-high because solvothermal transport is efficient, but SS-5/TS-2 reports multiphase products and manual separation, indicating phase-control risk.

**F4 — Amorphization tendency.**  
The target is zero-dimensional and composed of isolated ICl₄ and O₂ clusters, so it lacks extended mixed connectivity that usually promotes glassy disorder. The described structural contents are below 40 atoms, and there is no multi-cation size mismatch. Insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS for amorphization tendencies specifically, so the score is mainly from the target structural description and general crystallization reasoning **[Reasonable Inference]**.
