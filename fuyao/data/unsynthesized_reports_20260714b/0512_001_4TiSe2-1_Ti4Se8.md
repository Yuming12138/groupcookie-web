[Target] Exact-composition TiSe₂ in the closest CS/SS/TS entry also has E_hull = 0.0 eV/atom, independently supporting a stable TiSe₂ composition. [CS-1; SS-1; TS-1] Remaining uncertainty is not bulk thermodynamic stability but whether the lower-symmetry P321 variant is experimentally accessible rather than relaxing to the common P-3m1 analogue. [Target vs CS-1/SS-1/TS-1; Reasonable Inference]

#### F2 — Route 1: Direct Solid-State

The route avoids >1000 °C and high pressure, since exact TiSe₂ analogues are synthesized at 480–900 °C in sealed silica tubes. [CS-1; SS-1; TS-1] Deductions arise from vacuum/dry-Ar handling, volatile Se in sealed tubes, and multistep heating/grinding/reannealing. [CS-1; SS-1; TS-1] The severity is moderate rather than extreme because the equipment is standard sealed-tube/furnace hardware. [CS-1; SS-1; TS-1]

Elemental Ti is a refractory metal, so precursor activity and cation diffusion are not ideal; this lowers the kinetic score.
