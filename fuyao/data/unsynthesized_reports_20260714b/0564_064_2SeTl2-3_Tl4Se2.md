### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

#### F1 — Thermodynamic Stability

The target E_hull is 0.016 eV/atom, which falls well inside the ≤0.05 eV/atom high-synthesizability range (Target). Thermodynamically similar Tl₄PbSe₃ and Tl₄SnSe₃ have E_hull values of 0.0136 and 0.0371 eV/atom and are experimentally synthesized by melt routes, supporting that this metastability range is accessible (TS1, TS2). Structural analogues also include stable or low-hull Tl–Se compounds, including Tl₅Se₂Br at E_hull = 0 and Tl₄PbSe₃ at 0.0136 eV/atom (SS1, SS3). Uncertainty remains because low E_hull indicates thermodynamic accessibility but not necessarily that the reported orthorhombic molybdenite-like polymorph is the easiest Tl₂Se polymorph to isolate.

#### F2 — Route 1

Route 1 uses moderate temperature, similar to Tl₅Se₂Br synthesis near 500 °C in evacuated quartz tubes (CS3, SS1), so no high-temperature deduction is applied. The main severity penalties come from vacuum sealing, volatile Se/Tl behavior, multiple thermal steps, and Tl toxicity; vacuum sealed synthesis is explicitly used in CS3/SS1 and TS4, while volatility and toxicity are physicochemical inferences from elemental Tl/Se **[Reasonable Inference]**. CS and SS strongly support the route; TS supports related evacuated ampoule selenide synthesis but not binary Tl₂Se specifically (TS4). Remaining uncertainty is phase competition within the binary Tl–Se phase diagram **[Reasonable Inference]**.

#### F3 — Route 1

Kinetics are favorable because Tl is low-melting and can wet/react with Se, giving more liquid-assisted transport than refractory oxide solid-state synthesis **[Reasonable Inference]**. The long anneal in Tl₅Se₂Br indicates that ordering and homogenization may be slow even when formation is feasible (CS3, SS1). Structurally similar Tl₅Se₂Br contains comparable Tl–Se bond lengths and Tl three-coordinate motifs, supporting transferability but not identical topology (SS1). The uncertainty is whether the target’s orthorhombic 2D sheet forms directly or requires reconstructive polymorph selection.

#### F2 — Route 2

Bridgman growth remains below 1000 °C, consistent with 600–700 °C melt-grown Tl₄SnSe₃/Tl₄PbSe₃ analogues and 800 °C chemically similar chalcogenide growth (SS2, SS3, TS1, TS2, CS2). Severity remains moderate because sealed ampoules, vacuum/dry Ar handling, multiple steps, and toxic Tl are required; dry Ar/glovebox handling is explicitly reported for structurally similar Tl chalcogenides (SS2, SS3). CS supports Bridgman methods for Bi₂Se₃/Bi₄Se₃, but direct binary Tl₂Se Bridgman data are insufficient from CS/SS/TS. The method is therefore plausible but equipment- and control-intensive.

#### F3 — Route 2

Melt growth improves kinetics by eliminating long-range solid-state diffusion barriers, as shown by Tl₄MSe₃ analogues grown from molten stoichiometric mixtures (SS2, SS3, TS1, TS2). Directional cooling can improve crystal quality and phase selection, as demonstrated for Bi₂Se₃-type compounds by Bridgman-Stockbarger growth (CS5). The structural reconstruction score is not maximal because the target’s orthorhombic molybdenite-like binary structure is not directly matched by the tetragonal I4/mcm analogues (SS2, SS3). Uncertainty lies in possible incongruent melting or polymorphic transitions **[Reasonable Inference]**.

#### F2 — Route 3

Thermal evaporation uses moderate substrate/annealing temperatures and high vacuum, directly supported by CS1. The route is penalized for high-vacuum requirements, post-deposition annealing, volatile species, and Tl toxicity; CS1 specifically reports vacuum deposition and subsequent heating, while volatility/toxicity concerns are **[Reasonable Inference]**. SS and TS provide insufficient direct evidence for thin-film deposition of structurally or thermodynamically similar Tl₂Se-like phases, so their route-specific support is neutral. This route is less severe in temperature but more demanding in compositional control.

#### F3 — Route 3

Vapor availability is plausible because CS1 uses evaporation for chemically similar heavy chalcogenide systems, but Tl and Se vapor pressures make stoichiometric control difficult **[Reasonable Inference]**. CS1 explicitly notes defect structures, disordered phases, narrow composition ranges, and reproducibility issues, lowering the composition-control score. Thin-film growth may help stabilize a 2D metastable phase **[Reasonable Inference]**, but there is insufficient SS/TS evidence for direct crystallization of the target-like structure by deposition. Overall kinetics are acceptable, but phase purity is the dominant risk.

#### F2 — Route 4

Flux growth operates at moderate temperature, as TS5 uses 723–773 K and slow cooling in evacuated carbon-coated fused-silica tubes. It receives the same deductions for vacuum, sealed volatile chalcogenide flux, multistep processing, and Tl toxicity; TS5 explicitly supports evacuated carbon-coated tubes and slow-cooling flux protocols. CS and SS support melt/ampoule processing generally but provide insufficient direct evidence for binary Tl₂Se self-flux growth. The route is experimentally more complex than direct annealing but not harsher in temperature.

#### F3 — Route 4

Flux strongly improves reaction kinetics and crystal growth by providing a liquid transport medium, supported by the successful Tl₂Q flux route in TS5. Slow cooling over 14 days in TS5 supports high-quality crystal growth but also indicates that crystallization is time-dependent. The structural score is limited because no source directly confirms the binary orthorhombic Tl₂Se polymorph from a Tl–Se flux; this is a **[Reasonable Inference]** from Tl₂Q flux chemistry. The route is ranked best because it balances low thermal severity with enhanced transport and phase selection.

#### F4 — Amorphization Tendency

The target is a binary material with only one cation species, so the cation-radius mismatch check is not triggered (Target); CS/SS/TS mixed-cation analogues do not override this target-specific fact. Tl₂Se with E_hull = 0.016 eV/atom is best viewed as a near-ground-state or weakly metastable phase rather than a highly metastable compound (Target; TS1/TS2 show similar low-hull Tl selenides are synthesizable).
