### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

#### F1 — Thermodynamic stability

The target is reported at E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom, which corresponds to a ground-state prediction under the given thermodynamic model [target]. Chemically identical CdI₂ polytypes in CS are all extremely close to the hull, with E_hull values around 10⁻⁵–10⁻⁴ eV/atom [CS-1 to CS-5]. Structurally and thermodynamically similar entries are also very low-energy, including tetrahedral HgI₂ at ~0.0006 eV/atom and CdI₂ polytypes at ~0.0001–0.0005 eV/atom [SS-1 to SS-5; TS-1 to TS-5]. The remaining uncertainty is that low computed hull energy does not guarantee that the tetrahedral CdI₂ polymorph is kinetically accessible relative to common octahedral CdI₂ polytypes [CS-1 to CS-5].

#### F2 — Route 1 severity

Aqueous slow evaporation is among the mildest routes: room-temperature spontaneous evaporation is directly reported for CdI₂ [SS-2, TS-2], and aqueous evaporation is repeatedly reported in CS/SS/TS [CS-1, CS-2, CS-5; SS-3 to SS-5; TS-3 to TS-5]. No high temperature, pressure, special atmosphere, or sealed system is required in the cited solution-growth reports [CS-1, CS-2, CS-5]. The only major severity penalty is cadmium toxicity, because CdI₂ is the required precursor [CS-1, SS-2, TS-2; toxicity is Reasonable Inference].

#### F3 — Route 1 kinetics

Kinetics are favorable because CdI₂ is dissolved and recrystallized directly, avoiding solid-state diffusion [CS-1, CS-2, CS-5; SS-2; TS-2]. However, the structural reconstruction score is reduced because the cited CdI₂ products are octahedral CdI₆ layered polytypes, whereas the target requires corner-sharing CdI₄ tetrahedra [CS-1 to CS-5; SS-2 to SS-5; TS-2 to TS-5; target]. This route may yield crystals readily, but not necessarily the desired orthorhombic Pmc2₁ tetrahedral polymorph [CS-1 to CS-5].

#### F2 — Route 2 severity

The HgI₂ analogue route uses cooling of a saturated solution from the boiling point of 2-chloroethanol, which remains a low-temperature, ambient-pressure crystallization method [SS-1, TS-1]. No high-pressure, high-temperature, reactive-atmosphere, or sealed-ampoule condition is reported [SS-1, TS-1]. Toxicity remains the main penalty because both HgI₂ and CdI₂ are heavy-metal iodides, and CdI₂ would be used for the target [SS-1, TS-1; Cd toxicity is Reasonable Inference].

#### F3 — Route 2 kinetics

This route has the strongest structural kinetic argument because the HgI₂ analogue is also a 2D corner-sharing MI₄ tetrahedral iodide with M–I distances close to the target Cd–I distances [SS-1, TS-1; target]. Solution crystallization should provide high mobility and low nucleation barriers relative to solid-state synthesis [SS-1, TS-1]. The uncertainty is chemical transferability: CS shows CdI₂ usually crystallizing as octahedral CdI₆ sheets, so CS provides insufficient direct support for tetrahedral CdI₂ formation and is treated as neutral for the tetrahedral structural pathway [CS-1 to CS-5].

#### F2 — Route 3 severity

Vapor-phase CdI₂ growth is directly reported [CS-3, CS-4], but the conditions are less benign than solution growth because a sealed or controlled vapor environment is likely required to manage iodide volatility [CS-3, CS-4; sealed ampoule is Reasonable Inference]. CS does not specify exact atmosphere or temperature, so exact severity has uncertainty and SS/TS provide insufficient route-specific vapor evidence, treated as neutral for this route condition [SS-2 to SS-5; TS-2 to TS-5]. Cadmium toxicity and volatile iodide handling reduce the F2 score [CS-3, CS-4; Reasonable Inference].

#### F3 — Route 3 kinetics

CdI₂ has demonstrated vapor availability because CdI₂ crystals were grown from the vapor phase [CS-3, CS-4]. Direct vapor crystallization can access layered CdI₂ polytypes, but the reported vapor-grown analogues are octahedral CdI₆ structures rather than the target tetrahedral CdI₄ structure [CS-3, CS-4]. Composition control is moderately challenging because iodide volatility can shift stoichiometry during transport unless the sealed gradient is well controlled [CS-3, CS-4; volatility-control issue is Reasonable Inference].

#### F4 — Amorphization tendency

The target has only one cation species, so no cation-radius mismatch trigger applies [target]. The target is a simple binary CdI₂ sheet structure with corner-sharing tetrahedra, and there is no reported coexistence of edge-, face-, and corner-sharing polyhedra in the target [target]. The main uncertainty is not amorphization but polymorph selection among crystalline CdI₂ structures, since many CdI₂ polytypes are reported [CS-1 to CS-5; SS-2 to SS-5; TS-2 to TS-5].

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

The target is likely thermodynamically stable or at least very close to a ground-state phase because its reported E_hull is 0.0000 eV/atom [target]. The CS data strongly supports easy synthesis of crystalline CdI₂ from CdI₂ precursor by solution evaporation or vapor growth, but it mostly supports octahedral CdI₆ CdI₂ polytypes rather than the target tetrahedral CdI₄ polymorph [CS-1 to CS-5]. The SS/TS data provides the strongest structural support through tetrahedral layered HgI₂, which is made by low-temperature cooling crystallization from solution [SS-1, TS-1]. The most critical enabling factor is the direct availability and solution/vapor processability of CdI₂ as both precursor and product composition [CS-1 to CS-5; SS-2; TS-2]. The most critical limiting factor is polymorph control: CdI₂ readily forms multiple layered octahedral polytypes, while the desired orthorhombic tetrahedral Pmc2₁ motif is not directly demonstrated in the CdI₂ references [CS-1 to CS-5; SS-2 to SS-5; TS-2 to TS-5].
