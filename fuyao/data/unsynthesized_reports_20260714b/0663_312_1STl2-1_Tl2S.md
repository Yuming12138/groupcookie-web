### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

#### F1 — Thermodynamic Stability

The target E_hull is 0.0160 eV/atom, placing it well within the ≤0.05 eV/atom high-synthesizability range [Target]. Thermodynamic analogues also cluster near the hull, including Tl₂TeS₃ at 0.0264 eV/atom [TS1], Tl₄PbSe₃ at 0.0136 eV/atom [TS2], and Tl₆SBr₄ at 0.0044 eV/atom [TS4]. Chemically similar and structurally similar Tl/Bi chalcogenides include several experimentally reported stable or near-stable phases [CS1–CS4, SS1–SS4]. The remaining uncertainty is polymorphism: a low E_hull supports existence, but not necessarily preferential formation of the P-6m2 molybdenite-like polymorph [Target; Reasonable Inference].

#### F2 — Route 1: Direct Ampoule Reaction

Route 1 is penalized mainly for vacuum/sealed-ampoule handling, volatile sulfur, multiple heating/annealing steps, and toxic Tl precursors [CS1, SS2, SS3, SS4]. The temperature itself is not severe because related Tl sulfides/chalcogenides form at 350–800 °C, below the >1000 °C penalty threshold [CS1, SS2, SS3, SS4]. The uncertainty is whether high-temperature equilibration will produce the target 2D polymorph or a competing bulk Tl₂S phase [Target; Reasonable Inference].

#### F3 — Route 1: Direct Ampoule Reaction

Kinetics are helped by low-melting Tl and reactive sulfur, and related Tl sulfides are made in sealed ampoules at moderate temperatures [SS2, SS3, SS4]. However, long anneals in analogous Tl sulfides, including several weeks for Tl₄GeS₄ and 83 days for Tl₄SiS₄, indicate slow ordering or phase-selection issues [SS3, SS4]. Structural reconstruction into a molybdenite-like P-6m2 sheet is the main kinetic risk because the target is a specific layered polymorph [Target; Reasonable Inference].

#### F2 — Route 2: Solvothermal Route

Route 2 avoids high temperature and high pressure, supported by Tl₂TeS₃ solvothermal synthesis at 433 K [TS1]. It still involves sealed-vessel operation, multistep workup, and toxic thallium precursors [TS1; Reasonable Inference]. The TS source provides useful support for Tl–S solvothermal chemistry, while CS and SS provide insufficient direct evidence for binary Tl₂S solvothermal synthesis [insufficient evidence from CS; insufficient evidence from SS].

#### F3 — Route 2: Solvothermal Route

The solution/solvothermal environment gives high precursor activity and avoids long-range solid-state diffusion [TS1]. Slow cooling at 1 K/h in the Tl₂TeS₃ route suggests controlled crystal growth can occur at low temperature [TS1]. The key uncertainty is phase purity because TS1 reports possible impurities including unreacted sulfur, Tl₂S₂O₃, and β-Tl₂SO₄, which suggests oxidation/side-reaction control is important [TS1].

#### F2 — Route 3: Thin-Film/Vapor Route

Route 3 uses vacuum deposition, which is directly supported for TlBr-type halides in CS5, but not directly for Tl₂S [CS5; insufficient evidence from SS; insufficient evidence from TS]. The penalties arise from vacuum operation, toxic Tl, volatile sulfur/Tl fluxes, and multistep deposition plus annealing [CS5; Reasonable Inference]. It remains attractive only if the target polymorph is metastable and substrate/low-temperature trapping is needed [Target; Reasonable Inference].

#### F3 — Route 3: Thin-Film/Vapor Route

Thin-film kinetics can bypass bulk diffusion and may stabilize low-dimensional phases, consistent with low-temperature metastable halide deposition in CS5 [CS5]. However, the vapor pressures of Tl and S make composition control difficult, and there is no direct SS/TS evidence for vapor-grown Tl₂S [insufficient evidence from SS; insufficient evidence from TS]. The route therefore receives the lowest F3 despite its potential for polymorph control [Reasonable Inference].

#### F4 — Amorphization Tendency

The target contains only one cation, Tl, so there is no cation-radius mismatch trigger [Target]. The described P-6m2 Tl₂S sheet has a simple binary composition and no indication of a >40 atom unit cell [Target]. Its connectivity is described as edge-sharing STl₆ pentagonal pyramids rather than mixed vertex/edge/face-sharing polyhedra, so the amorphization risk is low [Target].

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

Tl₂S in the specified molybdenite-like P-6m2 structure is likely a **near-ground-state or mildly metastable** phase because its E_hull is only 0.0160 eV/atom [Target]. The CS/SS/TS analogues strongly support the general feasibility of Tl chalcogenide synthesis in sealed ampoules, melts, and low-temperature solvothermal conditions [CS1, SS2–SS4, TS1]. The most critical enabling factor is the low thermodynamic penalty combined with the soft, low-temperature Tl–S chemistry demonstrated in related Tl sulfides and thallium chalcogenides [Target, SS2, SS3, TS1]. The main limiting factor is polymorph selectivity: high-temperature bulk routes may form a different Tl₂S structure, while low-temperature routes may give poorly crystalline or impurity-containing sulfides [Target, TS1; Reasonable Inference]. Overall, synthesis is plausible, with the solvothermal route favored for accessing the layered polymorph and the sealed-ampoule route favored for robust bulk phase formation [TS1, CS1, SS2–SS4].
