### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

#### F1 — Thermodynamic stability

The target has E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom, so it scores in the highest stability bracket. Several chemically/structurally/thermodynamically similar fluorides and oxyfluorides are also on the hull, including EuMoO₄F/GdMoO₄F/TmMoO₄F (CS-1, CS-3, CS-4), CsRuF₆ (SS-1, TS-1), RbVOF₃ (SS-3, TS-2), and Cs₂IrF₆ (SS-4, TS-3). A caveat is that CsMn(OF₂)₂ was synthesized despite high E_hull ≈ 0.388 eV/atom (CS-5), showing the database hull is not a perfect predictor for solution-grown fluorides; nevertheless, the target’s hull value is favorable.

#### F2 — Route 1 condition severity

Route 1 is mild because analogous CsMn(OF₂)₂ and Cs₂IrF₆ crystallized by room-temperature slow evaporation (CS-5, SS-4, TS-3). The main penalty is hazardous chemistry: HF is directly used in CS-5 and SS-2/SS-3/TS-2, while Os/nitrosyl reagents are toxic by **[Reasonable Inference]**. There is insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS that a sealed or high-pressure system is required for Route 1.

#### F3 — Route 1 kinetics

Solution crystallization gives high precursor activity and avoids long-range solid diffusion, as shown by overnight slow evaporation for CsMn(OF₂)₂ (CS-5) and room-temperature evaporation for Cs₂IrF₆ (SS-4, TS-3). The reduced structural reconstruction burden is supported by pre-existing dissolved fluorometallate units in solution routes (SS-4, TS-3). The uncertainty is that none of CS/SS/TS confirms the stability or formation pathway of [OsNOF₅]⁻ in HF solution.

#### F2 — Route 2 condition severity

Hydrothermal CsVOF₃/RbVOF₃ forms at only 100–160 °C for 24 h (SS-2, SS-3, TS-2), so temperature severity is low. The route still requires HF and sealed hydrothermal vessels, and the multi-step dissolution/autoclave/wash sequence is explicitly described for structurally similar oxyfluorides (SS-2, SS-3, TS-2). CS-2 demonstrates that hydrothermal oxyfluorides can require much higher pressure, but for the V/Cs/Rb oxyfluorides the conditions are milder; therefore SS-2/SS-3/TS-2 are weighted more heavily for this target.

#### F3 — Route 2 kinetics

HF hydrothermal media activate oxide precursors, as V₂O₅ is used for CsVOF₃/RbVOF₃ (SS-2, SS-3, TS-2) and Mn₂O₃ dissolves/reacts in HF for CsMn(OF₂)₂ (CS-5). Ion transport is efficient in the hydrothermal medium, giving a high diffusion score. The structural score is limited because OsNOF₅ contains a nitrosyl ligand, and there is insufficient evidence from CS/SS/TS for hydrothermal nitrosyl incorporation.

#### F2 — Route 3 condition severity

Direct fluorination is substantially harsher because CsRuF₆/RbRuF₆ require F₂ at 350 °C (SS-1, SS-5, TS-1, TS-4), and Cs₂PtF₆ requires a diluted F₂ step plus prolonged heating in a Pt tube (TS-5). The F₂/NOF atmosphere is reactive and toxic, and specialized fluorination apparatus is implied by SS-1/SS-5/TS-1/TS-4/TS-5. The route avoids >1000 °C and GPa pressure, but gas handling dominates severity.

#### F3 — Route 3 kinetics

F₂ gas can fluorinate transition-metal chloride mixtures at relatively low temperature, as shown for Ru fluorides (SS-1, SS-5, TS-1, TS-4), which improves precursor activity. However, solid-state ordering around osmium is expected to be sluggish because dense Os compounds are refractory **[Reasonable Inference]**, and TS-5 shows some 5d fluorides may require long treatments. Structural reconstruction is only moderate because pure MF₆ fluorides in SS/TS do not directly map onto a layered OsNOF₅ nitrosyl oxyfluoride.

#### F4 — Amorphization tendency

The large Cs/Os size mismatch triggers one amorphization-risk criterion; analogous Cs fluorometallates also show large Cs coordination polyhedra combined with compact MF₆ octahedra (SS-1, SS-4, TS-1, TS-3). The target formula and described unit do not imply >40 atoms per cell, so C2 is not triggered. Mixed connectivity is not explicitly reported for the target, even though some SS/TS analogues have mixed corner/edge/face sharing; therefore C3 is not triggered for the target.

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

CsOsNOF₅ is likely a thermodynamic ground-state or very near-ground-state phase because its reported E_hull is 0.0000 eV/atom. The strongest analogue support comes from Cs-containing fluorometallates and oxyfluorides that crystallize from HF solution or mild hydrothermal media, especially CsMn(OF₂)₂ (CS-5), CsVOF₃/RbVOF₃ (SS-2, SS-3, TS-2), and Cs₂IrF₆ (SS-4, TS-3). The most critical enabling factor is high fluoride activity in solution, which can stabilize octahedral metal fluoride/oxyfluoride units while allowing Cs⁺ to template the lattice (CS-5, SS-2, SS-4, TS-2, TS-3). The most critical limiting factor is not the Cs–F framework but the controlled formation and retention of the Os–N–O nitrosyl ligand, for which CS/SS/TS provide insufficient direct evidence. If a stable [OsNOF₅]⁻ precursor or in situ nitrosylation chemistry is available, the material should be quite synthesizable; without that precursor control, competing Os fluorides/oxides are likely.
