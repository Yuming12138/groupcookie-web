### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic stability.**  
The target has E_hull = 0.0000 eV/atom, which places it in the strongest thermodynamic stability bin. TS further supports that related selenides such as ThMnSe3, CeYbSe3, NaCuHf2Se5, Gd2Se3, and Th2Se5 can be computed ground states or near-ground-state phases. CS also contains multiple chemically similar Ln–M–Se phases at or near the hull. The remaining uncertainty is that computed hull stability does not guarantee kinetic accessibility or phase purity.

**F2/F3 — Route 1.**  
The condition severity is moderate because EuZrSe3 was made in an evacuated quartz tube at 1273 K for 8 d, requiring sealed-tube handling but not high pressure (SS). The kinetic score is limited by refractory Hf diffusion [Reasonable Inference], although the nearly exact EuZrSe3 structural analogue strongly supports structural accessibility (SS). Hf selenide formation under similar sealed high-temperature conditions is supported by NaCuHf2Se5 (TS). The main uncertainty is whether Hf substitutes cleanly into the EuZrSe3-type Pnma framework without forming binary HfSe2/HfSe3-like impurities [Reasonable Inference].

**F2/F3 — Route 2.**  
KI-assisted LnYbSe3 syntheses use Ar glovebox loading, sealed tubes under ~10^-4 Torr, 1223 K holds, slow cooling, and washing, so the route has moderate severity (CS, SS, TS). Flux improves transport, but the structurally related KI products are mostly Cmcm rather than the target Pnma topology, so the structural reconstruction score is only moderate (CS, SS, TS). Insufficient evidence from SS for KI-assisted EuHfSe3 specifically; the route is plausible but less direct than Route 1. The uncertainty is possible stabilization of a different ABSe3 polymorph.

**F2/F3 — Route 3.**  
Sb2Se3 flux has strong chemical precedent for Ln–Lu–Se phases at 1000 °C for 14 d in sealed ampules (CS). It may improve crystal growth and provide a Se-rich medium [Reasonable Inference], but no Hf-containing Sb2Se3-flux analogue is provided, so Hf transport and phase selectivity are uncertain. The long dwell and slow-cooling profile indicate significant crystallization control requirements (CS). Insufficient evidence from TS for Sb2Se3 flux transfer to Hf phases, so this route is secondary.

**F2/F3 — Route 4.**  
NaCl flux enables lanthanoid sesquiselenide growth at 850 °C in evacuated ampoules (TS), giving a comparatively mild thermal route. However, that evidence is for binary lanthanoid selenides, not Eu–Hf ternaries, and Hf reactivity in NaCl flux is not demonstrated. SS indicates Zr/Hf-type ternary selenides often need high temperatures, excess chalcogen, or careful phase control, so kinetics may be limiting. Insufficient evidence from CS for NaCl flux in EuHfSe3-type compounds; this route is plausible but exploratory.

**F4 — Amorphization tendency.**  
The target has a large A/B cation size and coordination contrast: Eu is 8-coordinate while Hf is octahedral, which triggers the ionic-radius mismatch check [Reasonable Inference]. The structurally closest EuZrSe3 analogue has a 20-atom Eu4Zr4Se12 Pnma cell, so the unit-cell-size amorphization criterion is not triggered (SS). The target and EuZrSe3 contain edge- and corner-sharing motifs but no described face-sharing, so the mixed-connectivity criterion is not fully triggered (SS). The uncertainty is that Hf-rich amorphous selenium phases are known as a competing issue in SrZrSe3-type chemistry, where amorphous Zr–Se phases were reported (SS).

### SECTION 6 — Synthesizability Interpretation

EuHfSe3 is likely a ground-state or very near-ground-state phase because its reported E_hull is 0.0000 eV/atom, and several thermodynamically similar selenides are also stable on the hull (TS). The strongest synthesis support is structural: EuZrSe3 is essentially the Zr analogue of the target and was directly synthesized in Pnma by a sealed-tube elemental reaction (SS). Chemical analogues in the LnYbSe3 and LnLuSe3 families show that ternary rare-earth selenides are accessible by solid-state and flux routes, but they often crystallize in Cmcm rather than the exact Pnma target type (CS). The most critical enabling factor is the very close EuZrSe3 structural template, which strongly reduces uncertainty in the framework topology (SS). The most critical limiting factor is kinetic/phase control involving refractory Hf and volatile Se, especially avoiding Hf–Se binary or amorphous competing phases [Reasonable Inference], consistent with phase-control issues in SrZrSe3 synthesis (SS).
