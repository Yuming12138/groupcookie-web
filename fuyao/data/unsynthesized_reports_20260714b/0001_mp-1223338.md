### SECTION 5 — Scoring Rationale

**F1 — Thermodynamic Stability**  
The target sits at E_hull = 0.0500 eV/atom, which is exactly the ≤0.05 eV/atom threshold for a 90–100 score [Target]. CS contains multiple E_hull = 0 double perovskite analogues and one La₂NiMnO₆ analogue at 0.0427 eV/atom, so chemical analogues strongly support synthesizability [CS1, CS2, CS3, CS4, CS5]. TS analogues include near-threshold metastable oxides around 0.05–0.07 eV/atom, showing that similar thermodynamic offsets are experimentally accessible but not guaranteed as ground states [TS1, TS2, TS3, TS4, TS5].

**F2 — Route 1: Solid-State / Ceramic Oxide**  
The severity is moderate because close analogues use 1000–1300+ °C ceramic firing in air with repeated grinding, which triggers the >1000 °C and multi-step penalties [CS1, CS2, CS5, TS1]. The route avoids high pressure, vacuum, H₂/NH₃, and sealed volatile precursors, which keeps the score relatively high [CS1, CS4, CS5]. Remaining uncertainty is whether Rh₂O₃ or Rh metal reacts fast enough without flux or wet mixing [SS1, Reasonable Inference].

**F3 — Route 1: Solid-State / Ceramic Oxide**  
Reaction kinetics are the main weakness because oxide powders and Rh-bearing precursors are less active than nitrate or gel precursors [CS1, CS5, SS1]. The structural target is favorable because P2₁/c La₂NiMnO₆/La₂CoMnO₆ analogues match the desired double perovskite motif, but repeated heating is often needed for B-site ordering [Target, CS3, CS4, TS1]. The uncertainty is the rate of Mn/Rh ordering relative to Ni/Mn or Co/Mn ordering [CS5, TS1, Reasonable Inference].

**F2 — Route 2: Wet-Chemical Co-Precipitation / Citrate**  
The route has more processing steps than oxide grinding, including precipitation, washing, drying, calcination, pelletizing, and sintering, so it receives the multi-step penalty [CS3, SS3, TS3]. It also uses soluble Rh salts and strong bases/oxidants, which add handling risk compared with simple oxides [CS3, Reasonable Inference]. The route avoids high pressure and can be run in air, which is supported by La₂NiMnO₆ and related double perovskite data [CS3, TS3].

**F3 — Route 2: Wet-Chemical Co-Precipitation / Citrate**  
This route receives the best kinetic score because nitrate/co-precipitated/citrate precursors provide intimate cation mixing before crystallization [CS3, SS4, TS3]. The need for high-temperature sintering remains because the final phase is a crystalline ordered double perovskite, but shorter diffusion lengths should help Mn/Rh ordering [CS3, CS5, TS1, Reasonable Inference].
